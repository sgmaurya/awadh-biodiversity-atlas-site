## What this licence does not cover

- **Reference images downloaded from the web.** Pictures under `media/` that were not taken
  by a contributor keep their original licence. Each one must have a row in
  `media/credits.csv` (file, source URL, author, licence) before it is published; images
  without a credits row are kept for identification only and are not redistributed.
- **Quoted sources.** Short quotations and cited facts remain the work of their authors and
  are referenced in each profile.
- **Exact locations.** Published coordinates are rounded to two decimals (about 1 km).
  Precise locations of sensitive species are held privately and are not part of the
  published dataset.
