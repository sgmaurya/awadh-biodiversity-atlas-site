लहसुन / Garlic

Allium sativum L. — Amaryllidaceae

लहसुन — अवध और पूर्वांचल के मैदानों में शीतकाल में बोया जाने वाला एक अत्यंत महत्वपूर्ण कंद और मसाला पौधा है। इसके तीखे, सुगंधित शल्क-कंद (पोथी या जवे) और चपटे फीतेनुमा पत्ते भोजन में बघार और औषधीय उपयोग के लिए प्रसिद्ध हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources. Garlic is a scapose, bulbous herb propagated vegetatively across the plains as a cool-season annual. The underground storage organ is a compound or multiplex bulb situated on a flattened, disc-like basal stem. The compound bulb comprises multiple (typically six to thirty) sessile, oblong, curved secondary bulbs or bulblets, colloquially known as cloves (jawa or poti). Each clove is enveloped in a tough, white, silvery, or purplish membranous sheath (tunic) composed of protective scale leaves surrounding succulent storage parenchyma and a central vegetative bud. The entire cluster is enclosed within several common, papery, concentric outer tunics.

The radical leaves are distichous (arranged in two vertical ranks), linear-lanceolate, flat, distinctly keeled on the lower surface, and channeled above. The leaf sheaths are long, tubular, and closely overlapping, forming a sturdy cylindrical pseudostem above the soil surface. The leaf blade is acute at the apex, smooth, and glaucous green, possessing a solid cross-section completely devoid of the hollow central lumen characteristic of onions.

During the reproductive stage in late winter or spring, bolting plants send up a single, erect, solid, cylindrical flower scape from the center of the pseudostem. In hardneck forms, the young scape curls into a characteristic loop before straightening as the inflorescence expands. The inflorescence is a dense, globose terminal umbel enclosed initially within a long-beaked, membranous spathe that splits longitudinally.

The individual flowers are often rudimentary, sterile, and largely or completely replaced by dense clusters of small, ovoid, fleshy aerial bulbils (top-sets). Where true flowers develop, they are small, pedicellate, with a perianth of six free, lanceolate, scarious, greenish-white or pale rosy-purple tepals. The stamens are six, inserted at the base of the tepals, with three-cuspidate inner filaments. The ovary is superior, trilocular, containing two ovules per locule, though seed set is extremely rare in cultivated lineages due to genetic and developmental sterility.

हिंदी: इसके पत्ते चपटे, ठोस और फीतेनुमा होते हैं। जमीन के नीचे कई कलियों (जवों) का एक सफेद गांठदार कंद बनता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार एवं पारिस्थितिकी

Allium sativum is an obligate cultigen completely dependent on human vegetative propagation via cloves. It thrives under temperate and subtropical winters with bright sunlight and moderate moisture. Bulbing is photoperiod-sensitive, triggered when photoperiods lengthen and temperatures rise in February and March; during the shorter days of early winter, the plant directs energy into root establishment and foliage production.

The presence of organosulfur constituents, including allicin (Chopra et al. 1956, p. 11), serves as a biochemical defense against herbivorous pests and pathogens. In agricultural ecology, garlic acts as a botanical deterrent when planted around more vulnerable crops.

The dense fibrous root system remains shallow, spreading horizontally in the topsoil horizon.

हिंदी: इसकी गंध और रासायनिक तत्व हानिकारक कीटों और फफूंद को दूर रखते हैं। यह प्राकृतिक रूप से कीट-रोधी पौधा है।

Cultivation Calendar खेती का कैलेंडर

According to Duthie & Fuller (1893, Part III, pp. 34–35), the cloves are planted in October in drills, and the crop is ready for digging up in the hot weather when the leaves begin to turn yellow. The bulbs are then dried in the sun and stored for future use.

हिंदी: दुथी और फुलर (1893) के अनुसार लहसुन की कलियां अक्टूबर में कतारों में बोई जाती हैं, और गर्मियों में जब पत्तियां पीली पड़ने लगती हैं तब फसल खुदाई के लिए तैयार होती है। इसके बाद कंदों को धूप में सुखाकर संग्रहीत किया जाता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र एवं ऋतुचक्र

Following clove planting, adventitious roots emerge from the basal plate within a few days, followed by leaf emergence through the soil surface within a week or two. Vegetative growth continues vigorously through November, December, and January, building up photosynthetic leaf area in the cool climate.

In February, lengthening photoperiods and warming temperatures induce bulb differentiation. The leaf sheaths at the base thicken, and axillary buds swell into individual cloves, storing carbohydrates translocated from the foliage. As bulbs swell, no new leaves are produced.

By March, the outer leaf sheaths dry into protective papery tunics around the cloves. In some varieties, an aerial scape emerges, producing bulbils in the umbel, but viable botanical seeds are not produced. The above-ground foliage wilts and dries completely by April, marking the completion of the annual growth cycle and the beginning of bulb dormancy.

हिंदी: कलियां रोपने के बाद जड़ें और पत्ते निकलते हैं। सर्दियों में पौधा बढ़ता है और बसंत में कलियां मोटी होकर कंद बनती हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ एवं पारिस्थितिक संबंध

Although true flowering is sparse and sterile, aerial bulbils and occasional droplets secreted by rudimentary florets attract occasional visiting ants and hoverflies.

हिंदी: इसके विरल फूलों पर कभी-कभी चींटियां और सिरफिड मक्खियां आती हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Garlic is relatively robust against major insect outbreaks due to its repellent volatile profile, but it is routinely attacked by thrips. Thrips rasp the leaf surface and suck sap, producing silvery streaks and blotches that cause foliage to curl and dry from the tips down. Soil grubs occasionally damage the subterranean bulbs in sandy soils.

Fungal diseases include purple blotch, which causes sunken lesions on leaves that expand into purplish-brown patches, and leaf blight. In poorly drained fields, white rot attacks the bulb and roots, causing a cottony fungal growth dotted with black sclerotia. Management involves planting certified healthy cloves, well-drained raised beds, and broad-spectrum crop rotation.

हिंदी: थ्रिप्स कीट पत्तों का रस चूसते हैं और पर्पल ब्लॉच फफूंद पत्तों पर बैंगनी धब्बे बनाती है। उचित जल निकासी जरूरी है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि एवं मानव प्रासंगिकता

Garlic is a cornerstone of northern Indian culinary tradition and domestic herbal lore. It is universally used as a flavor base in curries, dals, gravies, and pickles, providing depth and aroma through tempering (tadka) in hot mustard oil or ghee. Duthie & Fuller (1893, Part III, pp. 34–35) note that the cloves are planted in October and the crop dug in the hot weather, and that garlic is much used as a spice and for flavouring pickles, and as a medicine for fevers, coughs, and nervous affections. Chopra et al. (1956, p. 11) record the bulb as carminative, expectorant and stimulant, containing allicin and useful in fevers and coughs. This is a record of published traditional use, not medical advice.

हिंदी: लहसुन भारतीय रसोई का प्रमुख मसाला है जो तड़के और पारंपरिक उपयोग के लिए प्रसिद्ध है।

Distribution वितरण

Allium sativum is cultivated across nearly every continent, adapted to temperate, Mediterranean, and subtropical climates. The species is believed to have originated in Central Asia (encompassing modern Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan) and northeastern Iran, derived from the wild ancestor Allium longicuspis. It was domesticated in deep antiquity and spread across the ancient world through trade routes.

Similar Species मिलती-जुलती प्रजातियाँ

Garlic (Allium sativum) is most closely allied with other cultivated members of the genus Allium. Onion (onion, Allium cepa, FLR-0144) produces hollow, cylindrical, fistular leaves with a circular cross-section, and a simple, single, tunicated bulb with concentric fleshy leaf bases rather than a compound cluster of cloves; its umbels produce fertile white flowers and viable black seeds. Leek (Allium ampeloprasum) or companion mustard produces flat, keeled leaves resembling garlic, but forms a thickened, elongated cylindrical blanched pseudostem rather than a distinct compound bulb composed of cloves.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण एवं नामकरण

Allium sativum was described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum. The genus Allium is placed within the subfamily Allioideae of the family Amaryllidaceae (historically grouped under Liliaceae or Alliaceae). Cultivated garlic is broadly classified into two main horticultural subspecies or varietal groups: the hardneck group (subsp. ophioscorodon), which produces a stiff, coiled flower stalk and aerial bulbils, and the softneck group (subsp. sativum), which rarely produces a scape and possesses pliable leaves easily braided for storage. In J. F. Duthie's Flora of the Upper Gangetic Plain, the species is noted as widely grown across the provinces for its medicinal and culinary virtues.

Original description. Allium sativum was named and described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum (vol. 1, p. 296).

Subspecies. Cultivated garlic comprises hardneck varieties (subsp. ophioscorodon), which produce a coiled flower scape with bulbils, and softneck varieties (subsp. sativum).

Family and order placement. The genus Allium is placed in the subfamily Allioideae of the family Amaryllidaceae, order Asparagales.

IUCN status rationale. Allium sativum is an ancient cultigen with worldwide agricultural distribution and is categorized as Not Evaluated (NE) on the IUCN Red List.
