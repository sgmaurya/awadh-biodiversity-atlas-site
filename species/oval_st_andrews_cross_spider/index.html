सेंट एंड्रयूज़ क्रॉस मकड़ी / Oval St Andrew's Cross Spider

Argiope aemula (Walckenaer, 1841) — Araneidae

सेंट एंड्रयूज़ क्रॉस मकड़ी — गर्मियों के अंत और मानसून के दौरान खेतों और झाड़ियों में एक पीली या भूरे रंग की मकड़ी जो अपना खड़ा जाला बुनती है। यह जोग मकड़ी से छोटी है और इसका रंग हल्का है। जाले के बीच में एक मोटी ज़िगज़ैग रेशम की पट्टी होती है। खेतों और बागों में एक साधारण मकड़ी।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription

Male size4–5 mm; brownish
ColorationPale yellow to tan-brown; subdued patterning compared to A. anasuja
AbdomenOval, pale with darker chevrons or stripes
LegsBanded, held in X-pose
WebVertical orb, 25–40 cm
StabilimentumVisible but less prominent than A. anasuja

Field tip: Smaller than Signature Spider; much paler coloration (tan, buff, or pale yellow rather than bold yellow-black); less striking appearance. Found in open grassland more often than garden settings.

Morphology आकारिकी

FeatureMeasurement
Female body length9–14 mm
Male body length4–5 mm
Female legspan22–32 mm
Web diameter25–40 cm

Female: Cephalothorax pale brown. Abdomen pale yellow, buff, or tan with darker chevron marks — pattern much less bold than other Argiope species. Legs banded in darker and lighter colours, held in X configuration. Eyes typical of orb-weavers.

Male: Brownish, much smaller, found at web periphery.

Web: Vertical orb with sticky spiral threads and non-sticky radials. Stabilimentum present but less elaborate than in A. anasuja.

Ecological Role पारिस्थितिक भूमिका

Insectivore: Captures flying insects in its web — small flies, grasshoppers, small moths, ants, and wasps. Less selective for prey type than jumping spiders, capturing whatever flies into the web.

Population control: In grassland and field-margin habitats, density can reach several webs per 10 m², providing cumulative pest regulation.

Predation: Preyed upon by pompilid wasps and small insectivorous birds.

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Nov–MayOverwintering as eggs; adults die with cooling

Single annual generation.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Field pest control: Natural predator of small flying insects in agricultural settings. Particularly important in organic farming systems without chemical insecticide use.

Safety: Harmless venom; does not bite unless severely provoked.

Habitat loss: Vulnerable to pesticide spraying and habitat conversion. Absent from fields under regular insecticide treatment.

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Argiope aemula is expected in open grassland, field margins, and hedgerows July through November in suitable areas. Prefers more open habitat than garden-dwelling Argiope anasuja. Webs constructed at 50 cm–1.5 m height in tall grass and low vegetation. Less abundant than other Argiope species in most villages, but locally common in fields managed without intensive insecticide spraying.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia — India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand.

In India: Widespread in suitable grassland and field-margin habitats.

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded across the state in open vegetation.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in field margins and grassland habitats; occurrence data needed.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described as Epeira aemula Walckenaer, 1841. Transferred to Argiope by later taxonomists. Type locality: Southern Europe/Mediterranean.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Araneae, family Araneidae. Genus Argiope Savigny, 1822.

IUCN status rationale. Not formally assessed. Common and stable in suitable habitat.
