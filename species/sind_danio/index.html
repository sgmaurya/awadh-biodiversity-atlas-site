सिंध डैनियो मछली / Sind Danio

Devario devario (Hamilton, 1822) — Cyprinidae

सिंध डैनियो मछली — मैदानी नदियों, स्वच्छ नालों और दलदली झीलों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, चौड़ी और अत्यंत सुंदर चमकीली देसी मछली है। इसका शरीर दोनों तरफ से चपटा और गहरा होता है, जिस पर पीछे की तरफ नीली और सुनहरी चमकदार धारियाँ होती हैं। यह पानी की ऊपरी सतह पर झुंड में तेजी से तैरती है और मच्छरों के लार्वा को बड़े चाव से खाती है। यह एक्वेरियम रखने वालों और प्रकृति प्रेमियों के बीच बहुत लोकप्रिय है।

Field tip: Look near the surface of clear, weed-edged streams and slow river pools. A small, deep-bodied fish swimming actively near the surface with vivid steel-blue longitudinal stripes running along the tail and extending into the caudal fin is the Sind Danio.

हिंदी: यह छोटी, चौड़ी मछली पानी की सतह पर तैरती है। इसके पिछले हिस्से और पूँछ पर चमकीली नीली धारियाँ होती हैं। इसके मुंह पर कोई मूंछ नहीं होती।

Morphology आकारिकी

Size as given by FishBase (Froese & Pauly 2025):

MeasurementRangeSource
Maximum length10 cm TLFroese & Pauly (2025), FishBase

Adult description:

Devario devario possesses a strongly compressed, deep, and oval-rhomboidal body profile (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal and ventral contours are equally and gracefully convex (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is relatively small and compressed, with prominent lateral eyes. The mouth is small, directed obliquely upward (superior), and completely devoid of rostral and maxillary barbels (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin has an exceptionally long base, originating slightly in advance of the anal fin, and is supported by 15 to 18 branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin is similarly elongate, bearing 16 to 19 branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The caudal fin is emarginate to slightly forked with equal rounded lobes (Hamilton 1822). The lateral line is complete, containing 35 to 40 scales, running in a pronounced downward curve along the lower abdomen (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Living specimens display dazzling iridescent coloration (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The dorsum is greenish-olive, and the belly is silvery-white. The posterior third of the flanks is adorned with three brilliant steel-blue or turquoise-blue longitudinal stripes divided by narrow golden-yellow lines, the middle stripe extending straight through the caudal fin rays (Hamilton 1822). The anterior half of the flanks exhibits subtle vertical bluish-golden bars and reticulations. The fins are hyaline to pale yellowish-green.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 10 सेमी तक लंबा, चपटा और गोल-चौकोर होता है। पीठ का पंख लंबा होता है। शरीर का पिछला भाग नीली और सुनहरी चमकदार धारियों से सजा होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Sind Danio is a diurnal, active, and gregarious surface-to-midwater schooling fish (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It prefers slow to moderately flowing streams, weed-fringed oxbow wetlands, canals, and perennial ponds with clear water and abundant aquatic vegetation.

Schools comprising tens to hundreds of individuals patrol the upper thirty centimetres of the water column (Daniels 2002). Their upturned mouths are specifically adapted for surface skimming, enabling them to seize terrestrial insects trapped in the surface film as well as emerging aquatic midges (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). When alarmed by predatory birds or ripples from the shore, the school executes synchronized, lightning-fast evasive dives into the safety of submerged Hydrilla beds (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की ऊपरी सतह पर बड़े झुंडों में बहुत फुर्ती से तैरती है। इसका मुँह ऊपर की ओर खुला होता है जिससे यह पानी की सतह पर गिरने वाले कीड़ों को तुरंत पकड़ लेती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian alluvial waters across the agricultural year (Talwar & Jhingran 1991; Daniels 2002):

Reproduction occurs during the monsoon season when warm rainwater floods low-lying fields and margins (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Courtship is vigorous, with males displaying heightened metallic blues and chasing females through shallow vegetation. Females scatter non-adhesive or semi-adhesive eggs among submerged grass blades. The eggs hatch after a few days, and tiny larvae cling to plant leaves before developing into free-swimming, plankton-feeding fry (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: बरसात के दिनों में जब खेतों और नालों में नया पानी भरता है, यह घास में अंडे देती है। कुछ ही दिनों में अंडों से बच्चे निकल आते हैं और वे पानी के सूक्ष्म जीवों को खाकर बढ़ते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Devario devario is an active omnivorous surface predator and micro-feeder (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Mosquito wrigglers and pupae (Culex and Anopheles), captured at the water-air interface (Daniels 2002).
  • Small terrestrial insects falling onto the water surface, including flying ants, aphids, and midges (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Aquatic insect larvae, including chironomids and mayfly nymphs.
  • Planktonic micro-crustaceans, such as water fleas (Daphnia) and copepods.
  • Fine filamentous algae and soft organic detritus browsed from submerged plant leaves.
हिंदी: यह पानी की सतह पर तैरने वाले मच्छरों के लार्वा, पानी में गिरने वाली चींटियों, छोटे कीड़ों और काई को खाती है। यह मच्छरों की रोकथाम में बहुत मददगार है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Piscivorous birds: Kingfishers, little egrets, and pond herons take advantage of surface schools in clear shallows.
  • Predatory fishes: Murrels like Channa punctata (Spotted Snakehead), small catfishes like Mystus tengara (Tengra Catfish), and Xenentodon cancila (Freshwater Garfish) attack schools from beneath.
  • Predatory aquatic insects: Giant water bugs and dragonfly naiads ambush fry in weed nurseries.
हिंदी: सतह पर तैरने के कारण इसे बगुले और किंगफिशर आसानी से पकड़ लेते हैं। पानी के नीचे से गिरई मछली और सुइया (garfish) इस पर हमला करती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Natural mosquito control: Because of its specialized surface-feeding habits and insatiable appetite for mosquito larvae, Devario devario is an exceptional indigenous biological control agent against vector mosquitoes transmitting malaria and filariasis (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Introducing schools into rural village tanks, ornamental ponds, and abandoned borrow pits dramatically reduces mosquito emergence (Daniels 2002).

Aquarium and nutritional trade: Popular worldwide in the ornamental fish hobby as the Sind Danio or Bengal Danio due to its iridescent blue sheen and peaceful schooling behavior. In rural eastern Uttar Pradesh, it is also caught alongside other small carplets in fine-mesh shore seines and consumed locally as a mineral-rich dietary supplement.

Traditional: Consumed as part of mixed wild catches in rural wetland fishing.

Today: Valued as an indigenous ornamental aquarium fish and natural biological mosquito predator.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing captive breeding techniques to evaluate larval consumption rates of dengue and encephalitis vectors in artificial containers.

हिंदी: यह मछली मच्छरों के लार्वा को खत्म करने के लिए बहुत उपयोगी है। अपनी सुंदरता के कारण यह एक्वेरियम में भी पाली जाती है और गाँव के लोग इसे भोजन के रूप में भी खाते हैं।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the northern and central Indian subcontinent, recorded from India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Widely distributed in the Indus, Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Mahanadi river basins, extending into the Krishna and Godavari systems (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ghaghara, Rapti, Gomti, and Ganges drainages and associated alluvial floodplain lakes.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident native in the Kuwano, Manwar, and Ami river basins and clear perennial tals.

हिंदी: यह भारत, पाकिस्तान, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश की नदियों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में यह गंगा, घाघरा और कुआनो नदी के स्वच्छ नालों में सामान्य है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Cyprinus devario in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 341. Type locality: Rivers and ponds of Bengal.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized across its range.

Family and order placement. Order Cypriniformes, family Cyprinidae, subfamily Danioninae. The genus Devario Heckel, 1843, comprises deep-bodied danionins separated from Danio by distinct osteological and molecular features.

Phylogenetic notes. Devario devario is the type species of the genus Devario. Molecular phylogenies place it in a well-supported clade of South Asian danionins characterized by broad bodies and surface-adapted morphology (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, stable wild populations throughout northern South Asia.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Devario devario है और यह Cyprinidae परिवार की Danioninae उपपरिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
