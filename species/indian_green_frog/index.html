हरा मेंढक / Indian Green Frog

Euphlyctis hexadactylus (Lesson, 1834) — Dicroglossidae

हरा मेंढक — गंगा के मैदान का सबसे आम मेंढक, जो गाँव के खेत और तालाब में हर जगह है। हरा, लंबा, और चपटा, पानी के किनारे की घास में बैठकर कीड़ों का शिकार करता है। इसकी टर्र-टर्र आवाज़ रात भर बरसात में गूँजती है, और पहली बरसात के बाद तालाब-गड्ढों में हज़ारों छोटे काले बच्चे दिख जाते हैं। बिल्ली से लेकर ऊदबिलाव तक सब इसे खाते हैं।

Field tip: A bright green, slender frog with a pointed snout, sitting at the water's edge in rice paddies and pond margins. The green colour and slender body distinguish it from toads and other frogs. In monsoon, the loud calling chorus at dusk and through the night identifies it.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: तालाब के किनारे की घास में तेज़ हरा, लंबा, चपटा। सिर नुकीला। आँखें बड़ी। बरसात की रात टर्र-टर्र की आवाज़ इसकी है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Body and limbs: The body is slender and slightly flattened, adapted for rapid movement on the ground and water surface. The hind limbs are long and powerful, with the toes webbed except the last one, allowing strong jumping and swimming.

Head and sensory organs: The head is moderate and pointed at the snout. The eyes are large and prominent, with golden irises. The tympanum is distinct and large. The vocal sac in males is single, under the throat, inflating to produce the characteristic call.

Skin: Smooth to minutely granular. The colour is bright green, sometimes with a yellow dorsal stripe and yellow spots or bars on the limbs. The ventral surface is white or cream, sometimes with darker markings.

Tadpole: Black, schooling, with a high tail fin; reaches 45 mm before metamorphosis.

हिंदी: शरीर लंबा, थोड़ा चपटा। पिछली टाँगें लंबी, ताकतवर, अँगुलियाँ झिल्ली से जुड़ी — छोड़ी हुई अँगुली को छोड़कर। सिर नुकीला, आँखें बड़ी, सुनहरी। गले में एक वोकल थैली। रंग हरा, कभी धारियों या धब्बों वाला।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

The frog of the working landscape. It lives at the margins of water—paddies, tanks, ditches and canals—where it hunts insects by day and night. It waits in the shade of grasses and emergent plants, snapping up flies, mosquitoes, small beetles and moths. It can tolerate temporary water loss and survives on damp ground without standing water. With the monsoon rains, males move to breeding pools and ditches and call from the water's edge or from floating plants. The calling is intense and continuous at dusk; males fight for calling positions and females are attracted to the calls. Females lay eggs in long strings that adhere to aquatic plants; the tadpoles develop rapidly in warm shallow water and metamorphose into froglets in 4–6 weeks.

Snakes (Checkered Keelback, Indian Cobra), Indian Pond Heron, owls and Bengal Monitor eat the adults; dragonfly nymphs and giant water bugs prey on tadpoles.

हिंदी: खेत-तालाब के किनारे की मेंढक। पानी-पीपल की छाया में बैठकर मक्खी, मच्छर, भृंग खाती है। रात-दिन शिकार। थोड़ी सूखी ज़मीन पर भी रह सकती है। बरसात में तालाबों में इकट्ठी होकर नर टर्र-टर्र करते हैं। मादा पौधों पर लड़ी में अंडे देती है। टैडपोल काले, झुंड में, चार-छह हफ़्ते में बड़े हो जाते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Adults: Two-Spotted Cricket, African Mole Cricket, mosquito, small flies, small beetles, Mound-building Termite swarms, and other arthropods.
  • Tadpoles: algae, detritus, and the film on water plants.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Snakes: Checkered Keelback, Indian Cobra, Indian Rat Snake, Common Krait at night.
  • Birds: Indian Pond Heron, Spotted Owlet, Black Kite, House Crow, cormorants.
  • Reptiles: Bengal Monitor, Checkered Keelback, Common Bronzeback Tree Snake.
  • Others: aquatic insects, dragonfly nymphs and giant water bugs on tadpoles.
  • Domestic animals: snakes in the field, cats and mongooses.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pest control. The most abundant frog in rice-growing areas; eats insects day and night in paddies and their margins. A productive paddy without chemical pesticides will have hundreds of these frogs per hectare. Their absence is often a sign of pesticide use.

Harmless. Does not bite or spit; not toxic. The myth that frogs cause warts is false.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use: none recorded for this species in the region, though other frogs are eaten.

Today. Common and abundant; a sign of a reasonably productive rice environment.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Frog populations in sprayed vs. unsprayed paddies and their effect on insect communities is an open question for village-scale research; a count of frogs per plot and insect catch in adjacent paddies would establish a local baseline.

हिंदी: धान के खेतों का सबसे आम मेंढक; कीटों का शिकार दिन-रात। खतरनाक नहीं, जहरीला नहीं। काटता नहीं। मेंढक से मस्से होने की बात झूठ। कीटनाशक इसे खत्म कर देते हैं; अगर मेंढक नहीं है तो खेत में विष का अंदाज़ा लगा लो।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to India and Sri Lanka; widespread across the Indo-Gangetic plain and peninsular India.

In India: Throughout the subcontinent from the Himalayan foothills to the south, in all major river systems and associated wetlands.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout the plains, particularly in paddy-growing areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in all rice paddies and wetland margins; likely very abundant.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. In which month do the first calling males appear in your village, and in which do they stop? / आपके गाँव में पहली बार नर कब से टर्र-टर्र करना शुरू करते हैं, कब बंद? — Walk pond edges at dusk from March to November.
  2. How many frogs can you count in one hour of standing at a pond edge at dusk? / एक घंटे में शाम को तालाब के किनारे कितने मेंढक दिखते हैं? — Note the loudest hour.
  3. Where are the egg strings, and on what plants are they laid? — Look in breeding pools in June and July.
  4. How many tadpoles are in one egg string, and how long until they metamorphose? — Collect eggs carefully and observe.
  5. Are frogs more abundant in paddies that are not sprayed than in sprayed paddies? / जहाँ कीटनाशक नहीं डाला जाता वहाँ मेंढक ज़्यादा होते हैं? — Compare two paddies, one sprayed and one not.

Field rule: A bright green, slender frog with a pointed snout and golden eyes, calling in monsoon pools with a rapid curr-curr-curr trill, is the Indian green frog; the slender body and green colour are the key.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Lesson in 1834 in Illustrations de Zoologie. Long placed in Rana and then Fejervarya; recently placed in Euphlyctis when that genus was redefined to include green frogs with certain morphological characters.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised, though populations vary geographically.

Family and order placement. Dicroglossidae, the true frogs of Asia; order Anura. The genus Euphlyctis contains several green Asian frogs.

Phylogenetic notes. Euphlyctis is a clade of Asian dicroglossids; the genus is well supported in molecular phylogenies.

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern. Extremely abundant and widespread; no range contraction documented.
