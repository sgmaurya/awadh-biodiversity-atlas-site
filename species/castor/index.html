अरंडी / Castor

Ricinus communis L. — Euphorbiaceae

अरंडी — बड़ी, हथेली जैसी कटी हुई पत्तियों वाला तेज़ी से बढ़ने वाला पौधा जो कूड़े के ढेर, नहर की पटरी और खाली ज़मीन पर अपने आप उग आता है। इसके काँटेदार फलों के बीज से अरंडी का तेल निकलता है जो दीये, दवा, साबुन और मशीन में काम आता है। लेकिन बीज में रिसिन नाम का तीखा ज़हर होता है: बिना गरम किए दबाया तेल या चबाए बीज जानलेवा हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitSoft-wooded shrub 1–4 m, sometimes a small tree; hollow, often reddish stems
LeavesHuge, 15–60 cm across, round in outline, palmately cut into 5–11 toothed lobes; stalk attached inside the blade edge
FlowersOn an upright spike: cream male flowers below, red-stigma'd female flowers above
FruitRound capsule 1.5–2.5 cm covered in soft spines, green turning brown, bursting into three
SeedsGlossy, mottled brown and grey like a tick, 1 cm, with a small white knob at one end
Smell and sapLeaves faintly rank when crushed; sap watery, not milky

Field tip: The biggest-leaved weed on any rubbish heap or canal bank. Confirm with the spiny green capsules and the beautiful mottled seeds. Handle the seeds with care and never let children play with them.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सबसे बड़ी, हथेली जैसी कटी पत्तियाँ; लाल-हरा खोखला तना; ऊपर काँटेदार गोल फल; अंदर चितकबरे चमकीले बीज। बीज बच्चों से दूर रखें।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (plains populations, northern India):

MeasurementRange
Height1–4 m, rarely 6 m
Stem diameter2–8 cm
Leaf diameter15–60 cm
Leaf lobes5–11
Capsule diameter1.5–2.5 cm
Seed8–12 mm

Stem: Hollow, smooth, green or red-purple with a waxy bloom, branching from near the base; woody only at the base in old plants.

Leaves: Alternate, peltate (the stalk joins the blade inside its margin), the lobes pointed and serrate, glossy green or bronze-red in some forms; a pair of glands at the top of the stalk.

Flowers: Monoecious, on a terminal panicle. Male flowers lower, with many branched stamens; female flowers above with a three-lobed ovary and red feathery stigmas. Wind-pollinated.

Fruit and seed: A three-celled capsule with soft prickles, drying and splitting explosively to throw the seeds a metre or more. Seeds carry an oily appendage (caruncle) that ants carry off, planting them further. The seed holds 40–55% oil and, in the meal, the lectin ricin.

हिंदी: तना खोखला, लाल या हरा; पत्ती का डंठल पत्ती के बीच से जुड़ा; फूल एक ही डाली पर नीचे नर और ऊपर मादा; सूखा फल फटकर बीज उछाल देता है। बीज में 40 से 55 प्रतिशत तेल और रिसिन ज़हर।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A classic pioneer: it comes up first and fastest on disturbed, nitrogen-rich ground, a rubbish heap, a cattle-shed margin, a fresh embankment, and can be two metres tall within a season. It is short-lived, a few years, and is replaced as the site settles. Though African in origin it has been in India so long, and is so widely grown, that it is naturalised across the plain.

Grazing animals avoid it; the leaves are unpalatable and the seed toxic. The flowers are visited by bees for pollen though pollination is by wind. Its chief ecological service is covering raw ground quickly and shading out worse weeds such as Congress Grass on village margins.

हिंदी: खाली, गंदी, खुदी हुई ज़मीन पर सबसे पहले उगता है और एक बरसात में दो मीटर हो जाता है। कुछ साल जीता है। पशु इसे नहीं खाते। गाँव के किनारे कांग्रेस घास को दबाने में इसकी छाया काम करती है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollen foragers: Asian Honey Bee and Dwarf Honey Bee gather pollen from the male flowers.
  • Seed carriers: ants take the caruncle-bearing seeds.
  • Fellow pioneers: shares waste ground with Congress Grass, Dhatura and Giant Milkweed.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Castor semilooper (Achaea janata) and the castor hairy caterpillar strip leaves in the monsoon.
  • Capsule borer (Conogethes punctiferalis) bores the fruit.
  • Leafhoppers and whitefly on crop plantings; grey rot of capsules in wet weather.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Toxicity first. The seed contains ricin, among the most poisonous plant substances known; a few chewed seeds can kill a child. Commercial castor oil is safe because ricin stays in the seed cake and is destroyed by heat; home-pressed cold oil is not. Keep seeds from children and livestock. The leaves and the cake are also toxic to animals.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use (documented uses; not medical advice):

  • Lamp oil. Castor oil lit the lamps of northern India before kerosene; in the villages of the district it was the diya oil of the poor.
  • Medicine. The purified oil is the classical purgative of the Indian and European pharmacopoeias, and the leaves, warmed, were applied for swellings and to the breast to increase milk; recorded in Kirtikar & Basu and Chopra. The pharmacopoeial oil is a processed product.
  • Soap and lubricant. Castor oil soap and castor grease are old village products; the oil does not go rancid quickly.
  • Boundary crop. Planted along bunds and in sugarcane borders for the seed and as a windbreak; intercropped with groundnut in drier districts.
  • Silk. The leaves feed the eri silkworm, a tradition of the north-east now trialled elsewhere.

Today. India grows around 80% of the world's castor, mostly in Gujarat and Rajasthan; the oil goes into lubricants, paints, plastics, cosmetics and biodiesel research. Eastern UP grows little, but wild plants are everywhere.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Castor oil as a feedstock for biodiesel and bio-based polymers, and the detoxified cake as a fertiliser, are well studied at industrial scale; the village-scale question is whether boundary-grown castor seed sells, and the safety problem of home pressing argues against anything but selling the seed to a mill.

हिंदी: पहली बात ज़हर की: बीज में रिसिन है, चबाए हुए दो-चार बीज बच्चे की जान ले सकते हैं। मिल का तेल सुरक्षित है, घर का कच्चा तेल नहीं। पुराने ज़माने में अरंडी का तेल दीये में जलता था, जुलाब में और साबुन में लगता था। आज भारत दुनिया का ज़्यादातर अरंडी उगाता है, पर गुजरात-राजस्थान में। यहाँ यह जंगली उगती है; बीज बेचना ही सुरक्षित रास्ता है।

To be expected on every rubbish heap, canal and railway bank and neglected village margin in the district, and occasionally planted along bunds. Among the easiest species in the atlas to confirm with a photograph of the leaves and spiny fruit.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to north-east Africa; naturalised throughout the tropics and subtropics of the world.

In India: Cultivated and wild in every state up to about 2,000 m.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common wild on waste ground throughout; grown as a minor oilseed.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected everywhere on disturbed ground.

Field rule: Giant palmately-cut leaves whose stalk joins the blade in from the edge, plus soft-spined round capsules with mottled seeds, is castor.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Linnaeus, Species Plantarum 2: 1007 (1753). Ricinus is the Latin for a tick, after the look of the seed; communis, "common".

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised, but the species is extremely variable in size, leaf colour and seed, and many cultivated forms have been named.

Family and order placement. Euphorbiaceae, the spurge family, order Malpighiales; the genus holds this single species.

Phylogenetic notes. Ricinus is isolated within the Acalyphoideae of the Euphorbiaceae with no close living relatives. Its genome was sequenced in 2010 in connection with oil breeding.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. A cosmopolitan weed and crop.
