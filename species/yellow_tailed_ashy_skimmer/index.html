येलो-टेल्ड ऐशी स्किमर / Yellow-tailed Ashy Skimmer

Potamarcha congener (Rambur, 1842) — Libellulidae

येलो-टेल्ड ऐशी स्किमर — एक छोटी असली व्याधपतंग (dragonfly) जिसके नर का शरीर हल्के भूरे रंग का (ashy) होता है और पूँछ (abdomen) का अंत पीला-नारंगी होता है। मादा समान रंग की होती है लेकिन कम चमकीली। यह उथले जलाशयों और दलदलों में पाई जाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmall dragonfly (body 30–34 mm; wingspan 40–46 mm)
BuildCompact; wings held open at rest
ColourAshy-grey thorax and abdomen with yellowish-orange tip
Abdomen tipYellow or orange-yellow (diagnostic)
EyesLarge, dark
BehaviourWeak to moderate flight; perches on vegetation or ground

Field tip: The distinctive yellow-orange abdomen tip combined with grey body makes this skimmer distinctive. It is smaller and less robust than most other dragonflies of the region.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length30–34 mm
Hindwing length20–23 mm
Wing span40–46 mm

Adult male: Head dark brown. Thorax ashy-grey to brownish-grey (the "ashy" coloration). Abdomen ashy-grey with yellow or orange-yellow coloration at the tip (segments 8–10). Wings transparent with dark pterostigma.

Adult female: Similar to male; coloration slightly less bright.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Potamarcha congener is a small, generalist dragonfly of shallow wetlands, marshes, and flooded fields. It is tolerant of variable water conditions and can be found in temporary and semi-permanent water bodies. Males perch on vegetation or open ground and pursue flying insects with moderate vigor.

Larval Stage

Naiads are small, brown, and found in shallow water among vegetation. Larval duration is approximately 8–10 weeks. Emergence occurs year-round.

Not documented as a pest. May assist in mosquito control in agricultural areas.

Look for the small, ashy-grey dragonfly with yellow-orange abdomen tip perching on vegetation. A photograph showing the yellow-tailed ashy skimmer, with date and location, would confirm a local record.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia; widespread from Pakistan and India to Myanmar and Thailand.

In India: Common in plains.

In Uttar Pradesh: Scattered records.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in shallow wetlands; unconfirmed locally.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Rambur described Potamarcha congener in 1842. Type locality: India. The genus Potamarcha suggests river-association (potam- = river; -archa = ruler). The species name congener (Latin: of the same kind) refers to shared characteristics with related species.

Subspecies. Monotypic.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae.

Phylogenetic notes. Potamarcha is a small genus of primarily Asian dragonflies. P. congener is one of the most widespread members.

IUCN status rationale. Listed as Least Concern (LC). Broad range and stable populations.
