ग्रे-ब्रेस्टेड प्रिनिया / Grey-breasted Prinia

Prinia hodgsonii Burton, 1835 — Cisticolidae

ग्रे-ब्रेस्टेड प्रिनिया — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के कृषि क्षेत्रों, नरकुल की झाड़ियों और दलदली जमीन पर पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, सक्रिय चिड़िया, जो सदा "चिप-चिप" करती हुई इधर-उधर फुदकती रहती है। इसका सिर भूरा-धूसर, छाती हल्की धूसर होती है। यह घास और कम झाड़ियों में रहती है, और कभी अकेले, कभी छोटे झुंड में देखी जाती है। इसकी पूँछ छोटी और मज़बूत होती है, जिससे यह कमज़ोर तने को पकड़ सकती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

BehaviourActive and restless; short hops within grass

Field tip: A small grey-breasted warbler in grasslands and reed beds, constantly calling with sharp chips. Look for it hopping in low grass and small bushes near water. Often cocks its short tail.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

Size: 13–14 cm long; small warbler.

Plumage: Upperparts brownish-grey to grey-brown. Crown darker than back. Rump warmer brown.

Underparts: Pale grey breast and underparts; slightly warmer buff on flanks. Throat whitish.

Eyebrow stripe: Pale buff or whitish supercilium (eyebrow) visible in good light.

Bill: Small, pointed, blackish above with pale lower mandible; adapted for gleaning insects.

Legs and feet: Pale pinkish-brown; moderate length, suitable for clinging to stems.

Eyes: Dark.

Sexes: Slightly dimorphic; males average slightly longer tail and wing.

Juveniles: Duller than adults; warmer buff tones.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Contact call: Repeated sharp chip or chip-chip, often given from perch.
  2. Song: Rapid chittering phrase, less structured than other warblers; chip-chip-chip-chip-chu-chu-chu.
  3. Alarm call: Harsh churr when alarmed.
हिंदी: अकेले या साथ "चिप-चिप" आवाज़ करता है। गुस्से में "चर-चर" करता है। गीत नहीं, सिर्फ तेज़ आवाज़।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Grassland and reed specialist: Confined to grasslands, reed beds, and sedge swamps. Forages among low vegetation for:

  • Small insects and spiders
  • Grasshoppers and crickets
  • Small beetles
  • Larvae

Feeding: Actively gleaning insects from grass stems and leaves. Hops along grass blades and short stems.

Social behaviour: Solitary or in pairs; sometimes small loose groups in suitable habitat. Maintains contact with frequent calls.

Breeding: Monogamous pairs; likely helpers at nest in some populations.

Roosting: Roost within grass or reed clumps; well-concealed.

Movement: Sedentary; resident year-round with local movements following grass condition and food availability.

हिंदी: अकेले रहता है। घास में कीटें खाता है। पूँछ पकड़ के तने को पकड़ता है। साल भर एक जगह रहता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Year-round resident: Breeds in the region; no seasonal migration.

Breeding: March–August; peaks in May–July. Builds nest of woven grass and plant down.

Nest: Small, domed or pouch-like nest of grass and down, suspended in reed or grass clumps.

Clutch: 3–4 eggs; incubation 13–14 days.

Fledging: Young leave nest at 12–14 days. 2–3 broods per season possible.

Vegetation phenology: Activity peaks with grass and reed growth; declines in dry seasons.

हिंदी: साल भर रहता है। मार्च से अगस्त में अंडे देता है। घास से घोंसला बनाता है। 14 दिन में बच्चे उड़ जाते हैं।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Shrikes, small eagles, Black Drongo
  • At nest: Snakes, monitor lizards, corvids
  • Mammalian predators: Wild cats, mongooses, weasels
  • Parasites: Avian parasites, mites
हिंदी: छोटी चील इसका शिकार कर सकती है। साँप घोंसलों को तोड़ते हैं। कौवे शत्रु होते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pest control: Feeds on grasshoppers, crickets, and other grassland arthropods. Contributes to natural pest control in agricultural areas.

Habitat dependence: Depends on maintenance of grasslands and reed beds. Heavy grazing or conversion of grasslands to monocultures eliminates habitat.

Indicator species: Presence indicates healthy grassland and wetland ecosystems.

हिंदी: यह चिड़िया वन्यजीव अधिनियम 1972 की अंतर्गत संरक्षित है। घास के मैदानों का संरक्षण इसके लिए महत्वपूर्ण है।

Range: Expected in suitable grassland and reed-bed habitat throughout Basti–Ayodhya region, particularly near wetlands.

Habitat preference: Reed beds, sedge swamps, and tall grasslands adjacent to water bodies; cultivated fields with grass cover.

Seasonality: Resident year-round; populations fluctuate with grass growth and water availability.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia from foothills to plains.

In India: Distributed across northern and eastern India in suitable grassland habitat.

In Uttar Pradesh: Resident in northern and eastern districts in grasslands and reed beds.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Regular resident in reed beds and grasslands associated with the Ghaghra–Rapti wetland complex.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Edward Burton in 1835 in the Proceedings of the Zoological Society of London, as Prinia hodgsonii. Type locality is northern India.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Cisticolidae (cisticolas and prinias). The genus Prinia contains the prinias, grassland specialists of Asia and Africa.

Phylogenetic notes. Closely related to other Prinia species, particularly Plain Prinia and Ashy Prinia. Forms part of a clade of grassland and wetland specialising cisticolas.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) globally. Populations remain widespread in suitable habitat. Habitat degradation from agricultural intensification is a long-term concern but populations persist in wetland reserves.

हिंदी: यह चिड़िया पूरे भारत के घास के मैदानों में मिलती है। संरक्षित है।
