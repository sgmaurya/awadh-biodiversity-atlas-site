एशियन जल घास / Asian Watergrass

Hygroryza aristata (Retz.) Nees ex Wight & Arn. — Poaceae

एशियन जल घास — उत्तर भारत के तालों, पोखरों और दलदली जलस्रोतों पर तैरने वाली एक अत्यंत विशिष्ट जलीय घास। इसके पत्तों के डंठल हवा से भरे हुए, फूले और स्पंज जैसे होते हैं, जो इसे पानी की सतह पर तैरते रहने में मदद करते हैं। पानी पर तैरते हुए इसके घने चटाईनुमा जाल जलपक्षियों और बत्तखों को सुरक्षित भोजन व विश्राम स्थल प्रदान करते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitFree-floating stoloniferous aquatic perennial grass forming dense surface mats (Bor 1960)
CulmsSpongy, 30–100 cm long, floating, rooting profusely at nodes with feathery pinkish-green roots (Bor 1960)
PetiolesSwollen, hollow, inflated into a spongy bladder functioning as a buoyant float
LeavesOvate-oblong, 3–8 cm long, 10–20 mm wide, obtuse, glaucous, papillose beneath (Bor 1960)
InflorescenceTriangular or ovate open panicle 5–8 cm long, emerging erect above the water (Bor 1960)
SpikeletsSolitary, lanceolate, 7–8 mm long, terminating in a long slender scaberulous awn 10–14 mm long (Bor 1960)

Field tip: Look on the surface of calm village ponds and oxbow tals in October for floating mats of short, broad leaves with conspicuously swollen, spongy leaf sheaths and delicate awned panicles.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पानी की सतह पर तैरती हुई चटाई जैसी घास, हवा से फूले हुए स्पंज जैसे पत्तों के डंठल, और पानी के ऊपर उठी हुई बालियाँ जिनमें लंबी मूँछें होती हैं।

Morphology शारीरिक संरचना

Biometrics (Bor 1960; Duthie 1888):

MeasurementRange
Floating culm length30–100 cm (Bor 1960)
Leaf blade length3–8 cm (Bor 1960)
Leaf blade width10–20 mm (Bor 1960)
Panicle length5–8 cm (Bor 1960)
Spikelet length7–8 mm (Bor 1960)
Awn length10–14 mm (Bor 1960)

Culms and roots: Stems long, spongy, branched, floating horizontally on the water surface; nodes emit dense whorls of pinnate, feathery adventitious aquatic roots that absorb dissolved nutrients.

Leaf blades and sheaths: Sheaths inflated, swollen, compressed, smooth, acting as buoyant pontoons. Leaf blades short, flat, ovate-oblong, obtuse or subacute, rounded at the base, glaucous green above and densely covered with microscopic water-repellent papillae beneath.

Inflorescence and flowers: Panicle terminal, shortly exserted, triangular-ovate with spreading capillary branchlets. Spikelets 1-flowered, narrowly lanceolate, awned, falling entire with the pedicel. Glumes obsolete; lemma chartaceous, 5-nerved, keeled, tapering into a slender straight scabrid terminal awn.

हिंदी: तने पानी में तैरते हैं और हर गांठ से बारीक जड़ों के गुच्छे लटकते हैं। पत्तियों का निचला हिस्सा हवा की थैली जैसा फूला होता है। बाली छोटी और त्रिकोणीय होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार एवं पारिस्थितिकी

Hygroryza aristata is an obligate wetland aquatic hydrophyte. It forms extensive monospecific or mixed floating mats that blanket quiet freshwater bodies, oxbow lakes, and backwaters of Gangetic rivers.

The swollen aerenchymatous leaf sheaths provide positive buoyancy, keeping the photosynthetic leaf surfaces upright even when water levels fluctuate sharply during monsoon floods. Flowering occurs in autumn from September to November. The feathery root tassels suspended beneath the mats provide rich foraging substrate and nursery shelter for small aquatic fish, dragonfly nymphs, and freshwater shrimps.

हिंदी: यह पूरी तरह पानी में रहने वाली घास है। इसके तैरते हुए जाल के नीचे छोटी मछलियाँ और जलीय कीड़े शरण लेते हैं, और बाढ़ के समय जलस्तर बढ़ने पर भी यह आसानी से तैरती रहती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र एवं ऋतुचक्र

PeriodPhenological Event
April–JuneSurvives severe pre-monsoon drawdown in perennial pool centers or as stranded stolons
July–AugustRapid vegetative branching spreads across newly inundated flood waters and ponds
September–NovemberErect flowering panicles rise above the water surface; spikelets set seed
December–MarchSeeds mature and drop into bottom sediments; winter vegetative mats persist in calm water

Vegetative fragmentation via breaking stolons represents the primary mode of colonization along interconnected drainage networks.

हिंदी: गर्मी में जब पानी सूखता है तब यह गहरे गड्ढों में बची रहती है। बरसात में तेजी से फैलती है और शरद ऋतु में फूलकर बीज पानी की तलहटी में छोड़ती है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबद्ध प्रजातियाँ एवं पारस्परिक क्रियाएं

  • Waterfowl: Resident moorhens, jacanas, and dabbling ducks forage on the floating mats and eat the ripe seeds.
  • Aquatic Invertebrates: Feathery root systems provide habitat for freshwater grass shrimp and aquatic beetle larvae.
  • Wetland Associates: Floats in community with Water Hyacinth, lotus, and floating pondweeds.
हिंदी: जलमुर्गी और बत्तखें इसकी चटाई पर चलकर भोजन तलाशती हैं और इसके बीज खाती हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट एवं रोग

  • Aquatic snail herbivory occasionally damages tender floating stolon tips.
  • Stem rot caused by aquatic Pythium species develops in heavily stagnant, warm shallow pools.
  • Water lily beetle larvae feed on emerging leaves during late summer humidity.
हिंदी: पानी के घोंघे और जलीय फफूंद कभी-कभी पुरानी पत्तियों और तनों को नुकसान पहुँचाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि एवं मानवीय उपयोगिता

Traditional Fodder and Wetland Uses:

  • High-quality cattle fodder. Buffaloes and cows are brought to ponds to graze the succulent floating mats, or the grass is raked out and fed green to dairy livestock across the plains.
  • Traditional grain collection. Historically, the grains were collected in times of famine and boiled into country porridge or popped like wild rice (Duthie 1888).
  • Aquatic weed buffer. Dense native mats reduce mosquito breeding and wave erosion on pond dykes, though excessive growth can choke open irrigation channels.
  • Traditional medicine. Classical treatises document root and cooling infusions used in rural lore for soothing burning sensations and bilious conditions. This is a record of traditional use, not medical advice.

Today. It is recognized as an ecologically beneficial native biofilter plant that absorbs excess agricultural runoff nutrients from village water bodies.

हिंदी: एशियन जल घास को भैंसों के लिए बहुत पौष्टिक हरा चारा माना जाता है। अकाल के समय ग्रामीण इसके बीजों को इकट्ठा करके खाते थे। यह तालाब के पानी को साफ रखने में भी सहायक है।

Distribution भौगोलिक वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical and subtropical South and Southeast Asia, from India and Sri Lanka through Nepal, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, and southern China.

In India: Widespread in lakes, marshes, and slow streams of Assam, West Bengal, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, and peninsular wetlands (Bor 1960).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded across sluggish water bodies and jheels of the Gangetic and tarai wetlands (Duthie 1888).

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected on natural tals, abandoned canal cuts, and village community ponds.

हिंदी: भारत के लगभग सभी राज्यों के प्राकृतिक तालों और दलदलों में यह जलीय घास पाई जाती है।

Field rule: Free-floating aquatic grass with swollen spongy leaf sheaths, short ovate-oblong leaves with water-repellent undersides, and small triangular awned panicles identifies Hygroryza aristata.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गिकी एवं वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Pharus aristatus by Anders Jahan Retzius in 1788 in Observationes Botanicae, vol. 5, p. 23. Transferred to Hygroryza by Christian Gottfried Daniel Nees von Esenbeck, published by Robert Wight and G.A. Walker-Arnott in 1833 in the Edinburgh New Philosophical Journal, vol. 15, p. 380.

Subspecies. Monotypic — Hygroryza aristata is the sole species in the monotypic genus Hygroryza.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the grass family Poaceae, subfamily Oryzoideae, tribe Oryzeae, in the order Poales.

Phylogenetic notes. Hygroryza is a specialised aquatic offshoot of the rice lineage (Oryzeae), sharing unisexual floral tendencies and 1-flowered spikelets lacking glumes with Oryza and Leersia.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated in the master expansion list; it remains common, widespread, and ecologically stable across South Asian freshwater wetlands.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वर्णन 1789 में रेट्ज़ियस ने किया था और 1834 में इसे हाइग्रोराइज़ा नामक एक एकल-प्रजाति वंश में वर्गीकृत किया गया, जो धान के कुल से बहुत निकट है।
