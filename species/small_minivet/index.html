सहेली / Small Minivet

Pericrocotus cinnamomeus (Linnaeus, 1766) — Campephagidae

सहेली — यह छोटी, गहरे रंग की चिड़िया पूरे साल खुली जंगलों और बागों में पेड़ों पर दिखती है। नर का शरीर काला है, किंतु पंखों और पेट पर चमकीला नारंगी रंग है। मादा भूरी और पीली है। ये चिड़ियाँ हमेशा झुंड में रहती हैं और एक-दूसरे से जुड़ी रहती हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmall minivet; 16–17 cm long
Male plumageBlack back and head; orange-red underparts; orange wing patches
Female plumageGrey-brown above; yellow-buff underparts; yellow wing patches
BehaviourGregarious; moves in flocks through trees; active gleaning
HabitatDeciduous and mixed woodlands; open areas with scattered trees

Field tip: A small, active bird with distinctive coloration. Males are striking black with orange-red; females grey-brown with yellow. Always in flocks; very social. Active foragers gleaning insects from leaves and branches.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Total length160–170 mm

Size: 16–17 cm long; weighs 12–16 g. Small, slender minivet.

Plumage (male): Black above; head, nape, back all black. Underparts brilliant orange-red or cinnamon-red. Wings black with orange-red patches on coverts and flight feathers. Tail black with red patches.

Plumage (female): Grey-brown above; head and back grey-brown. Underparts yellow-buff to pale yellow. Wings grey-brown with yellow patches. Tail grey-brown with yellow patches.

Bill: Small, slightly curved, dark; pointed for gleaning.

Eyes: Dark; small.

Legs and feet: Small, dark; adapted for perching and gleaning.

Sexes: Highly dimorphic. Males striking black and orange; females dull grey-brown and yellow.

Juveniles: Resemble females; duller coloration.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Contact calls: Soft, whistled notes; "tee-tee" or similar.
  2. Flock calls: Soft chirping and twittering to maintain flock cohesion.
  3. Song: Rarely heard; quiet warble.
हिंदी: नरम आवाज़ें करते हैं। झुंड में बातचीत करते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Highly gregarious: Lives in flocks year-round. Very social; maintains close flock cohesion through soft vocalizations.

Foraging: Actively gleans insects from leaves, branches and twigs. Feeds on:

  • Small insects (flies, aphids, scale insects)
  • Spiders
  • Small caterpillars
  • Insects gleaned from foliage

Flock foraging: Forages as a moving flock through tree canopy; all birds feeding while moving together.

Movement: Moves through trees with bounding flight; rarely on ground.

Social behaviour: Highly coordinated; flocks move together; birds often touch and interact.

हिंदी: झुंड में सब कुछ करता है। पेड़ों में कीड़े खोजता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Breeding season: April–July in eastern UP; peak May–June.

Nest: Small, hanging cup-nest suspended from branch tips. Built of plant fibre and spider web; delicate.

Clutch: 2–3 eggs; pale with markings.

Incubation and fledging: Female mainly incubates. Incubation 12–14 days; fledging 12–14 days. Both parents feed.

Broods: May raise 2 broods per season.

Moult: Complete post-breeding moult; June–August.

हिंदी: गर्मी में घोंसला बनाता है। पेड़ों पर झूलता है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected status: Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Schedule IV.

Agricultural value: Feeds on crop pests and insects; beneficial in orchards and cultivation areas.

Cultural relevance: Known to villagers; associated with wooded habitats; their presence indicates good tree cover.

Ecological role: Pest control; seed dispersal (occasionally); food for raptors.

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

This species is NOT a field record. The distribution below is based on published sources describing where Small Minivet should be looked for in the Basti–Ayodhya region.

Year-round presence: GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain. Expected resident in eastern UP in deciduous and mixed woodlands and areas with scattered trees year-round. Most vocal and visible during breeding season (May–June).

Where to look: Open deciduous forests; gardens and parks with mature trees; cultivation margins with scattered trees. Listen for soft flock calls.

Seasonality: Year-round; may show local movements in response to food and flowering times.

Detection: Relatively easy to locate by soft flock calls. Watch for moving flock through tree canopy; characteristic coordinated movement.

What a first record would be: A dated photograph showing male's distinctive black-and-orange plumage (or female's grey-and-yellow), with location GPS coordinates and date, submitted through the About page of this atlas.

हिंदी: पेड़ों में साल भर दिखता है। झुंड में देखा जाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent; resident.

In India: Widespread in deciduous forests and woodlands from foothills to peninsular India.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common resident in northern and central regions with suitable habitat.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in areas with deciduous trees and open woodlands.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1766. Muscicapa cinnamomea Linnaeus, 1766. Type locality: India.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies, with Indian populations representing P. c. cinnamomeus group.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Campephagidae (cuckoo-shrikes and minivets). Genus Pericrocotus comprises Asian minivets.

Phylogenetic notes. Pericrocotus is a well-defined Asian genus with several species of similar ecological ecology.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) globally. Population stable. Adaptable to varied habitats. Protected under Wildlife Protection Act 1972 in India.
