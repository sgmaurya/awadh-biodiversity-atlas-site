सालपर्णी / Salparni

Pleurolobus gangeticus (L.) J.St.-Hil. ex H.Ohashi & K.Ohashi — Fabaceae

सालपर्णी (शालपर्णी) — यह भारतीय जंगलों और झाड़ियों में उगने वाला एक छोटा बहुवर्षीय उपझाड़ी पौधा है। इसके पत्ते सरल, एकपर्णी और अंडाकार होते हैं, जिनकी निचली सतह पर हल्के सफेद रोएं होते हैं। बरसात के मौसम में इसके तनों के छोर पर सफेद या हल्की जामुनी तितलीनुमा फूलों की लंबी बालियां निकलती हैं। इसके बाद छोटे-छोटे मुड़े हुए फल बनते हैं जिन पर हुकदार बाल होते हैं जो जानवरों से चिपककर बिखरते हैं। यह आयुर्वेद के प्रसिद्ध 'दशमूल' का एक प्रमुख अंग है।

Field tip: Look in woodland clearings and orchard undergrowth for a low woody subshrub with single ovate leaves and narrow curved pods covered in tiny velcro-like hooked hairs.

हिंदी: जंगल के किनारों पर उगने वाला यह पौधा अपने एकहरे चौड़े पत्तों और छोटी मुड़ी हुई फलियों से पहचाना जाता है जिन पर चिपचिपे रोएं होते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

MeasurementRange
Stem habitSuberect, woody, slightly angular, stems reaching 3–4 ft. high (Hooker 1879)

Pleurolobus gangeticus is a slender suberect woody undershrub that grows upright or spreading among woodland grasses. The stems are slightly angular and woody towards the base, branching sparsely and thinly clothed with appressed short grey down. The leaves are characteristically single-foliolate, borne on slender petioles with scarious linear stipules. The leaf blade is ovate-oblong or elliptic, rounded or subcordate at the base, acute at the apex, subcoriaceous in texture, glabrous on the upper surface, and thinly covered with adpressed grey pubescence beneath.

The inflorescence consists of numerous terminal and axillary racemes with slender puberulous axes. The flowers are small, arranged in dense fascicles along the rachis. The calyx is campanulate, finely downy, with lanceolate teeth longer than the tube. The corolla is papilionaceous, white or faintly suffused with pinkish-lilac. The fruit is a flat, falcate pod, deeply notched along the lower suture into indehiscent joints. Each joint is covered with minute hooked hairs that adhere firmly to passing animals.

हिंदी: इस पौधे के तने तीन से चार फीट तक ऊंचे होते हैं। पत्ते एकहरे, नुकीले और नीचे से धूसर रोएंदार होते हैं। फलियां दरांती जैसी मुड़ी हुई और खंडों में बंटी होती हैं जिन पर बारीक कांटेदार रोएं पाए जाते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Salparni thrives in the semi-shade of deciduous woodland canopies and along forest clearings where filtered sunlight reaches the herb layer. It possesses deep woody taproots with active root nodules containing rhizobial bacteria that fix atmospheric nitrogen, enhancing local soil fertility.

The flowers bloom during the warm monsoon months from July to September, opening in sequence along the raceme rachis. They are visited by small solitary bees and lycaenid butterflies that seek both nectar and pollen. The jointed pods mature into the winter months; the persistent hooked hairs function as an efficient epizoochorous dispersal mechanism, hitching rides on mammalian fur, grazing livestock, and human clothing across forest margins.

हिंदी: यह पौधा वर्षा ऋतु में फूलता है और मधुमक्खियों को आकर्षित करता है। इसके बीजों वाले खंड मवेशियों और चरवाहों के कपड़ों से चिपककर दूर-दूर तक फैल जाते हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Foliage is perennial, though vegetative shoot growth flushes vigorously with the onset of pre-monsoon rains in June. Old shoots may die back partially during extreme frost or dry summer heat.

Flowering begins in July and continues through September, producing sequential clusters of small lilac or white blossoms. Pods form rapidly during September and October, ripening into dry brownish segmental loments from November through January. Seeds remain viable within their indehiscent pod joints until monsoon rains soften the seed coat.

हिंदी: सावन-भादों में इस पर फूल आते हैं और क्वार-कार्तिक में फलियां तैयार होती हैं। जाड़े में बीज पककर झड़ते हैं और अगली बारिश में नए पौधे उगते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

Pleurolobus gangeticus is a characteristic understory associate in dry and moist deciduous forests, growing under the canopy of Sal (Shorea robusta) and Palash (Butea monosperma).

The small flowers are visited for nectar and pollen by the Common Grass Yellow (Eurema hecabe) and native halictid bees. Ground-dwelling predatory carabid beetles and spiders find sheltered hunting grounds beneath its spreading low branches.

हिंदी: यह साल और पलाश के जंगलों के नीचे उगता है। पीली तितलियां और छोटी जंगली मक्खियां इसके फूलों पर रस चूसने आती हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Wild populations are remarkably hardy, though leaves are occasionally chewed by caterpillars of legume-feeding lycaenids and leaf-miner microlepidoptera. Pod borer larvae occasionally damage developing seeds inside the joints.

In waterlogged clay hollows, root rot caused by soil-borne fungal pathogens can occur during prolonged monsoon flooding. Powder mildew is sometimes observed on senescing foliage during humid late autumn.

हिंदी: सामान्यतः इस पर गंभीर कीट नहीं लगते, लेकिन ज्यादा पानी ठहरने पर जड़ सड़ सकती है और पत्तियों पर कभी-कभी फफूंद आ जाती है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Salparni is one of the ten venerable ingredients of 'Dashamula' (the ten roots formulation) in classical Ayurveda, widely gathered from wild woodland habitats for traditional wellness preparations. Ethnobotanical traditions value decoctions of the root for managing seasonal fevers, coughs, and digestive sluggishness; this is a record of traditional practice, not medical endorsement.

The plant has practical value in agroforestry as a nitrogen-fixing cover crop and green manure in orchards and field borders. Its foliage is moderately browsed by goats and cattle during the dry winter season when forage grasses become desiccated.

Traditional: Core ingredient of Dashamula root formulations in classical Indian medicine.

Today: Wild-harvested non-timber forest product traded in herbal commodity markets; candidate for farm bund cultivation.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Agronomic evaluation of root biomass production under organic intercropping in semi-arid plains.

हिंदी: यह दशमूल का प्रसिद्ध घटक है। किसान इसे मेड़ों पर हरी खाद और मिट्टी सुधार के लिए भी उगा सकते हैं। बकरियां इसकी पत्तियां चाव से खाती हैं।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across tropical Asia, including India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, China, Malaysia, and tropical Africa; introduced in parts of the West Indies.

In India: Found throughout the greater part of peninsular India, ascending into the outer Himalayas up to 5,000 feet, and across the northern plains.

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded in Dehra Dun, Rohilkhand, northern Oudh, Gorakhpur, and the Vindhyan hills.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Documented in regional floristic surveys for eastern Uttar Pradesh deciduous tracts; expected in undisturbed forest pockets.

हिंदी: यह पूरे भारत, नेपाल और उष्णकटिबंधीय एशिया में फैला हुआ है। उत्तर प्रदेश के मैदानी और तराई के वनों में सामान्य रूप से दर्ज है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First published by Jean Henri Jaume Saint-Hilaire in 1812 as Pleurolobus gangeticus in Nouv. Bull. Sci. Soc. Philom. Paris, 3, p. 192, based on Linnaeus's Hedysarum gangeticum (1753). Long known in Indian floras under Augustin Pyramus de Candolle's 1825 combination Desmodium gangeticum in Prodromus Systematis Naturalis Regni Vegetabilis, 2, p. 327.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Placed in family Fabaceae (subfamily Faboideae, tribe Desmodieae), order Fabales.

Phylogenetic notes. Recent phylogenetic revisions reinstated the genus Pleurolobus to accommodate single-foliolate species distinct from core Desmodium.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (not_evaluated) by IUCN. The species is widely distributed across tropical Asia and Africa.

हिंदी: पारंपरिक रूप से इसे डेस्मोडियम वंश में गिना जाता था, लेकिन आधुनिक वनस्पति विज्ञानियों ने इसे प्लूरोलोबस वंश में रखा है।
