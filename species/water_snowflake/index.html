कुमुदिनी / Water Snowflake

Nymphoides indica (L.) Kuntze — Menyanthaceae

कुमुदिनी — तालाब के पानी में तैरता पौधा, पत्तियाँ गोल, फूल पीले या हल्के नीले, पानी की सतह पर खिलते हैं। असली कमल का छोटा चचेरा भाई है — पत्तियाँ छोटी, पौधा सूक्ष्म, लेकिन जलीय जीवन का संरक्षक। मछली के अंडे और छोटे कीड़े इसी में छिपते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
FormFloating or partially submerged aquatic herb; leaves scattered or in loose rosettes
LeavesOrbicular to oval, 2–10 cm diameter, smooth waxy upper surface, often with darker blotches or mottling; underside purplish-red
Leaf stalkSlender, flexible; variable length depending on water depth
FlowersYellow, white or pale lilac; 5 petals, fringed or toothed margins; 1.5–2 cm across; borne on short stalks at leaf axils
FruitThin-walled capsule; many small seeds
RootsThin, fibrous, floating or anchored in mud; lack the air-filled chambers of water lilies

Field tip: Look for neat round leaves on a quiet water surface with delicate star-like yellow or white flowers with frilly petals. Smaller and more delicate than lotus or water lily; unlike water hyacinth, no swollen leaf stalks and no mat formation.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पानी की सतह पर गोल पत्तियाँ, पीले या हल्के फूल, पंखुड़ियाँ झालरेदार। कमल की पत्ती से छोटी, जलकुंभी की तरह गाढ़ी नहीं।

Habit: A perennial aquatic herb that can be entirely floating or partially rooted in mud. Forms loose colonies rather than dense mats. Growth form varies with water depth and clarity.

Leaves: Orbicular to oval, sometimes with heart-shaped base, smooth and waxy above (bright green), often with radial patterning or dark spots; undersurface usually purplish or reddish. Margin entire or sometimes minutely wavy.

Flowers: Pentamerous (5-petalled), 1.5–2 cm across. Petals pale yellow, white, or pale lilac, with distinctive fringed or irregularly toothed margins — this feature distinguishes it from most other aquatic plants. Flowers ephemeral, lasting less than a day.

Rootstock: Stoloniferous rhizomes that can be rooted to mud or free-floating. Produces daughter plants at nodes.

हिंदी: पत्तियाँ गोल या अंडाकार, चमकदार, अक्सर धब्बों वाली; तने लंबे, लचीले। फूल पाँच पंखुड़ियों वाले, झालरेदार किनारे। बीज छोटे, सामान्य।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A plant of still waters, ponds and shallow wetlands where it contributes to the ecological structure of aquatic communities. Unlike water hyacinth, which forms impenetrable mats and excludes light, Nymphoides maintains open-water habitat. Its leaves trap sediment and detritus, creating a substrate for algae and detritivorous invertebrates. The plants provide refuge for fish fry, mosquito larvae, dragonfly nymphs and small aquatic beetles.

The flowers are visited by small bees, butterflies and syrphid flies. In many regions, populations of Nymphoides correlate with healthy fish and amphibian communities; its presence is an indicator of clean, well-oxygenated water.

हिंदी: साफ़ पानी का निवासी। मछली के बच्चे, कीड़े और जलीय जीव इसमें छिपते और खाना खोजते हैं। छोटी मधुमक्खियाँ और तितलियाँ आती हैं।

Reproduces both vegetatively (by rhizomes and runners) and by seed. Stolon production is slower than invasive species like water hyacinth, maintaining equilibrium in stable ponds.

हिंदी: मानसून में तेज़ी से बढ़ता है। साल भर फूल खिल सकते हैं; सर्दी में धीमा।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Plant companions: water lily, lotus, Water Fern in the same ponds; does not displace them
  • Fauna: dragonfly nymph, aquatic insects, small fish fry, aquatic beetles, snails
  • Birds: Indian Pond Heron, Common Teal wade through for small prey

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional and current uses: None widely recorded in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Unlike lotus or water lily, which are harvested for seeds, leaves and medicinal use, Nymphoides is left undisturbed, which is ecologically beneficial.

Ecological benefit: Unlike the invasive water hyacinth, Nymphoides indica does not clog ponds, irrigation channels or reduce fish production. Its presence indicates healthy, balanced ponds.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The correlation between Nymphoides presence and fish recruitment in eastern UP village ponds has not been quantified; a comparison of ponds with and without this species could establish its role in supporting fisheries.

हिंदी: विशेष उपयोग नहीं है, पर इसका मतलब यही है कि यह हानिकारक नहीं है। स्वस्थ तालाब में यह बना रहता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Asia and Australia; native to India, widespread in wetlands from sea level to 2,000 m.

In India: Throughout the Indian peninsula, abundant in ponds and wetlands from the Western Ghats to Assam.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in still waters across the state, especially in the rice belt.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in village ponds, wetlands (jhils) and shallow lakes; likely common but under-recorded.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. Is water snowflake present in the same ponds as lotus, and do they coexist peacefully? / क्या कुमुदिनी और कमल एक साथ उगते हैं? — Survey five ponds in the monsoon.
  2. How many flowers are produced in a week on a single plant? — Mark a plant and count daily.
  3. What insects visit the flowers, and for how long does a flower stay open? — Watch for 2 hours after dawn.
  4. Are fish fry more abundant in ponds with Nymphoides than in bare ponds? / क्या कुमुदिनी वाले तालाब में मछली के बच्चे ज़्यादा हैं? — Net fry at the margins; count and compare two ponds.
  5. How long do the seeds remain viable in mud after the plant dies? — Collect seed capsules and try germinating after 1, 3, and 6 months.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described by Linnaeus as Villarsia indica in Species Plantarum (1753). Transferred to Nymphoides by Carl Ernst Otto Kuntze in Revisio Generum Plantarum (1891). Type locality: India.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised; Asian populations are treated as one taxon.

Family and order placement. Menyanthaceae, order Menyanthales. The family is a small group of aquatic and semi-aquatic herbs; Nymphoides and its relative Villarsia are the main genera in wetlands.

Phylogenetic notes. Molecular studies confirm Nymphoides as distinct from Nymphaea (water lilies, family Nymphaeaceae) and from Villarsia. It is more closely related to the floating-heart plants of temperate wetlands.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. A widespread and stable native species with no documented decline.
