लोबिया / Cowpea

Vigna unguiculata (L.) Walp. — Fabaceae

लोबिया — रब्बी और खरीफ दोनों में उगने वाली दाल, जो भूरी या बेज़ रंग की होती है, और अक्सर सफ़ेद धब्बे (आँख) वाली होती है। यही "काली आँख वाली मटर" (ब्लैक-आइड पी) है। पौष्टिक दाल, पेट के लिए हल्की। बसंत और सर्दी दोनों मौसम में खेतों में दिखती है। सूखे को सहन करता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
PlantHerbaceous, spreading or climbing, 30–60 cm or taller if supported
LeavesTrifoliolate, alternate, leaflets ovate or lanceolate, 5–10 cm
FlowersSmall, whitish to pale pink, in short racemes
PodsLong, slender, 15–25 cm, light brown to dark brown, slightly curved
SeedsMedium-sized, brown or tan with a white or pale "eye" (hilum), 8–10 mm

Field tip: A trailing or semi-climbing legume with distinctive brown seeds bearing a characteristic white "eye" or spot. Pods are longer and slenderer than black gram. Grown in both rabi and kharif seasons.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for Vigna unguiculata in northern India):

MeasurementRange
Plant height30–60 cm (trailing to semi-climbing)
Leaflet length5–10 cm
Pod length15–25 cm
Seed diameter8–10 mm

Stem: Herbaceous, branching, somewhat angular. Plant form is trailing to semi-climbing; may twine slightly around supports.

Leaves: Alternate, trifoliolate; terminal leaflet on a longer petiolule. Leaflets ovate to lanceolate, 5–10 cm long, entire, hairy.

Flowers: Small, whitish to pale pink or yellowish, in short axillary racemes. Characteristic smell; flowers are self-pollinated.

Pods: Long, slender, 15–25 cm, slightly curved or straight, light brown to dark brown at maturity, thin-walled. Contains 8–12 seeds.

Seeds: Medium-sized, round to slightly flattened, tan, brown, or red, typically with a distinctive white or pale "eye" (the hilum), 8–10 mm diameter.

हिंदी: लंबे डाली, भूरी दालें, सफ़ेद आँख वाली।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Cowpea is a warm-season pulse crop native to Africa and widely cultivated in South and Southeast Asia. It forms nitrogen-fixing associations with rhizobium bacteria, enriching soil nitrogen. The plant is drought-tolerant and thrives in warm conditions, making it suitable for both rabi and kharif seasons in eastern UP. The crop is relatively hardy and free of major pests and diseases in the region.

हिंदी: अफ्रीकी पौधा, अब एशिया में आम। सूखा सहता है। गर्मी के लिए अच्छा। मिट्टी में नाइट्रोजन बढ़ाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Crop rotation: Follows Paddy; precedes Wheat.
  • Garden companions: Grown with Black Gram and other pulses.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Pod borers: occasionally; hand-picking.
  • Root rot in waterlogged soil: ensure drainage.
  • Generally hardy; few pests.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Food use:

Cowpea is cooked as a dal (curry) or as whole seeds in traditional dishes. The dal is highly nutritious and light. The distinctive "eye" makes it easy to identify in markets. The plant is valued for both seed and leaf (young leaves are eaten as a vegetable in some regions).

Nutritional value:

Seeds are rich in protein, fibre, minerals, and B vitamins. The dal is easy to digest.

Soil improvement:

The nitrogen-fixing capability improves soil fertility for subsequent crops.

Cultivation advantages:

Low external input; relatively cheap seeds; drought-tolerant; suitable for both rabi and kharif; profitable return.

हिंदी: पौष्टिक दाल। सूखा भी सहती है। खरीफ और रब्बी दोनों मौसम में उगती है। मिट्टी को ठीक करती है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to Africa; cultivated throughout Africa, Asia, Americas.

In India: Widely cultivated; particularly common in the Indo-Gangetic plain.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated throughout; an important rabi and kharif pulse.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Cultivated in fields and gardens; common in local markets.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally Dolichos unguiculatus L., later reclassified as Vigna unguiculata (L.) Walp.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies; subsp. unguiculata is the common cultivar.

Family and order placement. Fabaceae, order Fabales. Genus Vigna comprises about 100 species.

Phylogenetic notes. Vigna unguiculata is closely related to black gram and mung bean.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated.
