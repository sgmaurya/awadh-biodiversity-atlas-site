थूहर / Pencil Tree

Euphorbia tirucalli L. — Euphorbiaceae

थूहर — लंबी, सीधी, पेंसिल-जैसी शाखाओं वाली झाड़ी, जो बाग़ों की सीमा और बाड़ के लिए लगाई जाती है। हरी, कोमल शाखाएँ खिलवाड़ों को आकर्षक लगती हैं, पर यह ज़हरीली है। डंडियों को तोड़ने से सफेद, गाढ़ा दूध निकलता है जो त्वचा पर जलन करता है। आँख में पड़ने से अंधापन का खतरा है। बहुत ज़हरीली, बहुत खतरनाक।

LatexMilky latex in every green stem; it burns skin and can damage the eyes. Never touch it to test the plant

Field tip: Look for a thin-branched, columnar shrub with prominent white latex when broken. The name "pencil tree" refers to the pencil-like stem thickness. It is commonly planted as a boundary or garden specimen. Never touch the latex or bring it near the eyes.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सीधी, पेंसिल-जैसी हरी डंडियाँ। तोड़ने से सफेद दूध। बिल्कुल ज़हरीला।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (cultivated and naturalized plants, tropical and subtropical regions):

Stem and branches: Erect, pencil-like, cylindrical or slightly four-angled, smooth or slightly ridged, green when young becoming grey-green with age. The plant has a distinctive columnar or tree-like form due to minimal branching.

Leaves: Alternate, simple, linear-lanceolate, minute (0.5–2 cm long), entire margins, early caducous — present mainly on new growth and absent from mature stems.

Flowers: Inconspicuous, arranged in terminal cymose clusters (cyathia), small and yellowish. The flowering period is May to September.

Fruit: A trilobate (three-lobed) capsule, 5–8 mm diameter, dehiscent, containing small brown seeds.

Latex: The entire plant contains a white, milky latex (sap) that is toxic and caustic.

हिंदी: सीधी-सादी डंडियाँ, बहुत कम पत्तियाँ या नहीं। सफेद दूध निकलता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Pencil tree is a succulent shrub native to East Africa and widely introduced to tropical and subtropical regions worldwide for ornamental and boundary planting. It tolerates poor soils, drought, and neglect, making it a common roadside and garden shrub. It is extremely hardy once established. The plant's thick, waxy stem reduces water loss; it is adapted to arid environments.

The latex-filled tissue protects the plant from herbivores. Insects rarely defoliate it. The plant hosts some scale insects and small beetles, but major pests are few.

हिंदी: सूखा और खराब ज़मीन सहन करता है। कीटों का शिकार नहीं। बहुत मजबूत, मजबूती से लगता है।

हिंदी: गर्मियों में बढ़ता है, फूल खिलता है। कटिंग लगाने से आसानी से लग जाता है। बहुत लंबी उम्र।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Insect hosts: Small scale insects and soft-bodied bugs; rarely major pests.
  • Boundary companions: Often planted alongside Nirgundi, lemongrass, and other boundary species.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Scale insects occasionally; rarely serious.
  • Root rot in waterlogged soil; otherwise very disease-resistant.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Hazard — Extreme Toxicity:

The latex of Euphorbia tirucalli is highly toxic and caustic. The plant contains irritant diterpenes and other latex compounds that cause:

  1. Skin contact: Immediate burning sensation, erythema (redness), blistering if contact is prolonged. Irritation can last hours.
  2. Eye contact: Severe pain, corneal damage, and potential temporary or permanent vision loss if not immediately flushed with water.
  3. Ingestion: Severe gastrointestinal irritation, vomiting, diarrhoea, and potentially fatal systemic effects (not well-studied in humans; lethal in animals).
  4. Skin sensitization: Repeated exposure can cause allergic contact dermatitis.

Medical management: Immediate water irrigation of affected area for at least 15 minutes; avoid touching eyes or face after contact. For eye exposure, irrigate immediately and seek medical care urgently. No specific antidote; treatment is supportive.

Use — Ornamental boundary only: The plant is cultivated solely for its ornamental value as a hedge or boundary marker. No traditional medicinal or food use in eastern UP. The extreme toxicity prohibits any culinary or medicinal application.

Precaution for children and animals: This plant must not be grown in households with young children or pets. The pencil-like stems and small leaves invite handling, which can be dangerous. Several fatal cases of accidental ingestion in livestock and serious injuries in children have been documented globally.

हिंदी: बिल्कुल ज़हरीला। त्वचा पर जले का निशान बनाता है। आँख में पड़ने से अंधा हो सकता है। खाने से मौत भी हो सकती है। बच्चों और जानवरों से दूर रखें।

Expected as a cultivated ornamental in garden boundaries and hedges throughout eastern UP, visible year-round. The white latex when stems are broken is diagnostic. A first local record would be a photograph showing the pencil-like branches and location (village boundary, garden, etc.), with date.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to East Africa (Sudan, Ethiopia, Eritrea); naturalized throughout tropical Africa, Asia, and introduced to Americas.

In India: Widely naturalized across tropical and subtropical regions; especially common in arid and semi-arid zones.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated and naturalized throughout the state as an ornamental and boundary plant.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a cultivated plant in gardens and boundaries throughout the districts.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum as Euphorbia tirucalli. The specific epithet tirucalli derives from the Tamil common name "tirukkali".

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Euphorbiaceae, the spurge family, order Euphorbiophora. The genus Euphorbia is one of the largest flowering plant genera, with over 2,000 species worldwide, many succulent and many highly toxic.

Phylogenetic notes. Euphorbia tirucalli is part of the African succulent-stem euphorbia radiation.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely naturalized; not assessed.
