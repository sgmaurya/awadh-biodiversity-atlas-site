दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग / Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle

Cylindera decempunctata (Dejean, 1825) — Carabidae

दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग — यह एक छोटा और पतला बाघ भृंग है जिसके दोनों पंखों पर पाँच-पाँच (कुल दस) सफेद धब्बे होते हैं। इसका सिर और वक्ष धातुई चमक वाले होते हैं, जबकि पंख गहरे रंग के होते हैं। इसका निचला भाग नीला या हरा होता है और किनारों पर सफेद रोएँ होते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeLength 9–10 mm (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
Head and pronotumMore or less strong metallic reflection; head long with prominent eyes; pronotum as long as or longer than broad, with white hairs at sides (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
ElytraDark with hardly any metallic reflection (or obscurely metallic), centre of disc smooth; five white spots on each elytron (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
Spot patternOne at shoulder, a round one on disc before middle, two irregular ones at middle, fifth near apex joining a narrow marginal white line (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
LegsMetallic with reddish or pitchy red trochanters (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
UndersideCyaneous or green, with distinct pubescence at the sides (Fowler 1912, p. 353)

Field tip: Look for a small tiger beetle with elytra subparallel-sided in the male and slightly widened behind in the female, showing five white spots on each elytron (Fowler 1912, p. 353).

हिंदी: इसकी लंबाई 9 से 10 मिमी होती है। इसके प्रत्येक पंख पर पाँच सफेद धब्बे होते हैं, जिनमें से पाँचवाँ धब्बा सिरे की सफेद रेखा से जुड़ा होता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (Fowler 1912, p. 353, as Cicindela decempunctata):

MeasurementRange
Length9–10 mm

Fowler (1912, p. 353) describes the adult as follows: "front parts with more or less strong metallic reflection, elytra dark with hardly any metallic reflection and with the white spots distinct, or obscurely metallic with the light spots indistinct, without distinct brighter band at the sides; the latter form appears to be the C. modica of Gestro. Labrum dark with testaceous spots, or testaceous with the anterior margin dark; head long, with the eyes prominent, rather plainly but finely striated, occiput long, very finely sculptured; pronotum fully as long as, or rather longer than broad, with the impressions distinct and the central line feeble, very finely sculptured; at the sides there are white hairs, which are very plain in some specimens, but are easily rubbed off, and therefore are absent in others; elytra subparallel-sided in the male, slightly widened behind in the female, very finely sculptured, with the centre of the disc smooth, with five white spots on each, one at shoulders, a round one on disc before middle, two irregular ones at the middle (one near margin and one a little behind, nearer suture, sometimes joined by a thin line), and the fifth near apex joining a narrow marginal white line, which is continued to apex; legs metallic, trochanters reddish or pitchy red; underside cyaneous or green, with distinct pubescence at the sides, episterna of prosternum bare and impunctate."

हिंदी: फाउलर (1912) के अनुसार इसका सिर लंबा और आँखें उभरी हुई होती हैं। नर के पंख लगभग समानांतर होते हैं और मादा के पंख पीछे से थोड़े चौड़े होते हैं। प्रत्येक पंख पर पाँच सफेद धब्बे होते हैं और निचला हिस्सा नीला या हरा होता है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Fowler (1912, p. 353) did not list the United Provinces among his localities for this species. However, GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya: four photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri in June and July 2025 and July 2026 by three observers. A first local record would be a photograph showing the five spots on each elytron with place and date, sent through the About page.

हिंदी: फाउलर (1912) ने इसे संयुक्त प्रांत से दर्ज नहीं किया था, लेकिन जीबीआइएफ (GBIF) में लखीमपुर खीरी से जून-जुलाई 2025 और जुलाई 2026 के चार अभिलेख हैं। बस्ती या अयोध्या से अभी कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Fowler (1912, p. 353) records it from India, Burma (Rangoon, Palon), Tonkin, and Cambodia.

In India: Fowler (1912, p. 353) lists Bengal: Birbhum, Murshidabad, and Rajmahal. He does not name the United Provinces.

In Uttar Pradesh: GBIF holds four photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri (June–July 2025 and July 2026, three observers).

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी: फाउलर (1912) ने इसे बंगाल, बर्मा, टोंकिन और कंबोडिया से दर्ज किया है। उत्तर प्रदेश में जीबीआइएफ पर लखीमपुर खीरी के चार अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Field rule: A tiger beetle with metallic front parts, elytra subparallel-sided in the male and slightly widened behind in the female, and five white spots on each dark elytron (the fifth joining a narrow apical marginal line) is Cylindera decempunctata (Fowler 1912, p. 353).

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Dejean, P. F. M. A. (1825). Species Général des Coléoptères, vol. 1, p. 145, as Cicindela decempunctata (cited in Fowler 1912, p. 353).

Subspecies. Not available in consulted sources. Fowler (1912, p. 353) notes that Cicindela modica Gestro, 1893 appears to be an obscurely metallic form with indistinct spots.

Family and order placement. Order Coleoptera, family Carabidae, genus Cylindera. Fowler (1912, p. 353) placed it in Cicindela (entry 131).

Phylogenetic notes. Placed in Cylindera in modern classifications (GBIF), whereas Fowler (1912, p. 353) treated it under Cicindela.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated on the IUCN Red List.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वैज्ञानिक वर्णन देज्याँ (1825) ने Cicindela decempunctata के रूप में किया था। वर्तमान में इसे Cylindera वंश और Carabidae कुल में रखा जाता है।
