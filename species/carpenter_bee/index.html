भँवरा / Carpenter Bee

Xylocopa fenestrata (Fabricius, 1798) — Apidae

भँवरा — कविता का भ्रमर, और गाँव की सबसे बड़ी मधुमक्खी: काली, चमकदार, अंगूठे के पोर जितनी, जिसकी गूँजती उड़ान दूर से सुनाई देती है। यह छत्ता नहीं बनाती, सूखे बाँस और लकड़ी में सुरंग खोदकर अकेली रहती है। लौकी, कद्दू, तोरी, अरहर, सेम और पलाश-सेमल के फूलों की सबसे बड़ी परागक यही है; बैंगन और टमाटर के फूल को झकझोरकर पराग निकालना भी इसी का काम है। डंक है, पर यह शायद ही कभी इस्तेमाल करती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size20–26 mm, stout; the largest bee of the district
ColourGlossy black all over; the wings dark with a violet or blue sheen in sunlight
MaleSimilar but with a pale patch or band on the face and often a paler, browner wing base
FlightLoud, deep hum; fast, direct, hovering briefly at each flower
NestA neat round hole 12–15 mm across in dead wood or bamboo, with a pile of sawdust beneath
BehaviourVisits large open flowers; may bite the base of a tubular flower to reach the nectar

Field tip: The big black buzzing bee at the gourd flowers in the morning, or the clean round hole in the end of a dry bamboo pole with a heap of sawdust under it. Bumblebees, which are hairy and banded, do not occur in the plains; any very large black bee here is a carpenter bee.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सुबह लौकी-कद्दू के फूल पर बड़ी, काली, चमकदार, ज़ोर से गूँजने वाली मक्खी; या सूखे बाँस के सिरे में साफ़ गोल छेद जिसके नीचे बुरादे का ढेर। मैदानों में इतनी बड़ी काली मक्खी भँवरा ही है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length20–26 mm
Wingspan40–50 mm
Nest tunnel diameter12–15 mm
Nest tunnel length15–40 cm
Cells per tunnel5–12

Body: Robust, the thorax nearly bare and shining, the abdomen flat and black; the hind legs of the female carry dense pollen-collecting hairs. The mandibles are heavy, for chewing wood; the tongue long.

Sting: The female can sting but is docile and does so only when handled or trapped; the male has no sting and patrols territories noisily, bluffing.

Nest: The female bores into dry wood against the grain for a short distance then along it, partitioning the tunnel with walls of chewed wood pulp into cells, each provisioned with a loaf of pollen and nectar on which one egg is laid. Old nests are reused and enlarged over years.

हिंदी: छाती चिकनी-चमकदार, पेट चपटा काला, मादा की पिछली टाँगों पर पराग के लिए घने बाल, जबड़े लकड़ी काटने लायक भारी। मादा का डंक है पर पकड़ने पर ही; नर के पास डंक नहीं, वह गूँजकर डराता है। सुरंग में मिट्टी की नहीं, चबाई लकड़ी की दीवारें, हर खाने में पराग-शहद की रोटी पर एक अंडा।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A solitary bee, though several females may share an entrance and the daughters often stay in the natal tunnel. It flies from before sunrise and is the first bee on the gourd flowers, which open at dawn and close by mid-morning; it is the principal pollinator of bottle gourd, pumpkin, ridge gourd and bitter gourd in Indian studies, and a major one of pigeon pea, beans, passion flowers and the big spring trees, Palash and Semal. On brinjal and tomato it grips the flower and vibrates its flight muscles to shake pollen out, "buzz pollination", which honey bees cannot do.

It bores only dead, dry wood, so it is a nuisance in bamboo rafters and old furniture but harmless to living trees. Bee-eaters, Black Drongo and Indian Roller catch adults; woodpeckers open nests.

हिंदी: अकेली रहने वाली मक्खी, हालाँकि कई मादाएँ एक ही प्रवेश-द्वार बाँट लेती हैं। सूरज निकलने से पहले उड़ती है और लौकी-कद्दू के फूलों पर, जो सुबह खुलकर दोपहर से पहले बंद हो जाते हैं, सबसे पहले पहुँचती है। बैंगन-टमाटर के फूल को पकड़कर झकझोरती है — "बज़ परागण" — जो मधुमक्खी नहीं कर सकती। सिर्फ़ सूखी लकड़ी खोदती है, जीवित पेड़ नहीं। पतरंगा, भुजंगा और नीलकंठ इसे पकड़ते हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Stage / seasonEvent
March–MayNesting; females provision cells in bamboo and wood
Egg to adult, 6–8 weeksLarva eats its pollen loaf, pupates in the cell
June–JulyFirst brood emerges; second nesting in the monsoon
October–NovemberSecond brood; adults feed up for winter
December–FebruaryAdults overwinter in the tunnels, emerging on warm days

Two or three generations a year in the plains; an adult may live a year.

हिंदी: बसंत और बरसात में दो-तीन पीढ़ियाँ; अंडे से मक्खी छह-आठ हफ़्ते; सर्दी सुरंग में बिताती है, गरम दिनों में बाहर।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Pollen and nectar: Bottle Gourd, Bitter Gourd, pumpkin and all cucurbits; Pigeon Pea, beans; Brinjal and Tomato by buzz pollination; Palash, Semal, Amaltas, Giant Milkweed, Wild Passion Fruit, Lantana.
  • Nest wood: dry Solid Bamboo, dead branches of Neem, Mango and Babool, house timber.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: bee-eaters, Black Drongo, Indian Roller, Common Myna.
  • In the nest: parasitic bee-flies (Bombyliidae), cuckoo bees, mites, and the larvae of beetles; woodpeckers break tunnels open.
  • Spiders: Giant Wood Spider webs take some.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollination. The main service: yields of gourds and pulses in Indian field trials rise markedly with carpenter bee visits, and the bee works flowers at the hours when they are receptive. A farmer who keeps a few dry bamboo poles standing near the vegetable plot keeps the bees.

Damage. Tunnels weaken dry bamboo and softwood in roofs, doors and fences over years; the holes let in moisture and termites. Painting or oiling timber discourages boring; the bee avoids living and treated wood.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use. None practical; but the bhramar of Sanskrit and Hindi poetry, the black bee drunk on lotus and mango blossom, is this insect, and the bhanwra of folk song. It is more present in the district's culture than any other bee.

Today. Taken for granted; declining where dead wood and bamboo are tidied away.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Trap-nests (bundles of dry bamboo) are used in Indian research to raise carpenter bee numbers in cucurbit and pulse fields; a village could test whether a few bundles raise gourd yields.

हिंदी: परागण ही असली सेवा है — लौकी, कद्दू, अरहर, सेम का उत्पादन इसके आने से बढ़ता है, और यह फूल के सही समय पर आती है। सब्ज़ी के खेत के पास कुछ सूखे बाँस खड़े रखना इसे रखना है। नुकसान: सूखे बाँस और नरम लकड़ी की छत-बाड़ में सुरंगें; तेल या रंग लगी लकड़ी इसे नहीं सुहाती। कविता और लोकगीत का भ्रमर-भँवरा यही है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South Asia from Pakistan and India to Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bangladesh and Myanmar.

In India: Throughout the plains and foothills.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common everywhere.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in every village.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. At what hour do the first carpenter bees reach the gourd flowers? / भँवरे लौकी के फूल पर सबसे पहले कितने बजे आते हैं? — Sit by the vines from first light.
  2. Where are the nest holes in your house or shed? — Look for sawdust piles; count holes.
  3. Does a vine with many bee visits set more fruit? — Count visits and fruit on two vines.
  4. Do bundles of dry bamboo hung near the plot get colonised? / सूखे बाँस के बंडल टाँगने से भँवरे बसते हैं क्या? — Try it in March.
  5. Which birds chase them? — Watch at a flowering semal.

Field rule: A very large, glossy, all-black bee with dark iridescent wings and a loud hum, at big flowers or a round hole in dry bamboo, is the carpenter bee.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Johann Christian Fabricius in 1798 as Apis fenestrata in Supplementum Entomologiae Systematicae, p. 274. Fenestrata, "windowed", for the pale patch at the wing base.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Apidae, subfamily Xylocopinae, tribe Xylocopini; order Hymenoptera. Xylocopa ("wood-cutter") has some 500 species worldwide, about forty in India.

Phylogenetic notes. The Xylocopinae are large-bodied solitary and weakly social bees; the carpenter bees' buzz pollination and tolerance of heat make them the main large pollinators of the tropics outside the honey bees.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Common.
