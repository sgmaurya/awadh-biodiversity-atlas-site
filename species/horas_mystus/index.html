होराज मिस्टस मछली / Hora's Mystus

Mystus horai Jayaram, 1954 — Bagridae

होराज मिस्टस मछली — मैदानी नदियों, स्वच्छ धाराओं और गहरे तालों में पाई जाने वाली एक मध्यम-छोटी देसी कैटफिश (टेंगरा) है। इसका शरीर बिना छिलके का, चिकना और चमकीला भूरा-चांदी जैसा होता है। इसके मुँह पर आठ मूंछें होती हैं और इसके गलफड़े के ठीक पीछे तथा पूँछ के आधार पर गहरे काले धब्बे होते हैं। इसकी पीठ पर एक अलग चर्बीदार पंख (adipose fin) होता है जिसके और कांटेदार पंख के बीच साफ खाली जगह होती है। यह खाने में बहुत स्वादिष्ट और लोकप्रिय होती है।

Field tip: Look in slow, weed-edged river pools and oxbow lakes. A scaleless bagrid catfish with eight barbels, a distinct gap between the rayed dorsal fin and the adipose fin, and dark blotches behind the gill cover and at the base of the tail, is Mystus horai.

हिंदी: यह टेंगरा जाति की मछली है जिसकी पीठ के कांटेदार पंख और चर्बीदार पंख के बीच साफ खाली जगह होती है। इसके कंधे और पूँछ के पास काले निशान होते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description:

Mystus horai has a moderately elongate, streamlined body with a gently arched back and a compressed caudal peduncle (Jayaram 1954). The head is depressed and conical, comprising roughly one-fourth of the standard length (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The snout is rounded and obtuse; eyes are moderately large, situated in the upper half of the head, and covered by a thin translucent skin (Jayaram 1954). The mouth is small, transverse, and terminal, with jaws of equal length (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Teeth are sharp, villiform, and arranged in an unbroken curved band across the palate (Jayaram 2006).

Eight barbels are present: two nasal barbels extending beyond the hind border of the eye; two long maxillary barbels reaching backwards to the pelvic fin origin or the anterior rays of the anal fin; and four mandibular barbels along the chin, with the outer pair reaching the pectoral fin base (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin is supported by a stout osseous spine that is serrated along its posterior margin, followed by 7 branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The adipose fin is well developed, but unlike Mystus bleekeri, it is clearly separated from the base of the rayed dorsal fin by a distinct interspace equal to or greater than the dorsal fin base length (Jayaram 1954). The pectoral fins have a strong spine armed with 10–12 sharp internal teeth (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin carries 11 to 12 branched rays. The caudal fin is deeply forked with pointed lobes (Jayaram 1954).

Coloration is shining silvery-brown on the dorsum, with brassy reflections on the flanks and white on the belly (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). A distinct, rounded dark blotch is situated behind the operculum above the pectoral fin base, and a small dark spot marks the base of the caudal fin (Jayaram 1954). Fins are hyaline with a dusky yellowish-grey wash.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 16.5 सेमी तक लंबा, चिकना और बिना छिलके का होता है। मुंह पर आठ मूंछें होती हैं। पीठ पर कांटेदार पंख और उसके पीछे थोड़ी दूरी पर चर्बी वाला पंख होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Hora's Mystus is a nocturnal and crepuscular benthic catfish inhabiting rivers, perennial canals, and oxbow wetlands (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It prefers flowing river reaches with sandy-muddy bottoms, deep scour holes, and weed-fringed banks.

During daylight hours, adults shelter under sunken logs, undercut clay banks, or inside root masses of riparian shrubs and submerged macrophytes (Jayaram 2006). At dusk, they emerge to forage across the riverbed and shallows (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Using their sensitive maxillary barbels, they probe into the surface silt to detect hidden midge larvae, worms, and small molluscs. During the high-water monsoon season, they move onto inundated floodplains and connecting drainage canals to forage on abundant invertebrate blooms, returning to deeper river pools as dry-season low flows set in (Jayaram 2006).

हिंदी: यह रात के समय सक्रिय रहने वाली मछली है। दिन में यह पानी के नीचे जड़ों और मिट्टी के खोह में छिपी रहती है और रात में बाहर निकलकर कीचड़ में कीड़े और छोटी मछलियां खोजती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Spawning coincides with the southwest monsoon rains from June to August (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Rising river levels and cooler water temperatures trigger courtship. Pairs spawn in shallow, vegetated floodwaters over submerged grasses and weed roots. Females deposit clusters of adhesive yellowish eggs that attach to plant roots. The eggs hatch after a few days into tiny larvae that absorb their yolk reserves before actively feeding on rotifers, cladocerans, and midge larvae (Jayaram 2006).

हिंदी: मानसून में पानी भरने पर यह मछली उथले किनारों की घास में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडों से बच्चे निकलकर पौधों की जड़ों में छिप जाते हैं और तेजी से बढ़ते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Mystus horai is an opportunistic carnivorous and detritivorous bottom feeder (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Benthic insect larvae, including chironomid midges, caddisflies, and mayfly nymphs (Jayaram 2006).
  • Small freshwater crustaceans, including water fleas (Daphnia), ostracods, and tiny prawns (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Small forage fish fry and minnows ambushed in the dark.
  • Aquatic oligochaete worms and small snails extracted from mud.
  • Soft organic detritus browsed incidentally from silt.
हिंदी: यह मांसाहारी मछली है जो तलहटी के कीचड़ में मिलने वाले कीड़ों, मच्छरों के लार्वा, छोटे झींगों और छोटी मछलियों के बच्चों को खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Piscivorous fishes: Large river predators like Wallago attu (Striped Catfish), Channa marulius (Great Snakehead), and Bagarius bagarius (Goonch) hunt mystus catfishes.
  • Wading birds: Night herons, pond herons, and egrets spear adults foraging in shallow mud banks.
  • Water snakes: Checkered keelbacks pursue them among shoreline weeds.
  • River otters: Hunt schooling bottom fish along perennial river stretches.
हिंदी: बोआल, सौर, गूंज, बगुले और उदबिलाव इस मछली का शिकार करते हैं। इसके नुकीले कांटे इसे शिकारियों से कुछ सुरक्षा प्रदान करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Nutritional and market value: Hora's Mystus is a popular and nutritious small indigenous food fish across northern India. Captured from wild rivers, tals, and canals using bamboo funnel traps, cast nets, and shore seines. Its flesh is white, tender, and tasty, supplying vital animal protein, calcium, iron, and essential fatty acids to rural families at an accessible market price.

Floodplain wetland role: In flooded rice paddies and drainage canals, wild schools feed on pest insects and mosquito larvae, contributing to natural biological pest suppression in rural watersheds (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Traditional: Consumed as an everyday staple fish in rural households along river corridors.

Today: Sold fresh in morning village fish markets (haats) as a popular, affordable small catfish.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing captive breeding and polyculture potential for Mystus horai in community earthen ponds.

हिंदी: यह टेंगरा मछली के रूप में ग्रामीण इलाकों में बहुत चाव से खाई जाने वाली देसी मछली है। यह खेतों के कीड़ों को खाकर किसानों की मदद करती है और उत्तम पोषण देती है।

Hora's Mystus is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in perennial rivers, oxbow lakes (tals), weed-rich village tanks, and major canal networks. Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the northern Indian subcontinent, distributed across India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Found throughout the Indus, Ganges, and Brahmaputra river drainages, from foothill rivers to the plains (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, and Gomti river networks and associated floodplain depressions.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident native species in the Ghaghara, Kuwano, and Ami river corridors and major oxbow wetlands.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by K. C. Jayaram in 1954 in Records of the Indian Museum, Volume 51, Part 4, page 536. Type locality: Kalrai, Punjab, India.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its range.

Family and order placement. Order Siluriformes, family Bagridae. The genus Mystus Scopoli, 1777, comprises small-to-medium Old World bagrid catfishes.

Phylogenetic notes. Mystus horai was named in honour of Dr. Sunder Lal Hora, former Director of the Zoological Survey of India. It is distinguished from allied bagrids by its distinct dorsal-adipose interspace and specific barbel lengths (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, secure wild populations across northern Indian river systems.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Mystus horai है और यह Bagridae परिवार की सदस्य है। इसका नाम प्रसिद्ध भारतीय प्राणीशास्त्री डॉ. सुंदर लाल होरा के सम्मान में रखा गया है।
