खरेटी / False Mallow

Malvastrum coromandelianum (L.) Garcke — Malvaceae

खरेटी — एक छोटा, सूखी मिट्टी का खरपतवार जो गर्मी में उत्तर भारत के खेतों और बंजर भूमियों में उगता है। इसके सूक्ष्म पीले रंग के फूल और छोटी-छोटी, गोल, खुरदरी गेंदों जैसी फली इसे आसानी से पहचान देते हैं। पत्तियाँ नरम, लेकिन इसकी जड़ें गहरी होती हैं, जिससे यह सूखे में भी जीवित रहता है। बस्ती में यह सर्दियों की फसलों की बोई के दौरान खेतों में आता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
FormProcumbent to erect annual herb, 20–60 cm tall, much-branched
LeavesSmall, ovate to linear, 1–3 cm long, soft, with minute hairs, margins crenate
FlowersSmall, yellow, 6–8 mm diameter, solitary in leaf axils, five petals
FruitGlobular or hemispherical schizocarp, 4–5 mm diameter, with 5–8 rounded mericarps, rugose
StemSlender, greenish, hairy, often becoming woody at base

Field tip: Look for a small, spreading weed with tiny yellow flowers and peculiar spiky ball-shaped fruits. The plant is soft and easily pulled, but the deep taproot can regrow if left in the soil.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: छोटा पौधा, पीले फूल, गेंद जैसी खुरदरी फली। रेतीली मिट्टी को पसंद करता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (specimens from eastern UP fields):

FeatureMeasurement
Plant height20–60 cm
Leaf length1–3 cm
Flower diameter6–8 mm
Fruit diameter4–5 mm

Stem and bark: Slender, greenish or brownish, hairy, branching from base. Root is a deep taproot that resists pulling.

Leaves: Alternate, ovate to linear-lanceolate, 1–3 cm long, hairy on both surfaces, margins crenate, petioles short.

Flowers: Solitary in the leaf axils, small, yellow, five petals, with a distinctive epicalyx of 3 linear segments below the calyx.

Fruit: A schizocarp that splits into 5–8 one-seeded mericarps, each rugose and reticulate on the surface, resembling a tiny spiky ball.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

A thermophilic, xerophytic weed that grows on dry, well-drained soils of the plains. Establishes quickly on wastelands and field margins. The deep taproot allows it to access moisture even in dry seasons. Flowers are visited by small bees and flies. The fruit is dispersed by wind when dry.

हिंदी: जून में अंकुरण, अगस्त में फूल, सितंबर तक फल।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Small bees and flies visit flowers.
  • Host: Caterpillars of some butterflies feed on Malvaceae leaves.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf beetles occasionally feed on foliage.
  • Usually disease-free.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Today: A minor weed in eastern UP, most common on sandy or gravelly wastelands. It rarely competes seriously with crops because it is small and matures quickly. Its deep root makes manual removal difficult, but it does not persist in irrigated or regularly plowed fields.

Control: Allow natural senescence in fallow fields; pull seedlings in crops if numerous.

हिंदी: बस्ती में एक छोटा, कम महत्वपूर्ण खरपतवार। रेतीली मिट्टी में ज्यादा आता है।

Expected in sandy wastelands, field margins, and pathways of Basti district during the monsoon and early autumn (June–October). More common in dry seasons. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, though none yet from Basti–Ayodhya.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to Americas; introduced and naturalized across tropical Asia, including India.

In India: Throughout the plains and drier regions, up to 1,500 m.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common on sandy and gravelly wastelands.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Naturalized resident; recorded from sandy and disturbed sites.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described by Linnaeus as Sida coromandeliana in 1756; transferred to Malvastrum by Garcke in 1849.

Subspecies. Monotypic.

Family and order placement. Order Malvales, family Malvaceae.

Phylogenetic notes. Malvastrum is related to Sida and Hibiscus.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Naturalized and abundant; no threat.
