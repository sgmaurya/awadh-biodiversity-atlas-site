पेंटेड लेडी तितली / Painted Lady

Vanessa cardui (Linnaeus, 1758) — Nymphalidae

पेंटेड लेडी तितली — दुनिया की सबसे व्यापक तितली, जो हर महाद्वेश में (अंटार्कटिका को छोड़कर) पाई जाती है। यह एक रंगीन तितली है, जिसके पंखों पर नारंगी, काली और सफेद पैच होते हैं। इसका नाम "पेंटेड" आता है इसके विविध रंगों से। यह तितली एक असाधारण प्रवासी है — कभी-कभी इसके लाखों का झुंड एक बार में एक क्षेत्र से दूसरे क्षेत्र में चला जाता है। इसके कैटरपिलर 300 से ज़्यादा पौधों पर खा सकते हैं, इसलिए यह दुनियाभर में पाई जाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Upperwing colorReddish-orange with black patches and white spots on forewing apex
HindwingOrange and brown with white spots; black spots near margin
UnderwingMarbled tan and brown with smaller spots; resembles lichen-covered bark
SizeWingspan 48–65 mm; medium-sized butterfly
BodyThorax and abdomen covered with white scales (hoary appearance)
FlightFast, erratic; males often perch on flowers

Field tip: Look on thistles, mallows, or other flowers in open areas. The distinctive reddish-orange upperwing with black and white patches is diagnostic. Quick, darting flight with frequent flower visits. Often seen mud-puddling on damp soil.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: नारंगी पंख, काले और सफेद धब्बे। तेजी से उड़ती है। फूलों और कंटीली पौधों पर।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (adult specimens from the Gangetic plains):

MeasurementRange
Forewing length21–27 mm
Wingspan48–65 mm
ColourationHighly variable; seasonal forms differ

Seasonal forms: Spring and summer forms are brighter orange; autumn forms are paler with reduced black patches. This is a thermal polyphenism response to temperature.

हिंदी: आकार मध्यम। रंग मौसम के अनुसार बदलते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Highly mobile and migratory. Males perch on flowers and fly out to intercept passing butterflies. The species is famous for its irregular but dramatic migratory episodes: in some years, waves of millions arrive in a region, breed for 2–3 generations, then disappear. Migration is triggered by weather and resource availability. It is one of the most successful and widespread butterflies globally, largely due to its extreme polyphagy (ability to feed on hundreds of plant species) and high fecundity.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants (300+ species, including):

  • Thistles (Cirsium, Carduus spp.)
  • Mallows (Malva, Althaea spp.)
  • Nettles (Urtica spp.)
  • Legumes (clover, alfalfa)
  • Crucifers (cabbage, mustard)
  • Many other plant families

Adult food:

  • Nectar from virtually any flower
  • Mud-puddling for minerals

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Flycatchers, drongos, and other insectivorous birds prey on adults
  • Parasitoid wasps: Multiple species of wasp parasitize caterpillars and pupae
  • Spiders: Web and hunting spiders
  • Ants: Predatory ants attack pupae

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollinator and minor pest. The Painted Lady is a significant pollinator of wild and cultivated flowers. Occasionally, large-scale migrations can cause temporary crop damage if caterpillars are abundant, but localized damage is rarely significant. The species is primarily beneficial as a pollinator.

हिंदी: पूरे विश्व में सबसे आम तितली। फूलों को परागित करती है। कभी-कभी कीट की तरह दिखती है, पर आम तौर पर फायदेमंद है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Expected in Basti and Ayodhya districts in open fields and agricultural areas. The Painted Lady is one of the most frequent migrants to the region; waves of individuals may arrive seasonally, particularly during warm months (March–November). Breeding occurs on thistles and mallows when populations settle. GBIF holds published records from these districts.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Found on all continents except Antarctica; one of the most widely distributed butterflies in the world.

In India: Present throughout the country; particularly abundant during migration events.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common migrant across all districts; some local breeding populations.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Seasonal arrival of migratory waves; local breeding on thistle and mallow patches.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1758 in Systema Naturae, 10th ed., p. 474, from European specimens.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies; V. c. cardui (nominate subspecies) occurs worldwide, including India.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae (brush-footed butterflies).

Phylogenetic notes. Vanessa cardui is closely related to Vanessa atalanta (Red Admiral); recent molecular work confirms their sister relationship within the genus.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. One of the most abundant and widespread butterflies globally with no conservation concerns.
