पलास गल / Pallas's Gull

Ichthyaetus ichthyaetus (Pallas, 1773) — Laridae

पलास गल — यह एक बड़ी सफेद गल है जो सर्दियों में बड़ी झीलों पर आती है। यह पक्षी मछली खाता है और कभी-कभी अन्य पक्षियों का शिकार भी करता है। इसके सिर पर काली टोपी जैसी होती है। सर्दियों में झीलों पर झुंडों में दिखाई देती है।

Field tip: A large, powerful white gull with distinctive black cap on head. Much larger than other gulls commonly seen in eastern UP. Dark slate-grey back and wings with dark tips distinctive. Pale bill and powerful appearance identify it as a large predatory gull.

Size: 58–68 cm long; wingspan 145–160 cm. A large gull, among the largest in the region. Weight 850–1200 g.

Plumage (winter): Head white with black cap extending from forehead over crown to nape. Neck white. Mantle (back) dark slate-grey. Wings dark slate-grey to blackish with white tips and white spots or "mirrors" on primaries. Rump white. Tail white. Underparts pure white. Underwings white.

Plumage (breeding, not seen in UP): Head and neck entirely black except for small white spots on eyelids.

Bill: Large, powerful, pale yellow or greenish with red or dark spot on lower mandible. Hooked tip, adapted for gripping fish and tearing prey.

Legs: Long, pale greenish-yellow or pale.

Feet: Webbed.

Eyes: Pale or pale yellow; alert and intelligent.

Tail: Square-ended white tail.

Sexes: Males slightly larger than females.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Calls: Harsh, loud calls "kee-ow" or similar. Aggressive and loud, especially when feeding.
  2. Contact calls: Various harsh notes.
  3. Alarm calls: Sharp, loud calls.
हिंदी: जोर से चिल्लाता है। "की-ऊ" सी आवाज़। झुंडों में शोर मचाता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Piscivore: Feeds almost entirely on fish. Powerful predator capable of taking substantial fish.

Feeding: Swims on surface and dives to catch fish. Also takes fish from other birds (kleptoparasitism). Scavenges dead fish.

Fish prey: Takes fish of various sizes. Capable of taking sizeable fish. Prefers live fish hunted in water.

Kleptoparasitism: Robs other birds of fish and other food. Aggressive interactions with other waterbirds.

Predatory behaviour: Powerful and aggressive. Dominant over other gulls and waterbirds at feeding sites.

Social behaviour: Often solitary or in small flocks on water. Less gregarious than other gulls. Aggressive toward conspecifics.

Flight: Powerful, heavy flight. Can be swift and manoeuvrable despite size.

Swimming: Powerful swimmers. Spend most of time on water or in flight.

हिंदी: मछली शिकार करता है। दूसरी चिड़ियों से मछली छीनता है। अकेला या छोटे झुंडों में रहता है। बहुत आक्रामक होता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Winter visitor: Arrives in November–December from northern breeding grounds. Departs by March–April.

Breeding range: Central Asia, particularly around the Caspian Sea and other Central Asian water bodies. Breeds April–July.

Breeding grounds: Does not breed in Uttar Pradesh. Breeds in remote Central Asian lakes.

Migration: Long-distance migrant. Moves south to winter in south-central Asia and the Indian subcontinent.

Wintering: Solitary or in small flocks on large water bodies. Occupy same wintering areas year to year.

Moult: Complete moult. Breeding plumage acquired before departure from wintering grounds.

Timing in UP: Arrival November–December; peak December–February; departure March–April.

हिंदी: नवंबर में आता है, अप्रैल तक रहता है। बड़ी झीलों पर सर्दी काटता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Chick predators (breeding only): Jackals, large raptors, foxes, corvids

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected status: All wild birds in India are protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972. Hunting is illegal.

Fish predator: Feeds on fish and may remove substantial quantities. Can impact fish populations in smaller water bodies.

Interaction with fish farming: Predates farmed fish. Causes damage in some aquaculture operations.

Water body health indicator: Presence indicates fish productivity and suitable habitat. Indicator of large, suitable water bodies.

Cultural significance: Known locally with various names. Historically hunted in some regions for sport or persecution.

हिंदी: यह पक्षी विधि अधिनियम 1972 के अंतर्गत संरक्षित है। इसका शिकार करना कानूनी रूप से निषिद्ध है। मछली पालन में नुकसान पहुँचा सकता है।

Pallas's Gull is a winter visitor to the Indo-Gangetic plain and is expected on larger water bodies throughout eastern Uttar Pradesh where fish populations are substantial. GBIF holds published records from these districts. The species appears to be uncommon in the region but recorded from major lakes and reservoirs. The large size, distinctive black cap, and powerful appearance make it identifiable. Peak occurrence should be December–February. A first record would require a dated photograph with GPS coordinates and date, submitted through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds in Central Asia around the Caspian and Aral seas and adjacent regions. Winters in Central Asia and south to north-central Asia and occasionally northern India.

In India: Winter visitor to northern India, particularly large water bodies of the Indo-Gangetic plain. Uncommon to rare.

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter visitor to larger lakes and reservoirs, particularly in central and eastern regions. Uncommon.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Winter visitor to larger water bodies. Expected November–April. Apparently uncommon; presence dependent on water body size and fish populations.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Pallas in 1773. Type locality: Caspian Sea region.

Subspecies. I. i. ichthyaetus (Pallas, 1773) — Caspian region populations. Some subspecific variation; Indian populations may be of western subspecies.

Family and order placement. Order Charadriiformes; family Laridae (gulls, terns, and skimmers). Genus Ichthyaetus contains large predatory gulls, formerly in genus Larus.

Phylogenetic notes. Ichthyaetus ichthyaetus is closely related to other large predatory gulls. Formerly classified in Larus but now placed in separate genus Ichthyaetus.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) globally. Populations are large and stable. No major conservation threats, though human persecution historically occurred.
