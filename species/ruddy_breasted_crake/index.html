रडी-ब्रेस्टेड क्रेक / Ruddy-breasted Crake

Porzana fusca (Linnaeus, 1766) — Rallidae

रडी-ब्रेस्टेड क्रेक — यह छोटी, भूरी रेल (crake) पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के दलदलों और नरकुल के बिस्तरों में साल भर पाई जाती है। इसकी सबसे बड़ी पहचान इसकी लाल-भूरी छाती है, जो इसे दूसरी रेलों से अलग करती है। यह एक छिपी हुई, शर्मीली चिड़िया है जो घने नरकुल में रहती है और रात को अधिक सक्रिय होती है। इसकी कर्कश आवाज़ से अक्सर इसकी उपस्थिति ज्ञात होती है।

Field tip: A small, cryptic crake with a distinctive rusty or rufous breast. Very difficult to see as it stays deep in reed beds and marsh vegetation. More often heard (harsh, distinctive calls) than seen. Call is a loud, harsh krrp-krrp-krrp or took-took, given particularly at dusk.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

Size: 19–22 cm long; wingspan 28–32 cm. Weight 45–60 g.

Plumage: Crown and nape olive-brown or greyish. Back and rump olive-brown. Wings darker olive-brown with darker flight feathers. Tail short, brownish. Lores and eye-ring greyish. Cheeks olive-brown. Throat and breast rufous or rusty-red (diagnostic). Belly buff-white. Undertail coverts barred black and white (diagnostic).

Bill: Relatively long, pointed, greenish or yellow-green.

Legs and feet: Long, reddish or orange; adapted for wading.

Eyes: Dark.

Sexes: Similar; females slightly paler on breast.

Vocalisations

  1. Call: Loud, harsh krrp-krrp-krrp or took-took-took, given particularly at dusk and throughout the night. Very distinctive and loud for the bird's size.
  2. Other calls: Sharp chip and various clicking sounds.
  3. Song: The harsh calls serve as territorial advertisement.
हिंदी: ज़ोर से "क्रर्प-क्रर्प" आवाज़। शाम को तेज़ गाता है। रात भर बोलता है। कठोर आवाज़ वाली चिड़िया।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Cryptic and secretive: Stays deep in dense reed beds and marsh vegetation. Extremely difficult to observe directly. More often detected by voice.

Feeding: Omnivorous; insects, small crustaceans, snails, seeds and plant material. Walks through shallow water and probes in mud.

Feeding behaviour: Walks methodically through marsh vegetation; picks insects from stems and leaves; probes in mud for invertebrates; eats fallen seeds.

Activity pattern: Largely nocturnal and crepuscular; most vocal at dawn and dusk; remains active throughout the night.

Solitary and territorial: Solitary or in pairs; territorial throughout the year. Males call to maintain territories and attract females.

Roosting: In dense marsh vegetation; roosting sites well-concealed.

हिंदी: दलदलों में छिपा रहता है। रात को ज्यादा सक्रिय होता है। आवाज़ से अपना इलाक़ा चिन्हित करता है। अकेले या जोड़ों में रहता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Resident: Year-round resident; breeds locally (April–September).

Breeding: Nest is a well-concealed cup of aquatic vegetation placed in reed clumps or dense marsh growth. Lays 5–7 pale buff eggs with brownish spots.

Clutch: 5–7 eggs.

Incubation: 16–18 days; both parents incubate (unusually for crakes).

Fledging: Chicks fledge in approximately 18–20 days. Precocial; leave nest soon after hatching.

Moult: Complete annual moult post-breeding.

हिंदी: साल भर यहाँ रहता है। अप्रैल से सितंबर तक प्रजनन करता है। घने नरकुल में घोंसला बनाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Primary food items:

  • Aquatic insects and insect larvae
  • Small crustaceans (shrimp, small crabs)
  • Snails and molluscs
  • Aquatic worms
  • Seeds of aquatic plants (seasonally)
  • Small fish (occasionally)

Obtained by walking through shallow water, probing in mud, and picking from vegetation.

हिंदी: कीड़े, छोटे केकड़े और घोंघे खाता है। पानी में चलते हुए शिकार करता है। बीज भी खाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Raptors: Marsh harriers and water eagles may predate on crakes
  • Snakes: Water snakes and other snakes hunt in wetlands
  • Corvids: Crows may predate eggs and chicks
  • Monitor lizards: May occasionally take chicks

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Rice paddy inhabitant: Common in rice paddies during flooding; does not cause damage to crops.

Pest control: By feeding on insects and small invertebrates in rice paddies, may assist in natural pest control.

Hunting history: Hunted in some parts of its range historically, though hunting is now prohibited in India.

Legal status: Protected under the Wildlife Protection Act 1972.

More often detected by distinctive harsh calls (particularly at dusk and night) than by visual observation. Numbers may increase during monsoon when wetlands are fully inundated. Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia. Resident and partial migrant throughout.

In India: Widespread resident throughout the Indian peninsula in suitable marsh and wetland habitat.

In Uttar Pradesh: Resident throughout the state; expected in reed beds and rice paddies.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Year-round resident in reed beds, rice paddies and marsh vegetation.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1766 in Systema Naturae (12th edition) as Rallus fuscus. Type locality is India.

Subspecies. Monotypic; only subspecies is P. f. fusca.

Family and order placement. Order Gruiformes; family Rallidae (rails, coots and crakes). Genus Porzana comprises the crakes, small secretive rails of worldwide distribution.

Phylogenetic notes. Closely related to other Asian crakes including Baillon's crake and spotted crake.

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern globally. Widespread and common resident throughout the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia in suitable wetland habitat.
