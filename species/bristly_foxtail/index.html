चिपकू घास / Bristly Foxtail

Setaria verticillata (L.) P. Beauv. — Poaceae

चिपकू घास — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश की बंजर और खेत की मेड़ों पर उगने वाली एक सामान्य घास, जिसकी बालियाँ (inflorescence) कपड़ों से चिपक जाती हैं — इसीलिए इसका नाम "चिपकू" है। यह मानसून में तेजी से बढ़ती है और पशुओं के लिए अच्छा चारा प्रदान करती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size30–90 cm tall annual grass
GrowthTufted, upright stems; branching base
LeavesLinear, 10–25 cm long, 4–8 mm wide; slightly hairy
InflorescenceCompact cylindrical panicle (spike-like), 5–12 cm long; golden or purple-tinged
BristlesShort, stiff bristles below each spikelet; barbed, causing them to adhere to clothing and animal fur
The testPull the panicle on your sleeve — bristles catch and adhere; "sticky" feeling

Field tip: Look for a medium-height tufted grass with a compact spike-like inflorescence; if you brush the panicle on your clothes, it sticks readily due to barbed bristles. Abundant in disturbed areas after monsoon.

Morphology आकारिकता

Biometrics (typical measurements in the Gangetic plain):

MeasurementRange
Plant height30–90 cm
Leaf length10–25 cm
Leaf width4–8 mm
Panicle length5–12 cm
Bristle length3–8 mm

Stems: Tufted, erect, smooth, with several nodes.

Leaves: Linear, acute, with parallel veins. Leaf sheaths are clasping, open on one side.

Inflorescence: A dense, spike-like panicle with spikelets arranged in whorls (verticillate). The characteristic feature is the presence of 2–6 stiff, barbed bristles below each spikelet. These bristles catch easily on fabric and fur, aiding seed dispersal.

Spikelets: Small, with two florets; the lower is sterile, the upper fertile.

हिंदी: घनी बाली जो कपड़े से चिपक जाती है। पत्ता नरम। बाली सोने-भूरे रंग की।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Setaria verticillata is a warm-season annual grass native to tropical Asia and Africa. It is a pioneer species, rapidly colonizing disturbed ground. The barbed bristles are an adaptation for dispersal via animal fur and clothing; the "sticky" panicles readily detach and travel considerable distances. The plant is favoured by disturbance and thrives in fields with frequent cultivation.

हिंदी: मानसून में तेजी से उगता है। बंजर जमीन पर सबसे पहले। बालियों से बीज दूर तक फैलते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Grazing animals: Grazed when young and tender; coarser when mature.
  • Seed dispersal: Barbed bristles stick to animal fur and fabric, enhancing long-distance dispersal.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Generally disease-free; no major pests.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Fodder: When young and tender (July–August), the leaves and shoots are palatable to cattle and buffalo. As the plant matures, it becomes coarser and less preferred.

Seed dispersal and crop agriculture: The barbed bristles are an effective mechanism for dispersal but can be a nuisance as they adhere to clothing, making the plant a minor impediment in field harvesting and threshing.

Pasture pioneer: Helps stabilize disturbed soil in the early monsoon.

हिंदी: जवान पौधे का चारा अच्छा है। परिपक्व हो जाने पर कड़ा हो जाता है। बालियों की चिपचिपाहट से बीज दूर-दूर तक फैलते हैं।

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Bristly Foxtail is to be expected in disturbed areas, field margins, and wastelands throughout Basti district during the monsoon and early dry season (June–November). GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain. The first confirmed local record would be a photograph showing the characteristic spike-like inflorescence with barbed bristles, along with place and date.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Asia and Africa; now found in many warm regions worldwide.

In India: Widespread in grasslands and disturbed areas; particularly common in monsoon regions.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common throughout the state in disturbed habitats.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Frequent in field margins, wastelands, and monsoon-flooded areas.

Field rule: A medium grass with a compact, spike-like inflorescence bearing stiff barbed bristles that stick to clothing is Setaria verticillata.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. The species was originally described by Linnaeus as Panicum verticillatum in 1753. It was transferred to Setaria by Jean-Baptiste Beauvois in 1812 in Essai d'une Nouvelle Agrostographie. The epithet verticillata refers to the verticillate (whorled) arrangement of spikelets.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Poales, family Poaceae, subfamily Panicoideae, tribe Setarieae.

Phylogenetic notes. Setaria contains about 50 species; S. verticillata is closely related to cultivated foxtail millet (S. italica).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely distributed; no conservation concerns.
