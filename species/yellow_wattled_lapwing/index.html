ज़र्दी टिटहरी / Yellow-wattled Lapwing

Vanellus malabaricus (Bosc, 1792) — Charadriidae

ज़र्दी टिटहरी — यह चिड़िया खेतों और सूखी जगहों पर रहती है। इसके चेहरे के नीचे पीली झलरें होती हैं, जिससे पहचान बहुत आसान है। यह बहुत तेज़ आवाज़ में चिल्लाती है, खासकर अगर कोई इसके घोंसले के पास जाए। हमेशा जोड़ों में या छोटे झुंड में रहती है। बाँस और घास में छिपी हुई घोंसली बनाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

SizeSmall lapwing, 26–27 cm
PlumageBrown above, white below; distinctive yellow face wattles
Face markingsYellow wattles below eyes; black stripe through face

TailShort; mostly white with black terminal band

Field tip: The only lapwing in eastern UP with yellow face wattles below the eyes. The yellow wattles combined with the brown and white plumage pattern are diagnostic. The loud call is a dead giveaway. Look in fallow fields, grasslands, and river margins where the bird runs rapidly on the ground.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange

Plumage: Crown brown. Lore and supercilium white. Face with complex pattern of white, brown, and black. Yellow wattles below eye are the most distinctive feature — small, flesh-coloured to yellow fleshy structures. Upper back and wings brown. Lower back and rump white. Tail mostly white with black terminal band and black central feathers. Underparts white; flanks may show buff wash.

Head: Crown brown; face patterned. Yellow wattles below eye are characteristic. Eyes dark; forward-facing.

Bill: Straight, relatively short, dark-coloured.

Legs: Long, yellow to greenish-yellow; placed far forward on body for rapid running. Toes relatively long.

Sexes: Similar; males may have slightly more pronounced wattles and darker facial markings.

Juveniles: Duller than adults; wattles less prominent initially; facial markings less distinct.

Vocalisations

Highly vocal, particularly during breeding and alarm:

  1. Main call: A loud, distinctive pee-wit or ti-tee, repeated rapidly.
  2. Alarm call: A loud, insistent chip-chip-chip when disturbed or defending territory.
  3. Territorial call: Loud calls given from elevated perches or in flight.
हिंदी: बहुत तेज़, कर्कश आवाज़ दिन भर सुनाई देती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Ground forager: Feeds on the ground, consuming:

  • Insects and insect larvae
  • Small invertebrates
  • Occasionally seeds and plant material

Feeding technique: Runs in short bursts on open ground, stopping abruptly to pick insects from the surface. Uses visual foraging; may probe in soft ground.

Territorial: Highly territorial; pairs defend feeding and breeding territories year-round. Males are aggressive toward intruders.

Social structure: Solitary, in pairs, or in small flocks. Pairs remain together year-round.

Breeding: Monogamous; both parents defend territory fiercely.

Habitat generalist: Unlike River Lapwing (restricted to sandbars), Yellow-wattled Lapwing is a generalist of open, undisturbed ground including grasslands, fallow fields, and cultivated margins.

Resident: Year-round resident in eastern UP on suitable open ground.

हिंदी: खेतों और मैदानों में रहता है। बहुत आक्रामक और क्षेत्रीय है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Resident: Present year-round in suitable grassland and cultivated habitat.

Breeding: February to August; peak March–June. Highly vocal during breeding season.

Nesting: Ground nest — a simple scrape in grass, fallow field, or sandy ground. Minimal lining with dry grass. Nest camouflaged by surrounding vegetation.

Eggs: 3–4 buff or olive eggs with dark markings; incubation approximately 24–26 days by both parents.

Chicks: Precocial; leave nest within hours. Parents guard chicks; chicks feed themselves immediately.

Fledging: Chicks become independent at approximately 35 days.

Roosting: On ground in short grass or on rocks; may roost communally in post-breeding period.

हिंदी: साल भर रहता है। बसंत से गर्मी तक प्रजनन करता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Raptors: Shikra and other small eagles
  • Snakes: Monitor lizards, grass snakes
  • Crows and corvids: Depredate eggs and chicks
  • Jackals and wild dogs: May hunt along margins
  • Habitat disturbance: Ploughing and agricultural activity may destroy nests
हिंदी: बाज़, साँप, कौवे का खतरा। खेतों की जुताई से घोंसले नष्ट हो जाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected species: Listed in Schedule IV of the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, amended 2022. All wild birds are protected.

Pest control: By consuming grasshoppers, beetles, and other ground invertebrates, the species provides some benefit in agricultural pest management, though this is not a primary role.

Agricultural compatibility: Unlike some lapwings that avoid cultivation, Yellow-wattled Lapwing tolerates and even uses fallow fields and crop margins for foraging and breeding, making it compatible with traditional agricultural landscapes.

Habitat indicator: Its presence indicates healthy, undisturbed grasslands and fallow fields with low pesticide use.

हिंदी: विधि अधिनियम 1972 के तहत संरक्षित है। खेतों में कीटों को नियंत्रित करने में मदद करता है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

The Yellow-wattled Lapwing is expected as a year-round resident in grasslands, fallow fields, and open cultivated areas of Basti and Ayodhya districts. The species should be looked for on the ground in open areas, particularly in early morning and late evening when it is most active. The loud calls and territorial displays are often the best indication of presence. The combination of yellow wattles on the face, brown upper parts, white underparts, and yellow legs is diagnostic. A first record would be a clear photograph showing the yellow face wattles, with date and location.

हिंदी: खुली जगहों पर, खेतों और घास के मैदान में देख सकते हैं। तेज़ आवाज़ सुनो।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent and parts of Southeast Asia, primarily in open grassland and cultivated habitats.

In India: Widespread in suitable open ground habitat throughout the mainland.

In Uttar Pradesh: Distributed throughout open areas and grasslands; present in all districts with suitable habitat.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in grasslands, fallow fields, and cultivated margins throughout the region.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Louis-Auguste-Guillaume Bosc in 1792. Type locality: India.

Subspecies. Monotypic — Vanellus malabaricus malabaricus. No subspecies are recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Charadriiformes; family Charadriidae (plovers and lapwings). The genus Vanellus contains lapwings found globally; V. malabaricus is endemic to the Indian subcontinent.

Phylogenetic notes. Molecular studies confirm Vanellus malabaricus as distinct from other Vanellus species. The yellow wattle structure represents a unique adaptation among lapwings and may play a role in species and sex recognition.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) on the IUCN Red List. The species has a wide range and tolerates open cultivated and grassland habitats, though it remains dependent on undisturbed ground suitable for ground nesting.
