ग्रेट ऑरेंज टिप तितली / Great Orange Tip

Hebomoia glaucippe (Linnaeus, 1758) — Pieridae

ग्रेट ऑरेंज टिप तितली — यह एक बड़ी सफ़ेद तितली है जिसके आगे के पंखों (forewings) पर चमकीली नारंगी-लाल रंग की चोटी होती है। इसके पिछले पंख (hindwings) पूरी तरह सफ़ेद होते हैं। इसकी उड़ान तेज़ और शक्तिशाली होती है। यह तितली शहरों और गाँवों के बागों में, खासकर जहाँ सब्जियों या जंगली सरसों के पौधे हों, देखी जाती है। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश में यह एक साधारण तितली है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
WingspanLarge; 70–85 mm
Ground colorWhite or cream
Forewing apexBright orange-red to deep orange patch (very prominent)
HindwingWhite, unblemished
BodyStout, robust
FlightFast, strong, direct flight; flies at medium to high height

Field tip: Look for a large white butterfly with a striking bright orange or red-orange patch on the forewing apex, flying with strong, swift flight over gardens, cultivated areas, and forest edges. The size and prominent orange patch make identification easy.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Adult wingspan70–85 mm
Body length24–30 mm

Adult butterfly: Large, white or cream base. Forewing has a large, prominent bright orange to orange-red patch covering the apex and extending down the costa. The hindwing is entirely white. Sexual dimorphism present with females sometimes duller orange.

Caterpillar: Green or pale green, smooth, with fine hairs, feeding on Cruciferae.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Great Orange Tip is a strong, swift flyer characteristic of open woodland, gardens, and cultivated areas. It is a powerful flyer that travels greater distances than smaller butterflies. Males perch on elevated points and vegetation, defending territories and pursuing females. The species is attracted to a variety of flowers and host plants. It is more common in areas with tree cover and flowering vegetation.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants (Cruciferae/Brassicaceae):

  • Mustard (Brassica spp.)
  • Radish (Raphanus sativus)
  • Wild cruciferous plants
  • Various garden vegetables

Adult nectar sources:

  • Flowers on trees, in gardens, and on wildflowers

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Large birds: Hunt flying adults
  • Dragonflies: Large species can catch this butterfly
  • Spiders: Web spiders and active hunters
  • Parasitoid wasps: Attack caterpillars and pupae

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollinator service. The species visits a variety of flowers in gardens and wild areas, providing pollination services.

Garden butterfly. Common in home gardens and cultivated areas where vegetables are grown.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South Asia, Southeast Asia, East Asia.

In India: Distributed throughout the subcontinent from plains to lower elevations.

In Uttar Pradesh: Present in gardens and cultivated areas throughout the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in cultivated and garden habitats year-round.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1758 from Asian specimens. The genus Hebomoia comprises large orange-tipped Asian pierid butterflies.

Subspecies. The nominate subspecies H. g. glaucippe occurs throughout the Indian subcontinent.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Pieridae.

Phylogenetic notes. Hebomoia is an Asian genus related to Ixias and other orange-tipped whites, characterized by large size and prominent orange apical patches.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Common and widespread with stable populations.
