टिकेल्स ब्लू फ़्लाईकैचर / Tickell's Blue Flycatcher

Cyornis tickelliae (Blyth, 1843) — Muscicapidae

टिकेल्स ब्लू फ़्लाईकैचर — घने पेड़ों की छाया में बैठी नीली चिड़िया, जो हल्के-हल्के रंगों से चमकती है। उड़ते कीटों को धीरे-धीरे पकड़ती है और गीत जैसी मीठी आवाज़ करती है। जंगल की मध्यम ऊँचाई पर बसी इस चिड़िया को खोजना थोड़ा मुश्किल पर अगर देख लो तो कभी नहीं भूलोगे।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmall; 16–17 cm total length
Male plumageBrilliant blue above (forehead, crown, back); buff or peachy-orange underparts; blue on wings and tail
Female plumageDull blue-grey above; greenish-grey; buff underparts
BillSmall, pointed, black
LegsOrange-yellow
BehaviourPerches low to mid-storey; sallies for insects; active but stays in understory

Field tip: A small blue flycatcher of forested areas and woodland edges. Males are brilliant blue and buff; females duller. Prefers shaded forest undergrowth where light is dim. Often feeds by perching quietly then making quick sallies for insects. Song is melodious and distinctive.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें:
नर चमकदार नीला, पेट खुबानी रंग का। मादा हल्का नीला-हरा। पेड़ों की मध्य ऊँचाई पर रहता है। उड़ते कीटों को पकड़ता है। मीठी आवाज़।

Size: 16–17 cm long; small flycatcher.

Plumage (male): Brilliant cobalt-blue on crown, forehead, nape, and back. Wings and tail blue-black edged blue. Lores and eyebrow-stripe blue (unlike some congeners). Underparts peachy-buff or orange-buff on breast and belly; white on mid-belly and undertail-coverts.

Plumage (female): Dull blue-grey on crown, back, and wings; greenish tinge on lower back and rump. Underparts buff-white; throat whitish; breast buff.

Bill: Small, pointed, slightly hooked; black.

Legs and feet: Orange-yellow; conspicuous in field.

Eyes: Dark brown.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Song: Melodious series of clear, liquid notes.; resembles some warblers.
  2. Contact call: Soft tuc or whit notes between pair members.
  3. Alarm call: Harsh chit-chit when disturbed.
हिंदी: नर गहरा नीला और खुबानी रंग का। मादा हल्का नीला-हरा। टाँगें पीली। आवाज़ मीठी और संगीत जैसी।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Feeding ecology: Restless and acrobatic flycatcher; forages in low to mid-canopy. Makes short sallies from perches to catch insects in mid-air and gleans from foliage. Feeds on:

  • Small insects and flies
  • Small moths and butterflies
  • Tiny beetles and other small arthropods
  • Rarely takes small spiders

Habitat use: Prefers shaded forest interior and forest edges; avoids open country. Often found in sal forests and mixed deciduous forests. Stays in understory and mid-canopy layers.

Social behaviour: Generally solitary or in pairs. Some individuals may join mixed feeding flocks in non-breeding season.

Breeding: Monogamous and territorial; breeds in cavities in trees (often using old cavities).

Activity: Diurnal and crepuscular; more active during dawn and late afternoon.

हिंदी: जंगल की छाया में रहता है। अकेले या जोड़े में। हल्के-हल्के कीटों को हवा में पकड़ता है। पत्तों से भी कीड़े तोड़-तोड़ कर खाता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Breeding: April–July; single or double brood. Nests in tree cavities or crevices.

Nesting: Uses natural cavities or old woodpecker holes. Clutch 3–4 eggs; incubation 12–13 days; fledging 12–15 days.

Territorial behaviour: Males sing from high perches during breeding season to maintain and advertise territory.

Moult: Gradual moult in post-breeding season (June–September).

Activity pattern: Resident year-round; no seasonal migration.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Shikra, other small raptors
  • Mammalian predators: Palm squirrel (at nest), snakes
  • Nest predators: Snakes, monitors (rarely), corvids if cavity exposed
हिंदी: छोटी चिड़ियों की तरह इसे भी बड़ी चिड़ियों से खतरा है। घोंसले को साँपों से बचाना पड़ता है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected status: Tickell's Blue Flycatcher is protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, Schedule I. All wild birds listed are protected; hunting and trapping are prohibited.

Pest control: Beneficial in agricultural areas and gardens because it feeds on many small insects that may damage crops or ornamental plants.

Forest indicator: Presence indicates healthy forest structure with adequate cavities for nesting and understory vegetation for feeding.

Cultural significance: Its melodious song and brilliant blue plumage make it popular among birdwatchers and in local folklore.

हिंदी: यह पक्षी विधि अधिनियम 1972 की पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है। बागों और खेतों के कीटों को खा कर मदद करता है। इसकी मीठी आवाज़ लोगों को पसंद है।

GBIF holds published records from these districts. The species is expected in deciduous and mixed forests and forest edges throughout eastern Uttar Pradesh.

Habitat requirements: Forested areas with adequate understory vegetation and tree cavities for nesting. Requires shade and moderate tree cover.

Year-round presence: Resident; can be encountered year-round in suitable habitat.

Nearest published records: Ali & Ripley (1968) and subsequent surveys document presence in forested parts of eastern Uttar Pradesh.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across Indian subcontinent from sea level to about 2000 m. Resident throughout range.

In India: Distributed across peninsular and central India; northern limit in the Gangetic plains.

In Uttar Pradesh: Present in forested areas, particularly in eastern and central regions.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Resident in sal and mixed deciduous forests and forest edges. Less common in heavily cultivated areas; favours areas with intact tree cover.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Blyth in 1843 as Muscicapa tickelliae from peninsular India. Later placed in genus Cyornis.

Subspecies. Monotypic; C. t. tickelliae throughout the range.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Muscicapidae (Old World flycatchers). Genus Cyornis contains numerous blue flycatcher species across Asia.

Phylogenetic notes. Sister species to C. rubeculoides based on morphology and recent molecular studies; both part of the Asian blue flycatcher radiation.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC). Widely distributed in suitable habitat; population stable. Not significantly threatened by direct persecution or habitat loss, though forest degradation may impact local populations.

हिंदी: यह नीली बुलबुल की एक प्रजाति है। भारत में सबसे ज्यादा घनी जंगलों में पाई जाती है। विश्व भर में संख्या में स्थिर है।
