वीपिंग फिग / Weeping Fig

Ficus benjamina L. — Moraceae

वीपिंग फिग — एक विशाल, घनी छायादार सदाबहार अंजीर वृक्ष जिसकी लचीली टहनियाँ नीचे की ओर झूलती हैं। इसकी चिकनी, चमकदार हरी पत्तियाँ हवा में लहराती हैं और छोटे गोल अंजीर पकने पर नारंगी-लाल हो जाते हैं। यह पक्षियों और चमगादड़ों का प्रमुख भोजन स्थल है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitSpreading evergreen tree up to 30 m tall with slender drooping branchlets (Duthie 1915)
BarkSmooth, light grey, exuding copious sticky white milky latex when cut
LeavesOvate-elliptic, 5–12 cm long, glossy dark green with an abrupt slender drip tip (Duthie 1915)
VenationFine, very dense, parallel secondary veins joining an intramarginal loop
Syconia (Figs)Axillary, paired, sessile, globose, 8–12 mm across, ripening yellow to dark red (Duthie 1915)
Aerial RootsSlender aerial roots descending from horizontal branches, sometimes forming pillar boles

Field tip: Look for drooping branchlets with shiny oval leaves that possess a distinct tail-like drip tip and remarkably dense, fine parallel veins.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: झूलती हुई पतली टहनियाँ, चिकनी चमकदार पत्तियाँ जिनके सिरे पतली पूँछ जैसे नुकीले होते हैं, और जोड़ों में लगने वाले छोटे लाल अंजीर।

Morphology शारीरिक संरचना

Biometrics (Duthie 1915; Brandis 1906):

MeasurementRange
Tree height15–30 m (Duthie 1915)
Trunk diameter40–120 cm (Brandis 1906)
Petiole length1.0–2.5 cm (Duthie 1915)
Leaf blade length5–12 cm (Duthie 1915)
Leaf blade width2.5–6.0 cm (Duthie 1915)
Fig diameter8–12 mm (Duthie 1915)

Crown and branchlets: A massive, broad-spreading evergreen canopy supported by slender, pendulous branches. Slender aerial roots hang from larger boles and may root upon meeting moist soil.

Leaves: Alternate, simple, coriaceous, glabrous, shining above, ovate to broadly elliptic. The apex terminates in an acuminate drip-tip (Duthie 1915). The primary lateral veins are extraordinarily numerous and close together, merging into an intramarginal vein.

Inflorescence and Flowers: The flowers are minute and borne inside hollow receptacles (syconia). Syconia are axillary, paired, sessile, globose or slightly obpyriform, ripening from yellow to orange and deep reddish-purple.

हिंदी: पेड़ की शाखाएं नीचे लटकती हैं। पत्तियाँ चमकीली, चिकनी और घनी शिराओं वाली होती हैं। फल तने या टहनियों के जोड़ों में बिना डंठल के लगते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार एवं पारिस्थितिकी

Ficus benjamina begins life often as a hemiepiphyte, germinating in the fork of another tree where seeds are deposited by frugivores. Aerial roots gradually descend to envelop the host trunk, eventually establishing direct contact with the ground.

The dense weeping canopy provides perennial shade and microclimatic humidity in riparian corridors. Because individual trees produce syconia asynchronously throughout the year, the tree acts as a critical keystone resource during periods when seasonal fruits are unavailable.

हिंदी: यह अंजीर अन्य पेड़ों पर उगकर धीरे-धीरे अपनी जड़ों से उन्हें घेर लेता है। साल भर रुक-रुक कर फल देने के कारण यह पक्षियों के लिए अकाल का भोजन सिद्ध होता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र एवं ऋतुचक्र

PeriodPhenological Event
February–AprilNew foliage flushes with bright light green leaves during the onset of summer
May–JulySyconia develop and mature; peak mutualistic fig-wasp activity occurs
August–OctoberMonsoon fruiting provides abundant figs; seeds disperse widely via water and animals
November–JanuaryEvergreen maintenance with intermittent small flushes in warm microclimates

Fig wasps enter the receptive syconia through the tiny ostiole, fertilising the gall flowers while ovipositing, completing an obligate pollination mutualism.

हिंदी: बसंत ऋतु में नई कोपलें फूटती हैं। गर्मी और बरसात में अंजीर पकते हैं जिन्हें खाकर पक्षी दूर-दूर तक बीज बिखेरते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबद्ध प्रजातियाँ एवं पारस्परिक क्रियाएं

  • Pollinators: Obligate mutualist fig wasps (Eupristina verticillata) pollinate the internal flowers.
  • Frugivores: Ripening figs attract Indian Flying Fox, Common Myna, and House Crow, which feed extensively in the canopy.
  • Grove Companions: Often planted alongside Peepal and Banyan near shrines and estate gardens.
हिंदी: इसके अंजीर खाने के लिए कौए, मैना और चमगादड़ बड़ी संख्या में आते हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट एवं रोग

  • Thrips (Gynaikothrips uzeli) cause characteristic leaf-folding and curling on young foliage.
  • Fig stem borers (Batocera rufomaculata) attack stressed or aging branches.
  • Scale insects and mealybugs occasionally infest tender terminal shoots in humid weather.
हिंदी: कोमल पत्तियों पर थ्रिप्स और तने में छेदक कीट नुकसान पहुँचा सकते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि एवं मानवीय उपयोगिता

Traditional and Landscape Uses:

  • Ornamental and shade tree. Widely cultivated along roadsides, campus avenues, and parks for its graceful weeping foliage and dense shade.
  • Hedge and topiary. Capable of tolerating vigorous pruning, making it popular in garden culture.
  • Traditional medicine. The milky latex and aerial roots have documented applications in traditional formulations for cutaneous irritations and poultices in classical treatises (Kirtikar & Basu 1935). This is a record of traditional use, not medical advice.

Today. It remains one of the most widely propagated ficus species in subtropical horticulture, prized for its evergreen hardiness and air-filtering capabilities.

हिंदी: वीपिंग फिग को छायादार और सुंदर सजावटी पेड़ के रूप में बाग़ों और सड़कों के किनारे लगाया जाता है। इसके दूधिया रस का उपयोग पारंपरिक नुस्खों में किया जाता रहा है।

Distribution भौगोलिक वितरण

Global range: Native across South Asia, Southeast Asia, southern China, and northern Australia; widely cultivated throughout tropical and subtropical regions globally.

In India: Wild in the sub-Himalayan tract, northeast India, Andaman Islands, and Western Ghats; extensively planted in towns and groves across the northern plains (Brandis 1906).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from botanical gardens, avenues, and semi-natural forest pockets across central and eastern districts (Duthie 1915).

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as planted ornamental specimens in urban and institutional gardens and occasional riverine escapes.

हिंदी: भारत के जंगलों के अलावा यह बाग़-बगीचों और सड़कों पर सर्वत्र लगाया जाता है।

Field rule: Drooping slender branchlets with glossy, elliptic leaves having extremely close parallel veins and small paired sessile figs separate Ficus benjamina from all other local figs.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गिकी एवं वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1767 in Mantissa Plantarum, p. 129. The specific epithet benjamina is thought to derive from the Sanskrit banjan (banyan) or Arabic luban jawi via trade nomenclature.

Subspecies. Several horticultural cultivars exist; wild populations in Asia are traditionally treated under the nominotypical variety Ficus benjamina var. benjamina.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the family Moraceae within the order Rosales. It is classified in subgenus Urostigma, section Malvanthera, characterised by its hemiepiphytic habit and monoecious syconia.

Phylogenetic notes. Molecular phylogenies of Ficus place F. benjamina in a well-supported clade of Asian hemiepiphytic figs adapted to forest canopies.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN Red List due to its extremely vast native geographical distribution and ubiquitous worldwide cultivation.
