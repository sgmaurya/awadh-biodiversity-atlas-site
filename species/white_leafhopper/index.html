सफ़ेद फुदका / White Leafhopper

Cofana spectra (Distant, 1918) — Cicadellidae

सफ़ेद फुदका — धान और गेहूँ के पत्तों पर सफ़ेद बहुत छोटे कीड़े, पत्तों को पीला करके नुक़सान पहुँचाते हैं। पत्ते से रस चूसते हैं और वायरस फैलाते हैं।

Field tip: Look for tiny white insects on rice or wheat leaf blades, especially on the underside. Insects jump rapidly when disturbed. Damage appears as pale yellowing or stippling on older leaves.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Adult length2–3 mm
Nymph length0.5–2 mm

Adult: Tiny leafhopper, pale white to cream-coloured, with membranous clear wings folded roof-like over the back. Body elongate, slightly flattened. Eyes dark, prominent. Legs long, enabling quick jumping escape response. Sexual dimorphism minimal.

Nymph: Pale whitish, similar in form to adult but smaller and wingless. Body warty; appears slightly translucent.

हिंदी: बिल्कुल छोटा, सफ़ेद, तेज़ उड़ने वाला।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The White Leafhopper is a common phloem-feeder on rice and other grasses throughout tropical Asia. Nymphs and adults aggregate on leaf blades, particularly on the undersurface, and withdraw phloem sap continuously. This feeding causes localized chlorosis and yellowing of leaves. The species is also known as a vector of various plant viruses affecting rice and other grasses. Population dynamics are closely linked to rice crop calendars; populations build up during the monsoon months and remain high through the post-monsoon season.

हिंदी: धान के पत्तों पर हज़ारों की संख्या में बैठते हैं और रस चूसते हैं। वायरस फैलाते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Primary hosts:

  • Rice (Oryza sativa)
  • Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

Secondary hosts:

  • Maize (Zea mays)
  • Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)
  • Wild grasses

Highly polyphagous within grasses; prefers rice and wheat.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Parasitoid wasps: Anagrus and other parasites
  • Spiders: minute spiders; jumping spiders (occasional)
  • Coccinellid beetles: ladybirds consume nymphs and adults
  • Entomopathogenic fungi: Beauveria and Metarhizium in wet conditions
  • Predatory bugs: occasional predators

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

The White Leafhopper is a minor to moderate pest of rice and wheat in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Direct feeding damage is usually light unless populations are extremely high, but the insect's role as a virus vector is significant. Several viruses affecting rice production are vectored by this species (rice tungro virus, rice stripe virus). Management typically involves monitoring populations and, if necessary, cultural practices (removal of alternate hosts, avoiding excessive nitrogen fertilization which promotes leafhopper reproduction).

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Distant in 1918 in Annals and Magazine of Natural History as Kolla spectra based on specimens from South Asia. Transferred to Cofana by later revisers.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are formally recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Hemiptera (true bugs), family Cicadellidae (leafhoppers). The family comprises thousands of phloem-feeding species of varying economic importance.

Phylogenetic notes. Cofana spectra is part of a group of Asiatic leafhoppers specialised on grasses.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. As a widespread, abundant species, no conservation assessment is warranted.
