लॉन्ग-लेग्ड मार्श स्किमर / Long-legged Marsh Skimmer

Trithemis pallidinervis (Selys, 1873) — Libellulidae

लॉन्ग-लेग्ड मार्श स्किमर — एक छोटी-मध्यम आकार की व्याधपतंग जिसके नर का शरीर नीले-लाल रंग का होता है। इसका नाम इसकी लंबी पिंडलियों (long legs) से आता है, जो अन्य skimmers की तुलना में बहुत लंबी होती हैं। यह दलदलों और पानी के पौधों वाली जगहों में पाई जाती है।

Field tip: The conspicuously long legs distinguish this skimmer from others. Males show purple-blue or reddish coloration, while females are brown.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

Adult male: Head dark brown. Thorax predominantly purple-blue or reddish-purple with black stripes. Abdomen purple-blue or reddish with black bands. Legs noticeably long and dark. Wings transparent.

Adult female: Head brown. Thorax and abdomen brown to yellowish-brown with darker markings. Legs similarly elongated.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Trithemis pallidinervis is a medium-sized dragonfly of marshes, wetlands, and flooded grasslands. It frequents areas with emergent vegetation and perches on plants and open ground. Males are moderately territorial and pursue rivals and prey with moderate vigor. The long legs may provide advantages in catching prey or navigating through marshy vegetation.

Larval Stage

Naiads are robust, brownish, and found in shallow water among vegetation. Larval duration is approximately 10–12 weeks. Emergence occurs year-round with seasonal peaks.

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. Expected in marshes and flooded grasslands throughout eastern UP, year-round. Look for the purple-blue or reddish dragonfly with conspicuously long legs perching on marsh vegetation. A photograph showing the long legs and body colour, with date and location, would confirm a local record.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia; widespread from Pakistan and India to Myanmar, Thailand, and Indonesia.

In India: Common in plains and foothills.

In Uttar Pradesh: Scattered records.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in marshes; unconfirmed locally.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Selys Longchamps described Trithemis pallidinervis in 1873. Type locality: India. The genus Trithemis comes from Greek tri- (three) + thema (band), referring to dark bands on the wings of some species. The species name pallidinervis (Latin: pale-veined) refers to pale venation.

Subspecies. Monotypic.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae.

Phylogenetic notes. Trithemis is a diverse African-Asian genus of skimmers. T. pallidinervis is one of several Asian members.

IUCN status rationale. Listed as Least Concern (LC). Broad range and stable populations.
