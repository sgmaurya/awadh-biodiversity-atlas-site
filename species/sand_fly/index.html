बालू मक्खी / Sand Fly

Phlebotomus argentipes Annandale & Brunetti, 1900 — Psychodidae

बालू मक्खी — यह छोटी, हल्के गुलाबी-भूरे रंग की मक्खी है जो रात में काटती है और मुख्य रूप से घरों की दरारों और पशुशालाओं में रहती है। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश और बिहार में यह बहुत खतरनाक है क्योंकि यह "काला आज़ार" (विसेरल लीशमेनिएसिस) नामक भयानक बीमारी फैलाती है। यह बीमारी लीशमेनिया परजीवी से होती है, जो मक्खी के काटने से फैलता है। काला आज़ार से बुखार, वज़न घटना, और पेट बढ़ना होता है, और इलाज न मिले तो यह जानलेवा हो सकता है। इसीलिए इसकी रोकथाम में घर के अंदर और बाहर कीटनाशक का छिड़काव, बेड पर जाली लगा मच्छरदानी, और जानवरों को दवा से बचाव बहुत ज़रूरी है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeVery small; body length 2–3 mm (half the size of a mosquito)
ColourPale yellowish-brown or tan; slightly iridescent in certain light
WingsNarrow, pointed wings held erect and close together above the body like a tent or roof
MouthpartsProboscis (feeding tube) protrudes forward; used to pierce skin and draw blood
LegsRelatively long; slender; pale coloured
BehaviourNocturnal; most active just after dusk; rests in dark crevices during the day
SoundNearly silent flight; no audible buzz like mosquitoes

Field tip: A tiny sand fly is nearly impossible to see without a light, but its bite is sharp and itchy. In endemic areas, fine mesh nets on windows and bed nets are the telltale sign that sand flies are present. If you are bitten at night by an insect you cannot see, and the bite is on exposed areas like the face and hands, it is likely a sand fly.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बहुत छोटी मक्खी, हल्के रंग की, रात को काटती है। मच्छर से आधी। खिड़की पर बारीक जाली का संकेत है कि ये मक्खियाँ हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length2.0–3.0 mm
Wingspan5.0–6.5 mm

Structure: Tiny, fragile insect. Head is small, dominated by large compound eyes placed laterally. The proboscis is fine and pointed, used for penetrating skin and feeding on blood. Antennae are relatively long and filiform. Thorax is compact. Wings are narrow, elongate, and held erect over the body. The anterior wing margin has more densely spaced setae than the posterior margin. Legs are slender and relatively long.

Sexual dimorphism: Females are slightly larger. Males have clasping structures at the posterior end of the abdomen.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A nocturnal, anthropophilic (human-loving) insect confined to habitation. Females require a blood meal to produce eggs. They are attracted to body heat and carbon dioxide. Feeding occurs primarily between dusk and midnight. During the day, both males and females rest in dark crevices in walls, under furniture, in bedding, and in livestock shelters. They do not form swarms but are solitary or in small groups.

Males feed on plant nectar and do not take blood. Females lay 50–100 eggs in moist, dark habitats with decaying organic matter. Larvae are aquatic or semi-aquatic.

हिंदी: रात को ही काटती है, दिन में दरारों में सोती है। मादा को खून चाहिए अंडे के लिए, नर नहीं काटता।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

StageDuration

Generations: Multiple overlapping generations per year. Peak emergence coincides with warm, humid months.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Blood sources (females):

  • Human blood (preferred)
  • Animal blood (cattle, buffaloes, dogs)

Plant diet (males and females):

  • Nectar and plant juices

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Spiders: Web-spinning spiders capture flies.
  • Ants: Nocturnal foraging ants prey on eggs and larvae.
  • Fungi: Entomopathogenic fungi (Beauveria basiana) infect adults in humid conditions.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Kala-azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis). Phlebotomus argentipes is the exclusive vector of visceral leishmaniasis in South Asia. The disease is caused by the protozoan parasite Leishmania donovani, transmitted through the bite of infected females. Kala-azar is endemic in eastern Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, where it has been documented in multiple health surveys and epidemiological studies. The India Ministry of Health and Family Welfare 2023 guidelines cite kala-azar as a significant public health concern in these states, with transmission occurring year-round but peaking in warm, humid months. The disease presents as prolonged fever, weight loss, hepatosplenomegaly (enlarged liver and spleen), and anaemia. Untreated cases are fatal. Approximately 5–10 percent of persons infected with L. donovani develop a post-kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis (PKDL) months or years after treatment.

Prevention and control. The standard prevention measures are: (1) indoor residual spraying (IRS) with approved insecticides on walls, furniture, and animal shelters; (2) bed nets, preferably insecticide-treated nets; (3) fine-mesh screens on windows and doors; (4) early diagnosis and treatment of infected persons with liposomal amphotericin B or paramomycin, both of which reduce transmission; (5) environmental sanitation to reduce larval habitats (moist organic matter in cracks and crevices); (6) awareness and health education.

Chemical spraying must be carried out by trained public health workers, as resistance to some insecticide classes has been documented.

Research direction. Vector-control strategies using insecticide-treated bed nets, targeted indoor spraying, and building sealed housing are being evaluated. Vaccination research is in preclinical stages.

हिंदी: काला आज़ार के लिए रोकथाम: (१) घर की दीवारों और खिड़कियों पर कीटनाशक छिड़काव, (२) मच्छरदानी पर जाली, (३) घर को बंद रखना, (४) संक्रमित को दवा देना। पशु भी संक्रमित हो सकते हैं।

Expected in villages and towns of Basti district wherever human habitation exists, particularly in areas with poor housing and gaps in vector control. The presence of fine-mesh window screens and insecticide-treated nets in households is evidence of active transmission. Entomological surveys by health workers occasionally capture specimens using CDC light traps. A record would be a specimen collected with place and date, preferably by a trained vector surveillance worker.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South Asia: India, Bangladesh, and Nepal. Absent from Southeast Asia and Africa, where other Phlebotomus species transmit kala-azar.

In India: Endemic in eastern states, particularly Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, and parts of Jharkhand.

In Uttar Pradesh: Present in eastern districts; kala-azar endemic in Basti, Gonda, Ballia, and adjacent districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Endemic resident; vector control measures are required.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  1. What is the abundance of sand flies in houses with fine-mesh window screens compared to houses without screens? / जाली लगे घरों में बालू मक्खियाँ कम होती हैं या ज़्यादा? — Collect flies using CDC light traps before and after screening.
  2. Do insecticide-treated bed nets reduce sand fly bites and kala-azar transmission in a village? — Compare infection rates between net-using and non-using households.
  3. What is the parasitism rate of Leishmania in field-collected Phlebotomus argentipes? — Molecular detection of L. donovani DNA in wild-caught flies.
  4. How many blood meals does a female sand fly take before laying eggs? — Observed in the laboratory or by examining dissected wild females.
  5. Are domestic animals (cattle, buffaloes, dogs) a reservoir for Leishmania donovani in the endemic area? / क्या यहाँ के पशु भी काला आज़ार के परजीवी से संक्रमित होते हैं? — Test sera and tissues of domestic animals for L. donovani.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Annandale and Brunetti described the sand fly as Phlebotomus argentipes in 1900 in the Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal (Vol. 69, p. 165). The type locality was Raxaul, on the Nepal–India border in Bihar. The genus Phlebotomus is from Greek phlebos (blood) and tomos (cut), referring to blood feeding. The species name argentipes refers to the silver-coloured feet.

Subspecies. Monotypic. However, cryptic genetic variation has been documented across its endemic range.

Family and order placement. Order Diptera, family Psychodidae (moth flies and sand flies), subfamily Phlebotominae.

Phylogenetic notes. Phlebotomus argentipes is the only sand fly vector of kala-azar in South Asia, making it of major public health significance.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. An anthropophilic vector; conservation status is not applicable.
