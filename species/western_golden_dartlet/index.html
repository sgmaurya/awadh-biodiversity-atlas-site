वेस्टर्न गोल्डन डार्टलेट व्याध पतंग / Western Golden Dartlet

Ischnura rubilio Selys, 1876 — Coenagrionidae

वेस्टर्न गोल्डन डार्टलेट व्याध पतंग — धान के खेतों, दलदली घासों और पानी भरी नालियों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, सुनहरी-पीली और नीली डैमसेल्फ़्लाई है। इसके नर का सीना चमकीला हरा और काला होता है, पेट का आगे का हिस्सा सुनहरा-पीला या नारंगी होता है और पूँछ पर चमकदार आसमानी नीला धब्बा होता है। यह घासों के बीच बहुत धीरे उड़ती है और फसल के हानिकारक कीटों का शिकार करती है।

Field tip: Look low among grassy borders of rice paddies, waterlogged drains, and marshy puddles. The miniature size paired with a brilliant golden-yellow abdomen capped at the rear by a bright sky-blue patch on segment 8 makes the male easily recognized in the field.

हिंदी: धान के खेतों में उड़ने वाली इस छोटी डैमसेल्फ़्लाई का पेट सुनहरा-पीला होता है और पूँछ के अंत में चमकीला नीला निशान होता है।

Adult description:

Ischnura rubilio is a delicate, slender-bodied damselfly displaying distinctive golden-yellow and blue coloration (Fraser 1933). The head has a velvety black vertex bearing tiny, circular, pale green or bluish postocular spots; the frons and clypeus are pale green (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is bronzy-black on the dorsum, bisected by fine green antehumeral stripes, while the lateral sides are bright apple-green fading to pale yellowish-white beneath (Fraser 1933).

The male abdomen is slender and cylindrical: segments 1 through 6 are brilliant golden-yellow or deep orange-yellow, narrowly ringed with black at the intersegmental joints; segment 7 is dark brownish-black above; segment 8 is entirely bright azure-blue; and segments 9 and 10 are velvety black dorsally with blue lateral markings (Subramanian 2005). The anal appendages are small, pale yellow, and conical (Fraser 1933).

Females are polymorphic, occurring in an andromorphic (male-coloured) phase and a common heteromorphic phase (Fraser 1933). Heteromorphic females have an olivaceous or dull orange-brown thorax with black markings, and the abdomen is dull yellow overlaid by a continuous, broad bronze-black dorsal stripe running from segment 1 to 10 (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the wings are narrow, petiolate, and transparent, with bi-coloured forewing pterostigmata in males (Fraser 1933).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is tiny, fragile, and pale translucent green or light yellowish-brown, camouflaged among flooded grass roots and algal mats. The caudal gills are elongate, lanceolate, and adorned with delicate marginal setae that assist in cutaneous respiration in warm, shallow water.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 20 से 23 मिमी लंबा होता है। इसका पेट आगे से सुनहरा-पीला और पीछे से नीला होता है। मादा हल्के भूरे और हरे रंग की होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Western Golden Dartlet is a sun-loving, low-flying inhabitant of shallow, grassy, and open freshwaters (Subramanian 2005). It thrives in agricultural landscapes, frequenting weed-filled field drains, waterlogged borrow pits, and grassy edges of rice fields (Fraser 1933). Its flight is low, cautious, and fluttering, rarely ascending more than ten to twenty centimetres above surrounding grass blades.

Males perch prominently on horizontal grass blades or sedge culms, defending tiny mating territories spanning less than half a metre of shoreline (Subramanian 2005). They regularly flick their wings when neighbouring males approach. Copulation occurs in the tandem wheel position perched among dense grasses. Oviposition is performed by the female unaccompanied by the male, inserting eggs into submerged grassy vegetation.

हिंदी: यह डैमसेल्फ़्लाई धूप में सक्रिय रहती है और घासों के ऊपर बहुत नीचे उड़ती है। नर घास की पत्तियों पर बैठकर अपने छोटे से क्षेत्र की रखवाली करता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Females insert elongate eggs into the soft tissue of submerged aquatic grasses, sedges, and plants like Ipomoea aquatica (Water Spinach). The eggs hatch after a few days into tiny aquatic nymphs. Naiads develop rapidly through several instars over a few weeks, an adaptation to life in temporary monsoon pools that dry up within months. Multiple overlapping generations are produced throughout the active warm season.

हिंदी: मादा पानी में डूबी घासों में अंडे देती है जो कुछ दिनों में फूटते हैं। इसका निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में तेजी से बड़ा होकर वयस्क बन जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Ischnura rubilio is an insectivorous predator throughout its aquatic and adult existence (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small disease-transmitting mosquitoes, such as Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Anopheles culicifacies (Malaria Mosquito).
  • Minute agricultural sap-sucking pests of rice, including Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper).
  • Micro-dipterans, including midges (Chironomidae) and fruit flies along field margins.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito larvae and pupae, ostracods, copepods, and tiny aquatic oligochaetes in shallow water.
हिंदी: यह छोटी डैमसेल्फ़्लाई धान के फुदकों और मच्छरों को खाकर किसानों की मदद करती है। इसका जलीय निम्फ मच्छरों के लार्वा को नष्ट करता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented preying upon Ischnura rubilio in Gangetic plain agroecosystems:

  • Spiders: Grass-dwelling orb-weavers and wolf spiders (Lycosa) stalk resting adults on paddy bunds.
  • Predatory insects: Larger dragonflies like Crocothemis servilia (Scarlet Skimmer) and Orthetrum sabina (Slender Skimmer) ambush flying adults.
  • Amphibians: Small juvenile frogs like Euphlyctis cyanophlyctis (Skittering Frog) catch adults perched low over water.
  • Fish: Surface-feeding freshwater fish readily devour nymphs in shallow pools.
हिंदी: छोटी मकड़ियाँ, बड़े व्याध पतंग और मेंढक इस नन्ही डैमसेल्फ़्लाई को अपना शिकार बनाते हैं। मछलियाँ पानी में इसके निम्फ को खाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Beneficial insect in paddy ecosystems: The Western Golden Dartlet is a valuable beneficial predator in rural rice paddies of northern India (Subramanian 2005). Because its population explodes during the monsoon kharif season, it feeds voraciously on nymphs and adults of the brown planthopper (Nilaparvata lugens Brown Planthopper) and the green leafhopper (Nephotettix virescens Green Leafhopper), helping suppress viral vector outbreaks without synthetic chemical insecticides.

Mosquito control: Both adults and aquatic naiads contribute to natural suppression of vector mosquitoes (Culex and Anopheles) in stagnant rural ditches.

Traditional: No traditional uses are known.

Today: Recognized by agricultural entomologists as a key beneficial bio-control agent in lowland rice farming.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing field predation efficiency on planthopper swarms under integrated pest management regimes.

हिंदी: धान के खेतों में यह एक अत्यंत लाभकारी कीट है। यह फसल को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले कीटों और बीमारी फैलाने वाले मच्छरों का प्राकृतिक रूप से सफाया करता है।

The Western Golden Dartlet is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in shallow flooded ditches, grassy margins of rice fields, and temporary roadside pools. Look carefully among low grass blades in full morning sunshine from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph with date and location, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Confined to South Asia, occurring widely across India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Fraser 1933).

In India: Found across the northwestern dry plains, Rajasthan, Punjab, the Gangetic floodplains, central India, and eastward to West Bengal and Bihar (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from irrigation canals, agricultural wetlands, and floodplains across western, central, and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a widespread resident breeding damselfly in rural paddy fields, borrow pits, and seasonal drainage depressions.

हिंदी: यह प्रजाति भारत और पड़ोसी देशों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के मैदानी इलाकों में धान के खेतों और पानी भरी नालियों में यह आसानी से मिल सकती है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Edmond de Selys-Longchamps in 1876 (originally under the name Ischnura aurora rubilio) in Bulletin de l'Académie royale des Sciences, des Lettres et des Beaux-Arts de Belgique, (2) 41, page 287. Type locality: "Inde" (India).

Subspecies. Monotypic — historically treated as a subspecies of Ischnura aurora (Brauer, 1865), but modern taxonomic revisions elevate Ischnura rubilio to full species status for populations across the Indian subcontinent.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Zygoptera, family Coenagrionidae. The genus Ischnura Charpentier, 1840, comprises the forktail damselflies, renowned for their wide distributions and female polychromatism.

Phylogenetic notes. Morphological and molecular studies distinguish continental Asian Ischnura rubilio from Australasian and Pacific Ischnura aurora based on male caudal appendage structure and penile morphology (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure South Asian endemic that flourishes in heavily modified agricultural landscapes.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Ischnura rubilio है (पहले इसे Ischnura aurora rubilio कहा जाता था)। यह Coenagrionidae परिवार की सदस्य है और प्रकृति में सुरक्षित है।
