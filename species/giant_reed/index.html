नल / Giant Reed

Arundo donax L. — Poaceae

नल — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश की नदियों और तालाबों के किनारों की सबसे आम लंबी घास, जो 3-4 मीटर तक बढ़ती है। इसकी मजबूत डंडियों को घरों की छत बनाने, पशु बाड़े की बेड़ी बनाने, और टोकरियों व डलियों को बुनने में लगाया जाता है। नदी के किनारे की मिट्टी को बाँधकर बाढ़ में कटाव रोकती है। गाँवों में इसके पत्तों को पशुओं के लिए चारे में मिलाया जाता है।

Field tip: Look for a tall reed-like grass taller than a man, with thick woody stems, long grey-green leaves, and a large feathery plume in late monsoon, growing in dense stands along riverbanks and canal margins.

Rhizomes and roots: Extensive rhizomes that spread horizontally; secondary roots branch from each node. The rhizome system is so robust that it is nearly impossible to eradicate once established.

Stems (culms): Stout, woody, hollow between the prominent nodes, with many branches from upper nodes. The stem is greenish-yellow, becoming brown with age.

Leaves: Alternate, lanceolate, 30–60 cm long, 2–4 cm wide, with prominent parallel veins and a central keel. Leaf sheaths are clasping. Margins are slightly rough (serrate).

Inflorescence: A large, dense, plume-like panicle, 30–60 cm long, composed of many small spikelets. The panicle is initially greenish, becoming purplish or golden at maturity; highly ornamental.

Flowers and reproduction: Primarily vegetative reproduction via rhizome fragments. Sexual reproduction by seed is rare and often unsuccessful due to pollen sterility in cultivated plants.

हिंदी: डंडी मजबूत, लकड़ी जैसी, खोखली। पत्ता लंबा, भूरा-हरा। फूल — सोने जैसा बड़ा पंख।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Arundo donax is a highly invasive perennial grass, native to the Mediterranean and southwestern Asia, but now naturalized along waterways worldwide. It thrives in riparian zones, wetlands, and disturbed areas. The extensive rhizome network binds soil and reduces erosion on riverbanks. It is drought-tolerant once established and can tolerate saline soils. The plant grows rapidly during the monsoon and in late summer.

The dense stands create a monotypic vegetation that shades out other riparian plants. However, traditional riparian management in eastern UP has long incorporated naal as a useful component of the landscape.

हिंदी: नदी के किनारे की मिट्टी को बाँधती है। बाढ़ में कटाव रोकती है। सूखा सहनशील। तेजी से बढ़ती है। गहरी जड़ें।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Riparian birds: Reedy areas provide nesting and foraging habitat for weavers and other grassland birds.
  • Pollinators: Wind-pollinated; some insects visit flowers.
  • Competitors: In dense stands, it excludes many other wetland plants.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf spots in humid seasons (minor).
  • Generally resistant to major pests due to robust growth.
  • No significant diseases reported from eastern UP.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional uses:

  • Building and roofing: The stout, woody stems are cut and dried and used to frame houses and make roof thatch (chhanai or ghasa). In traditional construction, bundles of reed are lashed together to form walls and roofing material.
  • Basketry and weaving: Finer stems are split and used to weave baskets (tokri, daliyā), fans, and other craft items.
  • Fodder: Leaves and tender shoots are fed to cattle and buffalo, though the nutritional value is moderate.
  • Erosion control: Deliberately planted on riverbanks and canal margins to prevent soil erosion and hold embankments.

Today: Uses are largely traditional in rural areas. Modern construction materials have displaced reed for roofing, but it remains important in villages.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Arundo is being studied globally for biomass energy generation and biofuel production.

हिंदी: पारंपरिक रूप से घरों की छत बनाने में, टोकरियाँ बुनने में, और पशु बाड़ों के लिए उपयोग होता है। पशु चारा भी है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Giant Reed is to be expected along the banks of the Ghaghra and Rapti rivers and their associated irrigation canals throughout Basti and Ayodhya districts, forming dense stands in wet areas and on embankments. GBIF holds published records from these districts. The plant is most visible during the monsoon and late summer (August–November) when the plume-like inflorescence is visible. A first confirmed local record would be a photograph with place and date.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the Mediterranean and southwestern Asia; now naturalized in warm and temperate regions worldwide.

In India: Widespread along rivers and wetlands in most regions with summer rainfall.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common along all major rivers and their associated waterways.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Very common along the Ghaghra, Rapti, and irrigation canals; forms dense riparian stands.

Field rule: A tall reed (2–4 m) with thick woody hollow stems, long grey-green leaves, and a large feathery golden plume in late summer is Arundo donax.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Carl Linnaeus described this species as Arundo donax in 1753 in Species Plantarum. The genus name Arundo comes from the Latin for reed, and donax is a classical name for a cane or reed used for musical instruments.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Poales, family Poaceae (grass family), subfamily Arundinoideae.

Phylogenetic notes. Arundo is a basal genus in the Poaceae with only one widely cultivated species.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Globally widespread and locally invasive; does not meet threatened criteria.
