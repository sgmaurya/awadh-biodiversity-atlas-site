ऐशी स्किमर व्याध पतंग / Ashy Skimmer

Orthetrum taeniolatum (Schneider, 1845) — Libellulidae

ऐशी स्किमर व्याध पतंग — सूखी रेतीली नदियों, पथरीले नालों और धूप से तपी पगडंडियों पर पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी और बहुत फुर्तीली धूसर-नीली व्याध पतंग (dragonfly) है। इसके वयस्क नर का सीना और पेट हल्के राख जैसे नीले-सफेद पाउडर (pruinescence) से ढका होता है। यह अक्सर जमीन पर पड़े पत्थरों, रेत या सूखी मिट्टी पर चपटी होकर बैठती है और पास से गुजरने वाले छोटे कीड़ों पर झपटती है।

Field tip: Look along dry canal embankments, sunny sandy riverbeds, and unpaved rural tracks near water. A small dragonfly perched flat against bare, sun-warmed sand or rock, coated in pale powdery ashy-blue dust and darting out to snatch flies, is the Ashy Skimmer.

हिंदी: सूखी पगडंडियों और रेत पर चपटी होकर बैठने वाली इस छोटी पतंग का शरीर राख जैसे नीले पाउडर से ढका होता है। यह पौधों के बजाय पत्थरों और रेत पर बैठना पसंद करती है।

Adult description:

Orthetrum taeniolatum is the smallest member of the genus Orthetrum occurring in the Indian subcontinent (Fraser 1936). The head has a pale olivaceous or yellowish-white face; the vesicle and frons are pale blue-grey in mature males, and the compound eyes are pale bluish-grey above and whitish-green beneath (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax in young individuals is pale yellowish-brown with dark brown lateral stripes, but in mature males it becomes completely enveloped in a dense layer of pale powdery ashy-blue pruinescence (Fraser 1936).

The male abdomen is slender, tapering evenly from the base to the apex: in mature adults, segments 1 through 8 are coated in uniform powdery ashy-blue pruinescence; segments 9 and 10 often remain partly blackish, bearing short, pointed, black anal appendages (Subramanian 2005).

Females never develop blue pruinescence (Fraser 1936). Their ground colour is warm isabelline-yellow or pale sandy-brown, with narrow black antehumeral stripes on the thorax and fine black lateral carinae on the abdomen (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the wings are completely hyaline without basal amber patches, and the pterostigma is long, narrow, and bright ochreous-yellow framed in dark brown (Fraser 1936).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is small, squat, and pale sandy-brown or greyish, living shallowly buried in coarse sand and fine gravel of slow-flowing streams and seepage pools. The body is covered in short stiff hairs that trap fine sand grains, rendering the nymph invisible to predatory fish.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 21 से 24 मिमी लंबा होता है और उस पर राख जैसा नीला पाउडर चढ़ा होता है। मादा रेतीले-भूरे रंग की होती है जिस पर बारीक काली रेखाएं होती हैं। इसका निम्फ रेत में छिपा रहता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Ashy Skimmer is a highly heat-tolerant, ground-dwelling dragonfly of open, sun-baked environments (Subramanian 2005). It inhabits sandy riverbeds, gravel bars, dry canal margins, stone quarries, and sunlit paths near agricultural drains (Fraser 1936). Unlike marsh-dwelling skimmers that perch on emergent weeds, Orthetrum taeniolatum perches almost exclusively on bare mineral substrates—such as sun-warmed rocks, dry sand, or baked mud.

Adults are active during the hottest hours of the day, thriving in intense solar radiation (Subramanian 2005). Males establish small territories on exposed rocks or patches of sand, darting up to capture passing flies or drive away rival males before returning immediately to the exact same perching spot (Fraser 1936). When perching, they hold their wings pressed flat against the rock surface to absorb warmth during cool mornings or raise their abdomen to minimize sun exposure at midday. Mating takes place on the ground. Oviposition is exophytic: the female hovers low over shallow, sandy-bottomed seepage pools, rapidly dipping the tip of her abdomen into the water to release eggs into the sand.

हिंदी: यह पतंग तेज गर्मी और कड़ी धूप को बहुत आसानी से सहन कर लेती है। यह पत्थरों और सूखी जमीन पर बैठकर अपने इलाके की निगरानी करती है और वहीं धूप सेकती है।

Females hover over shallow sandy shallows and dip their abdomen into the surface film, releasing eggs directly into the substrate. The eggs hatch after a few days into tiny, sand-dwelling nymphs. Naiads develop over several weeks through multiple instars, feeding on benthic midge larvae and aquatic worms in warm shallow water. At maturity, the nymph crawls onto a shoreline rock at dawn to shed its skin and emerge as an adult dragonfly.

हिंदी: मादा रेत वाले उथले पानी में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडे फूटते हैं और रेत में रहने वाला निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में बड़ा होकर पत्थर पर चढ़कर पतंग बन जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Orthetrum taeniolatum is an active predatory carnivore of ground-level and flying insects (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small dipteran flies basking on warm sand and stones, including house flies (Musca domestica House Fly) and eye flies (Siphunculina funicola Eye Fly).
  • Disease-transmitting mosquitoes, such as Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Aedes aegypti (Yellow Fever Mosquito).
  • Small agricultural leafhoppers and midges (Chironomidae) active along field margins.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito wrigglers, midge larvae, small aquatic beetles, and oligochaete worms in sand beds.
हिंदी: यह पतंग पत्थरों और जमीन पर बैठने वाली मक्खियों, छोटे मच्छरों और फुदकों का शिकार करती है। इसका निम्फ पानी की रेत में रहने वाले कीड़ों को खाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented in Gangetic plain freshwater ecosystems:

  • Lizards: Garden lizards (Calotes versicolor) and agamid lizards stalk perching adults on stones and sandbars.
  • Predatory birds: Ground-foraging insectivorous birds, such as wagtails, larks, and bee-eaters, capture adults on open tracks.
  • Predatory insects: Larger robber flies (Asilidae) ambush adults perching on exposed rocks.
  • Fish: Small stream fishes feed on developing naiads in gravel shallows.
हिंदी: गिरगिट, पत्थरों पर घूमने वाले पक्षी और शिकारी मक्खियाँ इस पतंग को पकड़ते हैं। पानी के अंदर मछलियाँ इसके निम्फ को खाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Biological control of rural nuisance flies: The Ashy Skimmer provides effective natural suppression of filth flies and biting insects around rural agricultural settlements (Subramanian 2005). Because it hunts from warm, bare ground near village cattle sheds, dirt tracks, and irrigation drains, it captures significant numbers of nuisance flies like Musca domestica (House Fly) and vector mosquitoes.

Pest hopper predation: Adults foraging along field borders capture leafhoppers and planthoppers dispersing between irrigated crop fields (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal, economic, or cultural uses are recorded.

Today: Valued by naturalists as an indicator of open, sandy, and rocky freshwater ecosystems.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Investigating the solar reflectance and thermal tolerance properties of ashy pruinescence under extreme summer surface heat.

हिंदी: यह पतंग गाँव के रास्तों और मवेशियों के बाड़ों के पास बैठने वाली मक्खियों और मच्छरों को खाकर ग्रामीणों को राहत पहुँचाती है।

The Ashy Skimmer is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh on sandy riverbanks, gravelly canal embankments, and sunny dirt tracks near watercourses. Look flat on bare, sun-baked stones or dry sand during warm midday hours from March through June and again from September through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph showing the powdery ashy-blue body resting on sand, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across the southern Palaearctic and Oriental realms, occurring from Greece, Turkey, and the Middle East eastward through Iran, Pakistan, India, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Fraser 1936).

In India: Recorded across the northwestern dry plains, Rajasthan, Punjab, the lower Himalayas, central India, and eastward across the Gangetic floodplains to West Bengal (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from river floodplains, canal systems, and semi-arid wastelands throughout western, central, and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident breeding dragonfly along the sandy shores of the Kuwano and Ghaghara rivers and dry canal embankments.

हिंदी: यह मध्य-पूर्व से लेकर भारत और बांग्लादेश तक पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में नदियों के रेतीले किनारों और सूखी नहरों पर यह सामान्य रूप से मौजूद है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Wilhelm Gottlieb Schneider in 1845 as Libellula taeniolata in Stettiner Entomologische Zeitung, 6, page 111. Type locality: "Rhodos" (Rhodes, Greece).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its extensive southern Palaearctic and Oriental range.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae. The genus Orthetrum Newman, 1833, comprises the skimmers, characterized by complex wing venation and male pruinescence.

Phylogenetic notes. Orthetrum taeniolatum is the smallest member of the genus in Asia, distinguished from O. glaucum and O. luzonicum by its compact size, clear wings, and ground-perching ecological niche (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant, secure, and drought-hardy species widely distributed across dry and alluvial regions.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Orthetrum taeniolatum है और यह Libellulidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका अस्तित्व पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
