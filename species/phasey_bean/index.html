फेसी बीन / Phasey Bean

Macroptilium lathyroides (L.) Urb. — Fabaceae

फेसी बीन — यह सड़क के किनारों, नहर की पटरियों और परती जमीन पर उगने वाला एक सीधा या थोड़ा फैलने वाला जंगली दलहनी पौधा है। इसकी पत्तियाँ तीन-तीन के समूह में होती हैं। इसके फूल गहरे लाल, मैरून या काले-बैंगनी रंग के होते हैं जो लंबी डंडियों पर खिलते हैं। इसकी फलियां बहुत लंबी, पतली और गोल होती हैं जो पकने पर ऐंठकर खुलती हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

An erect or sprawling herbaceous legume with a woody taproot. Taproot well developed, bearing active nitrogen-fixing nodules. Stems erect, branching from near the base, sometimes twining lightly in tall vegetation, cylindrical, striate, densely appressed-pubescent with reflexed white hairs when young, becoming subglabrous with age.

Leaves alternate, pinnately trifoliolate; petiole slender, puberulous; stipules lanceolate, striate, persistent; leaflets oblong-lanceolate, ovate or elliptic, acute or obtuse at the apex, cuneate at the base, entire, dark green and glabrous above, paler and appressed-pubescent beneath.

Inflorescence a long, stiff, erect terminal raceme held well above the leafy canopy, bearing flowers in pairs along the upper axis. Calyx tubular-campanulate, five-lobed, appressed-hairy. Corolla papilionaceous, conspicuous, dark maroon-red, reddish-purple or chocolate-brown; standard petal orbicular, reflexed; wing petals large, obovate, spreading, much darker than the other petals; keel petal incurved, spirally twisted at the tip.

Fruit a linear, cylindrical, straight or slightly curved pod (legume), appressed-pubescent when young, becoming glabrescent, dehiscing elastically by two spirally twisted valves. Seeds numerous, oblong-cylindrical, mottled brown or black, smooth.

हिंदी: पौधा सीधा बढ़ता है। तीन पत्तियों का समूह होता है। फूल गहरे लाल-बैंगनी रंग के होते हैं। फलियां पतली और सीधी होती हैं जो पकने पर मुड़कर चटकती हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Native to the Neotropics (tropical America and the Caribbean), now widely naturalized across the tropical and subtropical regions of India. It thrives in open, sun-exposed sites with sandy or gravelly loam, often colonizing railway verges, canal embankments, and waste grounds.

The plant forms a symbiotic relationship with soil bacteria to fix atmospheric nitrogen, allowing it to flourish in nutrient-poor soils. The dark maroon flowers possess a specialised tripping mechanism tailored for larger, heavy-bodied bees. When ripe, the dry pods twist open explosively on hot afternoons, catapulting the hard seeds several metres from the parent plant.

हिंदी: यह पौधा धूप वाली खुली जगहों और सूखी जमीन पर आसानी से उगता है। पकने पर इसकी फलियां आवाज के साथ चटककर बीजों को दूर फेंकती हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seeds germinate with the first substantial rains of the southwest monsoon in June and July. Vegetative development is vigorous during July and August, producing tall flowering shoots.

Flowering begins in August and continues throughout the post-monsoon period until November. Pods mature successively along the raceme, releasing seeds from September through December. With the onset of winter chill and drought in December, the plants wither and senesce, surviving as fallen hard seeds that persist in the soil until the next rainy season.

हिंदी: जून-जुलाई की बारिश में बीज अंकुरित होते हैं। अगस्त से नवंबर के बीच फूल और फलियां आती हैं। सर्दियों में पौधा सूख जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

The showy flowers are pollinated primarily by large bees, particularly carpenter bees (Xylocopa) and leafcutter bees (Megachile), which have the strength to depress the wing petals and expose the reproductive organs.

Often found growing alongside native and naturalized legumes and weeds such as dhaincha, rattleweed, and sarphonka. Caterpillars of polyphagous lycaenid butterflies occasionally feed on the flower buds.

हिंदी: इसके फूलों पर बड़ी भंवरी और जंगली मधुमक्खियाँ परागण के लिए आती हैं। यह अक्सर ढैंचा और अन्य जंगली मटर प्रजातियों के साथ दिखता है।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Pod borer caterpillars sometimes bore into developing green pods. Bean rust and leaf-spot fungi can affect foliage during prolonged humid periods in September.

हिंदी: कभी-कभी फलियों में छेद करने वाले कीड़े लग जाते हैं और पत्तियों पर फफूंद के धब्बे पड़ते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

In eastern Uttar Pradesh, phasey bean occurs mostly as a ruderal weed of roadsides, uncultivated fields, and waste areas.

हिंदी: यह सड़कों और बंजर जमीन पर उगने वाला खरपतवार है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical America and the West Indies; widely naturalized across the Old World tropics, including Africa, Asia, and northern Australia.

In India: Naturalized throughout peninsular India, the Gangetic plain, and the central Indian scrub belts.

In Uttar Pradesh: Distributed along roadsides, railway tracks, and open waste spaces across the central and eastern plain.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected along transport embankments, canal verges, and open scrub wastelands.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Phaseolus lathyroides by Carl Linnaeus in 1763 in Species Plantarum, edition 2, volume 2, page 1018. Transferred to Macroptilium by Ignatz Urban in 1928 in Symbolae Antillanae, volume 9, page 457.

Subspecies. Often treated in older Indian floras under the synonym Phaseolus semierectus L. (Hooker 1876). Monotypic in our region.

Family and order placement. Placed in family Fabaceae (subfamily Faboideae, tribe Phaseoleae), order Fabales.

Phylogenetic notes. Separated from Phaseolus on the basis of its laterally twisted keel and distinctive floral structure.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN Red List. It is a widely naturalized and adaptable tropical species with no known conservation threats.

हिंदी: यह मटर कुल (फैबेसी) का पौधा है। लिनियस के 1763 के वर्णन के बाद अर्बन ने 1928 में इसे मैक्रोप्टिलियम में रखा था।
