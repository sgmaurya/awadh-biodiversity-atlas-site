फूलचुही / Pale-billed Flowerpecker

Dicaeum erythrorhynchos (Latham, 1790) — Dicaeidae

फूलचुही — सबसे छोटी चिड़ियों में से एक, जो फूलों पर मँडराती है और शहद पीती है। इसका रंग अक्सर हरा-पीला होता है, पर फूलों के साथ भी रंग बदल सकता है। फलों की झाड़ियों पर भी दिखती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmallest bird in region; 9–10 cm total length
PlumageOlive-green above; yellow-green underparts; slight sexual dichromatism
MalesBright crimson or orange-red on rump and under tail-coverts
FemalesDuller; greenish-yellow
BillSmall, pointed, pale (giving name); slightly downturned
TailShort, squared
EyesDark

Field tip: Tiniest flowerpecker; constantly on the move. Look for it flitting around flowers and fruiting plants. High-pitched calls and rapid movements make it noticeable despite small size. Solitary or in pairs; restless and energetic.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें:
सबसे छोटी चिड़िया। हरे-पीले रंग की। नर के नीचे लाल रंग। बहुत तेज़ी से फूलों पर मँडराती है। पीली चोंच।

Morphology आकारिकी

Size: 9–10 cm long; tiniest bird in region; weight only 6–8 g.

Plumage: Olive-green above; yellowish-green underparts. Males show bright crimson or orange-red on rump and under tail-coverts (variable intensity). Females duller; lack bright red colouration.

Bill: Small, pointed, pale yellowish (diagnostic); slightly downturned, adapted for nectar feeding.

Tail: Short, squared or slightly graduated.

Legs: Very short; dark.

Eyes: Dark brown; relatively large for body size.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Contact call: High-pitched, thin tseep or chip notes.
  2. Song: Rapid series of high-pitched chips; simple and repetitive.
  3. Alarm: Sharp chit or tit notes.
हिंदी: ऊपर हरा, नीचे पीला-हरा। नर के नीचे लाल। बहुत छोटी। चोंच पीली और नुकीली। आवाज़ तीक्ष्ण और उच्च।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Foraging ecology: Mainly a bird of fruit and mistletoe berries (Loranthaceae), taking nectar occasionally (Grimmett et al. 2011). Also takes small insects and spiders, particularly when feeding young. Feeds on:

  • Nectar from flowers (primary)
  • Small insects and spiders
  • Small fruits and berry pulp
  • Mistletoe berries (Loranthaceae)

Feeding behaviour: Highly active and restless; hovers briefly at flowers before moving to next bloom. Rapid wing-beats (like hummingbirds). Can visit 100+ flowers per hour.

Habitat use: Prefers open country with flowering and fruiting plants. Gardens, orchards, cultivated areas, and forest edges particularly suitable.

Social behaviour: Solitary or in pairs. Territorial; males defend flower patches. Joins mixed feeding flocks opportunistically.

Breeding: Cavity nester; breeds year-round with peaks in some seasons depending on flowering.

Activity: Diurnal; active throughout the day; rests during extreme heat midday.

हिंदी: फूलों पर मँडराती है। नेक्टर पीती है। छोटे कीड़े भी खाती है। बहुत तेज़ गति से एक फूल से दूसरे फूल पर जाती है। अकेली या जोड़े में।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Breeding: Can breed year-round; peaks vary with flowering availability.

Nesting: Builds small cup-shaped or pouch-like nest; hangs from branch or twig. Uses old nests or builds new ones. Clutch 2–3 eggs; incubation 10–12 days; fledging 12–14 days.

Territorial behaviour: Males defend flower-rich areas; singing and displays less prominent than in some species.

Moult: Gradual moult in post-breeding season.

Activity: Resident year-round; no seasonal migration.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Shikra, other small raptors; rarely caught due to small size and fast movements
  • Mammalian predators: Snake (occasionally at nest)
  • Nest predators: Snakes, monitors, occasionally crows or wasps
हिंदी: बहुत तेज़ी से उड़ने के कारण शिकारी से बचती है। घोंसलों को साँपों से खतरा।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected status: Pale-billed Flowerpecker is protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, Schedule IV. All wild birds listed are protected; hunting and trapping are prohibited.

Pollination role: Important pollinator of flowers in orchards and gardens; particularly valuable for mistletoe flowers.

Benefit to agriculture: Beneficial in that it feeds on small insects and helps pollination of flowers.

Horticultural significance: Indicator of flower and fruit availability in gardens and orchards.

हिंदी: यह पक्षी विधि अधिनियम 1972 की सुरक्षित है। फूलों की परागकण करने में मदद करता है। बागों के लिए उपयोगी है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent; resident and non-migratory throughout.

In India: Widespread in open and cultivated habitats across subcontinent.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in gardens, orchards, and cultivated areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Common year-round in gardens, orchards, cultivated areas, and open country with flowering plants.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Latham in 1790 from India. Type locality peninsular India.

Subspecies. Monotypic; D. e. erythrorhynchos throughout range, though some recognize subspecific variation.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Dicaeidae (flowerpeckers). Genus Dicaeum comprises 44+ species distributed across Africa, Asia, and Australia.

Phylogenetic notes. Part of larger sunbird-flowerpecker radiation in Old World tropics; represents Asian adaptation to nectar feeding.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC). Common and widespread; population stable. Adaptable to human-modified landscapes. Not threatened.

हिंदी: यह फूलचुही परिवार की एक प्रजाति है। एशिया में बहुत आम है। संख्या में स्थिर है। सबसे छोटी चिड़ियों में से एक।
