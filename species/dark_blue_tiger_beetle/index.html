गहरा-नीला बाघ भृंग / Dark Blue Tiger Beetle

Cicindela cyanea Fabricius, 1787 — Carabidae

गहरा-नीला बाघ भृंग — यह एक बड़ा बाघ भृंग है जिसका ऊपरी भाग गहरे नीले और हल्की हरी झलक वाले लगभग काले रंग का होता है। इसके पंखों पर कोई धब्बा नहीं होता, हालांकि इसकी एक दुर्लभ किस्म में पीली धारी होती है। इसके सिर, वक्ष और पंखों के किनारों पर चमकदार हरा रंग होता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeLength 22–23 mm (Fowler 1912, p. 406)
Upper surfaceDark cyaneous with a slight greenish tinge, almost black; sides of labrum, head, pronotum, and elytra shining green (Fowler 1912, p. 406)
Head and pronotumHead broad, clypeus and front green, eyes less prominent than in C. bicolor; pronotum very slightly narrowed at base (Fowler 1912, p. 406)
ElytraDull, immaculate, with very fine sculpture; broad and gently rounded (Fowler 1912, p. 406)
UndersideMore or less violaceous; abdomen without reddish margins and apex; pubescence very scanty (Fowler 1912, p. 406)
Variety dejeaniElytra ornamented throughout their length with a yellow stripe starting from the shoulder and widened behind (Fowler 1912, p. 406)

Field tip: Look for a very large (22–23 mm), dull dark blue-black, unspotted tiger beetle with green margins and a dark-edged abdomen, much larger than Cicindela bicolor (Fowler 1912, p. 406).

हिंदी: इसकी लंबाई 22 से 23 मिमी होती है। यह गहरे नीले-काले रंग का बड़ा भृंग है जिसके पंखों पर कोई धब्बा नहीं होता और किनारों पर चमकदार हरा रंग होता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (Fowler 1912, p. 406):

MeasurementRange
Length22–23 mm

Fowler (1912, p. 406) describes the adult as follows: "Much larger than C. bicolor, with which it has been confused, and of a darker, duller and more uniform colour; upper surface dark cyaneous, with a slight greenish tinge, almost black, with the sides of the labrum, head, pronotum, and elytra shining green, the colour on the latter being duller, the clypeus and front of the head are also green; head broad, eyes not as prominent as in C. bicolor; pronotum very slightly narrowed at base, elytra dull, immaculate, with the sculpture very fine, but more distinct than in the allied species; underside more or less violaceous, abdomen without the reddish margins and apex, and with the pubescence much more scanty, and almost wanting, but apparently fugitive."

For var. dejeani Hope, Fowler (1912, p. 406) notes: "This variety has the elytra ornamented throughout their length with a yellow stripe, starting from the shoulder and becoming widened behind. It has occurred in Chota Nagpur with the type-form, and there is a specimen in the British Museum from Berhampur; it has also been recorded from Murshidabad. It appears to be a very rare insect."

हिंदी: फाउलर (1912) के अनुसार यह Cicindela bicolor से काफी बड़ा होता है और इसका रंग अधिक गहरा और एकसमान होता है। इसके पेट के निचले हिस्से के किनारे और सिरा लाल नहीं होते, जैसा कि C. bicolor में दिखता है।

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya: two photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri, in July 2025 and July 2026, by two observers. A first local record would be a photograph showing the large, dark cyaneous, unspotted upper surface (or the yellow-striped var. dejeani) with place and date, sent through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Described by Fabricius (1787, p. 155); Fowler (1912, p. 406) records it from eastern India.

In India: Fowler (1912, p. 406) lists Bengal: Chota Nagpur, Palkot, and Sahibganj (plus Berhampur and Murshidabad for var. dejeani).

In Uttar Pradesh: GBIF holds two photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri (July 2025 and July 2026, two observers).

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी: फाउलर (1912) ने इसे बंगाल के छोटा नागपुर, पालकोट और साहिबगंज से दर्ज किया है। उत्तर प्रदेश में जीबीआइएफ पर लखीमपुर खीरी के दो अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Fabricius, J. C. (1787). Mantissa Insectorum, vol. 1, p. 155, as Cicindela cyanea (cited in Fowler 1912, p. 406).

Subspecies. Not available in consulted sources. Fowler (1912, p. 406) treats Cicindela dejeani Hope, 1831 as a variety and lists C. obliquivittata Fleutiaux, 1898 under it.

Family and order placement. Order Coleoptera, family Carabidae, genus Cicindela (Fowler 1912, p. 406, entry 182).

Phylogenetic notes. Fowler (1912, p. 406) placed it alone in his Group 17, noting that it has nothing to do with C. bicolor and shows affinities towards the C. aurofasciata section.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated on the IUCN Red List.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वैज्ञानिक वर्णन फैब्रिकियस (1787) ने Cicindela cyanea के रूप में किया था। यह Carabidae कुल और Cicindela वंश का सदस्य है।
