बड़ा पनकौआ / Great Cormorant

Phalacrocorax carbo (Linnaeus, 1758) — Phalacrocoracidae

बड़ा पनकौआ — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश की बड़ी झीलों, जलाशयों और नदियों में ठंड के मौसम में मिलने वाला एक बड़ा, काली चिड़िया, जो पानी में गहराई तक गोता लगाकर मछलियों का शिकार करती है। इसकी अंग्रेजी भाषा में प्रसिद्ध नाम "cormorant" संभवतः फ्रेंच "corbeau marin" (समुद्री कौआ) से आया है, और यह नाम सटीक है क्योंकि यह पक्षी कौए जितना काला होता है। इसकी पूँछ मजबूत होती है, जिससे पानी में तेज़ी से तैर सकता है।

Field tip: A large all-black diving bird on lakes and rivers in winter, often with neck and head showing above water. Much larger than Little Cormorant. Hunts by diving; watch for it submerging and resurfacing far away.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

Size: 80–100 cm long; one of the largest diving birds in region.

Plumage: All black or blackish-brown; appears glossy in good light with subtle blue and green iridescence. Feathers water-resistant but not waterproof; birds spend time perched with wings partially spread to dry.

Head and neck: Dark throughout. Long, slender neck adapted for underwater foraging.

Breeding plumage: In breeding birds, white thigh-patches appear; face skin may develop warty texture.

Bill: Long, straight, hooked at tip; dark grey to black. Highly adapted for gripping slippery fish.

Throat pouch: Bare yellow gular pouch visible in breeding birds; less obvious in non-breeding birds.

Eyes: Bright green to yellow-green in breeding birds; pale yellow in non-breeding birds.

Legs and feet: Dark grey; placed far back on body (adapted for diving).

Sexes: Males larger than females; males show more prominent breeding features.

Juveniles: Duller brown, often with paler underparts; gradually acquire adult plumage.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Grunts and croaks: Low grunting calls at roost or when disturbed.
  2. Contact calls: Soft croaks between pair members.
  3. Alarm calls: Harsh hisses when threatened.
हिंदी: शांत चिड़िया है। कभी-कभी "क्रांक-क्रांक" जैसी आवाज़ निकालती है। गुस्से में हिसहिसाती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Piscivore predator: Great Cormorants hunt fish exclusively by diving deep underwater. They pursue fish actively, using their feet as rudders and wings for propulsion. Preferred fish include:

  • Medium-sized cyprinids (carp family)
  • Catfish
  • Murrels and snakeheads
  • Other freshwater fish

Diving ability: Can dive to 10 m or deeper. Swim underwater with remarkable agility.

Feeding: Dive repeatedly throughout the day. Single dive lasts 20–60 seconds.

Social behaviour: Highly gregarious; roost together in large numbers. Breed colonially.

Flight: Strong fliers; fly in V-formation on migration. Characteristically fly low over water.

Perching behaviour: Often perch on rocks, dead trees, or man-made structures with wings spread to dry plumage.

हिंदी: झुंड में रहता है। मछलियों का शिकार करता है। गहरा गोता लगा सकता है। पंखों को सुखाता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Partial migrant: Resident and migrant; breeding populations occur but winter populations much larger. Numbers peak November–February.

Breeding: Breeding birds may remain year-round; breed in colonies on cliffs or tall trees (January–May in some regions).

Clutch: 3–4 eggs; incubation 28–31 days.

Fledging: Young remain in nest 50–60 days. Single brood per year.

Wintering: Large influxes from November through February from higher latitudes and colder regions.

Fish availability: Closely follows water levels and fish populations; movements across region following conditions.

हिंदी: ठंड में ज़्यादा संख्या में आता है। नवंबर से फरवरी तक पाया जाता है। कुछ पक्षी साल भर रहते हैं। मछलियों की कमी में चले जाते हैं।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Large eagles, occasionally Steppe Eagle
  • At nest: Humans (persecution), large snakes
  • Mammalian predators: Foxes, wild boar (unlikely due to nesting site)
  • Parasites: Parasitic copepods (water parasites affecting fish and birds)
हिंदी: बड़ी चील इसका शिकार कर सकती है। मानवों का शिकार एक समस्या है। मछलियों की बीमारियाँ इसे संक्रमित कर सकती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Fishery impact: Historically persecuted due to competition with human fisheries. Consumes significant quantities of fish, which may compete with commercial fish catches or aquaculture.

Ecological service: Removes diseased and weak fish; controls invasive fish species in some cases.

Persecution: Shot and killed in many areas despite legal protection. Considered a pest by fishermen.

Water quality indicator: Presence indicates relatively unpolluted water bodies with adequate fish populations.

हिंदी: यह चिड़िया वन्यजीव अधिनियम 1972 की अंतर्गत पूरी तरह संरक्षित है। मछुआरों द्वारा अक्सर शिकार किया जाता है, लेकिन कानूनन इसका शिकार निषिद्ध है। जलाशयों के स्वास्थ्य का संकेत है।

Habitat preference: Large water bodies with adequate fish populations; lakes, reservoirs, and slow-moving rivers.

Seasonality: Partial migrant; more abundant November–March. Some year-round residents in suitable habitat.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Northern hemisphere; Europe, Asia, and North Africa. Multiple subspecies recognised.

In India: Distributed on larger water bodies across northern and central India during winter.

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter visitor to larger lakes and reservoirs; some year-round residents.

Field rule: Great Cormorant is the largest cormorant in the region, nearly twice the size of Little Cormorant. Its large size, long neck, and large hooked bill are diagnostic. Note its yellow throat pouch (if visible) and bright green eyes in breeding birds.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1758 in Systema Naturae (10th edition), volume 1, page 107, as Pelecanus carbo. Type locality is Europe.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies recognised:

  • P. c. sinensis (Blainville, 1840) — Asian populations (occurs in India)
  • P. c. carbo — European form
  • P. c. maroccanus — North African form

Populations wintering in Uttar Pradesh belong to P. c. sinensis.

Family and order placement. Order Suliformes; family Phalacrocoracidae (cormorants and shags). The genus Phalacrocorax contains the large diving cormorants.

Phylogenetic notes. Closely related to other cormorant species across the Northern Hemisphere. Represents a highly specialised diving adaptation within the order.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) globally. Populations remain widespread despite persecution in many areas. Protected under Indian law, though enforcement remains inconsistent.
