मार्वल घास / Marvel Grass

Dichanthium annulatum (Forssk.) Stapf — Poaceae

मार्वल घास — उत्तर भारत के चरागाहों और खेतों की मेड़ों पर उगने वाली एक बहुवर्षीय, अत्यधिक पौष्टिक चारा घास। इसके तने की गांठों पर बारीक सफेद रोओं का एक स्पष्ट छल्ला बना होता है, जिससे इसे आसानी से पहचाना जा सकता है। बरसात के बाद इसमें उँगलियों जैसे फैले हुए बैंगनी-भूरे बालों वाले फूल गुच्छों में निकलते हैं, जिन्हें मवेशी बड़े चाव से चरते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitTufted perennial grass 30–100 cm tall, forming dense leafy tussocks (Bor 1960)
CulmsErect or geniculately ascending, smooth; nodes densely bearded with white hairs (Bor 1960)
LeavesLinear, 5–30 cm long, 3–6 mm wide, glaucous green, scabrid on the margins (Duthie 1888)
LiguleShort, membranous, ciliate, 1–2 mm long (Bor 1960)
InflorescenceSubdigitate panicle of 2–9 purplish-pink racemes 3–7 cm long clustered at culm apex (Bor 1960)
AwnsSlender, twisted, geniculate, 1.5–2.5 cm long, emerging from fertile spikelets (Bor 1960)

Field tip: Inspect pasture tussocks in autumn for grass stems whose nodes show a neat ring of soft white hairs, bearing finger-like clusters of silky purplish racemes with fine twisted awns.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: तने की गांठों पर सफेद रोओं की अंगूठी जैसी पट्टी, मेड़ों पर घने गुच्छे, और सिरे पर उँगलियों जैसे फैले बैंगनी-भूरे बालों वाले फूलों के गुच्छे।

Morphology शारीरिक संरचना

Biometrics (Bor 1960; Duthie 1888):

MeasurementRange
Culm height30–100 cm (Bor 1960)
Leaf blade length5–30 cm (Duthie 1888)
Leaf blade width3–6 mm (Bor 1960)
Ligule length1–2 mm (Bor 1960)
Raceme length3–7 cm (Bor 1960)
Awn length1.5–2.5 cm (Bor 1960)

Culms and nodes: Culms tufted, densely branched at the base, ascending from a decumbent or creeping rootstock. Internodes glabrous; nodes prominently bearded with a distinct ring of spreading white silky hairs.

Leaf blades and sheaths: Sheaths terete, tight, glabrous, usually shorter than internodes. Leaf blades flat, linear, tapering to a fine point, glabrous or sparsely pilose near the base, glaucous green.

Inflorescence and spikelets: Inflorescence consists of 2–9 subdigitate, shortly pedunculate racemes. Spikelets paired, one sessile and fertile, the other pedicelled and male or neuter. Glumes herbaceous; lower lemma empty; upper lemma bearing a slender twisted brownish geniculate awn.

हिंदी: तना नीचे से शाखादार और ऊपर सीधा होता है। पत्तियाँ चपटी और चिकनी होती हैं। फूल की बालियों में बारीक मुड़े हुए मूँछ जैसे कांटे होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार एवं पारिस्थितिकी

Dichanthium annulatum is an adaptable, deep-rooted C4 perennial grass that thrives across warm alluvial plains. It shows outstanding tolerance to rotational grazing and moderate drought, reviving rapidly upon receipt of moisture.

The fibrous root network binds loose alluvial topsoil, reducing erosion along farm embankments. Flowering occurs from September to December, producing wind-borne pollen that disperses across open meadows. The mature awned spikelets catch on the coats of livestock, ensuring zoochorous dispersal throughout village grazing commons.

हिंदी: यह घास अधिक चराई और सूखे को सहने में पूरी तरह सक्षम है। इसकी जड़ें मिट्टी को मजबूती से बाँधती हैं और बालियां मवेशियों के शरीर से चिपककर फैलती हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र एवं ऋतुचक्र

PeriodPhenological Event
June–JulyDormant tussocks break into vigorous vegetative growth with the arrival of monsoon rains
August–SeptemberTillering peaks and lush leafy foliage carpets pasture commons and field bunds
October–DecemberFlowering panicles emerge with silky awned racemes; seed maturation occurs
January–MayFoliage cures naturally into dry standing hay; root crowns remain dormant in the soil

Seeds germinate abundantly in moist surface soils during warm rainy intervals in July.

हिंदी: बरसात शुरू होते ही पुरानी जड़ों से नई हरी घास फूटती है। क्वांर-कार्तिक में फूल खिलते हैं और जाड़ों में बीज पककर चरागाहों में बिखर जाते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबद्ध प्रजातियाँ एवं पारस्परिक क्रियाएं

  • Herbivores: Domestic cattle, buffaloes, and goats graze the succulent vegetative tillers.
  • Granivores: Grassland munias and sparrows forage for fallen seeds in early winter.
  • Pasture Companions: Grows in community association with Doob Grass, Kans Grass, and Jungle Rice.
हिंदी: गाय और भैंसें इसे बहुत पसंद करती हैं और जाड़े के दिनों में छोटी चिड़ियाँ इसके बीज चुगती हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट एवं रोग

  • Ergot fungus (Claviceps species) occasionally infects spikelets during prolonged late-monsoon drizzle.
  • Rust caused by Puccinia duthiae produces orange-brown pustules on aging autumn foliage.
  • Grasshoppers feed on the leafy blades in late summer pastures.
हिंदी: बरसात के अंत में पत्तियों पर कभी-कभी गेरुई के धब्बे और टिड्डियों का प्रकोप दिखाई देता है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि एवं मानवीय उपयोगिता

Pastoral and Soil Conservation Value:

  • Prime livestock fodder. Regarded across northern and western India as one of the finest natural pasture grasses, highly palatable and relished by all classes of stock both fresh and as hay.
  • Bund stabilization. Extensively protected on agricultural field bunds to reinforce soil structure and prevent water run-off.
  • Thatching and bedding. Coarser dry stems are occasionally cut for livestock bedding and temporary rural thatch.
  • Traditional medicine. Folk traditions record the use of root decoctions as cooling diuretic washes in rural veterinary care. This is a record of traditional use, not medical advice.

Today. It is widely promoted by agricultural universities and ICAR institutes for reseeding degraded village pasture lands and community rangelands.

हिंदी: मार्वल घास को पशुओं के लिए सर्वोत्तम देशी चारा माना जाता है। इसे मेड़ों को मजबूत करने और पशुओं के लिए सूखा चारा तैयार करने में बड़े पैमाने पर उपयोग किया जाता है।

Distribution भौगोलिक वितरण

Global range: Native across northern and eastern Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, South Asia, and into Southeast Asia; introduced to Australia and the Americas.

In India: Widespread in plains and low hills throughout Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, and peninsular India (Bor 1960).

In Uttar Pradesh: Abundantly recorded in grasslands, waste ground, and pasture lands of the Gangetic plain (Duthie 1888).

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected on village grazing lands, river embankments, and uncultivated farm borders.

Field rule: Tufted perennial grass with a conspicuous ring of white hairs at each stem node and subdigitate silky purplish racemes with fine twisted awns identifies Dichanthium annulatum.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गिकी एवं वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Andropogon annulatus by Pehr Forsskål in 1775 in Flora Aegyptiaco-Arabica, p. 173. Transferred to Dichanthium by Otto Stapf in 1917 in Prain's Flora of Tropical Africa, vol. 9, p. 178.

Subspecies. Monotypic in standard Indian taxonomic treatments; morphological variants exist in spikelet pubescence and raceme numbers across arid and humid biomes.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the grass family Poaceae, subfamily Panicoideae, tribe Andropogoneae, in the order Poales.

Phylogenetic notes. Dichanthium forms an intricate polyploid complex closely allied to Bothriochloa and Capillipedium, sharing paired awned spikelets.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated in the master expansion list; it is an abundant, widespread, and ecologically dominant pasture grass across the Old World tropics.
