स्कारलेट बास्कर व्याध पतंग / Scarlet Basker

Urothemis signata (Rambur, 1842) — Libellulidae

स्कारलेट बास्कर व्याध पतंग — बड़े तालाबों, झीलों और कमल के पोखरों के पास पाई जाने वाली एक अत्यंत आकर्षक, मध्यम आकार की लाल रंग की व्याध पतंग (dragonfly) है। वयस्क नर का पूरा शरीर सिंदूरी लाल होता है और पिछले पंखों के आधार पर गहरे लाल-भूरे रंग के बड़े धब्बे होते हैं जिनमें सुनहरी नसें चमकती हैं। इसके पेट के अंत पर काले त्रिकोणीय निशान होते हैं। यह धूप में ऊँची टहनियों पर बैठकर धूप सेकती है और उड़ने वाले कीड़ों को हवा में पकड़ती है।

Field tip: Look along open shorelines of large perennial tanks and oxbow marshes. A robust, blood-red dragonfly perching prominently on the highest exposed twig or reed tip, displaying broad golden-red patches at the base of its hindwings and black spots on the tail, is the Scarlet Basker.

हिंदी: तालाबों के किनारे सबसे ऊँची टहनी पर बैठने वाली इस चमकीली लाल पतंग के पिछले पंखों की जड़ में गहरा लाल-सुनहरा धब्बा और पूँछ पर दो काले त्रिकोण होते हैं।

Adult description:

Urothemis signata is a moderately large, heavily built libellulid dragonfly exhibiting conspicuous sexual dichromatism (Fraser 1936). In mature males, the head is bright blood-red on the frons and vertex; the compound eyes meet broadly across the top of the head, coloured deep crimson-red above and olivaceous-grey below (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is robust, densely clothed in fine reddish hairs, and uniform bright scarlet-red dorsally and laterally, without dark stripes (Fraser 1936).

The male abdomen is stout, slightly depressed, and brilliant scarlet-red throughout: segments 1 through 7 are uniform red; segment 8 bears a conspicuous, triangular, velvety black mid-dorsal marking; segment 9 bears a larger, diamond-shaped black patch; and segment 10 is red with short, sharp, black-tipped anal appendages (Subramanian 2005).

The female is greenish-yellow or warm golden-brown (Fraser 1936). Her thorax has dark brown humeral sutures, and her abdomen is ochreous-yellow marked with a continuous black dorsal stripe expanding into triangular spots on segments 8 and 9 (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the forewings are completely hyaline, but the hindwings carry a large, diagnostic, dark reddish-brown basal patch reticulated with bright golden-yellow veins and bordered by an amber halo; the pterostigma is long and bright reddish-brown (Fraser 1936).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is broad, flattened, and dark brownish-grey, dwelling in mud and organic debris at the base of emergent reed beds. The abdomen bears strong, sharp dorsal spines on segments 4 to 8 and long lateral spines on segments 8 and 9, serving as effective anti-predator armour against carnivorous fishes.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 25 से 28 मिमी लंबा और सिंदूरी लाल होता है जिसके अंत में काले निशान होते हैं। पिछले पंखों पर सुनहरी नसों वाला लाल धब्बा होता है। मादा सुनहरी-भूरी होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Scarlet Basker is a sun-loving, highly territorial dragonfly of expansive lentic wetlands (Subramanian 2005). It inhabits large perennial tanks, oxbow lakes, slow river meanders, and irrigation reservoirs containing rich emergent vegetation like Typha latifolia (Common Cattail) and floating Nelumbo nucifera (Indian Lotus) (Fraser 1936).

Adults are active throughout warm sunny hours (Subramanian 2005). Males spend prolonged periods basking in intense sunshine on prominent, elevated vantage points—such as dead tree snags, fishing stakes, or tall reed tips—holding their wings angled downward and their abdomen tilted toward the sun. They make swift, powerful territorial sorties to intercept passing insects or drive off rival males, returning reliably to the exact same perch. Mating pairs fly in tandem over open water before copulating perched on shoreline bushes. Oviposition is exophytic: the female hovers close to the water surface, rhythmically tapping her abdomen against emergent weeds to wash off clutches of eggs.

हिंदी: यह पतंग तेज धूप में बैठना पसंद करती है। नर पानी के बीच गड़े बांस या सूखी लकड़ी के सबसे ऊँचे सिरे पर बैठकर अपने इलाके की निगरानी करता है और शिकारियों को भगाता है।

Females fly in low hovering arcs across calm water, tapping the surface near floating leaves of Nelumbo nucifera (Indian Lotus) or submerged weed beds to disperse hundreds of eggs. The eggs hatch after a few days into predatory nymphs. Naiads develop over several weeks through multiple instars in bottom silt, feeding voraciously on aquatic insect larvae. At maturity, the nymph crawls up an emergent cattail reed at dawn to metamorphose into an adult dragonfly.

हिंदी: मादा पानी के ऊपर मँडराते हुए सतह पर अंडे छोड़ती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडे फूटते हैं और पानी की तलहटी में रहने वाला लार्वा कुछ हफ्तों में बड़ा होकर वयस्क बन जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Urothemis signata is an aggressive aerial predator throughout its adult life (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Swarming wetland mosquitoes, including Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Culex tritaeniorhynchus (Rice-field Mosquito).
  • Agricultural pests active around wetlands, including Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper).
  • Medium-sized flies, non-biting midges (Chironomidae), small damselflies, and flying termites.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito larvae, chironomid bloodworms, mayfly nymphs, and small tadpoles in bottom silt.
हिंदी: यह बड़ी लाल पतंग हवा में उड़ते हुए मच्छरों, धान के कीटों और छोटी मक्खियों को पकड़कर खाती है। इसका जलीय निम्फ पानी में रहने वाले कीड़ों के लार्वा को नष्ट करता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented in Gangetic plain freshwater ecosystems:

  • Birds: Insectivorous water birds, such as green bee-eaters, kingfishers, and black drongos, ambush perching and flying adults over open water.
  • Predatory dragonflies: Larger anisopterans like Anax guttatus attack smaller libellulids in open airspace.
  • Spiders: Wetland orb-weavers occasionally snare low-flying adults in reed beds.
  • Fish: Murrels and large walking catfishes feed on bottom-dwelling nymphs in muddy tanks.
हिंदी: जल-पक्षी जैसे नीलकंठ, किंगफिशर और भुजंगा उड़ती हुई लाल पतंग को पकड़ लेते हैं। पानी के अंदर बड़ी शिकारी मछलियाँ इसके निम्फ को खाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Natural biological control: The Scarlet Basker is an effective natural predator of insect pests in irrigated agricultural plains of northern India (Subramanian 2005). Because it hunts continuously over open water and adjacent rice fields, it captures large quantities of sap-sucking hoppers like Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and leafhoppers that cause hopper-burn in kharif crops.

Suppression of disease vectors: Adults and aquatic naiads consume significant volumes of Culex mosquitoes that breed in rural irrigation systems and transmit filariasis and Japanese encephalitis (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal, ritual, or economic uses are recorded.

Today: Highly valued by naturalists and photographers for its brilliant crimson color and cooperative perching behavior.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing the territorial foraging radius of Urothemis signata relative to village mosquito production sources.

हिंदी: यह पतंग धान की फसलों के हानिकारक कीटों और बीमारी फैलाने वाले मच्छरों का भारी मात्रा में शिकार करके किसानों और ग्रामीणों को बहुत लाभ पहुँचाती है।

The Scarlet Basker is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in large perennial wetlands, oxbow lakes (tals), and canal reservoirs fringed with cattails and water lilies. Look on high, exposed perches and dead stakes in full sunshine from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph showing the blood-red body, dark-patched hindwings, and black abdominal spots, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across the Indo-Malayan and Australasian realms, ranging from India and Sri Lanka across Southeast Asia to southern China, the Philippines, Indonesia, New Guinea, and northern Australia (Fraser 1936).

In India: Recorded throughout peninsular India, the Western and Eastern Ghats, central India, and across the Gangetic floodplains to Assam and the Andaman Islands (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from wetland sanctuaries, oxbow lakes, and large rural water bodies throughout central and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident breeding species in permanent wetlands, tals, and large canal reservoirs.

हिंदी: यह भारत से लेकर दक्षिण-पूर्व एशिया और ऑस्ट्रेलिया तक पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के बड़े तालाबों और झीलों में यह स्वाभाविक रूप से मौजूद है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Jules Pierre Rambur in 1842 as Libellula signata in Histoire naturelle des insectes. Névroptères, page 117. Type locality: "Java" (Indonesia).

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies are recognized across the Oriental realm. The population inhabiting the mainland of northern India and the Gangetic plain is classified as the nominotypical subspecies Urothemis signata signata (Rambur, 1842).

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae. The genus Urothemis Brauer, 1868, comprises basker dragonflies characterized by broad hindwings, colourful basal wing patches, and open perching habits.

Phylogenetic notes. Urothemis is allied to Macrodiplax and Aethriamanta within the subfamily Urothemistinae, defined by specialised wing venation and robust territorial basking behaviour (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure wetland dragonfly throughout its vast tropical range.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Urothemis signata है और यह Libellulidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका अस्तित्व पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
