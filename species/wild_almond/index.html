जंगली बादाम / Wild Almond

Sterculia foetida L. — Malvaceae

जंगली बादाम — एक बड़ा, पर्णपाती वृक्ष जो गाँव के बागों, मंदिरों और आम के बागों में लगाया जाता है। पत्तियाँ बहुत बड़ी, हथेली जैसी, पाँच या छह भागों में बँटी होती हैं। फूल छोटे, भूरे-लाल, गुच्छों में आते हैं जिनमें अजीब गंध होती है। फल बड़े, खुली पोडें जिनमें बादाम जैसे बीज होते हैं जिन्हें भून कर खाया जाता है। पेड़ बहुत तेजी से बढ़ता है।

Field tip: In village commons and homesteads, look for a large tree with enormous palm-like leaves divided into 5–7 lobes. The flowers are unremarkable and smell bad. The distinctive feature is the large red boat-shaped seed pods that split open to reveal large, almond-like seeds. These seeds are roasted and eaten locally.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बहुत बड़ी, हथेली जैसी पत्तियाँ जिनमें 5-7 भाग हों। नाव जैसी लाल पोडें जो खुल जाती हैं। भूरे-लाल फूलों की गंध अजीब होती है।

Trunk and bark: Straight, large, with greyish-brown, slightly rough bark. Heartwood pale yellow, moderately hard and durable.

Leaves: Alternate, deciduous, large, palmately lobed with 5–7 lobes, 15–30 cm across. Lobes are elliptic-lanceolate. Petiole long, 10–15 cm. Bright green above, paler beneath, slightly hairy.

Flowers: Small, about 1 cm, dark reddish-brown, bisexual, in compound pendulous panicles. Characteristic pungent smell (hence the epithet foetida). Flowers February to April.

Fruit: A cluster of 4–5 follicles (seed pods), each 7–10 cm long, boat-shaped, reddish, papery. Each follicle opens spontaneously to release typically 4–6 large, smooth, almond-like seeds, 2–3 cm long. Seeds are red, brown, or mottled.

हिंदी: पत्तियाँ हथेली जैसी, 5-7 भागों में। फूल छोटे, भूरे-लाल, बुरी गंध वाले। फल नाव के आकार के, लाल, खुल जाते हैं। बीज बादाम जैसे, खाने लायक।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Wild Almond is a deciduous tree planted in villages and homesteads of the Indo-Gangetic plain. It tolerates moderate rainfall and is drought-hardy once established. Rapid growth makes it attractive for shade and timber. The tree grows quickly and matures in 8–10 years. The large seeds are dispersed locally; their weight means they do not travel far. The pungent flowers are less attractive to insects than other species, though they are visited by flies and some bees.

हिंदी: गाँव के बागों में लगाया जाता है। तेजी से बढ़ता है। गर्मी सहन करता है। फूलों की बदबू कुछ को भाती है, कुछ को नहीं।

Seedlings germinate readily in monsoon. Growth is very rapid; a sapling can reach 3–4 m in a single season with adequate water. Lifespan reaches 40–50 years or more.

हिंदी: दिसंबर में पत्तियाँ गिरती हैं। फरवरी में नई पत्तियाँ और फूल। अगस्त तक फल तैयार। अक्टूबर तक बीज गिरते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Shade companion: Often planted alongside Mango, Jackfruit and other homestead trees.
  • Seed dispersal: Birds and mammals may consume fallen seeds, though germination is better in wet soil.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf-eating caterpillars and beetles in monsoon, rarely serious on mature trees.
  • Termites may attack weakened wood but are not a major problem in healthy trees.
  • No significant fungal diseases in the semi-arid to moderate-rainfall zone.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional and ethnobotanical use:

  • Roasted seeds. The large seeds are roasted and eaten as a nut, resembling almonds in taste and texture, though with a stronger, more earthy flavour. This is the primary use.
  • Timber. Pale yellow wood is used for light construction, tool handles, and fuel.
  • Shade and shelter. Planted extensively in homestead gardens for shade in hot months.

Today. The seeds remain a minor forest product and homestead resource. The tree is valued primarily for shade and timber.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The roasting and storage of Sterculia seeds for local sale deserves documentation; the nutritional content of seeds compared to true almonds would merit investigation.

हिंदी: बीजों को भून कर खाया जाता है — बादाम जैसे स्वाद के साथ। लकड़ी ईंधन और तख़्तों के लिए काम आती है। बागों में छाया के लिए बहुत महत्वपूर्ण है।

GBIF holds published records from Basti and surrounding districts. Wild Almond is expected in village homestead gardens, temple groves, and along roadsides throughout eastern Uttar Pradesh. The large size and rapid growth make it a common feature of settlements. Look for the distinctive huge palmate leaves and boat-shaped reddish seed pods splitting open in late summer.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical Africa and cultivated throughout tropical and subtropical Asia, including the Indian subcontinent.

In India: Widely cultivated in homestead gardens and village commons from the plains up to about 1,000 m elevation.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in all districts of the plain in homesteads and roadside plantations.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Frequent in village gardens and commons, occasionally on wastelands.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  1. How many Wild Almond trees are there in your village, and what is their age? / आपके गाँव में कितने जंगली बादाम के पेड़ हैं, और कितने पुराने? — Walk the settlement; map each tree and estimate age from trunk diameter.
  2. Do local people still roast and eat the seeds, and how much is consumed per family per year? / क्या स्थानीय लोग अब भी बीज भून कर खाते हैं? — Survey ten households; record seed consumption and market prices if any.
  3. Which insects visit the pungent flowers? — Thirty minutes observation at flowering trees, morning and evening.
  4. What is the germination rate of naturally-fallen seeds in monsoon soil? — Monitor seeds under parent trees.
  5. How fast does a sapling grow from seed, and when does it first flower? / बीज से उगा पेड़ कितनी तेजी से बढ़ता है? — Plant seeds and measure monthly growth over two years.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum as Sterculia foetida. Type locality is tropical Africa (Ghana region).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Malvales, family Malvaceae. The genus Sterculia contains about 150 species of tropical trees, many with large palmate leaves and distinctive seed pods.

Phylogenetic notes. Sterculia foetida is the type species of Sterculia and represents a tropical African–Asian lineage within Malvaceae.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. The species is common in cultivation across its range and is not threatened.
