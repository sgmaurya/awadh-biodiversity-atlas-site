ड्वार्फ गौरमी मछली / Dwarf Gourami

Trichogaster lalius (Hamilton, 1822) — Osphronemidae

ड्वार्फ गौरमी मछली — जलकुंभी से भरे तालाबों, पोखरों और धान के खेतों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, अत्यंत सुंदर और चमकीली देसी मछली (खोलिसा) है। इसके शरीर पर सिंदूरी लाल और फिरोजी-नीले रंग की तिरछी धारियाँ होती हैं। इसके पेट के पंख लंबे, पतले धागे जैसे स्पर्शकों (feelers) में बदल चुके होते हैं, जिनसे यह पौधों और भोजन को छूकर महसूस करती है। इसके पास हवा में सांस लेने वाला विशेष अंग होता है। यह दुनिया भर के एक्वेरियम में अपनी सुंदरता के लिए प्रसिद्ध है।

Field tip: Look among dense floating vegetation in calm village tanks and oxbow wetlands. A tiny, oval, paper-thin fish with thread-like pelvic feelers and dazzling scarlet and turquoise vertical stripes, rising periodically to gulp air at the surface, is the Dwarf Gourami.

हिंदी: यह छोटी, गोल-चपटी मछली जलीय पौधों के बीच तैरती है। नर के शरीर पर लाल और नीली चमकदार धारियाँ होती हैं और पेट से दो लंबे धागे जैसे पंख निकलते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Size as given by FishBase (Froese & Pauly 2025):

Adult description:

Trichogaster lalius has a compact, oval, and strongly compressed body profile (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal and ventral contours are equally and deeply convex, tapering abruptly to a short caudal peduncle (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is small and compressed; eyes are moderately large and lateral (Hamilton 1822). The mouth is small, terminal, and directed obliquely upward; the preorbital and lower margin of the preoperculum are finely serrated (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Teeth are small, conical, and arranged in narrow bands on the jaws (Daniels 2002).

A specialized auxiliary air-breathing structure—the labyrinth organ—is housed inside a suprabranchial cavity above the gills, enabling direct respiration of atmospheric oxygen (Daniels 2002). The pelvic fins are inserted slightly ahead of the pectoral fins and are modified into a single, elongate, hair-like sensory ray (feeler) capable of independent movement in all directions (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin is elongate, originating above the pectoral fin base, supported by 15–17 sharp, stiff spines and 7–8 soft branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin has a similarly long base, carrying 17–18 spines and 13–15 soft rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The caudal fin is rounded and complete (Hamilton 1822). The body is covered with moderate ctenoid scales, with 26 to 28 scales in the lateral series (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Sexual dichromatism is pronounced (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Males display radiant coloration: the cheeks and chest are deep turquoise-blue, and the flanks are traversed by a series of alternating, bright scarlet-red and sky-blue diagonal bars extending onto the dorsal and anal fins (Hamilton 1822). Females are much plainer, displaying a silvery-olive body with subtle dusky vertical bars and shorter pelvic feelers (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 9.5 सेमी तक लंबा, चपटा और गोल होता है। नर का रंग बेहद चमकदार लाल और फिरोजी नीला होता है, जबकि मादा हल्के स्लेटी रंग की होती है। पेट के दो पंख लंबे धागे जैसे स्पर्शकों में बदल चुके होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Dwarf Gourami is a peaceful, diurnal benthopelagic fish adapted to densely vegetated, standing freshwaters (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It inhabits perennial village ponds, weed-choked oxbow wetlands (tals), sluggish canals, and shallow drainage ditches rich in floating macrophytes like Eichhornia, Lemna, and Salvinia.

Adults swim slowly through underwater plant tangles, extending their thread-like pelvic feelers forward to taste and touch surrounding objects and food particles (Daniels 2002). The labyrinth organ allows them to flourish in shallow, stagnant, and oxygen-depleted waters by rising to the surface every few minutes to take in atmospheric air (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). In addition to picking prey off leaves, they can spit small droplets of water at terrestrial insects perched on overhanging leaves to knock them into the water (Daniels 2002). They maintain small personal territories among weed clusters and are peaceful towards non-conspecific fishes.

हिंदी: यह मछली शांत और घने पौधों वाले पानी में धीरे-धीरे तैरती है। अपने धागे जैसे पंखों से यह चीजों को छूकर महसूस करती है। यह पानी की सतह से हवा में सांस लेती है और पत्तियों पर बैठे कीड़ों पर पानी की बूंदें फेंककर उन्हें गिरा देती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian alluvial waters across the agricultural year (Talwar & Jhingran 1991; Daniels 2002):

Breeding takes place during the monsoon rains from June to August (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The male constructs a floating bubble-nest on the water surface by binding air bubbles with mucus and weaving in bits of aquatic plant fragments. The male courts the female beneath the nest, embracing her in a nuptial clasp. As buoyant eggs are released, they float upward into the bubble-nest, where the male collects and secures them with additional bubbles. The male guards the nest fiercely against all intruders. The eggs hatch after a few days, and the male continues to guard the fry until they become free-swimming (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: बरसात के समय नर मछली पानी की सतह पर लार और बुलबुलों से तैरता हुआ घोंसला बनाती है। मादा के अंडे देने के बाद नर अंडों की रखवाली करता है। कुछ दिनों में बच्चे निकल आते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Trichogaster lalius is an opportunistic micro-carnivore and insectivore (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Mosquito larvae and pupae (Culex, Anopheles, and Aedes), captured near the water surface (Daniels 2002).
  • Planktonic micro-crustaceans, including water fleas (Daphnia, Moina) and copepods (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Small terrestrial insects knocked down from overhanging foliage, including aphids, springtails, and tiny flies.
  • Epiphytic algae, diatoms, and soft decaying plant detritus browsed from submerged leaves.
  • Tiny chironomid midge larvae and aquatic worms inhabiting weed beds.
हिंदी: यह पानी की सतह पर रहने वाले मच्छरों के लार्वा, सूक्ष्म जलीय जीवों (zooplankton), जलीय पौधों पर जमी काई और पत्तियों पर बैठने वाले छोटे कीड़ों को खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Predatory fishes: Snakeheads like Channa punctata (Spotted Snakehead), walking catfishes like Clarias magur (Indian Magur), and climbing perch Anabas testudineus (Climbing Perch) hunt gouramis in weed beds.
  • Piscivorous birds: Pond herons, little egrets, and common kingfishers capture adults when surfacing to breathe.
  • Water snakes: Checkered keelbacks pursue them among floating water hyacinth mats.
  • Predatory aquatic insects: Giant water bugs and dragonfly naiads ambush fry in weed nurseries.
हिंदी: गिरई मछली, देशी मागुर, कवाई, बगुले और पानी के साँप पानी की घास में इस छोटी सुंदर मछली का शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Biological mosquito vector control: The Dwarf Gourami is an outstanding native biological control agent against disease-vector mosquitoes in northern India (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Because it naturally inhabits stagnant, weed-choked village tanks, roadside ditches, and flooded rice fields where mosquitoes breed prolifically, it relentlessly consumes mosquito larvae and pupae, significantly reducing malaria and filariasis vectors in rural communities (Daniels 2002).

Global aquarium ornamental trade: Widely considered one of the top ten freshwater ornamental fishes in the world. Wild and captive-bred stocks generate significant commercial value globally under the name "Dwarf Gourami" (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). In rural eastern Uttar Pradesh, it is also caught in fine-mesh nets from weed-rich tals and cooked whole in small mixed-fish curries.

Traditional: Consumed as part of small subsistence catches from village ponds across Awadh.

Today: Celebrated worldwide as a premier ornamental aquarium fish and public health biocontrol agent.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Establishing community-based ornamental breeding cooperatives in rural Basti to commercialize native Trichogaster lalius color varieties.

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की घास में छिपे मच्छरों के लार्वा को खाकर मलेरिया और अन्य बीमारियों को रोकने में बहुत मदद करती है। अपनी सुंदरता के कारण यह पूरी दुनिया के एक्वेरियम में बहुत पसंद की जाती है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the northern and central Indian subcontinent, distributed across India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Found throughout the Indus, Ganges, and Brahmaputra river drainages, from the sub-Himalayan plains to central India (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, and Gomti river basins in weed-rich oxbow wetlands and village ponds.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as an abundant resident native in perennial tals, oxbow wetlands, and vegetated drains of the Kuwano and Ghaghara systems.

हिंदी: यह भारत, पाकिस्तान, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश के मैदानी इलाकों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में तालाबों, तालों और नहरों में यह बहुत सामान्य रूप से उपस्थित है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Trichopodus lalius in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 120. Type locality: Ponds and freshwater rivers of Gangetic provinces.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its continental range.

Family and order placement. Order Perciformes (or Anabantiformes), family Osphronemidae, subfamily Luciocephalinae. Long placed in the genus Colisa Cuvier, 1831, modern systematic revisions assign it to Trichogaster Bloch & Schneider, 1801.

Phylogenetic notes. Trichogaster lalius is the smallest member of the genus Trichogaster. It is distinguished from allied gouramis by its distinctive coloration, high dorsal spine count, and compact body depth (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, abundant, and secure populations throughout South Asian freshwater wetlands.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Trichogaster lalius है और यह Osphronemidae परिवार की सदस्य है। इसे पुरानी पुस्तकों में Colisa lalia लिखा गया है।
