छोटा चाहा / Jack Snipe

Lymnocryptes minimus (Brünnich, 1764) — Scolopacidae

छोटा चाहा — शीतकाल में उत्तरी यूरेशिया से उत्तर भारत के मैदानी भागों में प्रवास करने वाला एक छोटा आर्द्रभूमि पक्षी है। यह सामान्य चाहा की तुलना में काफी छोटा होता है और खतरे की आहट पर उड़ने के बजाय कीचड़ या घनी घास में दुबक कर बैठ जाता है। इसकी पीठ पर सुनहरी पट्टियाँ और सिर पर दोहरा भौंह जैसा निशान होता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeVery small, compact wader; distinctly smaller than Common Snipe
CrownVelvety-black crown with no pale median stripe; split supercilium
MantleDark with iridescent metallic gloss and two bright buff-yellow longitudinal stripes
BillStraight, slender, horny-green with fleshy base; relatively short for a snipe
FlightDirect, relatively slow and fluttering; drops back into cover quickly
Flush behaviorFreezes and flushes only when almost stepped upon; flushes silently

Field tip: Look along muddy seepage edges with short sedges around jheels and waterlogged paddies. Unlike other snipes that flush at a distance with a harsh call, the Jack Snipe crouches motionlessly until nearly trodden on and flushes silently without a zig-zag flight.

हिंदी:
यह पक्षी दलदली किनारों पर घास में पूरी तरह छिपकर बैठता है।
सिर के बीच में कोई पीली धारी नहीं होती और पीठ पर दो चमकीली पीली रेखाएँ दिखती हैं।
पास पहुँचने पर ही बिना आवाज़ किए फड़फड़ाते हुए उड़ता है और तुरंत आगे गिर जाता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements as given by Baker (1929):

MeasurementRangeSource
Wing106–116 mmBaker (1929)
Tail45–50 mmBaker (1929)
Tarsus23–25 mmBaker (1929)
Culmen38–44 mmBaker (1929)

The adult has the crown to nape velvety-black, stippled with rufous, bordered by a broad pale buff supercilium that splits above the eye into two narrow pale lines (Baker 1929). A dark loral streak runs from the base of the bill through the eye, and a second dark streak passes below the ear-coverts. The back and scapulars are black, glossed with purple and bottle-green, with the outer webs of the scapulars rich buff forming two conspicuous longitudinal bands down the mantle (Baker 1929; Blanford 1898).

The underparts are white on the belly and vent, while the lower neck, breast, and flanks are mottled with brown, rufous, and dull white. The tail has 12 rectrices, wedged and pointed, dark brown with rufescent-buff borders (Baker 1929). The bill is dark horny, fleshy at the base, and the legs are pale olive-green.

Vocalisations

Remarkably silent during its winter stay in northern India. When flushed from marsh vegetation, it departs without the rasping 'scaap' alarm call characteristic of other snipes. On northern subarctic breeding grounds, males produce a mechanical rhythmic display sound compared to the sound of a distant galloping horse.

हिंदी:
शरीर कॉम्पैक्ट और पूंछ नुकीली होती है जिसमें बारह पंख होते हैं।
पीठ पर हरी और बैंगनी चमक वाली पट्टियाँ होती हैं जो दूर से सुनहरी दिखाई देती हैं।
सर्दियों के दौरान यह भारत के मैदानी भागों में कोई आवाज नहीं निकालता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Jack Snipe is a solitary and secretive wader. It frequents short, dense marsh vegetation, damp tussocky meadows, and muddy shallows along drainage ditches and oxbows rather than broad open mudflats. During daytime foraging, it probes probing deep mud with rapid, sewing-machine-like vertical head movements, often bobbing its entire body rhythmically as if mounted on springs.

It feeds largely at dawn, dusk, or under nocturnal conditions, resting quietly during midday tucked under a tuft of grass or sedge. Unlike gregarious shorebirds, individuals maintain solitary feeding stations and do not associate in tight foraging flocks.

हिंदी:
यह पक्षी अकेले रहना पसंद करता है और झुंड नहीं बनाता है।
भोजन तलाशते समय शरीर को ऊपर-नीचे हिलाते हुए कीचड़ में चोंच डालकर कीड़े खोजता है।
दिन के समय घास के घने झुरमुटों में शांत बैठा रहता है और भोर में सक्रिय होता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

A strictly non-breeding winter migrant to the plains of eastern Uttar Pradesh. Birds arrive from high-latitude breeding territories in northern Europe and Siberia during October and November, remaining through the winter months. Departs northward again by late March or early April (Baker 1929).

Breeding occurs across subarctic peat bogs, wet tundra, and mossy fens in northern Eurasia. No breeding or nesting takes place anywhere in the Gangetic plain or Uttar Pradesh.

हिंदी:
पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश में यह केवल शीतकाल में अक्टूबर से अप्रैल के मध्य दिखाई देता है।
यह भारत में घोंसला नहीं बनाता; इसका प्रजनन उत्तरी यूरेशिया और साइबेरिया के दलदलों में होता है।
वसंत ऋतु की शुरुआत में मार्च-अप्रैल तक यह अपने उत्तरी प्रजनन क्षेत्रों की ओर लौट जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Forages on moist substrates and shallow waterlogged mud. Diet consists primarily of benthic and littoral invertebrates extracted by deep probing:

  • Small aquatic insects, midge larvae, and water beetles
  • Freshwater oligochaete worms and small annelids extracted from wetland silt
  • Small freshwater molluscs and occasional seeds of marsh plants ingested incidentally
हिंदी:
कीचड़ और गीली मिट्टी के भीतर मौजूद छोटे कीड़े-मकोड़े, केंचुए और लार्वा इसका मुख्य भोजन हैं।
यह अपनी संवेदनशील चोंच को दलदल में चुभोकर अकशेरुकी जीवों को तलाशता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Susceptible to predation by wetland raptors and terrestrial carnivores that hunt along marsh margins. Documented predators in Gangetic wetland habitats include marsh harriers, falcons, owls, and small mammalian carnivores such as the golden jackal and jungle cat. Cryptic plumage and freezing behaviour serve as the primary defence against aerial raptors.

हिंदी:
दलदली इलाकों में रहने वाले शिकारी पक्षी जैसे दलदली शिकरा और उल्लू इसका शिकार करते हैं।
सियार और जंगली बिल्ली भी घास में छिपे पक्षी को पकड़ने का प्रयास करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Provides ecological services by feeding on aquatic invertebrate larvae, including midge larvae in waterlogged paddy fields and drainage depressions. It causes no damage to crops or agricultural produce.

Historically hunted as a game bird by wildfowlers across Gangetic floodplains. Today, all wild birds in India are strictly protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, and hunting or trapping is prohibited by law.

हिंदी:
यह धान के खेतों और जलमग्न नालियों में हानिकारक कीटों के लार्वा खाकर किसानों की मदद करता है।
यह वन्यजीव संरक्षण अधिनियम 1972 के तहत संरक्षित है और इसका शिकार करना गैरकानूनी है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds across northern Eurasia from Scandinavia eastwards across Siberia to the Kolyma River; winters south to western Europe, the Mediterranean, northern Africa, and southern Asia (Baker 1929).

In India: Winter visitor across northern and central India, occurring through the Gangetic plains, Assam, and southwards sparingly to the peninsula (Baker 1929; Blanford 1898).

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter migrant across lowland wetlands, jheels, and river floodplains of the plain.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a scarce winter visitor to well-vegetated marshy margins and waterlogged paddy tracts.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Morten Thrane Brünnich in 1764 as Scolopax minima in Ornithologia Borealis, p. 49; type locality: Christiansø, Denmark. Later transferred to the monotypic genus Lymnocryptes Kaup, 1829 (treated as Lymnocryptes minima by Baker 1929 and Gallinago gallinula by Blanford 1898).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the family Scolopacidae within the order Charadriiformes, occupying a distinct basal lineage among the snipes and woodcocks.

Phylogenetic notes. Distinct from true snipes of the genus Gallinago in having only 12 rectrices, a sternum with two posterior notches on each side, an iridescent plumage gloss, and distinct vocal display behaviour.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) by BirdLife International (2025) due to its extremely large geographic breeding range and global population size.
