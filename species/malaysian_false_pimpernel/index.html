मलेशियाई फाल्स पिम्परनेल / Malaysian False Pimpernel

Torenia crustacea (L.) Cham. & Schltdl. — Linderniaceae

मलेशियाई फाल्स पिम्परनेल — यह धान के खेतों की मेड़ों, दलदली किनारों और नम बगीचों में उगने वाला एक बहुत ही नाजुक और छोटा पौधा है। इसके तने चार कोनों वाले होते हैं और जमीन पर फैलकर बढ़ते हैं। इसके छोटे दो-होंठ वाले फूल हल्के नीले या बैंगनी रंग के होते हैं, जिनका निचला होंठ तीन भागों में बंटा होता है।

Field tip: Look along wet paddy bunds for a tiny prostrate herb with square stems, paired toothed leaves, and small two-lipped purplish flowers.

हिंदी: नम क्यारियों में चौकोर तने, आमने-सामने दाँतेदार पत्तियाँ और छोटे नीले-बैंगनी दो-होंठ वाले फूल से इसे पहचानें।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

A tiny, delicate annual herb of damp and marshy habitats. Root system fibrous and shallow, spreading in waterlogged mud. Stems slender, diffusely branched from the base, procumbent, often rooting at the lower nodes, quadrangular with distinct angles, glabrous or sparsely puberulous along the ridges.

Leaves opposite, simple, shortly petiolate; blade ovate, broadly ovate or elliptic, acute or obtuse at the apex, rounded or cuneate at the base, margins shallowly serrate or crenate-dentate, glabrous or sparsely hispidulous along the veins, pale green below.

Flowers solitary in leaf axils or forming loose terminal subracemes on slender, stiff, erect pedicels. Calyx tubular-campanulate, five-ribbed and five-toothed, green, divided to about one-third of its length, scarcely or slightly enlarged in fruit. Corolla bilabiate, pale violet, purplish or bluish-white; tube cylindrical; upper lip erect, broad, emarginate; lower lip spreading, three-lobed, with a small yellow spot in the throat. Stamens four, didynamous, lower pair with curved appendiculate filaments.

Fruit an oblong-ellipsoid or ovoid bivalved capsule, equalling or slightly shorter than the persistent calyx. Seeds numerous, minute, ellipsoid, pale yellow, with fine surface alveolations.

हिंदी: पौधा नाजुक और छोटा होता है। तना चार कोनों वाला होता है। पत्तियाँ छोटी और अंडाकार होती हैं। फूल हल्के बैंगनी रंग के और गले में पीले धब्बे वाले होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A widespread native tropical and subtropical wetland herb common across tropical Asia, including the Gangetic plains. It thrives in high humidity and wet soils, carpeting paddy field embankments, muddy seasonal pools, seepage drains, and shady sandy riverbanks during and after the monsoon.

The plant flowers abundantly under warm, humid conditions. Its small flowers are visited by tiny solitary bees and flies, but autonomous self-pollination readily occurs if insect visits are scarce. It plays an ecological role in binding surface mud on wet agricultural bunds and along seasonal waterways.

हिंदी: यह पौधा नमी और दलदल वाली मिट्टी में अच्छी तरह बढ़ता है। धान के खेतों की मेड़ों पर यह मिट्टी को बांधकर रखने में मदद करता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seeds germinate with the arrival of the monsoon rains in July and August. Plants develop quickly in water-saturated soil, branching diffusely across the mud surface.

Flowering begins in August and continues through the post-monsoon months into December and January. Tiny capsules ripen within a few weeks of flowering, releasing abundant minute seeds into the receding water or mud. As the soil dries out completely in February and March, the plants die back, leaving dormant seeds that withstand the dry summer months.

हिंदी: अगस्त में बारिश के समय यह उगता है और दिसंबर तक फूल-फल देता रहता है। फरवरी-मार्च में मिट्टी सूखने पर पौधा समाप्त हो जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

Micro-pollinators including tiny halictid bees, syrphid flies, and small beetles visit the blossoms for nectar.

In wet agricultural habitats, it is commonly associated with wetland herbs such as comb rungia, prostrate wild petunia, and water snowflake. Small frogs and water beetles seek shelter under its damp trailing stems.

हिंदी: इसके सूक्ष्म फूलों पर छोटी मक्खियाँ आती हैं। मेंढक और छोटे जलीय कीड़े इसके गीले तनों के नीचे शरण लेते हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

No major pests are reported for this non-crop wetland herb, though damping-off fungi may attack crowded seedlings in stagnant warm water.

हिंदी: इस पौधे पर कोई गंभीर कीट नहीं लगते, कभी-कभी अत्यधिक पानी जमा होने से तना सड़ सकता है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

In the paddy-growing areas of eastern Uttar Pradesh, it is regarded as a harmless or minor non-competitive weed of rice field bunds and drainage channels. Because it stays low to the ground and thrives on wet edges rather than within dense crop stands, it rarely competes significantly with rice.

हिंदी: यह धान की मेड़ों पर उगने वाला एक हानिरहित पौधा है जो मुख्य फसल को कोई खास नुकसान नहीं पहुँचाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native across tropical and subtropical Asia, northern Australia, and parts of tropical Africa; naturalized in the Neotropics.

In India: Native and common throughout the wetter parts of India, from the Gangetic plains to the Western Ghats and North-East.

In Uttar Pradesh: Distributed along river basins, wetland borders, and irrigated lowlands throughout the central and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected along wet lowlands, oxbow marshes, and irrigated paddy bunds throughout the region.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Capraria crustacea by Carl Linnaeus in 1767 in Mantissa Plantarum, page 87. Transferred to Torenia by Ludolf von Chamisso and Diederich von Schlechtendal in 1827 in Linnaea, volume 2, page 570.

Subspecies. Often treated in older Indian works under the synonyms Vandellia crustacea (L.) Benth. (Duthie 1911; Hooker 1884) or Lindernia crustacea (L.) F.Muell. Monotypic in our region.

Family and order placement. Placed in family Linderniaceae, order Lamiales. Formerly included in Scrophulariaceae before molecular studies established Linderniaceae as a distinct family.

Phylogenetic notes. Morphologically intermediate between Lindernia and Torenia, but modern phylogenetic classifications place it in Torenia based on calyx and floral characteristics.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) on the IUCN Red List due to its immense geographic range, abundance in wetland habitats, and lack of significant threats.
