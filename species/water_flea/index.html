पानी का पिस्सू / Water Flea

Daphnia O.F. Müller, 1785 — Daphniidae

पानी का पिस्सू (डैफ्निया) — तालाब की सबसे छोटी और सबसे आम मेहनत-कश मछली, बस आधा मिलीमीटर बड़ी, पारदर्शी जैसे कांच की, जिसे नंगी आँख से मुश्किल से देख सको। पानी में अरबों-खरबों होती है और हरी, खेत के मेड़ का शैवाल और बैक्टीरिया खाती है। ये सब छोटी मछलियों और कीड़ों का भोजन है और इसके ज़रिये ही तालाब की पूरी ख़ुराक की श्रृंखला चलती है। दुनिया की प्रयोगशालाओं में विकास और आनुवंशिकता सीखने के लिए सबसे ज़्यादा इसी से सीखा जाता है। इसकी बहुत सारी जातियाँ हैं, जिन्हें अलग करने के लिए माइक्रोस्कोप लगता है।

Field tip: Extremely small, transparent creatures visible in still water when the water is held up to the light. A hand lens or simple microscope reveals the large eye and beating legs. In a jar of pond water, hundreds may be visible. The only way to identify the species is with a microscope, which is why this profile treats the genus.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बहुत छोटा, पारदर्शी, आधा मिलीमीटर। तालाब के पानी में दिखता है प्रकाश में। एक बड़ी आँख, पंख जैसी टाँगें, डोलती रहती है। जाति अलग करने के लिए माइक्रोस्कोप ज़रूरी है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (plains water fleas, genus Daphnia):

MeasurementRange
Body length0.5–3 mm
Head length0.2–0.8 mm
Large antennae length0.3–1.5 mm
Eggs per brood10–100
Generations per season10–20 in warm water

Body: The body is enclosed in a transparent carapace (shell-like covering) that is folded over the back and sides. The carapace is smooth and has no spines except in some species where seasonal forms bear defensive spines. The head is large and contains the single, large, dark eye. Below the head is the mouth.

Appendages: Two pairs of antennae: the first pair is longer and branched, used for swimming and sensory reception; the second pair is smaller and sensory. Six pairs of legs (thoracic appendages) lie beneath the body and are leaf-like, used both for swimming and for filtering food from the water. A short telson (tail-like structure) at the abdomen bears claws or spines used for gripping.

Reproduction: Females are larger than males. Females can reproduce without males (asexual reproduction) under normal conditions, producing clones that grow rapidly. Males appear only in autumn and winter, triggering sexual reproduction and the production of resting eggs (spores) that survive the dry season.

हिंदी: शरीर स्वच्छ आवरण में, पीठ पर घुमा हुआ। सिर बड़ा, एक बड़ी आँख। दो जोड़ी एंटीना — बड़ी जोड़ी तैरने की, छोटी संवेदना की। छह जोड़ी पत्ती जैसी टाँगें, खाना छानने में मदद। मादा आमतौर पर नर के बिना बच्चे दे सकती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Daphnia are the foundation of freshwater food webs. Each individual is a tireless filter-feeder, beating its leaf-like legs in a rhythm that draws water through the space between the legs and the body. Fine setae (hairs) on the legs trap microscopic algae, bacteria and organic detritus. The water enters through a gap at the head and exits through an opening in the carapace. An individual can filter its own body weight in water every few minutes.

Living at the mercy of temperature and water chemistry, daphnia respond to changing conditions in reproduction. In warm months with abundant food, females reproduce asexually, making identical clones that mature in days and begin reproducing at once. In autumn, when day length shortens and temperature drops, males appear, females produce resting eggs, and the population goes dormant. In temporary ponds that dry in summer, the resting eggs are the only survival stage; when the pond refills with monsoon rain, the eggs hatch and the population explodes.

They are food for small fish (Zebrafish, Mola Carplet), large zooplankton, dragonfly nymphs, and water birds. A single water flea eaten by a small fish provides calories that are passed up the food chain.

Different species have subtle differences in size, shape and setae that require a microscope to distinguish; they also have different food preferences and tolerance for salinity and pollution. Daphnia pulex and D. magna are the laboratory strains used worldwide in research.

हिंदी: पानी को छानती है लगातार। सूक्ष्म शैवाल, बैक्टीरिया, गाद खाती है। गरमी में मादा अकेली बच्चे देती है, कुछ दिन में बड़े हो जाते हैं। सर्दी में नर बनते हैं, बीजाणु बनते हैं। सूखे के बाद बारिश में बीजाणु निकल आते हैं। छोटी मछली, ड्रैगनफ्लाई, पक्षी इसे खाते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Algae: chlorophytes (green algae), diatoms, blue-green algae (cyanobacteria).
  • Bacteria: heterotrophic bacteria.
  • Detritus: particulate organic matter from decaying plants and animals.
  • Pollen and spores: occasional in seasonal blooms.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Fish: Zebrafish, Mola Carplet, Mozambique Tilapia, Stinging Catfish larvae.
  • Aquatic insects: Dragonfly nymphs, large water bugs, aquatic beetles.
  • Zooplankton: Larger copepods and other filter-feeding zooplankton.
  • Water birds: Indian Pond Heron, ducks, which filter or stab for zooplankton.
  • Parasites: Bacteria, fungi and protozoan parasites that reduce populations.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Research. Daphnia are among the most studied organisms in developmental biology, toxicology and evolution. Their rapid reproduction, transparency and genetic tractability make them ideal for laboratory work. Daphnia magna and D. pulex have been completely sequenced and are used in toxicology testing and in studying ageing. The genus is a model for understanding life-history evolution and the response to environmental stress.

Bioindicator. The presence, absence and community composition of daphnia in a pond reflect water quality. Pollution, acidification and algal blooms change the species present.

Aquarium fish food. Cultured daphnia are sold as fish food in aquarium shops and are grown in home aquaria for feeding fish.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use: none recorded for the region. Not eaten or used by people.

Today. Abundant in every pond; an invisible but essential component of every freshwater ecosystem.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The daphnia fauna of the Gangetic plain was catalogued in the 1970s but is not updated. A current survey of daphnia species composition in different pond types (clean, polluted, temporarily flooded) would document changes in diversity and provide a baseline for monitoring water quality. A hand lens and simple guidance can enable a school-level survey.

हिंदी: दुनिया की प्रयोगशालाओं में विकास, ज़हर परीक्षण, उम्र बढ़ने का अध्ययन सबसे ज़्यादा इसीसे होता है। पानी की सफ़ाई का सूचक। मछली की खिचड़ी। गाँव के तालाब में हर जगह।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Cosmopolitan in freshwater systems on every continent except Antarctica; the genus originated in the Holarctic region.

In India: Throughout; abundant in all freshwater ecosystems.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout; ubiquitous in ponds and wetlands.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in all still-water habitats; likely very abundant.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. What is the abundance of daphnia (count per mL of water) in five different ponds in your block, and how does it correlate with water clarity? / पाँच अलग तालाबों में पानी की सफ़ाई और डैफ्निया की संख्या का संबंध? — Collect water samples; count under a hand lens.
  2. Are male daphnia present in the ponds in winter, and when do they first appear? — Examine samples from November to March.
  3. What happens to the daphnia population in a pond during the dry season? — Sample the same pond monthly from March to October.
  4. Does the daphnia community change after the monsoon in a pond that has been dry? — Compare pre- and post-monsoon samples.
  5. Can daphnia from a clean pond survive in water from a polluted pond? — Culture clean-water daphnia in polluted water and observe survival.

Field rule: A tiny, transparent crustacean with a single large eye, beating leaf-like legs, and jerky swimming is a daphnia; the genus is identified by eye, but species requires a microscope.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. The genus Daphnia was established by O.F. Müller in 1785 (Entomostraca) to group the small water fleas with a characteristic morphology. Dozens of species have since been described and redescribed; the taxonomy remains unsettled.

Subspecies. The genus is divided into subgenera and species complexes that reflect evolutionary history, but the Indian fauna is poorly studied.

Family and order placement. Daphniidae, the daphnia family; order Cladocera (the branched-antenna crustaceans). Daphniidae is the most abundant genus in the order in freshwater.

Phylogenetic notes. Daphnia underwent a recent diversification, probably in the Quaternary; molecular phylogenies suggest multiple colonisations of Indian freshwater from different source regions.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. The genus is hyper-abundant and unlikely to be at risk globally, though individual species may be locally extinct or endangered due to habitat loss and pollution.
