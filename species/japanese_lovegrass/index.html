जापानी लव घास / Japanese Lovegrass

Eragrostis tenella (L.) P.Beauv. ex Roem. & Schult. — Poaceae

जापानी लव घास — उत्तर भारत के खेतों, बगीचों और रास्तों के किनारे उगने वाली एक अत्यंत कोमल, नाजुक और सुंदर छोटी मौसमी घास, जिसे 'भौनी' भी कहा जाता है। बरसात के बाद इसमें धुएँ या बारीक जाली जैसे हल्के, फूले हुए और हवा में लहराने वाले पुष्पगुच्छ निकलते हैं। इसके सूक्ष्म दानों को छोटी चिड़ियाँ और चींटियाँ बड़े चाव से बटोरती हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitDelicate tufted annual grass 10–50 cm tall with slender erect or ascending culms (Bor 1960)
CulmsVery slender, wiry, glabrous, branching freely from the lower nodes (Bor 1960)
LeavesLinear, narrow, 3–15 cm long, 2–5 mm wide, glabrous, tapering to a fine point (Duthie 1888)
LiguleA dense line of fine white ciliate hairs at the sheath mouth (Bor 1960)
InflorescenceOpen, oblong or pyramidal feathery panicle 5–15 cm long with spreading capillary branches (Bor 1960)
SpikeletsMinute, oblong, 1.5–3.0 mm long, 4–8 flowered; paleas prominently ciliate on keels (Bor 1960)

Field tip: Look along garden paths and sandy field borders in October for tiny, wiry grass tufts topped by cloud-like, feathery panicles that shimmer like mist in early morning light.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: छोटी और नाजुक घास, पत्ती के जोड़ पर बारीक सफेद बाल, और धुएँ जैसी हल्की-फुल्की बालियाँ जिनमें बहुत छोटे-छोटे दाने लटकते हैं।

Morphology शारीरिक संरचना

MeasurementRange

Culms and habit: Culms densely tufted, slender, glabrous, geniculately ascending or erect, branching profusely from lower nodes. Nodes glabrous, purplish or pale green.

Leaf blades and sheaths: Sheaths compressed, smooth, shorter than internodes, bearing prominent tufts of long white hairs at the collar. Leaf blades flat, soft, linear-acuminate, glabrous or sparsely pilose near the base.

Inflorescence and spikelets: Panicle delicate, open, effuse or contracted, oblong to pyramidal; branches solitary, capillary, spreading horizontally or ascending, bearing long white hairs in the axils. Spikelets minute, strongly compressed, 4–8 flowered, greenish or purplish. Paleas persistent, keels pectinately ciliate with long stiff spreading hairs.

हिंदी: तने बहुत पतले और तारों जैसे होते हैं। पत्तियों की जड़ पर सफेद बालों का गुच्छा होता है। फूल की डालियाँ बहुत बारीक और चारों तरफ फैली होती हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार एवं पारिस्थितिकी

Eragrostis tenella is an opportunistic annual pioneer adapted to disturbed alluvium, garden beds, and cultivated crop margins. It thrives in light sandy loams where competition from robust perennial sod-formers has been temporarily cleared.

Its rapid life cycle enables it to germinate, grow, and set seed within a brief monsoon window. The effuse, capillary branches vibrate in light breezes, releasing micro-pollen into the air. The tiny caryopses fall into soil fissures and are collected in large numbers by harvester ants, which transport them to subterranean chambers and assist in localized seed dispersal.

हिंदी: यह पौधा खाली पड़ी जमीन और जुते हुए खेतों में बहुत जल्दी उगता है। हवा के हल्के झोंकों से इसका पराग बिखरता है और जमीन पर गिरे बीजों को चींटियाँ अपने बिलों में ले जाती हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र एवं ऋतुचक्र

PeriodPhenological Event
June–JulySeeds germinate rapidly with the onset of the monsoon rains
August–SeptemberTillers branch quickly and delicate feathery panicles emerge
October–NovemberProfuse flowering and seed shedding occur across sandy paths and fields
December–MayPlants dry out completely and die, leaving persistent seed banks in the soil

Seed dormancy breaks during the intense dry summer heat, ensuring synchronized monsoon emergence upon seasonal wetting.

हिंदी: बरसात शुरू होते ही इसके बीज उगते हैं। भादों-क्वांर में फूल आते हैं और जाड़े की शुरुआत में बीज गिराकर पौधा सूख जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबद्ध प्रजातियाँ एवं पारस्परिक क्रियाएं

  • Granivores: Silverbills, munias, and house sparrows feed on the tiny fallen seeds.
  • Harvester Ants: Seed-harvesting ants transport and bury seeds across village paths.
  • Weed Associates: Grows alongside Doob Grass, Nutgrass, and Jungle Rice in disturbed ground.
हिंदी: छोटी चिड़ियाँ और मुनिया इसके बारीक बीजों को चुनती हैं और दूब घास के साथ यह बगीचों में आम उगती है।

Pests and Diseases कीट एवं रोग

  • Leaf spot caused by Curvularia species occasionally appears on foliage in humid weather.
  • Damping-off fungi attack dense stands of young germinating seedlings during continuous rainfall.
  • Smut fungi rarely infect developing spikelets.
हिंदी: लगातार बारिश के मौसम में कभी-कभी छोटे पौधे गलने लगते हैं और पत्तियों पर फफूंद आ जाती है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि एवं मानवीय उपयोगिता

Pastoral and Garden Relevance:

  • Minor pasture forage. Readily grazed by cattle, sheep, and goats while green, but contributes modest biomass due to its delicate size.
  • Garden weed. A ubiquitous, easily weeded annual weed of vegetable nursery beds, lawns, and flower borders.
  • Erosion binding. Rapidly forms a delicate protective soil cover on newly exposed alluvial heaps and roadside cuts.
  • Traditional medicine. Folk records describe using whole-plant infusions as soothing washes for eye irritation and skin inflammation. This is a record of traditional use, not medical advice.

Today. It is recognized as an ecologically harmless native annual grass that provides late-monsoon seed forage for declining farmland bird species.

हिंदी: कोमल होने के कारण मवेशी और बकरियां इसे आसानी से चरती हैं। बगीचों में यह खरपतवार के रूप में उगती है लेकिन आसानी से उखाड़ी जा सकती है। इसके बीज छोटी चिड़ियों का पेट भरते हैं।

Distribution भौगोलिक वितरण

Global range: Native across tropical and subtropical Africa and Asia; widely naturalised throughout the warm regions of the world.

In India: Abundant across all states in plains and lower hill tracts from the sub-Himalayas to Cape Comorin (Bor 1960).

In Uttar Pradesh: Extremely common in gardens, waste ground, and cultivated soils of the Gangetic plain (Duthie 1888).

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected on village footpaths, open field borders, and sandy alluvium.

हिंदी: यह पूरे भारत और विश्व के उष्णकटिबंधीय भागों के खेतों और बगीचों में पाई जाने वाली सामान्य घास है।

Field rule: Delicate annual grass with wiry culms, prominent long white hairs at the leaf sheath mouth, and effuse feathery panicles bearing minute spikelets with ciliate-keeled paleas identifies Eragrostis tenella.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गिकी एवं वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Poa tenella by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum, vol. 1, p. 69. Transferred to Eragrostis by Palisot de Beauvois, published by J.J. Roemer and J.A. Schultes in 1817 in Systema Vegetabilium, vol. 2, p. 576.

Subspecies. Morphological varieties based on panicle compactness are recognized in older floras (e.g., var. plumosa), though modern taxonomists consider them continuous variants of Eragrostis tenella.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the grass family Poaceae, subfamily Chloridoideae, tribe Eragrostideae, in the order Poales.

Phylogenetic notes. Eragrostis is a large, morphologically diverse C4 genus of roughly four hundred species, characterized by multi-flowered laterally flattened spikelets.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated in the master expansion list; it is a ubiquitous, abundant annual grass across tropical Asia and Africa.
