बाकला / Broad Bean

Vicia faba L. — Fabaceae

बाकला — सर्दी की दाल और सब्जी, जिसके बड़े, सपाट दानों को भून कर या उबालकर खाया जाता है। पौधा बहुत मजबूत, कद में बड़ा, सफेद फूल काले धब्बों के साथ। फलियाँ लंबी, मोटी, और भीतर से सफेद। हल्की दाल, पौष्टिक, सर्दी में खूब उपज। मिट्टी को भी अच्छा करती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
PlantErect herbaceous, sturdy, 40–80 cm tall, unbranched or few branches
LeavesPinnately compound, alternate, leaflets oblong, 2–5 cm; prominent tendrils
FlowersLarge, white with black spots/patches, in short axillary clusters
PodsLarge, thick-walled, 10–15 cm long, green becoming dark brown at maturity, monopodial
SeedsLarge, flattened, kidney-shaped, 12–15 mm, tan to brown with hilum line

Field tip: A tall, sturdy winter legume with large white flowers bearing black spots, distinctive thick pods, and large seeds. The plant is upright and unbranched, unlike trailing cowpea varieties. The pods are much larger than other pulses grown in eastern UP.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for Vicia faba in northern India):

Stem: Erect, sturdy, angular, unbranched or with few lateral branches, bright green, becoming woody with age.

Leaves: Alternate, pinnately compound, with a terminal tendril (not a leaflet). Leaflets oblong, 2–5 cm long, entire, blue-green. The compound leaf may have 4–8 leaflets plus the terminal tendril.

Flowers: Large and showy, 2.0–2.5 cm diameter, white or cream-coloured with distinctive black or dark purple patches or blotches on the petals and standard. The flowers are fragrant. Borne in short axillary clusters (2–4 flowers per cluster).

Pods: Large, thick-walled, 10–15 cm long, 2.5–3.5 cm wide, initially green becoming grey-brown at maturity and finally dark brown. The pods are leathery and monopodial (single pod per cluster, unlike clusters in other Vicia species).

Seeds: Large, flattened, kidney-shaped, 12–15 mm long, tan, brown, or grey-brown, with a white or pale hilum line.

हिंदी: सीधा, मजबूत पौधा, कोई तना नहीं। बड़ी पत्तियाँ, सूंघतार। बड़े, सफेद फूल काली पच्छियों सहित। बहुत बड़ी दालें।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Broad bean is a cool-season legume native to the Mediterranean region and domesticated across Eurasia. The plant is a nitrogen-fixer and is valued in crop rotations for soil improvement. It is well-suited to the rabi season in northern India and is relatively hardy. The plant is moderately tolerant of cold and can withstand the winter chill of eastern UP. Major pests are few; the plant is relatively disease-resistant.

हिंदी: भूमध्यसागरीय पौधा, पर अब सर्दी के मौसम में सारे देश में लगाया जाता है। ठंड सह सकता है। मिट्टी को नाइट्रोजन देता है। कीटों से कम प्रभावित।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Crop rotation: Follows Paddy; precedes Wheat.
  • Garden companions: Grown with Black Gram, Chickpea, and other rabi pulses.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Bruchids (seed beetles): occasionally damage stored dry seeds; manage with dry storage.
  • Rust and leaf spots: in humid conditions; usually minor.
  • Root rot in waterlogged soil: ensure good drainage.
  • Generally hardy; few serious problems in eastern UP.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Vegetable use:

Young, tender pods (10 cm long) can be harvested and cooked as a vegetable, whole or in pieces. The immature seeds (beans) inside the young pod are eaten; they are mild and tender. This use is less common in eastern UP but known.

Pulse use:

Mature, dry seeds are cooked as a dal (curry) or boiled whole. The large seeds require longer cooking (45–60 minutes) compared to smaller pulses. The dal is hearty and nutritious, though considered less fine than black gram or lentil. The taste is earthy and mild.

Nutritional value:

Seeds are rich in protein, fibre, minerals (especially iron), and B vitamins. The pulse is highly nutritious and particularly valuable for vegetarians.

Soil improvement:

The nitrogen-fixing capability improves soil fertility for subsequent crops. Broad bean is often grown specifically for this soil-building purpose.

Cultivation advantages:

Hardy, requires minimal external inputs, seeds are relatively cheap, high nutritional value, profitable return to farmers, and excellent for soil improvement.

हिंदी: कच्ची फलियाँ सब्जी की तरह भी खाई जा सकती हैं, पर सर्दी के क्षेत्रों में दाल ज़्यादा आम है। दाल पौष्टिक, पर पकाने में समय लगता है। मिट्टी को बहुत ठीक करती है।

Expected as a cultivated rabi pulse throughout Basti district, visible from October to February. The tall, sturdy plant with large pods is distinctive in fields and vegetable plots. The mature dry seeds are sold in all markets as a pulse.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the Mediterranean and Near East; cultivated worldwide in temperate and cool subtropical regions.

In India: Widely cultivated in cooler regions and at higher elevations; especially common in the Indo-Gangetic plain during winter.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated throughout; an important rabi pulse, particularly in cooler areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Cultivated in fields and gardens; common in local markets during the rabi season.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum as Vicia faba. The specific epithet faba is the Latin name for the bean.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Fabaceae, order Fabales. Genus Vicia comprises about 150 species, mostly in temperate regions of Eurasia and Africa.

Phylogenetic notes. Vicia faba is distinguished from other Vicia species by its large seeds and large, thick pods with black-spotted flowers.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; crop status.
