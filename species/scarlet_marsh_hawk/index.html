स्कारलेट मार्श हॉक व्याध पतंग / Scarlet Marsh Hawk

Aethriamanta brevipennis (Rambur, 1842) — Libellulidae

स्कारलेट मार्श हॉक व्याध पतंग — बारहमासी तालाबों, तालों और जलकुंभी से भरे दलदली जलाशयों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी और चमकीले लाल रंग की व्याध पतंग (dragonfly) है। इसके वयस्क नर का पेट छोटा, चौड़ा और सिंदूरी लाल होता है, जबकि पिछले पंखों के आधार पर एक छोटा गहरा लाल-सुनहरा धब्बा होता है। यह जलकुंभी के पत्तों और सूखी टहनियों के सबसे ऊपरी सिरे पर धूप सेकती है और उड़ते हुए मच्छरों का शिकार करती है।

Field tip: Look along quiet edges of weed-choked village tanks and oxbow lakes. A small, compact dragonfly with a bright scarlet-red abdomen and a small amber-red patch at the base of each hindwing, perching upright on exposed reed tips, is the Scarlet Marsh Hawk.

हिंदी: छोटे आकार की इस पतंग का पेट चमकीला सिंदूरी लाल और पिछले पंखों की जड़ में लाल-भूरा धब्बा होता है। यह अक्सर जलकुंभी या नरकट की नोक पर सीधी बैठी नजर आती है।

Adult description:

Aethriamanta brevipennis is a distinctly compact and stout libellulid displaying marked sexual dimorphism (Fraser 1936). In mature males, the head has a dark reddish-brown frons and vertex; the compound eyes meet broadly on top of the head, coloured dark reddish-brown dorsally and olivaceous-grey ventrally (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is dark golden-brown to chocolate-brown, covered in fine brownish pubescence, without bright coloured antehumeral stripes (Fraser 1936).

The male abdomen is noticeably short, slightly depressed, and bright scarlet-red on all segments from 1 to 10 (Subramanian 2005). The terminal anal appendages are short, pointed, and bright red (Fraser 1936).

Females are olivaceous-yellow to golden-brown (Fraser 1936). The synthorax is warm yellowish-brown, and the abdomen is stout, dull golden-yellow, marked with a prominent continuous black mid-dorsal stripe extending across segments 2 through 9 (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the wings are hyaline; the forewings are completely clear, while the hindwings bear a distinct dark golden-amber or reddish-brown basal patch extending to the first antenodal vein (Fraser 1936). The pterostigma is dark reddish-brown, framed between thickened black veins (Subramanian 2005).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is compact, flattened, and greenish-brown or dark mottled silt-coloured, clinging to submerged aquatic weeds such as Hydrilla and roots of Eichhornia. The body surface is smooth with short lateral abdominal spines, providing effective concealment among fine aquatic foliage.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 17 से 20 मिमी लंबा और गाढ़ा सिंदूरी लाल होता है। मादा सुनहरी-भूरे रंग की होती है जिसके पेट पर काली धारी होती है। इसका जलीय निम्फ जलकुंभी की जड़ों में छिपता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Scarlet Marsh Hawk is a classic percher of standing, heavily vegetated freshwater wetlands (Subramanian 2005). It inhabits perennial village ponds, weed-choked oxbow depressions, and stagnant marshy canals where dense floating vegetation flourishes (Fraser 1936).

Adults are active during warm sunny hours (Subramanian 2005). Males maintain small territories of three to six metres around dense patches of emergent lotus or reed stems. They perch characteristically on the highest, most exposed tip of a dry stem, holding their wings slightly depressed and angled downward against the midday heat (Fraser 1936). When an insect flies within range, the male darts forward in a swift aerial sally, captures the prey, and immediately returns to the same perch to feed (Subramanian 2005). Mating occurs in a brief tandem flight over the water. Oviposition is exophytic: the female hovers low over open pockets of water among floating weeds and taps the surface repeatedly to wash free fertilized eggs while the male hovers closely above to guard her from rivals.

हिंदी: यह पतंग तालाबों के ऊपर नरकट या सूखी टहनियों की नोक पर बैठती है। उड़ते हुए कीड़े को झपटकर पकड़ती है और फिर उसी टहनी पर लौट आती है। मादा पानी में तैरती घास के पास अंडे देती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian alluvial wetlands across the agricultural year (Fraser 1936; Subramanian 2005):

October–November (Post-monsoon)Peak adult abundanceMaximum numbers perching on emergent reeds and lotus stalks

February–April (Spring / Zaid)Emergence of spring broodFresh adults emerge with warming temperatures around permanent tanks (Fraser 1936)

Females lay several hundred tiny oval eggs directly into shallow water among dense vegetation. The eggs hatch after a few days into predatory nymphs. Naiads develop over several weeks through repeated molts, stalking small aquatic organisms among submerged plants. When mature, the nymph crawls up an emergent grass blade at sunrise, shedding its exuvia to take flight as a teneral dragonfly.

हिंदी: बरसात के बाद यह पतंग सबसे अधिक दिखाई देती है। मादा पानी में अंडे देती है, जो कुछ दिनों में फूटकर निम्फ बनते हैं। निम्फ पानी की घास में कई हफ्ते बिताकर वयस्क बनते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Aethriamanta brevipennis is a predatory carnivore in both aquatic larval and aerial adult stages (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small flying dipterans, including Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Anopheles culicifacies (Malaria Mosquito).
  • Small agricultural pests over waterlogged margins, including Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper) and Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper).
  • Midges, small damselflies, winged ants, and small aquatic moths.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito wrigglers, chironomid midge larvae, small aquatic beetles, and tiny cladoceran crustaceans.
हिंदी: वयस्क पतंग हवा में उड़ते हुए मच्छरों, धान के फुदकों और छोटी मक्खियों को खाती है। पानी में इसका निम्फ मच्छरों के अंडों और लार्वा को खाकर उन्हें खत्म करता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural enemies in Gangetic plain freshwater ecosystems:

  • Birds: Insectivorous wetland birds, such as kingfishers, bee-eaters, and pond herons, capture perched and flying dragonflies.
  • Predatory dragonflies: Larger libellulids and aeshnids occasionally prey upon smaller Aethriamanta adults.
  • Spiders: Wetland orb-weavers snare adults flying near shoreline reeds.
  • Fish: Murrels, catfishes, and barbs prey on aquatic naiads among weed beds.
हिंदी: बगुले, किंगफिशर और अन्य शिकारी पक्षी वयस्क पतंग का शिकार करते हैं। पानी में रहने वाली मछलियाँ इसके जलीय निम्फ को खा जाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Biological pest control: The Scarlet Marsh Hawk provides valuable ecosystem services in agricultural landscapes (Subramanian 2005). Because it perches near drainage canals, village tanks, and rice field edges, it hunts significant numbers of sap-feeding leafhoppers and planthoppers that damage paddy crops.

Vector mosquito suppression: Both adults and aquatic naiads actively feed on Culex and Anopheles mosquitoes, aiding natural suppression of disease vectors in rural villages (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal, cultural, or domestic uses are recorded.

Today: Recognized by naturalists as a bioindicator of standing freshwater wetlands rich in aquatic vegetation.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Investigating how the clearance of native macrophyte beds impacts Aethriamanta brevipennis larval density.

हिंदी: यह पतंग धान की फसलों को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले फुदकों और बीमारी फैलाने वाले मच्छरों को खाकर किसानों की मदद करती है। इसका कोई औषधीय उपयोग नहीं है।

The Scarlet Marsh Hawk is expected in eastern Uttar Pradesh across perennial village tanks, oxbow marshes, and sluggish canals with dense floating vegetation. Look along sunny pond borders and emergent reeds from August through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A clear photograph showing the compact scarlet abdomen and small dark amber patch at the base of the hindwing, submitted via the About page, would provide the first confirmed record for the project area.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widely distributed across South and Southeast Asia, recorded from India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia (Fraser 1936).

In India: Found across peninsular India, the Western Ghats, central India, the Gangetic plain, and the northeastern states (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from perennial lakes, oxbow waterbodies, and canal systems across central and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a breeding resident in weed-rich ponds, oxbow lakes, and marshy lowlands.

हिंदी: यह भारत और दक्षिण-पूर्व एशिया में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के तालाबों और दलदली इलाकों में यह एक स्थायी रूप से रहने वाली प्रजाति है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Pierre Rambur in 1842 as Libellula brevipennis in Histoire naturelle des insectes. Névroptères, page 114. Type locality: "Bombay" (Mumbai, India).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized across its range.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae. The genus Aethriamanta Kirby, 1889, includes small, compact skimmers characterized by reduced antenodal venation and brightly coloured bodies.

Phylogenetic notes. Aethriamanta brevipennis is closely allied to Aethriamanta gracilis, but easily distinguished by its open venation with only six antenodal cross-veins on the forewing and broader abdomen (Fraser 1936).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) globally on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an adaptable wetland species that maintains secure populations throughout South Asia.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Aethriamanta brevipennis है और यह Libellulidae परिवार से संबंधित है। यह प्रजाति पूरे दक्षिण एशिया में सुरक्षित है।
