बड़ा ओकब्लू / Large Oakblue

Arhopala amantes (Hewitson, 1862) — Lycaenidae

बड़ा ओकब्लू — अवध के वनों और नदी तटीय वृक्ष कुंजों में पाई जाने वाली एक बड़ी लाइकेनिड तितली। नर के पंख ऊपर से गहरे चमकीले बैंगनी-नीले और नीचे से बादामी घेरे वाले भूरे धब्बों से सजे होते हैं। यह तितली पेड़ों के ऊंचे छतरी वाले भाग में उड़ती है और इसकी इल्लियां लाल चींटियों की देखरेख में पलती हैं।

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पेड़ों की ऊंची डालियों पर बैठने वाली बड़ी नीली तितली। उड़ते समय पंखों का ऊपरी हिस्सा गहरा चमकीला बैंगनी-नीला दिखता है और बैठने पर नीचे का हिस्सा भूरा-चित्तीदार दिखाई देता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

The sexes differ noticeably in the extent of the upperside blue coloration. In males, the metallic purple-blue suffusion occupies almost the entire wing surface up to a narrow black terminal thread. In females, the ground colour is dark fuscous brown, with the brilliant blue area restricted to the wing bases and central discal zones.

The underside pattern provides essential field identification characters: both fore and hind wings are deep chocolate-brown crossed by broken, sinuous darker brown spots, each encircled by a fine pale ochraceous or whitish border. The tornal region of the hindwing is adorned with a cluster of vibrant metallic green and blue scales surrounding a small black spot, directly above a slender filamentous tail on the lower margin.

Wing venation and structural characters align closely with other large oriental members of the genus. The antennae are relatively short and gently clubbed, and the palpi are ascending with a pointed terminal joint. When viewed against dark bark, the irregular underside marbling breaks up the outline of the resting insect completely.

हिंदी: नर तितली के पंख ऊपर से लगभग पूरे चमकीले बैंगनी-नीले होते हैं, जबकि मादा में किनारे चौड़े भूरे होते हैं। पंखों का निचला हिस्सा गहरा कत्थई होता है जिस पर सफेद घेरे वाले धब्बे और पिछले पंख के सिरे पर एक बारीक पूंछ होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Arhopala amantes is predominantly an arboreal butterfly that spends most of its daylight hours high in tree canopies along riverine woodland corridors. Adults are strong and rapid fliers, often perching on sunlit foliage at canopy edges where males defend territories against rival butterflies.

When settled with wings folded upright, the butterfly presents an effective dead-leaf camouflage, blending invisibly against bark, lichen, and dried leaves. Flight occurs in short, swift darts before the insect abruptly disappears into the foliage. Resting posture is typically head-downwards on vertical twigs.

Adults occasionally descend during the cooler morning hours or late afternoon to feed on damp sand, mineral-rich mud along seasonal streams, or fermented tree sap leaking from trunk fissures. They avoid the intense heat of midday by resting within shaded interior branches.

During territorial contests, rival males perform vertical looping flights that ascend several metres above the canopy line before both individuals break off and dive back into the foliage.

हिंदी: यह तितली ज्यादातर समय ऊंचे पेड़ों की चोटियों पर बिताती है और बहुत तेज उड़ती है। जब यह पंख बंद करके बैठती है तो सूखे पत्ते जैसी दिखती है। सुबह के समय यह कभी-कभी जमीन पर आकर गीली मिट्टी या तने के रस का सेवन करती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

The life cycle of the Large Oakblue is closely tied to its symbiotic relationship with arboreal weaver ants (Oecophylla smaragdina). Females deposit pale, disc-shaped eggs singly or in small clusters on the tender young leaves and shoot tips of host trees.

The larvae are woodlouse-shaped (onisciform), flattened, and pale green to brownish pink, possessing specialised dorsal nectar organs and tentacle organs that exude sweet secretions. Weaver ants continuously attend the caterpillars, sheltering them in leafy nests during the day and guarding them aggressively against parasitoid wasps and predators. In return, the ants harvest nutrient-rich honeydew from the larva.

Pupation occurs inside a shelter of leaves bound together with ant silk, and the emerging adult must leave the ant nest promptly before its scales dry and harden. Multiple broods occur throughout the warm months, peaking in late monsoon.

During winter months, the rate of larval growth slows considerably, and individuals may overwinter as late-instar larvae or pupae within weaver ant shelters, emerging in early spring when fresh foliage develops.

हिंदी: इसकी इल्लियां चपटी होती हैं और लाल चींटियों के घोंसलों में सुरक्षित रहती हैं। इल्लियां चींटियों को मीठा रस देती हैं और बदले में चींटियां उनकी दुश्मनों से हिफाजत करती हैं। प्यूपा भी चींटियों के पत्तों वाले घोंसले के भीतर ही बनता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Wynter-Blyth (1957), as listed by Funet, records the larva on Terminalia catappa, T. paniculata, T. tomentosa, Lagerstroemia microcarpa, L. flos-reginae (now Jarul, L. speciosa) and Hopea jucunda.

Females select vigorously growing young shoots attended by active weaver ants for oviposition. The caterpillars feed strictly on tender juvenile leaves, avoiding tough mature foliage.

Adults feed primarily on extrafloral nectaries, leaking tree sap from bark fissures, honeydew dropped by homopteran bugs, and moist mineral soils along stream beds. They visit flowering forest trees and shrubs, taking nectar in brief sips.

हिंदी: इसकी इल्लियां टर्मिनेलिया (जंगली बादाम) और जारुल जैसे पेड़ों की कोमल पत्तियों को खाती हैं। वयस्क तितलियां फूलों के रस, पेड़ों के तने से रिसने वाले गोंद-रस और गीली मिट्टी से खनिज तत्व चूसती हैं।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Larvae benefit from formidable protection by host weaver ants, but unguarded caterpillars are attacked by parasitoid wasps and tachinid flies. Spiders lurking among canopy leaves ambush resting caterpillars and pupae when ants are absent.

Adults flying in open canopy glades are captured by insectivorous birds, while crab spiders and praying mantises ambush butterflies visiting flowers or tree sap leaks.

Lizards hunting along thick tree limbs also stalk basking adults when they settle to sun themselves during early morning hours.

हिंदी: इल्लियों की रखवाली चींटियां करती हैं, लेकिन अकेले छूट जाने पर परजीवी ततैये और मक्खियां उन्हें मार देती हैं। उड़ती हुई तितलियों को शिकारी चिड़ियां, मकड़ियां और सूंड़े पकड़ लेते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

The Large Oakblue has no adverse impact on agricultural crops or plantation forestry, as its larval feeding is confined to wild and planted native broadleaf trees without causing defoliation.

Because of its strict reliance on mature trees and weaver ant colonies, it serves as a sensitive bioindicator of undisturbed woodland canopies and healthy riverine ecosystems.

Conservation of old-growth riparian trees directly supports populations of this butterfly by preserving required host foliage and nest sites for its ant symbionts.

हिंदी: यह तितली फसलों को कोई नुकसान नहीं पहुंचाती। यह स्वस्थ जंगलों और पुराने पेड़ों की निशानी है। नदी किनारे पुराने पेड़ों की रक्षा करने से इस तितली और इसके साथ रहने वाली चींटियों का जीवन सुरक्षित रहता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Distributed across the Indian subcontinent from the lower Himalayas through central and southern India, eastwards to Assam, Myanmar, and Sri Lanka.

In India: Recorded widely across deciduous and semi-evergreen tracts in northern, central, and peninsular India (Varshney & Smetacek 2015).

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from terai forest tracts, riparian woodlands, and alluvial plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in riverine tree corridors and moist woodland patches supporting native combretaceous trees.

हिंदी: यह तितली भारत, श्रीलंका और म्यांमार के वनों में फैली हुई है। उत्तर प्रदेश में यह तराई के जंगलों और मैदानी इलाकों के नदी तटीय वृक्षों में पाई जाती है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Arhopala amantes was originally described as Amblypodia amantes by William Chapman Hewitson in 1862 (Specimen of a Catalogue of Lycaenidae in the British Museum, p. 13). The species was later transferred to the genus Arhopala Boisduval.

Subspecies. Monotypic in northern India; peninsular populations are often referred to regional subspecies.

Family and order placement. Placed in the lycaenid subfamily Theclinae, tribe Arhopalini. Members of this tribe are characterized by dense metallic blue or purple uppersides, complexly patterned brown undersides, and obligate or facultative associations with ants.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated globally by the IUCN Red List. It is a widespread woodland lycaenid without identifiable extinction risks.

हिंदी: इस तितली का वैज्ञानिक विवरण 1862 में विलियम हेविटसन ने दिया था। यह लाइकेनिडे कुल की सदस्य है और इसे वैश्विक स्तर पर कोई खतरा नहीं है।
