चंडूल / Crested Lark

Galerida cristata (Linnaeus, 1758) — Alaudidae

चंडूल — खुले मैदानों की भूरी चिड़िया, सिर पर खड़ी पंखों की कलगी (क्रेस्ट) के साथ, जो गाँव की पगडंडियों, खेतों और बंजर जमीन पर साल भर दिखती है। जमीन पर दौड़ता है, कभी उड़ता भी है। तेज आवाज़ में गीत गाता है, खासकर सुबह-शाम।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeMedium lark; 17–19 cm total length
CrestProminent, pointed, erectile crest on crown
PlumageBuff-brown overall; pale underparts; streaked back
TailBuff with dark central feathers; outer feathers tipped white
BillYellowish, slightly curved, relatively long
LegsLong, pale yellow
BehaviourWalks on ground; runs when disturbed; sings from ground or low perch

Field tip: An open-country bird with a distinctive pointed crest that is often raised. Brown and buff plumage blends with soil. Walks on ground; sings from low perches or while flying. Common on roadsides, in fields, and wasteland.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें:
सिर पर खड़ी कलगी (सबसे भरोसेमंद पहचान)। भूरा-खाकी रंग। लंबी टाँगें। खुले मैदानों में जमीन पर चलता है। तेज आवाज़।

Size: 17–19 cm long; medium-sized lark.

Plumage: Crown and crest buff-brown; pointed crest feathers are longer and darker. Back buff-brown with darker streaking. Wings buff with darker edges. Underparts pale buff-white; breast lightly streaked. Throat white. Tail buff with dark central feathers; outer feathers white-tipped.

Bill: Yellowish, relatively long and slightly curved; adapted for picking seeds and probing ground.

Legs: Long, pale yellow; adapted for running on ground.

Eyes: Dark brown.

Sexes: Similar; males may have slightly longer crest.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Song: Loud, clear, melodious series of phrases. Often delivered from low perch or in flight. Song repertoire rich with mimicry.
  2. Contact call: Soft chirping notes.
  3. Alarm call: Sharp prip or tirr notes when disturbed.
हिंदी: सिर पर खड़ी कलगी भूरी और लंबी। पीठ भूरी-खाकी, गहरी धारियों के साथ। पेट हल्का, पीला-सफेद। टाँगें लंबी, पीली। गीत तेज़, साफ़।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Foraging ecology: Ground forager; walks and runs on open ground searching for food. Picks seeds and insects from soil surface or plants. Feeds on:

  • Seeds of grasses and herbs
  • Small insects (grasshoppers, beetles, ants)
  • Plant matter (buds, leaves)
  • Spiders and small arthropods

Habitat use: Strongly associated with open country: grasslands, agricultural fields, stubble fields, wastelands, and roadsides. Avoids dense forests; prefers areas with sparse vegetation.

Social behaviour: Solitary or in pairs during breeding; may gather in loose flocks post-breeding. Less gregarious than some lark species.

Breeding: Resident breeder; can breed multiple times per year (2–3 broods) depending on rainfall and food availability.

Singing behaviour: Males sing prominently to defend territories and attract mates; song given from ground perches or during low aerial display flights.

Activity: Diurnal; most active at dawn and late afternoon.

हिंदी: खुले मैदानों में जमीन पर दौड़ता है। बीज और कीड़े खोजता है। अकेले या जोड़े में। तेज़ आवाज़ में गाता है, खासकर सुबह। साल में कई बार अंडे देता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Breeding: Breeds year-round with intensity peaks in cooler months (October–March). Multiple broods per year.

Nesting: Nest a simple scrape on ground lined with grass. Clutch 3–5 eggs; incubation 11–13 days; fledging 9–10 days.

Territorial behaviour: Males defend territories vigorously; singing prominent in breeding season.

Moult: Gradual moult in post-breeding season.

Activity: Year-round resident; more vocal in breeding season.

हिंदी: साल भर यहीं रहता है। ठंडे महीनों में (अक्टूबर-मार्च) ज्यादा गीत गाता है। जमीन पर छोटे घोंसले बनाता है। 3-5 अंडे देता है। तेजी से बढ़ता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Kestrels, larger shrikes, crows
  • Mammalian predators: Mongoose, jackal (occasionally), snakes (at nest)
  • Nest predators: Crows, snakes
हिंदी: शिकारी चिड़ियों का शिकार। सांपों और जंगली जानवरों से घोंसलों को खतरा। जमीन पर घोंसला बनाना खतरनाक है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected status: Crested Lark is protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, Schedule IV. All wild birds listed are protected; hunting and trapping are prohibited.

Agricultural value: Mixed significance: feeds on agricultural crop seeds (wasteful) but also on insect pests. In balance, pest-control benefit likely outweighs seed loss.

Common and adaptable: One of the most common larks in eastern UP; adapted to human-dominated landscapes and agricultural areas.

Cultural value: Featured in folk songs and poetry in many Indian languages; singing ability appreciated.

हिंदी: यह पक्षी विधि अधिनियम 1972 की सुरक्षित है। फसलों के बीजों को खाता है पर कीटों को भी खा कर फायदा पहुँचाता है। लोकगीतों में इसका जिक्र है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: North Africa, Middle East, southern Europe, and across Asia. Multiple subspecies; G. c. cristata (European), G. c. leautungensis (Indian subcontinent).

In India: Distributed across Indian subcontinent in suitable open country habitat.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common and widespread in agricultural and open areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Very common in grasslands, agricultural fields, stubble fields, and roadsides throughout the region.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  2. What is the diet composition in different seasons, and what percentage of dietary intake consists of agricultural crop seeds versus wild seeds and insects?
  3. Does crest size or morphology correlate with male territory quality or breeding success?
  4. क्या जलवायु परिवर्तन से इस चिड़िया के गीत या व्यवहार में कोई बदलाव आया है?
  5. What is the breeding success rate when nests are in open fields versus areas with shelter, and how does predation pressure affect survival? (A child can monitor nest sites and track fledging success.)
  6. क्या शहरीकरण (urbanization) से इस पक्षी की आबादी प्रभावित हो रही है?

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1758 from Europe. Type locality Europe.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies recognized geographically. G. c. leautungensis occurs in Indian subcontinent and is distinguishable from European G. c. cristata.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Alaudidae (larks). Genus Galerida contains 3–4 species, all with prominent crests.

Phylogenetic notes. Part of larger Alaudidae radiation in Africa and Eurasia; sister genus to Alauda (skylarks).

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC). Widespread and abundant; population stable. Adaptable to human-modified landscapes. Not threatened by direct persecution or habitat loss.
