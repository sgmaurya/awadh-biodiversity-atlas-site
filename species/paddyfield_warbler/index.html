पैडीफ़ील्ड वार्बलर / Paddyfield Warbler

Acrocephalus agricola (Hume, 1863) — Acrocephalidae

पैडीफ़ील्ड वार्बलर — यह छोटी, भूरी गायन चिड़िया सर्दियों में पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के धान के खेतों और नरकुल के बिस्तरों में आती है। यह बहुत साधारण दिखने वाली चिड़िया है, जिसे दूसरी छोटी भूरी चिड़ियों से अलग करना कठिन है, पर इसकी मधुर गायन आवाज़ से पहचानी जा सकती है। यह अकेली रहती है और घने नरकुल में छिपी रहती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmall warbler; 13–14 cm total length
PlumagePlain buff-brown above; whitish below
CrownBrown; slightly darker than back
UnderpartsBuff-white; throat paler
BillStraight, thin, pointed; dark tip
TailBrown; slightly graduated
BehaviourSkulking; stays in reed beds; sings from reeds
VocalisationsMelodious warbling song; sharp calls

Field tip: A small, plainly coloured brown warbler in reed beds and paddy fields. Very similar to other reed warblers; song is the best identification feature. Song is musical and continuous, given from exposed reed tops or in flight. Often difficult to see well but regularly heard singing.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Total length130–140 mm

Tail55–65 mm
Bill12–14 mm
Tarsus18–21 mm

Size: 13–14 cm long; wingspan 17–22 cm. Weight 11–15 g.

Plumage: Above warm buff-brown or olive-brown. Crown slightly darker brown than back. Back and rump uniform brown. Wings and tail darker brown with no contrasting patches. Underparts buff-white or cream, with buff-brown wash on flanks. Undertail coverts buff.

Bill: Thin, pointed, dark with pale base — typical warbler bill for gleaning.

Legs and feet: Thin, pale brown or flesh-coloured.

Eyes: Dark iris.

Sexes: Similar; no obvious sexual dimorphism.

Vocalisations

  1. Song: Loud, continuous, melodious warbling with repeated phrases and trills. Often given from exposed reed tops or in flight. Unmistakable and diagnostic once learned.
  2. Call: Harsh churr or sharp zit notes given when flushed or alarmed.
  3. Contact calls: Soft chirps.
हिंदी: मधुर, सुंदर गायन करता है। "ज़िप-ज़िप" की आवाज़। लगातार गाता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Reed specialist: Entirely dependent on reed beds, paddy fields with tall vegetation, and tall grass areas. Gleaning insectivore moving through reed stems.

Feeding: Insects and insect larvae gleaned from reed stems and leaves. Tiny invertebrates and occasionally berries.

Feeding behaviour: Active gleaner moving through reed stems, picking insects and spiders. Often hops vertically up reed stems.

Skulking behaviour: Extremely secretive; stays deep in reed cover. Often heard but difficult to see. Cryptic coloration aids concealment.

Territorial: Establish individual wintering territories. Males sing throughout winter, especially at dawn and dusk.

Roosting: In reed beds or tall grass.

हिंदी: नरकुल में छिपा रहता है। कीड़े को धीरे से चुनता है। ख़ूब गाता है। अकेले में रहता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Winter visitor: Absent during breeding season; arrives in eastern UP from October and remains through February–March.

Breeding range: Central Asia and southern Russia, from Kazakhstan to western Siberia. Breeds April–August in extensive reed beds.

Migration: Long-distance migrant.

Arrival and departure: Vanguard arrivals October (early); main arrival October–November; peak December–February; departure February–March.

Wintering: Solitary on individual reed beds; often returns to same territories year after year.

Moult: Complete moult occurs before arrival; non-breeding plumage.

हिंदी: अक्टूबर में आता है, मार्च तक रहता है। मध्य एशिया में प्रजनन करता है। अकेले में सर्दी काटता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Primary food items:

  • Aquatic and reed insects
  • Insect larvae from reed stems
  • Small spiders
  • Tiny invertebrates
  • Occasionally small berries

Gleaned from reed stems, leaves and among reed tufts during methodical foraging.

हिंदी: नरकुल के कीड़े खाता है। छोटे जानवर निकालता है। बहुत कम खाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Raptors: Small falcons may predate on warblers
  • Snakes: Water snakes may hunt in reed beds
  • Corvids: Crows occasionally predate small birds

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pest control: Assists in control of reed insects and small invertebrates by gleaning from rice paddies and reed beds.

Rice paddy association: Named for its association with paddy fields during winter stay. No conflict with farming.

Legal status: Protected under the Wildlife Protection Act 1972. Populations stable across wintering range. Breeding populations stable but dependent on maintaining reed bed habitat.

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

GBIF holds published records from these districts. Expected from October to March in reed beds, rice paddies and tall grass areas. More often heard (continuous singing) than seen. Most vocal at dawn and dusk, especially males establishing and defending territories. Numbers depend on water availability and reed bed extent. The first local record would be a photograph showing the plain plumage and typical reed warbler posture, with place and date, submitted through the About page.

हिंदी: अक्टूबर से मार्च तक नरकुल में देखे जाने की उम्मीद है। आवाज़ से पहचानना आसान है। पहला रिकॉर्ड फोटोग्राफ के साथ तारीख़ और जगह दिखानी चाहिए।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds in Central Asia and southern Russia; winters across the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia.

In India: Winter visitor to the plains (October–March); occurs across the Indian peninsula.

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter visitor and passage migrant; recorded state-wide in suitable habitat.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Winter visitor from October to March; expected in reed beds and rice paddies.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Hume in 1863 in Journal, Asiatic Society of Bengal as Acrocephalus agricola. Type locality is India.

Subspecies. Monotypic; only subspecies is A. a. agricola.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Acrocephalidae (warblers and reed warblers). Genus Acrocephalus comprises reed warblers of Europe, Asia, Africa and Australasia.

Phylogenetic notes. Closely related to other reed warblers including Blyth's reed warbler and common reed warbler.

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern globally. Widespread on wintering grounds. Breeding populations stable on Central Asian steppes.
