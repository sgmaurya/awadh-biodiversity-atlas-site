चितकबरा भेंडुक / Marbled Toad

Duttaphrynus stomaticus (Lütken, 1864) — Bufonidae

चितकबरा भेंडुक — गाँव के दूसरे भेंडुक, जो आम भेंडुक से चिकना, हल्का, धब्बेदार और सूखी ज़मीन का ज़्यादा शौक़ीन है। पहली बरसात की रात में ज़मीन से निकलकर गड्ढों और नालियों में इकट्ठा होता है, जहाँ नर की लंबी, घरघराती आवाज़ रात भर गूँजती है। दो हफ़्ते में अंडे, टैडपोल और छोटे भेंडुकों की फ़ौज। यह ज़हरीला नहीं काटता; इसकी त्वचा की ग्रंथियाँ कुत्ते और साँप को मुँह बिगाड़ने भर का कड़वा रस देती हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size50–85 mm; females larger
ColourPale olive, grey or sandy, marbled with darker blotches; often a pale line down the back; belly whitish
HeadWithout the bony black ridges that run along the eyes of the common toad; the skin of the head smooth
SkinWarty but less so than the common toad; the parotoid glands behind the eyes oval, moderate
ToesWebbed about halfway; a pale tubercle on the heel
CallA long, rattling, pulsed trill, lower and more drawn-out than the common toad's

Field tip: Two toads share the villages of the plains. The common toad is darker and rougher with black-rimmed ridges on the head; this one is paler, smoother, marbled, and without ridges. In a rain pool in June the long rattling calls are its; the common toad's are shorter and higher.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: आम भेंडुक गहरा, खुरदुरा, सिर पर काली हड्डी की लकीरों वाला; यह हल्का, चिकना, धब्बेदार, सिर पर लकीर नहीं। जून की बरसात के गड्ढे में लंबी घरघराती आवाज़ इसकी है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (northern Indian populations):

MeasurementRange
Snout–vent length, male50–70 mm
Snout–vent length, female60–85 mm
Parotoid gland length8–14 mm
Egg diameter1.5 mm, in strings
Eggs per female3,000–8,000
Tadpole, full grown20–25 mm

Head and skin: The snout short and rounded, the tympanum (eardrum) distinct, the cranial crests absent or very faint. Dorsal skin with small rounded warts, each sometimes tipped with a dark spine in breeding males; the parotoid glands secrete bufotoxins.

Limbs: Hind limbs short, for hopping and walking rather than leaping; the toes with a serrated fringe in some populations.

Male: Smaller, with a single vocal sac under the throat and dark nuptial pads on the inner fingers in the breeding season.

हिंदी: थूथन छोटा गोल, कान का पर्दा साफ़ दिखता है, सिर पर हड्डी की लकीरें नहीं। त्वचा पर छोटे गोल मस्से; कानों के पीछे की ग्रंथियाँ कड़वा ज़हर बनाती हैं। टाँगें छोटी — यह उछलता कम, चलता ज़्यादा है। नर छोटा, गले के नीचे एक थैली, बरसात में अँगुलियों पर काले गद्दे।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The dry-country toad of the subcontinent, from the Indus valley to the Gangetic plain, more tolerant of heat and drought than its commoner cousin and more often found away from houses, on bunds and in fields. It spends the dry months buried in loose soil or under stones and emerges with the first heavy rain, when the whole population moves to the nearest temporary water in a night. Breeding is explosive: pools that will be dry in a month are full of eggs within days, and the tadpoles grow fast to beat the drying.

Adults eat whatever moves at night and fits: beetles, crickets, termite swarms, ants, caterpillars; a toad in a vegetable plot is a free pest controller. Snakes (Checkered Keelback, Indian Rat Snake), Indian Pond Heron, owls and Bengal Monitor take them despite the skin toxins; dogs learn.

हिंदी: उपमहाद्वीप का सूखी ज़मीन का भेंडुक, सिंधु से गंगा के मैदान तक; आम भेंडुक से ज़्यादा गरमी और सूखा सहता है और घरों से दूर मेड़ों और खेतों में मिलता है। सूखे महीनों में मिट्टी में दबा रहता है, पहली भारी बारिश में पूरी आबादी एक रात में पास के गड्ढों में पहुँच जाती है। प्रजनन तूफ़ानी: महीने भर में सूखने वाले गड्ढे दिनों में अंडों से भर जाते हैं। सब्ज़ी की क्यारी का भेंडुक मुफ़्त का कीट-नियंत्रक है।

हिंदी: जून-जुलाई में निकलना और प्रजनन; अंडे दो-तीन दिन, टैडपोल तीन-पाँच हफ़्ते; जुलाई-अगस्त में नाखून भर के हज़ारों भेंडुक पानी से निकलते हैं; नवंबर से मई तक ज़मीन में।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Adults: Two-Spotted Cricket, African Mole Cricket, beetles, Mound-building Termite swarms, ants, caterpillars including Tobacco Cutworm, and anything else small that moves at night.
  • Tadpoles: algae such as Water Silk, and the film on the pool bottom.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Snakes: Checkered Keelback, Indian Rat Snake, Common Krait at night.
  • Birds: Indian Pond Heron, Spotted Owlet, Black Kite, House Crow on toadlets.
  • Others: Bengal Monitor, Giant Water Bug on tadpoles and small toads, dragonfly nymphs on tadpoles.
  • Dogs: mouth one and spit it out; the toxin is a lesson, rarely fatal.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pest control. A toad eats its own weight in insects in a few nights; cutworms, crickets, beetles and termites in a kitchen garden go down where toads are left alone. Nothing need be done except not killing them and leaving a stone or a flowerpot to shelter under.

Harmless. It does not bite, does not spit, and its skin toxin affects only an animal that mouths it; wash hands after handling. The folk belief that toads cause warts is false everywhere it is held.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use: none recorded for the region.

Today. Common; declining locally where rain pools are filled, pesticides reach breeding water, and roads cross migration routes on the first wet nights.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The breeding ecology of plains toads is well described; the open local question is population: whether toads are as common on sprayed vegetable land as on unsprayed, which a season of night walks could answer.

हिंदी: एक भेंडुक कुछ रातों में अपने वज़न जितने कीड़े खाता है — कटवर्म, झींगुर, भृंग, दीमक। कुछ करना नहीं, बस मारना नहीं और छिपने को पत्थर या गमला छोड़ देना। काटता नहीं, थूकता नहीं; त्वचा का ज़हर सिर्फ़ मुँह में लेने वाले जानवर पर; छूने के बाद हाथ धो लें। भेंडुक से मस्से होने की बात झूठ है। गड्ढे भरने, कीटनाशक और पहली बरसात की रात की सड़कें इसे कम कर रहे हैं।

To be expected alongside the common toad across the district, perhaps more on open bunds and drier ground, and in every rain pool chorus of June and July. A photograph showing the smooth, ridgeless head is a confirmed record; a phone capture of the call is as good.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Iran and Afghanistan through Pakistan to northern and central India, Nepal and Bangladesh.

In India: The Indus and Gangetic plains, Rajasthan, central India and the Deccan.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout the plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected, with the common toad, in every village and field.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. Which of the two toads is in your yard? / आपके आँगन में कौन सा भेंडुक है? — Photograph the head: ridges or none.
  2. On which night of the monsoon does the first chorus start? — Note the date and the rain.
  3. How many days from eggs to toadlets in a rain pool? / गड्ढे में अंडे से छोटे भेंडुक तक कितने दिन? — Watch one pool daily.
  4. What does a toad under a torch eat in ten minutes? — Watch one hunt at a light.
  5. Are toads fewer in a sprayed vegetable plot? — Walk a sprayed and an unsprayed plot at night and count.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Christian Frederik Lütken in 1864 as Bufo stomaticus in Videnskabelige Meddelelser fra den Naturhistoriske Forening i Kjøbenhavn 1863: 305, from "Assam", probably in error for a drier locality. Placed in Duttaphrynus by Frost and colleagues in 2006 when the Asian toads were separated from Bufo.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised; populations vary in colour with soil.

Family and order placement. Bufonidae, the true toads; order Anura, class Amphibia. Duttaphrynus, named for the Indian herpetologist S.K. Dutta, holds about 30 South and South-east Asian toads.

Phylogenetic notes. Sister to a group of dry-country toads of the Middle East and Central Asia; the Indian population shows little genetic structure across the plains.

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern: wide range, large population, tolerant of disturbed habitats.
