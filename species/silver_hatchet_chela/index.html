सिल्वर हैचेट चेला मछली / Silver Hatchet Chela

Chela cachius (Hamilton, 1822) — Cyprinidae

सिल्वर हैचेट चेला मछली — नदियों, स्वच्छ नालों और दलदली तालों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, अत्यंत चपटी और कुल्हाड़ी (hatchet) के आकार की चमकीली चांदी जैसी देसी मछली है। इसका पेट गले से लेकर गुदा तक एक तेज चाकू जैसी धारदार बनावट वाला होता है। यह पानी की बिल्कुल ऊपरी सतह पर तैरती है और पानी पर गिरने वाले कीड़ों तथा मच्छरों के लार्वा को झपटकर खाती है। अपनी फुर्ती और चांदी जैसी चमक के कारण यह पानी में दूर से पहचानी जाती है।

Field tip: Look in slow, clear river shallows and oxbow lake edges. A small, paper-thin silvery fish swimming directly below the surface film, with a sharp belly keel starting right under the throat and an elongate filamentous pelvic fin ray, is the Silver Hatchet Chela.

हिंदी: यह छोटी मछली पतले सिक्के या कुल्हाड़ी जैसी चपटी होती है और चांदी जैसी चमकती है। इसके पेट की धार गले से शुरू होती है और इसके पेट के पंख से एक बारीक धागे जैसा तंतु निकलता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description:

Chela cachius exhibits a markedly deep, narrow, and strongly compressed body profile resembling a miniature hatchet (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal profile is nearly straight, while the ventral profile is deeply convex and terminates in an acute, knife-like (trenchant) keel extending continuously from the throat (isthmus) all the way to the vent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is small and pointed, with prominent lateral eyes (Hamilton 1822). The mouth is small, terminal, and directed obliquely upward; barbels are entirely absent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The pectoral fins are elongate and pointed, reaching backwards to the pelvic fin origin (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The pelvic fins are small, but the outer ray is uniquely prolonged into a delicate, trailing filamentous thread that often reaches the anterior rays of the anal fin (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The dorsal fin is short, containing 2 unbranched and 7–8 branched rays, inserted far back along the caudal third of the body (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin has a long base with 20 to 25 branched rays (Hamilton 1822). The caudal fin is deeply forked with equal pointed lobes. The lateral line is complete, containing 51 to 68 tiny scales, running along the lowest curve of the belly (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Living specimens are brilliant burnished silver with a translucent greenish-yellow back and pure silvery flanks (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). A shimmering electric-blue and golden longitudinal band extends along the middle of the flank from the gill cover to the caudal base (Hamilton 1822). All fins are translucent with pale yellowish bases, lacking dark spots or blotches.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 9.6 सेमी तक लंबा, बहुत चपटा और चांदी जैसा चमकदार होता है। पेट का निचला हिस्सा बहुत तेज धार जैसा होता है। इसके पेट के पंख से एक लंबा धागे जैसा कांटा निकलता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Silver Hatchet Chela is a surface-dwelling, pelagic schooling fish adapted to both flowing streams and standing floodplain wetlands (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It frequents clear to moderately turbid shallows of rivers, canals, oxbow lakes, and perennial village tanks.

Schools of twenty to fifty individuals swim constantly within the upper ten centimetres of the water column (Daniels 2002). The sharp ventral keel and streamlined profile minimize water resistance as they cruise the surface in search of food (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). When threatened by underwater predators, schools dart forward simultaneously or leap out of the water in dazzling silver arcs (Daniels 2002). They are sensitive to heavy organic pollution and require reasonably well-oxygenated surface water.

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की बिल्कुल ऊपरी सतह पर बड़े झुंडों में तैरती है। खतरा महसूस होने पर यह पानी से बाहर हवा में उछल जाती है। यह साफ और ऑक्सीजन वाले पानी में रहना पसंद करती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian alluvial waters across the agricultural year (Talwar & Jhingran 1991; Daniels 2002):

Breeding coincides with the southwest monsoon (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Rising river levels trigger courtship displays, with males circling females near the surface. Females deposit numerous non-adhesive or weakly adhesive eggs that scatter among submerged grasses and weeds. The eggs hatch after a few days, and the tiny larvae immediately take up surface-oriented positions among emergent vegetation, growing rapidly on rotifers and micro-crustaceans (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: मानसून में पानी बढ़ने पर यह मछली उथले किनारों की घास में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडों से बच्चे निकलकर पानी की सतह पर तैरने लगते हैं और तेजी से बड़े होते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Chela cachius is an active surface micro-carnivore and insectivore (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Mosquito wrigglers and pupae (Culex, Anopheles, and Aedes), plucked from the surface film (Daniels 2002).
  • Small terrestrial insects trapped in the surface tension, including winged ants, small beetles, aphids, and midges (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Aquatic insect larvae, including chironomid midges and mayfly nymphs.
  • Micro-crustaceans, including water fleas (Daphnia, Moina) and copepods.
  • Floating microscopic organic matter and fine filamentous algae browsed opportunistically.
हिंदी: यह पानी की सतह पर तैरने वाले मच्छरों के लार्वा, पानी में गिरने वाले कीड़े-मकोड़े, चींटियों और छोटे जलीय जीवों को खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Surface-hunting predatory fishes: Needlefish like Xenentodon cancila (Freshwater Garfish), small snakeheads like Channa punctata (Spotted Snakehead), and catfishes like Ompok bimaculatus (Pabda) strike from below.
  • Piscivorous birds: Common kingfishers, little egrets, and river terns prey heavily on schooling chela.
  • Water snakes: Keelbacks pursue schools among shoreline vegetation.
  • Predatory diving beetles: Target fry and fingerlings in vegetated shallows.
हिंदी: सुइया मछली, गिरई, पाबदा कैटफिश, बगुले और किंगफिशर पानी की सतह पर तैरने वाली इस मछली का शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Natural mosquito vector control: The Silver Hatchet Chela is an exceptional native larvivorous fish (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). By patrolling the topmost surface layer of water bodies where mosquito larvae breathe, it consumes substantial numbers of vector larvae, contributing significantly to natural disease suppression in rural agricultural landscapes (Daniels 2002).

Local food and aquarium trade: Harvested in large numbers by local fishing communities using cast nets and fine-mesh seine nets in the Ghaghara, Kuwano, and Rapti river basins. Consumed whole, fried or curried, it supplies vital calcium, phosphorus, and essential fatty acids to rural diets. It is also collected occasionally for the ornamental aquarium trade as an attractive schooling fish.

Traditional: Consumed as an everyday small fish in rural households along river corridors.

Today: Sold fresh in rural fish markets (haats) as an affordable, nutrient-rich small indigenous fish.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Evaluating stocking densities of Chela cachius in village irrigation ponds for biological mosquito suppression.

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की सतह पर रहकर मच्छरों के लार्वा को नष्ट करती है, जिससे मलेरिया और डेंगू की रोकथाम होती है। ग्रामीण लोग इसे भोजन के रूप में भी बहुत पसंद करते हैं।

The Silver Hatchet Chela is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in perennial rivers, clear flowing streams, oxbow lakes (tals), and major canal shallows. Look near the water surface in calm shallows and weed margins from August through January. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A specimen photograph showing the deep compressed silvery body, acute throat-to-vent keel, and filamentous pelvic fin ray, submitted via the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the project area.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South Asia, natively occurring in India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Myanmar (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Distributed throughout the major river systems of northern, central, and peninsular India, including the Indus, Ganges, Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, and Godavari basins (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, Gomti, and Ramganga river networks.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident native species in the Ghaghara and Kuwano river basins, oxbow lakes, and connecting drainage canals.

हिंदी: यह भारत, पाकिस्तान, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश की नदियों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में यह गंगा, घाघरा और कुआनो नदी प्रणालियों में सामान्य रूप से उपस्थित है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Cyprinus cachius in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 259. Type locality: River Ganges about the commencement of the delta.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized across its range.

Family and order placement. Order Cypriniformes, family Cyprinidae, subfamily Danioninae (or Chedrinae). The genus Chela Hamilton, 1822, comprises deep-bodied, keeled Asian minnows.

Phylogenetic notes. Chela cachius is the type species of the genus Chela. It is distinguished from allied genera like Laubuka and Salmostoma by its throat-to-vent keel, filamentous pelvic ray, and small scale size (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, stable wild populations throughout South Asian river systems.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Chela cachius है और यह Cyprinidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
