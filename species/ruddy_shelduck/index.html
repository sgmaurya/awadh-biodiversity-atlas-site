सुर्खाब / Ruddy Shelduck

Tadorna ferruginea (Pallas, 1764) — Anatidae

सुर्खाब — एक रंगीन, बड़ी सर्दियों की बत्तख जो अक्टूबर-नवंबर में मध्य एशिया से आती है और अप्रैल तक रहती है। नर का रंग गहरा नारंगी-लाल होता है, मादा हल्की। इसका नाम "चकवा" भी है, जो हिंदी साहित्य में प्रेम और विरह का प्रतीक है। घाघरा और राप्ती नदियों पर झुंड में देखी जा सकती है।

Field tip: A large, distinctive cinnamon-rufous duck with contrasting pale head and dark neck collar (males) — is a Ruddy Shelduck. Females are paler buff. Flies low with rapid wingbeats; often seen in pairs or flocks on large water bodies in winter.

Size: 58–67 cm long; wingspan 110–130 cm. Weight 900–1400 g (males heavier).

Plumage (male): Distinctive cinnamon-rufous overall. Head and neck pale buff or whitish; broad black collar around lower neck. Back and wings deeper cinnamon. Rump and tail black. White on upper wing coverts visible in flight; metallic green speculum.

Plumage (female): Paler buff-cinnamon overall. Head whitish; no clear black collar (sometimes faint). Wing pattern similar to male.

Bill: Dark grey or blackish; medium length and slightly flattened.

Legs and feet: Pink or reddish; long and stout.

Sexes: Dimorphic; males darker and more distinctly patterned.

Vocalisations. Vocal species; variety of calls:

  1. Contact call: A whistled whee-oo or piu-piu, given by males.
  2. Alarm call: Sharp, loud quacking when disturbed.
हिंदी: नर की तीक्ष्ण सीटी जैसी आवाज़। परिवार में ही गुणगान करता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Omnivorous feeder: Ruddy Shelducks are highly versatile, feeding on:

  • Aquatic grasses and vegetation
  • Grass and grain in agricultural fields (wheat, barley)
  • Aquatic invertebrates (insects, small crustaceans)
  • Seeds and tubers

Feeding behaviour: Dabble at water surface; upend in shallow water; graze on land. Both nocturnal and diurnal feeders.

Social behaviour: Moderately gregarious in winter; form flocks. Pairs maintain strong bonds; male stays with female throughout wintering.

Roosting: Often roost on open water or islands; some roosting in reed beds reported. Return to same roost sites nightly.

Territorial: Winter-resident birds may defend feeding territories locally, though not as strongly as breeding birds.

Predators: Eagles, marsh harriers, wild dogs may take adults. Chicks vulnerable to corvids and aquatic predators in breeding areas.

हिंदी: जोड़ों में या छोटे झुंड में रहता है। घास और अनाज दोनों खाता है। बहुत सावधान रहता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Winter visitor: Arrives October–November from breeding grounds; departs by April–May. Does not breed in India.

Breeding range: Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Mongolia, Tibet) and southern Russia. Breeds March–July at elevation 0–4000 m.

Migration: Breeds and nests in Central Asian wetlands; southward migration begins August–September. Arrives in Indian subcontinent October–November; remains through March–April.

Wintering: Concentrated at large lakes, river systems, and wetlands. Pairs remain together during wintering season.

Moult: Pre-basic moult before autumn migration; acquire fresh plumage in summer breeding grounds.

हिंदी: अक्टूबर में आता है, अप्रैल तक रहता है। जोड़ों में मध्य एशिया से सर्दियों के लिए आता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Marsh Harrier, Peregrine Falcon
  • Mammalian predators: Jackal, wild dog, fox, hyena
  • At nest (breeding grounds): Corvids, weasels, martens
  • Parasites: Waterfowl helminths
हिंदी: बड़े शिकारी चिड़ियाँ और शिकारी जानवर इसका शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Grain damage: Small flocks can cause minor damage to wheat and barley by gleaning in harvested and newly sown fields; generally less destructive than geese.

Cultural significance: Known as chakwa in Hindi literature, representing marital devotion and separation. Classical texts (Sanskrit and Hindi) use the bird symbolically. In Indian folklore, the chakwa and chakvi (male and female) are separated each night and reunite at dawn.

Hunting history: Historically hunted for food in some regions; now protected in India under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972.

हिंदी: हिंदी साहित्य में "चकवा" प्रेम और विरह का प्रतीक है। कभी-कभी खेतों को नुकसान पहुँचाता है, पर सुरक्षित है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Ruddy Shelducks are expected at the Ghaghra and Rapti river systems in eastern UP from October through April. Mishra et al. (2019) recorded this species at Bakhira Bird Sanctuary during October 2017–March 2018. Yashmita-Ulman & Singh (2025) recorded it at Parwati Arga from March 2024–February 2025, and Yashmita-Ulman & Singh (2022) recorded it in Ayodhya district wetlands. Published habitat and range information place this species in larger water bodies and river systems during winter months, with peak numbers in December–February.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds in Central Asia and Mongolia; winters across the Middle East, North Africa, and South Asia.

In India: Regular winter visitor to northern and central India, particularly in the northwestern and northeastern parts of the Gangetic plain.

In Uttar Pradesh: Regular winter visitor, with concentrations along major river systems.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Winter visitor to the Ghaghra and Rapti river systems. Recorded at Bakhira Bird Sanctuary and Parwati Arga. Present October–April, with highest numbers December–February.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Tadorna ferruginea was described by Peter Simon Pallas in 1764 in Reise durch verschiedene Provinzen des Russischen Reichs, volume 2, page 713, as the "rust-coloured duck" from Central Asia.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised; all populations belong to a single, panmictic species.

Family and order placement. Order Anseriformes; family Anatidae (ducks, geese, swans); genus Tadorna contains the shelducks, medium-sized dabbling ducks between "true ducks" and geese.

Phylogenetic notes. Tadorna ferruginea is one of seven Tadorna species; it occupies a basal position within the genus. Recent molecular phylogenetics places it sister to the South African shelducks.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC). Global population is large and stable. The species occurs in many protected areas and has benefited from wetland conservation in Central Asia and wintering grounds.
