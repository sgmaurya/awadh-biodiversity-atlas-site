कॉमन सार्जेंट तितली / Common Sergeant

Parathyma perius (Linnaeus, 1758) — Nymphalidae

कॉमन सार्जेंट तितली — यह काले और सफेद निशानों वाली एक सुंदर तितली है जिसकी निचली सतह सुनहरी-पीली होती है। इसके अगले पंखों पर सफेद पट्टी कई हिस्सों में बंटी होती है और पिछले पंख की सफेद पट्टी पर काले गोल बिंदुओं की एक कतार होती है। निचली सतह पर इन बिंदुओं के भीतर हल्के नीले रंग की किनारी होती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Wingspan60–76 mm (Bingham 1905, p. 316); 60–70 mm (Evans 1932)
Upperside groundBlack in male, blackish brown in female, with pure white markings (Bingham 1905, p. 315)
Forewing cell streakClavate discoidal streak twice divided, with a large cone-shaped spot beyond (Bingham 1905, p. 315; Evans 1932: divided into separate portions)
Hindwing key markPostdiscal white macular band inwardly margined by a series of round black dots (Bingham 1905, p. 315; Evans 1932)
UndersideGolden ochraceous yellow; white markings heavily margined with black; hindwing postdiscal band margined inwardly with pale blue bearing black spots (Bingham 1905, pp. 315–316)
BodyAbdomen transversely and narrowly barred with bluish white above; pure white beneath (Bingham 1905, p. 316)

Field tip: Look for the divided white streak in the forewing cell and the row of round black dots along the inner edge of the hindwing postdiscal white band, especially visible on the golden-yellow underside (Bingham 1905, pp. 315–316; Evans 1932).

हिंदी: इसके पंखों का फैलाव 60 से 76 मिमी होता है। काले पंखों पर सफेद निशान और निचली सुनहरी-पीली सतह पर पिछले पंख की सफेद पट्टी के भीतर काले बिंदुओं की कतार इसकी पहचान है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRangeSource
Expanse (male and female)60–76 mmBingham (1905, p. 316, as Athyma perius)
Expanse60–70 mmEvans (1932, as perius)

Bingham (1905, pp. 315–316) describes the adult as follows: "Upperside black (in the female blackish brown) with the following white markings:— Fore wing: a clavate discoidal streak, twice divided, a cone-shaped large spot beyond; a much curved discal macular band, the spots composing it in interspaces 1a, 1 and 2 broad, rectangular, in interspace 3 small, subtriangular, in interspace 4 circular or oval, in 5 and 6 elongate, shifted obliquely inwards; a postdiscal sinuous line of narrow transverse spots, and a subterminal line of obscure short lunules. Hind wing: the subbasal band continued subbasally across, broad and not macular, traversed only by the veins; a postdiscal macular band, inwardly margined by a series of round black dots, and a subterminal line of obscure lunules as on the fore wing. Underside golden ochraceous yellow; the white markings as on the upperside but heavily margined and defined with black; interspace 1a with subbasal and preapical patches in interspace 1 of fore wing fuliginous black: postdiscal band on hind wing margined inwardly with pale blue, bearing a superposed series of black spots, the pale blue extended along the veins crossing the band; both fore and hind wing with a prominent terminal narrow black band; the cilia white alternated with brown. Antennae black; head with a spot of golden ochraceous between the eyes; thorax with a band or two of bluish spots anteriorly and posteriorly; abdomen transversely and narrowly barred with bluish white; beneath, the palpi, thorax and abdomen pure white; in the female the abdomen has a double lateral row of minute black dots."

Larval Stage

Bingham (1905, p. 316) quotes de Nicéville on the larva ("Cylindrical, of equal thickness throughout its length; head larger than the following segment, black, thickly covered with obtuse castaneous spines; body pale green; beneath and legs castaneous, armed with a subdorsal series of long and a spiracular series of shorter spined castaneous tubercles") and pupa ("Brown, richly gilt, head ending in two points, thorax foliaceous").

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार इसकी ऊपरी सतह काली (मादा में काली-भूरी) होती है जिस पर शुद्ध सफेद निशान होते हैं। निचली सतह सुनहरी-पीली होती है और पिछले पंख की सफेद पट्टी के भीतरी किनारे पर हल्के नीले रंग के साथ काले बिंदुओं की कतार होती है।

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya: one photographed record from Lakhimpur Kheri on 10 December 2025, and two photographed records from Maharajganj on 18 May 2022 by one observer. Distribution वितरण

Global range: Bingham (1905, p. 316) gives: "Throughout the Himalayas; the hills of Central, Eastern and Western India; Southern India, but not, so far as is known, Ceylon; Burma; Tenasserim; extending to Siam and the Malay Peninsula."

In India: Evans (1932) gives "S. India — Simla — Burma"; Bingham (1905, p. 316) records it throughout the Himalayas and the hills of Central, Eastern, Western, and Southern India.

In Uttar Pradesh: GBIF holds one photographed record from Lakhimpur Kheri (10 December 2025) and two photographed records from Maharajganj (18 May 2022, one observer).

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार यह पूरे हिमालय और मध्य, पूर्वी, पश्चिमी व दक्षिण भारत की पहाड़ियों से बर्मा व मलय प्रायद्वीप तक पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में जीबीआइएफ पर लखीमपुर खीरी और महाराजगंज के अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Field rule: A black-and-white nymphalid with a divided white streak in the forewing cell and a row of round black dots along the inner margin of the hindwing postdiscal band (highlighted in pale blue on a golden-ochraceous underside) is Parathyma perius (Bingham 1905, pp. 315–316; Evans 1932).

हिंदी: काले-सफेद पंखों वाली तितली जिसके अगले पंख में बंटी हुई सफेद लकीर और निचले पिछले पंख पर सुनहरे-पीले रंग के बीच काले बिंदुओं की कतार हो, वह कॉमन सार्जेंट तितली है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Linnaeus, C. (1758). Systema Naturae, 10th ed., p. 471, as Papilio perius (cited in Bingham 1905, p. 315).

Subspecies. Not available in consulted sources (Evans 1932 treats it as perius, L.).

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae, genus Parathyma. Bingham (1905, p. 315) placed it in Athyma (entry 331).

Phylogenetic notes. Placed in Parathyma in GBIF, whereas Bingham (1905, p. 315) and Evans (1932) treated it as Athyma perius.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated on the IUCN Red List.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वैज्ञानिक वर्णन लिनियस (1758) ने Papilio perius के रूप में किया था। वर्तमान में इसे Parathyma वंश और Nymphalidae कुल में रखा जाता है।
