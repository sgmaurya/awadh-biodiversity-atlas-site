निर्गुंडी / Nirgundi

Vitex negundo L. — Lamiaceae

निर्गुंडी — खरपतवार की तरह उगने वाली झाड़ी, जिसके पत्तों की सुगंध तेज़ और अलग होती है। पत्तियाँ उँगलियों जैसी बँटी हुई, नीले-बैंगनी फूलों के गुच्छे। सूखी और बंजर ज़मीन पर भी पनपती है और दवाई में काम आती है। गाँव के पास सूखे खेतों और नालों में आम दिख जाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

The testCrush a leaf; it has a sharp, distinctive peppery smell

Field tip: Look in dry, degraded fields and along canal banks for a multi-stemmed shrub with palmately compound, strongly aromatic leaves. The violet flower spikes from May onward are distinctive; the dark berries ripen in late summer.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सूखी जमीन पर उगने वाली झाड़ी, पत्ती उँगलियों जैसी बँटी, नीले-बैंगनी फूलों के खड़े गुच्छे, और गहरे बेर। पत्ती को रगड़ने से तेज़ सुगंध आती है।

Stem and branches: Quadrangular, hairy when young, becoming smooth and greyish-brown with age. Aromatic when bruised.

Leaves: Opposite, palmately compound with 5 leaflets (rarely 3), all on a common petiole. Leaflets are lanceolate, toothed, with a prominent midrib. The leaf surface is slightly hairy and strongly aromatic.

Flowers: Small, sessile, four-lobed corolla, violet-blue or lilac, arranged in dense terminal spikes. Flowering is profuse during the monsoon.

Fruit: A small, hard drupe about 5 mm in diameter. When ripe, the fruit turns black and contains a single seed.

हिंदी: पत्ती एक डंठल पर 5 (कभी 3) नुकीली पत्तियों की बनी होती है। फूल बैंगनी-नीले, सुगंधित। फल छोटा, काला, गोल।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Nirgundi is a pioneer shrub of disturbed and degraded lands, capable of growing in poor, shallow soils where few other plants survive. It fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root nodules, slowly enriching the soil. Its aromatic foliage deters some herbivores; the fruits, despite their small size, are taken by birds, which disperse the seeds. The flowers are a nectar source for wild bees and butterflies in the monsoon when other blooms are scarce.

The shrub is easily propagated from seed or cuttings and is sometimes planted as a hedge or for slope stabilization in eroded areas.

हिंदी: खराब जमीन पर भी उग जाती है। हवा से नाइट्रोजन खींचती है और मिट्टी को सुधारती है। फूल मधुमक्खियों और तितलियों को खींचते हैं। बीज से और कलम से आसानी से लग जाती है।

हिंदी: अप्रैल-सितंबर में फूल। बीज बरसात के बाद अंकुरित होते हैं। दूसरे साल फल आता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Wild bees and Asian Honey Bee visit the flowers.
  • Seed dispersal: Small birds feed on and disperse the ripe fruit.
  • Companions in degraded areas: often found with Lantana and other pioneer shrubs.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Minor leaf beetles and caterpillars feed on foliage; rarely serious.
  • No significant fungal diseases reported on mature plants in semi-arid climates.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use (documented uses; not medical advice):

  • Medicinal use. The leaves and roots have been recorded in Ayurvedic and folk medicine for treating fever, arthritis, skin disorders, and as an anti-inflammatory. Kirtikar & Basu list it as a traditional remedy for fever and rheumatism.
  • Insect and pest control. Extracts of the leaves are recorded in agricultural texts as a traditional insecticide and are tested in research for repelling agricultural pests.
  • Livestock fodder. In dry areas, the leaves are sometimes lopped for cattle fodder, though they are less palatable than preferred species.

Today. The shrub is grown in some gardens for its ornamental flowers and aromatic foliage. Commercial herbal formulations include it.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Studies exist on the chemical composition of the leaves (essential oil, alkaloids) and preliminary screening for antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activity. Whether traditional uses are supported by controlled trials is an open question.

हिंदी: पत्ती दवाई में काम आती है — बुखार, सूजन, त्वचा। कीटनाशक के रूप में भी परीक्षण किया गया है। पशुओं को भी खिलाई जाती है।

To be expected as scattered plants in dry fields, along canal banks, and on degraded land across Basti and eastern UP. More common in years of drought. Any shrub found with characteristic palmately compound, aromatic leaves and violet-blue flowers is worth recording.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical and subtropical Asia, from Pakistan and India through Southeast Asia to southern China, Malaysia and Australia.

In India: Throughout the peninsula and the Gangetic plain; the only Vitex species that extends into the drier regions.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widespread in semi-arid to sub-humid areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in degraded fields and fallow land.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. Which insects visit the flowers, and how long does each spend on a spike? — Thirty minutes at a flowering shrub in July, at different times of day.
  2. Do birds actually eat the black berries, or do they fall to the ground and rot? — Watch a fruiting shrub for half an hour in September.
  3. How quickly does nirgundi grow from a cutting in sandy soil compared to loam soil? — Plant cuttings in pots with different soils; measure height weekly.
  4. What colour is the essential oil of fresh and dried leaves, and what does it smell like? / ताज़ी और सूखी पत्तियों का तेल कैसा गंध और रंग का होता है? — Press leaves in a cloth; record observations.
  5. Does an old nirgundi shrub have nitrogen-rich soil around its base compared to bare ground nearby? — Test soil samples with a simple colour-change kit.

Field rule: A shrub with palmately compound, strongly aromatic leaves and violet-blue flowers in spikes, growing in poor ground, is nirgundi.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum, Vol. 2, p. 638. The epithet negundo is derived from the Sanskrit vernacular name.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised, though some populations show variation in leaf shape.

Family and order placement. Lamiaceae, the mint family, in the order Lamiales. The genus Vitex holds over 250 species, mostly tropical.

Phylogenetic notes. Vitex negundo is the type species of the genus and is closely related to other Asian Vitex species, sharing the characteristic palmately compound leaves.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread and abundant across its range; locally increasing in disturbed habitats.
