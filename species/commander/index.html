कमांडर तितली / Commander

Moduza procris (Cramer, 1777) — Nymphalidae

कमांडर तितली — कदम और हलदू के पेड़ों के पास पाई जाने वाली एक बड़ी, आकर्षक और चटक कत्थई-लाल तितली है। इसके पंखों के बीच में एक चौड़ा और चमकदार सफेद पट्टा होता है जो उड़ते समय दूर से ही चमकता है। यह बहुत तेज गति से उड़ती है और हवा में पंख फैलाकर तैरती है। इसकी इल्ली कदम की पत्तियों पर रहती है और रक्षा के लिए पत्ती की नस पर खास तरह का जाल बनाती है।

Field tip: Look in moist woodland along canals, rivers, and village gardens where mature kadam or kaim trees grow. A large, fiery chestnut-red butterfly gliding with wings flat, showing a blazing pure white band across the middle of both wings, is unmistakable.

हिंदी: कदम के पेड़ों के पास उड़ने वाली इस बड़ी कत्थई तितली के पंखों पर बना चौड़ा सफेद पट्टा धूप में चमकता है। यह पत्तियों पर पंख पूरे फैलाकर बैठती है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics recorded from adult specimens (Kehimkar 2008; Evans 1932):

MeasurementRangeSource
Wingspan60–75 mmKehimkar (2008)

Adult description: The Commander is an imposing nymphalid butterfly of striking coloration, showing slight sexual dimorphism (Bingham 1905). The upperside ground colour in both sexes is deep reddish-chestnut or bright rufous-brown (Wynter-Blyth 1957). The forewing cell contains three short black transverse lines enclosing bright rufous patches, followed by an irregular discal series of pure white, semi-hyaline spots that form a prominent oblique band (Evans 1932). The outer margin of the forewing is broadly bordered with brownish-black, enclosing a series of small white subapical specks (Bingham 1905).

The hindwing upperside continues the broad white discal band from the abdominal margin across the disc toward the costa (Kehimkar 2008). Beyond the white band lies a series of diffuse black-bordered submarginal hastate spots and fine dark lines along the crenulated margin (Bingham 1905).

The underside provides an extraordinary contrast: the base of both wings is washed with pearly silvery-blue and soft greenish-grey, while the postdiscal area is rich fiery ferruginous-red, vividly setting off the dazzling white central band (Kehimkar 2008). The body is stout, dark chestnut above and pale grey beneath; the eyes are large, naked, and dark brown (Bingham 1905).

Larval Stage इल्ली अवस्था

The caterpillar is solitary, dark olive-green or chocolate-brown with distinctive branched lateral spines and two long, club-tipped cephalic horns (Kunte 2000). In early instars, the caterpillar displays a remarkable behavioural adaptation: it strips the leaf tissue along the midrib, leaving the bare vein intact, and builds an extended perch of its own frass bound with silk where it rests in cryptic stillness (Kunte 2000). The pupa is greenish-brown with conspicuous flattened thoracic projections and golden wing-cases (Kehimkar 2008).

हिंदी: इसका शरीर मजबूत और पंखों का फैलाव 60 से 75 मिमी होता है। ऊपरी पंख गहरे कत्थई-लाल और निचला हिस्सा नीलापन लिए सफेद होता है। इसकी इल्ली पत्ती की नस पर जाला बनाकर बैठती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Commander is a powerful, active butterfly of riverine forests, moist rural woodlots, and cultivated groves where rubiaceous trees thrive (Kehimkar 2008). Adults are fond of warm sunshine and establish regular flight pathways along forest tracks, nullahs, and canal levees (Kunte 2000). Their flight is rapid, undulating, and confident: a series of sharp, downward wing flaps followed by an extended glide with wings held slightly tilted or horizontally flat (Wynter-Blyth 1957).

Males are strongly territorial and choose prominent leaves high on trees from which they launch aggressive sorties against other passing butterflies, including much larger species (Kehimkar 2008). During the warmest part of the day, males descend to damp river sand, wet stones, and muddy ditch banks to imbibe mineral salts (Kunte 2000). Adults visit flowers occasionally, but show a much stronger preference for rotting fruits, wet animal droppings, and the sap exuding from wounded tree trunks (Kehimkar 2008).

हिंदी: यह तितली बहुत फुर्तीली और अपने इलाके की रक्षा करने वाली होती है। यह दूसरी तितलियों को खदेड़ती है और दोपहर में गीली रेत या सड़े हुए फलों पर रस पीने आती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian plains across the agricultural calendar (Kunte 2000; Kehimkar 2008):

December–January (Rabi winter)Larva & PupaLarval development slows during cool winter nights
February–April (Spring / Zaid)Adult emergenceSpring brood emerges, visiting blooming orchards and riverbeds (Wynter-Blyth 1957)
May–June (Pre-monsoon summer)Adult & EggRestricted to moist riverine microhabitats and canal groves (Kunte 2000)

Eggs are laid singly on the upper surface of leaves, usually at the extreme tip of a leaf (Kunte 2000). The egg is dome-shaped, covered with hexagonal facets and short spine-like hairs. Hatching occurs within a few days. The young larva immediately begins creating its frass chain along the leaf midrib, a defensive strategy that elevates it above moisture and deters crawling invertebrate predators (Kunte 2000). Larval development lasts a few weeks, and the pupal period lasts about ten days to two weeks before adult emergence. Several generations succeed each other throughout the year.

हिंदी: मादा तितली कदम की पत्ती की नोक पर अंडा देती है। कुछ दिनों में इल्ली निकलती है। इल्ली पत्ती की नस पर जाला बनाती है और लगभग एक महीने में तितली बनती है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Documented larval host plants from published Indian lepidopteran accounts (Kunte 2000; Kehimkar 2008):

  • Neolamarckia cadamba (Kadam Tree, family Rubiaceae) — primary host tree in moist village groves and temple precincts (Kunte 2000).
  • Mitragyna parvifolia (Kaim, family Rubiaceae) — native flood-tolerant tree widely utilized in low-lying Awadh woodlands.
  • Haldina cordifolia (Haldu, family Rubiaceae) — deciduous timber tree utilized along river terraces (Kehimkar 2008).
  • Mussaenda frondosa (family Rubiaceae) — ornamental and wild shrub accepted by larvae in garden borders (Kunte 2000).

Adults obtain nutrients by mud-puddling on wet sand and gravel, sipping fermentation juices from fallen guavas and mangoes, and imbibing tree sap (Kehimkar 2008).

हिंदी: इसके कैटरपिलर कदम, हलदू और कैम के पेड़ों की पत्तियां खाते हैं। बड़ी तितलियाँ सड़े हुए आम, अमरूद और नम मिट्टी से अपना पोषण लेती हैं।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Common insectivorous predators of the Gangetic plain likely to take the Commander:

  • Birds: Forest and woodland birds, such as black drongos, rufous treepies, and kingfishers, prey upon adults in flight or while mud-puddling.
  • Spiders: Large orb-weavers (Neoscona species) and hunting huntsman spiders prey on resting adults.
  • Insects: Praying mantises ambush adults settling on tree trunks; predatory assassin bugs target caterpillars on foliage.
  • Parasitoids: Chalcid wasps and tachinid flies parasitise both larvae and pupae.
हिंदी: भुजंगा, पपीहा और नीलकंठ जैसी चिड़ियाँ उड़ती हुई तितली को पकड़ती हैं। इल्लियों पर परजीवी मक्खियों और ततैयों का खतरा रहता है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Forestry and agriculture: The Commander is not an agricultural pest (Kunte 2000). Its larvae feed on forest and ornamental trees belonging to the family Rubiaceae, particularly Neolamarckia cadamba (Kadam Tree). While a few sapling leaves may be consumed, mature trees suffer no damage, and timber or fruit yields are entirely unaffected.

Cultural and ecological value: With its large size, brilliant chestnut coloration, and habit of frequenting venerable kadam trees associated with traditional Awadh culture, the Commander is an iconic flagship insect for the preservation of native riparian flora (Kehimkar 2008).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal or direct economic uses are documented.

Today: Celebrated by nature observers and butterfly enthusiasts as one of the most magnificent nymphalids of rural India.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing how the conservation of riverine Mitragyna and Haldina groves supports breeding metapopulations of this species.

हिंदी: यह तितली फसलों को किसी प्रकार का नुकसान नहीं पहुँचाती। कदम और हलदू के पेड़ों पर इसका वास प्राकृतिक सुंदरता और जैव विविधता का प्रतीक है।

Look around sunlit branches of kadam and kaim trees, or along wet sandy patches near village ponds from August through December. Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South and Southeast Asia, ranging from India and Sri Lanka east through Myanmar, Thailand, Indochina, Malaysia, and Indonesia to the Philippines (Bingham 1905).

In India: Distributed throughout the Western Ghats, central India, the lower Himalayas, the Gangetic plain, and the northeastern states (Evans 1932; Kehimkar 2008).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from riverine tracts, terai forest reserves, and mature countryside groves across eastern and central districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident in moist village woodlots, canal plantations, and areas retaining ancient kadam and kaim trees.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Pieter Cramer in [1777] as Papilio procris in De Uitlandsche Kapellen, Volume 2, page 15, plate 106, figures E, F. Type locality: Java, Indonesia.

Subspecies. Several geographic subspecies are recognized across the Oriental region. The population inhabiting mainland India and the Gangetic plain is classified as Moduza procris procris (Cramer, 1777), often historically placed under Limenitis procris.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae, subfamily Limenitidinae, tribe Limenitidini. The genus Moduza Moore, 1881, contains robust nymphalids adapted to tropical riverine and forest habitats.

Phylogenetic notes. Moduza is closely related to the Holarctic genus Limenitis, distinguished by genitalic characters, distinctive early-instar frass-building behavior, and wing venation (Bingham 1905).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It remains common and widely distributed throughout its expansive South Asian range.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Moduza procris है। यह Nymphalidae परिवार की तितली है। प्राकृतिक रूप से यह सुरक्षित और सामान्य है।
