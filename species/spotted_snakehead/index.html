गिरई / Spotted Snakehead

Channa punctata (Bloch, 1793) — Channidae

गिरई — देसी शिकारी मछली जो गंगा प्रणाली के तालाबों और नहरों में पाई जाती है। गहरे हरे-भूरे रंग की, शरीर पर सफ़ेद धब्बे। झुंडों में दूसरी मछलियों का शिकार करती है, बड़े माँस पर दाँत। स्वादिष्ट माँस; अन्य मछलियों को नियंत्रित करती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size20–35 cm
BodyRobust, slightly compressed; elongate head
HeadLarge, flat, snout-like; small gill slits on cheeks
ColorationDark greenish-brown; white or pale spots on body and fins
TeethLarge, sharp, saw-like; on both jaws
FinsLong dorsal and anal fins; rounded pectorals
EyesMedium, positioned on top of head

Field tip: A robust predatory fish with a large flattened head, powerful jaws with sharp teeth, and dark coloration with pale spots. Found in still ponds and weedy water. Highly distinctive snakehead appearance. Jumps out of water when disturbed.

हिंदी: बड़े सिर वाली शिकारी मछली; सफ़ेद धब्बे; तीव्र दाँत।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (field populations):

MeasurementRange
Standard length20–35 cm
Weight100–300 g
Head length20–25% of body length
Dorsal fin rays40–50
Anal fin rays30–35

Body structure: Robust, moderately compressed. Streamlined for ambush predation. Characteristic snakehead form with large head.

Head: Very large and flattened. Snout rounded, overhanging the mouth. Eyes moderately large, positioned dorsally. Gill openings small, restricted to the sides; equipped with air-breathing labyrinth organ.

Teeth: Large, strong, sharp, arranged in bands on both jaws. Designed for grasping and holding large prey.

Coloration: Dark greenish-brown or olive-brown on the back; paler on sides. Body and fins marked with white, pale yellow, or cream-coloured spots. Dark bars may be visible on the fins.

Fins: Dorsal and anal fins long, running nearly the full length of the body. Caudal fin rounded. Pectoral fins small and rounded. All fins may show spot patterns.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

An ambush predator that hunts small fish by lying still among aquatic vegetation and striking with explosive speed. Possesses a labyrinth organ (accessory breathing organ) allowing it to survive in oxygen-poor water and to breathe air from the surface. Can leave water and move across land for short distances to reach new water bodies, particularly during monsoon. Builds nests; both parents guard eggs and fry. Highly aggressive when spawning; readily jumps out of water if threatened.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Small fish (minnows, carps, other snakeheads)
  • Aquatic insect larvae and adults
  • Frogs and tadpoles
  • Shrimp and crustaceans
  • Small water snakes and aquatic lizards

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Fish: Large snakeheads and catfish cannibalize smaller individuals
  • Birds: Cormorants, herons, and painted-snipes
  • Reptiles: Python and large water snakes
  • People: Hook and line fishing; spearing

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Food. A highly valued food fish in eastern UP; sold at high prices in markets. Meat is firm, white, flavourful. Often curry or grilled. Nutritious; rich in protein.

Protection status. Protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972.

Aquaculture. Traditionally cultured in ponds; valued as a "predator stock" to control overpopulation of smaller fish and maintain ecosystem balance.

Pest control. By preying on small fish and aquatic insects, snakeheads reduce populations of mosquito vectors and other pests.

Today. Still abundant in suitable habitat; easily caught in ponds. Populations stable where wetlands are preserved.

हिंदी: अत्यंत कीमती खाने वाली मछली; माँस मजबूत और स्वादिष्ट। विधि अधिनियम 1972 के अंतर्गत संरक्षित है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. To be expected in weedy ponds, marshes, and still-water sections of wetlands throughout the district. Most active at dawn and dusk; may hear distinctive croaking calls during breeding season (July–September). A photograph or fresh catch with place and date is a confirmed record.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the Gangetic system and other major river systems of South and Southeast Asia.

In India: Throughout the Ganga, Brahmaputra, and eastern river systems.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in ponds and wetlands of the eastern plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in ponds, marshes, and floodplain lakes; abundant during monsoon.

Field rule: A snakehead with white or pale spots on a dark body, medium size (20–35 cm), and a flattened snakehead form is a Spotted Snakehead.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Marc Elieser Bloch in 1793 in Naturgeschichte der ausländischen Fische. Originally placed in Ophicephalus; transferred to Channa by later authors. The genus name Channa derives from the Indian vernacular name.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognised.

Family and order placement. Channidae, the snakehead or murrel family; order Perciformes. Channa is a genus of Asian snakeheads characterized by accessory air-breathing organs.

Phylogenetic notes. Channa punctata is part of a large Asian radiation of snakeheads, adapted to freshwater wetlands with seasonal flooding.

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern (LC). Widespread and abundant in suitable freshwater habitat; stable in range; adaptable to human-modified wetlands.
