गॉडी बैरन तितली / Gaudy Baron

Euthalia lubentina Cramer, 1779 — Nymphalidae

गॉडी बैरन तितली — यह गहरे हरे-भूरे रंग की एक सुंदर तितली है जिसके पंखों पर सफेद धब्बों के साथ-साथ चमकदार लाल (किरमिजी) रंग के धब्बे होते हैं। मादा के अगले पंखों पर सफेद पट्टी अधिक चौड़ी होती है और पिछले पंख का पिछला भाग नीले-हरे रंग से चमकता है। इसके निचले भाग में भी लाल धब्बे और किनारे स्पष्ट दिखाई देते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Wingspan68–84 mm (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
Male uppersideDark greenish brown; forewing with two crimson bars in/beyond cell bordered with black and discal and preapical small white spots; hindwing with curved postdiscal series of four or five crimson spots and subterminal velvety-black spots (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
Female uppersidePaler; forewing discal series of white spots very large and irregular; hindwing suffused with greenish blue on terminal posterior half (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
UndersideMale dark purplish brown slightly suffused with ochraceous; female brown with tornal half of hindwing bluish green; both sexes with four black-bordered crimson spots at hindwing base and crimson markings (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
Antennae and palpiAntennae dark brown with club crimson beneath; palpi crimson beneath in male, striped pink in female (Bingham 1905, p. 278)

Field tip: Look for a dark greenish-brown baron with bright crimson spots on the hindwing and forewing cell, and in females a broad irregular white band on the forewing (Bingham 1905, p. 278).

हिंदी: इसके पंखों का फैलाव 68 से 84 मिमी होता है। गहरे हरे-भूरे पंखों पर लाल और सफेद धब्बे इसकी मुख्य पहचान हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRangeSource
Expanse (male and female)68–84 mmBingham (1905, p. 278)

Bingham (1905, p. 278) describes the male: "Upperside dark greenish brown. Fore wing: a bar across middle and a bar beyond apex of cell crimson bordered with black; a slightly oblique transverse discal series of small white spots from costa to interspace 1, followed by a preapical curved row of four similar spots and a transverse subterminal series of elongate black spots forming an obscure band. Hind wing: a crescent-shaped black loop near apex of cell-area; a curved postdiscal series of four or five crimson spots outwardly bordered with black, the subcostal spot the largest, followed by a subterminal series of velvety-black subquadrate spots, the anterior three and the tornal spot outwardly crimson. Underside dark purplish brown suffused slightly with ochraceous, the markings as on the upperside but larger and more clearly defined, and in addition:— fore wing: two small black spots at base; basal half of costal margin crimson; hind wing: four crimson spots bordered with black at base; costal and dorsal margins crimson... Antennae dark brown, club beneath crimson; head, thorax and abdomen dark greenish brown; beneath, the palpi and the fore legs crimson, the rest pale brown."

For the female, Bingham (1905, p. 278) adds: "Similar, paler. Upperside. Fore wing: the transverse crimson bands in cell obscure with a broad black-bordered white band interposed, the discal series of white spots very large, very irregular in shape. Hind wing: the ground-colour suffused with greenish blue on terminal posterior half of wing; markings similar to those of the male. Underside brown, the tornal half of the hind wing bluish green... the palpi beneath with a stripe of pink, the fore legs whitish."

Larval Stage

Bingham (1905, p. 279) quotes Moore on the larva ("Armed with ten pairs of long, horizontally projected, very delicately-branched spines. Colour grass-green with a dorsal row of large purplish-brown angulated spots each with or without a small pure white diamond-spot in its middle... the lateral spines green tipped with purple-brown") and A. Grote (apud Moore) on the pupa ("Green, but with two lateral brown marks, each with a dirty-white centre").

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार नर का ऊपरी भाग गहरा हरा-भूरा होता है जिस पर लाल और सफेद धब्बे होते हैं, जबकि मादा में सफेद धब्बे बहुत बड़े होते हैं और पिछले पंख का पिछला आधा हिस्सा नीले-हरे रंग का होता है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Bingham (1905, pp. 278–279) listed "Oudh" in its range. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya: two photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri in April 2024 and November 2025 by two observers at the same spot, and one photographed record from Barabanki in October 2020. A first local record would be a photograph showing the dark greenish-brown wings with crimson spots (and broad white forewing spots in the female) with place and date, sent through the About page.

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) ने इसके वितरण में "Oudh" (अवध) का नाम दर्ज किया है। जीबीआइएफ (GBIF) में लखीमपुर खीरी से दो और बाराबंकी से एक अभिलेख है, पर बस्ती या अयोध्या से अभी कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Bingham (1905, pp. 278–279) gives: "The lower foot-hills of the Himalayas from Hardwar to Sikhim, but recorded from Mussooree, at 10,000 ft.; Oudh; Bengal; eastward through Bhutan, Assam, Cachar to Burma, Tenasserim, Siam, Malay Peninsula and Sumatra. On continental India southward from Bombay."

In India: Evans (1932, p. 155) gives "Bombay — Bengal. Kangra — Burma" for lubentina indica.

In Uttar Pradesh: Bingham (1905, p. 279) records "Oudh"; GBIF holds two photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri in April 2024 and November 2025, and one from Barabanki in October 2020.

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार यह हिमालय की निचली पहाड़ियों, अवध, बंगाल, असम और दक्षिण भारत से बर्मा व सुमात्रा तक पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में जीबीआइएफ पर लखीमपुर खीरी और बाराबंकी के अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Field rule: A dark greenish-brown baron with black-bordered crimson spots on the forewing cell and hindwing (plus broad white forewing spots and bluish-green hindwing suffusion in the female) is Euthalia lubentina (Bingham 1905, p. 278).

हिंदी: गहरे हरे-भूरे पंखों पर काले किनारे वाले लाल धब्बे (और मादा में बड़े सफेद धब्बे) वाली तितली गॉडी बैरन तितली है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Cramer, P. (1779). De Uitlandsche Kapellen (Papillons Exotiques), vol. 2, pl. 155, figs. C, D, as Papilio lubentina (cited in Bingham 1905, p. 278).

Subspecies. Evans (1932, p. 155) treats mainland Indian populations (Bombay — Bengal, Kangra — Burma) as Euthalia lubentina indica Fruhstorfer.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae, genus Euthalia (Bingham 1905, p. 278, entry 296).

Phylogenetic notes. Treated in Euthalia by Bingham (1905, p. 278), Evans (1932, p. 155), and GBIF.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated on the IUCN Red List.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वैज्ञानिक वर्णन क्रेमर (1779) ने Papilio lubentina के रूप में किया था। यह Nymphalidae कुल और Euthalia वंश की सदस्य है।
