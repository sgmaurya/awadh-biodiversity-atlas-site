पत्ती लपेटक / Rice Leaf Folder

Cnaphalocrocis medinalis (Guenée, 1854) — Crambidae

पत्ती लपेटक — धान की पत्तियों को पेंच घुमाकर अंदर रहने वाली छोटी सूंडी, जो पत्ती के अंदर से रस और ऊतक चूसती है। मानसून में धान के खेत में नई पत्तियों पर रोल बने दिखाई देते हैं। पूरी पत्ती सूख जाती है। यह कीट धान की तीसरी सब से बड़ी बीमारी है। लेकिन इसके दुश्मन इतने मजबूत हैं कि कीटनाशक के बिना ही नियंत्रण हो सकता है। भेड़िया मकड़ी, ड्रेगनफ्लाई, और ततैयें सब इसे खाते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Adult mothSmall, pale yellowish or tan moth; wingspan 20–24 mm; wings with fine patterns
LarvaPale green to whitish caterpillar, 13–16 mm; smooth and slender
Leaf damage signLeaf blade rolled lengthwise into a tubular structure; edges are fastened with silk
Inside the rollLarva visible inside, feeding on the inner surface; dark frass (droppings) at the opening
Plant stage affectedMost visible at flag leaf stage (heading time); also on earlier leaves during vegetative stage
Damage extentOne to several rolled leaves per shoot; severe infestations cause visible leaf browning and wilting

Field tip: Walk through the rice field at the heading stage (August–September). Look at the flag leaves and the leaves below. If you see leaves rolled lengthwise like a cigar, gently unroll one: inside will be a pale green caterpillar and dark frass. The larva may drop to the ground if the leaf is disturbed. This is the leaf folder. Wet, humid conditions during the monsoon favour high populations.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: धान की पत्तियाँ सिगार की तरह लुढ़की हुई हों। खोलकर देखो अंदर सफ़ेद-हरी सूंडी है। पत्ती के अंदर काली बूँदें (मल) भी दिखेंगी। पत्तियाँ सूखी नज़र आएँगी।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult moth: Small, delicate, pale yellowish or tan coloured moth. Wings are membranous with fine patterns and scales. Antennae are thread-like. The insect is active during the early morning and at dusk. Wings are held roof-like over the body when at rest.

Egg: Small, oval, slightly flattened, pale, laid singly or in clusters on the rice leaf surface, particularly on the underside.

Larval stage: Four instars. The larva is pale green to whitish, smooth-bodied, slightly translucent, with a brown head. The body is slender and tapers toward the rear. Larvae are cryptic, spending their entire larval life inside the rolled leaf.

हिंदी: पतंगा पीले-भूरे रंग का, छोटा, कमज़ोर उड़ान। सूंडी हल्की हरी, चिकनी, पत्ती के अंदर। अंडे बहुत छोटे, पत्ती पर।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A highly specialized feeder that rolls rice leaves and feeds exclusively inside the rolled leaf. Females lay eggs singly on rice leaves; upon hatching, the larva immediately begins rolling the leaf by drawing the leaf margins together with silk. The larva then feeds on the inner surface of the rolled leaf, consuming the soft tissue and chlorophyll. The larva pupates inside the rolled leaf or in the leaf sheath. The entire lifecycle is cryptic, with the larva and pupa hidden inside the leaf roll, which makes detection and control difficult.

The insect is more abundant in high-nitrogen, well-managed paddies, where vigorous new foliage provides optimal larval food. Populations can build rapidly under warm, humid monsoon conditions, and multiple generations occur in a single kharif season. The insect also transmits rice viruses, though this is a secondary concern compared to leaf damage.

Natural enemies include parasitoid wasps (particularly Apanteles species), parasitoid flies, spiders that search within the canopy, and dragonflies that hunt adults over the field.

हिंदी: धान की पत्तियों को पेंच देता है और अंदर सब समय रहता है। कीटनाशक अंदर नहीं पहुँचते, इसलिए बाहर से नियंत्रण मुश्किल। ड्रेगनफ्लाई, ततैयें, और मकड़ियाँ इसे खाती हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Rice Paddy: the primary and almost exclusive host
  • Wild rice species (Oryza sativa var. spontanea): occasionally infested
  • Other grasses: rarely feeds on other grasses if present in or near the paddy
  • No documented feeding on alternative crops or weeds

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Parasitoid wasps: Apanteles and Trichogramma species (larval and egg parasitoids)
  • Parasitoid flies: tachinid flies that parasitize larvae inside the leaf roll
  • Spiders: web-building spiders (green_lynx_spider, long_jawed_spider) and hunting spiders within the rice canopy
  • Dragonflies and damselflies: Wandering Glider, Ground Skimmer, and damselflies hunt adult moths
  • Frogs: Skittering Frog and other frogs consume adults and pupae
  • Avian predators: Black Drongo and other insectivorous birds
  • Entomopathogenic fungi: Beauveria bassiana and Isaria species under high humidity

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Important pest. The rice leaf folder is the third most damaging insect pest of rice in India, after the brown planthopper and the stem borers. Heavy infestations can roll a large share of the leaf area, reducing photosynthesis and delaying maturation. The insect is particularly destructive at the heading stage, when damage to the flag leaf directly reduces grain filling.

What works (Traditional, Today, Research direction):

  • Traditional: Removal of infested leaves by hand (removal of folded leaves, though labour-intensive). Flooding the paddy to expose pupae and larvae.
  • Today: Scouting and counting rolled leaves per tiller; spraying only when damage exceeds a threshold (30% rolled leaves). Selective insecticides (spinosad, neem) applied to the rice canopy when larvae are active. Most importantly: conservation of natural enemies by avoiding broad-spectrum sprays, particularly in the early vegetative stage when natural enemies are establishing.
  • Research direction (a lead, not a result): Habitat management for parasitoids and spiders. Field margins with flowering plants (mustard, local herbs) that provide nectar and pollen for parasitoids. The village-level practice of tolerating some leaf rolling while relying on natural enemies is proven and costs nothing.
हिंदी: धान का तीसरा सब से बड़ा दुश्मन। हल्के संक्रमण में कुछ मत करो, दुश्मन सँभाल लेंगे। ज़्यादा हो तो नीम का छिड़काव। सब से अहम: कीटनाशक सड़क पर, न खेत में पहली बार। मकड़ियों, ड्रेगनफ्लाई, ततैयों को बचाना मायने रखता है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Expected in every kharif rice field in Basti district, particularly during the monsoon months (August–September). Incidence varies with weather: high humidity and warm temperature favour rapid population buildup. Populations are highest in fields with high nitrogen application and vigorous plant growth. A simple field record — count of rolled leaves per 10 tillers, date, location — would be a valuable first confirmed sighting.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Asia, from India through Southeast Asia to Japan and Australia.

In India: All rice-growing regions; particularly abundant in monsoon-season rice.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout the rice-growing belt, with high incidence in eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in all kharif rice fields. Infestations increase from July and peak in August–September during the monsoon and early post-monsoon period.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. How many rolled leaves per tiller indicate a population that requires control in your field? / आपके खेत में कितनी मुड़ी पत्तियाँ नियंत्रण के लिए काफ़ी हैं? — Compare fields with different roller densities and measure yield reduction.
  2. What parasitoid wasp species emerge from field-collected rice leaf folders? — Bring rolled leaves indoors in a mesh-covered container and observe wasp emergence.
  3. Do rice paddies bordered by flowering plants (like fenugreek or mustard) have lower leaf folder populations than fields without such borders? — Compare paired fields with and without flowering borders.
  4. How does the timing of nitrogen application relate to the incidence of leaf folders in the same paddy over the season? — Compare early-applied and late-applied nitrogen on the same field block.
  5. What proportion of leaf roller pupae survive winter in rice stubble, and how many adults emerge in the spring? — Collect stubble samples over winter and rear them for emergence.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Guenée in 1854 in Histoire Naturelle des Insectes. Lépidoptères as Phalaena medinalis. The genus Cnaphalocrocis was erected by Lederer in 1863. The species name medinalis refers to its relationship to Medina, an older genus name.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Crambidae (grass moths and allies), subfamily Spilomelinae. This family includes many crop pests of grasses and other plants.

Phylogenetic notes. Cnaphalocrocis medinalis is one of several rice-infesting crambid moths. Its leaf-rolling behaviour is unique among major rice pests and makes it easy to identify.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Like most agricultural pest insects, it is not assessed by the IUCN.
