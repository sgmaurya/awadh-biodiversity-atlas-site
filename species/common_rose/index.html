साधारण गुलाब / Common Rose

Pachliopta aristolochiae (Fabricius, 1775) — Papilionidae

साधारण गुलाब — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश की बाड़ी, झाड़ी और रास्ते की किनारे पाई जाने वाली एक मध्यम आकार की लाल और काली तितली है। इसके पंखों का रंग चमकीला लाल-केसरी होता है जिन पर मोटी काली सीमाएँ होती हैं। इसके अगले पंखों की नोक काली होती है। इसकी इल्ली दहेजा और जंगली अरिस्टोलोकिया की पत्तियों खाती है, जिससे यह तितली भी ज़हरीली हो जाती है। यह खतरनाक नहीं है लेकिन यह काली और सफ़ेद तितलियों के लिए एक "अच्छा कॉपी" (Batesian mimicry) मॉडल है। इसकी धीमी, लहराती उड़ान और सुंदर रंग गाँवों की प्राकृतिक विविधता का एक महत्वपूर्ण अंश हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeMedium swallowtail butterfly; body length 25–32 mm; wingspan 85–110 mm
ColorationUpper wings are bright red or scarlet-orange; lower wings have crimson-red coloring with white bands
Forewing ApexBlack apex (wing tip) with a row of white or pale spots along the outer edge
HindwingHas a white median band and white marginal spots on a deep red background
BodyBright red head and thorax; black and red abdominal segments
FlightSlow, fluttering, and weak; flies close to host plants

Field tip: Look for a bright red butterfly with a slow, lazy flight near Aristolochia (dahejia) plants. The red-and-black coloring, combined with the slow flight, readily identifies the Common Rose. It is the toxic model that other non-toxic butterflies (such as female Common Mormons) mimic.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for adults in northern Indian plains):

MeasurementRange
Body length25–32 mm
Wingspan85–110 mm
Forewing length38–45 mm

Body: The head is bright red with a short, club-like antenna. The thorax is robust and red above, black below. The abdomen consists of seven black segments, each with red margins.

Wings:

  • Dorsal (upper) surface: The forewing is bright crimson-red with a black apex (outer tip) containing a diagonal band of white or pale yellow spots. The hindwing is bright red or orange-red, crossed by a broad white median band. The wing margins are bordered in black.
  • Ventral (lower) surface: Similar to the upperparts but slightly paler, with white spots more prominent.

Chemical Defense: The caterpillar feeds on Aristolochia species, which contain aristolochic acids. The adult butterfly stores these toxins in its tissues, rendering it unpalatable to birds. The bright red-and-black coloration serves as an aposematic (warning) signal.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Slow flyer and host-plant specialist. Unlike swift swallowtails, the Common Rose has a weak, fluttering flight and stays close to the ground in search of its Aristolochia host plant. Males patrol along hedgerows looking for females. The bright coloration advertises the butterfly's toxicity, and birds quickly learn to avoid it. Because of this chemical defense, it serves as the primary model for Batesian mimicry; several non-toxic species have evolved to mimic its appearance, gaining protection from predators.

Host-plant fidelity. The female butterfly is highly specialized on Aristolochia species and rarely lays eggs elsewhere. This makes it an excellent indicator of the presence of its host plant.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plant:

  • Aristolochia species (Aristolochia / Dahejia), particularly Aristolochia indica and Aristolochia tagala. This is the only host plant for this specialized feeder.

Adult nectar sources:

  • Lantana camara (Indian Lantana), wild composites, and flowering weeds.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Although chemically defended, some specialist birds (like the paradise flycatcher) have learned to safely handle this species.
  • Spiders: Crab spiders and jumping spiders on flowers.
  • Parasitoid Wasps: Small wasps parasitize the eggs and chrysalis.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Indicator species. The presence of Common Rose butterflies indicates the presence of Aristolochia plants in the area, which are used in traditional herbal medicine.

Educational model. Because of its spectacular coloration and toxicity, the Common Rose is used in schools and gardens to teach children about chemical defense, aposematism, and mimicry.

To be expected in Basti district wherever Aristolochia plants grow—along garden boundaries, wastelands, and field margins. Look for bright red butterflies with slow, lazy flight near damp spots on roads. Check Aristolochia plants for caterpillars with cream and black bands and black tentacle-like appendages. The first local record would be a photograph with place and date; send it to the atlas contact on the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent, from the sea coast to 2,000 metres elevation in the hills.

In India: Widespread throughout the peninsula and the Gangetic plain.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common resident in all rural districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in all blocks where Aristolochia is cultivated or wild.

Field rule: A medium-sized, bright red swallowtail with a black apex, white spots on the forewing tip, a white median band on the hindwing, and a slow, fluttering flight is the Common Rose.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Fabricius described the Common Rose as Papilio aristolochiae in 1775 in his Systema Antliatorum, p. 32. The type locality is India. The specific epithet refers to the host plant Aristolochia.

Subspecies. The nominate subspecies P. a. aristolochiae occurs throughout India and Nepal.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Papilionidae (swallowtails), subfamily Papilioninae.

Phylogenetic notes. Pachliopta is a genus of toxic swallowtails distributed across Asia, closely related to the African Papilio species.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Common and widespread, under no conservation threat.
