चाँदनी / Crape Jasmine

Tabernaemontana divaricata (L.) R.Br. — Apocynaceae

चाँदनी — रात में खिलने वाली सुगंधित झाड़ी, जिसके सफ़ेद, सर्पिल, सुंदर फूल चाँदनी रात को चमकते हैं। बाग़ों और मंदिरों में पूजा के फूल के लिए लगाई जाती है। पूरा पौधा, ख़ासकर दूध, थोड़ा विषाक्त है। पर उसकी खुशबू और सुंदरता इसे हर गाँव के बाग़ में रखती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

The testHighly fragrant white flowers with spiral arrangement of petals; opposite, glossy leaves; twin orange fruits when mature

Field tip: Look for a planted evergreen shrub with opposite, glossy, dark green leaves and clusters of small, intensely fragrant white flowers with spiralled petals. The plant flowers spring and monsoon. All parts are slightly toxic; handle carefully.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सफ़ेद, सुगंधित फूल, पँखुड़ियाँ घूमती हुई। रात को गंध बहुत। पत्तियाँ चमकदार, गहरी हरी। नारंगी फलियाँ।

Morphology आकारिकी

Stem and branches: Erect to spreading, much-branched, green when young, becoming woody and grey with age.

Leaves: Opposite, oblong-elliptic to oblanceolate, 10–15 cm long, 4–8 cm wide, dark green, glossy and smooth above, paler beneath; veins prominent; entire margin; short petiole.

Flowers: Small to medium, white, highly fragrant especially at night, 2–3 cm diameter, with five petals arranged in a distinctive spiral or pinwheel pattern (characteristic of the genus). Borne in terminal or axillary clusters. The central corona is white or cream.

Fruit: Twin follicles (characteristic of Apocynaceae), each 10–15 cm long, green initially, turning orange-yellow when mature, splitting to release black seeds with silky appendages.

Latex: White, milky, present throughout the plant; mildly toxic.

हिंदी: पत्तियाँ सामने-सामने, चमकदार, गहरी हरी। फूल सफ़ेद, सुगंधित, पँखुड़ियाँ घूमती हुई। नारंगी, लंबी फलियाँ।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Crape Jasmine is a tropical shrub, native to India, cultivated widely across South Asia and beyond. It grows best in warm, well-drained soils and is moderately drought-tolerant. The fragrant flowers are visited by moths and other night-flying insects, particularly in the evening and at night when the fragrance is strongest. The plant is frost-sensitive but thrives in eastern UP's warm season. It is purely ornamental and does not naturalize.

Toxicity: The plant is mildly toxic. The latex can cause dermatitis in sensitive persons. All parts contain alkaloids. This is documented ethnobotanically but the plant is safe for casual contact.

हिंदी: गर्म जलवायु में अच्छा बढ़ता है। रात को गंध ज़्यादा आती है। कीटों को आकर्षित करता है। सब कुछ थोड़ा ज़हरीला, पर छुने से नुकसान नहीं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Moths and night-flying insects visit the fragrant flowers at dusk.
  • Garden companions: Often planted with Kaner and other ornamental shrubs in temple gardens.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Scale insects on stems.
  • Mealybugs on leaves and shoots.
  • Leaf-eating beetles; rarely serious.
  • No significant fungal diseases in well-drained cultivation.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Ornamental and cultural use (documented use):

Crape Jasmine is cultivated primarily for its intensely fragrant white flowers, which are used in garlands, religious ceremonies, and decoration. In temples and gardens across South Asia, the flowers are offered to deities. The plant is valued for its fragrance, particularly in the evening. The white, spiral flowers are highly valued aesthetically.

Traditional use (with caveat): The plant has entries in classical Ayurvedic texts and ethnobotanical accounts, but specific documented uses are limited. The leaves are mildly toxic and are not used internally.

Today. Crape Jasmine is grown widely for its ornamental beauty and fragrance. It is not a major commercial plant but is present in most South Asian gardens of middle and upper-income households.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The ethnobotany of Tabernaemontana in South Asia, including local names and uses, could be better documented.

हिंदी: फूल पूजा में चढ़ाए जाते हैं। रात को गंध से पहचानते हैं। पत्तियाँ थोड़ी जहरीली हैं; आमतौर पर आंतरिक रूप से उपयोग नहीं किया जाता।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to South Asia and Southeast Asia; cultivated worldwide in warm regions.

In India: Native to southern and eastern India; widely cultivated in gardens.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated in gardens and parks.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Occasional to common in ornamental gardens and temple settings.

Field rule: A cultivated shrub with opposite, glossy, dark green leaves and clusters of small, white, intensely fragrant flowers whose petals are arranged in a spiral pinwheel pattern, is Crape Jasmine.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described by Linnaeus as Vinca divaricata in Species Plantarum (1753). Moved to Tabernaemontana by Brown in 1810. The epithet divaricata means "spreading" or "divaricate."

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised, though horticultural varieties exist.

Family and order placement. Apocynaceae (dogbane family), order Gentianales. Tabernaemontana is a tropical genus of about 100 species.

Phylogenetic notes. Tabernaemontana divaricata is closely related to Ervatamia divaricata and other South Asian Apocynaceae.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Native to South Asia; widely cultivated.
