ताम्र तितली / Danaid Eggfly

Hypolimnas misippus (Linnaeus, 1764) — Nymphalidae

ताम्र तितली — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के खुले मैदानों, बागों और घास के मैदानों में पाई जाने वाली एक दिलचस्प तितली। इसका नाम "अंडा" आकार के निशान से आया है जो पंखों पर होते हैं। यह तितली एक बहुरूपी (polymorphic) प्रजाति है — नर नीले-काले रंग के होते हैं, लेकिन मादा अक्सर सफेद और नारंगी रंग की होती हैं। यह एक मिमिक्री का उदाहरण है, जहाँ मादा दूसरी तितलियों की तरह दिखती हैं ताकि शिकारियों से बचा जा सके।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Male wingsDeep blue-black or dark purple on upperwings; iridescent blue in males of certain populations
Female formsTwo main mimetic forms: white form (mimics Danaus sp.) or dark form (mimics other Nymphalidae)
Wing spot patternSeveral white or pale spots ("eggs") on the wing surface
SizeWingspan 55–80 mm
FlightSlow, leisurely; often close to the ground
UnderwingsBrown or tan with eye-spots

Field tip: Look in open areas near flowering shrubs. Males are easy to spot: dark blue-black wings with white spots. Females vary dramatically in colour; they often perch on grasses. A slow, fluttering flight close to grass is typical.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: नर गहरे नीले-काले, सफेद धब्बों वाले। मादा सफेद या नारंगी हो सकती हैं। धीमी उड़ान, घास के पास।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (adult specimens from the Gangetic plains):

MeasurementRange
Forewing length24–32 mm
Wingspan55–80 mm
Male colourationDeep blue-black, iridescent blue patches on hindwings
Female colourationPolymorphic: white-cream with red or orange patches, or dark brown (mimetic)

Wing pattern: Males show a distinctive pattern with white spots on dark backgrounds. Females show strong Batesian mimicry, copying the wing patterns of unpalatable Danaus butterflies to avoid predation.

हिंदी: नर गहरे रंग, मादा अलग-अलग रंगों की हो सकती हैं। यह एक मिमिकरी की अद्भुत मिसाल है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Slow flyer and flower visitor. Males perch on leaves or low vegetation, occasionally making short flights to flowers or mud puddles. Females fly slowly and spend much time laying eggs on host plants. The species is one of the best examples of Batesian mimicry in butterflies: females of H. misippus directly mimic unpalatable Danaus butterflies, fooling predators into avoiding them. Males are bold and curious, sometimes landing on moving objects.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants:

  • Ruellia species (Ruellia brittoniana, Ruellia patula) — primary hosts
  • Water hyssop and related plants

Adult food:

  • Nectar from various flowers
  • Mud-puddling on damp soil for minerals

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Drongo and other insectivorous birds prey on adults
  • Parasitoid wasps: Lay eggs inside caterpillars
  • Spiders: Web predators and hunters
  • Predatory insects: Robber flies and mantises

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Beneficial pollinator. The Danaid Eggfly is an important pollinator in agricultural and natural areas, visiting a wide range of flowers and dispersing pollen. Its presence indicates healthy grassland and field ecosystems.

हिंदी: यह तितली फूलों को परागित करती है और खेतों में जैविक विविधता का संकेत है।

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Expected in open fields and grasslands throughout Basti and Ayodhya districts where Ruellia host plants occur. Active year-round in villages and cultivated areas. Most visible during and after the monsoon (July–November). GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Africa, Arabian Peninsula, and Indo-Australian region from India to northern Australia.

In India: Found throughout the country, from plains to 2,000 meters in the Himalayas.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common resident across all districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Found in open fields and grasslands throughout all blocks; year-round resident.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  1. What percentage of female Danaid Eggflies in your area are mimetic forms vs. dark forms? — Survey and photograph females; classify by mimetic pattern.
  2. On which Ruellia species is the caterpillar most commonly found? — Examine local Ruellia plants; record host preferences.
  3. How do predatory birds respond to female mimetic forms vs. males? — Observe predation attempts in the field.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1764 in Systema Naturae, 12th ed., p. 772, from specimens from the East Indies.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies; H. m. misippus is the East Indian form, occurring in eastern UP.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae (brush-footed butterflies).

Phylogenetic notes. Hypolimnas misippus is remarkable for its extreme sexual dimorphism and mimicry polymorphism; evolutionary studies show female mimetic forms are maintained by strong predation pressure.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. It is a common, widespread species with no conservation concerns.
