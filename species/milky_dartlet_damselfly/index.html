मिल्की डार्टलेट व्याध पतंग / Milky Dartlet Damselfly

Agriocnemis lacteola Selys, 1877 — Coenagrionidae

मिल्की डार्टलेट व्याध पतंग — उथले जलाशयों, दलदली गड्ढों और धान के खेतों की मेड़ों पर पाई जाने वाली अत्यंत नन्ही और दूधिया सफेद रंग की डैमसेल्फ़्लाई है। इसके नर का पेट ऊपर से दूधिया सफेद होता है और सीना काले तथा हल्के हरे रंग का होता है। यह घासों के बिल्कुल नीचे तैरती हुई सी उड़ती है। यह मच्छरों और धान के सूक्ष्म कीटों का शिकार करके पर्यावरण को संतुलित रखती है।

Field tip: Look low among dense grassy borders of shallow ponds and flooded ditches. The tiny size combined with the gleaming, chalky milky-white upper surface of the male abdomen flying slowly through dark green sedges is completely unmistakable.

हिंदी: पानी के किनारे उगी घनी घासों में बहुत नीचे उड़ने वाली इस नन्ही तितलीनुमा पतंग का सफेद पेट हरी पत्तियों के बीच दूध की तरह चमकता हुआ दिखाई देता है।

Adult description:

Agriocnemis lacteola is among the smallest zygopterans in the subcontinent, displaying delicate morphology and distinctive chalky-white coloration in mature males (Fraser 1933). The male head has a velvety black vertex marked with large, pale apple-green or bluish postocular spots; the frons and clypeus are pale green (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is velvety black on the dorsum, adorned with two narrow, pale green or whitish antehumeral stripes; the lateral pleura are pale greenish-yellow fading to pure white beneath (Fraser 1933).

The male abdomen is exceptionally slender and thread-like: segments 1 through 8 are coated on the dorsal surface with an opaque, chalky milky-white or porcelain-white layer (Subramanian 2005). Segment 8 often carries a narrow black apical ring, while segments 9 and 10 are predominantly black with pale blue or yellowish lateral markings (Fraser 1933). The anal appendages are small, pale yellow, and conical.

Females are dimorphic and lack the milky white abdominal coating (Fraser 1933). In the common heteromorphic form, the thorax is yellowish-brown or olivaceous with black stripes, and the abdomen is dull yellow or greenish-yellow marked dorsally with a continuous, broad bronze-black longitudinal band from segment 1 to 10 (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the wings are narrow, petiolate, and completely transparent, with pale yellowish-brown pterostigmata (Fraser 1933).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is tiny, fragile, and pale translucent green or light yellowish-grey, camouflaged amongst submerged roots of Eichhornia crassipes (Water Hyacinth) and sedges. The caudal gills are elongate, narrow, and rounded at the tips, adapted for extracting oxygen in stagnant water.

हिंदी: इसका पेट केवल 16 से 18 मिमी लंबा होता है। नर का पेट दूध जैसा सफेद और सीना काला-हरा होता है। मादा के पूरे पेट पर काली पट्टी होती है। इसका जलीय निम्फ पारदर्शी हरा होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Milky Dartlet Damselfly is a low-flying, shade-tolerant dweller of dense emergent vegetation in calm, shallow wetlands (Subramanian 2005). It inhabits stagnant village tanks, marshy ditches, waterlogged pasture depressions, and shallow flooded fringes of rice paddies (Fraser 1933). Because of its minute size and fragile build, it rarely ascends above the shelter of grassy blades, staying within ten to twenty centimetres of the water.

Adults are active during calm sunny periods from mid-morning to early afternoon (Subramanian 2005). Males perch horizontally on thin grass culms or sedges, their milky white abdomens reflecting sunlight. Territorial encounters consist of subtle wing-fluttering displays without aggressive chases. Mating occurs in tandem amongst vegetation. The female oviposits alone (endophytically) into the submerged tissues of fine grassy weeds and floating leaves.

हिंदी: यह डैमसेल्फ़्लाई बहुत शांत और धीमी गति से उड़ने वाली होती है। यह तेज हवाओं से डरती है और घासों के अंदर ही रहकर छोटे कीड़ों को खोजती है।

Females insert microscopic eggs singly into the submerged stems and leaves of aquatic grasses, sedges, and plants like Ipomoea aquatica (Water Spinach). The eggs hatch after a few days into tiny aquatic nymphs. Naiads develop rapidly through several instars over a few weeks, preying upon micro-invertebrates before crawling up an emergent grass stem to emerge as winged adults. Several generations are completed annually.

हिंदी: मादा पानी के अंदर डूबी घासों में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडे फूटते हैं और पानी में रहने वाला निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में वयस्क बनकर बाहर आ जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Agriocnemis lacteola is an obligate micro-predator throughout its life history (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small disease-carrying mosquitoes, such as Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Anopheles culicifacies (Malaria Mosquito).
  • Tiny agricultural pests of rice crops, including Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper).
  • Micro-dipterans, including midges (Chironomidae), fungus gnats, and tiny fruit flies.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito wrigglers, ostracods, water fleas (Cladocera), and microscopic aquatic worms in shallow water.
हिंदी: यह सूक्ष्म शिकारी कीट धान के खेतों के फुदकों, छोटे मच्छरों और पानी पर भिनभिनाने वाले कीड़ों को खाकर जैविक नियंत्रण में महत्वपूर्ण भूमिका निभाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented preying upon Agriocnemis lacteola in Gangetic plain agroecosystems:

  • Spiders: Wetland orb-weavers and small nursery web spiders (Pisaura) capture flying adults in dense grasses.
  • Predatory dragonflies: Larger co-occurring dragonflies, such as Orthetrum sabina (Slender Skimmer) and Crocothemis servilia (Scarlet Skimmer), readily prey on perching dartlets.
  • Amphibians: Tiny metamorphs of Euphlyctis cyanophlyctis (Skittering Frog) catch adults perching near the water line.
  • Aquatic bugs: Giant water bugs and backswimmers prey upon the tiny aquatic naiads.
हिंदी: घास में जाला बनाने वाली मकड़ियाँ, बड़ी व्याध पतंगें और पानी के मेंढक इस नन्ही सफेद डैमसेल्फ़्लाई को पकड़कर खाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Agroecological benefits in rice cultivation: Agriocnemis lacteola is an effective natural bio-control ally in paddy farming systems of northern India (Subramanian 2005). Because it forages at the extreme base of rice stems where planthoppers like Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) aggregate, it directly attacks early pest stages that larger dragonflies flying overhead cannot reach.

Public health contribution: By consuming significant numbers of vector mosquitoes (Culex and Anopheles) breeding in stagnant village ditches, it helps suppress rural vector-borne disease transmission (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional uses are recorded.

Today: Valued by agricultural ecologists as a bio-control agent in lowland rice ecosystems.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing the micro-habitat vertical stratification of Agriocnemis predation relative to chemical insecticide application heights.

हिंदी: यह कीट धान के पौधों की जड़ों के पास जाकर हानिकारक फुदकों को खाता है, जहाँ बड़े शिकारी नहीं पहुँच पाते। यह किसानों के लिए अत्यंत उपयोगी कीट है।

The Milky Dartlet Damselfly is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in shallow flooded ditches, grassy edges of village tanks, and waterlogged rice fields. Look closely among thick grass blades close to the water on calm sunny mornings from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph with date and location, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South and Southeast Asia, including India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, southern China, and Indonesia (Fraser 1933).

In India: Recorded throughout the northern alluvial plains, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Odisha, and central India (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from riverine wetlands, drainage canals, and agricultural plains across central and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a common resident breeding damselfly in rural paddy fields, borrow pits, and seasonal village marshes.

हिंदी: यह भारत और दक्षिण-पूर्व एशिया में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के मैदानी इलाकों के दलदलों और धान के खेतों में इसकी उपस्थिति स्वाभाविक है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Edmond de Selys-Longchamps in 1877 as Agriocnemis lacteola in Bulletin de l'Académie royale des Sciences, des Lettres et des Beaux-Arts de Belgique, (2) 43, page 60. Type locality: "Bengale" (Bengal, India).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized across its South and Southeast Asian range.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Zygoptera, family Coenagrionidae. The genus Agriocnemis Selys, 1869, comprises the dartlets or wisp damselflies, renowned as some of the smallest dragonflies and damselflies on earth.

Phylogenetic notes. Agriocnemis lacteola is closely allied to A. pygmaea and A. femina, but readily separated by its distinct male chalky-white pruinescence and unique penile terminalia (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure wetland species across its wide Oriental distribution.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Agriocnemis lacteola है और यह Coenagrionidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
