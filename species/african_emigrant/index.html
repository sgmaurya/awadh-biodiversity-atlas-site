अफ़्रीकन एमिग्रेंट तितली / African Emigrant

Catopsilia florella (Fabricius, 1775) — Pieridae

अफ़्रीकन एमिग्रेंट तितली — यह एक मध्यम आकार की पीली-नारंगी तितली है जो पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के खुली जगहों, खेतों और बागों में आम तौर पर देखी जाती है। इसके पंख चमकीले पीले या नारंगी-पीले होते हैं जिन पर काली धारियाँ नहीं होती या बहुत कम होती हैं। यह तितली तेज़ उड़ान भरती है और पूरे साल अलग-अलग इलाकों में घूमती रहती है। इसकी इल्ली विभिन्न फलीदार पौधों, विशेषकर खदिरा (neem) और सुभाबुल पर पाई जाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
WingspanMedium to large; 55–70 mm
Ground colorPale yellow to bright yellow-orange
MarkingsMinimal; occasional black or dark spots
ForewingYellow with occasional dark border; strong contrasting wing pattern in some forms
HindwingYellow or pale yellow; uniform coloration
FlightStrong, fast, erratic flight; flies high

Field tip: Look for a bright yellow butterfly with strong flight, flying high over open grassland and cultivated areas. The uniform yellow coloration and strong flight distinguish it from smaller yellow species like Grass Yellows.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पीली या नारंगी-पीली तितली, तेज़ उड़ान, खुली जगहों पर उड़ती है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Adult wingspan55–70 mm
Body length20–26 mm
Forewing length25–32 mm
Caterpillar length (mature)26–30 mm

Adult butterfly: Pale yellow to bright golden-yellow. Wings are smooth and unblemished in many individuals; some show faint greyish borders or spots. Sexual dimorphism present; females sometimes duller or with greenish tint on underside.

Caterpillar: Green or yellowish-green, smooth, with faint stripes, feeding on Leguminosae.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The African Emigrant is a strong, fast flyer characteristic of open habitats. It is dispersive and migratory, moving through the landscape in response to vegetation phenology and rainfall. Populations fluctuate seasonally, with peaks during the monsoon and post-monsoon months when host plants are actively growing. Males are territorial and perch on elevated points or vegetation, taking flight to investigate passing insects or females.

Stage / SeasonEvent

Larva (caterpillar)Green; passes through 4 instars; develops over 14–18 days
PupaSuspended chrysalis; green or brown; emerges in 10–14 days
AdultYear-round; peak June–October; fluctuating abundance

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants (Leguminosae/Fabaceae):

  • Neem (Azadirachta indica)
  • Subabul (Leucaena leucocephala)
  • Various Acacia and Cassia species
  • Wild legume herbs

Adult nectar sources:

  • Garden flowers, wildflowers, and weeds

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Insectivorous birds hunt flying adults
  • Dragonflies: Effective predators of weak-flying individuals
  • Spiders: Web spiders catch adults
  • Parasitoid wasps: Attack caterpillars and pupae

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollinator service. The African Emigrant visits a variety of flowers, providing pollination services.

Legume association. The species is associated with legume-rich habitats and woodlands.

The species is expected year-round throughout Basti district in open grasslands, cultivated areas, and areas with legume vegetation. Peak abundance June–October. Look for bright yellow fast-flying butterflies in open habitats. A photograph with date and location would confirm a local record.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Australia.

In India: Widespread throughout the subcontinent in suitable habitats.

In Uttar Pradesh: Present in grassland and agricultural areas throughout the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in open habitats year-round; peak monsoon to October.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Fabricius in 1775 from East Indian specimens. The genus Catopsilia comprises yellow emigrants found across Africa and Asia.

Subspecies. The nominate subspecies C. f. florella occurs throughout Asia.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Pieridae.

Phylogenetic notes. Catopsilia belongs to the Coliadinae, a subfamily of pantropical yellows, characterized by strong migration and dispersal behaviour.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Common and widespread with seasonal population fluctuations.
