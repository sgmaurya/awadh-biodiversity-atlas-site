सफेद आक / White Aak

Calotropis procera (Aiton) Dryand. — Apocynaceae

सफेद आक — रेगिस्तानी झाड़ी, जो सूखी और बंजर जमीन पर उगती है जहाँ और कुछ नहीं पनपता। पत्तियाँ मोटी, नीली-हरी, और पाउडर से ढकी हुई। फूल सुगंधित, हलके गुलाबी या सफेद। तना तोड़ने पर दूधिया, ज़हरीली रस निकलती है। पूरा पौधा ज़हरीला है, पर मंदिरों में पवित्र माना जाता है। परंपरागत दवाई में जगह है, पर अब सावधानी से ही काम लिया जाता है।

Field tip: A stocky shrub with thick, grey-blue, waxy leaves in the driest parts of fields. The waxy flower crown is distinctive. The latex is caustic and should not be touched.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सूखी जमीन की झाड़ी, मोटी, नीली-हरी पत्तियाँ, सफेद-गुलाबी फूल। तना तोड़ने पर दूधिया जहर निकलता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Stem and branches: Soft-wooded, becoming woody only at the base with age. The entire plant is full of latex.

Leaves: Opposite, decussate, broad, oval to oblong, with a cordate base. The leaf is thick, waxy, and covered with a white or blue-grey bloom (epicuticular wax). This waxy coating reduces water loss in arid environments.

Flowers: The corolla is pale pink to white, with a crown-like structure (corona) with 5 upright horns. The flowers are fragrant and arranged in dense, terminal umbels. Pollination is by insects.

Fruit: Two erect, inflated, membranous follicles (pod-like fruits) arise from a single flower. The pods are initially green and turgid, ripening to a pale brown and splitting open to release many seeds with silky hairs.

Seed: Numerous, flat, with a parachute of hair (comose seeds), allowing wind dispersal.

Latex: The entire plant is full of a white, caustic latex containing cardiac glycosides and other toxic compounds.

हिंदी: पत्ती मोटी, नीली-हरी, पाउडर से ढकी। फूल सफेद-गुलाबी, ताज। फल दो, फूले हुए, हरे से भूरे। बीज पंख वाले।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

White aak is a shrub of the arid and semi-arid zones, native to Africa and the Arabian peninsula but now widespread in India. It is remarkably drought-tolerant, its thick waxy leaves and reduced transpiration allowing survival in very dry areas. The plant is a colonizer of wastelands and degraded soils. The flowers are visited by butterflies and other insects. The plant is the larval food of the monarch butterfly, which is one of the few insects able to detoxify the cardiac glycosides. Birds and wind disperse the parachute-like seeds.

The shrub is not browsed by goats or cattle because of the latex toxin.

हिंदी: अफ्रीकी झाड़ी, अब पूरे भारत में है। सूखी जमीन में भी जीवित रहती है। बिना पानी के भी पनपती है। परागण कीटों द्वारा। शाही तितली इसकी पत्ती खाती है।

हिंदी: मार्च-सितंबर में फूल। अगस्त-नवंबर में फल और बीज। बीज बरसात में अंकुरित होते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Butterflies and bees visit the flowers.
  • Larval food: Host plant for monarch butterfly, which sequesters the plant's toxins for protection.
  • Seed dispersal: Wind carries the parachute-like seeds long distances.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Few pests because of the toxins; mainly insects that feed on the plant are monarch butterflies and a few specialized beetles.
  • No significant fungal diseases in arid zones.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Caution: Toxicity

The entire plant, from root to latex to seeds, contains cardiac glycosides (calotropin, calactin) and other toxic alkaloids. The latex is caustic and can cause burns and dermatitis. The plant must never be ingested; handling should be with care. This is not a plant for a household with children.

Traditional and sacred use:

  • Sacred plant. In Hindu traditions, white aak is considered sacred and is used in religious rituals and offerings at temples.
  • Traditional medicine. The plant is listed in Ayurvedic and folk medicine texts for treating various ailments. However, all uses should be undertaken only under qualified guidance.
  • Latex use. The latex has been recorded in folk medicine as a caustic (for removing warts) and as a component in traditional ointments, but modern use is limited because of its toxicity.

Today. The plant is primarily valued for its sacred and cultural significance. It is also promoted in some agroforestry programs for land rehabilitation because of its drought tolerance and ability to grow in poor soils. Commercial herbal formulations sometimes include it, but sales are limited.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Phytochemical studies have identified the cardiac glycosides. Whether traditional uses are supported by modern trials is an open question. The plant's ecological value for conservation of arid lands is recognized.

हिंदी: पवित्र पौधा, मंदिर में चढ़ता है। परंपरागत दवाई में है, पर ज़हरीला है। लेटेक्स से मस्से हटाते हैं, पर खतरनाक है। सूखी जमीन को अच्छा बनाने में मदद करता है।

To be expected in dry, degraded fields and wastelands across Basti district, and occasionally along roadsides. It is not common in the eastern plains and is more typical of the drier regions of western UP and Rajasthan.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to Africa and the Arabian peninsula; widely naturalized in tropical and subtropical regions of Asia, including India.

In India: Widespread in arid and semi-arid zones; increasingly common in degraded lands.

In Uttar Pradesh: More common in western and central UP; occasional in the eastern plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Occasional in dry, degraded fields.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. How much latex does one plant produce in a month, and does it change with season? — Carefully (with gloves) score stems; collect latex; measure yield.
  2. Do monarch butterflies actually lay eggs on white aak in your area, and what percentage of larvae survive? — Watch for adult monarchs; find eggs; count caterpillars and pupae.
  3. How far can the parachute seeds disperse on a windy day? — Mark the parent plant; search downwind for seedlings; measure distances.
  4. Does white aak actually remove moisture from the air through its waxy leaves, or is the wax just a water-repellent coating? — Compare water loss on aak leaves versus other leaves in the same environment.
  5. How many seeds does one mature pod produce, and what is the germination rate? / एक फल से कितने बीज आते हैं, और उनकी अंकुरण दर क्या है? — Collect pods; count seeds; sow and monitor germination.

Field rule: A soft, grey-blue, waxy-leaved shrub with pale pink or white waxy flowers and inflated green fruit pods, growing in dry wastelands, is white aak. The latex is toxic.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described as Asclepias procera by Aiton in 1811 in Hortus Kewensis, and transferred to Calotropis by Dryander. The epithet procera means "tall" in Latin.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Apocynaceae, the dogbane family, in the order Gentianales. The genus Calotropis holds two species, C. gigantea and C. procera, both widespread in tropical Asia.

Phylogenetic notes. Calotropis procera is the drought-adapted species, while C. gigantea prefers more moist conditions. Both are naturalized in India; their relationship to African taxa is being studied.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Naturalized and increasingly common in degraded lands.
