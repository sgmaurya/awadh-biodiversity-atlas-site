सामान्य जेजेबेल / Common Jezebel

Delias eucharis (Drury, 1773) — Pieridae

सामान्य जेजेबेल — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के खेतों, बागों और सड़क किनारे झाड़ियों में उड़ने वाली एक सुंदर मध्यम आकार की सफ़ेद और नारंगी तितली। इसके पंखों का ऊपरी हिस्सा सफ़ेद होता है और निचले हिस्से पर गहरे नारंगी या लाल रंग के धब्बे होते हैं, जो पंखों को पलटते ही देखाई देते हैं। इसका कैटरपिलर विशेष रूप से गाड़ और जंगली आम के पेड़ों पर लगने वाली छत्राकार बेलों (mistletoes) को खाता है। यह तितली इन परजीवी पौधों को नियंत्रित करने में महत्वपूर्ण भूमिका निभाती है, इसीलिए यह किसान की अप्रत्यक्ष दोस्त है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeMedium-sized butterfly, wingspan 60-70 mm
ForewingWhite ground colour with a broad black border on outer margins
HindwingWhite with a narrow black border; underside shows bright orange-red or crimson spots
UndersideStriking pattern: black and orange-red, with characteristic red spots on a white or pale yellow base
FlightSlow, fluttering, close to the ground; often seen hovering around mistletoe-bearing trees
LarvaSpindle-shaped caterpillar, yellowish with black bands

Field tip: A white butterfly with black borders on the wings, often seen resting on foliage. Flip it gently and you see the stunning red-spotted underside. Look for it on trees with mistletoe, especially wild mango (Mangifera indica) and Loranthus species. Slower flyer than most butterflies.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सफ़ेद पंख, काली सीमाएँ, और पलटते ही तले पर नारंगी-लाल धब्बे। गाड़ के पेड़ों और जंगली आम पर। धीमी उड़ान।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for north Indian populations):

MeasurementRange
Body length20-28 mm
Wingspan60-70 mm
Forewing length28-35 mm

Wings:

  • Dorsal (upper) surface: Forewing white with a broad black border along the costa (front edge) and apex (tip), plus a wider black patch on the outer margin. Hindwing white with a narrow black border around the outer margin.
  • Ventral (under) surface: Highly variable and beautiful: a mix of white, pale yellow and black, with a distinctive pattern of bright orange-red, crimson or brick-red spots on the hindwing and lower forewing. The pattern is unique to each individual.

Body: Black head and thorax with some white scaling; dark abdomen.

Sexual dimorphism: Females are larger and may have slightly yellower or more extensive patterns on the underside. Males have a small sex brand on the hindwing.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants:

  • Mistletoes of the family Loranthaceae, including:
  • Loranthus ceylonicus (Fig Mistletoe)
  • Dendrophthoe falcata (Mango Mistletoe)
  • Scurrula parasitica
  • These are parasitic plants growing on mango, wild mango, sal, and other host trees.

Adult nectar sources:

  • Various flowering plants and shrubs; especially attracted to flowers on the same trees hosting mistletoes.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Spiders: Crab spiders and jumping spiders on vegetation.
  • Birds: Flycatchers and other insectivorous birds.
  • Parasitoid wasps: Lay eggs in caterpillars on mistletoe stems.
  • Ants: Tend aphids on mistletoes and may prey on young caterpillars.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Indirect benefit — mistletoe control.

  • Parasitic plant management: By feeding on mistletoes, the caterpillar indirectly helps control these parasitic plants that weaken fruit and timber trees.
  • Orchard indicator: The presence of Common Jezebel indicates the presence of mistletoe, which can guide farmers to infested trees for removal.

Pollination. Adults contribute to pollination while feeding on nectar.

Today. Stable populations in areas where mistletoes persist on trees; distribution tied closely to mistletoe availability.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Documenting which mistletoe species in Basti are preferred by Delias caterpillars, and comparing mistletoe biomass on trees with high vs. low butterfly activity, could quantify the butterfly's role in natural mistletoe control.

हिंदी: गाड़ की बेलें कमजोर करती हैं, लेकिन इस तितली का कैटरपिलर उन्हें खाता है। यह किसान की परोक्ष मदद करती है।

To be expected on mistletoe-bearing trees throughout Basti district, especially on wild mango, sal and other large trees with parasitic mistletoes. Most visible during June to November when they are most active. Look in gardens and orchards where mistletoes are common.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across Africa, southern Asia, and Southeast Asia. Common throughout the Indian subcontinent.

In India: Distributed throughout the country where mistletoes occur.

In Uttar Pradesh: Found in all agricultural and forested regions; particularly common in areas with mistletoe-bearing trees.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected on wild mango and other mistletoe-hosting trees throughout the plains.

Field rule: A white butterfly with black margins on the forewing and narrow black margin on the hindwing, with striking orange-red spots on the underside, is the Common Jezebel.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Drury in 1773 in Illustrations of Exotick Entomology, Vol. 1, p. 49, from India. Type locality is India. The genus name Delias comes from the Greek mythological figure Delias. The specific name eucharis also comes from Greek mythology.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies recognized geographically. The nominate form Delias eucharis eucharis occurs throughout India including Uttar Pradesh.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Pieridae (whites, yellows and related butterflies). Subfamily Pierinae.

Phylogenetic notes. Delias is closely related to other white butterfly genera and shares a common ancestor with Old World whites. The genus contains about 60 species, mostly from Asia and Africa, and is notable for mistletoe specialization.

IUCN status rationale. Not formally assessed by the IUCN Red List. Widely distributed and stable, with no known conservation threats.
