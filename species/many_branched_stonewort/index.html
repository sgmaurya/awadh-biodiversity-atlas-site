मैनी-ब्रांक्ड स्टोनवॉर्ट / Many-branched Stonewort

Nitella hyalina (DC.) C.Agardh, 1824 — Characeae

मैनी-ब्रांक्ड स्टोनवॉर्ट — ताजे और स्वच्छ पानी के तालाबों, पोखरों और तालों में उगने वाला एक बहुशाखित जलीय शैवाल है जो दिखने में किसी सूक्ष्म पौधे जैसा लगता है। इसके तने पर कई परतों में महीन हरी शाखाएं चक्राकार रूप में निकलती हैं, जो एक पारदर्शी श्लेष्मीय आवरण से ढकी रहती हैं। यह पानी में तैरते सूक्ष्म कणों को नीचे बैठाकर जल को स्वच्छ और पारदर्शी बनाए रखने में महत्वपूर्ण भूमिका निभाता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitSubmerged, delicate, bushy charophyte alga growing rooted in fine mud
BranchingHeteroclemous; nodes bear two distinct tiers of branchlets forming dense whorls

Field tip: Gently lift submerged aquatic tangles from clean winter ponds. Unlike rough, encrusted stoneworts, this species feels remarkably soft, bushy, and gelatinous due to its enveloping mucilage, with two sets of branchlets at each node giving it a fluffy appearance.

हिंदी:
यह पानी के भीतर बहुत कोमल और घनी झाड़ी जैसा दिखाई देता है।
अन्य स्टोनवॉर्ट की तरह यह खुरदुरा नहीं होता बल्कि छूने में लसलसा और मुलायम लगता है।
साफ पानी वाले तालाबों में नीचे कीचड़ पर यह हरी कालीन की तरह फैला रहता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

Nitella hyalina is a monoecious charophyte with slender, ecorticate main axes. The thallus is differentiated into nodes and internodes, with the internodal cells consisting of large, multinucleate single cells that lack any outer cortical sheath. The branching pattern is distinctly heteroclemous: each mature stem node produces a primary whorl of larger, once- or twice-furcated branchlets, flanked immediately above and below by accessory whorls of shorter, simpler branchlets that impart a dense, bushy appearance to the plant.

The branchlets terminate in two- or three-celled dactyls with mucronate end cells. Fertile heads develop within axils of upper whorls and are enveloped in a conspicuous gelatinous mucous cloud. Antheridia and oogonia occur together at the fertile branchlet nodes; antheridia are spherical, reddish-orange when mature, and sit adjacent to the subglobose, yellowish-green oogonia crowned by a small, persistent coronula composed of two tiers of five cells each.

हिंदी:
इसके मुख्य तने पर कोई छाल जैसी परत नहीं होती और वह पारदर्शी कोशिकाओं से बना होता है।
प्रत्येक गांठ से दो प्रकार की शाखाएं निकलती हैं जिससे यह बहुत घना दिखता है।
इसके प्रजनन अंग एक पारदर्शी जैल जैसी परत के भीतर सुरक्षित रहते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A benthic submerged macrophyte inhabiting stagnant to very slowly flowing freshwaters. It thrives primarily in water bodies characterized by low turbidity, alkaline to circumneutral pH, and moderate nutrient levels. The plants anchor in soft, fine silt or muddy sediment through a network of colourless rhizoids that also absorb minerals from the substrate.

Dense beds of this alga function as natural biofilters. By dampening wave action and stabilizing loose benthic silt, they accelerate the settling of suspended solids and maintain water clarity. The expansive, intricately divided thalli create an extensive three-dimensional surface for periphyton colonisation and provide microhabitat for rotifers, cladocerans, and aquatic insect nymphs.

हिंदी:
यह तालाब की तलहटी में कीचड़ को जमाकर पानी को मटमैला होने से रोकता है।
इसकी बारीक शाखाओं के बीच छोटे जलीय जीव और कीड़े छिपते हैं और भोजन पाते हैं।
यह केवल साफ और स्थिर पानी में ही अच्छी तरह पनपता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Exhibits a distinct seasonal phenology in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Following the monsoon rains, dormant oospores buried in pond sediments germinate as water levels stabilize and turbidity subsides. Vegetative expansion peaks during the cool post-monsoon and winter months through early spring.

Gametangia develop on mature thalli during mid-to-late winter. After fertilization, the oogonia ripen into thick-walled, dark brown or black oospores marked with distinctive spiralling striae. As seasonal ponds dry down during the intense heat of late spring and summer, the vegetative thalli disintegrate, leaving desiccation-resistant oospores in the dry mud ready to regenerate upon the arrival of the next monsoon.

हिंदी:
वर्षा ऋतु के बाद जब तालाबों का पानी स्थिर और साफ होता है, तब इसके बीजाणु अंकुरित होते हैं।
सर्दियों और वसंत में यह तेजी से बढ़ता है और इसमें प्रजनन अंग बनते हैं।
गर्मी में पानी सूखने पर पौधा नष्ट हो जाता है और इसके मजबूत बीज कीचड़ में सुरक्षित रहते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

Forms a critical component of submerged wetland plant communities across the Gangetic plain. It frequently co-occurs with other submerged macrophytes:

  • Grows alongside submerged angiosperms including Hydrilla and Hornwort
  • Shares shallow wetland margins with other macro-algae such as Stonewort
  • Provides an anchor substrate for diverse epiphytic diatoms and filamentous green algae
  • Shelters aquatic invertebrates and serves as a nursery area for small freshwater fish fry
हिंदी:
यह जल के भीतर Hydrilla और Stonewort के साथ मिलकर सघन जलीय वनस्पति बनाता है।
छोटी मछलियां और जलीय कीड़े इसके झुरमुटों में अंडे देते हैं और शरण लेते हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Not subject to conventional agricultural diseases. However, excessive cultural eutrophication and high nutrient loading from domestic runoff cause rapid overgrowth by dense surface duckweeds and thick mats of cyanobacteria. The resultant severe light attenuation suppresses charophyte photosynthesis and leads to the decline and collapse of submerged beds.

Herbivorous aquatic snails, water beetles, and foraging dabbling ducks consume parts of the vegetative thalli and ingest ripe oospores.

हिंदी:
पानी में बहुत अधिक कचरा और रासायनिक खाद बहकर आने से यह शैवाल सड़ने लगता है।
जलीय घोंघे और बत्तखें इसकी कोमल शाखाओं को खाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Plays an indirect yet invaluable role in rural agricultural landscapes by maintaining clean water supplies in village irrigation tanks and community ponds. By binding sediments and absorbing dissolved excess phosphorus and nitrogen, it naturally clarifies village reservoirs used for minor irrigation and cattle watering.

It presents no hazard of weed choking comparable to aggressive alien weeds.

हिंदी:

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Cosmopolitan distribution across warm-temperate and tropical regions of Asia, Europe, Africa, the Americas, and Australasia (Agardh 1824).

In India: Widespread across the subcontinent, recorded from freshwaters of northern India, the Gangetic basin, and peninsular states (Groves 1924).

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from perennial lakes, oxbows, and floodplain water bodies across the Gangetic plain.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a widespread submerged component of undisturbed freshwater tanks and wetland edges.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described by Augustin Pyramus de Candolle in 1815 as Chara hyalina in Flore Française, ed. 3, 5: 247; type locality: France. Later transferred to the genus Nitella by Carl Adolph Agardh in 1824 in Systema Algarum, p. 126.

Subspecies. Several morphological varieties have been described historically, but modern taxonomists generally treat it as a widely distributed, polymorphic species.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the family Characeae within the order Charales and class Charophyceae, representing an ancient lineage of green algae closely related to terrestrial land plants.

Phylogenetic notes. Charophycean green algae share critical cytological features with embryophytes, including a phragmoplast during cell division, cellulose synthase rosette complexes, and protective oospore envelopes.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species, but widely regarded as a common and ecologically secure charophyte across its global range.
