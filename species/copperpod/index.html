पीला गुलमोहर / Copperpod

Peltophorum pterocarpum (DC.) K. Heyne — Fabaceae

पीला गुलमोहर — एक बहुत सुंदर, बड़ा पेड़ जिसके फूल सुनहरे पीले रंग के होते हैं। इसके फूलों में नारंगी रंग की पंक्तियाँ होती हैं, जिससे फूल और भी सुंदर लगते हैं। पेड़ की पत्तियाँ दो बार पंखनुमा होती हैं, यानी बहुत महीन और नाज़ुक दिखती हैं। फूलों की खुशबू हल्की होती है। यह एक बहुत लंबा, शक्तिशाली पेड़ है जो सड़कों के किनारे लगाया जाता है।

Field tip: A large tree with feathery, finely divided bipinnate leaves and brilliant golden yellow flowers with orange-red markings. Flowers appear in long sprays at branch tips in May–June, creating a spectacular golden canopy. The pod is distinctive and papery.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बहुत महीन, पंखनुमा पत्तियाँ। सुनहरे पीले फूल, नारंगी रंग की पंक्तियाँ। बहुत बड़ा, शक्तिशाली पेड़।

Trunk and bark: Stout, erect, with gray-brown bark that becomes deeply fissured with age. Wood is reddish, hard, moderately durable.

Leaves: Bipinnate, large, with a central rachis bearing 10–20 secondary branches (pinnae), each bearing 10–25 small, linear leaflets. The foliage is light, feathery, allowing dappled shade to fall through.

Flowers: Regular, bright golden yellow with orange-red markings and lines on the petals, fragrant, arranged in short racemes at the end of young twigs. Flowers open in succession over weeks. Pollinated by bees.

Fruit: A legume pod, flat, reddish-brown when mature, papery-thin, 5–10 cm long, containing 1–3 seeds.

हिंदी: बिल्कुल महीन, नाज़ुक पत्तियाँ। सुनहरे फूल, नारंगी धारियाँ। चपटी, भूरी, कागज़ जैसी फली।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A large, fast-growing legume tree from tropical Southeast Asia, now widely planted as an avenue and ornamental tree in tropical and subtropical regions. Peltophorum pterocarpum thrives in warm climates and tolerates poor soils. It is drought-tolerant once established and fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root nodules. The tree grows rapidly, achieving a large size within 10–15 years. It flowers in May–June, producing masses of brilliant golden-yellow flowers with orange-red markings, making the crown appear to glow. The flowers attract bees and butterflies. The feathery foliage casts dappled shade, allowing plants to grow beneath. The tree is semi-deciduous and may drop some or all leaves in dry seasons. It has naturalized in some tropical areas.

हिंदी: बहुत तेज़ी से बढ़ता है, विशाल हो जाता है। गर्मी में खूब फूल खिलते हैं। हल्की छाया देता है। नाइट्रोजन जमा करता है।

हिंदी: अप्रैल में नई पत्तियाँ, मई-जून में खूब फूल, सितंबर-अक्टूबर में फली पकती है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinator visitors: Asian Honey Bee, Giant Honey Bee, and butterflies visit the flowers.
  • Shade-dependent companions: Neem, Guava, and other plants grow in the dappled shade beneath Copperpod.
  • Avenue companion: Often planted alongside Rain Tree, Jamun, and gulmohar on roadsides.
  • Nitrogen fixation: Enriches soil through nitrogen-fixing nodules.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf-eating caterpillars: Occasionally, defoliating caterpillars feed on young leaves; damage is light.
  • Scale insects: Rarely, scale insects infest the trunk; natural predators keep populations in check.
  • Leaf spots: Fungal spots are occasional in humid monsoon conditions; rarely cause significant damage.
  • Pod rot: Moisture-loving fungi occasionally cause mold on developing pods in wet conditions.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Ornamental value: The primary value of Copperpod is its spectacular golden flowers with orange-red markings. It is extensively planted as an avenue tree in Indian cities and towns, creating a spectacular display in May–June. The feathery foliage is also ornamental.

Timber: The reddish, hard wood is suitable for construction and fuel. It is moderately valued for timber.

Shade and soil improvement: The dappled shade allows plants to grow beneath the tree. As a nitrogen-fixing legume, it enriches the soil.

Public spaces: Extensively used in roadside plantations, parks, and public gardens for its ornamental flowers and ability to provide shade without complete darkness.

हिंदी: फूलों के लिए प्रसिद्ध। सड़कों और बाग़ों में लगाया जाता है। लकड़ी अच्छी होती है। हल्की छाया देता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical Southeast Asia, particularly the Philippines and Indonesia. Now widely planted in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide.

In India: Extensively planted in cities and towns throughout India as an avenue and park tree.

In Uttar Pradesh: Increasingly planted in urban and roadside areas throughout the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Present in town avenues and public gardens; records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described as Inga pterocarpus by Candolle in 1825, later transferred to Peltophorum by K. Heyne. The genus name Peltophorum is from Greek pelta (shield) and phorum (bearing), referring to the shield-like appendage on some flower parts. The specific epithet pterocarpus means "winged fruit."

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Fabaceae, subfamily Mimosoideae, order Fabales. Related to acacias and other legume trees.

Phylogenetic notes. Peltophorum pterocarpum is the type species of the genus and has been extensively used in landscaping and agroforestry.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; no conservation concern.
