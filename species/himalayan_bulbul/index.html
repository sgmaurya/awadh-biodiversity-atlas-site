हिमालयन बुलबुल / Himalayan Bulbul

Pycnonotus leucogenys (J.E.Gray, 1835) — Pycnonotidae

हिमालयन बुलबुल — हिमालय और तराई क्षेत्र की एक विशिष्ट बुलबुल। बेकर (1922) के अनुसार इसके गालों पर बड़े सफेद धब्बे होते हैं, सिर पर भूरी कलगी होती है और दुम के नीचे चमकीला गंधक जैसा पीला रंग होता है। यह अक्सर गांवों, खेती और घाटी के नालों व झाड़ियों में पाई जाती है तथा झाड़ियों में छोटा कटोरेनुमा घोंसला बनाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitActive, crested passerine of hill scrub, compounds, and foothill forest edges
SizeMedium-sized bulbul, similar in proportions to a [[red_vented_bulbulRed-vented Bulbul]]
Head & CrestForehead and crest hair-brown, edged with greyish-white; ear-coverts white with black border (Baker 1922, p. 389)
UpperpartsOlive-brown upper plumage; tail brown on basal half, black on terminal half with white tips (Baker 1922, p. 389)
UnderpartsPale earthy-brown below, whitish on abdomen; under tail-coverts bright sulphur-yellow (Baker 1922, p. 390)
Soft PartsIris brown or hazel; bill black; legs and feet brown to blackish brown (Baker 1922, p. 390)

Field tip: Look for the hair-brown crest, prominent white cheek patch bordered by black, and sulphur-yellow under tail-coverts in foothill scrub (Baker 1922).

हिंदी:
इसके गालों पर बड़ा सफेद पैच होता है, सिर पर भूरी कलगी होती है और पूंछ के नीचे चमकीला पीला रंग होता है (बेकर 1922)।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements as given by Baker (1922):

MeasurementRange
BiometricsLength about 200 mm.; wing 80 to 93 mm. ; tail about 85 mm.; tarsus about 20 inm,; culmen about 14 to 16 mm. (Baker 1922 as Molpastes leucogenys leucogenys)

The Himalayan Bulbul is recognized by its hair-brown crest and bright facial pattern. Stuart Baker (1922, p. 389) described the plumage: "Forehead and crest hair-brown, each feather narrowly edged with greyish white ; lores black, with a white line above them ; cheeks, round the eye, chin and throat black ; ear-coverts white, with a black patch behind them and another patch below them striped white and brown".

Upper plumage is olive-brown, while the lower plumage is pale earthy-brown, whitish on the abdomen, with the under tail-coverts bright sulphur-yellow (Baker 1922, pp. 389–390). Soft parts: iris brown or hazel; bill black; legs and feet brown to blackish brown (Baker 1922, p. 390).

हिंदी:
बेकर (1922) के अनुसार इसकी लंबाई लगभग 200 मिमी, पंख 80 से 93 मिमी, पूंछ लगभग 85 मिमी, टार्सस 20 मिमी और कल्मेन 14 से 16 मिमी होता है। इसके गाल सफेद और पूंछ के नीचे का भाग गंधक जैसा पीला होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Stuart Baker (1922, p. 390) recorded: "Though found constantly wherever there are villages and cultivation, this Bulbul is occasionally found haunting nullahs and ravines which are comparatively well-wooded."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1922) के अनुसार यह गांवों, खेतों और अच्छी तरह से वनाच्छादित नालों व खड्डों में पाई जाती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Stuart Baker (1922, p. 390) recorded that the White-cheeked Bulbul "breeds from May to July at all heights from 2,600 to 7,000 feet".

Stuart Baker (1922, p. 390) described the nest: "making a nest very similar to that of the Red-vented birds but generally less solid and well-built and decidedly smaller- It is made of the usual materials, grass, leaves, fine twigs and stems of plants mixed with odd scraps of moss, lichen and roots and lined either with fine roots or grass. It is placed low down in a bush or small tree, seldom more than 5 or 6 feet above the ground and sometimes within a foot of it. The site selected may be either in a garden or a compound or in light jungle and low scrub."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1922) के अनुसार यह 2,600 से 7,000 फीट की ऊंचाई पर मई से जुलाई में झाड़ियों में घास, पत्तियों और पतली जड़ों से छोटा कटोरेनुमा घोंसला बनाती है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya.

In Uttar Pradesh, published records are known from Balrampur (eleven records), Lakhimpur Kheri (six records), and Bahraich (two records), gathered by fifteen observers between 2016 and 2025. These are all located in the northern Terai belt adjacent to Nepal.

A first record would be a photograph of the bird with place and date, sent through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Stuart Baker (1922, p. 390) gave the distribution: "The Himalayas from Afghanistan and the extreme North-West to Bhutan and the hills of Assam, North of the Brahmaputra as far East as the Dihang River".

In Uttar Pradesh, published records are restricted to the northern border districts of Balrampur, Lakhimpur Kheri, and Bahraich in the Terai foothill strip. GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1922) के अनुसार इसका वितरण अफगानिस्तान से लेकर भूटान और असम के हिमालयी क्षेत्रों तक है। उत्तर प्रदेश में इसके रिकॉर्ड बलरामपुर, लखीमपुर खीरी और बहराइच के तराई क्षेत्रों से हैं।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by John Edward Gray in 1835 in Hardwicke's Illustrations of Indian Zoology (Vol. 2, pl. 35, fig. 3), under the basionym Brachypus leucogenys. The type locality is Darjeeling, Himalayas.

Synonyms and historical names. Blanford and Oates and Stuart Baker (1922, p. 389) treated the species under the genus Molpastes (Molpastes leucogenys). Today it is placed in Pycnonotus.

Subspecies. Stuart Baker (1922, p. 389) treated the northern birds as Molpastes leucogenys leucogenys; his entry 406 (leucotis, White-eared Bulbul) is now treated as a distinct species (Pycnonotus leucotis).

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes, family Pycnonotidae (bulbuls), genus Pycnonotus.

IUCN status rationale. Evaluated as Least Concern (LC) by the IUCN Red List (BirdLife International 2016).

हिंदी:
जॉन एडवर्ड ग्रे ने 1835 में इसका वर्णन किया था। बेकर ने इसे Molpastes leucogenys के तहत रखा था। यह पिक्नोनोटिडी कुल का सदस्य है और संकटमुक्त (LC) है।
