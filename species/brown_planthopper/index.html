भूरा फुदका / Brown Planthopper

Nilaparvata lugens (Stål, 1854) — Delphacidae

भूरा फुदका — धान का सबसे ख़तरनाक कीट, जो इतना छोटा है कि किसान तब देखता है जब खेत के बीच में पौधे झुलसकर गोल पीले-भूरे दाग़ बना चुके होते हैं। चार मिलीमीटर का भूरा फुदकने वाला कीड़ा जो पौधे की जड़ के पास पानी के ऊपर बैठकर रस चूसता है, हज़ारों की तादाद में। कीटनाशक का अंधाधुंध छिड़काव इसे मारने के बजाय बढ़ाता है, क्योंकि उसके दुश्मन पहले मरते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size3–4.5 mm; adults long-winged (4.5 mm) or short-winged (3 mm)
ColourYellowish-brown to dark brown, with a pale line down the middle of the back
ShapeWedge-shaped, wings held roof-like; jumps when disturbed
Where on the plantAt the base of the tillers, just above the water, in clusters; not on the leaves
NymphsPale cream to brown, wingless, in the same place
Damage"Hopperburn": circular patches of yellowed, then dried, then collapsed plants spreading outward from a centre
HoneydewSticky, with sooty mould on the lower stems

Field tip: Part the rice plants at the base and tap a tiller over the water: brown hoppers jump and fall onto the surface in dozens. The leafhoppers on the leaves are something else; this insect lives at the waterline. A patch of scorched-looking plants in an otherwise green field in September is hopperburn until proven otherwise.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: धान के पौधे को जड़ के पास से अलग करके डंठल को पानी पर थपथपाइए — भूरे फुदके दर्जनों की तादाद में पानी पर गिरेंगे। ये पत्तियों पर नहीं, पानी के ठीक ऊपर तने पर रहते हैं। सितंबर में हरे खेत के बीच झुलसा हुआ गोल दाग़ "हॉपरबर्न" है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length, long-winged4–4.5 mm
Body length, short-winged2.5–3.5 mm
Egg1 mm, banana-shaped, in batches in the leaf sheath
Nymphal stages5, over 12–15 days
Eggs per female200–600

Adult: Two forms. Long-winged (macropterous) adults fly and migrate, founding new colonies; short-winged (brachypterous) adults cannot fly but mature faster and lay more eggs, and appear when the colony is crowded and the plant is good. The hind tibia carries a movable spur, the mark of the Delphacidae.

Mouthparts: Piercing-sucking; feeds from the phloem of the stem, removing sap faster than the plant can replace it.

Eggs: Inserted in rows inside the leaf sheath and midrib, invisible from outside.

हिंदी: दो रूप — लंबे पंख वाले जो उड़कर नए खेत बसाते हैं, और छोटे पंख वाले जो उड़ नहीं सकते पर जल्दी बढ़ते और ज़्यादा अंडे देते हैं। अंडे पत्ती की म्यान के अंदर छिपे रहते हैं। पिछली टाँग पर एक हिलने वाला काँटा इस कुल की पहचान है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

An insect that eats only rice and the grasses nearest to it, and has followed rice across Asia. Long-winged adults ride the monsoon winds hundreds of kilometres, arriving in the eastern Gangetic plain in August; a few founders, a month, and a field can hold ten thousand hoppers per square metre. They feed at the base of the plant, in the humid shade, and when a colony drains a patch the plants collapse and the hoppers walk to the next.

Left alone, the paddy field has a great many things that eat it: wolf spiders and Green Lynx Spider, Long-jawed Orb-weaver stretched over the water, the mirid bug Cyrtorhinus that sucks its eggs, the nymphs and adults of Wandering Glider and other dragonflies, damselflies, frogs, and fungi. The classic finding of rice entomology is that broad-spectrum insecticides kill these first and the hopper rebounds to outbreak; "resurgence" is the word, and the brown planthopper is its textbook case.

हिंदी: सिर्फ़ धान और उसके पास की घास खाता है। लंबे पंख वाले मानसूनी हवाओं पर सैकड़ों किलोमीटर उड़कर अगस्त में पूर्वी मैदानों में पहुँचते हैं; महीने भर में एक वर्ग मीटर में दस हज़ार तक। खेत में इसके बहुत दुश्मन हैं — भेड़िया मकड़ी, लंबे जबड़े वाली मकड़ी, मिरिड बग जो अंडे चूसता है, व्याधपतंग, मेंढक। मोटे कीटनाशक पहले इन्हें मारते हैं और फुदका फिर फूट पड़ता है — इसे "रिसर्जेंस" कहते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Rice, Paddy, above all; also wild rice and a few related grasses, not the common field weeds.
  • Virus transmission: carries rice grassy stunt and ragged stunt viruses from plant to plant.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Spiders: wolf spiders (Pardosa), Green Lynx Spider, Striped Lynx Spider, Long-jawed Orb-weaver.
  • Bugs: the mirid Cyrtorhinus lividipennis, which eats the eggs; water bugs at the surface.
  • Dragonflies and damselflies: Wandering Glider, Ground Skimmer, Blue Dartlet.
  • Frogs: Skittering Frog and Asian Common Toad at the field edge.
  • Parasitoids and fungi: egg-parasitic wasps (Anagrus, Oligosita); the fungus Metarhizium.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

The pest. The most destructive insect of rice in Asia; outbreaks in the 1970s drove the modern science of rice pest management. In India it hits the kharif crop of the eastern plains in some years with losses from a tenth of the yield to whole fields. Eastern UP has seen outbreaks in the 2000s and 2010s.

What works (the IRRI and ICAR consensus):

  • Resistant varieties, which exist and are the first line.
  • No insecticide in the first 40 days after transplanting, so the predators establish; spraying early is the surest way to cause an outbreak.
  • Scouting at the plant base weekly from tillering; action only above a threshold (around 10 hoppers per hill at flowering), and then with a selective insecticide directed at the base, not a broad-spectrum spray on the canopy.
  • Not over-fertilising with nitrogen, which makes the plant a better host.
  • Draining the field for a few days when hoppers appear, which exposes them.
  • Alleyways in the planting to let light and predators in.

Today. A recurring threat managed well by farmers who scout and badly by those who spray on sight.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). Resistance-breaking biotypes of the hopper and insecticide resistance are both active research problems; the practical village-level lead is the "no early spray" rule, which is proven and costs nothing.

हिंदी: एशिया के धान का सबसे विनाशकारी कीट; पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश में कुछ सालों में पूरे खेत नष्ट। जो काम करता है: प्रतिरोधी किस्में; रोपाई के पहले 40 दिन कोई कीटनाशक नहीं, ताकि दुश्मन बस जाएँ (जल्दी छिड़काव ही प्रकोप का सबसे पक्का कारण है); हर हफ़्ते जड़ के पास गिनती और सिर्फ़ सीमा पार होने पर चुनिंदा दवा तने पर; नाइट्रोजन कम; फुदका दिखे तो कुछ दिन खेत सुखाना; रोपाई में गलियारे।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Rice-growing Asia and Australia, from Pakistan to Japan and the Pacific.

In India: All rice regions; most damaging in the eastern and southern states.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout the rice belt, with eastern districts most affected.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in every paddy field in the kharif.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. How many hoppers fall when you tap ten hills in your field this week? / इस हफ़्ते दस पौधों को थपथपाने पर कितने फुदके गिरते हैं? — Count weekly from tillering; a record over a season is worth more than a book.
  2. Which spiders live at the base of the rice? — Look while you count.
  3. Did the field that was sprayed early get more hoppers than the one that was not? / जिस खेत में जल्दी छिड़काव हुआ, क्या उसमें ज़्यादा फुदके आए? — Compare two neighbouring fields.
  4. When do the first long-winged adults arrive at the lights? — Note the date each year.
  5. Which variety in the village burns first? — Ask around after an outbreak.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Stål in 1854 as Delphax lugens in Öfversigt af Kongliga Vetenskaps-Akademiens Förhandlingar 11: 246. The genus Nilaparvata was erected by Distant in 1906 in the Fauna of British India. Lugens, "mourning", for the dark colour.

Subspecies. None; but "biotypes" that overcome particular resistance genes in rice are recognised and tracked.

Family and order placement. Delphacidae, the planthoppers with a tibial spur, in the Fulgoromorpha of the Hemiptera; order Hemiptera.

Phylogenetic notes. Its genome was sequenced in 2014; the symbiotic yeast-like fungi in its body, which supply nutrients the rice sap lacks, are part of why it thrives on so poor a diet.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated; a pest whose reduction is the goal.
