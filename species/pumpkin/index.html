कद्दू / Pumpkin

Cucurbita moschata (Duch. ex Lam.) Duch. ex Poiret — Cucurbitaceae

कद्दू (सीताफल, घुइयाँ) — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के हर खेत और बाग़ में बरसात के मौसम में दिखने वाली एक बड़ी, भारी बेल। इसके फूल बड़े, नारंगी-पीले, सुंदर होते हैं। फल बहुत बड़ा, गोल या लंबा, भूरा या पीला-नारंगी होता है, और कई किलो का हो सकता है। गाँवों में यह सब्जी तरह-तरह से खाई जाती है — सब्जी के रूप में पकाया जाता है, दलिया या खीर में मिलाया जाता है, और हलवा भी बनता है। बीज भी खाए जाते हैं। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश में इसे सीताफल कहते हैं क्योंकि यह भगवान राम की पत्नी सीता का पसंदीदा फल माना जाता है।

Field tip: Look for very large vines with enormous deeply lobed leaves and spectacular large orange-yellow flowers in July–August. The heavy round or oblong orange-brown fruits appear in September–October and remain on the vine.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बहुत बड़ी, भारी बेल, विशाल पत्तियाँ, बड़े नारंगी फूल, विशाल गोल या लंबा फल, भूरा या पीला-नारंगी छिलका।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (cultivated pumpkin, northern India):

MeasurementRange

Leaf length20–40 cm

Fruit diameter20–40 cm or more
Fruit weight5–30 kg

Stems: Thick, robust, herbaceous, angular, covered in stiff hairs, climbing via thick branched coiled tendrils.

Leaves: Alternate, palmately lobed with 5 distinct lobes, very large, 20–40 cm long and wide. Rough-hairy on both surfaces. Midrib and secondary veins prominent. Petiole (leaf stalk) thick, 10–20 cm.

Flowers: Unisexual, very large and showy, 8–12 cm long. Bright orange-yellow, funnel-shaped (corolla with five fused lobes). Male flowers borne on long pedicels (stalks), more numerous; female flowers on short thick pedicels with an immature fruit at base. Calyx five-lobed. Flowers in early morning; fragrant.

Fruit: A pepo (modified berry), round to oblong or pyriform (pear-shaped), 20–40 cm or more in diameter, weighing 5–30 kg or more depending on variety and growing conditions. Rind is thick, hard, orange, brown, or pale yellow at maturity. Interior flesh (mesocarp) is dense, fibrous, yellow or orange. Seeds are large, flattened, cream or brown.

हिंदी: विशाल बेल, विशाल पत्तियाँ जो 40 सेंटीमीटर तक हो सकती हैं। बड़े नारंगी फूल। फल बहुत भारी, कई किलो, कड़े छिलके वाला।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

An annual herbaceous vine native to Central America, cultivated worldwide in tropical and warm-temperate regions. In the Basti plains, it is a major monsoon and autumn crop. Growth is vigorous and rapid; from seed to flower takes 45–60 days, and from flower to mature fruit takes 60–80 days. The plant requires well-drained soil, good moisture during growth, and warm temperatures.

The large flowers are visited by Asian Honey Bee and other bees, which are essential for fruit set. The trailing vines rapidly cover ground and suppress weeds.

हिंदी: यह अमेरिकी पौधा है; गर्मी और बारिश में तेज़ी से बढ़ता है। अच्छी सिंचाई चाहिए। फूल बहुत बड़े, मधुमक्खियों को आकर्षित करते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Asian Honey Bee and other bees visit large flowers.
  • Soil benefit: Heavy vines shade soil and suppress weeds efficiently.
  • Companion planting: Sometimes intercropped with beans on same trellis or field.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Red pumpkin beetle (Aulacophora foveicollis): feeds on leaves and flowers; can defoliate seedlings.
  • Fruit fly (Bactrocera cucurbitae): damages developing fruits.
  • Powdery mildew: white coating on leaves; controlled by sulfur dust or spray.
  • Downy mildew: damages leaves in wet monsoon; worse in overcrowded fields.
  • Stem rot (fungal): from waterlogging or excessive moisture around base.
  • Generally hardy: Mature plants are less vulnerable; disease pressure increases in very wet years.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Food use:

The flesh is cooked as a vegetable with spices, made into sweet halwa (pudding) with jaggery and ghee, or added to dal and rice dishes. The seeds are roasted and eaten as snacks. Young fruits are sometimes used raw in salads.

Nutritional value: Pumpkin flesh is rich in beta-carotene (provitamin A), vitamins C and E, and fiber. It is low in calories (26 kcal per 100 g).

Cultivation advantages: Pumpkin is a reliable monsoon crop in eastern UP, requiring minimal input beyond land preparation and sowing. A single plant yields 1–5 fruits depending on variety and care. The crop is long-season (4–5 months) but relatively trouble-free compared to other vegetables.

Varieties: Landrace varieties differ in size, shape, and flesh color (orange, yellow, white). Improved varieties are also available from agricultural institutions.

Yield: Under good management, 20–30 tons per hectare are achievable; small-scale farmers typically harvest 5–15 tons.

Storage: Unlike most vegetables, pumpkin fruits keep for weeks to months if stored in a dry, cool place, making it valuable for food security.

हिंदी: गर्मी में सब्जी, हलवा, खीर भी बनता है। बीज भी खाए जाते हैं। फल महीनों रख सकते हैं। सीताफल (सीता की पसंद) कहते हैं।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to Central and South America; now cultivated worldwide in tropical and warm-temperate regions.

In India: Grown in all tropical and subtropical states; commercially important in many regions.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated in all vegetable-growing districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Very common monsoon and autumn crop.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Jean Louis Marie Poiret in 1804 (building on earlier work by Duch. and Lam.). The genus Cucurbita is derived from Latin, meaning gourd or squash. The species name moschata means "musk-scented," alluding to the aroma of some cultivars.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised; however, many cultivars exist.

Family and order placement. Cucurbitaceae (gourd family), order Cucurbitales.

Phylogenetic notes. Cucurbita moschata is one of three major domesticated Cucurbita species (with C. pepo and C. maxima), each of independent domestication in the Americas.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; wild ancestors no longer extant.
