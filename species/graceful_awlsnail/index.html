ग्रेसफुल ऑवलस्नेल / Graceful Awlsnail

Allopeas gracile (T.Hutton, 1834) — Achatinidae

ग्रेसफुल ऑवलस्नेल — बागीचों, गमलों और सड़े पत्तों के नीचे बेहद आम मिलने वाला एक नन्हा, लंबा और सूई के आकार का स्थलीय घोंघा। इसे स्थानीय रूप से 'सूई घोंघा' या 'पतला घोंघा' भी कहते हैं। इसका खोल हल्का पीला-भूरा और पारदर्शी होता है। यह रात के समय या बारिश के बाद नम जमीन पर रेंगकर सड़ी पत्तियों और काई को खाता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitMinute, very slender, turreted-cylindrical, slow-moving terrestrial land snail
Shell ShapeElongate, tower-shaped, awl-like spire composed of seven to nine convex whorls
ColorationTranslucent pale straw-yellow, horn-coloured, or glassy greenish-white
ApertureOval-rhombic, thin, completely lacking internal teeth or calcareous folds
AnimalPale yellowish-white, slender, carrying two pairs of delicate retractile tentacles

Field tip: Lift potted plants or decaying leaf litter in moist garden corners; look for numerous tiny, slender, tower-shaped yellowish shells crawling over damp earth.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: सूई जैसा लंबा और संकरा खोल, हल्का पीला-पारदर्शी रंग, मुंह पर कोई दांत न होना, और गमलों के नीचे झुंड में मिलना।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description: Small, slender, elongate-subulate terrestrial pulmonate snail. Shell dextral, thin, translucent, pale straw-yellow to horn-coloured; spire elongate, turreted, with seven to nine moderately convex whorls separated by an impressed suture; apex obtuse, rounded, smooth. Surface adorned with fine, delicate, arched axial growth striae, somewhat stronger below the suture. Aperture small, oval-rhombic, slightly oblique; peristome thin, sharp, unreflexed, outer margin arched forward; columella straight, vertical, slightly calloused, completely devoid of plicae, folds, or teeth; umbilicus very small, narrow, partly covered by columellar reflection. Living animal pale translucent yellowish-white, eye-bearing upper tentacles slender and darkly pigmented at tips.

हिंदी: यह एक लंबा और संकरा सूई जैसा घोंघा है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

  • Nocturnal Foraging: Highly photonegative; emerges from soil crevices at dusk and crawls actively over damp ground on rainy and dewy nights.
  • Gregarious Clustering: Dozens of individuals congregate together beneath moist stones, rotting logs, or flowerpots during daytime dry hours.
  • Parthenogenetic Fecundity: Capable of self-fertilization and parthenogenesis, allowing single isolated individuals to establish massive colonies.
  • Epiphragm Sealing: Forms a thin protective membranous mucus seal across the aperture during dry spells to survive desiccation.
हिंदी: यह रात के समय भोजन की तलाश में निकलता है। दिन में यह गमलों के नीचे झुंड में छिपा रहता है। यह अकेला भी अपनी संख्या तेजी से बढ़ा सकता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

  • Egg Deposition: Lays clutches of tiny spherical pearl-like white eggs in moist humus and root cavities (Mitra et al. 2005).
  • Short Incubation: Eggs hatch within two weeks into miniature transparent snails with two-whorled shells (Mitra et al. 2005).
  • Rapid Growth: Matures within six to eight weeks under warm monsoon temperatures, producing multiple overlapping broods.
  • Drought Survival: Aestivates safely beneath topsoil during scorching summer months, reviving within minutes of wetting.
हिंदी: यह मिट्टी में मोती जैसे छोटे सफेद अंडे देता है। दो हफ्तों में बच्चे निकलते हैं और बरसात में बहुत तेजी से बड़े होते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Decomposing Litter: Decaying fallen leaves of mango, guava, and neem.
  • Micro-algae and Fungi: Grazes on green algal films and saprophytic fungal spores on damp bricks.
  • Tender Seedlings: Occasionally rasps tender root hairs and fallen fruit fragments in gardens.
हिंदी: यह सड़े-गले पत्तों, काई और मिट्टी में मौजूद फफूंद को खाता है। कभी-कभी यह नन्हे पौधों की कोमल जड़ों को भी कुतरता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Carnivorous Snails: The two-toned hunter snail (Gulella bicolor) is its most specialized predator, tracking its mucus trails.
  • Birds: Common mynas and house sparrows feed on awlsnails exposed on pathways after rain.
  • Invertebrates: Ground beetles, predatory flatworms, and firefly larvae feed heavily on juvenile snails.
हिंदी: शिकारी घोंघा (Gulella bicolor), मैना, गौरैया और भृंग इन्हें बहुत चाव से खाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

  • Minor Garden Pest: Can cause minor damage to delicate seedling roots and seedlings in plant nurseries if populations surge.
  • Soil Calcium Cycling: Their thin calcareous shells break down rapidly upon death, recycling accessible calcium back into topsoil.
  • Detrital Breakdown: Plays a positive role in converting coarse organic waste into fine particulate matter in compost beds.
हिंदी: कभी-कभी नर्सरी के नन्हे पौधों को यह नुकसान पहुंचाता है, लेकिन आमतौर पर यह सड़े पत्तों को साफ कर मिट्टी को उपजाऊ बनाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Pantropical; believed to be native to tropical South and Southeast Asia, now accidentally transported worldwide across tropical and subtropical zones.

In India: Abundant and widespread across every state and union territory in rural and urban gardens.

In Uttar Pradesh: Ubiquitous across the Gangetic plain from Varanasi, Allahabad, Lucknow, and Ayodhya.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Abundant in home gardens, potted plant collections, school nurseries, and orchards throughout Basti and Ayodhya.

हिंदी: यह घोंघा पूरी दुनिया के गर्म देशों में पाया जाता है। उत्तर प्रदेश के हर बागीचे, गमले और बाड़ के नीचे यह मौजूद है।

Field rule: A minute, translucent, straw-yellow, awl-shaped turreted shell with seven to nine whorls and a toothless aperture crawling under flowerpots identifies Allopeas gracile.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Thomas Hutton in 1834 as Bulimus gracilis in Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal, 3, pp. 81–93.

Subspecies. Monotypic; previously placed in Opeas Albers, now correctly assigned to Allopeas H.B. Baker.

Family and order placement. Placed in the family Achatinidae (formerly Subulinidae), order Stylommatophora, class Gastropoda.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN; abundant, resilient, and thriving globally across anthropogenic environments.
