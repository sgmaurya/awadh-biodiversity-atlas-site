लार्ज रेज़रबेली मिनो मछली / Large Razorbelly Minnow

Salmostoma bacaila (Hamilton, 1822) — Cyprinidae

लार्ज रेज़रबेली मिनो मछली — मैदानी नदियों, स्वच्छ धाराओं और नहरों में पाई जाने वाली एक पतली, लंबी और चमकीली चांदी जैसी बहुत फुर्तीली देसी मछली (chalwa) है। इसका पेट दोनों तरफ से दबा हुआ और निचले हिस्से पर उस्तरे (razor) जैसी तेज धार वाला होता है। यह पानी की बिल्कुल ऊपरी सतह पर तेजी से तैरती है और पानी में गिरने वाले कीड़ों तथा मच्छरों के लार्वा पर झपटती है। ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में इसे बड़े चाव से खाया जाता है।

Field tip: Look near the surface of flowing rivers and clear canal waters. A slender, silvery blade-like fish swimming rapidly in schools just beneath the surface film, with an upward-directed mouth and an extremely high scale count along its lower lateral line, is the Large Razorbelly Minnow.

हिंदी: यह पतली, लंबी और उस्तरे जैसी धारदार पेट वाली चमकीली चांदी जैसी मछली है। इसका मुंह ऊपर की ओर खुला होता है और यह पानी की सतह पर बहुत तेजी से तैरती है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description:

Salmostoma bacaila has an elongate, slender, and strongly compressed body that tapers gradually from behind the head to the slender caudal peduncle (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal profile is nearly straight, while the ventral profile is moderately convex and terminates in a sharp, razor-like (trenchant) keel extending behind the pelvic fins to the vent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is pointed and compressed; eyes are large and situated in the upper anterior half of the head (Hamilton 1822). The mouth is wide, obliquely directed upward, with the lower jaw bearing a prominent symphysial knob that fits into an emargination of the upper jaw (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Barbels are completely absent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The pectoral fins are elongate and pointed, equal to or longer than the head, nearly reaching the pelvic fins (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The pelvic fins are small. The dorsal fin is inserted far back on the body, originating above the anterior third of the anal fin, and possesses 2 unbranched and 7 branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin has 14 to 17 branched rays. The caudal fin is deeply forked with equal, sharply pointed lobes (Hamilton 1822). The lateral line is complete, containing 86 to 110 very small cycloid scales, dipping deeply downward in an elongated curve along the lower belly (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Live specimens are lustrous silver with an iridescent greenish-grey back and pure mirror-like flanks (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). A brilliant, narrow silvery-blue longitudinal stripe runs along the mid-flank in sunlight (Hamilton 1822). The fins are hyaline to pale yellowish, without distinct spots or blotches.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 18 सेमी तक लंबा, तलवार जैसा पतला और चपटा होता है। मुँह ऊपर की तरफ बड़ा होता है। तराशे बहुत बारीक होते हैं और शरीर पर 86 से 110 तराशे एक कतार में होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Large Razorbelly Minnow is a pelagic, rheophilic surface-dwelling fish that thrives in flowing riverine waters (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It inhabits perennial rivers, large sandy streams, canal networks, and clear oxbow lakes.

Schools of dozens of individuals cruise tirelessly in the topmost ten centimetres of the water column (Daniels 2002). The slender, blade-like body and upward mouth are superbly engineered for fast surface pursuit (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). They hunt visually, snapping up surface-skating insects, falling terrestrial prey, and small fish fry that venture into the shallows. When startled by kingfishers or predatory catfishes, schools burst into coordinated leaps, scattering across the surface in silvery flashes before reforming instantly (Daniels 2002). They avoid stagnant, heavily weed-choked waters with low dissolved oxygen (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

हिंदी: यह मछली नदियों और नहरों के बहते पानी की सतह पर झुंड में बहुत तेजी से तैरती है। यह पानी की सतह पर गिरने वाले कीड़ों को देखकर झपटती है और खतरा होने पर पानी से बाहर कूदती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seasonal phenology in northern Indian alluvial river systems across the agricultural year (Talwar & Jhingran 1991; Daniels 2002):

Spawning takes place during the southwest monsoon from July to August (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Rising river levels stimulate courtship flights, with males pursuing females in fast surface chases over flooded sandy shallows. Females release hundreds of small, non-adhesive eggs that drift with gentle currents over sand and gravel beds. The eggs hatch after a few days, and the tiny larvae immediately take up surface positions, feeding actively on unicellular plankton and rotifers (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: बरसात में जब नदियों में नया पानी बढ़ता है, यह रेतीले उथले पानी में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में बच्चे निकल आते हैं और वे पानी की सतह पर सूक्ष्म जीवों को खाकर तेजी से बढ़ते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Salmostoma bacaila is an active surface predator and carnivorous feeder (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Terrestrial insects that drop onto the water surface, including winged ants, small beetles, grasshoppers, and flies (Daniels 2002).
  • Aquatic insects and larvae, particularly chironomid midges, mayfly naiads, and water striders (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Mosquito wrigglers and pupae (Culex and Anopheles) snatched at the surface film.
  • Small fish fry and juvenile carplets swimming near the surface.
  • Planktonic micro-crustaceans, including water fleas (Daphnia) and copepods.
हिंदी: यह मुख्य रूप से मांसाहारी मछली है। यह पानी की सतह पर गिरने वाले कीड़ों, मच्छरों के लार्वा, पानी की मक्खियों और छोटी मछलियों के बच्चों को खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Riverine predatory fishes: Catfishes like Wallago attu (Striped Catfish), Bagarius bagarius (Goonch), and Channa striata (Snakehead Murrel) ambush schools from the depths.
  • Piscivorous birds: River terns, little egrets, Indian cormorants, and common kingfishers hunt surface schools in clear waters.
  • Freshwater garfish: Xenentodon cancila (Freshwater Garfish) pursues minnows at the water surface.
  • River otters and softshell turtles: Hunt schools in shallow sandy channels.
हिंदी: बोआल, गूंज, सौर और सुइया मछली पानी के नीचे से इस पर हमला करती हैं। बगुले, पनकौवे और किंगफिशर इसे सतह से झपट लेते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Nutritional value and fisheries role: The Large Razorbelly Minnow (commonly known as chalwa) is one of the most widely consumed small indigenous fishes across northern and eastern India. It is captured in abundant quantities in the Ghaghara, Kuwano, and Rapti river networks using cast nets, shore seines, and bamboo traps. Its flesh is sweet, tender, and nutritious; because it is consumed whole with its head and bones, it provides rich dietary calcium, protein, vitamin A, and essential micronutrients to rural communities.

Public health ecosystem service: By relentlessly consuming surface-swimming mosquito larvae in canals, streams, and flooded ditches, Salmostoma bacaila contributes significantly to natural mosquito vector suppression in rural agricultural districts (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Traditional: A highly valued everyday small fish in eastern Uttar Pradesh, fried whole or cooked into dry spicy curries.

Today: Sold in substantial volumes in rural fish markets (haats) along riverbanks as an affordable, fresh food fish.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing polyculture compatibility of Salmostoma bacaila in village aquaculture ponds as an indigenous surface predator for mosquito control.

हिंदी: यह चलवा मछली के नाम से बहुत प्रसिद्ध और स्वादिष्ट देसी मछली है। ग्रामीण इसे पूरा खाते हैं जिससे शरीर को भरपूर कैल्शियम और प्रोटीन मिलता है। यह मच्छरों को खाकर बीमारियों से भी बचाती है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South Asia, natively distributed in India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Afghanistan (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Distributed throughout the Indus, Ganges, Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, and Godavari river drainages, from the Himalayan foothills to the plains (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, Gomti, and Sarda river networks and large canal systems.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident native species in the Ghaghara and Kuwano river basins, sandy tributary streams, and connecting waterways.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Cyprinus bacaila in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 265. Type locality: Freshwater rivers of Gangetic provinces.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its continental South Asian range.

Family and order placement. Order Cypriniformes, family Cyprinidae, subfamily Danioninae (or Chedrinae). The genus Salmostoma Swainson, 1839, comprises the razorbelly minnows, characterized by elongate bodies and sharp abdominal keels.

Phylogenetic notes. Salmostoma bacaila is distinguished from congeners such as Salmostoma phulo and Salmostoma acinaces by its higher lateral line scale count (86–110 versus fewer than 70) and elongate pectoral fins (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, abundant wild populations across South Asian river systems.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Salmostoma bacaila है और यह Cyprinidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
