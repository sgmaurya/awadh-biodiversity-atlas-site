कॉमन जे तितली / Common Jay

Graphium doson (C. Felder, 1861) — Papilionidae

कॉमन जे तितली — एक सुंदर काली और सफेद तितली जो बागों, खेतों और जंगल के किनारों में देखी जाती है। इसके पंखों पर सफेद धब्बे और धारियाँ काली पृष्ठभूमि पर होती हैं। नर तितलियाँ अक्सर आपस में लड़ाई करती हैं। यह तितली नीबू, संतरे और अन्य साइट्रस पौधों पर पाई जाती है। इसके कैटरपिलर रूट (Ruta) और अन्य Rutaceae पौधों को खाते हैं, जो भारतीय रसोई में उपयोग होने वाली औषधीय जड़ी-बूटियाँ हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Wing colorBlack or dark brown with white or pale patches and spots
ForewingWhite band or patch on dark background; white spots near apex
HindwingWhite spots in dark field; some specimens show greenish or bluish tint
TailHindwing has a short tail on the margin (swordtail shape)
BodyBlack thorax and abdomen
SizeWingspan 50–70 mm
AntennaeClubbed, black with white base

Field tip: Look in citrus gardens and near roadside Ruta plants. Males often perch on leaves, bobbing up and down. The distinctive black and white patterning and short tail on hindwings are diagnostic. Fast flight, but often perches for long periods.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: काली तितली, सफेद धब्बे और धारियाँ। निबू और नीबू के पौधों पर। पंख के किनारे पर छोटी पूँछ।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Percher and territorial. Males perch on leaves or plant stems, bobbing their abdomens; they chase away other butterflies and insects with aggressive flight displays. Females lay eggs singly on host plants. The butterfly is most active during warm hours and visits flowers frequently.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants:

  • Ruta graveolens (Rue; commonly called Adesso in Hindi) — primary host
  • Citrus species (lemon, orange, lime)
  • Other Rutaceae plants

Adult food:

  • Nectar from various flowers
  • Occasionally mud-puddling

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Flycatchers and other insectivorous birds
  • Parasitoid wasps: Lay eggs in caterpillars and pupae
  • Spiders: Web predators and hunters
  • Ants: Attack pupae and small caterpillars

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollinator and minor defoliator. The Common Jay is an important pollinator of flowers in gardens and agricultural areas. Its caterpillars feed on Ruta and citrus plants; in regions with high butterfly densities, defoliation can be noticeable but rarely causes economic damage. The species is generally beneficial as a pollinator.

हिंदी: फूलों को परागित करती है। निबू के पौधों पर कैटरपिलर होते हैं, पर आम तौर पर नुकसान नहीं होता।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Expected in Basti and Ayodhya districts in citrus gardens, vegetable gardens with Ruta, and forest edges. Most visible during monsoon and post-monsoon (July–November). GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Asia, from India through Southeast Asia to Indonesia and Papua New Guinea.

In India: Found throughout the country, from plains to 2,000 meters in the Himalayas.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common resident across all districts, particularly in citrus- and Ruta-growing areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Found in village gardens and forest edges throughout the year.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by C. Felder in 1861 in Lepidoptera Heterocera from Borneo specimens.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies; G. d. doson (nominotypical) occurs in South and Southeast Asia, including India.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Papilionidae (swallowtail butterflies; despite being in Papilionidae, lacks the prominent tails of other swallowtails).

Phylogenetic notes. Graphium doson is part of the subgenus Graphium sensu stricto; closely related to other Graphium species including G. agamemnon.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. A common, widespread species with no conservation concerns.
