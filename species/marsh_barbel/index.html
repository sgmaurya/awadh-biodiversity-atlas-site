तालमखाना / Marsh Barbel

Hygrophila auriculata (Schumach.) Heine — Acanthaceae

तालमखाना (जलदर्शन, जल-नीरोग, तालमखाना) — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के तालाबों, पोखरों और धान के खेतों की पानी वाली ज़मीन पर उगने वाला एक जलीय पौधा है। यह भारतीय आयुर्वेद में एक महत्वपूर्ण औषधि के रूप में जाना जाता है, विशेष रूप से मूत्र संबंधी विकारों और गुर्दे की स्वास्थ्य के लिए। पत्तियों की व्यवस्था अलग-अलग होती है — पानी में पत्तियाँ पतली होती हैं, जबकि हवा में उगी पत्तियाँ चौड़ी और मजबूत होती हैं। बैंगनी या गुलाबी फूल गर्मी के महीनों में दिखते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Plant habitAquatic to semi-aquatic herb, 30–60 cm, erect to spreading in water
LeavesPolymorphic; submerged leaves fine and threadlike, emergent leaves lanceolate to oblong, 5–15 cm
FlowersPurple, pink, or lavender, solitary at leaf axils, 1.5–2 cm
StemSoft, succulent, often purplish, rooting at nodes
RootsAdventitious from nodes; feathery submerged roots
HabitatStrictly aquatic; cannot grow in dry land

Field tip: In shallow ponds and rice fields in summer, look for a soft-stemmed plant with variable leaves (thin in water, broad in air) and purple flowers. The plant floats partly submerged in water. It spreads by rooting at the nodes where stems touch mud.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पानी में रहने वाला पौधा, नरम तने, बैंगनी फूल, पत्तियाँ अलग-अलग तरह की।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Shoot length30–60 cm

Stems and branches: Soft, succulent, angular, often purplish, unbranched or sparingly branched, rooting at nodes.

Leaves: Highly polymorphic; submerged leaves fine and filiform; emergent leaves simple, lanceolate to oblong, margins entire or slightly toothed, apex acute or obtuse.

Flowers: Solitary at leaf axils, nearly sessile or shortly pedicelled, bright purple, pink, or white. Calyx five-lobed; corolla two-lipped, upper lip emarginate, lower lip three-lobed.

Fruits: Capsules, oblong, 8–10 mm, containing small, smooth seeds.

हिंदी: पानी में पत्तियाँ बहुत पतली, हवा में चौड़ी, फूल बैंगनी या गुलाबी, बहुत नरम पौधा।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

An aquatic or semi-aquatic plant, strictly dependent on waterlogged or submerged conditions. It grows in shallow water (10–100 cm deep) and spreads vegetatively by rooting at the nodes, forming mats that trap sediment and create microhabitats for aquatic insects. The polymorphic leaves are an adaptation to its amphibious nature: submerged leaves maximize water contact for nutrient absorption, while emergent leaves conduct photosynthesis more efficiently. The plant flowers profusely in summer heat and is pollinated by insects visiting the water.

हिंदी: पानी के बिना नहीं रह सकता, गर्मी में अच्छे से बढ़ता है, अपने आप फैलता है, जलीय कीटों के लिए घर बनाता है।

The plant persists through winter in regions with permanent water; it is killed by frost in areas with seasonal ponds.

हिंदी: गर्मी में तेज़ी से बढ़ता है, सर्दी में धीमा हो जाता है, पानी रहे तो साल भर रहता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Aquatic companions: Indian Lotus, Water Hyacinth, Hydrilla in the same wetlands
  • Aquatic fauna: provides shelter for Rice-field Mosquito and other aquatic insects; eaten by waterfowl

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Rarely seriously damaged by pests; aquatic insects use the plant but do not defoliate it significantly. Fungal infections occasionally affect emergent leaves in humid conditions but do not threaten the submerged portions.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Agricultural impact. In rice paddies, the plant can become a minor weed if it overgrows, but it is often tolerated or even encouraged for its medicinal and ecological value. It does not directly compete with rice, though it may shade seedlings.

Ethnobotanical use (documented traditional knowledge; not medical advice). In Ayurvedic medicine, the whole plant is dried and used in decoctions and powders, primarily for kidney and urinary support. The plant is listed in classical Ayurvedic texts such as Bhava Prakasha and Dhanvantari Nighantu for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditional use recommends it for dysuria and nephrolithiasis, though no efficacy claim is intended.

Today. Commercially cultivated in some Ayurvedic herb-growing regions and sold as dried herb, powder, and extract in Ayurvedic markets. The plant has become an ingredient in herbal formulations for kidney health, particularly in South India where it is better known.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The plant contains various phytochemicals and has been the subject of preliminary scientific investigations regarding kidney health in published research; clinical studies remain limited.

हिंदी: आयुर्वेद में गुर्दे और पेशाब के लिए महत्वपूर्ण दवा, बाज़ार में भी मिलता है, लेकिन इसे गाँवों में कम जाना जाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical Africa; now naturalized across tropical Asia.

In India: Widespread in the Indo-Gangetic plain, coastal regions, and peninsular India, particularly in wetlands.

In Uttar Pradesh: Found in ponds, streams, and rice-growing areas throughout the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Likely present in rice fields and ponds but not yet formally confirmed.

Field rule: A water-rooted plant with variable leaves (thin in water, broad in air) and purple axillary flowers, rooting at nodes to spread, is Hygrophila auriculata.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described originally by Schumacher as Ruellia auriculata in 1827; later transferred to Hygrophila by Heine. The epithet auriculata refers to the earlike appendages on some leaf forms.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Acanthaceae (the acanthus family), order Lamiales. A genus of about 100 aquatic and semi-aquatic species.

Phylogenetic notes. Hygrophila is well-established within Acanthaceae; Asian and African species form distinct clades.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread, now naturalized across tropical Asia. No conservation concern.
