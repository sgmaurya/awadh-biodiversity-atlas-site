ऑलिव बार्ब मछली / Olive Barb

Systomus sarana (Hamilton, 1822) — Cyprinidae

ऑलिव बार्ब मछली — नदियों, तालों और बड़े जलाशयों में पाई जाने वाली एक मध्यम आकार की बहुत लोकप्रिय और स्वादिष्ट देसी कार्प (barb) मछली है। इसका शरीर जैतूनी-हरा (olive) और चांदी जैसा चमकदार होता है, तथा इसके मुंह पर चार छोटी मूंछें (barbels) होती हैं। यह पानी की तली में घोंघे, कीड़े और जलीय पौधे खाती है, और ग्रामीण बाजारों में एक कीमती खाद्य मछली के रूप में बिकती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

Field tip: Look in deep pools of slow-flowing rivers and oxbow wetlands. A sturdy silvery-olive barb with four distinct barbels, dark crescent spots on individual scales forming faint horizontal stripes, and a serrated dorsal spine is the Olive Barb.

हिंदी: मध्यम आकार की इस मछली का रंग जैतूनी और चांदी जैसा होता है। इसके मुंह पर चार मूंछें होती हैं और तराशों पर काले निशान क्षैतिज धारियों जैसे दिखते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Size as given by FishBase (Froese & Pauly 2025):

MeasurementRangeSource
Maximum length42 cm TLFroese & Pauly (2025), FishBase

Adult description:

Systomus sarana has an elongate, moderately deep, and laterally compressed body (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal profile rises gradually to the origin of the dorsal fin, while the ventral profile is smoothly curved (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is moderately small and blunt, with prominent nostrils and two pairs of barbels: the rostral barbels are shorter than the orbit, while the maxillary barbels are noticeably longer, extending past the eye margin (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin is inserted roughly midway between the snout tip and caudal base, supported by a stout bony spine serrated finely on its posterior edge and 8 branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin has 5 branched rays. The pectoral fins are pointed, and the caudal fin is deeply forked with equal pointed lobes (Hamilton 1822). The lateral line is complete with 30 to 34 scales (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Live coloration is rich olive-grey to dark brownish-green on the back, fading to gleaming silver and white on the belly (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Each scale along the upper flanks bears a dark basal spot, producing several faint longitudinal stripes along the body (Hamilton 1822). The fins are dusky yellow to orange-tinted, with the caudal fin often displaying a narrow blackish trailing edge.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 42 सेमी तक लंबा, मजबूत और चपटा होता है। मुंह पर दो जोड़ी मूंछें होती हैं। पीठ का पंख कांटेदार और पूँछ बीच से कटी हुई होती है। शरीर पर हल्की धारियाँ नजर आती हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Olive Barb is an active benthopelagic fish inhabiting river channels, deep lakes, canals, and floodplains (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It prefers clear to moderately turbid waters with sandy or muddy substrates rich in organic detritus and benthic microfauna.

Adults form loose schools that forage along the bottom and middle water layers (Daniels 2002). Using its sensory barbels and subterminal mouth, the fish browses over bottom silt and submerged logs, probing for hidden snails, insect nymphs, and aquatic worms (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). During the dry season, schools congregate in deeper pools of perennial rivers like the Ghaghara and Kuwano, dispersing into inundated floodplain lakes (tals) during the high-water monsoon season.

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की निचली और मध्य सतहों में झुंड बनाकर रहती है। अपनी मूंछों की मदद से यह तलहटी के कीचड़ में कीड़े और घोंघे तलाशती है।

Spawning coincides with the onset of the southwest monsoon rains (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Ripe adults undertake short upstream migrations to flooded grass meadows and inundated margins where water flow is gentle. Females lay thousands of non-adhesive or weakly adhesive yellowish eggs among submerged vegetation. The eggs hatch after a few days, and tiny fry remain sheltered in vegetated nurseries before joining riverine populations as waters recede in autumn (Daniels 2002).

हिंदी: मानसून में पानी बढ़ने पर यह मछली उथले किनारों पर जाकर अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में बच्चे निकल आते हैं और बरसात के मौसम में तेजी से बड़े होते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Systomus sarana is an opportunistic omnivore with a marked preference for benthic invertebrates and aquatic plants (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Small benthic molluscs, including freshwater snails and bivalve spat, crushed with powerful pharyngeal teeth (Daniels 2002).
  • Aquatic insects, including chironomid midge larvae, caddisfly cases, and odonate naiads (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Submerged macrophytes, filamentous algae, and organic bottom detritus.
  • Micro-crustaceans, including ostracods, copepods, and small freshwater shrimps.
हिंदी: यह घोंघे, छोटे सीप, जलीय कीड़े, काई और सड़े हुए पौधों के अंश खाती है। इसके गले के मजबूत दांत घोंघों के खोल को आसानी से तोड़ देते हैं।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Large predatory fishes: Major riverine predators like Wallago attu (Striped Catfish), Channa marulius (Great Snakehead), and Bagarius bagarius (Goonch) prey on juveniles and sub-adults.
  • Piscivorous birds: Herons, cormorants, and storks capture adults in shallow oxbow wetlands.
  • River otters: Smooth-coated otters actively hunt schooling barbs in perennial rivers.
  • Marsh crocodiles: Juveniles and adults are taken opportunistically in undisturbed river sanctuaries.
हिंदी: बड़ी शिकारी मछलियाँ (जैसे बोआल और सौर), बगुले, पनकौवे और उदबिलाव इस मछली का शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Commercial and nutritional food value: The Olive Barb is one of the most commercially important indigenous carps in northern and eastern India. Its flesh is tender, flavorful, and highly sought after in local fish markets, fetching higher prices than many cultured carps. It provides high-quality animal protein, omega-3 fatty acids, and essential trace minerals to local consumers.

Aquaculture potential: Systomus sarana is widely recognized by fisheries scientists as a premier candidate for medium-carp aquaculture and wetland ranching, as it thrives on natural plankton and supplemental feeds without disrupting major carps (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Traditional: Prized across Awadh and Purvanchal as a premium festival fish (sarana).

Today: Harvested from wild rivers and reservoirs using gill nets and seine nets; sold fresh in regional markets.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Optimizing induced breeding protocols in local hatcheries to produce fingerlings for stocking seasonal floodplains.

हिंदी: यह बहुत स्वादिष्ट और पौष्टिक मछली है, जिसे बाजारों में ऊँचे दामों पर खरीदा जाता है। यह गाँव के तालाबों और मत्स्य पालन के लिए बहुत उपयुक्त प्रजाति है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South Asia, natively occurring in India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, and Myanmar (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Distributed throughout the major river systems of northern, central, and peninsular India, including the Indus, Ganges, Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, Godavari, and Krishna basins (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, Sarda, and Gomti drainage systems and large floodplain reservoirs.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a native resident in the Ghaghara, Kuwano, and Ami river corridors and major oxbow lakes.

हिंदी: यह भारत, पाकिस्तान, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश की प्रमुख नदियों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश की घाघरा और गंगा नदी प्रणालियों में यह व्यापक रूप से उपस्थित है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Cyprinus sarana in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 307. Type locality: Ponds and rivers of India (Gangetic provinces).

Subspecies. Monotypic in modern taxonomic frameworks. Several nominal subspecies and regional forms previously described under Puntius sarana have been elevated to distinct species or synonymized (Menon 1999).

Family and order placement. Order Cypriniformes, family Cyprinidae, subfamily Barbinae. Long placed in Puntius, modern systematic and molecular revisions assign it to the genus Systomus McClelland, 1838.

Phylogenetic notes. Systomus sarana represents the type species of the sarana species-complex, distinguished from allied taxa by its 4 barbels, serrated dorsal spine, and higher lateral line scale count (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread distributions, though local populations face pressure from overharvesting and river regulation.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Systomus sarana है और यह Cyprinidae परिवार की सदस्य है। इसे पहले Puntius sarana के नाम से जाना जाता था।
