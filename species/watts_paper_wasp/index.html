वॉट्स पेपर वास्प / Watt's Paper Wasp

Polistes wattii Cameron, 1900 — Vespidae

वॉट्स पेपर वास्प — गांवों के छप्परों, बरामदों और पेड़ों की टहनियों पर खुले छत्ते बनाने वाली एक चमकीली पीले रंग की सामाजिक ततैया। इसका पतला और लचीला शरीर पूरी तरह सुनहरे-पीले रंग का होता है। यह फसलों के लिए मित्र कीट है क्योंकि यह हानिकारक इल्लियों को पकड़कर अपने लार्वा को खिलाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitSlender, social paper wasp constructing unenclosed umbrella-shaped paper combs
ColorationUniformly pale golden-yellow to honey-yellow without dark thoracic bands
AbdomenElongate, spindle-shaped with very fine transverse pale sutures
LegsLong and yellow, trailing loosely below the body during hovering flight
WingsNarrow, smoky-yellow to hyaline with a ferruginous anterior margin

Field tip: Look beneath the eaves of mud houses or cattle sheds for small open paper combs suspended by a central stalk and attended by slender yellow wasps.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पूरी तरह सुनहरी पीली ततैया, लंबे लटकते पैर, और मकानों के छप्परों के नीचे डंडी से लटके कागजी छत्ते।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description: Slender and elongate social wasp. Head seen from front broadly triangular, clypeus convex with an acute apex; mandibles stout, short, and toothed at apex. Antennae 12-segmented in females, 13-segmented in males, uniformly reddish-yellow. Pronotum narrowed anteriorly, posterior margins extending back to tegulae; mesoscutum flat and minutely punctate. First metasomal segment sub-pedunculate, gradually widening towards apex; subsequent gastral segments smooth and velvety. Body coloration almost entirely pale honey-yellow, occasionally with faint rufous suffusion on the vertex or scutum.

हिंदी: मध्यम आकार की ततैया जिसका रंग एकसमान पीला-शहद जैसा होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

  • Diurnal Activity: Actively forages during warm daylight hours from mid-morning to late afternoon, showing high heat tolerance in the Gangetic summer.
  • Social Organization: Primitively eusocial; founded in spring by one or more fertile queens (gynes) that establish a single-tier unenclosed comb made of chewed wood pulp mixed with saliva.
  • Thermoregulation: Workers collect water droplets and fan their wings vigorously over nest cells to cool developing larvae during peak pre-monsoon heat waves.
  • Defensive Temperament: Generally non-aggressive when foraging on flowers, but defends the nest vigorously with painful stings if the structure is bumped or disturbed.
हिंदी: यह एक सामाजिक ततैया है जो चबाए हुए सूखे रेशों से कागजी छत्ता बनाती है। गर्मी में यह छत्ते पर पानी छिड़ककर पंखों से हवा करती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

  • Colony Initiation: Overwintered mated females emerge in late February or March and select sheltered sites beneath eaves, beams, or mango branches.
  • Brood Development: Eggs hatch into white legless grubs that hang suspended in open hexagonal cells, fed continuously on chewed masticated caterpillar paste.
  • Pupation: Mature larvae spin silky white caps over their cell mouths and transform into pupae, emerging as adult workers after a brief pupal rest.
  • Seasonal Cycle: Colonies expand through summer and monsoon, reaching peak worker population by September; males and next-generation queens emerge in autumn before colony disintegration.
हिंदी: वसंत ऋतु में रानी ततैया छत्ता शुरू करती है। मानसून के अंत तक कॉलोनी बड़ी हो जाती है और सर्दियों में समाप्त हो जाती है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Larval Prey: Caterpillars of armyworms, looper moths, cabbage butterflies, and small beetle larvae hunted among crop foliage.
  • Adult Diet: Floral nectar, overripe fallen fruit juices from jamun and mango, and sugary honeydew secretions.
  • Water Foraging: Frequently visits puddles, village handpumps, and moist soil margins to gather moisture for nest cooling and mastication.
हिंदी: वयस्क ततैया फूलों का रस पीती है, लेकिन अपने बच्चों के लिए फसलों को नुकसान पहुंचाने वाली इल्लियों का शिकार करती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian Predators: Black drongos, green bee-eaters, and house sparrows capture adults on the wing or pick foragers from foliage.
  • Reptilian Predators: Indian garden lizards and house geckos stalk resting wasps near outdoor lights and nest colonies.
  • Parasitoids: Strepsipteran stylopid endoparasites (Xenos) and pyralid moth larvae (Pyralis) infest and consume wax combs and pupae.
हिंदी: भुजंगा (ब्लैक ड्रोंगो), गिरगिट और छिपकलियां इन्हें खा जाते हैं। कुछ परजीवी कीट इनके छत्तों पर हमला करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

  • Biological Pest Suppression: Extremely valuable predator in rural Awadh, consuming significant numbers of pest caterpillars in vegetable patches and orchards.
  • Stinging Hazard: Can deliver painful stings if nests near doorways, verandahs, or well-curbs are inadvertently disturbed.
  • Passive Coexistence: When nesting above normal reach under high eves, colonies remain peaceful throughout the season and provide continuous pest patrol.
हिंदी: यह किसानों की सच्ची मित्र है जो इल्लियों को खाकर फसलों को बचाती है। छत्ते से छेड़छाड़ न की जाए तो यह डंक नहीं मारती।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to South and Southwest Asia, occurring from the Arabian Peninsula through Iran, Pakistan, and India to southern China.

In India: Widespread across the northern plains, Rajasthan, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, and the Gangetic basin.

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from Lucknow, Varanasi, Prayagraj, and surrounding agricultural tracts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected throughout village verandahs, orchard tree forks, and canal-side dwellings across Basti and Ayodhya.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Peter Cameron in 1900 as Polistes wattii in Annals and Magazine of Natural History, (7) 6(35), pp. 410–419.

Subspecies. No distinct subspecies are currently recognized across northern India.

Family and order placement. Belongs to the family Vespidae, subfamily Polistinae (paper wasps), within the order Hymenoptera.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN; widespread, common, and ecologically adaptable across rural human-modified habitats.
