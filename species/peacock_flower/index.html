कृष्णचूड़ा / Peacock Flower

Caesalpinia pulcherrima (L.) Sw. — Fabaceae

कृष्णचूड़ा — मंदिरों और बागों में लगाई जाने वाली झाड़ी, जिसके बहुरंगे, अलंकृत फूल पक्षी के पूँछ जैसे दिखते हैं — नारंगी पँखुड़ियों पर लाल धारियाँ। बरसात और गर्मी के अंत में खूब खिलता है। पत्तियाँ पंखनुमा, पहली नज़र में सागौन की पत्तियों जैसी। कृष्ण से इसका कोई संबंध नहीं; कृष्ण का मोर ही इसका नाम दिलाता है।

The testBipinnate leaves with numerous small leaflets; highly ornamental flowers with contrasting colours and prominent red stamens

Field tip: Peacock Flower is an ornamental shrub with distinctive bipinnate (doubly pinnate) leaves, resembling certain timber trees. The flowers are unmistakable: orange or yellow petals with red veins and bright red or orange stamens that extend far beyond the petals, resembling a peacock's tail. Flowers mostly in monsoon and post-monsoon.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पंखनुमा पत्तियाँ, बहुत छोटी पत्तियों की बनी। नारंगी, लाल फूल, बीच में लंबे लाल पुंकेसर, मोर की पूँछ जैसे।

Morphology आकारिकी

Stem and branches: Erect to spreading, much-branched, thorny (prickles on stems and branches), green when young, becoming woody and grey with age.

Leaves: Bipinnate (compound in two stages), 30–45 cm long. Pinnae are arranged in pairs, and each pinna bears many small (1–2 cm) leaflets. Leaves are sensitive in some plants, closing at night or under stress.

Flowers: Showy, 4–5 cm in diameter, borne in terminal racemes (flower clusters). Petals are orange, red, or yellow, with conspicuous red veins or spots. The five stamens are long (4–6 cm), bright red or orange, extending well beyond the petals, giving the flower its peacock-like appearance.

Fruit: A flattened, brown legume (pod), 8–12 cm long, with several seeds.

Thorns: Small prickles on stems and branches; handle with care.

हिंदी: पंखनुमा पत्तियाँ, बहुत छोटी पत्तियों की। तने पर कांटे। फूल नारंगी, लाल, पुंकेसर लंबे, लाल, मोर जैसे। फली लंबी, भूरी।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Peacock Flower is a tropical shrub, native to the West Indies and tropical America, widely cultivated as an ornamental in tropical and subtropical regions. In eastern UP it grows in warm, well-drained soils and is moderately drought-tolerant. The plant is a legume and may form nitrogen-fixing root nodules, though these are not always prominent. The showy flowers are visited by bees, butterflies, and occasionally hummingbirds (where present). The plant does not naturalize; it is purely ornamental.

हिंदी: वेस्टइंडीज़ का पौधा। गर्म जलवायु में अच्छा। सूखा भी सहन करता है। तितलियों को आकर्षित करता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Bees and butterflies visit the flowers.
  • Garden companions: Planted with gulmohar, Yellow Bells, and other ornamental shrubs.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf-eating beetles and caterpillars.
  • Scale insects on woody stems.
  • Powdery mildew in humid weather; not usually serious.
  • No major fungal diseases in eastern UP.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Ornamental and cultural use (documented use):

Peacock Flower is valued for its spectacular, ornamental flowers, which are used in decorations and religious contexts. The plant is sacred in some Hindu traditions, associated with Krishna and the peacock. The flowers are offered in temples and used in garlands. The common name "Krishna-chura" (Krishna's crest) refers to the peacock associated with Krishna.

Today. Peacock Flower is grown in gardens and parks across eastern UP for its beautiful flowers. The plant is moderately easy to maintain and responds well to pruning.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The ethnobotany of Caesalpinia species in South Asia and their cultural significance merit further documentation.

हिंदी: कृष्ण से जुड़ा, मोर से जुड़ा। मंदिरों में पूजा में आता है। बागों में सुंदरता के लिए लगाया जाता है।

Field rule: A semi-deciduous shrub with large, bipinnate leaves composed of many small leaflets, and showy flowers with orange or yellow petals and bright red stamens extending prominently beyond the petals, resembling a peacock's tail, is Peacock Flower.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described by Linnaeus as Poinciana pulcherrima. Moved to Caesalpinia by Swartz. The epithet pulcherrima means "most beautiful."

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Fabaceae (legume family), order Fabales. Caesalpinia is a tropical genus of about 100 species.

Phylogenetic notes. Caesalpinia pulcherrima is closely related to other ornamental Caesalpinia species, including C. sappan and C. crista.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; not threatened.
