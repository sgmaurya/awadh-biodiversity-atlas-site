वन कलमी / Obscure Morning Glory

Ipomoea obscura (L.) Ker Gawl. — Convolvulaceae

वन कलमी (obscure morning glory, field morning glory) — भारत की एक जंगली बेल जो खेतों की मेड़ों, बंजर जमीन और झाड़ियों पर उगती है। बेल नाज़ुक, चढ़ने वाली। पत्तियाँ छोटी, दिल जैसी। फूल पीले या नारंगी-पीले, सुबह खुलते हैं और दोपहर तक बंद हो जाते हैं। गर्मी की ऋतु में तेजी से बढ़ता है।

Field tip: A slender, hairy climber with small heart-shaped leaves and tiny yellow morning flowers. Often seen scrambling over hedgerows and field margins in summer. Flowers open early and close by afternoon, hence the common name "morning glory." Easily overlooked because of its delicate stature.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: दिल जैसी पत्तियाँ। पीले फूल जो सुबह खुलते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for plants in eastern India):

Stems: Slender, herbaceous, twining, hairy, branching, greenish or purplish, becoming woody at the base.

Leaves: Alternate, petiolate, ovate to cordate (heart-shaped), 2–6 cm long and 1.5–5 cm wide, hairy on both surfaces, entire or slightly lobed at the base, apex acute to acuminate.

Flowers: Solitary or in small clusters (2–3) in leaf axils, on hairy pedicels. The corolla is funnel-shaped, bright yellow to orange-yellow, 1.5–2.5 cm across. The tube is yellow; the limb opens in a star or funnel shape. The flowers open in early morning (around dawn) and close by mid-afternoon, a characteristic behaviour that gives this and related species the common name "morning glory." Stamens and style project slightly beyond the corolla. Flowers are visited by small bees and flies.

Fruit: A globular to slightly ovoid, membranous capsule, 0.8–1 cm across, with a persistent calyx at the base. The fruit contains 4 small, round seeds.

Pubescence: The entire plant is covered with soft, spreading hairs (pilose), which is diagnostic.

हिंदी: पूरा पौधा बालों से भरा है। पत्तियाँ दिल जैसी। सुबह सुबह पीले फूल खिलते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Ipomoea obscura is a warm-season climbing annual of Indian origin that grows in open, disturbed habitats from May through November. The plant grows weakly and does not compete strongly with established crops or shrubs, but it climbs readily on hedgerows, fences, and other vegetation. The plant prefers well-drained soils and is drought-tolerant once established.

The flowers are visited by small insects, including flies and tiny bees. The plant is self-compatible and produces viable seed readily. Seeds are dispersed by water, on farm machinery, and occasionally by livestock.

हिंदी: गर्मी में तेजी से बढ़ता है। पास-पास की चीज़ों पर चढ़ता है। सूखी ज़मीन पर भी चल सकता है।

Seeds are dormant and germinate when temperatures exceed about 15–20°C. The plant is an obligate warm-season annual in eastern UP and does not survive frost.

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Field margin companions: Found with other warm-season climbers in hedgerows and fence rows.
  • Climbing support: Scrambles over shrubs, hedges, and herbaceous plants.
  • Pollinator support: The flowers attract small bees and flies; provides nectar and pollen.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • No specific serious pests; leaves are occasionally eaten by generalist herbivores.
  • Generally healthy; fungal diseases are rare.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

As a weed:

Ipomoea obscura is a minor weed of warm-season cultivation in eastern UP. The plant is weak and does not compete strongly with crops, though it may twine on standing plants. It is easily controlled by hand-weeding and does not persist if the field is fallowed.

No traditional medicinal or ethnobotanical use is recorded for this species in India or Southeast Asia.

Today. The plant is a minor, sporadic weed of field margins and wastelands in eastern UP, more notable for its attractive flowers than for any agricultural significance.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The taxonomy and distribution of Ipomoea obscura in India are poorly known; field surveys could clarify its range and frequency.

हिंदी: खेतों की मेड़ों और बंजर जमीन पर आता है। गंभीर खरपतवार नहीं है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical India and Southeast Asia; distributed across tropical and subtropical regions.

In India: Widespread in all states, from the plains to about 1,500 m in the Himalaya.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in field margins and wastelands across the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in field margins and disturbed areas; GBIF records from the wider region.

Field rule: A hairy, slender climbing annual with small heart-shaped leaves and bright yellow morning flowers that close by afternoon is Obscure Morning Glory.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described as Convolvulus obscurus by Linnaeus (1771), and transferred to Ipomoea by John Bellenden Ker Gawler (1825). The epithet obscura may refer to the obscure or undistinguished nature of the flowers compared to showier species.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Convolvulaceae, order Solanales. The genus Ipomoea holds about 600–700 species, mostly of tropical and subtropical origin.

Phylogenetic notes. Ipomoea obscura is closely related to Ipomoea triloba and Ipomoea nil (other Ipomoea species common in India).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread and native; not assessed.
