बटेर / Common Quail

Coturnix coturnix (Linnaeus, 1758) — Phasianidae

बटेर — यह एक छोटा, छिपा रहने वाला गेम पक्षी है जो सर्दियों में खेतों और घास की जगहों में अक्टूबर से अप्रैल तक दिखता है। इसे खेतों में पैदल चलते समय अचानक उड़ता हुआ देखा जाता है, या इसकी विशिष्ट तीन-नोट कॉल सुनी जाती है। नर की आवाज़ एक प्रसिद्ध संकेत है कि सर्दी आ गई है। यह जमीन पर रहता है और अनाज व कीड़े खाता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeSmall; 16–18 cm long
PlumageMottled buff and brown; cryptic
HeadSmall, with buff and dark streaks
UnderpartsBuff-white with brown streaking
BehaviourGround-dweller; reluctant flier; flushed from grass
CallMale: distinctive "wet-my-lips" or three-note call

Field tip: In winter cultivation and grassland, flush by walking through grass; the bird rises suddenly with whirring flight and drops again after a short distance. Highly cryptic; more often heard than seen. Males call from favored perches at dawn and dusk.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें:
छोटा, जमीन पर रहने वाली चिड़िया।
भूरे और सफेद रंग की धारीदार।
घास में छिपा रहता है।
नर की विशिष्ट आवाज़: "वेट-माई-लिप्स"।

Size: 16–18 cm long; wingspan 34–39 cm. A small galliform bird.

Plumage (highly variable): Buff to warm brown overall, heavily mottled and streaked with darker brown. Head buff with brown crown-stripes. Throat white or buff. Breast and underparts buff to buff-white with brown streaking on flanks. Males generally warmer, rustier than females. Females duller, greyer. No sexual dichromatism in field marks.

Bill: Short, pointed, dark.

Legs and feet: Pale yellowish; medium length.

Eyes: Dark brown.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Male advertising call: Distinctive three-note wet-my-lips or wet-wet-wet, given from grassland perches at dawn and dusk; frequency around.
  2. Contact call: Soft chirping.
  3. Alarm call: Sharp, loud calls when flushed.
हिंदी: नर की विशिष्ट आवाज़ — "वेट-मेरे-होंठ"। घास में छिपा रहता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Ground-dwelling granivore and insectivore: Common Quails feed on:

  • Grass seeds and grain from stubble fields
  • Aquatic plant seeds
  • Insects and small invertebrates
  • Occasionally green plant shoots

Feeding behaviour: Walk and peck in grassland and cultivation, searching for seeds and insects. Mostly diurnal feeders.

Social behaviour: Solitary or in pairs; occasionally small loose gatherings in optimal habitat. Highly secretive; skulk in grass rather than flight.

Breeding: Male calls from favored calling sites; territory size small.

Predators: Martial eagle, sparrowhawks, jackals, domestic cats.

हिंदी: अकेला या जोड़ी में रहता है। घास में छिपा रहता है। बीज और कीड़े खाता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Winter visitor: Breeds in central Asia and Mediterranean; arrives October–November, departs April–May.

Arrival and departure: First birds arrive mid-October; numbers peak December–February; departure March–April.

Breeding: Occurs on breeding grounds (central Asia); does not breed in India.

Moult: Gradual post-breeding moult before migration.

Roosts: Ground roosts in dense grass; nocturnal, relatively quiet roosters.

हिंदी: अक्टूबर में आता है, अप्रैल तक रहता है। अपने देश में अंडे देता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Martial eagle, sparrowhawks, shikras, kestrels
  • Mammalian predators: Jackals, wild dogs, domestic cats, foxes
  • Parasites: Avian helminths
हिंदी: बाज़ और सियार इसका शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Potential pest: May occasionally damage sprouting crops, but generally beneficial due to insectivory.

Hunting: Historically a game bird; hunting now prohibited under Wildlife Protection Act 1972.

Protected status: Protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972.

हिंदी: यह विधि अधिनियम 1972 के अंतर्गत संरक्षित है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds across Palearctic from Spain to Japan; winters in Africa and southern Asia.

In India: Distributed across northern India as a winter visitor in grasslands and cultivation.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widespread winter visitor in grain fields and grassland.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Regular winter visitor in grain stubble and grassland; October to May with peak December–February.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1758 as Tetrao coturnix. Type locality is Europe.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies; C. c. coturnix (western form) and C. c. conturbans (Asian form) may both occur in India.

Family and order placement. Order Galliformes; family Phasianidae (pheasants, quails, partridges).

IUCN status rationale. Least Concern. Populations stable and widely distributed.
