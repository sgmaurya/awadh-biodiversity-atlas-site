ग्रे काउंट तितली / Grey Count

Tanaecia lepidea (Butler, 1868) — Nymphalidae

ग्रे काउंट तितली — यह गहरे भूरे रंग की एक तितली है जिसके दोनों पंखों के बाहरी किनारे पर राख जैसी धूसर रंग की एक लगातार पट्टी होती है। यह पट्टी अगले पंख के सिरे पर संकरी होती है और पिछले पंख के निचले कोने तक पहुँचते-पहुँचते पंख के लगभग एक-तिहाई हिस्से को घेर लेती है। इसकी निचली सतह गेरुई-भूरी और हल्की बैंगनी-धूसर होती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
WingspanMale and female 70–80 mm (Bingham 1905, p. 273); Evans (1932, p. 150) gives 70–80 mm for lepidea lepidea and 65–75 mm for lepidea miyana
UppersideDark brown, paler in the female, with very obscure black markings across cells and oblique discal fascia on forewing (Bingham 1905, p. 272)
Terminal bandAsh-grey continuous band along termen of both wings, broadening from apex of forewing to tornus of hindwing where it covers about one-third of the wing; narrowly bordered outwardly with brown in female; cilia white (Bingham 1905, p. 272)
UndersideMale ochraceous brown, female bright ochraceous; paler on hindwing; margins suffused with lilacine-grey; cells with dark brown sinuous lines and loop-like markings (Bingham 1905, pp. 272–273)
Male scent patchPatch of specialized dark scales above vein 4 on upperside of hindwing (Bingham 1905, p. 273)

Field tip: Look for a dark brown nymphalid butterfly with a broad ash-grey outer border running continuously along both forewing and hindwing, widening towards the hindwing tornus (Bingham 1905, p. 272).

हिंदी: इसके पंखों का फैलाव 70 से 80 मिमी होता है। गहरे भूरे पंखों के बाहरी किनारे पर राख जैसी धूसर पट्टी इसकी मुख्य पहचान है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRangeSource
Expanse (male and female)70–80 mmBingham (1905, p. 273)
Expanse (lepidea lepidea)70–80 mmEvans (1932, p. 150)
Expanse (lepidea miyana)65–75 mmEvans (1932, p. 150)

Bingham (1905, pp. 272–273) describes the adult: "Upperside dark brown, paler in the ♀, with very obscure black markings of transverse lines across the cells of both fore and hind wings and an oblique discal fascia on the fore wing ; an ash-grey continuous band along the termen of both fore and hind wings, gradually broadening from the apex of the fore, where it is very narrow, to the tornus of the hind wing, where it covers about one-third of the wing. In the ♀ this band is outwardly narrowly bordered with brown. Cilia white. Antennae, head, thorax and abdomen dark brown above; beneath, the antennae ochraceous, the rest dusky white washed with ochraceous. Underside : ♂ ochraceous brown, ♀ bright ochraceous. In both sexes the colours paler on the hind wing; the fore wing somewhat narrowly, the hind wing much more broadly suffused with lilacine-grey on the terminal margins and along the dorsal margin of the hind wing ; cells of both wings with dark brown sinuous transverse lines and loop-like markings ; both fore and hind wings crossed by somewhat diffuse broad discal and narrower postdiscal dark bands, prominent on the fore, obscure on the hind wing. Male with a patch of specialized dark scales above vein 4 on the upperside of the hind wing."

Larval Stage

Bingham (1905, p. 273) quotes Davidson & Aitken on the larva ("the usual Euthalia form; colour green with a dorsal row of light red ocelli with blue centres ; spines tipped with yellow") and pupa ("More narrowed at the head than E. garuda, green, all the points golden tipped with black, and a few large spots of gold between").

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार नर के पिछले पंख के ऊपरी भाग पर विशेष गहरे शल्कों का एक धब्बा होता है। डेविडसन और एटकेन के अनुसार इसकी इल्ली हरी होती है जिस पर नीले केंद्र वाले हल्के लाल गोल निशान और पीले सिरे वाले काँटे होते हैं।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Bingham (1905, p. 273) gives its range in the low hot valleys of the Himalayas eastward from Almora, as well as Orissa, Bengal, and southward. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya: four photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri by one observer on three dates (31 October 2023, 3 September 2025, and 15 November 2025). A first local record would be a clear photograph showing the dark brown wings and broad ash-grey terminal band with place and date, sent through the About page.

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) ने इसे अल्मोड़ा से पूर्व हिमालय की निचली गर्म घाटियों, उड़ीसा और बंगाल में दर्ज किया है। जीबीआइएफ (GBIF) में लखीमपुर खीरी से एक ही प्रेक्षक के चार अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती या अयोध्या से अभी कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Bingham (1905, p. 273) gives: "On the continent of India in the low hot valleys of the Himalayas eastward from Almora; in Orissa, Bengal and southwards in the Central Provinces, the Nilgiris, Canara, Mysore and Travancore. East of the Bay of Bengal from Assam to the Malay Peninsula."

In India: Low hot valleys of the Himalayas eastward from Almora, Orissa, Bengal, Central Provinces, Nilgiris, Canara, Mysore, Travancore, and Assam (Bingham 1905, p. 273).

In Uttar Pradesh: GBIF holds four photographed records from Lakhimpur Kheri by one observer (31 October 2023, 3 September 2025, and two on 15 November 2025).

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी: बिंघम (1905) के अनुसार यह भारत में हिमालय की निचली घाटियों से लेकर दक्षिण भारत, असम और मलय प्रायद्वीप तक पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में जीबीआइएफ पर लखीमपुर खीरी के चार अभिलेख हैं, पर बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई अभिलेख नहीं है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Butler, A. G. (1868). Annals and Magazine of Natural History, ser. 4, vol. 1, p. 71, as Adolias lepidea (cited in Bingham 1905, p. 272).

Subspecies. Evans (1932, p. 150) treats Indian races as lepidea miyana Fruhstorfer ("lepidea miyana, Fruh. (65-75). The Grey Count. N. Kanara, Orissa.") and lepidea lepidea Butler ("lepidea lepidea, But. (70-80). Kumaon— Assam."); neither range names the United Provinces.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Nymphalidae, genus Tanaecia (GBIF). Bingham (1905, p. 272, entry 291) placed it in Euthalia.

Phylogenetic notes. Placed in Tanaecia in modern classifications (GBIF), whereas Bingham (1905, p. 272) treated it as Euthalia lepidea.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated on the IUCN Red List.

हिंदी: इसका पहला वैज्ञानिक वर्णन बटलर (1868) ने Adolias lepidea के रूप में किया था। बिंघम (1905) ने इसे Euthalia में रखा था, जबकि आधुनिक वर्गीकरण में यह Tanaecia वंश में है।
