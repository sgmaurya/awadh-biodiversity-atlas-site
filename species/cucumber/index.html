खीरा / Cucumber

Cucumis sativus L. — Cucurbitaceae

खीरा — पूरे भारत में सबसे आम सब्जी; गर्मी और बरसात दोनों मौसमों में उगाई जाती है। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के हर गाँव और शहर के बाग़ों में यह पाई जाती है। खीरा एक लंबी, हरी, कांटेदार सब्जी है जो कच्ची खाई जाती है या अचार बनाया जाता है। इसका फूल पीला, सुंदर होता है और मधुमक्खियों को बहुत आकर्षित करता है। बेल बहुत तेज़ी से बढ़ती है और मचान (trellis) पर चढ़ाई जाती है। गर्मी में इसकी सब्जी ठंडी, पानी वाली, और पाचन में हल्की होती है।

Field tip: Look for sprawling or climbing vines with deeply lobed leaves and bright yellow flowers in spring and monsoon. Harvest the long green prickly fruits when 15–25 cm long and still tender (before they yellow).

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: नरम बेल, कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ, पीले फूल, लंबी हरी कांटेदार सब्जी।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (cultivated cucumber, northern India):

MeasurementRange
Vine length1–3 m
Leaf length10–20 cm
Flower diameter3–4 cm
Fruit length15–30 cm
Fruit diameter3–5 cm
Seed length8–10 mm

Stems: Tender, herbaceous, angular, hirsute, climbing via branched coiled tendrils.

Leaves: Alternate, deeply 3–5 lobed (the lobes themselves often toothed), rough-hairy on both surfaces. Midrib prominent. Petiole (leaf stalk) long, 5–10 cm.

Flowers: Unisexual, large, bright yellow, borne singly or in small clusters in the leaf axils. Male flowers more numerous, on longer pedicels; female flowers on short pedicels with an immature fruit at the base. Petals five, wrinkled. Calyx five-lobed.

Fruit: A pepo (modified berry), elongate cylindrical, 15–30 cm long, 3–5 cm diameter. Rind smooth or with small prickly bumps (tubercles), dark green. Flesh pale green or white, crisp, juicy, with numerous pale flattened seeds.

हिंदी: नरम, हल्की बेल, कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ। फूल बड़े, पीले, एकलिंगी। फल लंबा, गोल, हरा, कांटेदार, अंदर से हल्का, पानी वाला, कुरकुरा।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

An annual herbaceous vine native to South Asia (probably India), cultivated worldwide. In Basti, it is grown as both a spring crop (February–May) and a monsoon crop (June–September), though the spring crop is preferred because the monsoon rains cause disease. Growth is rapid; from seed to flowering takes 30–40 days, and from flower to harvest-ready fruit takes 8–10 days.

The large yellow flowers are visited by Asian Honey Bee and other bees for pollen and nectar. Cross-pollination by bees is essential for fruit development. The vines spread rapidly and can climb trellises vigorously.

हिंदी: यह भारत की अपनी सब्जी है। गर्मी और बरसात दोनों में उगती है, लेकिन गर्मी में बेहतर फल। बरसात में बीमारियाँ आती हैं। मधुमक्खियों के बिना फल नहीं बनते।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Asian Honey Bee and other bees visit flowers; essential for fruit set.
  • Field companions: Often grown with Ivy Gourd, Pointed Gourd, Bottle Gourd on shared trellises.
  • Benefit to soil: Vines shade soil and suppress weeds.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Red pumpkin beetle (Aulacophora foveicollis): severe on seedlings and young leaves.
  • Fruit fly (Bactrocera cucurbitae): damages developing fruit.
  • Powdery mildew: white coating on leaves, worse in spring crop; controlled by sulfur spray or dusting.
  • Downy mildew: damages leaves in humid conditions; remove infected leaves.
  • Angular leaf spot (bacterial): spreads in wet monsoon; avoid overhead watering.
  • Fusarium wilt: soil-borne; use disease-free seed and rotate fields.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Food use:

Cucumber is eaten fresh, sliced raw in salads, or used as a vegetable in cooked dishes. Pickling is common; unripe fruits are preserved in brine.

Nutritional value: Mostly water (96%), low calories (16 kcal per 100 g), with vitamin C and fiber.

Cultivation advantages: Cucumber is one of the easiest vegetables to grow. The plant is productive; a single vine yields 10–20 harvestable fruits over 6–8 weeks. Sowing directly in the field (not nurseries) is standard practice. The crop requires moderate water and benefits from trellising for space efficiency.

Varieties: Landrace varieties are common in Basti, varying in length and prickliness. Hybrid varieties and improved varieties like Poinsett are also available.

Yield: Under good management, 10–15 tons per hectare are achievable. Small-scale farmers typically harvest 3–6 tons.

Seasonal availability: Fresh cucumber is available year-round in vegetable markets, but the spring and monsoon crops are the main supplies. The vegetable is very affordable.

हिंदी: सबसे आम सब्जी; सलाद में कच्ची खाते हैं या पकाते हैं। अचार भी बनाता है। एक बेल से बहुत फल मिलते हैं। बहुत कम देखभाल से आती है।

Expected as a cultivated vegetable in every village garden and vegetable plot throughout Basti district. Visible April–May (spring crop) and August–September (monsoon crop). The plant is so ubiquitous that it is part of the standard village agriculture.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native probably to South Asia (India, Myanmar); now cultivated worldwide.

In India: Grown universally in home gardens and commercial vegetable fields.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated in all vegetable-growing districts, both plains and foothills.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Very common spring and monsoon vegetable crop.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

Anyone can investigate these | कोई भी इन प्रश्नों की जाँच कर सकता है

  1. How many flowers appear on a cucumber vine from sowing to end of season, and what percentage become fruit? — Count flowers and fruits daily.
  2. Does hand-pollination increase fruit set compared to bee pollination? — Hand-pollinate flowers and compare with unbothered flowers.
  3. Can cucumber seeds be saved from fruits harvested at full maturity, and what is germination rate? / क्या पके हुए खीरे से बीज निकालकर रखा जा सकता है? — Save seeds and test germination.
  4. How often should cucumber be harvested to maximize total yield? — Harvest at different intervals (every 2 days vs. every 4 days) and compare total yield.
  5. Which powdery mildew management method (sulfur spray, neem oil, removal of infected leaves) is most effective and least expensive? — Compare different control methods.

Field rule: A tender climbing vine with deeply lobed rough leaves, bright yellow flowers, and long slender green prickly cucumber fruits harvested tender is cucumber; it is the most common garden vegetable.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Carl Linnaeus described this species in 1753 in Species Plantarum (Vol. 2, p. 1011). The genus Cucumis is derived from Latin, of uncertain origin, possibly from a Greek word meaning "drunk" or from an Egyptian source. The species name sativus means "cultivated."

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Cucurbitaceae (gourd family), order Cucurbitales.

Phylogenetic notes. Cucumis sativus is closely related to Cucumis melo (muskmelon) and other Cucumis species. The genus has about 30 species, mostly African.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; wild populations unknown.
