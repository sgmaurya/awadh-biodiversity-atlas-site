प्लेन्स क्यूपिड तितली / Plains Cupid

Luthrodes pandava (Horsfield, 1828) — Lycaenidae

प्लेन्स क्यूपिड तितली — यह एक बहुत छोटी नीली तितली है जो खेतों और घास के मैदानों में आम है। इसके नर के पंख गहरे नीले होते हैं और मादा के पंख भूरे या नीले-भूरे होते हैं। इसके पंखों के नीचे (ventral side) हल्के रंग के होते हैं जिन पर छोटे काले और लाल धब्बे होते हैं। इसके पंखों के पिछले हिस्से (hindwing) में एक लाल रंग की धब्बा आँख जैसा होता है जो इसे पहचानने में मदद करता है। इसकी इल्ली दलहन पौधों पर पाई जाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
WingspanSmall; 26–32 mm
Male dorsalDark blue or violet-blue wings
Female dorsalGreyish-brown or brownish-blue
Ventral surfacePale brown with small black spots and distinctive red spots
Hindwing undersideRed or reddish eyespot (diagnostic)
FlightWeak, fluttering; stays low

Field tip: Look for small blue butterflies in grassland. The red or reddish spot on the hindwing underside is distinctive. Males are darker blue; females are brownish.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: छोटी नीली तितली, पंखों के पीछे लाल धब्बा, घास की जगहों पर।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Adult wingspan26–32 mm
Body length11–15 mm
Forewing length12–15 mm
Caterpillar length (mature)14–18 mm

Adult butterfly: Small. Males have dark blue or violet-blue upper wings; females are greyish-brown or dusky. Ventral surface is pale brown with small black spots and distinctive red or reddish spots. The hindwing underside has a prominent red eyespot.

Caterpillar: Green or yellow-green, small, feeding on Leguminosae.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Plains Cupid is a small, weak-flying butterfly of open grassland and cultivated areas. It stays low over vegetation, flying only short distances before landing. Males perch on grass and flowers, watching for females. The species is associated with legume-rich areas and is active year-round, with higher abundance in warm months.

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Stage / SeasonEvent
EggLaid on host plant; hatches in 3–5 days
Larva (caterpillar)Green or yellow-green; 3 instars; develops over 8–12 days
PupaSmall chrysalis on host plant; emerges in 6–9 days
AdultYear-round; multiple generations; peak in warm months

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Larval host plants (Leguminosae/Fabaceae):

  • Gram (chickpea) (Cicer arietinum)
  • Various wild legume herbs
  • Clover and related species
  • Other herbaceous legumes

Adult nectar sources:

  • Small wildflowers and weeds

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Spiders: Web spiders and hunting spiders
  • Small birds: Capture flying adults
  • Parasitoid wasps: Attack caterpillars
  • Ants: Predate eggs

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pollinator service. Visits small flowers and provides pollination.

Gram crop association. Found in gram-growing areas and wild legume habitats.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Horsfield in 1828 from Indian specimens. The genus Luthrodes comprises small Asian blue butterflies.

Subspecies. The nominate subspecies L. p. pandava occurs throughout the Indian subcontinent.

Family and order placement. Order Lepidoptera, family Lycaenidae.

Phylogenetic notes. Luthrodes is a genus of small Oriental blues, characterized by the distinctive red eyespot on the hindwing.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Common and widespread with stable populations.
