नर्सेज पिक्सी व्याध पतंग / Nurse's Pixie Damselfly

Ischnura nursei Morton, 1907 — Coenagrionidae

नर्सेज पिक्सी व्याध पतंग — उथले दलदलों, घास वाली नालियों और धान के खेतों के पास पाई जाने वाली भारत की सबसे छोटी और अत्यंत आकर्षक डैमसेल्फ़्लाई में से एक है। इसके नर का सीना और आगे का पेट चमकीले लाल-गुलाबी रंग का होता है, बीच का हिस्सा पीला-हरा और पूँछ पर चमकीला नीला धब्बा होता है। यह घासों के बिल्कुल नीचे उड़ती है और छोटे मच्छरों तथा फसल के कीटों को खाती है।

Field tip: Look in shallow grassy seepage pools, flooded ditches, and low vegetation along paddy bunds. When perching horizontally on a blade of grass, the combination of a scarlet-red anterior body, pale yellow midriff, and bright blue terminal spot on such a miniature insect is entirely distinctive.

हिंदी: पानी भरी नालियों और धान की मेड़ों पर उगने वाली घास में इस नन्ही डैमसेल्फ़्लाई को देखें। इसका आगे का लाल-गुलाबी रंग और पीछे नीला धब्बा इसे बहुत खास बनाता है।

Adult description:

Ischnura nursei is one of the smallest zygopterans in the Oriental region, displaying spectacular multicoloured patterning in mature males (Fraser 1933). The male head has a black vertex with small, circular, pale green postocular spots; the frons and labrum are pale citron-green (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is bright scarlet-crimson on the dorsum, bisected by a prominent black mid-dorsal stripe, while the lateral sides are bright greenish-yellow fading to pale yellowish-white beneath (Fraser 1933).

The male abdomen exhibits striking segmental colour zonation: segments 1 through 4 are bright scarlet-red; segments 5 and 6 are citron-yellow with narrow black apical rings; segment 7 is velvety black above; segment 8 is brilliant azure-blue; and segments 9 and 10 are black marked with red or blue highlights (Subramanian 2005). The forewing pterostigma in males is uniquely dimorphic: black at the inner half and bright carmine or pink on the outer half (Fraser 1933).

Females are polychromatic, occurring in an andromorphic (male-like) form and a heteromorphic form (Fraser 1933). Heteromorphic females lack the scarlet coloration, displaying an olive-green or orange-brown thorax with black dorsal lines and an abdomen that is dull greenish-yellow overlaid by a broad, continuous bronze-black dorsal band (Subramanian 2005). In both sexes, the wings are narrow, hyaline, and have petiolated bases (Fraser 1933).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is tiny, slender, and translucent green or pale brown. The caudal lamellae are elongate, tapered, and delicately fringed with fine microscopic setae, allowing respiration in shallow, warm, sun-warmed marsh water.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 14 से 17 मिमी लंबा होता है। इसका आगे का हिस्सा लाल, बीच का हिस्सा पीला और पिछला हिस्सा नीला होता है। मादा हल्के भूरे और हरे रंग की होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Nurse's Pixie Damselfly is a weak-flying, sun-loving inhabitant of shallow, ephemeral, and semi-permanent water bodies (Subramanian 2005). It thrives in open agricultural landscapes, frequenting weed-filled field drains, waterlogged borrow pits, and grassy edges of rice fields (Fraser 1933). Its flight is low, cautious, and fluttering, rarely venturing into open air more than ten to fifteen centimetres above surrounding grassy blades.

Males perch prominently on horizontal grass blades or sedge culms, defending tiny mating territories spanning less than half a metre of shoreline (Subramanian 2005). They regularly flick their wings when neighbouring males approach. Copulation occurs in the tandem wheel position perched among dense grasses. Oviposition is performed by the female unaccompanied by the male (non-contact guarding), depositing eggs into submerged grassy vegetation.

हिंदी: यह नन्ही डैमसेल्फ़्लाई धूप में सक्रिय रहती है और बहुत कम ऊंचाई पर उड़ती है। नर घास के तिनकों पर बैठकर अपने छोटे से क्षेत्र की रखवाली करता है और मादा अकेले अंडे देती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

The female uses her sharp ovipositor to insert elongate eggs into the soft tissue of submerged aquatic grasses, sedges, and plants like Ipomoea aquatica (Water Spinach). The eggs hatch after a few days. Naiads develop rapidly through several instars over a few weeks, an adaptation to life in temporary monsoon pools that dry up within months. Multiple overlapping generations are produced throughout the active warm season.

हिंदी: मादा पानी के अंदर डूबी घासों में अंडे देती है जो कुछ दिनों में फूटते हैं। इसका जलीय निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में तेजी से बड़ा हो जाता है ताकि पानी सूखने से पहले वयस्क बन सके।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Ischnura nursei is an insectivorous predator throughout its aquatic and adult existence (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small disease-transmitting mosquitoes, such as Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Anopheles culicifacies (Malaria Mosquito).
  • Minute agricultural sap-sucking pests of rice, including Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper).
  • Micro-dipterans, including midges (Chironomidae) and fruit flies along field margins.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito larvae and pupae, ostracods, copepods, and tiny aquatic oligochaetes in shallow water.
हिंदी: यह छोटी डैमसेल्फ़्लाई धान की फसलों को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले भूरे और हरे फुदकों तथा बीमारी फैलाने वाले मच्छरों को खाकर किसानों को फायदा पहुँचाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented preying upon Ischnura nursei in Gangetic plain agroecosystems:

  • Spiders: Grass-dwelling orb-weavers and wolf spiders (Lycosa) stalk resting adults on paddy bunds.
  • Predatory insects: Larger dragonflies like Crocothemis servilia (Scarlet Skimmer) and Orthetrum sabina (Slender Skimmer) ambush flying adults.
  • Amphibians: Small juvenile frogs like Euphlyctis cyanophlyctis (Skittering Frog) catch adults perched low over water.
  • Fish: Surface-feeding freshwater fish readily devour nymphs in shallow pools.
हिंदी: छोटी मकड़ियाँ, बड़े व्याध पतंग और पानी के किनारे बैठे छोटे मेंढक इस नन्ही डैमसेल्फ़्लाई को अपना शिकार बनाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Beneficial insect in paddy ecosystems: Nurse's Pixie Damselfly is a prime beneficial predator in rural rice paddies of northern India (Subramanian 2005). Because its population explodes during the monsoon kharif season, it feeds voraciously on nymphs and adults of the brown planthopper (Nilaparvata lugens Brown Planthopper) and the green leafhopper (Nephotettix virescens Green Leafhopper), helping suppress viral vector outbreaks without synthetic chemical insecticides.

Mosquito control: Both adults and aquatic naiads contribute to natural suppression of vector mosquitoes (Culex and Anopheles) in stagnant rural ditches.

Traditional: No traditional uses are known.

Today: Recognized by agricultural entomologists as a key beneficial bio-control agent in lowland rice farming.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing field predation efficiency on planthopper swarms under integrated pest management regimes.

हिंदी: धान के खेतों में यह एक अत्यंत लाभकारी कीट है। यह फसल को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले कीटों और बीमारी फैलाने वाले मच्छरों का प्राकृतिक रूप से सफाया करता है।

Nurse's Pixie Damselfly is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in shallow flooded ditches, grassy margins of rice fields, and temporary roadside pools. Look carefully among low grass blades in full morning sunshine from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph with date and location, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Confined to South Asia, occurring widely across India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Fraser 1933).

In India: Found across the northwestern dry plains, Rajasthan, Punjab, the Gangetic floodplains, central India, and eastward to West Bengal and Bihar (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from irrigation canals, agricultural wetlands, and floodplains across western, central, and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a widespread resident breeding damselfly in rural paddy fields, borrow pits, and seasonal drainage depressions.

हिंदी: यह प्रजाति भारत और पड़ोसी देशों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के मैदानी इलाकों में धान के खेतों और पानी भरी नालियों में यह आसानी से मिल सकती है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Kenneth J. Morton in 1907 as Rhodischnura nursei in Transactions of the Entomological Society of London, 1907(2), page 306, plate 24, figures 4–6. Type locality: "Deesa, N. Gujarat" (India).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized across its South Asian distribution.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Zygoptera, family Coenagrionidae. Described originally in the monotypic genus Rhodischnura Laidlaw, 1919; modern phylogenetic and morphological revisions place it firmly within the cosmopolitan genus Ischnura Charpentier, 1840.

Phylogenetic notes. Although historically placed in Rhodischnura due to its unique crimson coloration and dimorphic male pterostigma, genitalic morphology and genetic markers place it nested within Ischnura (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure South Asian endemic that flourishes in heavily modified agricultural landscapes.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Ischnura nursei है (पहले इसे Rhodischnura nursei कहा जाता था)। यह Coenagrionidae परिवार की सदस्य है और प्रकृति में सुरक्षित है।
