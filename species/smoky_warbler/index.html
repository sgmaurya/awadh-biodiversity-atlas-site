स्मोकी वार्बलर / Smoky Warbler

Phylloscopus fuligiventer (Hodgson, 1845) — Phylloscopidae

स्मोकी वार्बलर — सर्दियों में हिमालय और तिब्बत के ऊंचे इलाकों से तराई और गंगा के मैदानों में आने वाली एक छोटी, गहरे रंग की फुदकी है। यह नदी-नालों, पोखरों और दलदली झीलों के किनारे उगे घने नरकुलों और कास-पतवार में छिपकर रहती है। इसका गहरा धुंधला-भूरा रंग और छोटी पतली चोंच इसे सूखी घास और काई के बीच पहचानना कठिन बना देती है। यह जलस्रोतों के पास कीड़े-मकोड़े खाकर प्राकृतिक संतुलन बनाए रखती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitSmall, active, skulking leaf-warbler of wet thickets

Field tip: Look low down in tangled waterside vegetation, flooded tall grasses, and ditch margins in winter. Its exceptionally dark, soot-tinged upperparts, lack of wing bars, and habit of staying hidden within dense reed thickets separate it from brighter leaf-warblers.

हिंदी:
यह गहरे धुएं जैसे भूरे रंग की छोटी चिड़िया है जो नरकुल और दलदली झाड़ियों के एकदम निचले हिस्से में छिपी रहती है।
पंखों पर कोई सफेद धारी नहीं होती और भौंह बहुत हल्की धुंधली होती है।
जलकुंभी या कास के झुंडों में इसकी छोटी तीखी आवाज से इसकी मौजूदगी का पता चलता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements as given by Baker (1924 as Phylloscopus fuligiventer):

MeasurementRange
Wing52–60 mm (Baker 1924)

The Smoky Warbler is a small, compact, very dark leaf-warbler without wing bars. The upper plumage is deep smoky-brown, almost blackish-brown, sometimes faintly tinged with dark olive. The wings and tail are dark brown, the feathers narrowly edged with olive-brown. The supercilium is narrow and dull green, reaching behind the eye. Sides of the head are dull buff mixed with dusky-brown. The lower plumage is dull oil-green, with the breast and flanks strongly suffused with dusky-brown.

The bill is fine, blackish, with the lower mandible horn-brown. The iris is dark brown. The legs and feet are ochraceous-green or brownish. Sexes are similar in plumage.

Vocalisations. The call is a sharp, clicking tack or chick, often doubled, uttered from deep within dense cover. In winter quarters, the full song is rarely heard, consisting of a rapid, thin rattling trill given occasionally before spring migration.

हिंदी:
इसके शरीर का ऊपरी भाग गहरा धुंधला भूरा और निचला भाग मटमैला हरा-भूरा होता है।
नर और मादा देखने में बिल्कुल एक जैसे होते हैं।
यह झाड़ियों में छिपे रहकर एक छोटी तीखी 'टिक-टिक' जैसी आवाज निकालती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Phylloscopus fuligiventer is a dedicated skulker, strongly tied to damp, dense, marshy habitats during its winter stay in northern India. Unlike canopy-dwelling leaf-warblers, it spends most of its time within a metre of the mud or water surface, hopping through tangled reed stems, sedges, and decaying vegetation. It moves with quick, jerky hops, flicking its wings and tail nervously as it investigates crevices and leaf bases.

The species feeds solitary in winter, maintaining loose feeding territories within reedbeds. When disturbed, it drops silently into the deepest cover rather than taking flight, making observation difficult without patience.

हिंदी:
यह चिड़िया पानी के बिल्कुल पास, घनी घास और झाड़ियों में नीचे-नीचे फुदकती है।
यह खुले आसमान में कम ही उड़ती है और खतरा महसूस होने पर नरकुल के झुरमुट में समा जाती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Baker (1924, p. 461) had eggs taken in Tibet at almost 14,000 feet on 4 August 1921. In winter he gives it south throughout Assam and Eastern Bengal.

They remain in wetland margins throughout the cold winter months before departing northward toward montane breeding grounds as warm weather returns.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1924) के अनुसार यह तिब्बत के उच्च पर्वतीय क्षेत्रों में अगस्त के समय प्रजनन करती है।
बेकर के अनुसार जाड़े में यह असम और पूर्वी बंगाल तक आती है और गर्मी शुरू होने पर वापस लौट जाती है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

The species is an active insectivore, feeding primarily on small invertebrates gleaned from aquatic and semi-aquatic vegetation. Prey items include:

  • Midges, small flies, and mosquitoes
  • Leafhoppers and small wetland beetles
  • Small caterpillars and insect larvae
  • Aquatic spiders and tiny snails found on moist stems

It gleans prey deliberately from stems, undersides of leaves, and wet litter, occasionally making brief hovering sorties to snatch insects from open water margins.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Breeds in the Himalayas from central Nepal east through Bhutan, south-eastern Tibet, and south-western China. Winters in the Himalayan foothills, northern and north-eastern India, and Bangladesh.

In India: Winter visitor across the Gangetic plain, Assam valley, and adjacent foothill tracts.

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter visitor to river systems, jheels, and marshlands across the central, northern, and eastern plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Winter visitor to wet grasslands, canal edges, and riverine jheels; recorded on passage and wintering in the core districts.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Brian H. Hodgson in 1845 in the Proceedings of the Zoological Society of London (Part XIII, no. 146, p. 31), under the basionym "Horornis fuligiventer". The type locality is Nepal.

Subspecies. Three subspecies are generally recognized: the nominate P. f. fuligiventer across the central and eastern Himalayas, P. f. tibetanus in southern Tibet, and P. f. weigoldi in south-western China.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Phylloscopidae (leaf-warblers).

Phylogenetic notes. Phylloscopus fuligiventer is closely allied to the Dusky Warbler (Phylloscopus fuscatus), forming an adapted lineage of dark, ground-foraging leaf-warblers characteristic of montane scrub and marsh thickets.

IUCN status rationale. Evaluated as Least Concern (LC) by the IUCN Red List due to its extremely large geographical range and stable global population trends.
