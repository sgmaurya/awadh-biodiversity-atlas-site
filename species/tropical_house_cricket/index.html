ट्रॉपिकल हाउस क्रिकेट / Tropical House Cricket

Gryllodes sigillatus (Walker, 1869) — Gryllidae

ट्रॉपिकल हाउस क्रिकेट — घरों, पशुशालाओं और दीवारों की दरारों में रहने वाला एक हल्का भूरा झींगुर, जिसकी मादा पंखहीन और नर छोटे पंखों वाला होता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

Field tip: Pale tan cricket with distinct dark head and neck bars; females have no visible wings and a long ovipositor, while males possess truncated leathery wing pads.

हिंदी: हल्का पीला-भूरा रंग, सिर और गर्दन पर काली आड़ी पट्टियां, मादा बिना पंखों वाली और नर छोटे पंखों वाला होता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

A medium-sized, somewhat flattened, light-colored gryllid cricket showing pronounced sexual dimorphism in wing development. The head is relatively broad, rounded, pale yellowish-tan, characterized by a striking, dark transverse bar running between the eyes across the vertex. The compound eyes are dark and hemispherical; antennae are exceptionally long, slender, and thread-like, extending well past the tip of the cerci.

The pronotum is slightly wider than long, pale straw-yellow with a contrasting dark brown or blackish transverse band running along its posterior border, and mottled darker marks across the disc. The abdomen is segmented, pale beneath, with mottled darker dorsal bands.

Wing development is strongly dimorphic: adult males possess shortened tegmina that cover approximately half the abdomen, specialized with stridulatory veins (file and scraper) and a clear acoustic mirror. Adult females are completely flightless and apterous for practical purposes, bearing only minute, vestigial scale-like tegminal pads at the sides of the metanotum. The female bears a prominent, straight, needle-like ovipositor nearly as long as the abdomen. Hind legs are equipped with thickened femora and spinose tibiae adapted for rapid scurrying and jumping (Gershman et al. 2010).

हिंदी: इसका शरीर मध्यम और चपटा होता है। नर के पंख आधे पेट तक होते हैं जिनसे वह गाता है, जबकि मादा में पंख नहीं होते और पीछे लंबा सुई जैसा अंडा देने वाला अंग होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Gryllodes sigillatus is a strictly nocturnal, synanthropic species that conceals itself during daylight in dark, dry, narrow crevices. Preferred daytime retreats include voids under stones, cracks in brickwork, gaps behind household furniture, and cattle fodder racks.

At dusk, adult males emerge near the entrance of their crevices and begin persistent acoustic signalling. The song is a sharp, high-pitched, multi-pulse trill delivered through rapid tegminal friction. During courtship, the male transfers an elaborate spermatophore that includes an enormous gelatinous nuptial gift (spermatophylax), which the female consumes after copulation while sperm transfers into her spermatheca (Gershman et al. 2010; Walker 2003). Both sexes are opportunistic omnivores, scavenging on cereal grains, kitchen crumbs, dead insects, and organic detritus.

हिंदी: यह रात में बाहर निकलता है और दिन में दरारों में छिपा रहता है। शाम होते ही नर झनकार भरी आवाज़ में गाते हैं। यह जूठन और कचरा खाता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

In domestic and rural indoor settings, breeding occurs almost continuously through the year, aided by stable artificial warmth. In outdoor microhabitats, mating peaks during warm, humid monsoon and post-monsoon nights (July to October).

The mated female deposits eggs individually into loose, dry sand, dust, or powdery soil within sheltered crevices using her long ovipositor. Nymphs hatch within a few weeks, resembling miniature wingless adults. They pass through several molts over several weeks before reaching reproductive maturity.

हिंदी: घरों और पशुशालाओं की गर्माहट में यह साल भर प्रजनन करता है। मादा फर्श या दीवार की दरारों में अंडे देती है और दो-तीन महीने में बच्चे बड़े हो जाते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Omnivorous and generalist scavenger. Not dependent on specific living plants. Readily feeds on stored grains, flour dust, dry food scraps, fallen fruit peels, pet feed, dead insect remains, and organic litter around cattle sheds and rural homes. Often shares domestic niches with Gryllus bimaculatus and Gryllotalpa orientalis.

हिंदी: यह सर्वभक्षी है जो घर का जूठा अन्न, आटा, गिरे हुए फल और मृत कीड़े-मकौड़ों को खाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Commonly hunted around buildings by house geckos (Hemidactylus), huntsman spiders, wolf spiders, and centipedes. In domestic courtyards and outbuildings, foraging domestic fowl, babblers, and shrews actively seek out hiding crickets by day.

हिंदी: घरों की छिपकलियां, मकड़ियां और आंगन में चुगने वाली मुर्गियां व पक्षी इसे बहुत तेजी से पकड़कर खाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Gryllodes sigillatus is not an agricultural field crop pest, as it stays closely associated with buildings, courtyards, and storage yards. In rural households and grain warehouses, large populations can cause minor nuisance through food contamination or chew marks on paper and fabrics. Its loud, rhythmic night calling is a familiar sound of tropical evenings.

हिंदी: यह फसलों को नुकसान नहीं पहुंचाता, लेकिन घरों और गोदामों में कपड़ों या कागज को कुतर सकता है। इसकी रात की आवाज़ ग्रामीण जीवन का आम हिस्सा है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. Expected throughout Basti, Khalilabad, and rural villages across the district in masonry crevices, cattle sheds, and grain godowns. Look after dark with a torch along the base of whitewashed courtyard walls; a pale tan cricket with dark bands and short wings or no wings is this species. A first record would be a photograph of the adult with place and date, sent through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Pantropical and cosmopolitan across warm regions of the world, widely distributed across tropical Asia, Africa, the Americas, and Australasia.

In India: Found across all states, primarily in and around human settlements, towns, and villages.

In Uttar Pradesh: Documented from urban and rural habitations throughout the central and eastern Gangetic plain.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected throughout human-dominated landscapes, villages, and grain storage markets in the district.

हिंदी: यह दुनिया भर के उष्णकटिबंधीय क्षेत्रों में फैला हुआ है। भारत और उत्तर प्रदेश के गांवों व शहरों में यह इंसानी बस्तियों के पास रहता है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Walker (1869) in Catalogue of the Specimens of Dermaptera Saltatoria in the Collection of the British Museum, part 1, p. 46, under the original name Gryllus sigillatus. Later transferred to the genus Gryllodes Saussure, 1874. The specific name sigillatus refers to the marked or adorned pattern on the body.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized.

Family and order placement. Order Orthoptera, suborder Ensifera, family Gryllidae (true crickets), subfamily Gryllinae.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Abundant, widespread, and thriving in anthropogenic environments worldwide.

हिंदी: इसे 1869 में वाकर ने Gryllus sigillatus नाम दिया था। बाद में इसे Gryllodes जीनस में रखा गया। यह ग्रिलिडी परिवार का सदस्य है।
