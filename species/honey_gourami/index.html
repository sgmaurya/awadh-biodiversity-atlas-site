हनी गौरमी मछली / Honey Gourami

Trichogaster chuna (Hamilton, 1822) — Osphronemidae

हनी गौरमी मछली — जलकुंभी और जलीय पौधों से भरे तालाबों, तालों और धान के खेतों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी, अत्यंत सुंदर और शांतिप्रिय देसी मछली (चूना खोलिसा) है। इसके प्रजनन काल में नर का शरीर शहद जैसा सुनहरा-पीला या नारंगी हो जाता है, जबकि उसका गला और पेट मखमली काला हो जाता है। इसके पेट के पंख दो पतले धागे जैसे स्पर्शकों में बदले होते हैं। इसके पास हवा में सांस लेने वाला विशेष अंग होता है, जिससे यह गंदे और बिना ऑक्सीजन वाले पानी में भी जीवित रहती है। यह दुनिया भर के एक्वेरियम में बहुत लोकप्रिय है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description:

Trichogaster chuna has a compact, oval, and laterally compressed body that is slightly more elongate and slender than Trichogaster lalius (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal and ventral contours are smoothly curved, tapering gradually to a short caudal peduncle (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is small and compressed; eyes are moderately large and lateral (Hamilton 1822). The mouth is small, terminal, and directed obliquely upward; the preorbital bone is finely denticulated (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Teeth are small, conical, and arranged in narrow bands on the jaws.

A specialized auxiliary air-breathing structure—the labyrinth organ—is housed inside an expanded cavity above the gills, enabling direct respiration of atmospheric oxygen. The pelvic fins are positioned beneath the throat and are modified into a single, elongate, hair-like sensory ray (feeler) capable of independent movement (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin is elongate, carrying 17–18 sharp spines and 6–9 soft branched rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin has a long base, carrying 18–20 spines and 11–13 soft rays (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The caudal fin is rounded and complete (Hamilton 1822). The body is covered with small ctenoid scales, with 27 to 29 scales in the lateral series (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Sexual dichromatism is remarkable (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Breeding males display an intense honey-amber or glowing orange-gold body coloration; the throat, breast, belly, and anterior portion of the anal fin turn deep velvet-black, while the upper margin of the dorsal fin is edged with bright electric lemon-yellow (Hamilton 1822). Females and non-breeding males are warm silvery-fawn or light yellowish-brown, marked with a prominent, continuous dark brown mid-lateral horizontal stripe extending from the eye to the caudal base (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 13.7 सेमी तक लंबा, चपटा और गोल-लंबा होता है। नर का रंग सुनहरा-नारंगी और गला मखमली काला होता है। मादा के शरीर पर आंख से पूँछ तक भूरी धारी होती है। पेट पर धागे जैसे पंख होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Honey Gourami is a peaceful, shy, and diurnal benthopelagic fish adapted to densely vegetated, standing freshwaters (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It inhabits perennial village ponds, weed-choked oxbow wetlands (tals), sluggish canals, and shallow drainage ditches rich in floating macrophytes like Eichhornia, Lemna, and Salvinia.

Adults swim deliberately through underwater plant tangles, extending their thread-like pelvic feelers forward to touch surrounding stems and explore potential food items. The labyrinth organ allows them to flourish in shallow, stagnant, and oxygen-depleted waters by rising to the surface periodically to take in atmospheric air (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Like other small gouramis, they can spit small droplets of water at terrestrial insects perched on overhanging leaves to knock them into the water. They maintain small personal territories among weed clusters and are remarkably peaceful towards other fishes.

हिंदी: यह बहुत शांत और संकोची स्वभाव की मछली है जो घनी घास में छिपकर रहती है। यह अपने धागे जैसे पंखों से चीजों को छूती है, पानी की सतह से हवा में सांस लेती है और पत्तियों के कीड़ों पर पानी की धार फेंककर शिकार करती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Breeding takes place during the monsoon rains from June to August (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The male constructs a delicate floating bubble-nest on the water surface by binding air bubbles with mucus and incorporating small plant bits. The male displays his fiery orange and black colors, courting the female beneath the nest. When she accepts, the pair embraces in a nuptial clasp, releasing buoyant eggs that float up into the bubble-nest. The male gathers stray eggs and adds them to the nest, guarding it vigilantly against all intruders. The eggs hatch after a few days, and the male protects the fry until they become free-swimming.

हिंदी: बरसात के समय नर मछली पानी की सतह पर बुलबुलों का घोंसला बनाती है। अंडे देने के बाद नर अंडों की रखवाली करता है। कुछ ही दिनों में बच्चे निकल आते हैं और वे घास में छिपकर बढ़ते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Trichogaster chuna is an opportunistic micro-carnivore and insectivore (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Mosquito larvae and pupae (Culex, Anopheles, and Aedes), captured near the water surface.
  • Planktonic micro-crustaceans, including water fleas (Daphnia, Moina) and copepods (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Small terrestrial insects knocked down from overhanging foliage, including aphids, springtails, and tiny gnats.
  • Epiphytic diatoms, unicellular green algae, and soft decomposing plant detritus.
  • Chironomid midge larvae and tiny aquatic worms inhabiting weed beds.
हिंदी: यह पानी की सतह के मच्छरों के लार्वा, सूक्ष्म जलीय जीवों (zooplankton), जलीय पौधों पर जमी काई और पत्तियों पर बैठने वाले छोटे कीड़ों को खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Predatory fishes: Snakeheads like Channa punctata (Spotted Snakehead), walking catfishes like Clarias magur (Indian Magur), and climbing perch Anabas testudineus (Climbing Perch) hunt gouramis in weed beds.
  • Piscivorous birds: Pond herons, little egrets, and common kingfishers capture adults when surfacing to breathe.
  • Water snakes: Checkered keelbacks pursue them among floating water hyacinth mats.
  • Predatory aquatic insects: Giant water bugs and dragonfly naiads ambush fry in weed nurseries.
हिंदी: गिरई मछली, देशी मागुर, कवाई, बगुले और पानी के साँप पानी की घास में इस छोटी मछली का शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Natural mosquito vector control: The Honey Gourami is an effective native biological control agent against disease-vector mosquitoes in northern India (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Because it naturally inhabits stagnant, weed-choked village tanks, roadside ditches, and flooded rice fields where mosquitoes breed, it actively consumes mosquito larvae and pupae, contributing to rural public health.

Global aquarium ornamental trade: Celebrated worldwide as a premier ornamental aquarium fish under the name "Honey Gourami". Its small adult size, peaceful nature, and stunning breeding coloration make it one of the most beloved and traded labyrinth fishes globally (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). In rural eastern Uttar Pradesh, it is also caught in fine-mesh nets from weed-rich tals and eaten locally in small mixed-fish curries.

Traditional: Consumed as part of small subsistence catches from village ponds across Awadh.

Today: Valued worldwide as an elite ornamental aquarium fish and public health biocontrol agent.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing captive breeding techniques for Trichogaster chuna in rural earthen ponds to support local livelihood enterprises.

हिंदी: यह मछली पानी की घास में छिपे मच्छरों के लार्वा को खाकर मलेरिया और अन्य बीमारियों को रोकने में मदद करती है। अपनी सुंदरता के कारण यह पूरी दुनिया के एक्वेरियम में बहुत पसंद की जाती है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to the northern and eastern Indian subcontinent, distributed across India, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Found throughout the Ganges and Brahmaputra river drainages, from the sub-Himalayan plains to eastern India (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, and Gomti river basins in weed-rich oxbow wetlands and village ponds.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as an abundant resident native in perennial tals, oxbow wetlands, and vegetated drains of the Kuwano and Ghaghara systems.

हिंदी: यह भारत, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश के मैदानी इलाकों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में तालाबों, तालों और नहरों में यह सामान्य रूप से उपस्थित है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Trichopodus chuna in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 121. Type locality: Ponds and freshwater rivers of Gangetic provinces.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its range.

Family and order placement. Order Perciformes (or Anabantiformes), family Osphronemidae, subfamily Luciocephalinae. Long placed in the genus Colisa Cuvier, 1831, modern systematic revisions assign it to Trichogaster Bloch & Schneider, 1801.

Phylogenetic notes. Trichogaster chuna is closely allied to Trichogaster lalius, distinguished by its more slender body depth, higher dorsal spine count, and unique honey-and-black nuptial coloration (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, abundant, and secure populations throughout South Asian freshwater wetlands.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Trichogaster chuna है और यह Osphronemidae परिवार की सदस्य है। इसे पुरानी पुस्तकों में Colisa chuna लिखा गया है।
