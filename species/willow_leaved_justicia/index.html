नीली निर्गुंडी / Willow-leaved Justicia

Justicia gendarussa Burm. f. — Acanthaceae

नीली निर्गुंडी — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश की एक सामान्य झाड़ी जिसकी पत्तियाँ पतली, लम्बी और नरम होती हैं — विलो (सिसोन) की पत्तियों जैसी दिखती हैं। इसके पत्तों का पारंपरिक चिकित्सा में विस्तृत उपयोग होता है, विशेषकर सूजन और संक्रमण के लिए।

Field tip: Look for a small shrub with distinctly narrow, willow-like leaves and dense purple flower spikes in late monsoon and post-monsoon season.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements in the Gangetic plain):

Stems and branches: Herbaceous to woody, smooth or slightly hairy; slender; branching.

Leaves: Opposite, lanceolate to linear, soft, willow-like in appearance. Margins are entire or nearly so. The leaf base is acute; the apex pointed. Leaves are glabrous or sparsely hairy.

Flowers: Purple or blue-purple, small (about 0.8 cm), tubular with 2–lipped corolla. Flowers are arranged densely in terminal spikes or racemes.

Fruit: A linear capsule, 0.8–1 cm long, containing 2–4 small seeds.

हिंदी: पत्ती बहुत पतली, लम्बी, नरम, विलो जैसी। फूल बैंगनी। फूलों की बाली घनी।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Justicia gendarussa is native to South Asia, from India to Myanmar. It grows in open woodlands, disturbed areas, and margins of cultivation. The plant is hardy, drought-tolerant, and thrives in poor soils. It is often cultivated locally for medicinal use. The flowers attract pollinators.

हिंदी: खुली जगहों पर उगता है। सूखा सहनशील। औषधीय गुणों के लिए लगाया जाता है। तितलियाँ और कीड़े फूलों पर आते हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Minor leaf spots in humid seasons.
  • Generally pest-resistant and disease-free.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional medicinal use (documented uses; not medical advice):

  • Anti-inflammatory: Leaves are used in traditional preparations for inflammation and joint pain. The decoction or extract is applied internally or externally.
  • Antimicrobial: Traditionally used for skin infections and wounds; leaf paste applied topically.
  • Digestive: Leaf decoction used traditionally for digestive complaints.

Ornamental value: The delicate willow-like leaves and purple flowers make it attractive for gardens.

हिंदी: पारंपरिक रूप से पत्तियों का व्यापक उपयोग होता है — सूजन, जोड़ों का दर्द, त्वचा रोग के लिए। पत्तों को उबालकर या पीसकर लगाया जाता है।

Willow-leaved Justicia is to be expected in open areas, wastelands, and cultivated gardens throughout Basti district. GBIF holds published records from the core districts. The plant is most visible when flowering (August–November). A first confirmed local record would be a photograph showing the characteristic willow-like leaves and purple flower spikes with place and date.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South Asia, from India to Myanmar and beyond.

In India: Widespread in tropical and subtropical regions; commonly used in traditional medicine.

In Uttar Pradesh: Found in open areas and gardens throughout the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in open woodlands and waste areas; common status uncertain but likely.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Nikolaus Joseph Freiherr von Jacquin as Rhaphidophora gendarussa in 1781, later transferred to Justicia by others. The epithet gendarussa is derived from the Malay name of the plant.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Lamiales, family Acanthaceae (acanthus family).

Phylogenetic notes. Justicia is a large genus of tropical herbs and shrubs with about 600 species.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread; no conservation concerns.
