बोगा लाबियो मछली / Boga Labeo

Labeo boga (Hamilton, 1822) — Cyprinidae

बोगा लाबियो मछली — मैदानी नदियों, स्वच्छ धाराओं और गहरे तालों में पाई जाने वाली एक मध्यम आकार की बहुत फुर्तीली और उपयोगी देसी कार्प (माइनर कार्प) है। इसका शरीर लंबा, चिकना और नारंगी-चांदी जैसा चमकदार होता है। इसका मुँह नीचे की ओर होता है और ऊपरी जबड़े पर केवल एक जोड़ी बहुत छोटी मूंछें होती हैं। यह नदी की तलहटी में पत्थरों और रेत पर जमी काई को खुरचकर खाती है। यह ग्रामीण बाजारों में एक स्वादिष्ट और सस्ती खाद्य मछली के रूप में बिकती है।

Field tip: Look in flowing shallows and deep river pools with sandy-rocky substrates. An elongate, silvery-orange carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of minute maxillary barbels, and a subtle dark blotch behind the upper gill cover, is Labeo boga.

हिंदी: यह मध्यम आकार की लंबी मछली है जिसका रंग नारंगी-चांदी जैसा चमकता है। मुँह नीचे की तरफ होता है और केवल दो बारीक मूंछें होती हैं। कंधे पर हल्का काला निशान होता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description:

Labeo boga has an elongate, streamlined body that is subcylindrical in the thoracic region and compressed towards the caudal peduncle (Hamilton 1822). The dorsal profile rises gently from the snout to the origin of the dorsal fin, while the ventral contour is nearly straight (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The head is moderate, conical, and slightly compressed, comprising about one-fifth of the standard length (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The snout is moderately projecting, obtuse, and slightly overhangs the mouth, often studded with microscopic sensory pores (Hamilton 1822). The mouth is small, inferior, and transverse; the lips are fleshy, with the lower lip reflected and continuous with a distinct labial groove (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). A single pair of minute maxillary barbels is present, concealed within the labial fold; rostral barbels are completely lacking (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

The dorsal fin originates in advance of the pelvic fins and carries 2–3 unbranched and 9–10 branched rays, without a stiffened spine (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The anal fin is short, containing 2 unbranched and 5 branched rays. The pectoral fins are pointed, and the caudal fin is deeply forked with equal pointed lobes (Hamilton 1822). The lateral line is complete and nearly straight, with 37 to 39 scales (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Coloration is shining orange-tinted silver or brassy-yellow on the flanks, fading to pure white on the abdomen (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). The back is dark slaty-grey or olive. A diffuse, rounded dark shoulder spot is situated behind the upper margin of the gill cover (Hamilton 1822). The fins are pale yellowish-grey with orange-red flushes during the breeding season.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लगभग 30 सेमी तक लंबा, गोल-लंबा और मजबूत होता है। मुंह नीचे की तरफ होता है जिसके होंठ मोटे होते हैं। मुंह के पास केवल दो बहुत छोटी मूंछें होती हैं। शरीर का रंग नारंगी-चांदी जैसा होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Boga Labeo is an active benthopelagic fish adapted to flowing alluvial rivers and perennial channels (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). It prefers river channels, sandy-gravel glides, oxbow lakes, and deep perennial pools with continuous water movement and clean substrates.

Adults swim in small loose groups along the riverbed. Using their inferior mouths and fleshy scraping lips, they browse over submerged rocks, timber, and hard clay surfaces, scraping epilithic biofilms, diatoms, and microscopic algae (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). During the high-water monsoon season, they venture onto inundated floodplains and connecting canal waterways to graze on newly flooded vegetation, returning to main river scours as dry-season low flows set in. They avoid stagnant, heavily hypoxic pools and require reasonable dissolved oxygen levels (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

हिंदी: यह मछली नदियों के बहते पानी की तलहटी में रहना पसंद करती है। यह पत्थरों और रेत पर जमी काई को खुरचकर खाती है और भोजन की तलाश में तलहटी पर घूमती है।

Spawning takes place during the southwest monsoon from June to August (Talwar & Jhingran 1991). Strong river discharge and high dissolved oxygen levels trigger spawning migrations into flowing side streams and gravel shoals. Females release hundreds of demersal, non-adhesive eggs that settle among gravel crevices. The eggs hatch after a few days, and tiny larvae shelter in quiet marginal eddies before joining feeding schools on sandbars as waters recede.

हिंदी: मानसून में जब नदियों में नया पानी आता है, यह उथले पत्थरीले किनारों पर जाकर अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडों से बच्चे निकल आते हैं और वे तेजी से काई खाकर बड़े होते हैं।

Diet and Feeding Behaviour आहार

Labeo boga is primarily a benthic herbivore and periphyton grazer (Talwar & Jhingran 1991):

Natural diet components:

  • Filamentous green algae, blue-green algae, and unicellular diatoms scraped from rocks and sand.
  • Decaying aquatic plant fragments and soft organic detritus ingested from bottom silt (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).
  • Benthic micro-invertebrates, including chironomid midge larvae and tiny worms associated with periphyton mats.
  • Microscopic protozoans and rotifers filtered during juvenile life stages.
हिंदी: यह मुख्य रूप से शाकाहारी मछली है। यह पानी की तली में जमी काई, शैवाल, सड़े हुए पौधों के अंश और कीचड़ के सूक्ष्म कीड़े खाती है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Documented natural predators in northern Indian alluvial freshwater systems:

  • Riverine predatory fishes: Large predators like Wallago attu (Striped Catfish), Bagarius bagarius (Goonch), and Chitala chitala (Clown Knifefish) prey on boga schools.
  • Piscivorous birds: Herons, cormorants, and storks capture adults in shallow river glides.
  • River otters and softshell turtles: Hunt schooling carps in perennial river pools.
  • Water snakes: Prey on juveniles in weed-rich nursery shallows.
हिंदी: बोआल, गूंज, चितल, बगुले और उदबिलाव इस मछली का शिकार करते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Nutritional and commercial food value: The Boga Labeo is an important minor carp across northern and eastern India. It is captured in substantial quantities by commercial and artisanal fishers using cast nets, shore seines, and gill nets in the Ghaghara, Kuwano, and Rapti river basins. Its flesh is white, tender, and tasty, supplying vital animal protein, calcium, and essential fatty acids to rural families at an accessible market price.

Pond aquaculture role: Labeo boga is cultured as an auxiliary species in composite fish culture ponds alongside major Indian carps (Catla, Rohu, Mrigal) because its bottom-grazing habits utilize natural periphyton without competing aggressively with larger carps (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

Traditional: A traditional staple food fish widely eaten across Awadh and eastern Uttar Pradesh.

Today: Sold fresh in village markets (haats) and urban fish bazaars as an affordable minor carp.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Assessing induced breeding protocols for Labeo boga in rural hatcheries to enhance indigenous pond polyculture.

हिंदी: यह एक स्वादिष्ट और पौष्टिक देसी मछली है जिसे बाजारों में खूब पसंद किया जाता है। इसे तालाबों में रोहू और कतला के साथ भी आसानी से पाला जा सकता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South Asia, natively occurring in India, Pakistan, Nepal, and Bangladesh (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

In India: Distributed throughout the Indus, Ganges, Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, and Godavari river drainages (Menon 1999).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded throughout the Ganges, Ghaghara, Rapti, and Gomti river networks and major canal systems.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident native species in the Ghaghara and Kuwano river basins and associated floodplain waters.

हिंदी: यह भारत, पाकिस्तान, नेपाल और बांग्लादेश की प्रमुख नदियों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में घाघरा और कुआनो नदी प्रणालियों में यह सामान्य रूप से उपस्थित है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Francis Hamilton in 1822 as Cyprinus boga in An Account of the Fishes Found in the River Ganges and Its Branches, page 286. Type locality: River Brahmaputra.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are recognized across its continental range.

Family and order placement. Order Cypriniformes, family Cyprinidae, subfamily Labeoninae. The genus Labeo Cuvier, 1816, comprises herbivorous Asian and African carps.

Phylogenetic notes. Labeo boga belongs to the minor carp group of Labeo, characterized by its slender body, moderate scale count, and single pair of minute maxillary barbels (Talwar & Jhingran 1991).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It maintains widespread, abundant wild populations across South Asian river systems.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Labeo boga है और यह Cyprinidae परिवार की Labeoninae उपपरिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास सुरक्षित है।
