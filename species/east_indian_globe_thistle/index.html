गोरखमुंडी / East Indian Globe Thistle

Sphaeranthus indicus L. — Asteraceae

गोरखमुंडी — गोरखनाथ के नाम से जाना जाने वाला छोटा, कँटीला पौधा जो खेतों की खाली ज़मीन पर आसानी से उग आता है। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के किसानों के लिए यह एक कष्टदायक खरपतवार है, लेकिन आयुर्वेद में इसे सूजन और पाचन संबंधी समस्याओं के इलाज के लिए जाना जाता है। छोटे, गोल, कँटीले फूलों का समूह इसे पहचानना आसान बनाता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Plant habitSmall annual herb, 15–50 cm, bushy, spreading
LeavesSmall, linear to oblong, 1–3 cm, alternately arranged, somewhat spiny margins
FlowersGlobose heads, 0.5–1 cm diameter, purple to pink, densely packed spiny bracts
StemHerbaceous, angular, hairy, often reddish
RootsTap root system

Field tip: In a waste ground or fallow field from August onward, look for a small bushy plant with tiny leaves and distinctive round, spiky purple flower heads. The whole plant may feel prickly. Compare with Spiny Amaranth but the flower heads are much more globose and compact.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: छोटा, कँटीला पौधा, गोल-गोल बैंगनी फूल, खेत की बंजर ज़मीन पर।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Plant height15–50 cm
Leaf length1–3 cm
Leaf width0.5–1.5 cm
Flower head diameter0.5–1 cm

Stem and branches: Angular, hairy, often purple-tinged, branching from the base.

Leaves: Alternate, simple, linear-oblong, entire or with small spiny teeth, hairy on both surfaces.

Flowers: Composite heads, each consisting of numerous florets in a globe-like arrangement, surrounded by spiny involucral bracts. Florets purple, pink, or white.

Fruits: Achenes, small, 1–2 mm, dispersed by wind.

हिंदी: छोटी पत्तियाँ, गोल कँटीले फूल, बहुत छोटा पौधा।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A common annual herb of disturbed and waste ground, thriving in poor, dry soil where few other plants establish. It germinates after the monsoon and flowers abundantly through the cool season. The globose flower heads are adapted to wind dispersal, and the plant produces seeds profusely, making it a persistent weed in fallows and field margins. Though weedy, it offers some foraging value and is tolerated in some traditional agricultural systems.

हिंदी: खराब ज़मीन पर भी अच्छे से उगता है, बहुत बीज देता है, सर्दी में फूल आता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Month / seasonEvent

Seeds are short-lived; the plant persists through the seed bank in soil.

हिंदी: बरसात के बाद उगता है, सर्दी में फूल आता है, फरवरी तक सूख जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Field companions: Spiny Amaranth, Congress Grass, Dhatura in disturbed fields
  • Traditional associations: used in Ayurvedic preparations alongside Ashwagandha and Brahmi

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Rarely seriously damaged by insects in the field; its small size and spiny form provide natural protection. Occasionally browsed by goats and sheep if other forage is scarce.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Agricultural impact. A common weed in wheat and rabi crops of eastern UP, competing for moisture and soil nutrients. Control is by hand-pulling in early growth or by weeding in October–November. It does not persist if crops are regularly irrigated.

Ethnobotanical use (documented traditional knowledge; not medical advice). In Ayurvedic medicine, the whole plant is dried and used in decoctions and powders for fevers, inflammation, and digestive complaints. The plant is listed in classical texts including Bhava Prakasha for its cooling and digestive properties.

Today. Limited commercial use; occasionally sold in Ayurvedic markets as a dried herb.

हिंदी: किसानों को खरपतवार के रूप में परेशानी देता है। आयुर्वेद में सूजन और पाचन के लिए इस्तेमाल होता है।

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain. To be expected in August through February on fallow land, field margins, and waste ground throughout the region. More common in the cooler rabi season.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical Asia; widespread across the Indian subcontinent.

In India: Found across the Indo-Gangetic plain and in peninsular India.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widespread in both upland and alluvial plains.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Common as a weed in disturbed ground and field margins.

Field rule: A small, soft-stemmed herb with tiny leaves and very distinctive compact, round, spiky purple flower heads is Sphaeranthus indicus.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum, ed. 2. The name Sphaeranthus refers to the globe-shaped flower head (sphaera = sphere, anthos = flower).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Asteraceae (the daisy family), order Asterales. A widespread genus in tropical Asia and Africa.

Phylogenetic notes. The genus Sphaeranthus is basal within certain Asteraceae lineages; relationships to other globe-thistle genera are still being studied.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread and weedy; no conservation concern.
