स्क्विरल्स टेल / Squirrel's Tail

Colebrookea oppositifolia Sm. — Lamiaceae

स्क्विरल्स टेल — जिसे बिंदा या पानसिरू भी कहा जाता है, यह सूखी घाटियों, बीहड़ों और जंगलों के किनारों पर उगने वाली एक घनी और रोयेंदार झाड़ी है। इसके पूरे पौधे पर सफेद-धूसर रंग का मखमली रोआं होता है। सर्दियों में इसमें गिलहरी की पूंछ जैसी रोयेंदार और घनी सफेद बालियां निकलती हैं, जिसके कारण इसे स्क्विरल्स टेल कहा जाता है।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

A large, erect, spreading, soft-wooded shrub. Stems stout, woody at the base, branched; branches and branchlets quadrangular or subcylindrical, densely clothed with pale grey or whitish soft woolly tomentum.

Leaves opposite, simple, petiolate; petiole stout, woolly; blade oblong-lanceolate, acute or acuminate at the apex, tapering at the base, margins crenulate or serrulate, thick, soft, wrinkled above, densely white-tomentose and softly velvety beneath with prominently raised reticulate veins.

Inflorescence consisting of terminal and axillary panicles of numerous, dense, cylindrical, erect or drooping, woolly spikes resembling miniature furry tails. Species functionally dioecious. Flowers minute, crowded in dense verticillasters along the spike axis, sessile, white, accompanied by small linear downy bracts.

Calyx deeply five-partite; segments subulate, densely covered with long feathery (plumose) hairs, rapidly elongating in fruit to form a persistent, pappus-like feathery cluster. Corolla very small, white; tube short; limb four-lobed, lobes subequal, puberulous. Stamens four, exserted in male flowers, sterile and included in female flowers.

Fruit composed of one to four tiny, obovoid, compressed nutlets, hairy at the tip, enclosed within the enlarged, plumose, feathery calyx lobes.

हिंदी: पूरा पौधा सफेद मखमली रोयें से ढका होता है। पत्तियाँ मोटी और नरम होती हैं। फूल बहुत छोटे और सफेद होते हैं जो गिलहरी की पूंछ जैसी रोयेंदार बालियों में खिलते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A drought-resistant, light-demanding native shrub common on dry slopes, ravine edges, eroded banks, and forest clearings across the Sub-Himalayan tract and the northern plains. Its dense covering of pale tomentum reduces transpiration and shields tissues against intense solar radiation.

The species is dioecious, with male and female flowers produced on different plants. Flowering occurs synchronously across populations in winter and early spring. The feathery calyx segments act like a wind-dispersal parachute (anemochory), carrying the miniature nutlets across dry ravines and scrublands.

हिंदी: यह पौधा तेज धूप और सूखे को आसानी से झेल लेता है। इसके रोयें पत्तियों को सूखने से बचाते हैं और पंख जैसे बाह्यदल बीजों को हवा में उड़ाते हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Leaves fall partially during the cool winter months, with new velvety leaves unfolding in February and March.

Flowering begins in December and continues in full flush through January, February, and March. The fuzzy white spikes dominate the shrub's appearance throughout early spring. Fruits and feathery calyces ripen from March to May. By the onset of summer, the plumose fruiting calyces detach and drift on hot breezes. With the arrival of monsoon rains in July, seedlings emerge readily in open, sunlit eroded ground.

हिंदी: दिसंबर से मार्च के बीच इसमें सफेद बालियां खिलती हैं। मार्च से मई में फल पककर हवा में उड़ते हैं और बरसात में नए पौधे उगते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

The profuse, nectar-rich micro-flowers provide vital forage for winter-active honeybees, solitary bees, and syrphid flies.

In ravine scrub and woodland margins, it associates with dryland shrubs and trees such as white-bark acacia, adusa, and khair. Its dense bushy root system plays an important ecological role in stabilizing crumbling ravine banks and river terraces.

हिंदी: सर्दियों में मधुमक्खियाँ इसके फूलों से रस और पराग इकट्ठा करती हैं। यह पौधा बीहड़ों की कटती हुई मिट्टी को बांधकर रखता है।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Generally pest-free due to its woolly indumentum, though mealybugs occasionally infest shoot tips during dry, stagnant spring weather.

हिंदी: घने रोयें के कारण इस पर कीड़े कम लगते हैं, कभी-कभी सफेद मिलीबग डालियों पर दिख सकते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

In the rural regions of northern India, Colebrookea oppositifolia is valued for its soil-conserving properties along eroding stream banks, railway cuttings, and ravine margins.

Chopra et al. (1956, p. 74) record that the leaves of Colebrookea oppositifolia (vern. Binda) are applied to wounds and bruises, and a preparation of the root is used in epilepsy. This is a record of published traditional use, not medical advice.

हिंदी: यह मिट्टी के कटाव को रोकने के लिए बहुत उपयोगी झाड़ी है। पारंपरिक रूप से इसकी पत्तियों का उपयोग घाव और चोट के इलाज में किया जाता रहा है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native across South Asia and parts of Southeast Asia, including India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, and southwestern China.

In India: Widespread in the Sub-Himalayan tract, the Siwaliks, the Gangetic plain, Central India, and the Western Ghats.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common on dry Siwalik slopes, ravine systems, and scrub bluffs across the northern and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected on elevated ravine slopes, river terraces, and dry woodland edges.

हिंदी: यह झाड़ी भारत, नेपाल और म्यांमार के सूखे और पहाड़ी जंगलों में आम तौर पर पाई जाती है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by James Edward Smith in 1806 in Exotic Botany, volume 2, page 111, plate 115.

Subspecies. Monotypic genus; Colebrookea oppositifolia is the sole recognized species in the genus Colebrookea.

Family and order placement. Placed in family Lamiaceae (subfamily Pogostemonoideae), order Lamiales.

Phylogenetic notes. Characterized within Lamiaceae by its functionally dioecious flowers and plumose fruiting calyx lobes that aid in wind dispersal.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) on the IUCN Red List due to its wide range, stable populations, and absence of significant threats.
