हरा फुदका / Green Leafhopper

Nephotettix virescens (Distant, 1918) — Cicadellidae

हरा फुदका — धान के खेतों में सब जगह दिखने वाली एक छोटी, हरी, पारदर्शी, पत्तियों पर चिपकी रहने वाली बग। इसके पहले पैर पीछे की ओर मुड़े होते हैं और जब कोई पास आए तो यह कूदकर भाग जाती है — "फुदका" इसीलिए। यह धान के पत्तों की रस चूसती है और गुलाबी रंग के धब्बे बना देती है। इससे भी ज़्यादा ख़तरनाक यह है कि यह धान का टंगरो रोग फैलाती है, जिससे धान की पूरी फसल बर्बाद हो सकती है।

Field tip: On paddy leaves, a small bright green insect that hops sideways and jumps away when touched, leaving red puncture marks where it has fed, is a green leafhopper. Look on the undersides of leaves.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: धान के पत्तों के पीछे की ओर छोटी, हरी, पारदर्शी; पैर पीछे की ओर; छुओ तो कूद कर भाग जाती है; पत्ती पर लाल धब्बे रह जाते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length4.0–4.5 mm
Body width1.2–1.5 mm

Structure: Elongate-oval body, dorsum convex. Head rounded, width less than pronotum; antennae filiform, shorter than the body. Pronotum broad and short. Wing covers (elytra) are modified as wings (tegmina), held tent-like over the back, with fine venation visible. Legs are typical of Cicadellidae: hind legs with thickened femora adapted for jumping; tarsi with adhesive pads. The rostrum is sharp and needle-like, adapted for piercing plant tissue.

Colour variation: Specimens from different regions and times of year vary in intensity of green; populations reared on different host plants may show different shades.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A highly specialized sap-feeder, exclusive to grasses, particularly paddy rice. The adult feeds on the undersides of leaves, puncturing the epidermis and drawing phloem sap. Feeding damage is visible as pale stippling and reddening of the leaf. The insect also inoculates plant viruses during feeding, most notably rice tungro virus, a devastating pathogen that causes severe stunting and necrosis.

Females lay eggs in the stems and leaf sheaths of rice. Development from egg to adult takes 15–20 days in warm conditions, allowing multiple overlapping generations per monsoon season. The population builds rapidly on uncontrolled paddy. The insect is gregarious and shows a preference for young, tender plants.

हिंदी: धान की सब जातियों को खाता है; विशेषकर नई पत्तियों को पसंद करता है। रस चूसता है तो पत्ती के पीछे लाल धब्बे पड़ जाते हैं। यह रस चूसते हुए विषाणु भी फैलाता है — टंगरो विषाणु।

Generation time: 15–25 days depending on temperature.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Primary hosts:

  • Paddy rice (Oryza sativa)
  • Maize (Zea mays)
  • Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)
  • Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor)

Wild hosts:

  • Annual and perennial wild grasses

Feeding: Strictly phloem sap; no other food sources.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Parasitoid wasps: Anagrus, Gonatocerus; lay eggs in leafhoppers
  • Spiders: hunting spiders and web spiders
  • Predatory insects: antlions, lacewings, damselflies
  • Birds: weavers, munias, and other granivorous birds that also take insects
  • Fungi: entomopathogenic fungi infect the population when humidity is high

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Rice pest. The green leafhopper is one of the most important pests of rice in Asia. Damage occurs through two pathways: (1) direct feeding damage causing stippling and necrosis of leaves, leading to reduced photosynthesis and yield loss; (2) transmission of rice tungro virus, which causes systemic necrosis and severe stunting, with yield losses of 80–100% in severe cases.

Management. Integrated pest management includes: (1) varietal selection for resistance; (2) timing of planting to avoid peak populations; (3) water management — constant flooding suppresses the pest; (4) conservation of natural enemies; (5) use of selective insecticides (neem-based, pyrethroids) only when populations exceed economic threshold; (6) removal of volunteer rice between seasons.

Chemical sensitivity: The insect is susceptible to most broad-spectrum insecticides but resistance to some classes has been documented.

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use: none recorded for the region.

Today. Managed through a combination of agronomic and chemical approaches. In organic paddy, variety choice and natural enemy conservation are critical.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The impact of climate change on the seasonal phenology of Nephotettix virescens and its management in eastern UP has not been well documented.

हिंदी: धान की सबसे महत्वपूर्ण कीट है। इसके काटने से पत्ती खराब हो जाती है। इससे भी बड़ी बात, यह रोग फैलाता है। नियंत्रण: अलग किस्में, समय पर बीज, खेत में पानी रोके रखना, और कीटनाशकें ज़रूरत पड़ने पर।

Expected on paddy fields throughout Basti district during the monsoon and kharif season, particularly on young rice. Every paddy field is presumed to carry populations during the growing season. A specimen or clear photograph of a leafhopper on paddy with visible stippling damage is a confirmed record; molecular identification may be needed to confirm the species.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Tropical Asia from India through South-east Asia, southern China to the Philippines and Australia; now established in parts of Africa.

In India: All paddy-growing regions; a cosmopolitan rice pest.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in all rice-growing areas, particularly eastern UP.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Present during the kharif season in every paddy field.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  1. In a farmer's paddy, what is the relationship between leafhopper numbers and rice yield? / धान की फसल में फुदकों की संख्या और उपज में क्या रिश्ता है? — Count weekly and measure yield.
  2. Does screening the rice seedbed reduce the number of leafhoppers reaching the main paddy? — Compare covered and uncovered seedbeds.
  3. What proportion of the local population carries tungro virus? — Collect specimens and test.
  4. Do different rice varieties attract more or fewer leafhoppers? — Grow multiple varieties side by side.
  5. What is the density of parasitoid wasps in a paddy, and does it fluctuate with host density? — Collect leafhoppers and check for parasitism.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Distant as Deltocephalus virescens in 1918 in Annals and Magazine of Natural History. Later placed in Nephotettix. The species name refers to its green colour.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Cicadellidae (leafhoppers), order Hemiptera, suborder Auchenorrhyncha. Nephotettix is a derived genus within the subfamily Macrosteline.

Phylogenetic notes. The extreme host specificity of N. virescens for rice and other grasses suggests a long co-evolutionary history with these plants.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Abundant; widespread; increasing in range.
