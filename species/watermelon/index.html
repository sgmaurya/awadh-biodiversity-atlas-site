तरबूज / Watermelon

Citrullus lanatus (Thunb.) Matsum. & Nakai — Cucurbitaceae

तरबूज — गर्मियों का सबसे प्रिय फल, गर्मी की तपस में ठंडा और रसीला। पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के खेतों में अप्रैल से मई तक बीज बोए जाते हैं, और जून से अगस्त तक गाँवों के बाज़ारों में हरे-लाल, मीठे, रसीले तरबूज़ मिलने लगते हैं। इसका फूल पीला, बड़ा, और शानदार होता है, जिसे मधुमक्खियों खूब आकर्षित करते हैं। फल का गूदा लाल, नारंगी या पीला होता है, और बीज काले या सफेद होते हैं। गर्मियों में इसका रस शरीर को ठंडा और तरोताज़ा रखता है।

Field tip: Look for sprawling vines with large yellow flowers in May–June, and in May–June for large round or oval striped melons on the ground. The fruit is heavy and cannot climb; melons lie flat on the soil.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: बड़ी बेल, कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ, बड़े पीले फूल, विशाल गोल हरे-धारीदार फल, लाल-नारंगी-पीला गूदा।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (cultivated watermelon, northern India):

MeasurementRange

Leaf length10–20 cm

Fruit diameter20–60 cm
Fruit weight3–10 kg
Seed length7–10 mm

Stems: Trailing, angular, hirsute (hairy), green, lacking woody tissue.

Leaves: Alternate, deeply 3–5 lobed, the lobes themselves often lobed or toothed. Rough-hairy on both surfaces. Midrib prominent.

Flowers: Unisexual, yellow, large and showy. Male flowers more numerous, on longer pedicels (stalks); female flowers on shorter pedicels with a small fruit-like ovary at the base. Petals five, wrinkled. Calyx five-lobed. Stamens three (fused).

Fruit: A pepo (modified berry), globular, oblong, or ellipsoid, 20–60 cm diameter, weighing 3–10 kg. Rind (skin) is smooth, light green with darker green stripes or mottles, becoming lighter as the fruit matures. Interior flesh is crisp, juicy, red, orange, or yellow depending on cultivar. Numerous seeds embedded in flesh, ovate, black, brown, or white.

हिंदी: पत्तियाँ कटी-फटी, खुरदुरी। फूल बड़े, पीले, एकलिंगी (नर और मादा अलग)। फल बहुत बड़ा, गोल, हल्का हरा-गहरा हरा धारी, अंदर से लाल-नारंगी या पीला, बहुत रसीला।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

An annual herbaceous vine native to Africa, now cultivated widely in tropical and warm-temperate regions worldwide, including India. In the Basti plains, it thrives in hot summer weather. The plant requires well-drained soil, good sunshine, and consistent moisture during fruit development. Growth is rapid; from seed to flower takes 45–60 days, and from flower to harvest takes another 30–45 days.

The large yellow flowers are visited by Asian Honey Bee and other bees, which are essential for fruit set (pollination). The trailing vines spread rapidly and shade the ground, suppressing weeds.

हिंदी: यह अफ्रीकी पौधा है जो गर्मी में तेज़ी से बढ़ता है। अच्छी बारिश या सिंचाई चाहिए। मधुमक्खियाँ फूलों को परागण करती हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Asian Honey Bee and other bees visit flowers intensively.
  • Field companions: Often grown with Cucumber, Bottle Gourd, and other summer vegetables.
  • Benefit to soil: Trailing vines suppress weeds and shade the soil, reducing moisture loss.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Red pumpkin beetle (Aulacophora foveicollis): damages seedlings and young leaves; can be severe.
  • Fruit fly (Bactrocera cucurbitae): punctures fruits to lay eggs; larvae damage flesh.
  • Powdery mildew: white coating on leaves in humid conditions; sulfur spray helps.
  • Downy mildew: damages leaves in wet conditions; remove infected leaves.
  • Root rot: from waterlogging; ensure good drainage.
  • Generally hardy: Mature plants are less vulnerable than seedlings.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Food use:

The ripe fruit is eaten fresh as a cooling summer snack. The flesh is boiled as a vegetable in some regions. The seeds are eaten roasted and are nutritious.

Nutritional value: Watermelon flesh is 92% water, rich in vitamin C and lycopene (the red pigment). The fruit is low in calories (about 30 kcal per 100 g).

Cultivation advantages: Watermelon is a relatively easy summer crop in eastern UP, requiring no special care beyond land preparation, sowing, and regular watering. The plant is productive, and a single plant yields 2–6 mature fruits depending on variety and care.

Varieties: Mixed landrace varieties are common in Basti, varying in color and sweetness. Named varieties like Sugar Baby and others are available from agricultural institutions.

Yield: Under good management, 15–25 tons per hectare are achievable; small-scale farmers typically get 5–15 tons depending on water availability.

Seasonal importance: Watermelon is the quintessential summer fruit, available in markets from June to September in the region.

हिंदी: गर्मियों का सबसे मुख्य फल; गर्मी में ठंडा करता है। बीज भी खाए जाते हैं। एक बेल से कई फल मिलते हैं। सिंचाई अच्छी हो तो अधिक उपज मिलती है।

Expected as a cultivated vegetable in vegetable plots and summer fields throughout Basti district. Visible from May to August, with large yellow flowers and ripening melons best seen in May–June. The crop is ubiquitous in summer and familiar to all villagers.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to Africa; now cultivated worldwide in tropical and warm-temperate regions.

In India: Grown in all tropical and subtropical states; commercially important in many regions.

In Uttar Pradesh: Cultivated in all vegetable-growing districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Very common summer vegetable crop.

Research Questions शोध प्रश्न

  1. How many watermelons does a single plant produce from seed sown on a fixed date? — Sow seeds and count fruits from one plant at harvest.
  2. What percentage of flowers are female vs. male, and does hand-pollination increase fruit set? — Count flowers; hand-pollinate half and compare.
  3. Can watermelon be grown from seed saved from last season's fruit, and is the fruit true to type? / क्या पिछली फसल के तरबूज़ से बीज बचाकर अगली बार लगाया जा सकता है? — Save seeds and test in next season.
  4. How much water does a watermelon plant need from flowering to fruit maturity? — Measure soil moisture and irrigation.
  5. Which insects damage watermelon fruits most severely, and can netting reduce damage? — Try covering developing fruits with netting.

Field rule: A sprawling vine with large deeply lobed leaves, large bright yellow flowers, and huge smooth striped melons with red juicy flesh is watermelon; it is the most common summer fruit in village fields.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Thunberg in 1794 as part of the genus Cucumis. Later transferred to Citrullus by Matsumura and Nakai in 1916. The genus Citrullus is derived from Latin citrus, alluding to citrus-like fruits. The species name lanatus means "woolly," referring to the hairy plant.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised; however, two subspecies are sometimes recognized botanically (subspecies lanatus and subspecies colocynthis), but horticultural varieties are numerous.

Family and order placement. Cucurbitaceae (gourd family), order Cucurbitales.

Phylogenetic notes. Citrullus lanatus is closely related to the colocynth (Citrullus colocynthis), a bitter fruit used historically as a purgative.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; wild populations in Africa are not fully assessed.
