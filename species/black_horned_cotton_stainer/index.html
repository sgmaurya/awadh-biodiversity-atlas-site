ब्लैक-हॉर्न्ड कॉटन स्टेनर / Black-horned Cotton Stainer

Probergrothius nigricornis (Stål, 1861) — Pyrrhocoridae

ब्लैक-हॉर्न्ड कॉटन स्टेनर — वृक्षों और फूलों पर पाया जाने वाला चमकीला लाल-नारंगी और काला खटमल, जो पौधों और बीजों का रस चूसता है।

हिंदी: गहरा लाल-नारंगी शरीर, काले एंटीने और पंखों का पिछला हिस्सा काला, जो पेड़ों के तनों और पत्तियों पर दिखाई देते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

A relatively large, elongate-oval pyrrhocorid true bug displaying vivid aposematic red-and-black coloration. The head is triangular, brightly colored sanguineous red or orange-red, smooth, and equipped with prominent, rounded, lateral compound eyes; ocelli are absent, as is diagnostic for the family Pyrrhocoridae. The antennae are long, robust, four-segmented, and completely jet-black.

The pronotum is trapezoidal, slightly wider than long, narrowing anteriorly, with distinct raised lateral margins and a transverse depression behind the collar; its surface is bright reddish-orange with variable dark punctation. The scutellum is small, triangular, and bright red.

The hemelytra (forewings) cover the abdomen: the clavus and corium are bright orange-red, while the apical membrane is opaque, deep brownish-black, and distinctly veined. The rostrum (proboscis) is exceptionally long, slender, black, and extends backwards beneath the thoracic sterna beyond the hind coxae. The legs are slender, blackish or dark reddish-brown, with finely spinose tibiae and three-segmented tarsi. Nymphs are bright red with dark wing pads, resembling small wingless adults.

हिंदी: इसका शरीर लंबा और अंडाकार होता है। सिर, छाती और पंखों का अगला भाग लाल होता है, जबकि पंखों का पिछला झिल्लीदार हिस्सा और पैर काले होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Probergrothius nigricornis is a diurnal, gregarious true bug. Distant (1904, p. 109) records the species on flowers of Wedelia urticaefolia at Khandala in September. Rani et al. (2023) studied its seasonal cycle, migration and defensive behaviour on Pterygota alata, a tree of which it is a pest. Sharma & Livingstone (1978) studied the functional morphology of its reproductive organs.

Adults and nymphs aggregate on tree trunks and ground litter. Using their long, stylet-like rostrum, they pierce plant tissues and seeds to suck liquid nutrients. Their red-and-black colouring is that of a bug defended by distasteful secretions, as in other pyrrhocorids.

हिंदी: यह पेड़ों के तनों पर झुंड बनाकर रहता है। यह सूंड चुभोकर बीजों और पौधों का रस चूसता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Reproduction occurs during the warm months. In late spring, mating pairs wander coupled end-to-end (in tandem) over tree bark and leaf litter for extended periods.

The female lays batches of smooth, oval, cream-colored eggs in moist soil, under bark crevices, or among fallen leaf litter. Nymphs hatch within a fortnight and begin feeding gregariously. They molt through several nymphal instars over several weeks, developing darker wing pads with each molt. As the season progresses, newly emerged winged adults disperse or cluster on shaded tree trunks.

हिंदी: मादा छाल की दरारों में अंडे देती है। बच्चे लाल रंग के होते हैं और धीरे-धीरे पंख विकसित करते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Phytophagous bug feeding on tree seeds, plant sap and flower nectar. Distant (1904, p. 109) records adults visiting flowers of the composite herb Wedelia urticaefolia at Khandala in September. Rani et al. (2023) documented Probergrothius nigricornis as a pest feeding on Pterygota alata (Roxb.) R.Br.

हिंदी: यह बीजों और फूलों का रस चूसने वाला शाकाहारी कीट है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

No source opened for this atlas names a predator of this bug. Assassin bugs, spiders and ants are the groups that take pyrrhocorid nymphs elsewhere; none has been recorded on it here.

हिंदी: एटलस के खोले गए किसी स्रोत में इसके शिकारी का नाम नहीं मिलता।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Unlike the related red cotton bug (Dysdercus cingulatus), which is a major pest of commercial cotton crops, Probergrothius nigricornis is primarily a woodland and arboricultural bug. Rani et al. (2023) noted it as a pest of the tree Pterygota alata, but it causes no known agricultural damage to field crops in eastern Uttar Pradesh. It does not bite humans or domestic animals.

हिंदी: यह मुख्य रूप से पेड़ों और जंगली वनस्पतियों पर पाया जाता है और फसलों को नुकसान नहीं पहुंचाता। यह इंसानों या पालतू जानवरों को कोई नुकसान नहीं पहुंचाता।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia, recorded from India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Myanmar, Thailand, and Malaysia.

In India: Recorded from western India (Maharashtra), southern India, and across the subcontinent.

In Uttar Pradesh: Expected in wooded tracts, groves, and riverine corridors across the Gangetic plain.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in village groves, orchards, and woodland patches across the district.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Stål (1861) in Stettiner Entomologische Zeitung, vol. 22, p. 146, under the name Odontopus nigricornis (as given by Distant 1904). The species was long treated in Indian literature under Odontopus Laporte before being transferred to Probergrothius Kirkaldy. The specific epithet nigricornis means black-horned, referring to the entirely black antennae.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognized.

Family and order placement. Order Hemiptera, suborder Heteroptera, infraorder Pentatomomorpha, superfamily Pyrrhocoroidea, family Pyrrhocoridae (cotton stainers).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Abundant and widespread across South Asia with extensive host tree availability.

हिंदी: इसे 1861 में स्टॉल ने वर्णित किया था। यह पायरहोकोरिडी परिवार का कीट है, जिसे कॉटन स्टेनर कहा जाता है।
