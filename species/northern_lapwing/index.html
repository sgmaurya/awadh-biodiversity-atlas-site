नॉर्दर्न लैपविंग / Northern Lapwing

Vanellus vanellus (Linnaeus, 1758) — Charadriidae

नॉर्दर्न लैपविंग — जुते हुए खेतों और नम भूमि पर सर्दियों में दिखने वाली एक विशिष्ट कलगीदार टिटहरी। बेकर (1929) के अनुसार इसके सिर पर एक पतली, लंबी और ऊपर की ओर मुड़ी हुई काली कलगी होती है, पीठ कांस्य-हरी व चमकदार होती है तथा सीने पर एक चौड़ा काला पट्टा होता है। पूंछ के नीचे दालचीनी जैसा रंग होता है। यह सर्दियों में जुते खेतों में वायर-वर्म कीटों और कीड़ों को खाती है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
CrestLong, slender recurved crest of narrow glossy black feathers on crown (Baker 1929, p. 180)
UpperpartsBronze-green, highly glossed; scapulars marked with violet-purple (Baker 1929, p. 180)
Breast & ThroatThroat, foreneck, and breast black in breeding plumage; white in non-breeding (Baker 1929, p. 180)
Tail PatternWhite tail with a very broad black subapical band glossed with green (Baker 1929, p. 180)
Tail-CovertsUpper and under tail-coverts bright cinnamon (Baker 1929, p. 180)
Soft PartsIris brown; bill black; legs and feet orange-brown (Baker 1929, p. 180)

Field tip: Look for a plover with a wispy recurved crest and iridescent greenish back standing near water or wheeling in leisurely flapping flight (Baker 1929, p. 181).

हिंदी:
सिर पर पतली लंबी काली कलगी, पीठ पर चमकदार हरा-बैंगनी रंग और सीने पर काला पट्टा देखकर इसे दूर से पहचाना जा सकता है (बेकर 1929)।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements as given by Baker (1929):

MeasurementRange
BiometricsWing 220 to 236 mm. ; tail 108 to 119 mm. ; tarsus 44 to 48 mm. ; culmen 230 to 265 mm. (Baker 1929)

Stuart Baker (1929, p. 180) described the adult plumage: "Face, forehead, crown and long crest of narrow feathers black, glossed with green ; feathers round the eye, lower ear-coverts, sides of the head and neck white ; a black patch from the black face to the upper ear-coverts ; back, rump, scapulars and innermost secondaries bronze-green, highly glossed ; the scapulars marked with violet-purple ; upper tail-coverts cinnamon ; tail white with a very broad black subapical band, glossed green ; wing-coverts glossed deep blue, purplish in some lights; primaries and outer secondaries black with pale brown tips to the first four primaries ; throat, fore-neck and breast black... under tail-coverts cinnamon ; under wing-coverts black ; remaining lower plumage and axillaries white."

Baker (1929, p. 180) noted: "In non-breeding plumage the crown is brown rather than black; the face, chin, throat and fore-neck are white, more or less speckled or marked with brown or black; the scapulars, inner wing-coverts and innermost secondaries are edged with fulvous."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1929) के अनुसार इसके डैने 220 से 236 मिमी, पूंछ 108 से 119 मिमी, टार्सस 44 से 48 मिमी और चोंच 23 से 26.5 मिमी होती है। चोंच काली और टांगें नारंगी-भूरी होती हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Stuart Baker (1929, p. 181) recorded: "In India either in small flocks, in pairs, or singly. Its flight is a leisurely flapping but it indulges in much tumbling and twisting about and is capable of great speed when frightened. The well-known call is supposed to be syllabified in its name of 'Pee-wit' but is more a mewing cry than this word would express."

Baker (1929, p. 181) recorded that it "feeds on all kinds of insects, worms etc. and destroys many wire-worms on ploughed land."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1929) के अनुसार भारत में यह छोटे झुंडों, जोड़ों या अकेले दिखाई देती है। इसकी उड़ान धीमी और फड़फड़ाती हुई होती है तथा यह बिल्ली जैसी आवाज निकालती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Baker (1929, p. 181) recorded that the Lapwing breeds across Europe and temperate Asia: "The Lapwing breeds from the end of March to the end of May but most eggs are laid in early April. The eggs are laid in depressions in the earth... four in number."

In northern India, Baker (1929, p. 181) noted that it occurs "as far as the United Provinces, from October to March." GBIF records in Uttar Pradesh fall between December and February.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1929) के अनुसार यह मार्च से मई के दौरान यूरोप और उत्तरी एशिया में चार अंडे देकर प्रजनन करती है। उत्तर प्रदेश में इसके रिकॉर्ड दिसंबर से फरवरी के बीच हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Stuart Baker (1929, p. 181) described the diet: "It feeds on all kinds of insects, worms etc. and destroys many wire-worms on ploughed land."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1929) के अनुसार यह मिट्टी और पानी के पास मिलने वाले सभी प्रकार के कीड़ों, केंचुओं और जुते खेतों में पाए जाने वाले वायर-वर्म कीटों को खाती है।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Baker (1929, p. 181) noted its feeding habits on agricultural fields: "destroys many wire-worms on ploughed land." He also recorded: "Its eggs are famous all over Europe as an article of food for epicures." Protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.

हिंदी:

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya.

Published records from the northern sub-Himalayan belt are known from Bahraich (five records), Shravasti (four records), Balrampur (one record), and Lakhimpur Kheri (one record), reported by eight observers between 1996 and 2018 during the winter months of December to February.

A first record would be a photograph with place and date, sent through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Baker (1929, p. 180) gave the distribution: "The whole of Europe and North Asia. In Winter South to North Africa, India, Burma, the Indo-Chinese countries and South China."

In India: Baker (1929, p. 181) recorded: "In India the Lapwing is not uncommon in the North-West and North, as far as the United Provinces, from October to March but its range extends a good deal farther East and there are few years in which some are not seen in Cachar and Lakhimpur".

In Uttar Pradesh: Winter occurrences are concentrated along the northern Terai districts of Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, and Lakhimpur Kheri. GBIF holds no published record from Basti or Ayodhya.

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya; the nearest are in Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, and Lakhimpur Kheri.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1929) के अनुसार यह पूरे यूरोप और उत्तरी एशिया में पाई जाती है तथा सर्दियों में संयुक्त प्रांत (उत्तर प्रदेश) तक आती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के तराई जिलों में इसके शीतकालीन रिकॉर्ड हैं।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1758 in Systema Naturae (10th ed., Vol. 1, p. 148), under the basionym Tringa vanellus, with the type locality given as Sweden.

Subspecies. Monotypic. Baker (1929, p. 180) recognized no subspecies across its extensive breeding range across the Palearctic.

Family and order placement. Order Charadriiformes, family Charadriidae (plovers and lapwings), genus Vanellus.

IUCN status rationale. Evaluated as Near Threatened (NT) on the IUCN Red List (BirdLife International 2021).

हिंदी:
लिनिअस ने 1758 में स्वीडन से इसका वर्णन किया था। यह कैराड्रीइडी कुल का सदस्य है और प्रकृति संरक्षण संघ की रेड लिस्ट में संकट-निकट (NT) श्रेणी में है।
