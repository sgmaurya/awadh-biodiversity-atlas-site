रेडिश पॉन्ड स्नेल / Reddish Pond Snail

Radix rufescens (J.E.Gray, 1822) — Lymnaeidae

रेडिश पॉन्ड स्नेल — तालाबों, पोखरों और धान के खेतों में पानी के अंदर पाई जाने वाली एक बेहद आम, लाल-भूरे या बादामी रंग की जलीय घोंघा। इसे आमतौर पर 'लाल तालाब घोंघा' कहते हैं। इसका खोल बहुत पतला और नाजुक होता है, जिसका निचला हिस्सा बहुत फूला हुआ होता है। यह फेफड़ों से सांस लेता है और अक्सर पानी की सतह पर उल्टा तैरता हुआ काई चरता दिखाई देता है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
HabitDelicate, thin-shelled, air-breathing freshwater pulmonate snail on water weeds
Shell ShapeOvate, fragile, with a short, sharply pointed spire and an enormous body whorl
ColorationTranslucent reddish-brown, horn-coloured, or amber-red; animal greyish-speckled
ApertureVery large, ovate, occupying more than two-thirds of the total shell length
TentaclesFlat, broad, triangular tentacles with eyes situated at their inner bases

Field tip: Look on floating leaves of water lilies or submerged pond weed stems; watch for fragile reddish-brown oval snails crawling on vegetation or gliding inverted beneath the water film.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: पतला और नाजुक लाल-भूरा खोल, बहुत बड़ा फूला हुआ मुंह, चपटी तिकोनी मूंछें, और पानी की सतह पर उल्टा तैरना।

Morphology आकारिकी

Adult description: Medium-sized, thin, fragile freshwater pulmonate snail. Shell dextral, ovate, smooth, shiny, translucent reddish-horn to deep amber-brown; spire short, acutely pointed, composed of three to four small whorls; suture distinct, slightly impressed. Body whorl vastly expanded, inflated, occupying nearly 80 percent of total shell height, sculptured with fine, delicate longitudinal growth lines and obscure spiral striae. Aperture very large, wide, oval, outer lip thin, sharp, arched; columella twisted, with a gentle spiral fold, covered by a thin glistening white callus; umbilicus closed. Living animal pale olive-grey to purplish-brown, densely speckled with golden and black pigment dots; mantle edge thin; tentacles broad, flat, triangular, with eyes placed at their inner bases.

हिंदी: यह एक पतला और नाजुक जलीय घोंघा है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

  • Pulmonate Respiration: Possesses a vascularized pulmonary sac; periodically surfaces to open its pneumatic siphon to take in atmospheric air.
  • Surface Film Gliding: Glides upside down along the water surface tension film using rhythmic muscular contractions of its broad mucus-covered foot.
  • Algal Grazing: Rasps filamentous green algae, diatom films, and decaying macrophytes from submerged plant stems with its toothed radula.
  • Aestivation in Mud: Burrows into bottom mud and secretes a protective mucus barrier when seasonal ponds dry up in summer.
हिंदी: यह फेफड़ों से सांस लेने के लिए पानी की सतह पर आता है। यह पानी की सतह पर उल्टा चिपककर तैर सकता है और काई चरता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

  • Hermaphroditic Mating: Simultaneous hermaphrodite; reciprocally exchanges sperm with partners on aquatic vegetation.
  • Gelatinous Egg Masses: Deposits elongated, curved, transparent jelly-like egg masses containing numerous eggs on the undersides of lily pads (Subba Rao 1989).
  • Rapid Hatching: Embryos develop quickly, emerging as miniature fully-formed crawling snails (Subba Rao 1989).
  • Multivoltine Generations: Breeds continuously throughout the year in perennial waters, with peak abundance in winter and early spring.
हिंदी: यह कमल के पत्तों के नीचे जेली जैसी पारदर्शी पट्टी में ढेरों अंडे देता है। पंद्रह दिनों में नन्हे घोंघे निकलकर तैरने लगते हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

  • Submerged Macrophytes: Decaying leaves of Hydrilla, Ceratophyllum, water hyacinth, and peepal leaves fallen into water.
  • Algal Biofilms: Scrapes unicellular and filamentous green algae from submerged reeds and stones.
  • Detrital Silt: Ingests decomposing organic mud on the pond bottom.
हिंदी: यह जलकुंभी, हाइड्रिला और पानी में गिरे पत्तों की सड़न और हरी काई को खाकर अपना पेट भरता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Fishes: Bottom-feeding carps, snakeheads, and catfishes feed on snails by crushing their thin shells.
  • Waterbirds: Indian pond herons, jacanas, openbill storks, and common mynas (on mud margins) feed heavily on pond snails.
  • Aquatic Invertebrates: Belostomatid giant water bugs and dytiscid beetle larvae pierce and suck out snail tissues.
हिंदी: बगुले, घोंघखोर (ओपनबिल स्टॉर्क), बत्तखें और मछलियां इसके नाजुक खोल को फोड़कर इसे खा जाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

  • Veterinary Parasite Host: Intermediate host for the liver fluke (Fasciola gigantica), transmitting liver rot to grazing cattle, buffaloes, and sheep.
  • Aquaculture Benefits: Converts nuisance pond algae into valuable high-protein live food for commercial fish and domestic ducks.
  • Paddy Field Inhabitant: Common harmless resident of inundated rice paddies, feeding on algal scums without harming healthy rice plants.
हिंदी: यह मवेशियों में लिवर फ्लूक (यकृत कृमि) बीमारी फैलाने वाले परजीवियों का वाहक है, लेकिन तालाबों में मछलियों और बत्तखों के लिए बेहतरीन भोजन है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to South and Southeast Asia, from Pakistan and India through Bangladesh to Myanmar.

In India: Abundant across every state in freshwater wetlands, ponds, tanks, and rivers.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widely reported across the Gangetic plain from Gorakhpur, Ayodhya, Lucknow, and Varanasi.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in village ponds, oxbows of the Kuwana and Ghaghara, Bakhira wetland, and roadside ditches throughout eastern Uttar Pradesh.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by John Edward Gray in 1822 as Limnaea rufescens in Sowerby's The Genera of Recent and Fossil Shells, vol. 1.

Subspecies. Monotypic; formerly placed in Lymnaea Lamarck, now treated in the genus Radix Montfort.

Family and order placement. Placed in the family Lymnaeidae (pond snails), order Hygrophila, class Gastropoda.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN; ubiquitous, extremely abundant, and ecologically thriving across South Asian freshwaters.

हिंदी: जॉन एडवर्ड ग्रे ने 1822 में इसका वैज्ञानिक विवरण दिया था। यह लिम्नेइडी कुल का सदस्य है।
