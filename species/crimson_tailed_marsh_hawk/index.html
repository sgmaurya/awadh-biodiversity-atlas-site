क्रिमसन-टेल्ड मार्श हॉक / Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk

Orthetrum pruinosum (Rambur, 1842) — Libellulidae

क्रिमसन-टेल्ड मार्श हॉक — एक मध्यम आकार की व्याधपतंग जिसके नर का शरीर लाल-गुलाबी और सफ़ेद (pruinose coating) होता है, विशेषकर पूँछ पर। मादा भूरी-पीली होती है। यह दलदलों और उथले जलाशयों में पाई जाती है। वयस्क तेज़ी से उड़ते हैं और बड़े कीटों को पकड़ते हैं।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
SizeMedium dragonfly (body 38–44 mm; wingspan 52–62 mm)
BuildRobust; wings held open at rest
Male colourPredominantly red-pink body with white pruinose coating (giving pale appearance); crimson abdomen tip
Female colourBrown to yellowish-brown with darker markings
Abdomen tipDistinctly red or crimson-coloured (diagnostic)
BehaviourStrong flight; perches on ground, rocks, or vegetation

Field tip: The crimson-red abdomen tip in males is diagnostic. Males appear pale pink or whitish due to pruinose coating, while females are brown.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics:

MeasurementRange
Body length38–44 mm
Hindwing length26–30 mm
Wing span52–62 mm

Adult male: Head dark brown with pale frons. Thorax predominantly red with a thick pruinose (waxy) coating giving a pale pink or whitish appearance. Abdomen red, becoming darker red or crimson at the tip (segments 8–10). The pruinose coating masks the underlying bright coloration.

Adult female: Head brown. Thorax and abdomen brown to yellowish-brown with darker markings. No pruinose coating; retains bright coloration. Wings transparent with dark pterostigma.

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Orthetrum pruinosum is a strong-flying dragonfly of shallow, vegetation-rich wetlands and marshes. It is a robust hunter, feeding on moderate-sized flying insects. Males are territorial, perching on open ground or vegetation and pursuing rivals and prey with vigorous flight. Females forage among vegetation.

The species is moderately tolerant of environmental disturbance and can inhabit irrigated areas, ponds, and margins of rivers, making it a generalist indicator of wetland health.

Larval Stage

Naiads are robust, brownish, and found in shallow water among vegetation or on bottom substrates. Larval duration is approximately 10–12 weeks. Emergence occurs year-round, with seasonal peaks.

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Adult prey:

  • Small flies and midges
  • Mosquitoes
  • Dragonfly and damselfly nymphs
  • Small flying insects

Larval prey:

  • Aquatic insect larvae
  • Small crustaceans
  • Aquatic worms

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Birds: Kingfishers, bee-eaters, small eagles
  • Large dragonflies: Larger Anisopteran species
  • Spiders: Large wetland spiders
  • Fish: Fish fry and larger fish species

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Not documented as a pest. Feeds on small flying insects and may contribute to mosquito control in irrigation systems.

GBIF holds published records from core districts including Basti–Ayodhya. Expected in marshes and shallow ponds throughout eastern UP, year-round. Look for the male with distinctive crimson abdomen tip and pale pinkish (pruinose) body. A photograph showing the red abdomen tip and pale coloration, with date and location, would confirm a local record.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia; widespread from Pakistan and India to Myanmar, Thailand, and Indonesia.

In India: Common throughout plains and foothill regions.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widely recorded in wetland systems.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in marshes and shallow ponds; likely present and possibly already recorded.

  2. How does the pruinose coating affect body temperature? — Measure body temperature in males with and without coating.
  3. लाल पूँछ का रंग क्या कार्य करता है? (What is the function of the red tail colour?) — Speculate on thermoregulation or species/sex recognition.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Rambur described Orthetrum pruinosum in 1842. Type locality: India. The genus Orthetrum refers to the straight abdomen shape. The species name pruinosum (Latin: pruinose, waxy) refers to the males' white waxy coating.

Subspecies. Monotypic.

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae (skimmers and marsh hawks).

Phylogenetic notes. Orthetrum is a diverse genus of robust, generalist dragonflies found globally. O. pruinosum is one of the most widespread Asian members.

IUCN status rationale. Listed as Least Concern (LC). The species has a broad range and very stable populations in wetland habitats.
