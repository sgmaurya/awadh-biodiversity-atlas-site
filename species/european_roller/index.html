यूरोपियन रोलर / European Roller

Coracias garrulus Linnaeus, 1758 — Coraciidae

यूरोपियन रोलर — खुले मैदानों और खेती के आसपास बैठने वाला चमकीला आसमानी और बादामी पक्षी। बेकर (1927) के अनुसार इसका सिर, गला और पूरा निचला हिस्सा हल्का आसमानी-नीला होता है, जबकि पीठ हल्की कत्थई-बादामी होती है। डैनों पर गहरा नीला और काला रंग होता है। यह सुबह और शाम को सक्रिय होकर टिड्डों, भृंगों और छिपकलियों का शिकार करता है।

Field tip: Look for an unstreaked pastel turquoise roller with an all-cinnamon back perching on fence posts or trees in open country, performing acrobatic twisting flights (Baker 1927, p. 223).

हिंदी:
सिर, सीने और पेट पर एकसमान हल्का आसमानी नीला रंग तथा पीठ पर बादामी-कत्थई रंग देखकर इसे देशी नीलकंठ से अलग पहचाना जा सकता है (बेकर 1927)।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements as given by Baker (1927):

MeasurementRange
BiometricsWing 180 to 210 mm. ; tail 122 to 135 mm. ; tarsus 25 to 26 mm. ; culmen 30 to 35 mm. (Baker 1927)

Stuart Baker (1927, pp. 222–223) described the plumage of the eastern race Coracias garrula semenowi: "Forehead, narrowly, lores, next the lower mandible and chin brownish-white ; whole head, neck, and lower parts, under wing-coverts and axillaries pale blue, palest and tinged with green on the abdomen and vent, darkest and streaked with brilliant smalt-blue on the breast and throat ; back, scapulars and innermost secondaries light rufous-brown; rump and upper tail-coverts blue, generally the former deep blue, the latter pale but varying greatly and sometimes all pale blue ; central tail-feathers dull dark green ; lateral tail-feathers bluish-green with a broad pale blue terminal band and the outermost pair tipped with blue-black ; inner wing-coverts deep blue ; outer pale dull blue... quills black, overlain with deep blue on the outer webs and deep blue on the inner web underneath".

Colours of soft parts: iris pale brown to hazel-brown; bill brownish-black to black; legs and feet dull yellow (Baker 1927, p. 223).

हिंदी:
बेकर (1927) के अनुसार इसके डैने 180 से 210 मिमी, पूंछ 122 से 135 मिमी, टार्सस 25 से 26 मिमी और चोंच 30 से 35 मिमी होती है। चोंच काली और पैर हल्के पीले होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Stuart Baker (1927, pp. 223–224) noted: "Rollers are most active, especially in the hotter countries, during the mornings and evenings, when they are constantly on the move. Their flight is flapping and deliberate but they are capable of considerable speed when required and they spend much of their time performing acrobatic feats in the air, first twisting one way, then the other and often making a complete turn. As a rule their performances are accompanied by much harsh croaking and shrieking".

Baker (1927, pp. 223–224) observed: "During the heat of the day he perches high up on some branch or building, whence, though apparently asleep, he keeps an observant eye on all that happens around him, every now and then sallying forth to catch an insect on the wing or to seize some unlucky object whose movement on the grass below him has attracted his attention."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1927) के अनुसार यह सुबह और शाम के समय अधिक सक्रिय रहता है। दोपहर में यह किसी ऊंचे पेड़ या खंभे पर बैठकर नीचे जमीन पर कीड़ों की हलचल देखता रहता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Baker (1927, p. 223) recorded that semenowi breeds in May and June in holes in trees, buildings, or river banks and cliffs.

In Uttar Pradesh, GBIF records from central and eastern districts fall during autumn passage between August and October.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1927) के अनुसार यह मई और जून में कश्मीर तथा उत्तर-पश्चिम में प्रजनन करता है। उत्तर प्रदेश में इसके रिकॉर्ड अगस्त से अक्टूबर के बीच प्रवास के दौरान हैं।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Stuart Baker (1927, p. 224) recorded the food: "The main diet of the Roller consists of grasshoppers of all sizes, but there is not much that moves and lives and is sufficiently small that he will not seize and eat. They have been observed catching fish fry when trapped in shallow pools, whilst field-mice, lizards, beetles, however large, and all other insects are eaten by them."

हिंदी:
बेकर (1927) के अनुसार इसका मुख्य भोजन सभी आकारों के टिड्डे हैं। इसके अलावा यह भृंग, छिपकलियां, चूहे और उथले पानी में फंसी छोटी मछलियां भी पकड़ लेता है।

GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya.

Published records from the wider region come from Lucknow (twenty-three records), Lakhimpur Kheri (three records), and Gorakhpur (one literature record from 1991), reported by nine observers between 1991 and 2022 during August to October. Notably, nineteen of the twenty-seven published regional records come from a single observer at one site in Lucknow during August–October 2014–2015.

A first record would be a photograph with place and date, sent through the About page.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Baker (1927, p. 223) gave the distribution of semenowi: "Transcaspia and Turkestan to Kashmir, Baluchistan, Afghanistan and the North-West Frontier."

In India: Baker (1927, p. 223) noted that it is "one of the commonest birds in Kashmir during the Summer" and noted specimens from the Punjab.

In Uttar Pradesh: Records are largely confined to Lucknow, with sporadic records from Lakhimpur Kheri and a historical Gorakhpur citation. GBIF holds no published record from Basti or Ayodhya.

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya; the nearest published records are from Lucknow, Lakhimpur Kheri, and Gorakhpur.

हिंदी:
बेकर (1927) के अनुसार इसका नियमित दायरा कश्मीर और उत्तर-पश्चिम सीमांत तक है। उत्तर प्रदेश में इसके रिकॉर्ड मुख्य रूप से लखनऊ और लखीमपुर खीरी से हैं।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1758 in Systema Naturae (10th ed., Vol. 1, p. 107), under the binomial Coracias garrulus, with the type locality given as Sweden.

Subspecies. Baker (1927, pp. 222–223) recognized the eastern race Coracias garrula semenowi Loudon & Tschusi, 1902, noting that birds from Kashmir and Transcaspia are larger on average than western European birds.

Family and order placement. Order Coraciiformes, family Coraciidae (rollers), genus Coracias.

IUCN status rationale. Evaluated as Least Concern (LC) on the IUCN Red List (BirdLife International 2019).

हिंदी:
लिनिअस ने 1758 में स्वीडन से इसका वर्णन किया था। बेकर (1927) ने इसे कश्मीरी उपप्रजाति सेमिनोवी के तहत रखा था। यह कोरासिइडी कुल का सदस्य है और प्रकृति संरक्षण संघ की रेड लिस्ट में संकट-मुक्त (LC) है।
