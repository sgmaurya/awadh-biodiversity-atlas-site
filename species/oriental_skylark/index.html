भरत / Oriental Skylark

Alauda gulgula Franklin, 1831 — Alaudidae

भरत — पूर्वी उत्तर प्रदेश के खेतों और खुली भूमि में रहने वाली छोटी, गहरी भूरी चिड़िया। इसकी टेढ़ी-मेढ़ी उड़ान और तेज, चहचहाती आवाज़ इसे पहचान देती हैं। जमीन पर छिपता है और घास में घोंसला बनाता है। साल भर मैदानों में पाई जाती है।

Field tip: A small brown lark of open ground and fields. Seen running on the ground in stubble fields and waste areas. When flushed, flies up with erratic, bouncing flight, giving loud chattering calls. Often seen in pairs or small groups.

Morphology आकारिकी

Size: 17–18 cm long; wingspan 30–35 cm.

Plumage: Crown and back streaked brown and buff. Thin erectile crest on crown. Supercilium faint buff. Ear-coverts and throat buff. Underparts buff with breast streaks. Tail brown with white outer edges and tips. Wings brown with buff fringes.

Sexual dimorphism: Minimal; males slightly larger.

Head: Small crest visible when alert; faint pale supercilium.

Bill: Short, stout, pointed; adapted for pecking seeds.

Legs and feet: Pale; long hind claw, typical of larks.

Juveniles: Duller than adults with heavier streaking.

Vocalisations. Call repertoire:

  1. Flight call: A distinctive high-pitched chee-ip or chi-chirp, given in flight.
  2. Alarm call: Sharp, repeated chip-chip-chip.
  3. Song: A melodious warble, though not an elaborate singer compared to European skylark. Frequency.
हिंदी: उड़ते समय तीक्ष्ण "चीप" आवाज़। अलर्ट होने पर "चिप-चिप-चिप" कहता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Ground forager: Oriental Skylark is a terrestrial bird, spending most time on the ground. It feeds on:

  • Seeds of grasses and weeds
  • Small insects and their larvae
  • Termites and small beetles
  • Occasionally small snails

Foraging: Walks on ground, picking seeds and insects. Also hops and pecks. Occasionally makes short feeding flights.

Social behaviour: Solitary or in pairs during breeding season. Form loose groups outside breeding season.

Roosting: On the ground among grass and vegetation.

Predators: Kestrels, hooded crows, sometimes jackals. Chicks vulnerable to snakes and corvids.

Flight: Reluctant to fly unless flushed; when does fly, flight is erratic and bouncing.

हिंदी: जमीन पर दाने और कीड़े खोजता है। आमतौर पर अकेला या जोड़ों में। शिकारियों से बचने के लिए जमीन पर छिपता है।

Breeding: Resident breeder in eastern UP. Nests on ground in depression among grass and stubble, often hidden well. Clutch typically 3–4 eggs. May have 2–3 broods per season in suitable conditions.

Moult: Prebasic moult occurs post-breeding (July–September).

Site fidelity: Strong; returns to breeding territories in successive years.

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

  • Avian predators: Common Kestrel, Hooded Crow, Brown Shrike, rarely larger raptors
  • Mammalian predators: Jackals, foxes (chicks)
  • Nest predators: Snakes, mongooses, crows
  • Parasites: Mites and feather lice
हिंदी: बाजों और कौओं का शिकार होता है। जमीन पर घोंसला होने से सांपों को भी खतरा।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Protected species: The Oriental Skylark is protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972. Hunting is prohibited in India.

Agricultural benefit: Seeds of weeds are a major part of diet; also feeds on agricultural pest insects including grasshoppers and termites.

Pest control: Contributes to natural pest management of field insects, though the impact is minor.

Field indicator: Its presence indicates relatively healthy grassland and crop-residue habitats.

Cultural relevance: Mentioned in Hindi literature and culture; a familiar bird of village commons and fields.

Hunting history: In historical accounts, sometimes caught for food and kept as caged birds for song, but never at significant scale.

हिंदी: यह विधि अधिनियम 1972 के तहत संरक्षित है। इसका शिकार करना कानूनी रूप से निषिद्ध है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.

Year-round range: Expected throughout eastern UP on open grasslands, cultivated plains, and fields. GBIF holds published records from these districts. Particularly common in areas with rice and wheat cultivation and associated stubble fields.

Habitat preference: Open ground with short grass, stubble fields, waste ground, and field margins. Avoids dense vegetation and shade.

Peak season: Present year-round; may be more visible in post-harvest seasons when fields are open.

Detection: Often first noted by its flight calls when flushed from fields; early morning and evening activity periods are best.

What a first record would be: A dated photograph or clear observation noting the bird in eastern UP submitted through the About page, with location and date.

हिंदी: खेतों और खुली भूमि में साल भर अपेक्षित। धान और गेहूँ की कटाई के बाद अधिक दिखता है। घाघरा और राप्ती के पास के मैदानों में आम।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Indian subcontinent and adjacent areas of Asia. Resident populations across much of India and adjoining regions.

In India: Widespread resident across northern, central, and eastern India in suitable grassland habitats.

In Uttar Pradesh: Distributed throughout the state in cultivated and grassland areas, particularly common in eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Regular resident in cultivated fields and grasslands; year-round presence expected.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. The species was described by Franklin in 1831. The type locality is the Indian subcontinent.

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies across Asia. The Indian population is A. g. gulgula.

Family and order placement. Order Passeriformes; family Alaudidae (larks). The genus Alauda contains several lark species of open habitats.

Phylogenetic notes. Related to other ground-dwelling larks across Asia. Part of a clade of Eurasian larks.

IUCN status rationale. Assessed as Least Concern (LC) globally. Populations are stable despite habitat changes. The species remains adaptable to cultivated and grassland areas across its range. In India, it is protected under the Wildlife Protection Act 1972.
