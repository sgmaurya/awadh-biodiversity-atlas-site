बचटा / Caesar Weed

Urena lobata L. — Malvaceae

बचटा — भारत के गर्म प्रदेशों का एक वर्षीय खरपतवार, जो बस्ती के खेतों की मेड़ों, बंजर भूमियों और नम मिट्टी पर उगता है। इसके पत्ते ३-से-५ खंडों में बँटे हुए होते हैं, और गर्मी में गुलाबी या हल्के बैंगनी रंग के फूल खिलते हैं। फल पहलेदार और कँटीले होते हैं, जो जानवरों की खाल और कपड़ों में आसानी से चिपक जाते हैं। बीजों में रेशे हैं, और इन्हीं रेशों को प्राचीन समय में रस्सी बनाने में काम लिया जाता था।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
FormErect annual herb, 60–150 cm tall, with rough, hairy stems
LeavesPalmately lobed (usually 3–5 lobes), 5–10 cm across, rough texture, with 3–5 prominent veins
FlowersSmall, pink or pale mauve, five petals, 1.5–2 cm diameter, clustered at stem nodes
FruitBristly, netted, disk-shaped capsule with hooked spines around the margin, 6–8 mm diameter
StemCylindrical, greenish, densely covered with harsh hairs and small curved prickles

Field tip: Look for a hairy annual herb with distinctly lobed leaves that feel rough to touch. Pink or mauve flowers at the stem joints in monsoon and post-monsoon. The spiky fruits cling to clothes and animal fur.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: खुरदरे रोएँ वाला पौधा, पत्तियाँ हथेली जैसी खंडित। गुलाबी फूल, गर्मी के मौसम में। फल काँटेदार, कपड़े में चिपक जाते हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (typical measurements for specimens in eastern UP):

FeatureMeasurement
Plant height60–150 cm
Leaf width5–10 cm
Leaf lobe count3–5
Flower diameter1.5–2 cm
Fruit diameter6–8 mm

Stem and bark: Cylindrical, greenish, becoming woody at base, densely covered with stiff, short hairs and minute recurved prickles. Wood soft and fibrous.

Leaves: Alternate, palmately lobed (usually 3–5 lobes), roughly hairy on both surfaces, margins crenate, blade 5–10 cm wide, petioles 2–4 cm. The lobes are lanceolate and the middle lobe is longest.

Flowers: Solitary or in small clusters in the leaf axils. Five petals, pink or pale mauve, with a prominent darker centre. Epicalyx present (characteristic of Malvaceae) with 5–8 linear segments.

Fruit: A dehiscent capsule, disk-shaped, 6–8 mm diameter, with a net-like surface and 5 hooked spines protruding from the margin. Seeds black, small, reniform.

हिंदी: पत्तियाँ ३-५ खंडों में विभक्त, मोटे रोएँ वाली। गुलाबी फूल। फल नेट जैसा, काँटेदार, कपड़े से चिपक जाता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

A fast-growing, light-loving weed of warm regions that thrives in disturbed soils of eastern UP. Prefers moist, well-drained alluvial soils in agricultural margins and open fields. Germinates with the onset of the monsoon and flowers during the warm months. The spiny fruits are excellent at dispersal via animal fur and clothing, which is how it colonizes new areas. It serves as a generalist nectar source for butterflies and bees during its flowering season.

Seeds require warmth and moisture for germination. Lifespan is one season.

हिंदी: मई-जून में अंकुरण, गर्मी में फूल और फल। सर्दी तक पौधा सूख जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Visited by bees, butterflies, and hoverflies for nectar.
  • Fiber associations: Related to jute and okra in the Malvaceae family.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf-eating caterpillars and beetles occasionally feed on the foliage.
  • Powdery mildew may appear on stressed plants.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Historical use: In old texts, the fiber of Urena lobata is mentioned as a source of rough cordage in India and Southeast Asia, though it was never commercially significant compared to jute.

Today: Considered a minor weed in eastern UP. It grows as a volunteer in fallow fields and field margins, competing for space but rarely causing serious crop losses. Its presence is more of a nuisance because the spiny fruits contaminate harvested seed and grain.

Control: Farmers pull it during field preparation or allow it to grow in margins for pollinator support.

हिंदी: यह बस्ती में एक कम महत्वपूर्ण खरपतवार है। रेशे का ऐतिहासिक उपयोग था, लेकिन आजकल इसे फसल से दूर रखा जाता है।

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

To be expected in field margins, fallow fields, and disturbed ground in Basti district during the monsoon and autumn months (June–December). More common after a wet monsoon. Look in the edges of cotton and chickpea fields, and along irrigation canal banks. GBIF holds published records from these districts.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: A pantropical weed whose original home is debated; long established across South Asia, including India.

In India: Warm regions of peninsular and Indo-Gangetic India, below 1,500 m.

In Uttar Pradesh: Throughout the plains, particularly common in moist areas.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Common in field margins and disturbed ground; distributed across the district.

Field rule: Caesar Weed is hairy and has palmately lobed leaves with pink flowers and spiny burr-like fruits. Country Mallow has entire (unsplit) heart-shaped leaves. False Mallow is much smaller with yellow flowers.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 in Species Plantarum as Urena lobata, Vol. 2, p. 745. Type locality is the tropical Americas.

Subspecies. Monotypic.

Family and order placement. Order Malvales, family Malvaceae, the mallow family. Placed near Hibiscus and Abelmoschus.

Phylogenetic notes. Urena belongs to the Ureneae tribe within Malvaceae and is related to Abelmoschus (okra).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widespread and abundant; no conservation threat.
