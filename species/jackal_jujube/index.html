जैकाल जुजुबे / Jackal Jujube

Ziziphus oenopolia (L.) Mill. — Rhamnaceae

जैकाल जुजुबे — जंगलों, झाड़ियों और मेड़ों पर उगने वाली एक कांटेदार, फैलने वाली जंगली बेर की झाड़ी। इसे ग्रामीण इलाकों में 'मकई', 'सियार बेर' या 'बनकंटी' कहते हैं। इसकी टहनियों पर कांटों की तीखी जोड़ियां होती हैं, जिनमें से एक कांटा सीधा और दूसरा पीछे की ओर मुड़ा होता है। इसके छोटे काले पके फल सियार, बिज्जू और चिड़ियां बड़े चाव से खाते हैं।

Field tip: Look along rural hedgerows or ravines for a straggling thorny shrub with rusty twigs, asymmetrical leaves with silky golden undersides, and clusters of tiny black berries.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: एक सीधा और एक मुड़ा हुआ कांटा, पत्तों के नीचे सुनहरे रेशमी रोएं, और छोटे काले गोल जंगली बेर।

Morphology आकारिकी

Shrub description: Straggling or climbing, thorny, much-branched woody shrub. Bark greyish-brown, rough; branches slender, zigzag, young twigs densely clothed with rusty-brown or fulvous velvety tomentum. Stipular spines paired: one straight, slender, directed forward, the other shorter, stout, and sharply recurved downwards. Leaves alternate, distichous, ovate-lanceolate or elliptic, strongly oblique and asymmetrical at base, acute or shortly acuminate at apex, margins minutely serrulate or subentire, distinctly three-nerved from base; upper surface dark green, glabrous; lower surface densely covered with silky, appressed, golden or fulvous hairs. Inflorescence a small, dense, axillary cyme; pedicels short, tomentose. Flowers tiny, yellowish-green, five-merous. Fruit a small, globose or obovoid, fleshy drupe, one-seeded, ripening from green through reddish to glossy jet-black.

हिंदी: यह एक फैलने वाली कांटेदार झाड़ी है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

  • Grappling Thorns: Uses downward-pointing hooked spines to grip host bushes and clamber upward into sunlight.
  • Mammal and Bird Frugivory: Sweetish acidic black fruits attract golden jackals (Canis aureus), small Indian civets, and bulbuls that disperse seeds.
  • Drought Endurance: Deep taproots and silky-haired leaf undersides reduce transpiration during searing summer heat.
  • Thorny Sanctuary: Dense spiny tangles provide predator-proof nesting cover for laughing doves and babblers.
हिंदी: यह अपने मुड़े हुए कांटों से अन्य पेड़ों पर चढ़ती है। सियार और जंगली जानवर इसके मीठे-खट्टे फल खाकर बीज फैलाते हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

  • Monsoon Blooming: Tiny yellowish-green flowers open in clusters from May through September.
  • Fruit Development: Green drupes swell through the rainy season, turning glossy reddish-brown in late autumn.
  • Winter Ripening: Fruits turn completely black and ripen from October to December.
  • Seed Dormancy: Tough stony seeds remain dormant through the dry summer, germinating with the first heavy monsoon rains.
हिंदी: बरसात में फूल आते हैं और सर्दियों में छोटे काले बेर पकते हैं। बारिश में इनके बीज अंकुरित होते हैं।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Butterfly Host: Primary food plant for caterpillars of the pierid butterfly Pieris canidia and lycaenid blue butterflies (Tarucus).
  • Mammalian Dispersal: Frugivory by golden jackals gives the plant its common name 'jackal jujube' (siyar ber).
  • Bird Shelter: Favoured roosting and nesting shrub for common mynas and laughing doves.
हिंदी: नीली तितलियों की इल्लियां इसके पत्ते खाती हैं। सियार और मैना इसके फल खाकर बीज बिखेरते हैं।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Witches' Broom: Phytoplasma infection causes dense clustering of dwarfed twigs resembling a witches' broom.
  • Fruit Flies: Tephritid fruit fly maggots infest ripening black drupes during autumn.
  • Lepidopteran Defoliation: Hairy caterpillars occasionally feed on leaves during late monsoon.
हिंदी: बीमारी लगने पर टहनियां झाड़ू जैसी घनी हो जाती हैं और फलों में कभी-कभी कीड़े लग जाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

  • Live Protective Hedge: Extensively utilized in rural Awadh to create spiny living fences around orchards and fields.
  • Wild Edible Fruit: Rural children harvest and eat the small sour-sweet black berries during winter.
  • Ayurvedic Medicine: Bark and roots are rich in cyclopeptide alkaloids, used in traditional healing for wound poultices and stomach aches.
हिंदी: ग्रामीण बच्चे सर्दियों में इसके खट्टे-मीठे काले बेर खाते हैं। खेतों की मेड़ों पर इसे जानवरों से बचाव के लिए लगाया जाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South Asia, Southeast Asia, and northern Australia, from India and Sri Lanka to Malaysia and Queensland.

In India: Abundant across dry deciduous forests, scrub zones, and plains throughout the subcontinent.

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded across Gangetic scrub and ravine areas from Varanasi, Mirzapur, Lucknow, and Ayodhya.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected along railway embankments, Kuwana and Ghaghara scrub banks, and old village boundaries throughout Basti.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753 as Rhamnus oenopolia and transferred by Philip Miller in 1768 as Ziziphus oenopolia in The Gardeners Dictionary, ed. 8, no. 3.

Subspecies. Monotypic across northern India; placed in the family Rhamnaceae.

Family and order placement. Placed in the family Rhamnaceae (buckthorn family), tribe Zizipheae, in the order Rosales.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN; abundant, widespread, and ecologically resilient across disturbed landscapes.
