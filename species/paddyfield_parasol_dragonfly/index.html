पैडीफ़ील्ड पैरासोल व्याध पतंग / Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly

Neurothemis intermedia (Rambur, 1842) — Libellulidae

पैडीफ़ील्ड पैरासोल व्याध पतंग — धान के खेतों, पानी भरी नालियों और दलदली घासों में पाई जाने वाली एक छोटी और बहुत सुंदर व्याध पतंग (dragonfly) है। इसके पंखों के भीतरी आधे हिस्से पर सुनहरी-पीली या हल्की भूरी रंगत (tint) होती है, जो धूप में छतरी (parasol) की तरह चमकती है। नर का शरीर हल्का पीला-नारंगी या लाल-भूरा होता है। यह धान की बालियों और घासों पर नीचे बैठकर फसल को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले कीटों का शिकार करती है।

Field tip: Look low among standing rice crops, grassy field borders, and marshy ditches. A small, rusty-yellow or pale reddish dragonfly fluttering weakly among rice stalks and perching with its amber-tinted wings drooped downward around its body is the Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly.

हिंदी: धान के पौधों पर नीचे बैठने वाली इस छोटी पतंग के पंखों की जड़ सुनहरी-पीली होती है और बैठते समय यह अपने पंखों को छतरी की तरह नीचे झुका लेती है।

Adult description:

Neurothemis intermedia is a small, slender-bodied libellulid dragonfly displaying characteristic pale amber wing pigmentation (Fraser 1936). The head has a pale reddish-brown vertex; the frons is yellowish-brown with a subtle reddish flush in mature males, and the compound eyes are warm reddish-brown above, transitioning to olive-green and pale yellow below (Subramanian 2005). The synthorax is pale golden-yellow to rusty reddish-brown on the dorsum, densely clothed in soft golden hairs and lacking distinct dark stripes (Fraser 1936).

The male abdomen is slender, cylindrical, and uniform rusty-red or warm ochreous-orange: segments 1 through 10 are unmarked by bold black spots, although older individuals develop fine black lateral carinae (Subramanian 2005). The anal appendages are small, pale reddish-yellow, and slightly curved upward at the tips (Fraser 1936).

Females closely resemble males in general coloration, but have an olivaceous-yellow thorax and a warm golden-yellow abdomen (Fraser 1936). In both sexes, the wings are broad with extremely dense, complex reticulation; the basal half of both forewings and hindwings is washed with a delicate golden-amber or pale reddish-yellow tint that extends from the wing base to the nodus, while the distal half of the wing is completely hyaline (Subramanian 2005). The pterostigma is long, narrow, and bright reddish-brown (Fraser 1936).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is squat, broad, and pale brownish-grey, living among submerged root tangles of flooded grasses and rice plants. The abdomen bears small dorsal hooks and short lateral spines, providing camouflage in muddy agricultural drains.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 21 से 24 मिमी लंबा और हल्का लाल-नारंगी होता है। इसके पंखों का आधा हिस्सा सुनहरी-पीला और बाकी आधा पारदर्शी होता है। मादा हल्के पीले रंग की होती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly is a low-flying, weak-fluttering dweller of open agricultural plains and shallow wetlands (Subramanian 2005). It is closely tied to traditional lowland rice cultivation, frequenting irrigated paddy fields, weed-choked drainage channels, and seasonal rainwater pools (Fraser 1936). Unlike wide-ranging gliders, it rarely flies higher than one metre above the crop canopy, fluttering weakly between rice stems.

Adults are active throughout sunny hours (Subramanian 2005). Males perch prominently on the tips of rice leaves or emergent grass culms, drooping their broad wings downward in a characteristic parasol posture that shelters their body from direct overhead sun (Fraser 1936). When disturbed, they fly a few metres before resettling low in the crop. Mating occurs perched among crop foliage. Oviposition is exophytic: the female hovers low over shallow standing water in paddy fields, dipping her abdomen repeatedly to wash off clutches of eggs.

हिंदी: यह पतंग धान के पौधों के बीच बहुत नीचे उड़ती है और पत्तियों पर बैठकर अपने पंखों को नीचे झुका लेती है। यह तेज हवाओं से बचती है और धान के तनों के बीच ही शिकार करती है।

Females fly in low hovering arcs across calm water, tapping their abdomen against the surface film between rice hills to release hundreds of eggs that settle into the mud. The eggs hatch after a few days into predatory nymphs. Naiads develop rapidly through several instars over a few weeks, feeding on aquatic insect larvae in warm shallow paddy water. At maturity, the nymph crawls up a rice stalk at sunrise to shed its exuvia and emerge as an adult dragonfly.

हिंदी: मादा धान के पौधों के बीच पानी पर उड़ते हुए अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडे फूटते हैं और पानी में रहने वाला निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में बड़ा होकर तने पर चढ़कर पतंग बन जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Neurothemis intermedia is a dedicated predator of agricultural and wetland insects (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Swarming wetland mosquitoes, including Culex quinquefasciatus (Southern House Mosquito) and Culex tritaeniorhynchus (Rice-field Mosquito).
  • Destructive sap-feeding insect pests of rice, including Nilaparvata lugens (Brown Planthopper) and Nephotettix virescens (Green Leafhopper).
  • Small dipteran flies, midges (Chironomidae), and tiny moths fluttering inside the rice canopy.

Larval diet:

  • Mosquito larvae, midge bloodworms, small ostracods, and aquatic worms in shallow field water.
हिंदी: यह पतंग धान की फसलों को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले फुदकों, छोटे मच्छरों और मक्खियों को खाती है। इसका निम्फ पानी में रहने वाले मच्छरों के लार्वा को नष्ट करता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented in Gangetic plain freshwater ecosystems:

  • Predatory dragonflies: Larger anisopterans like Crocothemis servilia (Scarlet Skimmer) and Orthetrum sabina (Slender Skimmer) frequently prey upon perching parasols.
  • Spiders: Wetland orb-weavers and wolf spiders (Lycosa) stalk resting adults on paddy bunds.
  • Amphibians: Juvenile frogs like Euphlyctis cyanophlyctis (Skittering Frog) catch low-perching adults.
  • Birds: Cattle egrets and pond herons walking through flooded paddy fields snap up resting adults.
हिंदी: बड़े शिकारी व्याध पतंग, धान की मेड़ों पर रहने वाली मकड़ियाँ और बगुले इस छोटी पतंग को पकड़कर खाते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Agroecological benefits in rice farming: The Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly is one of the most beneficial natural predators in lowland rice agroecosystems of northern India (Subramanian 2005). Because it hunts inside the rice canopy where severe sap-sucking pests like the brown planthopper (Nilaparvata lugens Brown Planthopper) and green leafhopper (Nephotettix virescens Green Leafhopper) reside, it provides free, non-chemical biological suppression of crop hopper-burn.

Suppression of rural disease vectors: Adults and aquatic naiads actively prey upon Culex tritaeniorhynchus (Rice-field Mosquito), the principal rural vector of Japanese encephalitis that breeds abundantly in flooded rice fields (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal, economic, or cultural uses are recorded.

Today: Valued by agricultural entomologists as a key beneficial bio-control ally in sustainable rice farming.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Quantifying the functional response of Neurothemis intermedia predation on planthopper swarms under different rice planting densities.

हिंदी: यह पतंग धान के किसानों के लिए एक बहुत बड़ा मित्र कीट है। यह फसल को नुकसान पहुँचाने वाले कीटों और जापानी बुखार फैलाने वाले मच्छरों का सफाया करती है।

The Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh in flooded rice fields, grassy irrigation drains, and marshy borrow pits. Look low among rice stalks and weeds in full sunshine from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph showing the golden-amber basal wing patch and parasol perching posture, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South and Southeast Asia, including India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, Indochina, and southern China (Fraser 1936).

In India: Recorded throughout peninsular India, the Western and Eastern Ghats, central India, and across the Gangetic plains to Assam and West Bengal (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from agricultural floodplains, river valleys, and rural wetland mosaics throughout central and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a widespread resident breeding dragonfly in rural paddy fields and agricultural drainage systems.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Jules Pierre Rambur in 1842 as Libellula intermedia in Histoire naturelle des insectes. Névroptères, page 124. Type locality: "Bengale" (Bengal, India).

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies are recognized across the Oriental realm. The population inhabiting the mainland of northern India and the Gangetic plain has been treated as the nominotypical subspecies Neurothemis intermedia intermedia (Rambur, 1842).

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Anisoptera, family Libellulidae. The genus Neurothemis Brauer, 1867, comprises parasol dragonflies characterized by exceptionally dense wing reticulation and colourful basal wing patches.

Phylogenetic notes. Neurothemis intermedia is readily distinguished from congeners like N. fulvia and N. tullia by its pale amber basal wing tint and smaller, more slender body build (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure native species that thrives across agricultural floodplains.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Neurothemis intermedia है और यह Libellulidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
