खीरी कुकरी साँप / Kheri Kukri Snake

Oligodon kheriensis Acharji & Ray, 1936 — Colubridae

खीरी कुकरी साँप — उत्तर प्रदेश के खीरी क्षेत्र से वर्णित एक अत्यंत दुर्लभ और कम ज्ञात कुकरी साँप। स्मिथ (1943) के अनुसार इसका ऊपरी शरीर लालिमायुक्त या बादामी रंग का और पेट सफेद होता है। यह एक गैर-विषैला साँप है। वन्यजीव (संरक्षण) अधिनियम के तहत यह एक संरक्षित प्रजाति है।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Build & FormSmooth-scaled colubrid snake; described from North Kheri Division (Smith 1943)
Dorsal ColourFawn or buff-coloured above (reddish or pinkish in life) (Smith 1943)
Ventral ColourWhitish below, lacking dark markings (Smith 1943)
PatternUnpatterned dorsal surface; an immaculate form without bold bars (Smith 1943)

Field tip: Look for an unpatterned kukri snake with uniform reddish, fawn, or buff-coloured upperparts and a plain whitish underside (Smith 1943, p. 204).

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurements not available in consulted sources.

Smith (1943, p. 204) described the coloration of this form under Form II: "Fawn or buff- coloured above (reddish or pinkish in life), whitish below. This form, which is only an immaculate variety of Form I, may be found in any part of Tenasserim, Burma and Assam. Here I place Cantor’s violaceus from Bengal, and also Acharji & Ray’s kheriensis from the United Provinces, The latter, known only from a single individual, represents the extreme western range of the species."

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Known from northern India. Smith (1943, p. 204) noted that Acharji & Ray's kheriensis "known only from a single individual, represents the extreme western range of the species" (under Oligodon cyclurus).

In India: Described from North Kheri Division, Uttar Pradesh.

In Uttar Pradesh: One road-killed snake, 15 July 2025, State Highway 101 near Maleneya, Palia, Lakhimpur Kheri (India Roadkill Monitoring).

In Basti district / eastern UP: GBIF holds no record from Basti or Ayodhya.

हिंदी:
इसे उत्तर प्रदेश के उत्तरी खीरी प्रभाग से वर्णित किया गया था। लखीमपुर खीरी से 2025 का एक रोड-किल रिकॉर्ड है, किंतु बस्ती और अयोध्या से कोई रिकॉर्ड नहीं है।

Field rule: The Kheri Kukri Snake is distinguished from the Common Kukri Snake by its unpatterned reddish or buff-coloured dorsal surface, lacking bold transverse dark bars (Smith 1943, p. 204).

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by M. N. Acharji and H. C. Ray in 1936 in Records of the Indian Museum (vol. 38, p. 519), as Oligodon kheriensis, from North Kheri Division, U.P.

Subspecies. Monotypic. Sunk by Smith (1943, p. 204) as an immaculate variety (Form II) of Oligodon cyclurus. Modern authorities (Reptile Database, IUCN Red List) treat it as a distinct valid species.

Family and order placement. Order Squamata, suborder Serpentes, family Colubridae, genus Oligodon.

Phylogenetic notes. Not available in consulted sources.

IUCN status rationale. Evaluated as Least Concern (LC) on the IUCN Red List (Limbu et al. 2022).

हिंदी:
एम. एन. आचार्य और एच. सी. रे ने 1936 में इसे उत्तरी खीरी से वर्णित किया था। स्मिथ (1943) ने इसे 'ओलिगोडॉन साइक्लूरस' का एक रूप माना था, किंतु आधुनिक वैज्ञानिक इसे एक स्वतंत्र प्रजाति मानते हैं। आईयूसीएन रेड लिस्ट में यह संकट-मुक्त (LC) है।
