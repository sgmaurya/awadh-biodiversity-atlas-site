दादमर्दन / Candle Bush

Senna alata (L.) Roxb. — Fabaceae

दादमर्दन — एक विदेशी झाड़ी जो अब भारत के बाग़ों और बंजर जमीन पर पाई जाती है। इसके पीले फूलों की मोमबत्ती जैसी बालियाँ आकर्षक होती हैं — इसीलिए इसे कुछ लोग "कैंडल बुश" कहते हैं। इसकी पत्तियों और फलियों का उपयोग परंपरागत दवा में होता है, विशेषकर दाद और अन्य त्वचा रोगों के लिए।

Field tip: Look for a shrub with compound leaves and a distinctive yellow, candle-like flower spike (dense spike of small flowers). The entire plant is aromatic when leaves are crushed.

Morphology आकारिकी

Biometrics (cultivated plants in eastern India):

MeasurementRange
Height1.5–3 m

Flower spike length20–30 cm

Stem and bark: Woody, with grey-brown bark; branches ascending.

Leaves: Alternate, compound (pinnate-paripinnate), with 5–10 pairs of small, oval, glabrous leaflets and a terminal leaflet. The leaf rachis has small glands between the leaflets.

Flowers: Bright golden-yellow, small (about 1 cm), arranged densely in a terminal, cylindrical inflorescence (raceme), resembling a candle flame. Flowers appear in groups along the axis.

Fruit: A large, flat, elongated pod (legume), 15–25 cm long and 1.5–2 cm wide; brown when ripe. Contains many seeds.

Aromatic: Leaves and flowers are aromatic when crushed.

हिंदी: पत्ती चक्कर आकार की, जोड़े में। फूल सोने-पीले, मोमबत्ती जैसी बाली। फली लंबी, चपटी।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकता

Senna alata is native to tropical America but is now cultivated and naturalized in tropical and subtropical Asia, including India. The plant is drought-tolerant and grows in poor soils. It is fast-growing and often used as an ornamental shrub. The plant is fixed-nitrogen source, improving soil, though documentation from eastern UP is limited.

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pollinators: Bees and other insects visit the flowers.
  • Seed dispersal: Pods may be split by animals; seed dispersal is limited.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Leaf spots in humid seasons (minor).
  • Generally pest-resistant.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional medicinal use (documented uses; not medical advice):

  • Skin conditions: The crushed leaves and leaf paste are applied topically for fungal infections (dād), ringworm, and other skin complaints. The leaves contain compounds with antifungal activity, documented in ethnobotanical literature.
  • Laxative: The pods have traditionally been used as a gentle laxative; the active compounds are similar to those in commercial senna preparations.
  • Wounds: Leaf poultice applied to minor cuts and wounds for healing.

Today: Cultivated as an ornamental shrub in gardens for its striking flower spikes. The plant is also used locally in traditional health preparations, though usage has declined with availability of commercial products.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Antifungal compounds in Senna alata leaves are studied for potential pharmaceutical applications.

हिंदी: पारंपरिक रूप से दाद और त्वचा रोग की दवा है। पत्ते को कुचलकर लगाया जाता है। फली को ​हल्के रेचक के रूप में भी उपयोग करते हैं। अब बाग़ों में सजावट के लिए लगाया जाता है।

अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Candle Bush is occasionally cultivated in village gardens in Basti district, prized for its ornamental yellow flower spikes. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain. The plant is most visible during the monsoon and early post-monsoon season (August–November) when in flower. A first confirmed local record would be a photograph showing the candle-like inflorescence with place and date.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical America; cultivated and naturalized across tropical and subtropical regions of Asia and Africa.

In India: Widespread in gardens and disturbed areas; particularly common in peninsular India.

In Uttar Pradesh: Occasional garden cultivation; not widespread.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Rare; found only in cultivated gardens where it has been deliberately planted.

  2. What insects visit the yellow flower spikes, and which ones are the pollinators? / फूलों पर कौन से कीड़े आते हैं? — Observe for extended periods; identify pollinators.
  3. Can the leaf paste be prepared at home and applied safely to fungal infections? — This is a traditional use; consult with local practitioners about preparation and safety.
  4. How much seed can be obtained from one mature shrub in a year? — Collect and count seeds from several pods.
  5. Does candle bush spread naturally from cultivated plants, or is it only maintained by deliberate planting? — Check wastelands and disturbed areas near cultivated plants for volunteers.

Field rule: A shrub with compound leaves and a distinctly yellow, candle-shaped flower spike is Senna alata.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described by Linnaeus as Cassia alata in 1753. It was transferred to Senna by William Roxburgh in 1832 in Flora Indica. The genus name Senna is derived from the Arabic sana, and alata means "winged" (referring to the winged leaf rachis).

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised.

Family and order placement. Order Fabales, family Fabaceae (legume family), subfamily Caesalpinioideae (caesalpinoid legumes).

Phylogenetic notes. Senna is a large genus of about 350 species of tropical legumes.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Widely cultivated; no wild populations in India are assessed for conservation status.
