स्ट्रीम ग्लोरी व्याध पतंग / Stream Glory

Neurobasis chinensis (Linnaeus, 1758) — Calopterygidae

स्ट्रीम ग्लोरी व्याध पतंग — साफ और बहते हुए पानी वाली पहाड़ी तथा तराई की नदियों और नालों के किनारे पाई जाने वाली भारत की सबसे खूबसूरत और बड़ी डैमसेल्फ़्लाई में से एक है। इसके नर का पूरा शरीर पन्ना जैसा चमकदार हरा-नीला होता है और इसके पिछले पंख ऊपर से चमकदार धातुई हरे तथा नीचे से गहरे नीले-काले होते हैं। जब यह बहते पानी के ऊपर तितली की तरह लहराते हुए उड़ती है, तो इसके पिछले पंख धूप में हरे पन्ने की तरह चमकते हैं।

Field tip: Look along shaded, clear running streams with submerged aquatic vegetation. A large, slender damselfly dancing low over rapids and riffles whose clear forewings contrast with dazzling emerald-green hindwings flashing in sunbeams is the Stream Glory.

हिंदी: साफ बहते पानी के ऊपर तितली की तरह नाचती हुई उड़ने वाली इस बड़ी पतंग के पिछले पंख धूप में हरे पन्ने जैसे चमकते हैं और इसका शरीर चमकीला हरा होता है।

Adult description:

Neurobasis chinensis is a large, magnificent calopterygid damselfly displaying spectacular sexual dimorphism (Fraser 1934). In mature males, the entire head, synthorax, and abdomen are clothed in a glittering, metallic emerald-green or peacock-bronze cuticle that gleams brightly under direct sunlight (Subramanian 2005). The compound eyes are large, dark brown above, and emerald-green below (Fraser 1934). The legs are long, slender, jet black, and armed with numerous long, fine spines for perching on overhanging foliage (Subramanian 2005).

The male wings are broad, rounded, and non-petiolate, displaying an extraordinary dual coloration: the forewings are entirely transparent and hyaline, with shining metallic green venation, while the hindwings are completely opaque, coated on the dorsal surface with intense metallic emerald-green or brassy-green scales that shift to velvety blackish-violet on the underside (Fraser 1934). Males possess no true pterostigmata on either wing (Subramanian 2005).

Females are slightly larger and have an iridescent coppery-green body (Fraser 1934). Her wings are semi-transparent and washed with a warm golden-amber or light brownish tint; both forewings and hindwings carry prominent, creamy-white false pterostigmata (pseudopterostigmata) traversed by fine crossveins (Fraser 1934).

Larval Stage निम्फ अवस्था

The aquatic naiad is exceptionally elongate, stick-like, and dark greenish-brown, living clinging tightly to submerged aquatic mosses, roots, and waterweeds in rapid currents. The antennae are extraordinarily long, with the first segment exceeding the rest of the head; the three caudal gills are long, triquetral, and rigid, functioning as anchors in fast-flowing water.

हिंदी: नर का पेट 45 से 50 मिमी लंबा और चमकीला हरा होता है। इसके अगले पंख पारदर्शी और पिछले पंख धातुई हरे होते हैं। मादा के पंखों पर सफेद रंग का धब्बा होता है। इसका निम्फ लकड़ी जैसा लंबा होता है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

The Stream Glory is an obligate rheophilic insect tied to fast-flowing, clear perennial freshwaters (Subramanian 2005). It inhabits forested streams, clear rivulets, and shaded rivers with gravel or rock bottoms and abundant submerged waterweeds (Fraser 1934). It avoids stagnant or silt-laden muddy water completely, requiring high dissolved oxygen levels.

Adults are active during warm sunny hours, flying in sunlit gaps along shaded stream corridors (Subramanian 2005). Males maintain linear feeding territories of five to eight metres over riffles and flowing pools, perching on overhanging leaves or protruding twigs (Fraser 1934). During courtship, the male performs a dramatic fluttering display, hovering directly in front of the perching female while displaying his brilliant green hindwings. Mating takes place on riparian foliage. Oviposition is endophytic: the female descends beneath the water surface, sometimes remaining submerged for up to fifteen minutes, to drill eggs into submerged aquatic plants while using air trapped around her body hairs for underwater respiration (Fraser 1934).

हिंदी: यह पतंग केवल शुद्ध और तेज बहने वाले पानी में रहती है। नर हवा में तैरते हुए अपने हरे पंखों को मादा के सामने चमकाता है। मादा पानी के अंदर पूरी तरह डूबकर जलीय पौधों में अंडे देती है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Females submerge themselves completely underwater to deposit clutches of elongate eggs into the submerged stems of riverweeds like Hydrilla verticillata (Hydrilla). The eggs hatch after a few days into slender, predatory nymphs. Naiads develop over several weeks through multiple instars, clinging to submerged plants in fast currents and preying upon stream invertebrates. At maturity, the nymph climbs an emergent boulder or riparian tree trunk at dawn to transform into an adult damselfly.

हिंदी: मादा पानी के अंदर जाकर पौधों में अंडे देती है। कुछ दिनों में अंडे फूटते हैं और पानी के बहाव में रहने वाला निम्फ कुछ ही हफ्तों में बड़ा होकर पत्थर पर चढ़कर सुंदर पतंग बन जाता है।

Host Plants or Prey आहार

Neurobasis chinensis is a specialist predator of lotic insect communities (Subramanian 2005):

Adult diet:

  • Small flying insects emerging over clear running streams, including mayflies (Ephemeroptera) and caddisflies (Trichoptera).
  • Riverbank mosquitoes, including Culex tritaeniorhynchus (Rice-field Mosquito) and Anopheles culicifacies (Malaria Mosquito).
  • Small riverine dipterans, including blackflies (Simuliidae) and midges (Chironomidae).

Larval diet:

  • Blackfly larvae, mayfly nymphs, caddisfly larvae, and small aquatic worms in running water.
हिंदी: यह पतंग बहते पानी के ऊपर उड़ने वाले छोटे कीड़ों, मच्छरों और नदी की मक्खियों का शिकार करती है। इसका निम्फ पानी में रहने वाले कीटों के लार्वा को खाता है।

Predators / Natural Enemies शत्रु

Natural predators documented in Gangetic plain riverine ecosystems:

  • Birds: Stream-foraging birds, such as blue whistling thrushes, common kingfishers, and wagtails, regularly capture flying adults.
  • Predatory dragonflies: Large riverine clubtail dragonflies (Gomphidae) pursue calopterygids along stream corridors.
  • Spiders: Riparian fishing spiders (Dolomedes) ambush adults resting on emergent rocks and driftwood.
  • Fish: Trout, mahseers, and stream carps feed voraciously on developing nymphs dislodged from submerged plants.
हिंदी: नदी किनारे रहने वाले पक्षी (जैसे किंगफिशर), बड़ी शिकारी पतंगें और मकड़ियाँ इस सुंदर पतंग को पकड़ती हैं। नदी की मछलियाँ इसके निम्फ को खाती हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Pristine freshwater bioindicator: The Stream Glory is universally recognized as one of the most sensitive biological indicators of clean, unpolluted, well-oxygenated flowing freshwater in South Asia (Subramanian 2005). Because its nymphs cannot survive in silt-laden, stagnant, or chemically polluted water, the presence of breeding populations provides proof of high water purity in rural watersheds.

Pest control in riparian zones: By consuming adult blackflies and river mosquitoes, adults help suppress biting insect populations along rural stream corridors (Subramanian 2005).

Traditional: No traditional medicinal, economic, or cultural uses are recorded.

Today: Celebrated by wildlife enthusiasts and eco-tourism initiatives as one of the most magnificent wetland insects in India.

Research direction (a lead, not a result): Investigating the structural coloration mechanics of the multi-layered photonic crystals on the dorsal hindwings.

हिंदी: यह पतंग पर्यावरण और पानी की शुद्धता का सबसे बड़ा पैमाना है। जहाँ का पानी पूरी तरह साफ, पारदर्शी और ऑक्सीजन से भरपूर होगा, यह केवल वहीं पाई जाती है।

The Stream Glory is expected across eastern Uttar Pradesh along shaded, clear perennial rivers and forest streams, particularly in terai tracts and forested corridors of the Kuwano and Rapti drainage basins. Look dancing low over clear riffles and submerged waterweeds in sunlit forest gaps from July through November. GBIF holds published records from the wider Awadh–Purvanchal plain, none yet from Basti–Ayodhya. A photograph showing the iridescent green body and metallic green hindwings, submitted through the About page, would confirm the first local documentation for the district.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Widespread across South and Southeast Asia, occurring from India and Nepal eastward through Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, Indochina, southern China, Malaysia, and Indonesia (Fraser 1934).

In India: Recorded throughout the Western Ghats, the lower Himalayas, the northeastern states, central India, and riverine corridors of the Gangetic plain (Prasad & Varshney 1995; Mitra 2002).

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded from terai forest streams, river valleys, and protected riparian sanctuaries across northern and eastern districts.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected as a resident breeding species in clear, perennial forest reaches and spring-fed tributaries of local rivers.

हिंदी: यह भारत और दक्षिण-पूर्व एशिया की स्वच्छ नदियों में पाई जाती है। उत्तर प्रदेश के तराई क्षेत्रों और साफ पानी वाली नदियों के किनारे इसका वास है।

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Described by Carl Linnaeus in 1758 as Libellula chinensis in Systema Naturae, 10th edition, Volume 1, page 545. Type locality: "China".

Subspecies. Multiple subspecies are recognized across Asia. The population inhabiting the Indian subcontinent is classified as the nominotypical subspecies Neurobasis chinensis chinensis (Linnaeus, 1758).

Family and order placement. Order Odonata, suborder Zygoptera, family Calopterygidae. The genus Neurobasis Selys, 1853, comprises broad-winged damselflies (demoiselles) with metallic wing pigmentation and non-petiolate wings.

Phylogenetic notes. Calopterygidae is an ancient zygopteran lineage characterized by complex courtship displays, wing pigmentation, and extreme rheophily, easily separated from Coenagrionidae by numerous antenodal crossveins (Subramanian 2005).

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated (NE) on the global IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. It is an abundant and secure species in clean freshwater rivers throughout South and Southeast Asia.

हिंदी: इसका वैज्ञानिक नाम Neurobasis chinensis है और यह Calopterygidae परिवार की सदस्य है। प्रकृति में इसका वास पूरी तरह सुरक्षित है।
