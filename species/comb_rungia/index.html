कॉम्ब रंगिया / Comb Rungia

Rungia pectinata (L.) Nees — Acanthaceae

कॉम्ब रंगिया — यह बगीचों, मेड़ों और झाड़ियों की छाँव में जमीन पर फैलने वाला एक छोटा और हरा-भरा पौधा है। इसके तने चार कोनों वाले होते हैं और जमीन पर जड़ें जमाते हुए बढ़ते हैं। इसके छोटे दो-होंठ वाले फूल नीले, बैंगनी या सफेद रंग के होते हैं, जो एक तरफा घनी कंघी जैसी बालियों में खिलते हैं।

Field tip: Look for dense, comb-like flower spikes where the leafy bracts line up like teeth, bearing tiny two-lipped pale blue flowers.

हिंदी: कंघी जैसी एकतरफा बालियों में लगे छोटे नीले या बैंगनी फूल और चार कोनों वाले तने इसकी पहचान हैं।

Morphology आकारिकी

Measurement not available in consulted sources.

A small, spreading annual or biennial herb of shady ground. Root system fibrous, rooting readily from the lower stem nodes in moist soil. Stems procumbent, decumbent or ascending, much-branched from the base, slender, distinctly four-angled, glabrous or minutely puberulous along the angles.

Leaves opposite, simple, almost sessile or on very short petioles; blade elliptic-lanceolate or oblong-elliptic, acute or subobtuse at the apex, tapering at the base, margins entire, lineolate on both surfaces with microscopic cystoliths, thin, dark green above, paler beneath.

Inflorescence a dense, one-sided (secund or pectinate) spike, borne terminal or in upper leaf axils on very short peduncles. Bracts dimorphic and arranged in four ranks: fertile bracts broadly elliptic or obovate, cuspidate, puberulous, with conspicuous, broad, scarious, whitish or hyaline ciliate margins; sterile bracts narrower, oblong-lanceolate, also with scarious margins. Calyx five-partite, segments linear-subulate with scarious edges.

Corolla small, bilabiate, pale blue, lavender, lilac or whitish, with darker purple blotches in the throat; upper lip emarginate, lower lip three-lobed. Stamens two; anther cells superposed, the lower cell bearing a small white tail-like appendage at its base.

Fruit an ovoid or oblong compressed capsule, pubescent, scarious on the flat faces, splitting elastically into two valves. Seeds two to four, orbicular, compressed, minutely echinulate, brown.

हिंदी: पौधा जमीन पर फैलकर बढ़ता है। पत्तियाँ आमने-सामने अंडाकार होती हैं। फूल छोटी बालियों में खिलते हैं जो एक तरफ से कंघी जैसी दिखती हैं। बीज छोटे और भूरे होते हैं।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

A native Gangetic plain herb that thrives in the dappled shade of mango groves, bamboo thickets, canal embankments, and village waste grounds. It requires moderate soil moisture and shelter from direct sun during the dry winter and spring months.

The dense comb-like spikes protect the developing flower buds behind overlapping scarious-margined bracts. Flowers open successively from the base of the spike towards the tip. Small butterflies and hoverflies visit the two-lipped flowers, utilizing the lower lip as a landing platform to probe for nectar.

हिंदी: यह पौधा आम के बगीचों और झाड़ियों की छायादार जमीन पर नमी में तेजी से बढ़ता है। इसके फूलों पर छोटी तितलियाँ और कीट मंडराते हैं।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Seedlings appear following the monsoon in October and November. Branching is extensive through November and December, forming low green mats.

Flowering begins in December and continues vigorously through February and March. Seed capsules ripen from January through April, releasing their tiny seeds by the elastic splitting of the capsule valves. As dry westerly winds intensify in April and May, the vegetative parts dry out, though plants in perpetually damp orchard soils may persist longer.

हिंदी: अक्टूबर-नवंबर में नए पौधे उगते हैं। दिसंबर से मार्च तक फूल और फल आते हैं। अप्रैल-मई की तेज धूप में पौधा सूख जाता है।

Associated Species / Interactions सहजीवी प्रजातियाँ

Serves as a nectar plant for small pierid butterflies, grass blues (Zizina and Pseudozizea), and small native halictid bees. Nymphalid butterflies also visit the flowers for nectar.

Often forms understorey communities alongside prostrate wild petunia, minnie root, and adusa.

हिंदी: छोटी नीली तितलियाँ और मधुमक्खियाँ इसके फूलों का रस पीती हैं। यह अक्सर अन्य देशी जड़ी-बूटियों के साथ उगता है।

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

Generally free from serious insect damage, though occasional leaf-chewing caterpillars feed on tender foliage. Powdery mildew can occur in deeply shaded, unventilated orchard understoreys during humid winter weather.

हिंदी: इस पौधे पर रोग कम लगते हैं, लेकिन बहुत घनी छाया में फफूंद के सफेद धब्बे दिख सकते हैं।

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

In the agro-ecosystems of eastern Uttar Pradesh, comb rungia acts as a harmless wild ground cover in orchards and agroforestry plots, protecting topsoil from desiccating winds and erosion without competing heavily with deep-rooted trees.

Chopra et al. (1956, p. 216, as Rungia parviflora) record that the bruised leaves are applied to contusions to relieve pain and reduce swelling. This is a record of published traditional use, not medical advice.

हिंदी: यह बगीचों में मिट्टी की नमी बनाए रखने वाला एक उपयोगी पौधा है। पारंपरिक चिकित्सा में इसकी पत्तियों का लेप मोच और सूजन में लगाया जाता रहा है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native across South Asia and Southeast Asia, including India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Myanmar, and China.

In India: Native and common throughout the tropical and subtropical plains, particularly in peninsular India and the Gangetic valley.

In Uttar Pradesh: Widely distributed in grove understoreys, river bluffs, and moist scrub borders across the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in shaded orchard floors and rural garden margins across all administrative blocks.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. First described as Justicia pectinata by Carl Linnaeus in 1756 in Centuria II. Plantarum, page 3. Transferred to Rungia by Christian Gottfried Daniel Nees von Esenbeck in 1847 in De Candolle's Prodromus, volume 11, page 470.

Subspecies. Often treated in older Indian works as Rungia parviflora Nees var. pectinata (L.) C.B.Clarke (Duthie 1911; Hooker 1885). Current consensus treats Rungia pectinata as a distinct species.

Family and order placement. Belongs to family Acanthaceae (subfamily Acanthoideae, tribe Justicieae), order Lamiales.

Phylogenetic notes. Characterized by superposed anther cells with white basal appendages and secundo-pectinate spikes.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated by the IUCN Red List. It is an abundant and ecologically secure native herb across South Asia.

हिंदी: यह अकेंथेसी कुल का पौधा है। लिनियस के 1756 के वर्णन को नीस ने 1847 में रंगिया जीनस में रखा था।
