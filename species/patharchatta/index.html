पत्थरचट्टा / Patharchatta

Bryophyllum pinnatum (Lam.) Oken — Crassulaceae

पत्थरचट्टा — सख्त पत्थर जैसी मोटी पत्तियों वाली झाड़ी, जिसको छूने पर हरा-भरा रहता है। किसी भी बंजर, सूखी जगह पर उग जाती है। पत्ती को तोड़ो तो नई पौध निकल आती है — "जीवन का पौधा" नाम इसीलिए है। दवा में भी लगता है।

Field tip: Look in dry, wastelands for a distinctive succulent with thick, glaucous leaves and tubular pink flowers in late winter. The leaf buds at the leaf margins produce miniature plantlets, and the plant is extremely easy to propagate.

हिंदी — कैसे पहचानें: मोटी, मांसल, पाउडर से ढकी पत्तियाँ। कड़ी और सूखी जगह में भी जीवित। पत्ती को तोड़ो तो कलम से नई पौध। गुलाबी फूल।

Stem and leaves: Thick, fleshy, erect, pale green. Leaves are pinnately compound or simple (variation exists), very thick and fleshy, with a white or glaucous (waxy) coating, giving them a pale appearance.

Flowers: Small, tubular, pale pink to greenish-yellow, fragrant, arranged in large, nodding terminal panicles.

Fruit: Four small, dry follicles (pods) per flower, opening to release many minute seeds.

Propagation: The leaf margins bear miniature plantlets (vegetative propagules) that develop adventitious roots and can be propagated directly into soil.

हिंदी: तना मांसल, हरा। पत्ती पंखनुमा या सरल, बहुत मांसल, पाउडर से ढकी। फूल गुलाबी-हरे, छोटे। पत्ती के किनारे पर छोटी पौध बनती है।

Behaviour & Ecology व्यवहार और पारिस्थितिकी

Patharchatta is a succulent from tropical South America that is now naturalized and widespread in tropical and subtropical Asia, including India. It grows on wastelands, roadsides, and disturbed ground, and is one of the most successful pioneer colonizers of degraded land. The plant tolerates extreme drought and poor soils. The flowers are visited by insects but the plant relies largely on vegetative reproduction via leaf-borne plantlets, making it extremely invasive in some regions.

The plant's ability to produce plantlets from leaf margins makes it nearly impossible to eradicate once established, and it can rapidly colonize large areas.

हिंदी: दक्षिणी अमेरिका का पौधा है, पर अब पूरे एशिया में फैल गया है। सूखी, बंजर जगह में जीवित रहता है। पत्ती से नई पौध बनती है, इसलिए जल्दी फैलता है।

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

The plant is essentially perennial through vegetative reproduction. Lifespan of individual plants is 2–5 years, but colonies persist indefinitely.

हिंदी: फरवरी-मार्च में फूल। पत्ती से कलम लगाने में कुछ हफ्तों में नई पौध।

Associated Species / Interactions संबंधित प्रजातियाँ

  • Pioneer species: Often colonizes with Lantana and other invasive weeds.
  • Pollinators: Small insects and bees visit the flowers.

Pests and Diseases कीट और रोग

  • Few pests because of the thick, fleshy, bitter leaves.
  • No significant fungal diseases.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Traditional and Ethnobotanical Use (documented uses; not medical advice):

  • Traditional medicine. Leaves are used in folk medicine for wounds, burns, and skin conditions. Listed in some Ayurvedic texts as a topical remedy.
  • Kitchen garden plant. Grown in pots and kitchen gardens for easy availability.
  • Propagation. The ease of propagation makes it one of the most easily shared houseplants.

Today. Patharchatta is grown as a houseplant and medicinal herb. It is also recognized as an invasive species in some regions and is sometimes removed from natural areas.

Research direction (a lead, not a result). The chemical composition of the leaves and the mechanisms of vegetative reproduction via plantlet formation would merit study.

हिंदी: पत्ती दवा में लगती है — जख़्म, जलना, त्वचा। घर के गमले में लगाई जाती है। आसानी से लग जाता है।

Distribution वितरण

Global range: Native to tropical Americas; naturalized throughout tropical and subtropical Asia, Africa, and Pacific islands.

In India: Widespread in cultivated settings and naturalized in disturbed habitats.

In Uttar Pradesh: Common in kitchen gardens and wastelands.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in cultivated and disturbed settings.

Field rule: A succulent with thick, glaucous, fleshy pinnate leaves and miniature plantlets at the leaf margins, in a wasteland or garden, is patharchatta.

Taxonomy & Classification वर्गीकरण

Original description. Originally described as Cotyledon pinnata by Lamarck in 1783. Transferred to Bryophyllum by Salisbury in 1806 and to Kalanchoe by subsequent authors. Currently classified as Bryophyllum pinnatum. The epithet pinnatum refers to the pinnate leaf structure.

Subspecies. Monotypic — no subspecies are currently recognised, though cultivars exist.

Family and order placement. Crassulaceae, the stonecrop family, in the order Saxifragales. The genus Bryophyllum is characterized by the production of plantlets on the leaf margins.

Phylogenetic notes. Bryophyllum pinnatum is a well-studied succulent in the Crassulaceae and is notable for its CAM (Crassulacean Acid Metabolism) photosynthesis, an adaptation to arid environments.

IUCN status rationale. Not evaluated. Naturalized and widespread; locally invasive in some regions.
