टू-स्ट्राइप्ड जंपर मकड़ी / Two-striped Jumper

Telamonia dimidiata (Simon, 1899) — Salticidae

टू-स्ट्राइप्ड जंपर मकड़ी — खेतों के किनारे की लंबी घास और झाड़ियों पर एक लाल-सफ़ेद या भूरे-सफ़ेद धारीदार कूदने वाली मकड़ी। अपने बड़े सामने की आँखें और धारीदार पैटर्न से आसानी से पहचान में आती है। दीवारों की तुलना में घास और पत्तियों पर अधिक आम।

Quick Identification त्वरित पहचान

FeatureDescription
Size7–10 mm body length; legspan 15–20 mm
PatternRed or brown with distinct white or pale longitudinal stripes
LegsBanded; striped pattern continues onto legs
EyesTwo large forward-facing principal eyes; turns to face observer
HabitatVegetation foliage and tall grass rather than walls
BehaviourActive daytime hunter; quick movements

Field tip: Red or brown base colour with white longitudinal stripes (not transverse bands like other Argiope). Found on vegetation, not walls. Distinguished from Plexippus paykulli by its larger size and striped pattern.

Morphology आकारिकी

FeatureMeasurement
Body length7–10 mm
Legspan15–20 mm

Cephalothorax: Dark brown to reddish, with pale longitudinal stripes on sides.

Abdomen: Oval, red to brown base colour with prominent longitudinal white or pale stripes.

Legs: Striped to match body — dark and pale bands; robustly built.

Eyes: Large principal eyes; typical Salticidae vision system with eight total eyes.

Ecological Role पारिस्थितिक भूमिका

Visual predator on vegetation: Active daylight hunter using vision-based prey location. Stalks and pounces on small arthropods.

Prey: Small flies, ants, small beetles, mites, small spiders.

Habitat generalist: Hunts in tall grass, on shrubs and low tree branches — more generalist than wall-dwelling jumping spiders.

Life Cycle / Phenology जीवन चक्र

Single annual generation.

Agricultural / Human Relevance कृषि प्रासंगिकता

Beneficial predator: Natural insect control in agricultural fields and gardens. Preys on small pests.

No human risk: Harmless; does not bite unless severely provoked.

Expected Locally स्थानीय रूप से अपेक्षित

Not a field record. Written from published accounts for eastern Uttar Pradesh. No confirmed local sighting yet — if you have seen it, that is what the atlas needs.
अभी तक स्थानीय पुष्टि नहीं। यह विवरण प्रकाशित स्रोतों से है, किसी अवलोकन से नहीं।

Telamonia dimidiata is expected on vegetation foliage and in tall grass in field margins and gardens March through November. More active in warm seasons.

Distribution वितरण

Global range: South and Southeast Asia — India, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Malaysia.

In India: Widespread in suitable vegetation.

In Uttar Pradesh: Recorded across the state.

In Basti district / eastern UP: Expected in grassland and field-margin habitats.
