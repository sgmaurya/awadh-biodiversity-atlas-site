About the atlas इस एटलस के बारे में

A bilingual natural history of rural eastern Uttar Pradesh, centred on the Basti and Ayodhya districts. 362 species so far, each written in Hindi and English, for a villager, a school student, a farmer and a researcher at once.

Why weeds and pests first

Because everyone recognises them, almost everyone destroys them, and almost nobody understands them. A weed is not just a weed and a pest is not just a pest. Every profile explains what the organism actually does — pollinator, decomposer, seed disperser, predator, pioneer — instead of sorting life into good and bad.

What it is for यह किसलिए है

To know what grows and lives here well enough to live from it without using it up. Munja grass was rope, mats and baskets in every home a generation ago; neem and reetha washed clothes and skin; rice husk is nearly a third silica, and researchers are turning it into building material; lantana, the invader, is being made into furniture; water hyacinth, the pond-choker, into paper and biogas. Some of this is memory being lost, some is a living craft, some is one study away from a village enterprise.

इस एटलस का उद्देश्य है कि हम अपने आसपास के पौधों और जीवों को इतना जानें कि उनसे टिकाऊ ढंग से जीविका चला सकें — जैसे मूँज की रस्सी और टोकरी, नीम और रीठे का साबुन, धान की भूसी से बनने वाली चीज़ें। जो हुनर खो रहे हैं उन्हें दर्ज करना, और जो नए रास्ते विज्ञान दिखा रहा है उन्हें गाँव तक पहुँचाना।

So each profile records the traditional uses that are documented, the uses still practised, and the research directions that exist — labelled as exactly that, with their sources. Nothing here is medical advice, and a species that is toxic or protected by law says so before any use is described. The atlas is a starting point for makers, self-help groups and students, not a set of instructions to follow blindly.

What this is, and what it is not यह क्या है

It is a checklist with accounts: what published sources say should live in this landscape, written up carefully in two languages. It is not a record of observations. No species here has yet been confirmed in the field by the project, and every page says so. Turning "expected" into "confirmed" is the work, and anyone can help.

How to contribute a sighting आपने देखा हो तो

You do not need to be a scientist. A photograph, a place and a date is a real scientific record. Note what you saw, where, and when; photograph it if you can, even badly.

आपने कोई पौधा, कीट, पक्षी या मेंढक देखा हो तो उसकी फोटो, जगह और तारीख़ लिख लीजिए। यही असली वैज्ञानिक जानकारी है।

A simple way to send sightings in is being set up. Until then, write to the project through the issue tracker.

Photographs

Most photographs here are borrowed from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, each credited with its author and licence on the species page. They are placeholders. As contributors photograph a species in this landscape, the local photograph replaces the borrowed one.

Licence and source

Text and data are CC BY 4.0: copy, print, translate and build on them, with credit. Photographs keep their own licences. The scripts are MIT. Everything is generated from the atlas vault, where the full history lives.

Privacy

The site counts page visits with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and keeps no personal data; it tells us which species and months people look at, nothing about who they are. There are no accounts, no trackers and no advertising.
