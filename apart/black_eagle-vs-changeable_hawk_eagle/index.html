How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Changeable Hawk-Eagle, as the Black Eagle profile puts it

Changeable Hawk-Eagle]] (Nisaetus cirrhatus)

Slender erect crest (in northern birds), feathered tarsus to toes, white or pale underparts heavily streaked with dark brown (Baker 1928, p. 85)

Black Eagle: The almost uniform dark blackish-brown plumage, bright yellow cere and feet, long paddle-shaped broad wings with slotted primaries, and long tail distinguish the Black Eagle in soaring flight (Baker 1928, p. 83).

Changeable Hawk-Eagle: Woodland eagle with upright posture, long slender black crest, streaked breast, and densely feathered tarsi down to toes.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Black EagleChangeable Hawk-Eagle
HabitLarge forest eagle frequenting heavy evergreen forest and dense canopy—
PlumageEntire plumage dark blackish-brown, paler below where pale feather bases show through (Baker 1928, p. 83)—
Head & EyeSmall white patch under eye; lores dull white with black bristles (Baker 1928, p. 83)—
Tail PatternBlackish tail faintly barred with grey; long and slightly rounded (Baker 1928, p. 83)—
WingsExceptionally broad, paddle-shaped wings held in a pronounced V when soaring—
Soft PartsIris dark brown; cere and gape bright yellow; feet yellow, claws black (Baker 1928, p. 83)—
Crest structure—Prominent, slender erectile black crest projecting from rear of crown
Polymorphism—Highly dimorphic plumage with common pale morph and rare uniform dark morph
Pale morph pattern—Dark brown above; white breast boldly streaked with black; barred belly and thighs
Dark morph pattern—Uniform dark chocolate-brown or sooty-black plumage with paler tail bands
Tarsal feathering—Slender, fully feathered tarsi extending completely to base of strong yellow toes
