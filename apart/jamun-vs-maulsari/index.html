Jamun or Maulsari? जामुन या मौलसरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जामुनJamunSyzygium cumini
मौलसरीMaulsariMimusops elengi

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jamun, as the Maulsari profile puts it

Larger leaves, larger purple fruit, different family (Myrtaceae vs. Sapotaceae)

जामुन — बड़ी पत्तियाँ, बड़ा बैंगनी फल

Jamun: Large evergreen tree with opposite, smooth, oblong leaves smelling of turpentine when crushed, greenish-white flowers, and purple-black ovoid berries in June–July identify the Jamun tree.

Maulsari: A large evergreen tree with dark glossy oblong leaves, intensely fragrant small white-yellow flowers in spring, and small sweet brown fruit in summer is maulsari; nothing else in UP is so fragrant.
