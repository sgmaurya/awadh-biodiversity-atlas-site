How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tropical Tiger Moth, as the Cajeta Fruit-piercing Moth profile puts it

Forewing brown with a small white central spot; wings held roof-like, diurnal/crepuscular

अगले पंख पर सफेद बिंदु, दिन और गोधूलि में सक्रिय

Cajeta Fruit-piercing Moth: Large leaf-like forewings combined with yellow hindwings having a broad black margin and lacking a central round black dot distinguish Eudocima cajeta.

Tropical Tiger Moth: A small moth with striking black forewing with white spots and bright red or orange hindwings, combined with a brightly coloured hairy caterpillar (black with yellow/orange bands) on papaya or milkweed, identifies the Tropical Tiger Moth.
