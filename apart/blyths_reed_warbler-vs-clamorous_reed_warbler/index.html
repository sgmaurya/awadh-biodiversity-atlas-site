How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blyth's Reed Warbler, as the Clamorous Reed Warbler profile puts it

Blyth's Reed Warbler]] (Acrocephalus dumetorum)

Smaller, more slender; less loud call

Blyth's Reed Warbler: A small brown warbler in reed beds with a distinctive "jir-jir-jir" flight call — is Blyth's Reed Warbler. The distinctive flight call is the best identification feature. Song (in breeding season or early spring) is also characteristic.

Clamorous Reed Warbler: The Clamorous Reed Warbler is a large, robust, brown warbler with an extremely loud, harsh, raucous call from reed beds. No other warbler in the region is this large and loud. If it is smaller and softer-voiced, it is likely Blyth's Reed Warbler.
