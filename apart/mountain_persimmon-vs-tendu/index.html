Mountain Persimmon or Tendu? माउंटेन पर्सिमन या तेंदू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tendu, as the Mountain Persimmon profile puts it

Diospyros melanoxylon]]

Unarmed mature tree; leaves larger and thicker with prominent reticulation; fruit edible and sweet

Mountain Persimmon: Spiny trunk and branches, thinner leaves, and globose bitter persimmons on an enlarged four-lobed calyx separate Diospyros montana from the unarmed, larger-leaved Diospyros melanoxylon.

Tendu: A deciduous tree with smooth, glossy, entire, leathery leaves and sticky black sweet fruit in summer, is tendu.
