Northern Lapwing or White-tailed Lapwing? नॉर्दर्न लैपविंग या व्हाइट-टेल्ड लैपविंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-tailed Lapwing, as the Northern Lapwing profile puts it

White-tailed Lapwing]] (Vanellus leucurus)

Entirely white tail without black subapical band, uniform brownish-grey plumage, long yellow legs, no crest (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Northern Lapwing, as the White-tailed Lapwing profile puts it

Much larger; black and white; prominent crest; black underparts

उत्तरी टिटहरी

Northern Lapwing: The long slender recurved black crest, glossy dark green upperparts, cinnamon upper and under tail-coverts, and broad black breast patch distinguish the Northern Lapwing from all other Indian plovers (Baker 1929, p. 180).

White-tailed Lapwing: A small pale sand-brown lapwing with distinctive white tail (visible perched and in flight), yellow legs, and loud whistling call is a White-tailed Lapwing. The white tail is diagnostic.
