Common Hawk-Cuckoo or Pied Cuckoo? पपीहा या चातक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पपीहाCommon Hawk-CuckooHierococcyx varius
चातकPied CuckooClamator jacobinus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Cuckoo, as the Common Hawk-Cuckoo profile puts it

Larger; striking black and white plumage; white wing patch; larger bill

चातक — बड़ा, काली-सफ़ेद

Common Hawk-Cuckoo: Medium grey and white cuckoo + loud two-syllable "piu-piu" or "brain-fever" call rising in pitch + spring arrival + fruit-eating = Common Hawk-Cuckoo. The call is unmistakable and diagnostic.

Pied Cuckoo: Large black-and-white bird + loud "piu-piu-piu" wailing call + arrives with monsoon + fruit-eating = Pied Cuckoo. The call is diagnostic and unforgettable.
