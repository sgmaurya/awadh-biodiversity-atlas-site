Sugarcane or Wheat? गन्ना या गेहूं?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गन्नाSugarcaneSaccharum officinarum
गेहूंWheatTriticum aestivum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sugarcane, as the Wheat profile puts it

Semi-perennial giant grass; stems are thick (2–4 cm diameter) and solid, containing sweet juice; grows up to 3–4 m.

गन्ना — विशाल ठोस तना, बारहमासी फसल

Sugarcane: Tall, solid, jointed stalks filled with sweet juice, growing in cultivated rows, with long narrow leaves showing a prominent white midrib, identify Saccharum officinarum.

Side by side आमने-सामने

SugarcaneWheat
StemsTall, stout, unbranched fibrous culms (2–6 m high, 2–5 cm thick); divided into distinct ringed nodes and sweet, juicy internodes; colors range from green and yellow to deep purple—
LeavesAlternate, narrow, blade-like leaves (1–2 m long, 3–6 cm wide) with a prominent white midrib down the center and finely toothed, sharp marginsSimple, flat, linear, grass-like (15–30 cm long, 1–2 cm wide), smooth, with a distinct ligule and auricles at the junction of the sheath and blade
FlowerLarge, silver-grey, feathery terminal panicle (called the "arrow"), 30–60 cm long, rising at the top of the stem in winter—
RootsDense fibrous adventitious roots at the base, with sturdy stilt roots emerging from the lower nodes to anchor the tall stem—
CultivationGrown in rows in plowed, irrigated agricultural fields; never wild—
Form—Tufted annual grass (60–120 cm tall) producing multiple upright, hollow stems (tillers) from the base
Inflorescence—Compact, terminal spike (5–12 cm long) called the "ear" or "head," carrying multiple spikelets arranged alternately on a zig-zag axis
Glumes & Awns—Spikelets are protected by tough outer scales (glumes); many regional varieties carry long, stiff, needle-like bristles called "awns" projecting from the spike
Fruit—Oval or oblong dry grain (caryopsis, 5–8 mm long) with a longitudinal groove down one side and a tuft of tiny hairs at the tip

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
