Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sweet Clover, as the Fenugreek profile puts it

Sweet Clover]] (Melilotus officinalis)

Much taller; flowers are small, yellow, arranged in long upright spikes; lacks bitter smell

Fenugreek: Erect winter annual herb with three-leaflet clover-like leaves, strong maple-curry scent from all parts, small white-yellow flowers in leaf axils, and long narrow pointed pods, is Fenugreek (Methi).

Sweet Clover: A winter annual legume with bright yellow flower spikes, three-leaflet leaves, and a sweet hay-like fragrance is senji.

The test—Crush leaves; strong fragrance of coumarin (sweet, hay-like smell)
