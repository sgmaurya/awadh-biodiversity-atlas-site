Black-tailed Godwit or Eurasian Curlew? ब्लैक-टेल्ड गॉडविट या यूरेशियन कर्ल्यू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Eurasian Curlew, as the Black-tailed Godwit profile puts it

Eurasian Curlew]] (Numenius arquata)

Much larger (50–66 cm); longer, more curved bill; different plumage

Black-tailed Godwit, as the Eurasian Curlew profile puts it

Black-tailed Godwit]] (Limosa limosa)

Longer legs; straighter, shorter bill; different structure

Black-tailed Godwit: A long-legged, long-billed wader with white rump and wholly black tail with white base is Black-tailed Godwit. The white rump and black tail are diagnostic in flight.

Eurasian Curlew: Eurasian Curlew is distinguished by its very long, distinctly decurved bill (longer than head and neck combined) and mottled brown plumage. The large size and distinctive "cur-LEW" call are diagnostic. No crown stripe unlike Whimbrel.
