How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blyth's Reed Warbler, as the Moustached Warbler profile puts it

Blyth's Reed Warbler]] (Acrocephalus dumetorum)

Lacks bold black facial marks on male; different call; usually in same habitat

Blyth's Reed Warbler: A small brown warbler in reed beds with a distinctive "jir-jir-jir" flight call — is Blyth's Reed Warbler. The distinctive flight call is the best identification feature. Song (in breeding season or early spring) is also characteristic.

Moustached Warbler: A small brown warbler in dense reed beds with males showing distinctive black "moustache" marks (loral and eye-stripe) is Moustached Warbler. The crackling "tzik-tzik" flight call and bold facial marks on males are the best identification features.
