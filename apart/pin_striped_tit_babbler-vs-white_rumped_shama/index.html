How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-rumped Shama, as the Pin-striped Tit-babbler profile puts it

White-rumped Shama]] (Copsychus malabaricus)

Much larger with long graduated tail, deep black and rufous plumage; lacking yellow underparts

Pin-striped Tit-babbler: The diminutive size, rufous crown, and bright pale-yellow breast adorned with delicate black pin-stripes distinguish the Pin-striped Tit-babbler from all other undergrowth species.

White-rumped Shama: The combination of an intensely deep chestnut-rufous belly, prominent white rump patch, and extremely long graduated tail distinguishes the White-rumped Shama from all other thrushes and robins.
