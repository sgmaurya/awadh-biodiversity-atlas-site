Common Krait or Common Trinket Snake? करैत या ट्रिंकेट साँप?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ट्रिंकेट साँपCommon Trinket SnakeCoelognathus helena

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Krait, as the Common Trinket Snake profile puts it

Highly venomous; has large hexagonal scales along spine; head not distinct from neck; nocturnal

करैत

Common Krait: Highly polished steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody, combined with enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales along the backbone, identifies Bungarus caeruleus.

Common Trinket Snake: A slender, diurnal, highly variable-coloured snake moving rapidly through grass or branches, with smooth scales throughout, is a Common Trinket Snake. It is harmless and feeds on other reptiles.

Length—90–150 cm total length; moderate to large for its group
Coloration—Highly variable: uniform yellowish-brown, striped, or banded; dark forms common
