Brown-headed Gull or Pallas's Gull? ब्राउन-हेडेड गल या पलास गल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्राउन-हेडेड गलBrown-headed GullChroicocephalus brunnicephalus
पलास गलPallas's GullIchthyaetus ichthyaetus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pallas's Gull, as the Brown-headed Gull profile puts it

Much larger (58–68 cm); massive bill; white head year-round

बड़ी गुलाब

Brown-headed Gull: A medium-sized white gull with reddish bill and legs, wintering on large water bodies in winter flocks. Brown or dusky head patch on white head is diagnostic for non-breeding Brown-headed Gull.

Pallas's Gull: A large white gull with black cap on head, dark slate-grey back, and powerful pale bill is Pallas's Gull. The size and black cap are diagnostic compared to other gulls.
