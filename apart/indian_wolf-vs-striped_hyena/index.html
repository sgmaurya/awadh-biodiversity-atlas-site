How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Hyena, as the Indian Wolf profile puts it

Striped Hyena]] (Crocuta crocuta)

Stockier build; vertical mane along back; rounded ears; different skull; powerful jaws for bone-cracking

Indian Wolf: Large canid (2–3 times heavier than a medium dog), grey-brown or buff coat, large head, yellow eyes, bushy dark-tipped tail, nocturnal solitary or paired movement in open grassland, with large tracks (~6–7 cm) in a near-straight line = Indian Wolf.

Striped Hyena: A large, stocky, powerfully-built carnivore with distinctive vertical black stripes on a tawny body, short tail, and a humped back, found in ravines and scrub, is a Striped Hyena and should be approached only at a distance with extreme caution.
