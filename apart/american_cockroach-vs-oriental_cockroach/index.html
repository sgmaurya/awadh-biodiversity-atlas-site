American Cockroach or Oriental Cockroach? अमेरिकी तिलचट्टा या पूर्वी तिलचट्टा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अमेरिकी तिलचट्टाAmerican CockroachPeriplaneta americana
पूर्वी तिलचट्टाOriental CockroachBlatta orientalis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Cockroach, as the American Cockroach profile puts it

Smaller (20–27 mm); dark brown to black body; female has vestigial wing stubs and cannot fly; prefers cooler areas

पूर्वी तिलचट्टा — छोटा आकार, काला रंग, पंखों का अभाव

American Cockroach, as the Oriental Cockroach profile puts it

Much larger (35–40 mm); reddish-brown with a yellow border on the pronotum; wings cover the entire tail; flies

अमेरिकी तिलचट्टा — बड़ा आकार, लाल-भूरा रंग, पंख बड़े

American Cockroach: Very large reddish-brown cockroach (35–40 mm) with a yellowish posterior margin on the pronotum and wings that cover the tail, running with extreme speed at night, is the American Cockroach.

Oriental Cockroach: Medium-sized dark brown or black cockroach (20–27 mm) with very short, non-functional wing stubs (female) or short wings (male), unable to fly, living in damp, cool drains is the Oriental Cockroach.
