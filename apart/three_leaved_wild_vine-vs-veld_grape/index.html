How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Veld Grape, as the Three-leaved Wild Vine profile puts it

Cissus quadrangularis]]

Stout, quadrangular, winged, fleshy green cactus-like jointed stems

Three-leaved Wild Vine: Trifoliolate leaves, herbaceous climbing stems with branched wiry tendrils, and small shiny black berries separate Causonis trifolia from succulent and cordate climbers.

Veld Grape: A climbing vine with distinctive four-angled or quadrangular stems and trifoliate (three-leafed) leaves is Cissus quadrangularis.
