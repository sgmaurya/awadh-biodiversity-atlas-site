How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gangetic Mystus, as the Rita Catfish profile puts it

Smaller body; much longer maxillary barbels extending beyond the caudal fin; lack of light lateral bands

पिरवा / टेंगरा — लम्बी मूंछें, छोटा शरीर

Gangetic Mystus: A small catfish with three pairs of barbels and serrated pectoral spines is a Gangetic Mystus. The spine causes pain if touched.

Rita Catfish: Large head, heavy scale-less body, three pairs of barbels (with short nasal barbels), a deeply forked tail, and pale lateral bands distinguish the Rita Catfish.
