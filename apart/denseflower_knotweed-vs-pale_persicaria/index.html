Denseflower Knotweed or Pale Persicaria? डेंसफ्लावर नॉटवीड या पेल पर्सिकेरिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pale Persicaria, as the Denseflower Knotweed profile puts it

Stems not uniformly red; flower spikes nodding and arching at tips; perianth veins anchor-branched; leaves often have dark chevron mark

पेल पर्सिकेरिया

Denseflower Knotweed, as the Pale Persicaria profile puts it

Stems completely glabrous, often tinged deep red; leaves longer, tapering; ochrea completely smooth without cilia; erect spikes

डेंसफ्लावर नॉटवीड

Denseflower Knotweed: A tall, completely hairless wetland knotweed with smooth reddish stems, eciliate ochrea sheaths, and strictly erect dense flower spikes is Persicaria glabra.

Pale Persicaria: A robust wetland knotweed with nodding pale greenish-pink flower spikes, swollen nodes, and anchor-branched perianth veins is Persicaria lapathifolia.
