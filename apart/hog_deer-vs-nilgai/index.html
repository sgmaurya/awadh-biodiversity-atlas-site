Hog Deer or Nilgai? पाड़ा या नीलगाय?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीलगायNilgaiBoselaphus tragocamelus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Nilgai, as the Hog Deer profile puts it

Boselaphus tragocamelus

Massive antelope standing over 130 cm at the shoulder, with horse-like neck, greyish or blue-grey coat, and short conical horns in males (Menon 2014)

Hog Deer: Small squat deer with short legs, dark reddish-brown coat, three-tined antlers in males, and a characteristic low-head ducking run through tall river grass.

Nilgai: Large size (horse-like), sloping back, white throat bib, and white leg rings; blue-grey coat with short horns in males, and sandy-brown coat without horns in females, identify the Nilgai.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Hog DeerNilgai
Body sizeSmall, squat, robust deer—
ColorationDark olive-brown or reddish-brown coat with pale underparts, often developing faint pale spots in summer—
Antlers (males)Slender three-tined antlers mounted on prominent hair-covered bony pedicels (Prater 1971)—
Running gaitRuns with the head held low, ducking under grass stems rather than leaping high like other deer—
TailModerate, bushy tail, brown above and pure white underneath, flashed as an alarm signal—
HabitatObligate dweller of wet alluvial grasslands, riverine reed beds, and floodplains—
Size—Large, horse-like antelope; head-body length 180–210 cm; weight 120–240 kg
Male Colour—Dark blue-grey coat; sloping back with high shoulders; short black horns
Female Colour—Sandy-brown to tawny-brown coat; smaller than the male; completely hornless
Throat Mark—A prominent, bright white patch (white bib) on the middle of the throat
Throat Beard—Adult males feature a distinctive, hanging tuft of stiff black hair on the throat
Leg Markings—White rings on the fetlocks and two white spots on each cheek

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
