How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-headed Bunting, as the White-capped Bunting profile puts it

Red-headed Bunting]] (Emberiza bruniceps)

Bright golden-yellow underparts, yellowish-green mantle, and chestnut-red head in breeding males without black throat

Red-headed Bunting: The male Red-headed Bunting's fiery coppery-red head and breast contrasting with a clear yellow belly, and uncrested profile, distinguish it from the crested bunting and rosefinch.

White-capped Bunting: The combination of grey crown, black mask and throat, white breast, rufous lower breast, and chestnut mantle separates the male White-capped Bunting (Baker 1926).
