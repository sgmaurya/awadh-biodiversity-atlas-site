Mango or Putranjiva? आम या पुत्रंजीवा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

आमMangoMangifera indica
पुत्रंजीवाPutranjivaPutranjiva roxburghii

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mango, as the Putranjiva profile puts it

Leaves narrower, drooping; flowers large and conspicuous; large fruit

पत्तियाँ झूलती हुई; बड़े फूल; बड़ा फल

Mango: Long narrow copper-red new leaves + mango smell when scratched + large fleshy fruit with one flat stone = mango. No other UP tree has this combination.

Putranjiva: A tall evergreen tree with simple long glossy lanceolate leaves, tiny greenish flowers in spring, and small round dark fruits, planted deliberately in temples and sacred spaces, is putranjiva.
