छह बिंदियाँ

Seven-spot Ladybird, as the Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird profile puts it

Has seven distinct, round black spots (not zigzag bands); slightly larger

सात-बिंदु लेडीबर्ड — सात गोल दाग़े, बड़ा

Seven-spot Ladybird: Bright red or orange-red beetle with exactly seven black spots (3 + 3 + 1), black pronotum with pale markings; both larva and adult consuming aphids actively on crop leaves: Seven-spot Ladybird (Coccinella septempunctata).

Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird: A small, dome-shaped orange-red beetle with six black spots arranged in distinctive zigzag lines (not round spots) and small white corner spots on the thorax identifies the Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird.
