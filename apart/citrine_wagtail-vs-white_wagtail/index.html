How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Wagtail, as the Citrine Wagtail profile puts it

Black and white pattern; no yellow

खंजन

Citrine Wagtail: A wagtail with a yellow or citrine-yellow head in open grasslands — is a Citrine Wagtail. The yellow head, grey back, and tail-wagging behaviour are diagnostic. Distinguished from Yellow Wagtail by larger size, longer tail, and citrine-yellow (not bright yellow) head colour in males.
