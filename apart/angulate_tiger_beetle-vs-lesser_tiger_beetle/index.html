Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Angulate Tiger Beetle, as the Lesser Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Considerably larger; bolder, thicker angular white bands

काफी बड़ा आकार (12-15 मिमी), मोटी कोणीय धारियां

Angulate Tiger Beetle: A medium-sized bronze tiger beetle on wet alluvial mud with distinct ivory-white angular bands and a sharply bent middle band identifies Cicindela angulata.

Lesser Tiger Beetle: A diminutive bronze tiger beetle running rapidly over wet mud with delicate narrow white lunules identifies Cylindera minuta.
