बंगाल गिद्ध

Indian Vulture: The pale sandy-buff body, stout yellowish-horny bill with dull dirty greenish cere and sparsely downy head distinguish the Indian Vulture from the Slender-billed Vulture. Its rump is creamy-white too, but "sometimes hardly visible" (Baker 1928, p. 16), so a white rump alone does not make a White-rumped Vulture.

White-rumped Vulture: White-rumped Vulture is the largest dark brown vulture with a sharply contrasting white rump patch. The contrast between dark body and white rump is the most diagnostic feature. No other large vulture in the region shows this pattern.
