How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Emigrant, as the African Emigrant profile puts it

Common Emigrant]] (Catopsilia pomona)

Slightly larger; white underside; less yellow overall

African Emigrant: A medium to large bright yellow butterfly with strong fast flight, flying high over open grassland, is the African Emigrant.

Common Emigrant: Medium size, rapid straight flight, uniform lemon-yellow or greenish-white wings, and presence of pink spots (in pomona form) or clear wings (in crocale form) on underwings identify the Common Emigrant.
