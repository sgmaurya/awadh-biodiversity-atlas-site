How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddy Grasshopper, as the Spathosternum Grasshopper profile puts it

Oxya fuscovittata

Larger, robust green grasshopper with dark lateral band, but lacking spathulate prosternal tubercle

Paddy Grasshopper: Green 20–35 mm grasshopper with brown lateral stripes + in paddy field + August–October = Oxya fuscovittata with high confidence. The only common green grasshopper of paddy fields in eastern UP.

Spathosternum Grasshopper: Small, slender, green or brownish grasshopper with a conspicuous dark brown lateral stripe running from behind each eye across the pronotum.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Paddy GrasshopperSpathosternum Grasshopper
SizeMedium-sized grasshopper; males 20–28 mm, females significantly larger at 28–35 mm—
ColourGreen body with distinctive dark brown lateral stripes running from the eyes to the pronotum margins—
WingsGreenish-brown forewings covering the abdomen, with translucent yellow-green hindwings visible in flight—
HindlegsLong and powerful yellow-green legs with comb-like spines on the tibia, adapted for strong leaping—
HabitatClosely associated with paddy fields, sugarcane fields, and adjacent tall grassy bunds—
Body size & color—Small, slender grasshopper, predominantly bright grass-green or yellowish-green, occasionally brownish
Lateral stripe—Conspicuous dark brown to blackish longitudinal band running from behind each eye across the pronotum
Tegmina—Slender, semi-transparent tegmina extending slightly beyond the apex of the hind femora
Hind legs—Pale green jumping legs with a pale yellowish-green tibia armed with fine spines
Habitat niche—Found in low grassy vegetation, jumping actively when walked through rather than taking long flights

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
