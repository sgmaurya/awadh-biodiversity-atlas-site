Paddyfield Pipit or Tree Pipit? रुगेल या ट्री पिपिट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tree Pipit, as the Paddyfield Pipit profile puts it

Less rufous; different facial pattern; prefers trees and forest edges

पेड़ की पिपिलिका — जंगल में

Paddyfield Pipit: Small, slender pipit + rufous-brown plumage + ground-walking behaviour + frequent tail flicks + thin "pip" call + rice paddies and grasslands = Paddyfield Pipit. The long hind claw and habitat preference are key identification features.

Tree Pipit: The Tree Pipit is a slender pipit with fine streaking, often seen perching on trees and launching to the ground. Its habit of returning from ground to tree is distinctive. If the bird stays on ground and appears darker and less buffy, it is likely Olive-backed Pipit.
