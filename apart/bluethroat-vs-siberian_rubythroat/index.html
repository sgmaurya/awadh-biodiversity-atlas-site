How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bluethroat, as the Siberian Rubythroat profile puts it

Bluethroat]] (Luscinia svecica)

Bright orange-rufous basal patches on tail feathers; male has vivid blue and rufous breast gorget; bold white supercilium

Bluethroat: A small brown songbird in reed beds with a blue throat (male) or blue-speckled white throat (female), and orange-buff tail bases — is a Bluethroat. The throat pattern and orange tail bases are diagnostic.

Siberian Rubythroat: The brilliant ruby-scarlet throat, double white facial stripes (eyebrow and cheek), uniform olive-brown upperparts, and plain brown unpatterned tail distinguish the male Siberian Rubythroat.
