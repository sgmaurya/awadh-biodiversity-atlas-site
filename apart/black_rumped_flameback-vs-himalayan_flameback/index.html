How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-rumped Flameback, as the Himalayan Flameback profile puts it

Dinopium benghalense

Single black line down white throat, black nape and mantle without brown wash on throat

Black-rumped Flameback: Small golden-yellow woodpecker with black rump + rapid drumming + sharp "kriek" call = Black-rumped Flameback. The black rump is diagnostic.

Himalayan Flameback: Golden-backed woodpecker with three toes, double black line enclosing a pale brownish throat streak, and white-streaked crown in female.
