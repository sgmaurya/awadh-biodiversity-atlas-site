How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson Marsh Glider, as the Fulvous Forest Skimmer profile puts it

Crimson Marsh Glider]]

Pinkish-crimson body; wings clear except for minor amber patch at the base

Crimson Marsh Glider: Medium size, pinkish-crimson body with purple venation at the wing bases, and frequent perching behavior identify the Crimson Marsh Glider.

Fulvous Forest Skimmer: Medium size, dark rust-brown wings with transparent black tips, and a preference for perching in shaded orchards and pond edges identify the Fulvous Forest Skimmer.
