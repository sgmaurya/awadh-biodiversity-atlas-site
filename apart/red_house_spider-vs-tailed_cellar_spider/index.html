Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tailed Cellar Spider, as the Red House Spider profile puts it

Crossopriza lyoni

Extremely long legs; abdomen has a distinct posterior pointed hump or tail projecting backwards

Red House Spider: Bulbous, spherical-bellied cobweb spider with bright orange-red legs; spins tangled web with sticky gum-foot lines in dark indoor corners.

Tailed Cellar Spider: Eight-eyed, extremely fragile-legged spider in a fuzzy web in a building corner, trembling when web is disturbed, = Crossopriza lyoni.

Size—6–8 mm body length; legspan 25–30 mm (very slender appearance)
Legs—Extremely long, thin, fragile-looking; 8–10 times body length
Body—Pale yellow, tan, or brownish; nearly transparent
Eyes—Eight, arranged in two groups of four
Web—Loose, irregular mesh (funnel-web) in corners; sticky and three-dimensional
Behaviour—Sits in web hub during day; active hunting at night
