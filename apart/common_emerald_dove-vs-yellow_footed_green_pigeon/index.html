Common Emerald Dove or Yellow-footed Green Pigeon? कॉमन एमराल्ड डव या हरियल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन एमराल्ड डवCommon Emerald DoveChalcophaps indica
हरियलYellow-footed Green PigeonTreron phoenicopterus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon, as the Common Emerald Dove profile puts it

Treron phoenicopterus

Arboreal canopy feeder; yellowish-green body with lilac shoulder patch and bright yellow feet

Common Emerald Dove, as the Yellow-footed Green Pigeon profile puts it

Much smaller (20 cm); iridescent green; small red spots on wings; ground feeder

हीरा कबूतर — छोटी, ज़मीन पर

Common Emerald Dove: Ground-dwelling dove with luminous metallic emerald-green wings, vinous-pink breast, and bright coral-red bill.

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon: Large green pigeon with bright yellow feet, wine-red wing patch, and loud "pis-pis" call from the canopy = Yellow-footed Green Pigeon. The combination of yellow feet and maroon wing is diagnostic.
