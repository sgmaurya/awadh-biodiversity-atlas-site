How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-wattled Lapwing, as the Great Thick-knee profile puts it

Yellow-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus malabaricus) — if sympatric

Much smaller (27 cm); yellow face wattles; yellow legs; grassland habitat

Great Thick-knee: Large bird + extremely thick, long greenish legs + large head with prominent forehead + long curved bill + pale yellow eyes = Great Thick-knee. No other large wader has such thick legs.

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
