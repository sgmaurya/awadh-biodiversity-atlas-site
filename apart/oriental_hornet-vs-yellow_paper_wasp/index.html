How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Hornet, as the Yellow Paper Wasp profile puts it

Much larger (25–35 mm); robust body; reddish-brown with a single broad yellow band on the abdomen; builds nests in walls or soil

लाल ततैया / भमड़ — बहुत बड़ा, गहरा डंक

Oriental Hornet: Large (18–25 mm), yellow and black banded hornet with robust body and smooth sting; papery nest on buildings or trees: Oriental Hornet.

Yellow Paper Wasp: Medium-to-large size (18–24 mm), slender yellow body with thin black bands, narrow waist, and open paper comb nest with a single hanging stalk identify the Yellow Paper Wasp.
