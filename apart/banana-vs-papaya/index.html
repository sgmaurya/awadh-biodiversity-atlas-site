Banana or Papaya? केला या पपीता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

फ़सलें केलाBananaMusa paradisiaca
पपीताPapayaCarica papaya

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Papaya, as the Banana profile puts it

Has a woody, scarred trunk; leaves are deeply lobed (not simple oblong); fruit clings to trunk

पपीता — छाता-नुमा पत्तियां, गोल पीले फल

Banana: Giant perennial herb with green pseudostem trunk, massive paddle-like leaves, and a large hanging purple inflorescence producing seedless curved yellow fruits, is the Banana.

Papaya: Fast-growing unbranched tree with hollow trunk, crown of huge palmately lobed leaves, and large hollow green-yellow fruits clinging directly to the upper trunk, is the Papaya (Papita).

Tree habit—Fast-growing, single-trunked herbaceous tree up to 3–9 meters high, with hollow, soft, spongy green wood
Flowers—Cream-white; male flowers in long, drooping branched spikes; female and bisexual flowers larger, sitting directly in leaf joints
Seeds—Numerous, small (3–5 mm), black, wrinkled, covered in a mucilaginous wet gel sac inside the central fruit cavity
