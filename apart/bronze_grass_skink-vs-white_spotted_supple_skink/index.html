How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-spotted Supple Skink, as the Bronze Grass Skink profile puts it

Riopa albopunctata

Slender cylindrical body with minute reduced limbs and smooth, unkeeled scales

Bronze Grass Skink: Small shiny bronze skink with 5 to 7 keels on each dorsal scale; breeding males show bright scarlet throat and flanks.

White-spotted Supple Skink: A small, shiny, green-brown skink with white-spotted scales, running rapidly through dry grass, is a White-spotted Supple Skink.
