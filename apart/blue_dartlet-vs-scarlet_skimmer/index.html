Blue Dartlet or Scarlet Skimmer? नीली व्याधपतंग या सिंदूरी व्याधपतंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Skimmer, as the Blue Dartlet profile puts it

Dragonfly; much larger; bright red; rests with wings spread wide open; eyes meet at top

Blue Dartlet: Slender, needle-like sky-blue body in males with black rings, wings held folded closed along the back at rest, and widely separated eyes confirm the Blue Dartlet.

Scarlet Skimmer: Medium-large size, uniform scarlet-red body with a flat, broad abdomen, thin black dorsal line, and lack of dark wing patches identify the Scarlet Skimmer.
