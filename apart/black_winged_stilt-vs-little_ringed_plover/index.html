Black-winged Stilt or Little Ringed Plover? आवाक या छोटा बटेर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Ringed Plover, as the Black-winged Stilt profile puts it

Little Ringed Plover]] (Charadrius dubius)

Much smaller (15–18 cm); short yellowish legs; double black-and-white breast band; short bill

Black-winged Stilt, as the Little Ringed Plover profile puts it

Black-winged Stilt]] (Himantopus himantopus)

Much larger; extraordinarily long pink legs; black wings and white body; thin needle-like bill

Black-winged Stilt: Slender black-and-white bird with extraordinarily long, bright pink legs and a thin, needle-like black bill wading in shallow water is a Black-winged Stilt.

Little Ringed Plover: Small shorebird with sandy-brown upperparts, a prominent bright yellow eye ring, and a bold black-and-white breast band running on sandbars with quick bursts, is a Little Ringed Plover.

Side by side आमने-सामने

SizeSlender wading bird (35–40 cm total length); stands high on extremely long legsSmall, compact shorebird (15–18 cm total length); short legs and neck
LegsDiagnostic extraordinarily long, thin, bright pinkish-red legs—
PlumagePure white head, neck, and underparts; glossy black wings and back—

FlightTrailing long pink legs extend far beyond the tail in flight; wings are long and pointed—
NestBuilds nests in loose colonies on exposed mudbanks or shallow islands—
Eye Ring—Conspicuous, bright yellow fleshy ring around the eye (key field mark)
Breast Band—Bold black band running across the upper breast, separated from the face by a white collar
Colouration—Sandy-brown upperparts; pure white forehead, throat, and all underparts
Foraging—Runs in quick, short dashes, stops suddenly, tilts head, and pecks at the mud
