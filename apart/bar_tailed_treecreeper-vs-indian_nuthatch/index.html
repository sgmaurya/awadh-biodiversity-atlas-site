Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Nuthatch, as the Bar-tailed Treecreeper profile puts it

Indian Nuthatch]] (Sitta castanea)

Blue-grey upperparts, deep chestnut underparts, straight chisel bill, climbs headfirst downwards on trunks (Baker 1922, p. 124)

Bar-tailed Treecreeper: The slender decurved bill, broadly fulvous-centred dark mantle, and regularly black-barred pale reddish-brown tail climbed with stiff rectrices distinguish the Bar-tailed Treecreeper (Baker 1922, p. 430).

Indian Nuthatch: Indian Nuthatch is the only small nuthatch with slate-grey back and chestnut underparts. Its small size, dark eye-stripe, and headfirst descent of tree trunks are distinctive. Note the sharp whistled calls.
