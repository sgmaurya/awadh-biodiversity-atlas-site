Garden Tent-web Spider or Kidney Garden Spider? बाग़ीचा तंबू-जाला मकड़ी या बागीचा मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बाग़ीचा तंबू-जाला मकड़ीGarden Tent-web SpiderCyrtophora cicatrosa
बागीचा मकड़ीKidney Garden SpiderAraneus mitificus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kidney Garden Spider, as the Garden Tent-web Spider profile puts it

Araneus mitificus

Pale apple-green smooth abdomen with a dark kidney-shaped patch; builds standard sticky vertical orb web

Garden Tent-web Spider: Small grey-green spider with two pairs of abdominal humps; weaves non-sticky tent-dome web with vertical string of egg sacs.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Garden Tent-web SpiderKidney Garden Spider
Abdomen structureElongated, tapering posteriorly, bearing two pairs of distinct conical shoulder and dorsal tubercles—
ColorationGreyish-green to brownish-white speckled with fine black dots and white blotches—
Web architectureComplex non-sticky horizontal dome or tent-like sheet web with a dense maze of barrier threads—
Egg chainConstructs a vertical bead-like string of disc-shaped pale green or brownish egg sacs above the hub—
Resting postureHangs inverted beneath the central apex of the horizontal dome web—
Form—Compact, globular spider; abdomen is large, rounded, and wider at the anterior end
Colour—Pale green to yellowish-white abdomen; cephalothorax is light green or yellow-green
Markings—A prominent, dark brown or black kidney-shaped (reniform) mark on the posterior dorsal abdomen; front of abdomen has a thin black outline
Web—Vertical, circular orb-web built between branches, usually with one missing sector (pie-slice) near the top
Retreat—Hides inside a folded, silk-lined green leaf at the edge of the web
Legs—Greenish-yellow, semi-translucent, with fine spines and faint darker bands on the joints

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
