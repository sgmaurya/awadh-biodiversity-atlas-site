Mango or Tendu? आम या तेंदू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तेंदूTenduDiospyros melanoxylon

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mango, as the Tendu profile puts it

Mango]] (Mangifera indica)

Leaves larger, differently shaped; white flowers in panicles; large fruit

Mango: Long narrow copper-red new leaves + mango smell when scratched + large fleshy fruit with one flat stone = mango. No other UP tree has this combination.

Tendu: A deciduous tree with smooth, glossy, entire, leathery leaves and sticky black sweet fruit in summer, is tendu.
