How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Flea, as the Cyclops Copepod profile puts it

Smaller, flatter, heart-shaped, no eye stalk; transparent with dark eye

छोटा, दिल जैसा, आँख स्पष्ट

Cyclops Copepod: A small, transparent crustacean with a single large median eye, long antennae and a forked tail is a Cyclops copepod; species identification requires a microscope.

Water Flea: A tiny, transparent crustacean with a single large eye, beating leaf-like legs, and jerky swimming is a daphnia; the genus is identified by eye, but species requires a microscope.
