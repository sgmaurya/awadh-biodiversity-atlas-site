Common Redshank, as the Spotted Redshank profile puts it

Common Redshank]] (Tringa totanus)

Smaller; darker grey in winter; orange-red (not pure red) legs; darker overall

Common Redshank: A medium wader with unmistakable bright red legs and reddish-orange bill, with white rump visible in flight, probing in mud — is a Common Redshank. The red legs are diagnostic. Greenshanks are paler and larger with greenish legs.

Spotted Redshank: A tall, pale grey wader with long red legs and red bill, often wading in deep water alone, is a Spotted Redshank. The longer legs, paler plumage, and deeper wading distinguish it from the Common Redshank.
