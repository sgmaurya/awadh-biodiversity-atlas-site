Downy Thorn-apple, as the Dhatura profile puts it

Similar to metel; flowers slightly smaller; native to Americas; rarer in UP

वैसा ही — लेकिन दुर्लभ

Dhatura, as the Downy Thorn-apple profile puts it

धतूरा (Datura metel)]]

Plant glabrous or slightly puberulous; flowers five-toothed, often purple; capsule erect or spreading

Dhatura: Large roadside herb + large funnel-shaped white flower opening at dusk + large spiny round fruit + strong smell = Datura metel. The spiny capsule is the single most reliable feature — nothing else common in eastern UP has it.

Downy Thorn-apple: A sprawling rank herb clothed entirely in soft velvety greyish fuzz with large white ten-toothed trumpet flowers and nodding spiny capsules is Datura innoxia.
