Finescale Razorbelly Minnow or Large Razorbelly Minnow? फुलो चेलवा या लार्ज रेज़रबेली मिनो मछली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Razorbelly Minnow, as the Finescale Razorbelly Minnow profile puts it

Salmostoma bacaila

Larger total length; significantly larger scales with only 42 to 46 in the lateral line

Finescale Razorbelly Minnow: Slender silvery surface minnow with knife-like ventral keel from pelvic fins to anal fin, and minute scales exceeding 80 in lateral line.

Large Razorbelly Minnow: A slender, elongate, surface-dwelling silvery minnow with an upward-directed mouth, an acute razor keel behind the pelvic fins, and 86–110 scales along the lateral line, is Salmostoma bacaila.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Finescale Razorbelly MinnowLarge Razorbelly Minnow
Body shapeElongate, strongly compressed laterally with a sharp, razor-like ventral keelElongate, slender, strongly compressed laterally with a sharp abdominal keel (Hamilton 1822)
Scale sizeMinute, exceptionally fine scales with 80 to 90 scales in the lateral line—
Mouth orientationStrongly oblique, superior mouth directed upwards for surface feeding—
ColorationTranslucent silvery-white with a brilliant glittering silver lateral stripe—
HabitsSurface-swimming pelagic shoaler leaping out of water to evade predatory fish—
Size—Medium-small freshwater cyprinid; reaches about 18 cm TL (Froese & Pauly 2025)
Mouth & Snout—Mouth wide, directed obliquely upward; lower jaw with a prominent symphysial knob (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Barbels—Completely absent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Colour—Translucent greenish-grey back with glistening, pure silver flanks and belly (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Scales—Extremely small, delicate, containing 86 to 110 scales along the lateral line (Hamilton 1822)
Fins—Dorsal fin small, inserted far back opposite the anal fin; pectoral fins elongate (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
