Red Avadavat or Scaly-breasted Munia? लाल मुनिया या तीतर मुनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तीतर मुनियाScaly-breasted MuniaLonchura punctulata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Avadavat, as the Scaly-breasted Munia profile puts it

Breeding male is bright crimson-red with white spots; red bill; lacks scales on underparts

लाल मुनिया — चमकीला लाल रंग, चोंच लाल

Red Avadavat: Very small finch + brilliant red breeding male or buff female + flock living + near water + grass seed feeding + soft "zee" calls = Red Avadavat. The breeding male's bright red plumage is unmistakable.

Scaly-breasted Munia: Very small chestnut-brown bird with a thick conical bill and a white belly covered in dark brown scale-like markings, flying in tight whispering flocks, is a Scaly-breasted Munia.
