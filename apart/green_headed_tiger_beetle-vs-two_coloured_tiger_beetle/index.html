Green-headed Tiger Beetle or Two-coloured Tiger Beetle? हरे-सिर वाला बाघ भृंग या दो-रंगा बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Two-coloured Tiger Beetle, as the Green-headed Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Much larger; front parts green, finely and rugosely sculptured, and elytra dark greenish cyaneous or bluish, much more blue than the front parts, without spots (Fowler 1912, p. 400)

काफी बड़ा, आगे का हिस्सा हरा और पंख बिना धब्बों वाले नीले (दो-रंगा बाघ भृंग)

Green-headed Tiger Beetle: A small tiger beetle with a brilliant metallic green head and pronotum, dark blue-black elytra, and a separated large round white spot before the apex is Cylindera erudita (Fowler 1912, pp. 362–363).

Two-coloured Tiger Beetle: A tiger beetle on firm dirt paths with a bright coppery-red head and thorax contrasting against solid unspotted deep blue or green elytra identifies Cicindela bicolor.
