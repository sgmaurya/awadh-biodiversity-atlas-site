Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Lark, as the Ashy-crowned Sparrow-Lark profile puts it

Crested Lark]] (Galerida cristata)

Much larger; prominent crest; different habitat (less open)

Ashy-crowned Sparrow-Lark, as the Crested Lark profile puts it

Ashy-crowned Sparrow-Lark]] (Eremopterix griseus)

Very small (14 cm); sparse crest; different habitat

Ashy-crowned Sparrow-Lark: A small, dumpy lark, always in flocks on open ground. Males show distinctive ashy crown and black-and-white facial pattern. Runs on ground; flies with whirring wings. Highly gregarious; easily identified by behaviour and flock association.

Crested Lark: Crested Lark is readily identified by its prominent, pointed, erectile crest and buff-brown coloration. Walking on open ground with upright posture and raised crest is diagnostic.
