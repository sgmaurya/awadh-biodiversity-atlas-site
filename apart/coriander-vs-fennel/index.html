Coriander or Fennel? धनिया या सौंफ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

धनियाCorianderCoriandrum sativum
सौंफFennelFoeniculum vulgare

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Fennel, as the Coriander profile puts it

Much taller; leaves are all thread-like (no broad lower leaves); flowers are yellow; seeds are oval

सौंफ — पीले फूल, बारीक धागे जैसे पत्ते

Coriander: Erect winter annual herb with dimorphic leaves (broad lower, thread-like upper), sweet citrus-spice scent, and flat compound umbels of white-pink flowers producing round ribbed dry seeds, is Coriander (Dhaniya).

Fennel: Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare) is distinguished by its tall glaucous stems, filiform thread-like leaf segments, bright yellow umbels, and sweet anise scent, whereas coriander has white-pink flowers and broad basal leaves.
