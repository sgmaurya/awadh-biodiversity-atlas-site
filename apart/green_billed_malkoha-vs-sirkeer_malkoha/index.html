Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green-billed Malkoha, as the Sirkeer Malkoha profile puts it

Green-billed Malkoha]] (Phaenicophaeus tristis)

Bright apple-green bill, dark slate-green body plumage, and red orbital patch; inhabits dense moist forest canopy, not dry scrub

Green-billed Malkoha: The slender greenish-grey body, bright apple-green bill, crimson orbital skin, and long graduated tail with white spots distinguish the Green-billed Malkoha from all heavy-bodied coucals.

Sirkeer Malkoha: The stout cherry-red bill with a yellow tip, sandy-brown plumage with stiff spiny shaft-streaks, and white-tipped graduated tail distinguish the Sirkeer Malkoha from coucals and other cuckoos.
