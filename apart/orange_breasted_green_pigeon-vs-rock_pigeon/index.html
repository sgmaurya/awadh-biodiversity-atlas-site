Orange-breasted Green Pigeon or Rock Pigeon? बरियल या कबूतर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rock Pigeon, as the Orange-breasted Green Pigeon profile puts it

Columba livia

Slate-grey body, iridescent neck, two black wing bars, and terrestrial habits

Orange-breasted Green Pigeon: Bright green pigeon with coral-red legs; male shows striking lilac and deep orange breast bands, female shows blue-grey nape and unbarred cinnamon undertail.

Rock Pigeon: Stocky body, slate-grey colouration (wild type) with two black wing bars, white rump, pink legs, and rolling groo-coo vocalizations identify the Rock Pigeon.

Breast colorationMale shows a delicate lilac band bordered below by a rich orange band; female plain green—
Head patternYellowish-green forehead and crown transitioning into blue-grey on the nape and hindneck—
Wing patternBlack primaries and greater wing-coverts broadly margined with striking bright yellow edges—
Undertail covertsRich unbarred cinnamon in male; pale cinnamon mottled with green in female—
Tail markingsDark slate-grey tail terminating in a wide pale grey terminal band and dark subterminal bar—
Size—Medium-sized stocky bird (30–35 cm total length); wingspan 60–68 cm
Colouration—Wild type is bluish-grey with iridescent green and purple neck patches
Wings—Two distinct black bands across the wings (wing bars) and a dark tail band
Feral Variants—High variation in colors (pure white, tan, checker, and dark grey-black)
Eyes—Bright orange or reddish-orange irises with dark pupils
Bare parts—Bill greyish-black with a white fleshy swelling (cere) at the base; legs pink
