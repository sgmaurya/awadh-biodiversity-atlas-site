Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sand Lark, as the Bengal Bushlark profile puts it

Sand Lark]] (Alaudala raytal)

Much paler sandy plumage, slender bill, strictly confined to river sandbars, lacks rufous wing patch (Baker 1926, p. 329)

Bengal Bushlark: The stout conical bill, heavily dark-spotted breast, and prominent chestnut wing panels identify the Bengal Bushlark (Baker 1926, pp. 337–338).

Sand Lark: The Sand Lark is the smallest and palest lark of open ground. If the bird is larger and more noticeable, it is likely Oriental Skylark or Crested Lark.
