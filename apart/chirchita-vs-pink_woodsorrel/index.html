Prickly Chaff Flower or Pink Woodsorrel? चिरचिटा या गुलाबी खट्टी मीठी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Prickly Chaff Flower, as the Pink Woodsorrel profile puts it

Tall erect herb; simple opposite leaves; long prickly flower spikes

सीधा खड़ा पौधा, कांटेदार लंबी बाली

Prickly Chaff Flower: Erect roadside herb + opposite hairy leaves + elongated spike + seeds with 2 stiff backwardly-reflexed spiny bracteoles that stick to clothing = Achyranthes aspera. Once you pull these seeds off your clothes, the identification is made.

Pink Woodsorrel: Stemless herb from a scaly underground bulb with trifoliolate notched leaves bearing orange glands beneath, and umbels of funnel-shaped purplish-pink flowers identifies Oxalis debilis.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Prickly Chaff FlowerPink Woodsorrel
Height & HabitErect, wiry annual/perennial herb or subshrub, 30–100 cm tall; stems square in cross-section—
LeavesOpposite, ovate to elliptic or obovate, 3–10 cm long, rough-hairy, margins smooth (entire)—
FlowersSmall (3–4 mm), greenish-white, densely clustered on long terminal spikes (10–30 cm long)—
Seeds/FruitsSmall utricles (4–6 mm long) with 2 stiff, spine-tipped reflexed bracteoles—
DispersalExtreme mechanical stickiness; seeds cling instantly to clothing, fur, and feathers—
StemWire-like, often tinged with red or purple; hairy at nodes—
Habit—Stemless perennial herb 10–30 cm tall arising from a scaly underground bulb (Gaur 1999)
Foliage—Radical, long-petioled, trifoliolate with obcordate leaflets notched at apex (Hooker 1874)
Glands—Underside of leaflets and calyx lobes bearing tiny translucent orange dots (Gaur 1999)
Inflorescence—Umbellate cymes of 4–12 flowers on slender radical scapes exceeding the leaves (Hooker 1874)
Corolla—Funnel-shaped, 1.5–2.2 cm across, purplish-pink or violet with deeper veins (Gaur 1999)
Bulbils—Clustered abundantly around the parent bulb, producing numerous daughter offsets
