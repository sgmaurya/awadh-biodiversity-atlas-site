Small Ramshorn Snail or River Snail? छोटा घोंघा या नदी घोंघा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

River Snail, as the Small Ramshorn Snail profile puts it

Turret-shaped (not disc-shaped); larger (15–25 mm); flowing-water habitat

लंबा-कुंड, बहुत बड़ा, नदी में

Small Ramshorn Snail, as the River Snail profile puts it

Flattened, disc-shaped shell (unlike turret-like Brotia); found in stagnant water vs. flowing rivers

चपटा, गोल, खड़े पानी में

Small Ramshorn Snail: An extremely small (5–8 mm), flattened, disc-shaped snail on aquatic plants in ponds and ditches is a small ramshorn snail. It is an intermediate host for parasitic flukes; its presence may indicate disease risk for livestock and, rarely, humans.

River Snail: A small (15–25 mm), dark, turret-shaped snail with spiral ridges on rocks in flowing river water is a river snail (Brotia costula). Safe to eat if properly cooked; disease risk if eaten raw.
