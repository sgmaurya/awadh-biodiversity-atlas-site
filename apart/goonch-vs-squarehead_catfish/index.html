Goonch, as the Squarehead Catfish profile puts it

Goonch]])

Large predatory catfish with massive mouth and sharp teeth, but body streamlined with dorsal saddles

Goonch: The largest catfish in eastern UP waters, with a massive flattened head, long maxillary barbels, and a powerfully built body, is a Goonch. No other catfish reaches this size.

Squarehead Catfish: An unmistakable, grotesque, toad-like catfish with a massive square head, minute eyes, and an immense mouth gaping from cheek to cheek, buried in mud, is Chaca chaca.
