Forked Fimbry or Slender Cyperus? फोर्कड फिम्ब्री घास या स्लेंडर साइपरस घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Slender Cyperus, as the Forked Fimbry profile puts it

Taller sedge; spikelets linear, slender, widely spaced in loose spikes

पतला साइपरस, लंबी बिखरी बालियाँ

Forked Fimbry, as the Slender Cyperus profile puts it

Leaves basal with ciliated ligule; spikelets ovate spirally imbricate; nutlet biconvex

पत्ती पर रोएं, बालियाँ अंडाकार

Forked Fimbry: Tufted sedge with compressed culms, ciliate ligular fringe, decompound umbellate anthela of chestnut-brown ovate spikelets, and biconvex longitudinally ribbed ivory nutlets identifies Fimbristylis dichotoma.

Slender Cyperus: Triangular-stemmed perennial sedge with an open, spreading compound anthela bearing linear needle-like reddish-brown spikelets spaced widely apart along a zig-zag rachis identifies Cyperus distans.
