Giant Catfish or Rita Catfish? मल्ला या रीता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रीताRita CatfishRita rita

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Catfish, as the Rita Catfish profile puts it

Extremely wide mouth extending past eyes; deeply forked tail but body is highly elongated and lacks lateral stripes; long anal fin

पढ़िन — बहुत बड़ा मुँह, लम्बा शरीर

Giant Catfish: Enormous mouth extending past the eyes, scaleless silvery-grey body, very short dorsal fin, and very long anal fin identify Wallago attu.

Rita Catfish: Large head, heavy scale-less body, three pairs of barbels (with short nasal barbels), a deeply forked tail, and pale lateral bands distinguish the Rita Catfish.
