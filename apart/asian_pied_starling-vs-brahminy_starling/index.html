Asian Pied Starling or Brahminy Starling? गुलाम मैना या ब्राह्मणी मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Pied Starling, as the Brahminy Starling profile puts it

Bold black-and-white plumage; orange orbital skin around the eye; lacks crest

अबलकी मैना — सफ़ेद और काला शरीर, आँख के पास नारंगी त्वचा

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Asian Pied StarlingBrahminy Starling
Size23–25 cm total length; stocky Passerine buildSmall, slender starling (19–21 cm total length); slightly smaller than the [[common_myna\
PlumageStriking black and white; black head, back, and upper breast; white cheek, rump, wing patch, and belly—
FaceBare orange-red skin patch around the eye (orbital ring)—
BillSturdy, orange-yellow with a pale tipYellow with a conspicuous greenish-blue base
FlightDirect and fast, showing a prominent white wing patch and rump flashSwift, direct flight with rapid wingbeats; wings show no white patches (unlike Common Myna)
VoiceLoud, varied, musical whistles, chattering, and minor mimicry—
Head & Crest—Glossy black crown and a long, drooping crest that lies flat along the back of the neck
Colouration—Pale grey upperparts; rich orange-rufous or chestnut underparts and cheeks
Tail—Dark brown tail with broad white tips on the outer tail feathers, visible in flight

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
