Chestnut-capped Babbler or Tawny-bellied Babbler? चेस्टनट-कैप्ड बैबलर या टॉनी-बेलीड बैबलर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tawny-bellied Babbler, as the Chestnut-capped Babbler profile puts it

Dumetia hyperythra

Lacks the chestnut-red crown cap, white forehead, and black lores; has uniform rufous-buff underparts and grey-brown crown (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Chestnut-capped Babbler: Grassland babbler with bright chestnut-red crown cap, white forehead and supercilium, stout black bill, and black-streaked white throat.

Tawny-bellied Babbler: Tawny-bellied babbler is a small, dark babbler with a distinctive tawny or rufous belly. If larger and paler with a long tail, it is likely a common babbler.
