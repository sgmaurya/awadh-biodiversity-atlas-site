Banded Phintella: A tiny, highly agile jumping spider with brilliant iridescent longitudinal bands of metallic blue-green and yellow running along both carapace and abdomen is Phintella vittata.

Imperial Jumping Spider: A jumping spider with an elevated, coppery-orange eye region bordered by white stripes, heavy dark front legs, and active daytime hunting on sunny leaves, is Thyene imperialis.
