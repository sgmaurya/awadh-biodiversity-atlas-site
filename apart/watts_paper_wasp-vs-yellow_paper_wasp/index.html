Watt's Paper Wasp or Yellow Paper Wasp? वॉट्स पेपर वास्प या ततैया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ततैयाYellow Paper WaspPolistes olivaceus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow Paper Wasp, as the Watt's Paper Wasp profile puts it

Rich olive-yellow coloration with distinct black scuto-scutellar markings

जैतून जैसी पीली चमक और सीने पर काले निशान

Watt's Paper Wasp: An entirely pale honey-yellow paper wasp without dark transverse thoracic bands identifies Polistes wattii.

Yellow Paper Wasp: Medium-to-large size (18–24 mm), slender yellow body with thin black bands, narrow waist, and open paper comb nest with a single hanging stalk identify the Yellow Paper Wasp.

Watt's Paper WaspYellow Paper Wasp
HabitSlender, social paper wasp constructing unenclosed umbrella-shaped paper combs—
ColorationUniformly pale golden-yellow to honey-yellow without dark thoracic bands—
AbdomenElongate, spindle-shaped with very fine transverse pale sutures—
LegsLong and yellow, trailing loosely below the body during hovering flight—
WingsNarrow, smoky-yellow to hyaline with a ferruginous anterior marginNarrow, translucent wings with a faint reddish-brown or smoky tint
Size—Medium-to-large wasp; body length 18–24 mm; wingspan 32–40 mm
Colouration—Bright yellow body marked with fine black bands and patterns; head is yellow
Body Shape—Slender, elongate body; abdomen connects to the thorax via a narrow waist (petiole)
Nest—An open, single-tier papery comb hanging from a central stalk under roofs or branches
Stinger—Females possess a smooth stinger that can sting repeatedly; males are stingless

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
