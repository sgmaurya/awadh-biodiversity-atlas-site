How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Shrike, as the Bay-backed Shrike profile puts it

Brown Shrike]] (Lanius cristatus)

Slightly larger; duller brown back; different face pattern; winter visitor

Bay-backed Shrike, as the Brown Shrike profile puts it

Smaller; grey crown, chestnut back, white wing patch; resident

बे-बैक्ड श्राइक

Bay-backed Shrike: Bay-backed shrike is a small shrike with a distinctively warm chestnut or bay back (never black or grey). If the back is black, it is a long-tailed shrike. If the back is duller and the bird is a winter visitor, it may be a brown shrike.

Brown Shrike: Brown Shrike shows a rusty-chestnut back with a black eye-stripe and white underparts. If the tail is rufous on a pale sandy bird, it is an Isabelline Shrike. If the back is dark slate-grey, it is a different species.
