How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Minivet, as the Large Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Small Minivet]] (Pericrocotus cinnamomeus)

Much smaller (16–17 cm); red and black male; female yellow; shorter tail

Large Cuckooshrike: A large (25–28 cm), uniformly grey-brown perching bird with a stout hooked bill and rounded shape, gleaning caterpillars in tree foliage, is a Large Cuckooshrike. Smaller shrike species are brightly coloured or patterned and sit on exposed perches.

Small Minivet: The smallest minivet; males striking black-and-orange, females dull grey-and-yellow. Always in organized flocks with soft contact calls. Active gleaners in trees; coordinated flock movement distinctive.
