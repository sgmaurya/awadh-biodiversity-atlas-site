How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coromandel Marsh Dart, as the Orange-tailed Marsh Dart profile puts it

Coromandel Marsh Dart]])

Entire abdomen uniform bright citron-yellow; entirely lacks the dark black middle segments

Coromandel Marsh Dart: Tiny damselfly with bright red (male) or bright yellow-orange (female) body, slow fluttering flight over marshy vegetation, often in tandem pairs, is the Coromandel Marsh Dart.

Orange-tailed Marsh Dart: A medium-sized damselfly of lotus ponds whose bright grass-green thorax is paired with an abdomen that is orange-red at both ends and velvety black in the middle is the Orange-tailed Marsh Dart.

Mid Abdomen—Segments 3 to 7 velvety black or dark brownish-black above (Fraser 1933)

Eyes—Olive-green above and pale greenish-yellow beneath (Fraser 1933)
Wings—Clear, hyaline with amber pterostigma framed in dark brown (Subramanian 2005)
