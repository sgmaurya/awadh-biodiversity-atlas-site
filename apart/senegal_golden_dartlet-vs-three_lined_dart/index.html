How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Senegal Golden Dartlet, as the Three-lined Dart profile puts it

Bright yellow-orange colour; different structure

सुनहरी व्याधपतंग

Senegal Golden Dartlet: Small damselfly with bright yellow-orange head and thorax (male) or polymorphic females (yellow, green, or brown forms), in shallow vegetation-rich water.

Three-lined Dart: Blue damselfly with three prominent black longitudinal stripes (one central, two lateral) on the thorax distinguishes this species.
