How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher, as the Small Minivet profile puts it

Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher]] (Culicicapa ceylonensis)

Smaller; yellower; duller; different head pattern

Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher: Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher is a small grey-headed bird with yellow underparts that hangs upside-down in foliage. No other small flycatcher in eastern UP during winter combines these features.

Small Minivet: The smallest minivet; males striking black-and-orange, females dull grey-and-yellow. Always in organized flocks with soft contact calls. Active gleaners in trees; coordinated flock movement distinctive.
