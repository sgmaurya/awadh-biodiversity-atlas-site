Himalayan Bulbul, as the Red-vented Bulbul profile puts it

White cheeks; forward-curving crest; yellow vent patch

पहाड़ी बुलबुल — पीली पूंछ

Himalayan Bulbul: The prominent white ear-coverts with black border, hair-brown crest, and sulphur-yellow under tail-coverts distinguish the Himalayan Bulbul from all related species (Baker 1922).

Red-vented Bulbul: Black head with scaled breast, white rump, and a bright red vent patch is diagnostic for the Red-vented Bulbul. No other common bulbul in eastern UP has a red vent combined with a scaled black-and-brown breast.
