Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-headed Vulture, as the Cinereous Vulture profile puts it

Red-headed Vulture]] (Sarcogyps calvus)

Striking bare crimson head, deep black plumage with white leg patches, and reddish legs; smaller build

Cinereous Vulture: The immense wingspan, uniform blackish-brown plumage lacking white rump or pale wing ruffs, heavily feathered dark thighs, and massive heavy bill identify the Cinereous Vulture.

Red-headed Vulture: A very large dark vulture with a distinctive bright red bare head and neck. No other large vulture in eastern UP shows this red head coloration. Soars at great heights; descends to carrion.
