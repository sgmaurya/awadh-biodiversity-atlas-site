Red-wattled Lapwing or Yellow-wattled Lapwing? टिटहरी या ज़र्दी टिटहरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

टिटहरीRed-wattled LapwingVanellus indicus
ज़र्दी टिटहरीYellow-wattled LapwingVanellus malabaricus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-wattled Lapwing, as the Red-wattled Lapwing profile puts it

Yellow-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus malabaricus)

Lacks black throat; sandy-brown plumage; bright yellow facial wattle; prefers dry areas

Red-wattled Lapwing, as the Yellow-wattled Lapwing profile puts it

Red-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus indicus) — if present

Larger (29–32 cm); red face wattles; black and white plumage; different habitat

Red-wattled Lapwing: Medium size, long yellow legs, black head and chest, red facial wattle, and loud "did-he-do-it" alarm calls identify the Red-wattled Lapwing.

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
