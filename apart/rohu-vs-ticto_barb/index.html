Rohu or Ticto Barb? रोहू या टिकटो?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रोहूRohuLabeo rohita
टिकटोTicto BarbPethia ticto

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Ticto Barb profile puts it

Much larger (20–60 cm); head bigger; mouth terminal and large

बहुत बड़ी, बड़ा मुँह

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.

Ticto Barb: A small, moderately compressed barb with dark spots along the lateral line, yellowish fins, and occurring in schooling behaviour in shallow water is a ticto barb.
