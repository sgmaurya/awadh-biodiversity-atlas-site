Indian Lotus or Water Snowflake? कमल या छोटी कुमुदिनी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटी कुमुदिनीWater SnowflakeNymphoides indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Lotus, as the Water Snowflake profile puts it

Much larger leaves (15–25 cm), held above water; flowers very large (10–15 cm); root anchored deep

कमल — बहुत बड़ी पत्तियाँ

Indian Lotus: A large, peltate leaf held above water on a long stalk = lotus. Leaf floating on water surface = water lily. They are not related plants — different families, different evolutionary histories.

Water Snowflake: Small round leaves with frilly petals in yellow or pale lilac, delicate growth, no mat formation — water snowflake.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Seed PodFlat-topped, cone-shaped green receptacle (kamal gatta) containing multiple seed cavities—
RhizomeLong, segmented, cylindrical tubers (kamal kakdi) growing in mud, with ringed air channels inside—
HabitatPerennial standing water bodies like ponds, lakes, oxbow lakes, and temple tanks—
Form—Floating or partially submerged aquatic herb; leaves scattered or in loose rosettes
Leaf stalk—Slender, flexible; variable length depending on water depth
Fruit—Thin-walled capsule; many small seeds
Roots—Thin, fibrous, floating or anchored in mud; lack the air-filled chambers of water lilies
