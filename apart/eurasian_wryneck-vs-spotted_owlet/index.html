How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Owlet, as the Eurasian Wryneck profile puts it

Spotted Owlet]] (Athene brama)

Much larger; nocturnal; yellow eyes; hooting calls; roosts in holes during day

Eurasian Wryneck: A small (12–13 cm), cryptic, heavily mottled brown and grey bird found on the ground in open scrubland, with the remarkable ability to twist its head dramatically when alarmed, feeding on ground ants — is the Eurasian Wryneck. Among Indian birds, no other species shows this distinctive head-twisting behavior.

Spotted Owlet: Small size, white spots on greyish-brown upperparts, yellow eyes, lack of ear tufts, and loud chattering calls in pairs confirm the Spotted Owlet.
