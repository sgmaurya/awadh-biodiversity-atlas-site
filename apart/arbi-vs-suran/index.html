Taro or Elephant Foot Yam? अरबी या सूरन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अरबीTaroColocasia esculenta
सूरनElephant Foot YamAmorphophallus paeoniifolius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Taro, as the Elephant Foot Yam profile puts it

Smaller leaves, not tripinnate; tuber smaller and different shape

अरबी — पत्ती अलग, कंद छोटा

Taro: A very large-leaved herbaceous plant with heart-shaped glaucous leaves on long petioles, in waterlogged areas, with an underground starchy white corm is taro.

Elephant Foot Yam: An enormous, single, deeply lobed umbrella-shaped leaf on a large speckled petiole, with an underground white-fleshed tuber, is suran.
