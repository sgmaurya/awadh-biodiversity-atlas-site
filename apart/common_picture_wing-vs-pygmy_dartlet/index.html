How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pygmy Dartlet, as the Common Picture Wing profile puts it

A tiny damselfly\

Blue Dartlet]] (Agriocnemis pygmaea)

Common Picture Wing: Small dragonfly with distinctly patterned wings showing dark and light patches, perching low on vegetation near shallow water, is the Common Picture Wing.

Pygmy Dartlet: The tiniest damselfly of the region — body length under 22 mm, bright metallic blue (male) with black rings, in shallow vegetation-rich water.
