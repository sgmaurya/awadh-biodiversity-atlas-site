Asthma Weed or Tropical Milkweed? दूधी या काकतुंडी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asthma Weed, as the Tropical Milkweed profile puts it

Lower herb; different flower structure; typically found in more shaded areas

कम ऊँचा, अलग फूल

Asthma Weed: Hairy reddish stems with milky sap and dense round flower clusters in the leaf axils, on trodden ground, is dudhi.

Tropical Milkweed: A semi-woody bush with opposite, narrow leaves, showy orange-and-red flowers, slender seed pods, and white latex is Asclepias curassavica.
