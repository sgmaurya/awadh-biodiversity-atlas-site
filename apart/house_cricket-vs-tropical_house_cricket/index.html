How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tropical House Cricket, as the House Cricket profile puts it

Tropical House Cricket]])

House Cricket, as the Tropical House Cricket profile puts it

House Cricket]])

Straw-yellow with three dark bands across head; both sexes fully winged; females lack wing reduction

House Cricket: Pale straw-yellow body, distinct dark transverse bands across the head, fully developed wings in both sexes, and domestic habitat = Acheta domesticus.

Tropical House Cricket: Pale tan body with dark transverse bars across head and pronotum, male with shortened wing pads and female completely wingless = Gryllodes sigillatus.
