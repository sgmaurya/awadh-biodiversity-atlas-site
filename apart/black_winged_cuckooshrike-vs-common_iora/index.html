How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Iora, as the Black-winged Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Common Iora]] (Aegithina tiphia)

Smaller (14–15 cm); yellow and black; different structure

Black-winged Cuckooshrike: Black-winged Cuckooshrike has a pale grey body with sharply contrasting black wings. Drongos are all-black with longer, forked tails. The Cuckooshrike moves more deliberately and hangs from branches searching foliage.

Common Iora: A tiny, highly active gleaning bird. Males are strikingly black and yellow; females are green-yellow. Characteristic sharp, whistled calls. Often hangs upside-down while foraging in foliage.
