Slender blue-and-black body; legs black and simple; sun-loving lotic damselfly

Spence's Dartlet: A small blue-and-black damselfly of slow canals whose male has large triangular blue postocular spots, azure-blue thoracic stripes, and completely bright blue abdominal segments 8 and 9 is Spence's Dartlet.

Yellow Bush Dart: An extremely slender, thread-like damselfly flitting weakly in deep shade near water with a dark bronzy body, a white tail tip, and conspicuously expanded bright golden-yellow legs is the Yellow Bush Dart.
