Mrigal or Rohu? मृगल या रोहू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मृगलMrigalCirrhinus mrigala
रोहूRohuLabeo rohita

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Mrigal profile puts it

Silvery-grey with reddish fins; mouth is terminal with thick, transversely wrinkled lips; column feeder

रोहू — लाल पंख, झुर्रीदार होंठ

Mrigal, as the Rohu profile puts it

Paler; no reddish fins; straight lower lip (not wrinkled); bottom feeder

मृगल — सफेद, सीधा होंठ

Mrigal: Streamlined silver body, downward-pointing mouth with thin smooth lips, and yellowish-orange pelvic/anal fins identify the Mrigal.

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.
