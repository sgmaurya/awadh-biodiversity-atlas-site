Crested Lark or Rufous-tailed Lark? चंडूल या रेतल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Lark, as the Rufous-tailed Lark profile puts it

Crested Lark]] (Galerida cristata)

Tall pointed crest, heavily streaked plumage, larger size, lacks rufous tail flash (Baker 1926, p. 341)

Crested Lark: Crested Lark is readily identified by its prominent, pointed, erectile crest and buff-brown coloration. Walking on open ground with upright posture and raised crest is diagnostic.

Rufous-tailed Lark: The unstreaked dark brown body combined with deep rufous rump and black-tipped rufous tail identifies the Rufous-tailed Lark (Baker 1926, p. 350).

BehaviourWalks on ground; runs when disturbed; sings from ground or low perch—
Habit—Stocky, dark rufous-brown lark running briskly over dry ploughed earth and stubble
Rump & Upper Tail—Rump and upper tail-coverts deep rufous (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Tail Pattern—Tail deep rufous with broad blackish-brown tips (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Upperparts—Dark brown with obsolete paler edges and darker centres showing more definitely on crown (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Underparts—Lower plumage, axillaries and under wing-coverts deep rufous; throat and upper breast paler and streaked with black (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Bare Parts—Bill dark horny-brown, fleshy on base of lower mandible; legs and feet pale fleshy to fleshy-brown (Baker 1926, p. 350)
