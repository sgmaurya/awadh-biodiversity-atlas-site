Common Bronzeback Tree Snake or Common Krait? ब्रॉन्ज़बैक साँप या करैत?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Krait, as the Common Bronzeback Tree Snake profile puts it

Much larger (100+ cm), banded, nocturnal, on ground, venomous

करैत बड़ा, बैंडेड, रात का, जहरीला

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake: A slender, uniformly green or bronze-brown snake moving fast along a tree branch during the day with a long prehensile tail is a Common Bronzeback and is completely harmless.

Common Krait: Highly polished steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody, combined with enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales along the backbone, identifies Bungarus caeruleus.

Size60–100 cm; very slender body—
ColourOlive-green, yellow-green, or bronze-brown dorsally; unmarked or with faint bands—
HeadPointed, not wider than neck; eyes large and prominentSmall, flat, scarcely distinct from the neck; eyes are small, dark, with indistinct pupils
TailLong and prehensile (about 1/3 of total length); used for climbing—
ScalesSmooth and glossy; 15 rows at midbodyA row of distinctly enlarged, hexagonal vertebral scales running down the center of the back
BehaviourDiurnal; arboreal; fast-moving; no banding (unlike krait or kukri)Strictly nocturnal; inactive by day, coiling its body and hiding its head beneath the coils when threatened
BehaviourNever bites when handled; may open mouth in threat display—
Form—Slender, cylindrical body; skin is highly polished, glossy, and iridescent steel-black or blue-black
Markings—40–50 narrow white crossbands, often occurring in distinct pairs; bands are absent on the head/neck, starting midbody
