How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cucumber Moth, as the Beet Webworm profile puts it

Cucumber Moth]] (Diaphania indica)

Similar size; different wing pattern; clearer markings; different host plants

Beet Webworm, as the Cucumber Moth profile puts it

Beet Webworm]] (Spoladea recurvalis)

Smaller; paler colouration; more delicate wing pattern; different host plants

Beet Webworm: A pale greyish-white small moth with delicate net-like pattern on the wings, found on or near beet, chard, or spinach plants, is likely the Beet Webworm.

Cucumber Moth: A small white or pale yellow moth with black-tipped wings and dark markings, found on cucumber, melon, or gourd plants, is the Cucumber Moth.
