Indian Coral Tree or Palash? पांगारा या पलाश?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पांगाराIndian Coral TreeErythrina variegata
पलाशPalashButea monosperma

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Palash, as the Indian Coral Tree profile puts it

Leaves are rounder and velvety; orange-red flowers are shaped like parrot beaks, covered in grey velvet; bark has no spines

पलाश — शंक्वाकार लाल-नारंगी फूल, काँटों का अभाव

Indian Coral Tree: Medium deciduous tree with pale greenish-grey spiny bark, trifoliate leaves, and large spikes of bright crimson-red beak-like flowers appearing on bare branches in spring, is the Indian Coral Tree.

Palash: Crooked trunk, large leathery trifoliate leaves (three leaflets), and masses of bright orange-red parrot-beak shaped flowers on leafless branches in spring identify the Palash tree.
