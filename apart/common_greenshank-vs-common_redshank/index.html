Common Greenshank or Common Redshank? कॉमन ग्रीनशैंक या कॉमन रेडशैंक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन ग्रीनशैंकCommon GreenshankTringa nebularia
कॉमन रेडशैंकCommon RedshankTringa totanus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Redshank, as the Common Greenshank profile puts it

Smaller; bright red legs and bill base; more heavily streaked

रेडशैंक

Common Greenshank, as the Common Redshank profile puts it

Larger; greenish legs; more robust bill; paler plumage

ग्रीनशैंक

Common Greenshank: A large pale wader with greenish-grey legs, upturned bill, and white rump — is a Common Greenshank. Larger and paler than Redshank, with greenish (not red) legs. Often solitary. When in doubt, check bill angle — Greenshank's bill is subtly upturned.

Common Redshank: A medium wader with unmistakable bright red legs and reddish-orange bill, with white rump visible in flight, probing in mud — is a Common Redshank. The red legs are diagnostic. Greenshanks are paler and larger with greenish legs.
