How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purple Sunbird, as the Purple-rumped Sunbird profile puts it

Purple Sunbird]] (sister species)

Similar; slightly smaller; male has black belly extending onto chest; more purple overall on male; rare in eastern UP

Purple-rumped Sunbird: A small (10–11 cm), iridescent sunbird with the breeding male showing electric blue-purple upper body and deep maroon chest (visible in morning sun), visiting tubular flowers with a decurved bill — is the Purple-rumped Sunbird. In gardens with year-round flowering plants, the species is resident and present every day. The distinctive decurved bill and iridescent colouring separate it from similar-sized flowerpeckers.
