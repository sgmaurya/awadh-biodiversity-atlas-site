Pink Morning Glory or Blue Morning Glory? बेशरम या नीली घुइयाँ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीली घुइयाँBlue Morning GloryIpomoea nil

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue Morning Glory, as the Pink Morning Glory profile puts it

Annual climber; smaller; purple or blue flowers; no woody base

Pink Morning Glory, as the Blue Morning Glory profile puts it

Semi-aquatic woody shrub (not a vine); leaves are simple, lance-shaped; flowers are pale pink

बेहया / बेशर्म — झाड़ीनुमा पौधा, गुलाबी फूल, दलदली भूमि

Blue Morning Glory: Slender twining annual vine with hairy stems, deeply 3-lobed leaves, and large sky-blue or purple funnel flowers that open in the morning and close by afternoon, is the Blue Morning Glory.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Pink Morning GloryBlue Morning Glory
Growth formWoody shrub, 1–3 m tall; hollow stems (fistulosa = hollow)—
LeavesLarge, heart-shaped to ovate; 8–20 cm; soft, velvety texture; pale greenAlternately arranged, simple, broad (5–15 cm), deeply split into three pointed lobes (ivy-like)
FlowersLarge (5–8 cm) funnel-shaped; pale pink to lavender-pink; white throat; open in morning, close by afternoonLarge (5–8 cm wide), trumpet-like, opening bright sky-blue or purple in the morning, fading to pink-purple before closing
StemHollow, woody, greyish-green; exudes white milky latex when brokenCovered in dense, rough, spreading brown hairs (retrorse hairs) that point downward
Seed pods4-valved capsule; brown seeds; seeds have long silky hairs for wind dispersal—
Habitat clueDense thickets along roadsides, pond banks, canals — often 2–3 m wide monocultures—
Plant habit—Slender, fast-growing annual twining vine, climbing up to 2–5 meters over surrounding hedges
Toxicity—TOXIC SEEDS: The black seeds (Kala Dana) contain LSA alkaloids. Do NOT ingest. Highly toxic to humans and cattle.

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
