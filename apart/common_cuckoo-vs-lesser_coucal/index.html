How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Cuckoo, as the Lesser Coucal profile puts it

Cuculus canorus

Slaty-grey plumage with finely barred white underparts; slender pointed wings; tree-dwelling habits

Common Cuckoo: A medium-sized slate-grey (male) or rufous-brown (female) bird calling from exposed tree-tops with the famous loud cuc-koo call is a Common Cuckoo. The distinctive call is diagnostic.

Lesser Coucal: Small, slender coucal with glossy blue-black body and chestnut wings in summer, transforming to cryptic streaked brown plumage in winter.

Locomotion—Clambers deftly through tangled reed stalks and grass stems like a rail
