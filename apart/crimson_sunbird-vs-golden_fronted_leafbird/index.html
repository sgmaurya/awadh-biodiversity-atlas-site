How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson Sunbird, as the Golden-fronted Leafbird profile puts it

Crimson Sunbird]] (Aethopyga siparaja)

Much smaller nectarivore with crimson mantle, yellow rump, and long decurved bill

Crimson Sunbird: The glowing crimson upperparts and breast, bright golden-yellow rump, metallic crown, and elongated central tail feathers distinguish the male Crimson Sunbird from all other sunbirds in northern India.

Golden-fronted Leafbird: The completely grass-green plumage, bright golden-orange forehead patch, and turquoise shoulder epaulet distinguish the Golden-fronted Leafbird from all other canopy passerines.
