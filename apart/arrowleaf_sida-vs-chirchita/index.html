Arrowleaf Sida or Prickly Chaff Flower? अतीबला या चिरचिटा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Prickly Chaff Flower, as the Arrowleaf Sida profile puts it

Leaves opposite; flowers in long stiff terminal spikes; no showy yellow petals

पत्तियाँ आमने-सामने, कांटेदार लंबी बाली

Arrowleaf Sida: Tough fibrous subshrub with alternate rhomboid-lanceolate leaves cuneate and entire at base, bearing pale yellow morning-opening flowers and 8–10 awned mericarps identifies Sida rhombifolia.

Prickly Chaff Flower: Erect roadside herb + opposite hairy leaves + elongated spike + seeds with 2 stiff backwardly-reflexed spiny bracteoles that stick to clothing = Achyranthes aspera. Once you pull these seeds off your clothes, the identification is made.

Height & Habit—Erect, wiry annual/perennial herb or subshrub, 30–100 cm tall; stems square in cross-section

Dispersal—Extreme mechanical stickiness; seeds cling instantly to clothing, fur, and feathers
