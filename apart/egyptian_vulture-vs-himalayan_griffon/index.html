How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Egyptian Vulture, as the Himalayan Griffon profile puts it

Egyptian Vulture]] (Neophron percnopterus)

Smaller (55–70 cm); white with black flight feathers

Egyptian Vulture: Small vulture (smallest of all vultures in India) + predominantly white plumage + contrasting black flight feathers + pale bare yellow head and neck + buoyant soaring = Egyptian Vulture. No other small white raptor should be confused with it.

Himalayan Griffon: Himalayan Griffon is the largest and palest vulture regularly seen, with pale brown body, whitish neck, and yellow eye. If it is darker brown overall, it is likely White-rumped Vulture. If it has a distinctive white rump patch, it is definitely White-rumped, not Himalayan.
