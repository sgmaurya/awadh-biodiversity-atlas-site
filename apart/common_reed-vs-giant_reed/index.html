Common Reed or Giant Reed? सरकंडा या नल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सरकंडाCommon ReedPhragmites karka
नलGiant ReedArundo donax

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Reed, as the Common Reed profile puts it

Leaves are arranged in a flat, fan-like shape; stems are thicker, woody; flowers are denser and lighter

बड़ा नर्कुल — अधिक ऊँचाई, मोटी छड़ी जैसे तने

Common Reed, as the Giant Reed profile puts it

Similar height; but leaves are narrower and greyish; panicle is loose and feathery

सरकंडा — पत्ता पतला, भूरा-भूरा पंख

Common Reed: Giant perennial aquatic grass (2–6 m) with hollow bamboo-like stems, broad leaves wrapping the stem, and terminal feathery purplish-brown flower plumes waving in autumn marsh margins, is the Common Reed (Sarkanda).

Giant Reed: A tall reed (2–4 m) with thick woody hollow stems, long grey-green leaves, and a large feathery golden plume in late summer is Arundo donax.
