How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Painted Globular Frog, as the Variegated Balloon Frog profile puts it

Uperodon taprobanicus

Strikingly patterned with bright reddish-orange or scarlet patches on a dark brownish-black back, and arboreal habits

Painted Globular Frog: A round, short-legged, tiny-mouthed frog painted with orange blotches on brown, that inflates when handled, is the balloon frog.

Variegated Balloon Frog: Small plump burrowing frog with dark brown blotches on a greyish-brown ground, tiny mouth, and two spade-like metatarsal tubercles on the hindfoot.

Size50–75 mm; a stout, round frog—
ShapeBody globular, head small and pointed, mouth tiny, limbs short; cannot leap far—
ColourDark brown to grey-brown with large irregular orange, red or yellow blotches, especially along the sides and on the limbs; belly pale and spotted—
SkinSmooth above, slightly granular—
FeetFingers and toes with small discs at the tips; two spades on the heel for digging—
DefenceInflates with air to a ball, lowers its head and presents its back; the skin exudes a sticky, bitter fluid—
CallA deep, loud, resonant "whoooonk", repeated, from a pool after heavy rain; like a bullfrog's but shorter—
Body size—Small to medium-sized, plump, pear-shaped burrowing frog
Coloration—Pale greyish-brown or olive above, adorned with irregular dark brown or black blotches
Head & Mouth—Small, narrow head with an obtuse snout and very small mouth opening, lacking teeth
Skin texture—Smooth or feebly warty on the back, with small whitish granules on the flanks
Digits—Fingers free with blunt tips; toes short with rudimentary basal webbing
Metatarsal tubercles—Two shovel-shaped metatarsal tubercles on the hindfoot, adapted for backward burrowing
