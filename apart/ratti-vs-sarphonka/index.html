Rosary Pea or Wild Indigo? रत्ती या सरफोंका?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wild Indigo, as the Rosary Pea profile puts it

Wild indigo]] (Tephrosia purpurea)

Silvery leaves; purple flowers; pods linear and flattened

Rosary Pea: A thin twining climber with mimosa-like tiny leaflets, pale yellow flowers, and small beaked pods containing hard, shiny, scarlet-and-black seeds is ratti.

Wild Indigo: Low erect herb + feathery compound leaves (8–15 leaflets per leaf) + pink-purple pea-like flowers in short racemes + hairy linear pods = Tephrosia purpurea. The nitrogen-fixing nodules on the roots confirm the identification if you dig carefully.
