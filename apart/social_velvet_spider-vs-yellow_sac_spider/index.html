Social Velvet Spider or Yellow Sac Spider? सामूहिक मकड़ी या पीली थैली मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow Sac Spider, as the Social Velvet Spider profile puts it

Solitary; pale yellow-green with black-tipped jaws; hunts by wandering at night

पीली थैली मकड़ी — पीला-हरा रंग, एकाकी जीवन

Social Velvet Spider: Dull grey-brown, velvety spider living with dozens of others inside a large, dense, shared silk sheet wrapped around thorny branches is the Social Velvet Spider.

Yellow Sac Spider: Small, pale yellow-green spider (8–10 mm) with distinct jet-black-tipped jaws, hiding in a tube-like white silk sac inside a rolled leaf during the day, is the Yellow Sac Spider (C. melanostomum). Note: C. triviale and C. indicum are co-occurring sister species in UP, separable only by microscopy.
