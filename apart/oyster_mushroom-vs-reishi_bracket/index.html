Oyster Mushroom or Reishi? ढींगरी या रेशी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ढींगरीOyster MushroomPleurotus ostreatus
रेशीReishiGanoderma lucidum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Reishi, as the Oyster Mushroom profile puts it

Hard, woody, shiny upper surface; pores beneath (not gills); perennial

Side by side आमने-सामने

Oyster MushroomReishi
CapFan-shaped to broadly convex; 5–20 cm; smooth wavy margin—
Cap colourGrey to blue-grey when young; fades to cream or pale brown with age—
GillsWhite; crowded; decurrent (running down stalk); forking near base—
StalkShort (1–3 cm); off-centre or lateral; white; firm—
Spore printWhite to lilac-white—
GrowthAlways in overlapping tiered clusters on wood — never from soil—
SmellPleasant, distinctly mushroomy—
Bracket Form—Shelf-like bracket, 5–30 cm wide, kidney-shaped to fan-shaped with a lateral stalk
Upper Surface—Deep red-brown to purplish-black, concentric zones, highly glossy and lacquered-looking
Pore Surface—Creamy white below, pores tiny (4–6 per mm); turns brownish when bruised
Stipe (Stem)—Often present as a dark, twisted, shiny lateral stalk
Substrate—Found at the base of dead or dying hardwood trunks (attached to tree bases or roots)
Texture—Hard, tough, and woody when dry; non-edible

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
