White-backed Planthopper or Zigzag Leafhopper? व्हाइट-बैक्ड प्लांटहॉपर या जिग-जैग लीफहॉपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-backed Planthopper, as the Zigzag Leafhopper profile puts it

Sogatella furcifera

Delphacid planthopper with bright white stripe down the back; lives at the base of stems rather than upper leaves

White-backed Planthopper: Minute dark planthopper with a prominent creamy-white stripe down the middle of the back, congregating in dense colonies at the base of rice stems.

Zigzag Leafhopper: Small, slender, pale leafhopper found on upper rice foliage, bearing a bold, dark brown zigzag longitudinal stripe down its folded wings.

Body form—Slender, elongate-elliptic leafhopper with smoothly rounded crown tapering towards apex
Coloration—Whitish-grey to pale yellowish-ochraceous ground colour contrasting with bold dark zigzag stripes
Eyes—Dark brownish multifaceted compound eyes situated laterally on the rounded head
Feeding behavior—Rests along the upper leaf blades of rice, hopping swiftly sideways when approached
