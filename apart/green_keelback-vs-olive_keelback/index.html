Green Keelback or Olive Keelback? ग्रीन कीलबैक या ऑलिव कीलबैक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Keelback, as the Olive Keelback profile puts it

Rhabdophis plumbicolor

Heavy-bodied terrestrial snake with uniform bright grass-green coloration, heavily keeled scales, and chevron neck collar in young

Green Keelback: A bright green, keeled snake found near water is a Green Keelback. Darker, banded, or checkered water snakes are different species. The distinctive green coloration and keeled texture separate it from other water snakes.

Olive Keelback: Slender, docile water snake with uniform olive-green unspotted back, bright lemon-yellow belly, and upward-directed nostrils in village ponds.

Ventral coloration—Bright lemon-yellow, pale orange, or rich cream along the belly and lower flanks
Nostrils—Slit-like, positioned high on the dorsal surface of the snout for surface breathing
Scalation—Midbody scale rows 19; dorsal scales smooth anteriorly, becoming distinctly keeled towards the tail (Smith 1943)
Disposition—Inoffensive, docile snake that flattens its body when cornered but rarely attempts to bite
