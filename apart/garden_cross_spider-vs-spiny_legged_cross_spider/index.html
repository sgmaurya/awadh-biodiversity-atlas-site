How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Garden Cross Spider, as the Spiny-legged Cross Spider profile puts it

Argiope pulchella

Much larger body length; distinct pentagonal abdomen with bold yellow and black bands

Garden Cross Spider: Small black-and-white striped orb-weaver with distinctive red or orange spots on abdomen, in low vegetation, web 25–35 cm, = Argiope pulchella.

Spiny-legged Cross Spider: Small colorful orb-weaver with spiny legs and cross-patterned abdomen; sits in a four-rayed cross on a decorated orb web.

Abdomen pattern—Oval, flattened abdomen marked with bold transverse yellow, white, and brownish-black zigzag bands
Leg structure—Stout, yellowish-brown legs armed with prominent, erect black spines
Web decoration—Vertical orb web often fitted with a white zigzag silk stabilimentum band
Resting habit—Sits at the hub with legs held together in pairs, resembling a four-pointed cross
Defense reaction—Drops instantly from the web and may alter abdominal color when alarmed
