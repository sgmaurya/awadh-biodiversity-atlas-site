Muskmelon or Watermelon? खरबूजा या तरबूज?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Watermelon, as the Muskmelon profile puts it

Watermelon]])

Fruit is much larger (5–10 kg); rind is dark green with light stripes; flesh is red or yellow; less aromatic

Muskmelon: A trailing vine with deeply lobed leaves, yellow flowers, and round netted sweet fruits with orange flesh and musky aroma distinguishes muskmelon.

Watermelon: A sprawling vine with large deeply lobed leaves, large bright yellow flowers, and huge smooth striped melons with red juicy flesh is watermelon; it is the most common summer fruit in village fields.
