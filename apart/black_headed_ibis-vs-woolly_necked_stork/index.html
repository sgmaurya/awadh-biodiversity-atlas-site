Black-headed Ibis or Asian Woolly-necked Stork? मुंडा या लगलग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लगलगAsian Woolly-necked StorkCiconia episcopus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Woolly-necked Stork, as the Black-headed Ibis profile puts it

Larger; dark plumage; white neck patch

काला बड़ा सारस

Black-headed Ibis: A medium-sized white bird with a completely bare black head and long curved bill, feeding in flocks — is a Black-headed Ibis. The bare head is definitive.

Asian Woolly-necked Stork: A large black-and-white stork with a distinctive white woolly collar at the base of the neck — is a Woolly-necked Stork. The collar is unique and diagnostic.
