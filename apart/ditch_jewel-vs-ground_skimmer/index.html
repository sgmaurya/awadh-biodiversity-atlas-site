Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ditch Jewel, as the Ground Skimmer profile puts it

Ditch Jewel]])

Orange wings with a broad amber patch; does not perch on ground; lives in stagnant drains

Ditch Jewel: Small size, bright yellow-orange body in males with a distinct orange band in the middle of each wing, and habitat preference for polluted drains identify the Ditch Jewel.

Ground Skimmer: Small size, mature male has a powder-blue body with a black tail tip, female is yellow-brown, and habit of perching flat on the bare ground identify the Ground Skimmer.
