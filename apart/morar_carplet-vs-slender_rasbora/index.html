How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Morar Carplet: An elongate silvery cyprinid lacking barbels, with a blunt snout, an inferior crescent-shaped mouth bearing a sharp horny edge, and a deeply curved lateral line, is Cabdio morar.

Slender Rasbora: A slender, elongate silvery cyprinid lacking barbels, marked with a bold continuous blue-black mid-lateral stripe edged in gold running from eye to caudal tip, is Rasbora daniconius.
