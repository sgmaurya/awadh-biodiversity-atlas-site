Crescent Dictyopharid or Rust-colored Flatid? चंद्राकार डिक्टियोफेरिड या कत्थई चपटा फुदका?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rust-colored Flatid, as the Crescent Dictyopharid profile puts it

Broad rust-brown wings held tent-like; opaque; head truncate, without horn

कत्थई-भूरे खड़े पंख, कोई सींग नहीं

Crescent Dictyopharid, as the Rust-colored Flatid profile puts it

Head with long upturned horn; wings transparent with crescent band

सिर पर लंबा मुड़ा हुआ सींग, पारदर्शी पंख

Crescent Dictyopharid: Slender wetland planthopper with an upturned cylindrical head horn and transparent hyaline wings bearing a prominent dark brown crescent-shaped band across the tip identifies Orthopagus lunulifer.

Rust-colored Flatid: Broad wedge-shaped, moth-like planthopper with uniform rust-brown wings held vertically tent-like over the body and densely reticulate costal margins identifies Flata ferrugata.
