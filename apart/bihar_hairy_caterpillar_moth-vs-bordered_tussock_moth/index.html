Bihar Hairy Caterpillar Moth or Bordered Tussock Moth? बिहार भुआ पतंगा या किनारेदार टसर पतंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

Bihar Hairy Caterpillar Moth, as the Bordered Tussock Moth profile puts it

Wings pale straw-yellow with oblique speck rows; abdomen bright crimson-red

पंख हल्के पीले, पेट गहरा लाल और काला

Bihar Hairy Caterpillar Moth: A straw-buff moth with an oblique row of fine black dots across the forewing and a brilliant crimson-red abdomen spotted with black identifies Spilarctia obliqua.

Bordered Tussock Moth: A whitish to pale grey tussock moth with feathery male antennae and dark wavy submarginal borders on the wings identifies Lymantria marginata.
