Indian Gooseberry or Tamarind? आँवला या इमली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

आँवलाIndian GooseberryPhyllanthus emblica
इमलीTamarindTamarindus indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tamarind, as the Indian Gooseberry profile puts it

Leaves are true compound pinnate leaves with rounded leaflets; bark is dark brown, rough, and cracked; fruit is a brown pod

इमली — खुरदरी छाल, इमली की फली

Indian Gooseberry: Feather-like rows of tiny, needle-like green leaflets, smooth peeling greenish-grey bark, and round pale-green sour fruits marked with six vertical lines identify Phyllanthus emblica.

Side by side आमने-सामने
