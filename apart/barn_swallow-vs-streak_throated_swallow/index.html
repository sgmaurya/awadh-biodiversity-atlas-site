How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Barn Swallow, as the Streak-throated Swallow profile puts it

Barn Swallow]] (Hirundo rustica)

Larger; deeply forked tail; chestnut throat and crown; no streaks on white underparts

Barn Swallow: Small blue-black aerial bird with a chestnut throat and forehead, a dark breast collar, a deeply forked tail with long streamers, nesting under village roofs, is a Barn Swallow.

Streak-throated Swallow: A small, dark blue-black backed swallow with white underparts and distinctive black streaks on the white throat is a Streak-throated Swallow. Larger swallows with different throat patterns or longer tail wires are different species.
