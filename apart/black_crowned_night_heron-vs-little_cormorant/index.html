Black-crowned Night Heron or Little Cormorant? रातबगुला या पनकौवा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-crowned Night Heron, as the Little Cormorant profile puts it

Nocturnal; shorter neck; different bill shape; grey-and-black plumage

रात बगुला

Black-crowned Night Heron: Stocky, short-necked heron with a glossy black crown and back, grey wings, white belly, and large red eyes, roosting in groups during the day, is a Black-crowned Night Heron.

Little Cormorant: A small, all-black diving bird in freshwater that surfaces far from where it dived and perches with wings spread open — is a Little Cormorant. The glossy black colour and diving habit are definitive.
