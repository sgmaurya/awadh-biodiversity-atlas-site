How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-necked Stork, as the Bengal Florican profile puts it

Black-necked Stork]] (Ephippiorhynchus asiaticus)

Huge stork with massive dagger bill, bright red legs, glossy black neck, and white body; feeds in deep open wetlands

Bengal Florican: The male Bengal Florican's velvety black head, body, and neck contrasted with dazzling white wings, and the female's mottled tawny-buff bustard build, distinguish it from long-legged wetland storks and cranes.

Black-necked Stork: Black-necked Stork is unmistakable: large stork with black head, neck, and back; white belly; brilliant blue-grey legs; stout yellow bill. No other large stork in the region matches this pattern.
