Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker or Yellow-crowned Woodpecker? ब्राउन-कैप्ड पिग्मी कठफोड़वा या येलो-क्राउन्ड कठफोड़वा?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-crowned Woodpecker, as the Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker profile puts it

Yellow-crowned Woodpecker]] (Leiopicus mahrattensis)

Larger (18 cm); yellow on crown; larger bill

Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker, as the Yellow-crowned Woodpecker profile puts it

Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker]] (Yungipicus nanus)

Smaller; lacks yellow crest; brown cap instead; black and white wings

Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker: Brown-capped Pygmy is the smallest woodpecker in the region with a brown cap (both sexes), white back with dark barring, and restless behaviour on thin twigs. No other species combines all these features at this size.

Yellow-crowned Woodpecker: Yellow-crowned woodpecker is a small woodpecker with a distinctive golden crest (in males) and golden rump. If smaller and lacking the golden features, it may be a pygmy woodpecker. If larger with a grey head, it is a grey-headed woodpecker.
