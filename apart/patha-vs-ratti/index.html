Patha or Rosary Pea? पाठा या रत्ती?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पाठाPathaCissampelos pareira
रत्तीRosary PeaAbrus precatorius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rosary Pea, as the Patha profile puts it

Leaves are compound and pinnate (many small leaflets); fruits are hard and bright red-black beads

रत्ती — कई छोटी पत्तियाँ; बीज लाल-काले, कठोर

Patha: A soft-stemmed climber with distinctive large, round, velvety leaves that feel like cloth, twining over hedges and producing small black fruits is patha.

Rosary Pea: A thin twining climber with mimosa-like tiny leaflets, pale yellow flowers, and small beaked pods containing hard, shiny, scarlet-and-black seeds is ratti.
