How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Convolvulus Hawkmoth, as the Rosetta Hawkmoth profile puts it

Much larger; grey wings; abdomen boldly barred with pink and black

बहुत बड़ा, पंख सलेटी, पेट पर गुलाबी-काली धारियां

Convolvulus Hawkmoth: A medium-sized grey-brown hawkmoth with white cross-bands on the wings, hovering at morning glory or related flowers like a hummingbird, with a long coiled proboscis, is Agrius convolvuli.

Rosetta Hawkmoth: A sleek buff-brown hawkmoth with an oblique streaked forewing, a tiny black discal dot, and pinkish-red on the hindwing base identifies Hippotion rosetta.
