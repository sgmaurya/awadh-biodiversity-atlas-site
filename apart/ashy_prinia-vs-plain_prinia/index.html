Ashy Prinia or Plain Prinia? राखी फुदकी या मैदान फुदकी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plain Prinia, as the Ashy Prinia profile puts it

Paler; longer tail even in breeding; more grassland-associated; call slightly different

Ashy Prinia, as the Plain Prinia profile puts it

Ashy Prinia]] (Prinia socialis)

Ashy-grey back and head; orange underparts; tail is slightly shorter; louder ringing calls

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.
