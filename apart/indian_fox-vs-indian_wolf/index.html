Indian Fox or Indian Wolf? लोमड़ी या भेड़िया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Wolf, as the Indian Fox profile puts it

Variable sizes and colors; snout is wider; tail lacks the dense uniform black-tipped brush; bolder behavior

आवारा कुत्ता — घरेलू बनावट, शर्मीले व्यवहार का अभाव

Indian Fox: Small grey-sandy native dog-like animal with a very bushy tail ending in a distinct solid black tip, digging burrows in dry elevated fields, is the Indian Fox (Lomri).

Indian Wolf: Large canid (2–3 times heavier than a medium dog), grey-brown or buff coat, large head, yellow eyes, bushy dark-tipped tail, nocturnal solitary or paired movement in open grassland, with large tracks (~6–7 cm) in a near-straight line = Indian Wolf.
