How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Long-whiskered Catfish, as the Giant River Catfish profile puts it

Long-whiskered Catfish]] (Sperata aor)

Smaller (35–60 cm); similar barbel pattern but more slender body

Giant River Catfish, as the Long-whiskered Catfish profile puts it

Giant River Catfish]] (Sperata seenghala)

Larger (60–150 cm); 4 barbels but different colour pattern; body more robust

Giant River Catfish: The largest catfish in the region, with 4 barbels and reaching 60–150 cm or more. No other catfish grows to this size in eastern UP.

Long-whiskered Catfish: A large catfish (35–60 cm) with 4 barbels (whiskers), two of which are very long (reaching beyond gill slits), is a Long-whiskered Catfish. The long outer barbels are diagnostic.
