Oriental Pratincole or Small Pratincole? ओरिएंटल प्रैटिंकोल या स्मॉल प्रैटिंकोल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Pratincole, as the Oriental Pratincole profile puts it

Small Pratincole]] (Glareola lactea)

Noticeably smaller, pale sandy-grey plumage, black wing-linings without chestnut, shallow-forked tail (Baker 1929, p. 92)

Oriental Pratincole, as the Small Pratincole profile puts it

Larger (25 cm); darker plumage; deep forked tail

बड़ा प्रैटिंकोल

Oriental Pratincole: The combination of olive-brown upperparts, deep chestnut underwing-coverts, forked tail with white base, and black-bordered buff throat identifies the Oriental Pratincole (Baker 1929, p. 91).

Small Pratincole: Small grey-brown bird with white belly, long pointed wings, flying erratically over river catching insects = Small Pratincole. More compact and grey than terns, less colourful than larger pratincoles.

Size—Small pratincole, 17–18 cm; compact body
Plumage—Grey-brown above; white throat and belly
Head—Small, dark lore stripe; white supercilium faint

Legs—Short; dark coloured
Flight—Highly agile; tern-like flight; rapid wing beats
