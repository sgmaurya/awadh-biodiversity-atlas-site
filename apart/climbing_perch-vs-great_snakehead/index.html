Climbing Perch or Great Snakehead? कवई या सौर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कवईClimbing PerchAnabas testudineus
सौरGreat SnakeheadChanna marulius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Snakehead, as the Climbing Perch profile puts it

Longer, cylindrical, with a flat snake-like head; also air-breathing and walking; no spiny serrated gill cover

लंबी, सिर साँप जैसा

Climbing Perch: A hard, spiny, grey fish with a saw-edged gill cover that survives out of water and walks on wet ground is the climbing perch.

Great Snakehead: Large size (up to 1m), snake-like head with plate scales, dark blotches on flanks, and a prominent black ocellus ringed in orange on the upper caudal fin base identify the Great Snakehead.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Climbing PerchGreat Snakehead
Size10–20 cm, occasionally 25 cmVery large fish (typically 45–100 cm total length); can exceed 100 cm and 10 kg
ShapeOblong, somewhat compressed, with a large head and a mouth with small teeth—
ColourOlive to grey-brown above, paler below; a dark spot at the base of the tail and another behind the gill cover—
FinsLong dorsal and anal fins with strong spines; a rounded tailLong, single dorsal and anal fins running down the back; tail fin is rounded and dark
Gill coverThe rear edge serrated with sharp spines, which it spreads when caught: the "climbing" tool and the reason it is hard to hold—
BehaviourGulps air at the surface; survives in a bucket; crawls on land after rain—
Head—Depressed, snake-like head covered in large, plate-like scales; eyes set forward
Colouration—Greyish-green to dark brown dorsally; pale belly; 5–6 dark blotches on the flanks
Ocellus—A distinctive black spot ringed with bright orange (eye-spot) at the upper base of the tail

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
