How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Maidenhair Fern, as the Chinese Brake Fern profile puts it

Tiny fan-shaped leaflets; black wiry rachis; water-repellent leaves

बहुत छोटी पत्तियाँ, काला डंठल

Chinese Brake Fern: A delicate fern with linearly arranged, narrow leaflets in a ladder-like pattern, and linear sori along the leaflet margins is Chinese Brake Fern.

Maidenhair Fern: A fern with a jet-black wiry stalk and tiny fan-shaped leaflets that repel water is maidenhair fern (Adiantum capillus-veneris).
