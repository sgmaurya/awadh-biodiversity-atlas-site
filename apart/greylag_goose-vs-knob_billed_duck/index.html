How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greylag Goose, as the Knob-billed Duck profile puts it

Greylag Goose]] (Anser anser)

Similar size but lacks knob; greyish plumage

Greylag Goose: A large, overall grey-brown goose with an orange bill and orange legs, flying with loud honking calls — is a Greylag Goose. Bar-headed Goose is paler with bold black head bars.

Knob-billed Duck: The Knob-billed Duck is the largest duck in the region and the only one with a prominent black knob on the forehead (males). If a large dark duck with a forehead knob is seen, it is a Knob-billed Duck.
