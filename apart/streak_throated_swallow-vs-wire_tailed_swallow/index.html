How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wire-tailed Swallow, as the Streak-throated Swallow profile puts it

Wire-tailed Swallow]] (Vsigata)

Long filamentary tail wires; rufous cap; no throat streaks

Streak-throated Swallow: A small, dark blue-black backed swallow with white underparts and distinctive black streaks on the white throat is a Streak-throated Swallow. Larger swallows with different throat patterns or longer tail wires are different species.

Wire-tailed Swallow: Small swallow + chestnut cap and rump + dark blue back + extremely long tail wires + near water = Wire-tailed Swallow. The tail wires are diagnostic and unmistakable.
