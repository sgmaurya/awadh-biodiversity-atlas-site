Pink Shower Tree or Shisham? पिंक शावर या शीशम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पिंक शावरPink Shower TreeCassia javanica
शीशमShishamDalbergia sissoo

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Shisham, as the Pink Shower Tree profile puts it

Leaves pinnate with 3–5 round alternate leaflets; flowers minute cream

पत्तियाँ 3–5 गोल पर्णक वाली, छोटे फूल

Pink Shower Tree: Spreading parasol crown with pinnate leaves, large corymbs of fragrant rose-pink flowers fading to white in early summer, and long cylindrical woody pods distinguish Cassia javanica.

Shisham: Compound leaves with 3 to 5 alternating, roundish leaflets ending in a sharp point, zig-zag leaf stalks, and thin papery pods identify Dalbergia sissoo.
