Indian Robin or Pied Bushchat? कालचुरी या पड्डू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पड्डूPied BushchatSaxicola caprata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the Indian Robin profile puts it

Pied Bushchat]] (Saxicola caprata) — if found

Male black and white; female brown and white; smaller (13 cm); grassland bird

Indian Robin, as the Pied Bushchat profile puts it

Indian Robin]] (Copsychus fulicatus)

Sandy-black body; rusty-red patch under the base of the tail; holds tail cocked up high

Indian Robin: Small upright bird with characteristic tail-cocking + glossy black male with white rump (or rufous female with white rump) + clear whistled song = Indian Robin. The combination of tail-cocking behaviour and white rump is diagnostic.

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.
