Common Teal or Mallard? छोटी मुर्गाबी या नीलसर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीलसरMallardAnas platyrhynchos

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mallard, as the Common Teal profile puts it

Much larger (56–65 cm); male has green head, not chestnut

दुग्ध हंस

Common Teal, as the Mallard profile puts it

Much smaller; male has chestnut head with green eye-stripe

बहुत छोटी, लाल सिर, हरी पट्टी

Common Teal: Common Teal is the smallest winter duck, with males showing a chestnut head and green eye-stripe. If the duck is larger and the bill spatulate (spoon-like), it is a Shoveler. If the head is glossy green (not chestnut), it is a Mallard.

Mallard: A large duck with a glossy green head and white chest (males) — is a Mallard. This is by far the most familiar duck species globally. Females shown blue speculum in flight and paired with green-headed males in winter.
