Water Chestnut or Water Hyacinth? सिंघाड़ा या जलकुंभी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जलीय पौधे और घास सिंघाड़ाWater ChestnutTrapa bispinosa
जलकुंभीWater HyacinthEichhornia crassipes

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Hyacinth, as the Water Chestnut profile puts it

Swollen spongy leaf stalks but leaves are rounded and smooth-edged; large violet flower spikes

जलकुंभी — बैंगनी फूल, गोल पत्तियां

Water Chestnut: Rhombic saw-toothed leaves arranged in flat rosettes, with spongy air-swollen stalks and a two-horned hard fruit, uniquely identify the Indian Water Chestnut.

Water Hyacinth: Floating mat + round shiny leaves + swollen spongy petioles + feathery purple-black roots = water hyacinth. The inflated petiole is unmistakable once seen.
