Hairy Flower Chafer or Variegated Flower Chafer? हेयरी फ्लावर शेफर या वेरिगेेटेड फ्लावर शेफर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Variegated Flower Chafer, as the Hairy Flower Chafer profile puts it

Gametis versicolor

Variegated maroon and white elytra; completely lacking shaggy dorsal hairs and head horn

Hairy Flower Chafer, as the Variegated Flower Chafer profile puts it

Chiloloba acuta

Brilliant metallic emerald green covered in dense shaggy erect hairs; acute head horn

Hairy Flower Chafer: Metallic green flower chafer covered in a dense coat of shaggy brownish hairs, with a sharp vertical horn on the forehead.

Variegated Flower Chafer: Flattened flower chafer with variable creamy-white blotches on maroon or black elytra, flying with closed elytra.

Hairy Flower ChaferVariegated Flower Chafer
ColorationBrilliant metallic emerald-green to golden-green integument—
PubescenceDense, shaggy coat of erect brownish-yellow setae covering entire dorsum—

Flight mechanismRapid flight with closed elytra, characteristic of cetoniines—
Dorsal pattern—Highly variable, maroon, castaneous, or black marked with creamy-white maculae
Habitus—Compact, flattened-oval, typical broad cetoniine flower beetle silhouette
Flight habit—Flies swiftly with elytra closed over back via lateral posthumeral sinuses
Mouthparts—Maxillae with dense brush-like hairs adapted for harvesting pollen and nectar
Clinging grip—Robust legs with strongly curved tarsal claws clinging tightly to blossoms
