Indian Bull Frog or Jerdon's Bullfrog? पहाड़ मेंढक या चित्तीदार मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jerdon's Bullfrog, as the Indian Bull Frog profile puts it

Similar; slightly different coloring; drier habitat; less common in UP

जेर्डन का मेंढक

Indian Bull Frog, as the Jerdon's Bullfrog profile puts it

Much larger (up to 170 mm); breeding males turn bright yellow with blue vocal sacs; long, slender legs

भारतीय बुलफ्रॉग — बहुत बड़ा, चमकीला पीला-हरा

Indian Bull Frog: Very large green frog in pond + bright yellow breeding male + deep roaring call + monsoon season eastern UP = Indian Bull Frog. The size and yellow breeding color together are unique.

Jerdon's Bullfrog: Large size (6–10 cm), rough warty skin, pointed head with large tympanum, fully webbed toes, and a prominent spade-like metatarsal tubercle on the hind foot.
