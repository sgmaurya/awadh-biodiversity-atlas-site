How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the White-throated Bushchat profile puts it

Pied Bushchat]] (Saxicola caprata)

Male has orange-buff rump, not white; different distribution

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.

White-throated Bushchat: White-throated Bushchat males are distinguished by the combination of black cap, black back, white throat, and white rump. The white throat is the key diagnostic feature from all other similar species. Females require closer inspection but retain the distinctive white throat.
