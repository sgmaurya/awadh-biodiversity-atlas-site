How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Tailorbird, as the Plain Prinia profile puts it

Common Tailorbird]] (Orthotomus sutorius)

Bright green back; rust-coloured crown; long central tail feathers; hops in bushes, not grass

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.

Bill & LegsLong, slender, slightly decurved pale bill; pinkish-flesh legs—
BehaviourCreeps actively through dense low bushes; constructs stitched-leaf nestsConstantly hops in low vegetation; flicks and wags its tail from side to side
VoiceLoud, persistent, repetitive tuwit-tuwit-tuwit calls, heard year-round—
Colouration—Plain, unstreaked olive-brown upperparts; warm buffy-white throat, breast, and belly
Supercilium—A narrow, pale whitish stripe running from the bill to just behind the eye
Bare Parts—Bill is black in summer, turning brown in winter; legs are pale brownish-pink
