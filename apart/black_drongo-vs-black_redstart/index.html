Black Drongo or Black Redstart? भुजंगा या थिरथिरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भुजंगाBlack DrongoDicrurus macrocercus
थिरथिराBlack RedstartPhoenicurus ochruros

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Redstart, as the Black Drongo profile puts it

Much smaller; orange-red tail; winter visitor; pumps tail

छोटा, लाल पूँछ वाला

Black Drongo: A glossy black open-country bird on a wire, with a deep V-shaped forked tail and repeated sallies for insects, is Black Drongo in eastern UP farmland.

Black Redstart: A small black (male) or dark grey-brown (female) songbird with a striking orange-red tail base and rump, on rocky or open areas — is a Black Redstart. The orange-red tail is diagnostic. Males are striking and unmistakable.
