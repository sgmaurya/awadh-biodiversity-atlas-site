Oriental Fruit Fly, as the Peach Fruit Fly profile puts it

Oriental Fruit Fly]])

Very similar; wing patterns differ subtly; overlapping range and hosts; expert examination needed

Oriental Fruit Fly: Small reddish-brown fly with dark S-shaped markings on wings, found around ripening or fermenting mango and guava, with larvae tunnelling inside the fruit and causing internal rot.

Peach Fruit Fly: Small yellow-orange fly with pale banded wings, puncturing developing stone fruits (peach, ber) to lay eggs; infested fruit shows fermentation and rot with visible larvae inside: Peach Fruit Fly (Bactrocera zonata).
