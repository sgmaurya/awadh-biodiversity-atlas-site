How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Owlet, as the Brown Boobook profile puts it

Spotted Owlet]] (Athene brama)

Slightly smaller; white-spotted plumage; darker overall; different call

Brown Boobook: A small brown owl with large yellow eyes, no ear tufts, and distinctive boo-boo-boo or book-book call is a Brown Boobook. The lack of ear tufts and the specific call pattern are diagnostic.

Spotted Owlet: Small size, white spots on greyish-brown upperparts, yellow eyes, lack of ear tufts, and loud chattering calls in pairs confirm the Spotted Owlet.
