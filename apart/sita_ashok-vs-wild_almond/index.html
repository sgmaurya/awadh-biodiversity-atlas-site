Ashoka or Wild Almond? सीता अशोक या जंगली बादाम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सीता अशोकAshokaSaraca asoca
जंगली बादामWild AlmondSterculia foetida

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashoka, as the Wild Almond profile puts it

Very long, narrow, simple leaves; no lobing

बहुत लंबी, सँकरी, सरल पत्तियाँ

Ashoka: A tree with new pink-red pinnate leaves and small orange-red flowers directly on the trunk, in a temple garden, is the sacred ashoka.

Wild Almond: A large tree with enormous palm-shaped leaves split into 5–7 lobes and distinctive boat-shaped reddish seed pods splitting to release almond-like seeds is Wild Almond.

Side by side आमने-सामने

AshokaWild Almond
SizeSemi-evergreen tree, 6–12 m tall, with a dense, rounded crownLarge deciduous tree, 15–25 m tall, broad spreading crown
LeavesPinnate, 30–60 cm, with 6–10 pairs of elliptic leaflets, 8–15 cm; young leaves copper to pink-red, turning dark greenLarge, palmately lobed, 15–30 cm across, with 5–7 lobes
FlowersClusters of small, bright orange-red to yellow, fragrant flowers in dense bunches on the trunk and older branches (cauliflorous), December–MarchSmall, dark reddish-brown, clustered, malodorous (pungent smell)
FruitPod-like follicles, 15–25 cm long, flat, dark brown, ripening April–June4–5 boat-shaped follicles, 7–10 cm long, reddish, splitting to release seeds
BarkDark grey, smooth when young, becoming furrowedGreyish-brown, slightly rough
The testLook for the distinctive young pink leaves and cauliflorous orange flowersHuge palmate leaves and large boat-shaped seed pods splitting open identify it distinctly
Seeds—Large, oval, smooth, 2–3 cm long, resembling almonds, red or brown

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
