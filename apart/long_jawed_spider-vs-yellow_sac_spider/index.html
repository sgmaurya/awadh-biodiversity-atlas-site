How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Long-jawed Orb-weaver, as the Yellow Sac Spider profile puts it

Long-jawed Orb-weaver]] (Tetragnatha mandibulata)

Elongated body; extremely long chelicerae; builds horizontal webs over water

Long-jawed Orb-weaver: Slender, straw-yellow spider with very long legs and forward-projecting jaws, resting in a stick-like pose on reeds or building horizontal webs over water, is the Long-jawed Orb-weaver.

Yellow Sac Spider: Small, pale yellow-green spider (8–10 mm) with distinct jet-black-tipped jaws, hiding in a tube-like white silk sac inside a rolled leaf during the day, is the Yellow Sac Spider (C. melanostomum). Note: C. triviale and C. indicum are co-occurring sister species in UP, separable only by microscopy.
