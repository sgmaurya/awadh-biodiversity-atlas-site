How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rain Tree, as the Copperpod profile puts it

Rain Tree]] (Samanea saman)

Larger leaflets; pink and white flowers; umbrella-shaped crown

Copperpod: A large, fast-growing tree with finely feathery bipinnate leaves and spectacular golden-yellow flowers with orange-red markings in May–June. One of the brightest-flowering trees in the district.

Rain Tree: A massive, spreading tree with umbrella-shaped crown, feathery leaves that fold at dusk and in rain, pink and white silky flowers, and long flat sweet pods. One of the tallest and broadest trees in the district.
