Common Liverwort or Star Jelly? काई (गुच्छेदार) या काई (ज़मीन की जेली वाली काई)?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

काई (गुच्छेदार)Common LiverwortMarchantia polymorpha
काई (ज़मीन की जेली वाली काई)Star JellyNostoc commune

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Star Jelly, as the Common Liverwort profile puts it

Jelly-like, no shape, gone in a day of sun

जेली, आकार नहीं

Common Liverwort: A flat, fleshy green mat with lobed edges and umbrella-like stalked discs in the centre, on damp walls or rocks, is a liverwort of the genus Marchantia.

Star Jelly: Olive-green rubbery jelly lying loose on bare soil the morning after rain, gone to black crust in the sun, is Nostoc.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common LiverwortStar Jelly
FormFlat green thallus (plant body), 1–3 cm across, lobed or fan-shaped, arranged in rosettes—
LobesRounded, notched at the margin, overlapping like roof tiles—
UndersidePale, with root-hairs (rhizoids) and ventral scales; no true roots—
Reproductive structuresUmbrella-like stalked discs (gametophores) in the centre; male or female—
TextureFleshy, gelatinous, moist; dries to a dark scrap, revives when wetted—
ColourBright green to yellowish-green—
Wet—Rubbery, gelatinous sheets or lobed blobs 1–10 cm across, olive-green to blue-green or brownish, lying loose on soil
Dry—Shrinks to a thin, brittle, black or dark brown crust that looks like scraps of burnt paper
Feel—Firm jelly, slippery, slightly elastic; no roots, no leaves, no smell
Under a lens—Made of countless beaded threads in jelly; a hand lens shows the granular look
Where—Bare damp soil in the open: courtyards, path edges, lawns, bunds, field surfaces
When—Appears within hours of rain and vanishes into crust within a day or two of sun

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
