How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rusty Millipede, as the Forest Leaf Woodlouse profile puts it

Red colour; many leg pairs (>60); cylindrical body; much larger

Side by side आमने-सामने

Forest Leaf WoodlouseRusty Millipede
Size8–12 mm length; 4–5 mm width — smaller than a fingernail2–3 cm length; 2–3 mm diameter; cylindrical
BodyOval-elongated; 7 visible thoracic segments (pereonites); slightly flattened—
ColourMottled grey-brown to olive-brown with pale yellowish lateral patchesBright brick-red to rust-orange — unique; no other common UP millipede has this colouration
Legs7 pairs; visible from above; slender and relatively long~80–100 pairs; pale cream-yellow; two pairs per body segment
MovementFast — dashes immediately when uncovered; much faster than European pillbugsSlow smooth gliding motion driven by metachronal leg waves
DefenceCannot roll into a ball; relies on speed and camouflageCoils into tight spiral when disturbed — does NOT form a perfect closed ball
HabitatUnder damp leaf litter, loose bark, and garden mulchUnder leaf litter, damp logs, compost — post-monsoon garden soil
Smell—Faint almond-like smell (hydrogen cyanide) when handled — harmless

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
