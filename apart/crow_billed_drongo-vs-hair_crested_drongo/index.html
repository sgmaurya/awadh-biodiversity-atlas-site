How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hair-crested Drongo, as the Crow-billed Drongo profile puts it

Hair-crested Drongo]] (Dicrurus hottentottus)

Strongly upcurved outer tail feathers, hair-like crest plumes, and heavier downcurved bill

Crow-billed Drongo: The heavy, stout bill combined with a shallow tail fork, glossy steel-blue black plumage, and white-dotted under wing-coverts separates the Crow-billed Drongo (Baker 1924).

Hair-crested Drongo: Large black drongo with outward and upward curled tail outer tips, long downcurved bill, and hair-like crest plumes on forehead.
