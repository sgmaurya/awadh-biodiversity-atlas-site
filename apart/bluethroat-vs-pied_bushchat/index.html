Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the Bluethroat profile puts it

Pied Bushchat]] (Saxicola caprata)

Resident; male all-black back; different throat pattern

Bluethroat: A small brown songbird in reed beds with a blue throat (male) or blue-speckled white throat (female), and orange-buff tail bases — is a Bluethroat. The throat pattern and orange tail bases are diagnostic.

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.
