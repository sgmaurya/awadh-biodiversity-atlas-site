Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Lascar, as the Common Sergeant profile puts it

Upperside black with orange markings; underside chestnut-brown (Bingham 1905, pp. 344–345, entry 358 as hordonia)

काले पंखों पर नारंगी निशान और निचली सतह चेस्टनट-भूरी (कॉमन लस्कर तितली)

Common Lascar: A medium-sized butterfly gliding with flat wings around acacia and siris trees, marked with rich orange-yellow horizontal bands on dark brown upperside, is the Common Lascar.

Common Sergeant: A black-and-white nymphalid with a divided white streak in the forewing cell and a row of round black dots along the inner margin of the hindwing postdiscal band (highlighted in pale blue on a golden-ochraceous underside) is Parathyma perius (Bingham 1905, pp. 315–316; Evans 1932).
