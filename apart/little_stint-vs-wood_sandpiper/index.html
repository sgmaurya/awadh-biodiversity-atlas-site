Little Stint or Wood Sandpiper? लिटिल स्टिंट या वुड सैंडपाइपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Stint, as the Wood Sandpiper profile puts it

Much smaller; shorter bill; grey back; rufous cap in breeding

लिटिल स्टिंट

Little Stint: The tiniest wader seen in large flocks on mudflats, with hyperactive pecking behaviour and a very short bill — is a Little Stint. The size (12–14 cm) is diagnostic; no other sandpiper is as small or as gregarious.

Wood Sandpiper: A spotted wader with yellowish legs and pale underwings, in small flocks at shallow wetlands — is a Wood Sandpiper. The bold spotting and yellow legs distinguish it from Green Sandpiper. Paler and more spotted than Green Sandpiper.
