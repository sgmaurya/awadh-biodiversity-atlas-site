Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Grey Babbler, as the Jungle Babbler profile puts it

Larger; outer tail feathers are white (prominent in flight); pale forehead; prefers dry habitats

बड़ा धूसर बैबलर — पूँछ के बाहरी पंख सफेद

Jungle Babbler: Earthy grey-brown plumage, yellowish bill, staring pale-yellow eyes, and an obligate group size of 6–10 birds foraging on the ground.

Large Grey Babbler: The largest babbler, grey and buff-brown, always in large, boisterous flocks. Loud, repetitive calling makes flocks very conspicuous. Long graduated tail; moves through dense bushes in loose formation.
