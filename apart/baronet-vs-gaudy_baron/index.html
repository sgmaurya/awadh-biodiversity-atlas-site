Baronet or Gaudy Baron? बैरोनेट या गॉडी बैरन तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गॉडी बैरन तितलीGaudy BaronEuthalia lubentina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Baronet, as the Gaudy Baron profile puts it

Upperside tawny yellow with black markings; underside dark ochraceous red (Bingham 1905, p. 287, entry 307)

ऊपरी सतह पीली-भूरी और काले निशान वाली, निचली सतह गहरी गेरुई-लाल (बैरोनेट)

Baronet: Instantly recognized by its rich tawny-orange upperside with bold black bars, flat sun-basking posture, and bright red underside with white discal band.

Gaudy Baron: A dark greenish-brown baron with black-bordered crimson spots on the forewing cell and hindwing (plus broad white forewing spots and bluish-green hindwing suffusion in the female) is Euthalia lubentina (Bingham 1905, p. 278).

Female upperside—Paler; forewing discal series of white spots very large and irregular; hindwing suffused with greenish blue on terminal posterior half (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
Underside—Male dark purplish brown slightly suffused with ochraceous; female brown with tornal half of hindwing bluish green; both sexes with four black-bordered crimson spots at hindwing base and crimson markings (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
Antennae and palpi—Antennae dark brown with club crimson beneath; palpi crimson beneath in male, striped pink in female (Bingham 1905, p. 278)
