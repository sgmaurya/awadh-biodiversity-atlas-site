Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Lark, as the Bengal Bushlark profile puts it

Crested Lark]] (Galerida cristata)

Long upright crest on crown, heavily streaked upperparts, lacks rufous wing panel and wing-clapping flight (Baker 1926, p. 341)

Bengal Bushlark: The stout conical bill, heavily dark-spotted breast, and prominent chestnut wing panels identify the Bengal Bushlark (Baker 1926, pp. 337–338).

Crested Lark: Crested Lark is readily identified by its prominent, pointed, erectile crest and buff-brown coloration. Walking on open ground with upright posture and raised crest is diagnostic.
