How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pink Node Flower, as the Minnie Root profile puts it

Caesulia axillaris]]

Marsh herb with narrow serrulate leaves, axillary flower balls, and fibrous roots lacking tubers

Minnie Root: Fleshy finger-like tuberous roots combined with showy violet-blue funnel flowers and explosive water-sensitive brown capsules immediately identify Ruellia tuberosa.

Pink Node Flower: Succulent semi-aquatic stems, narrow leaves with clasping subauriculate bases, and tight sessile axillary globose flower balls immediately distinguish Caesulia axillaris.
