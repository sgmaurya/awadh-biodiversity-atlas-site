How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake, as the Indo-Chinese Sand Snake profile puts it

Common Bronzeback]] (Dendrelaphis tristis)

Arboreal tree snake with wide bronze vertebral stripe and pale flanks; blue skin revealed when puffed

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake: A slender, uniformly green or bronze-brown snake moving fast along a tree branch during the day with a long prehensile tail is a Common Bronzeback and is completely harmless.

Indo-Chinese Sand Snake: Smooth scales and 4 or 5 dark brown longitudinal stripes on a pale olive or buff body separate Psammophis condanarus from keeled-scaled keelbacks and arboreal bronzebacks (Smith 1943, p. 304).
