Flower Crab Spider or Green Lynx Spider? फूल मकड़ी या हरी मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Lynx Spider, as the Flower Crab Spider profile puts it

Green lynx spider]] (Oxyopidae)

Larger; cylindrical body, not flattened; very prominent eye tubercle; long spines on legs

Flower Crab Spider: Small, flattened spider matching the flower colour, with long front legs held to the sides, motionless on flowers: Flower Crab Spider (Thomisus genus).

Green Lynx Spider: Bright emerald-green body, long legs with prominent black spines, and active hunting on plant foliage identify Peucetia viridana.
