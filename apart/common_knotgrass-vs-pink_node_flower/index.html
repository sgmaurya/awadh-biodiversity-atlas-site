Common Knotgrass or Pink Node Flower? कॉमन नॉटग्रास या पिंक नोड फ्लावर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pink Node Flower, as the Common Knotgrass profile puts it

Caesulia axillaris]]

Semi-aquatic herb with erect or spreading stems, long narrow serrulate leaves, and white flower balls

Common Knotgrass, as the Pink Node Flower profile puts it

Polygonum plebeium]]

Prostrate ground-hugging herb forming dense mats with tiny leaves and shining nutlets

Common Knotgrass: Prostrate ground-hugging mat habit, silvery fringed stipular sheaths (ochreas), tiny leaves, and minute shining 3-gonous nutlets distinguish Polygonum plebeium.

Pink Node Flower: Succulent semi-aquatic stems, narrow leaves with clasping subauriculate bases, and tight sessile axillary globose flower balls immediately distinguish Caesulia axillaris.
