Garua Bachcha or Long-whiskered Catfish? गरुआ या आर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Long-whiskered Catfish, as the Garua Bachcha profile puts it

Larger; more robust body; different barbel proportions

आर — बड़ी, मजबूत

Garua Bachcha: A medium catfish with four pairs of very long, thread-like barbels (especially the maxillary pair) found in rocky flowing water is a Garua Bachcha.

Long-whiskered Catfish: A large catfish (35–60 cm) with 4 barbels (whiskers), two of which are very long (reaching beyond gill slits), is a Long-whiskered Catfish. The long outer barbels are diagnostic.
