Blue Pansy or Chocolate Pansy? ब्लू पैंज़ी या चॉकलेट पैंज़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्लू पैंज़ीBlue PansyJunonia orithya
चॉकलेट पैंज़ीChocolate PansyJunonia iphita

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chocolate Pansy, as the Blue Pansy profile puts it

Chocolate Pansy]] (Junonia iphita)

Dark brown ground colour; no blue patches; smaller eyespots

Blue Pansy, as the Chocolate Pansy profile puts it

Blue Pansy]] (Junonia orithya)

Blue iridescent patches on wings in addition to eyespots; more elaborate pattern

Blue Pansy: A medium to large brown butterfly with distinctive bright iridescent blue patches or bands visible in certain lighting, combined with eyespots, is the Blue Pansy.

Chocolate Pansy: A medium to large dark brown butterfly with large, prominent eyespots with dark blue or iridescent centers on both wings, is the Chocolate Pansy.
