House Mouse or Indian Gerbil? चुहिया या अंतरी चूहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अंतरी चूहाIndian GerbilTatera indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Mouse, as the Indian Gerbil profile puts it

Much smaller (7–10 cm), no dark dorsal stripe, different habitat (houses vs. fields)

बहुत छोटा, घर का चूहा, पट्टी नहीं

House Mouse: A delicate, tiny mouse with large ears, small eyes, and a thin tail found in kitchens and grain stores is a House Mouse.

Indian Gerbil: A tawny-brown mouse (15–20 cm) with a long thin tail, large ears, large dark eyes, and a dark stripe along its back in arid fields or grain stores is an Indian gerbil. It runs and bounds rapidly; most visible at dusk and dawn.
