Rita Catfish or Striped Catfish? रीता या टेंगरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रीताRita CatfishRita rita
टेंगराStriped CatfishMystus vittatus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Catfish, as the Rita Catfish profile puts it

Much smaller (max 18 cm); body has 4–5 distinct dark longitudinal stripes; shorter and less robust spines

टेंगरा — छोटा आकार, 4-5 धारियां

Rita Catfish, as the Striped Catfish profile puts it

Much larger and heavier; deeply forked tail with a shorter body stripe pattern (pale stripes, not dark ones); only three pairs of barbels

रीता — बड़ा आकार, तीन जोड़ी मूंछें

Rita Catfish: Large head, heavy scale-less body, three pairs of barbels (with short nasal barbels), a deeply forked tail, and pale lateral bands distinguish the Rita Catfish.

Striped Catfish: Small size (under 18 cm), scale-less body with 4–5 dark parallel stripes, and maxillary barbels extending to the pelvic fins identify Mystus vittatus.
