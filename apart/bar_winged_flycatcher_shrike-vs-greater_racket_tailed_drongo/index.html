How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo, as the Bar-winged Flycatcher-shrike profile puts it

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo]] (Dicrurus paradiseus)

Large [[black_drongo\

Bar-winged Flycatcher-shrike: The small size, pied coloration, glossy black back (or dark brown in female), clean white underparts, and prominent longitudinal white wing bar distinguish the Bar-winged Flycatcher-shrike.

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo: The prominent forward-curving forehead crest and twin long bare tail wires tipped with twisted flat rackets distinguish the Greater Racket-tailed Drongo from all other drongo species.
