How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purslane, as the Chicken Weed profile puts it

Portulaca oleracea

Much larger plant; leaves alternate, flat, wedge-shaped; nodes without prominent white wool

Chicken Weed: Delicate prostrate succulent with opposite tiny fleshy leaves, dense silvery hairs at every stem node, and yellow four-petaled flowers.

Purslane: A fleshy reddish mat with spoon-shaped glossy leaves, morning yellow flowers and a seed capsule that opens like a lid is kulfa; milky sap or needle leaves mean something else.
