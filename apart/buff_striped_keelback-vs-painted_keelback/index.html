How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Buff-striped Keelback, as the Painted Keelback profile puts it

Buff-striped Keelback]] (Amphiesma stolatum)

Terrestrial; smaller (40–70 cm); two bright buff stripes with black bars between them; found in grass and damp soil, not water

Painted Keelback: Any keeled, striped water snake found near wetlands and rice paddies is a Painted Keelback. It will flee into water when approached. The longitudinal yellow or orange stripes are distinctive.
