Bronze Featherback or Giant Catfish? मोई या मल्ला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मोईBronze FeatherbackNotopterus notopterus
मल्लाGiant CatfishWallago attu

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Catfish, as the Bronze Featherback profile puts it

Lacks scales; has long maxillary barbels; dorsal fin is small but not feather-like; head is wider

पढ़िन — शल्क रहित, बड़े मूँछ, चौड़ा मुँह

Bronze Featherback: Flat knife-like body, uniform copper-bronze colouration, a small feather-like dorsal fin, and a continuous anal fin confluent with the caudal fin identify the Bronze Featherback.

Giant Catfish: Enormous mouth extending past the eyes, scaleless silvery-grey body, very short dorsal fin, and very long anal fin identify Wallago attu.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Bronze FeatherbackGiant Catfish
SizeMedium-sized fish (typically 20–35 cm total length); weight 150–600 gLarge freshwater catfish, commonly growing 80–150 cm in length in river channels
Body shapeDeeply compressed, knife-like body with a flat back and a strongly curved belly—
ColourationUniformly bronze-brown, dark greyish-brown, or silvery-grey; paler belly—
FinsA small, feather-like dorsal fin; a very long anal fin running along the bellyVery short, tiny dorsal fin (only 5 rays) on the upper back; exceptionally long anal fin (85–95 rays) extending along the belly
ScalesExtremely small, fine scales covering the body and head—
HeadSmall head with a concave profile above the eyes; large mouth extending past eyes—
Form—Scaleless, elongated, laterally compressed body; tapers sharply towards the tail
Mouth—Enormous, wide mouth; the cleft of the mouth extends backwards beyond the eyes
Barbels—2 pairs of feelers; maxillary barbels are very long, extending past the pectoral fins; mandibular barbels short
Coloration—Uniform silvery-grey or greenish-brown on the back; shading to creamy white on the belly

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
