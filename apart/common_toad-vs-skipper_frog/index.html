Asian Common Toad or Skittering Frog? भेंडुक या कूद मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Skittering Frog, as the Asian Common Toad profile puts it

Smooth skin; smaller; extremely fast; skips on water surface

चिकना; छोटा; पानी पर कूदता है

Asian Common Toad, as the Skittering Frog profile puts it

Heavily warty; slower; parotoid glands behind eyes; no skittering

Asian Common Toad: Large, warty, slow-moving, brown — with two prominent raised ridges behind the eyes — on your garden path at night = Duttaphrynus melanostictus. Frogs are smooth-skinned and leap rapidly. Toads are warty and walk.
