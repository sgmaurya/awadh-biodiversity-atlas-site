Jungle Owlet or Spotted Owlet? जंगली उल्लू या चित्तीदार उल्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Owlet, as the Jungle Owlet profile puts it

Spotted Owlet]] (Athene brama)

Slightly larger (20–23 cm); white spotting on wing coverts (not streaked); different habitat (more urban); different calls

Jungle Owlet, as the Spotted Owlet profile puts it

Lacks the white spots; has fine, close rufous-brown bars; prefers denser forest

जंगली उल्लू — चित्तियों के स्थान पर धारियां

Jungle Owlet: A very small, brown, streaked (not spotted) owl with large yellow eyes and distinctive hooting calls in woodlands and gardens — this is the Jungle Owlet. The Spotted Owlet is slightly larger, shows white spots (not streaks), and has different calls; it is more urban.

Spotted Owlet: Small size, white spots on greyish-brown upperparts, yellow eyes, lack of ear tufts, and loud chattering calls in pairs confirm the Spotted Owlet.
