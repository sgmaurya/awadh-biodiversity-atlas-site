How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Signature Spider, as the Tropical Tent-web Spider profile puts it

Signature Spider]] [SPD-0001])

Sits at the center of a flat, vertical orb-web with a white X-shaped decoration; body is brightly colored yellow and black.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Signature SpiderTropical Tent-web Spider
Female size14–20 mm body length; legspan 30–40 mmMedium-sized; body length 10.0–15.0 mm
Male sizeMuch smaller — 5–6 mm; brownish, less boldly markedTiny; body length 2.5–3.5 mm (often goes unnoticed at the edge of the female's web)
Body colourBold yellow with black and silvery-white transverse bands — strongly contrasting—
LegsYellow with black bands; held in X-shape (legs paired I+II, III+IV)—
WebVertical orb, 30–50 cm diameter; anchored in low vegetation at 50 cm–2 m—
StabilimentumThick zigzag silk ribbon through web centre — the single most diagnostic feature—
PoseFemale sits head-down at web hub; legs paired in clear X-pattern—
SeasonPeak September–November; monsoon and post-monsoon—
Form—Stout, humped body; abdomen has a series of pairs of prominent tubercles (humps) on the back
Colour—Highly variable; ground colour ranges from grey, dark brown, to almost black, with white or yellowish spots
Web Structure—A horizontal, dense mesh sheet (dome) suspended by an irregular vertical scaffolding of silk lines above and below
Colony—Semi-social behavior; multiple females build individual webs within a shared framework of support silk

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
