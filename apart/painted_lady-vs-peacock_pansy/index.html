How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peacock Pansy, as the Painted Lady profile puts it

Peacock Pansy]])

Two large eyespots on upperwing; slower flight

Painted Lady: Medium-sized butterfly with distinctive reddish-orange upperwing marked with black and white patches; fast, erratic flight; seen on thistles and flowers year-round.

Peacock Pansy: Medium size, bright orange-brown upperwings, two very large peacock-like eyespots (with double blue-white pupils on the hindwings), and seasonal dry-season leaf camouflage identify the Peacock Pansy.
