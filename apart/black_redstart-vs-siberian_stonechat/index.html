Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Siberian Stonechat, as the Black Redstart profile puts it

Siberian Stonechat]] (Saxicola maurus)

White neck-patch (diagnostic); stockier build; different tail

Black Redstart, as the Siberian Stonechat profile puts it

Black Redstart]] (Phoenicurus ochruros)

All black (male) with orange-red tail; no white neck-patch; different tail colour

Black Redstart: A small black (male) or dark grey-brown (female) songbird with a striking orange-red tail base and rump, on rocky or open areas — is a Black Redstart. The orange-red tail is diagnostic. Males are striking and unmistakable.

Siberian Stonechat: A small bird with a white neck-patch (in males) and white rump patch, on grasslands during winter — is a Siberian Stonechat. The white neck-patch (especially diagnostic in males) and white rump are key field marks. Females duller but still show white rump.
