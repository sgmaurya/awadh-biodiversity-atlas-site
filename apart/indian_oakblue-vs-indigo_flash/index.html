Indian Oakblue or Indigo Flash? भारतीय ओकब्लू या इंडिगो फ्लैश?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भारतीय ओकब्लूIndian OakblueArhopala atrax
इंडिगो फ्लैशIndigo FlashRapala varuna

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indigo Flash, as the Indian Oakblue profile puts it

Wings upperside dark indigo-black, underside plain greyish with a single narrow white-edged band

ऊपरी सतह गहरी जामुनी-काली, निचली सतह पर एक सफेद रेखा

Indian Oakblue: Identify by medium size, low-shrub perching habit, light brown underside with neat ringed spots, and absence of bold silver streaks.

Indigo Flash: Notice the dark indigo-blue gloss of the male in flight, the smooth earthy brown underside with a neat white-edged line, and ber shrub associations.
