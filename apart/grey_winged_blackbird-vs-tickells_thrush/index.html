How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tickell's Thrush, as the Grey-winged Blackbird profile puts it

Tickell's Thrush]] (Turdus unicolor)

Pale slate-grey above with no distinct silvery-grey wing panel; whitish abdomen and under tail-coverts; yellow bill (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Grey-winged Blackbird: The black plumage of the male contrasting with a conspicuous ashy-grey wing panel and orange bill and eye-ring separates the Grey-winged Blackbird from all other regional thrushes (Baker 1924, p. 130).

Tickell's Thrush: Tickell's Thrush is distinguished by its uniform slate-blue coloration with no spots or bars anywhere on the plumage. The unicolorous appearance is diagnostic.
