Bonelli's Eagle or Crested Serpent Eagle? बोनेलीज़ ईगल या सर्प चील?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Serpent Eagle, as the Bonelli's Eagle profile puts it

Spilornis cheela

Prominent rounded black-and-white bushy crest, yellow bare face skin, and broad white tail band

Bonelli's Eagle: Slender, powerful eagle with gleaming white streaked underparts, feathered tarsi, and long grey tail with single broad dark terminal band.

Crested Serpent Eagle: Crested Serpent Eagle is unique with its distinctive erectile crest and bright yellow eyes. No other eagle in eastern UP has this combination of features. The yellow spotting on dark wings is also diagnostic.
