Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-headed Bunting, as the Black-headed Bunting profile puts it

Red-headed Bunting]] (Emberiza bruniceps)

Male has rich golden-chestnut or rufous-orange head, lacking solid jet-black hood; mantle olive-yellow (Baker 1926, p. 215)

Black-headed Bunting: The solid jet-black hood contrasting sharply with pure rich yellow underparts and orange-chestnut mantle identifies the male Black-headed Bunting (Baker 1926, p. 213).

Red-headed Bunting: The male Red-headed Bunting's fiery coppery-red head and breast contrasting with a clear yellow belly, and uncrested profile, distinguish it from the crested bunting and rosefinch.
