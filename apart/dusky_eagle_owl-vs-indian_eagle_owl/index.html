Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Eagle-Owl, as the Dusky Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Indian Eagle-Owl]] (Bubo bengalensis)

Rich warm tawny-rufous plumage with bold black blotches and streaks, and bright orange-red eyes; prefers rocky ravines and dry hillsides

Dusky Eagle-Owl: The uniform sooty-grey vermiculated plumage, erect dark ear tufts, bright lemon-yellow eyes, feathered tarsi, and preference for wooded water margins distinguish the Dusky Eagle-Owl.

Indian Eagle-Owl: Very large owl with bright orange eyes, prominent vertical ear tufts, and heavily streaked brown plumage nesting on clay cliffs or ruins is an Indian Eagle-Owl.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Dusky Eagle-OwlIndian Eagle-Owl
HabitLarge, robust, partly diurnal horned owl of wooded watersides and groves—
SizeImmense, powerful build comparable to the [[indian_eagle_owl\Very large owl (50–56 cm total length); wingspan around 130–145 cm
PlumagePale sooty greyish-brown above, finely vermiculated with broken wavy bars—
Ear TuftsProminent, tall, upright feather ear tufts rising from above the eyes—
EyesBrilliant, glowing lemon-yellow to deep golden irises—
Facial DiscPale greyish-white facial disc with dark shafts, edged by a narrow darker ruff—
CallDeep, resonant, hollow rumbling woo-woo-woo echoing over water at dusk—
Head—Massive round head with prominent vertical ear tufts (feather horns) and large staring orange eyes
Colouration—Tawny-brown upperparts, heavily streaked and barred with black; buff underparts with bold dark streaks
Flight—Heavy but completely silent flight on broad, rounded wings; glides low along ravines
Bare Parts—Bill is greenish-black; powerful legs and toes are heavily feathered down to the black talons
Sounds—Deep, resonant double hoot: bu-bo or tu-whoo, repeated at intervals
