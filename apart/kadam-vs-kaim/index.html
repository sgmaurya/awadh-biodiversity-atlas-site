Kadam Tree or Kaim? कदम्ब या कैम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कदम्बKadam TreeNeolamarckia cadamba
कैमKaimMitragyna parvifolia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kaim, as the Kadam Tree profile puts it

Also Rubiaceae; flower heads smaller, not as fragrant; fruit not fleshy

Kaim: A medium riparian tree, leafless in April, with dense round clusters of tiny white-yellow flowers and opposite simple leaves is kaim.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Kadam TreeKaim
Tree formLarge (15–45 m at maturity), straight trunk, spreading crown—
LeavesOpposite, large (13–32 cm), shiny, ovate-elliptic, with large stipulesSimple, opposite, oblong to elliptic, 5–8 cm, entire margins, dull green
FlowersGlobose heads (4–5 cm), orange-yellow → white, intensely fragrant — unmistakableSmall, white to yellowish, in dense rounded clusters (balls) at branch tips, April to May
FruitFleshy syncarpium breaking into 8,000+ tiny fruitlets when ripe—
BarkGrey-brown, rough, somewhat furrowed in older treesDark grey, rough, fissured
Season clueFirst globose orange flower heads appear with first monsoon rain (July)—
Size—Medium deciduous tree, 10–18 m, with a spreading crown
Fruits—Small, round, woody, 2-seeded capsules
The test—Look for a tree with opposite simple leaves and small, round flower clusters along riverbanks

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
