How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Grey Hornbill, as the Oriental Pied Hornbill profile puts it

Ocyceros birostris

Uniform brownish-grey plumage; much smaller bill and casque; lacks black-and-white contrasting plumage

Indian Grey Hornbill: Large grey-brown bird with a long tail, distinctive flap-and-glide flight, and a massive curved bill topped by a pointed casque, moving in the canopy of mature trees, is an Indian Grey Hornbill.

Oriental Pied Hornbill: Massive black-and-white canopy bird with immense yellow bill and casque, white belly, and white-tipped black tail.

Indian Grey HornbillOriental Pied Hornbill
SizeLarge bird (50–60 cm total length) with a long tail and a massive curved bill—
CasqueA prominent, pointed, horn-like growth (casque) on top of the upper bill, blackish-grey—
ColourationUniform brownish-grey overall; pale grey neck and underparts; dark primary feathers—
Tail PatternLong, graduated tail; grey with a black band near the end and broad white tipsBlack tail with all outer rectrices broadly tipped with pure white
FlightDistinctive undulating flap-and-glide sequence; wings make a rustling sound in flight—
BehaviourStrictly arboreal; moves actively in the canopy of mature fruiting trees—
Plumage pattern—Glossy greenish-black body contrasting with pure white belly, thighs, and tail tips
Casque morphology—Large, sharp-fronted yellowish-white casque marked with variable black patches
Facial skin—Bare circumorbital skin and throat patch; white with black anterior mark in males
Flight pattern—Characteristic alternating heavy flapping and long glides on broad rounded wings
