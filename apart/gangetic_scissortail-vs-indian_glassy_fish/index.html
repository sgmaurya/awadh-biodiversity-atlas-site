Gangetic Scissortail or Indian Glassy Fish? डेंडुआ या चंदा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Glassy Fish, as the Gangetic Scissortail profile puts it

Transparent body; no scissor-tail; smaller overall

पारदर्शी शरीर

Gangetic Scissortail: A small silvery minnow with a deeply forked tail bearing black bands on both lobes, found in schools in open water, is a Gangetic Scissortail.

Indian Glassy Fish: A tiny (6–8 cm), transparent fish with large eyes, visible internal organs, and a silvery longitudinal stripe is unmistakably an Indian Glassy Fish.
