How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blyth's Reed Warbler, as the West Himalayan Bush Warbler profile puts it

Plain greyish-olive upperparts; whitish unspotted breast; longer straighter bill; lacks graduated tail

ब्लाईथ की फड़की

Blyth's Reed Warbler: A small brown warbler in reed beds with a distinctive "jir-jir-jir" flight call — is Blyth's Reed Warbler. The distinctive flight call is the best identification feature. Song (in breeding season or early spring) is also characteristic.

West Himalayan Bush Warbler: A skulking, unstreaked olive-brown warbler in dense grass with a rounded graduated tail and diffuse dark spots across the upper breast is a West Himalayan Bush Warbler.
