How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-bark Acacia, as the Vilayati Kikar profile puts it

Native; yellowish-white bark; small pale yellow flower heads; pods are flat but not sweet

रोंज बबूल — सफेद छाल, देशी

Vilayati Kikar: Sprawling tree with zig-zag weeping branches, pairs of straight yellowish thorns, pale yellow cylindrical flower spikes (not round), and flat straight pods identify the invasive Vilayati Kikar.

White-bark Acacia: Look for the characteristic pale chalky yellowish-white bark, straight paired spines, and large branching panicles of globose creamy-yellow heads.
