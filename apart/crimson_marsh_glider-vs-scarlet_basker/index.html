How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson Marsh Glider, as the Scarlet Basker profile puts it

Crimson Marsh Glider]])

Crimson-pink body with broad amber-yellow wing bases; abdomen lacks triangular black dorsal spots

Crimson Marsh Glider: Medium size, pinkish-crimson body with purple venation at the wing bases, and frequent perching behavior identify the Crimson Marsh Glider.

Scarlet Basker: A robust blood-red dragonfly perching high over open water with broad dark reddish-brown basal patches on the hindwings and triangular black dorsal spots on abdominal segments 8 and 9 is the Scarlet Basker.
