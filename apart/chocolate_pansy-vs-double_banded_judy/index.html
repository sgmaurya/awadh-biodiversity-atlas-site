How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chocolate Pansy, as the Double-banded Judy profile puts it

Chocolate Pansy]])

Distinctly larger; uniform dark chocolate-brown with wavy cryptic bands and open gliding flight

Chocolate Pansy: A medium to large dark brown butterfly with large, prominent eyespots with dark blue or iridescent centers on both wings, is the Chocolate Pansy.

Double-banded Judy: A medium-small maroon-brown butterfly with two straight pale bands across the forewings, perching on shaded leaves with half-open wings and pivoting rapidly in circles, is the Double-banded Judy.

Upperside Ground—Deep purple-brown or rich maroon in males; paler ochreous-brown in females (Wynter-Blyth 1957)
Forewing Markings—Two distinct, parallel pale lilac-white transverse discal bands (Evans 1932)
Hindwing Shape—Angled sharply at vein 4, bearing two small black subapical spots (Bingham 1905)
Underside Pattern—Pale dull maroon-brown repeating the double pale bands and apical spots (Kehimkar 2008)
Unique Behaviour—Alights on leaves with wings half-open and rapidly jerks or pivots in circles (Kunte 2000)
Flight Style—Short, jerky, skipping flight close to dark forest floor undergrowth (Wynter-Blyth 1957)
