How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Comb Rungia, as the Malaysian False Pimpernel profile puts it

Rungia pectinata]]`

Stems erect or decumbent; flowers in dense one-sided spikes with scarious bracts

Comb Rungia: Look for the dense, one-sided comb-like spikes with broad white-bordered bracts and tiny two-lipped pale blue flowers.

Malaysian False Pimpernel: Look for the square stems, small paired serrate leaves, and tiny two-lipped purplish flowers in leaf axils.
