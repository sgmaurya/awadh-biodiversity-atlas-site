Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm or Piguet's Sludge Worm? हॉफमीस्टर्स स्लज वर्म या पिगिट्स स्लज वर्म?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Piguet's Sludge Worm, as the Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm profile puts it

Much smaller; constructs elevated chimney tubes of mud on pond beds

काफी छोटा, मिट्टी की ऊंची चिमनी बनाना

Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm, as the Piguet's Sludge Worm profile puts it

Lacks chimney tubes, paddle setae, and unsegmented tail zone

नलीदार चिमनी और चप्पू जैसे कांटे नहीं

Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm: A slender reddish-pink worm forming trembling carpets in polluted organic sludge, lacking hair setae and external gills, with a bonnet-tipped penis sheath identifies Limnodrilus hoffmeisteri.

Piguet's Sludge Worm: A tiny aquatic worm building small mud chimneys on pond sediment, featuring an unsegmented tail zone and oar-shaped dorsal paddle setae, identifies Aulodrilus pigueti.
