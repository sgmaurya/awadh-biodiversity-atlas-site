How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Fly, as the Black Soldier Fly profile puts it

House fly]] (Musca domestica)

Smaller (7-8 mm); grey thorax with dark stripes; different body shape; annoying around food

Black Soldier Fly: A black or dark grey fly with an amber or reddish head, seen in pairs around compost or manure heaps during warm months, is the Black Soldier Fly.

House Fly: Uniform grey-brown, small (6–7 mm), four dark thoracic stripes, found indoors and around food: House Fly.
