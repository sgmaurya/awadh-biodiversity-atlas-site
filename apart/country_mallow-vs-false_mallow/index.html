How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

False Mallow, as the Country Mallow profile puts it

False Mallow]] (Malvastrum coromandelianum)

Much smaller; tiny yellow flowers; smaller spherical fruit

Country Mallow, as the False Mallow profile puts it

Country Mallow]] (Sida cordifolia)

Heart-shaped leaves; larger flowers; hairier fruit

Country Mallow: Country Mallow has heart-shaped (cordate) leaves and yellow flowers. Caesar Weed has lobed leaves and pink flowers.

False Mallow: False Mallow is small with tiny yellow flowers and a distinctive spiky ball-fruit. Larger Malvaceae have bigger flowers and different leaf shapes.
