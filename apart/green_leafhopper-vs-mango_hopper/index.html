How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Leafhopper, as the Mango Hopper profile puts it

Green leafhopper]] (Nephotettix spp.)

Larger (4–5 mm); bright green; found on rice and grasses, not mango

Green Leafhopper: A small bright green leafhopper 4–5 mm, jumping sideways on the undersides of paddy leaves with visible stippling damage, is the green leafhopper.

Mango Hopper: Tiny, pale green to yellowish-brown wedge-shaped insect on the underside of mango leaves, jumping when disturbed, causing yellowing and bleaching of the leaf, almost certainly the mango hopper.

DamageFeeding causes wilting and pale stippling on leaves—
Shape and posture—Compact, held steeply roof-like when at rest; jumps when disturbed
Location on leaf—On the underside, in clusters or rows along the midrib
Nymphs—Pale greenish-yellow, wingless, in same location as adults
Damage sign—Leaves pale, bleached, or mottled; yellowing progresses to browning and drying
Honeydew—Sticky residue on leaves and branches; attracts sooty mould
