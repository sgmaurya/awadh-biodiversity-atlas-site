How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Deer, as the Barking Deer profile puts it

Spotted Deer]] (Axis axis)

Substantially larger; bright golden-rufous coat permanently patterned with white spots; long three-tined antlers

Barking Deer: The diminutive stocky build, bright unspotted chestnut coat, raised facial ridges, long hairy antler pedicels, and dog-like barking call distinguish the Barking Deer from all other deer.

Spotted Deer: The permanent bright white spots across a golden-rufous coat, three-tined lyrate antlers, and medium slender build distinguish the Spotted Deer from all other regional deer.

Barking DeerSpotted Deer
HabitSmall, solitary, stocky forest deer creeping through dense undergrowthGregarious, graceful medium-sized deer frequenting open forest and grasslands
PelageRich glossy chestnut-red or bright rufous-brown, unspotted in adultsRich golden-rufous or rufous-fawn densely marked with permanent white spots
AntlersShort single-forked horns mounted on long, hair-covered bony pedestalsSlender, lyriform, normally three-tined in adult stags, shed annually
Facial RidgesTwo prominent dark bony ridges running down the forehead (rib-faced deer)—
CaninesElongated, downward-projecting tusk-like upper canines in males—
PostureSloping carriage with hindquarters slightly higher than the withers—
Alarm CallExtremely loud, sharp, dog-like barking uttered repeatedly when alarmed—
Dorsal Stripe—Prominent dark dorsal stripe running along the spine, flanked by rows of spots
Underparts—Pure white on chin, throat, belly, inner thighs, and underside of tail
Tail—Moderately long, rufous above and pure white below, flared during alarm
Association—Constantly associates with gray langurs around forest clearings
