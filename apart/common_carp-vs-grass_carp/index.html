Common Carp or Grass Carp? कॉमन कार्प या ग्रास कार्प?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन कार्पCommon CarpCyprinus carpio
ग्रास कार्पGrass CarpCtenopharyngodon idella

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grass Carp, as the Common Carp profile puts it

More streamlined; greenish; mouth terminal; no barbels; only vegetation

हरा, पौधे केवल

Common Carp, as the Grass Carp profile puts it

Smaller mouth; two pairs of barbels

दाढ़ी है

Common Carp: A golden-scaled, heavy, bottom-feeding fish with a downward mouth and long barbels, clouding the water as it digs, is the common carp; rohu and catla have terminal mouths and no barbels.

Grass Carp: A large green cyprinid with a terminal mouth, no barbels, and eating water plants is grass carp. The absence of barbels (present in common carp) and the large size distinguish it from other carps in eastern UP ponds.
