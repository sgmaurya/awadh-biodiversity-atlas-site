How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Nirgundi, as the Willow-leaved Justicia profile puts it

Nirgundi]] (Vitex negundo)

Larger leaves; compound (palmate with 5 leaflets); larger flowers

Nirgundi: A shrub with palmately compound, strongly aromatic leaves and violet-blue flowers in spikes, growing in poor ground, is nirgundi.

Willow-leaved Justicia: A small shrub with very narrow willow-like leaves and purple flower spikes is Justicia gendarussa.
