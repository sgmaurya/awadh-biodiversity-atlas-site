How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher, as the Verditer Flycatcher profile puts it

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher]] (Cyornis tickelliae)

Smaller; different plumage pattern; more orange-buff in male

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher: Tickell's Blue Flycatcher is a small blue flycatcher with peachy-buff underparts (in males). Males are brilliant blue; females duller. Favours shaded forest understory. If the bird is larger or lacks the peachy underparts, it is likely a different species.

Verditer Flycatcher: A small, blue and white flycatcher. Males are brilliantly blue-green; females duller but still blue. Hunts insects by sallying from exposed perches. Restless and active; characteristic clear whistled calls.
