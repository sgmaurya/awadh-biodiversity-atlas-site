False Ashoka or Ashoka? अशोक (नकली) या सीता अशोक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अशोक (नकली)False AshokaMonoon longifolium
सीता अशोकAshokaSaraca asoca

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashoka, as the False Ashoka profile puts it

Leaves pinnate with leaflets; flowers orange-red, not yellow; planted in same temples

सीता अशोक — पंखनुमा पत्ती, नारंगी फूल

False Ashoka, as the Ashoka profile puts it

Leaves simple, not pinnate; flowers yellow, not orange-red; fruit berries, not pods

अशोक नकली — सरल पत्तियाँ, पीले फूल

False Ashoka: A tree with simple, glossy, lanceolate leaves, fragrant yellow flowers in the cool season, and small clustered orange fruits, planted in a temple garden, is false ashoka.

Ashoka: A tree with new pink-red pinnate leaves and small orange-red flowers directly on the trunk, in a temple garden, is the sacred ashoka.

Side by side आमने-सामने

False AshokaAshoka
SizeEvergreen to semi-evergreen tree, 8–15 m tall, with a dense, spreading crownSemi-evergreen tree, 6–12 m tall, with a dense, rounded crown
LeavesAlternate, simple, lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate, 10–20 cm, glossy green, entire marginsPinnate, 30–60 cm, with 6–10 pairs of elliptic leaflets, 8–15 cm; young leaves copper to pink-red, turning dark green
FlowersSmall, yellow or pale yellow, fragrant, in clusters (fascicles) in the axils, January–MarchClusters of small, bright orange-red to yellow, fragrant flowers in dense bunches on the trunk and older branches (cauliflorous), December–March
FruitCluster of small ovoid berries, 2–3 cm, smooth, greenish-yellow ripening to orangePod-like follicles, 15–25 cm long, flat, dark brown, ripening April–June
BarkGrey to dark grey, smooth becoming furrowed with ageDark grey, smooth when young, becoming furrowed
The testCrush a leaf; the scent is pleasant and distinctiveLook for the distinctive young pink leaves and cauliflorous orange flowers
