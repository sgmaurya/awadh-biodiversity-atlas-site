How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kidney Garden Spider, as the Theis's Orb-weaver profile puts it

Araneus mitificus

Pale green abdomen with a distinct black kidney-shaped patch near posterior end; smaller size

Theis's Orb-weaver: Nocturnal orb-weaver with pale creamy-yellow wavy folium along dorsal midline of abdomen and two yellow comma spots beneath.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Kidney Garden SpiderTheis's Orb-weaver
FormCompact, globular spider; abdomen is large, rounded, and wider at the anterior end—
ColourPale green to yellowish-white abdomen; cephalothorax is light green or yellow-green—
MarkingsA prominent, dark brown or black kidney-shaped (reniform) mark on the posterior dorsal abdomen; front of abdomen has a thin black outline—
WebVertical, circular orb-web built between branches, usually with one missing sector (pie-slice) near the top—
RetreatHides inside a folded, silk-lined green leaf at the edge of the web—
LegsGreenish-yellow, semi-translucent, with fine spines and faint darker bands on the jointsYellowish-brown, heavily banded with dark brown or blackish annulations and stiff spines
Abdomen pattern—Conspicuous creamy-yellow median longitudinal band (folium) bordered by wavy black lines
Ventral markings—Deep blackish patch enclosed between two distinct bright yellow comma-shaped spots
Carapace—Yellowish-brown to reddish-brown with a narrow dark brown median line
Web habit—Constructs a large vertical sticky orb web at dusk and rests in a curled leaf retreat by day

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
