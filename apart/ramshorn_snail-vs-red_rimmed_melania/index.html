How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ramshorn Snail, as the Red-rimmed Melania profile puts it

Ramshorn Snail]] (Indoplanorbis exustus)

Shell is flat, coiled like a rope (planorboid); lacks a pointed spire; red blood

Ramshorn Snail: Flat, discoidal shell (~12–18 mm) resembling a coiled ram's horn, left-handed coiling, and a dark reddish-brown body with thread-like tentacles identify the Ramshorn Snail.

Red-rimmed Melania: Small, screw-like conical shell (15–25 mm) with 10–12 whorls, light brown with distinctive reddish spots, crawling on muddy canal beds, is the Red-rimmed Melania.
