How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Flame Tree, as the Kassod Tree profile puts it

Delonix regia]]

Bipinnate feathery leaves and scarlet-orange flowers with wide spreading petals

Flame Tree: Large tree with smooth grey bark, feathery bipinnate leaves, a wide flat canopy, brilliant orange-red flowers in summer, and very long, flat, sword-like woody pods, is the Flame Tree (Gulmohar).

Kassod Tree: Flat linear pods and large upright terminal panicles of bright yellow flowers in autumn distinguish Senna siamea from the summer-flowering pendulous Cassia fistula.
