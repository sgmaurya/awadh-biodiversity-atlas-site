Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Quail, as the Grey Francolin profile puts it

Much smaller (15 cm); three pale lines on head; short tail; pale throat

बटेर — बहुत छोटी

Common Quail: Common Quail is a small, cryptic ground-dweller of grassland and stubble, identifiable by its small size, mottled plumage, and distinctive male call. The reluctant flight (short bursts) and ground behavior are characteristic.

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.
