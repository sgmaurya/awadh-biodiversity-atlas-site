Asian Watergrass or Wild Rice? एशियन जल घास या तिन्नी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तिन्नीWild RiceOryza rufipogon

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wild Rice, as the Asian Watergrass profile puts it

Tall rooted emergent grass; culms erect, not floating; leaves longer

लंबा खड़ा जंगली धान, तैरता नहीं

Asian Watergrass: Free-floating aquatic grass with swollen spongy leaf sheaths, short ovate-oblong leaves with water-repellent undersides, and small triangular awned panicles identifies Hygroryza aristata.

Wild Rice: A tall grass in standing water with drooping seed head and long-awned reddish-brown grain that ripens unevenly over many weeks. Leaves are narrow and long. Grows in wetlands year-round but flowers in monsoon–autumn.
