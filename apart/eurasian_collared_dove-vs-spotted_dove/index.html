Eurasian Collared Dove or Spotted Dove? धवल पंडुक या चितरोखा फाख्ता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

धवल पंडुकEurasian Collared DoveStreptopelia decaocto
चितरोखा फाख्ताSpotted DoveSpilopelia chinensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Dove, as the Eurasian Collared Dove profile puts it

Darker; back spotted; broad black collar with white spots on the back of neck

चित्तीदार पंडुक — काली गर्दन पर सफेद डॉट्स

Eurasian Collared Dove, as the Spotted Dove profile puts it

Larger (28 cm); uniform pale grey-buff; thin black collar on nape (not a spotted patch); no white spots on wings; call deeper "coo-coo-coo"

गुलाबी कबूतर — बड़ी, गहरी आवाज़

Eurasian Collared Dove: Sandy-grey body, neat black half-collar on the back of the neck, bright red eyes, and a three-syllable coo-COO-coo call.

Spotted Dove: Medium dove with black-and-white spotted necklace patch on lower neck + white spots on wing coverts + rufous tone + melodious "coo-coo-cuckoo" call = Spotted Dove. The neck patch is diagnostic.
