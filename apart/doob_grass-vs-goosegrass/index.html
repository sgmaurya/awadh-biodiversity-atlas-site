Doob Grass or Goosegrass? दूब या कोदई घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कोदई घासGoosegrassEleusine indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Goosegrass, as the Doob Grass profile puts it

Taller (30–60 cm); coarser; spikes in 2 rows; no stolons

लंबी; कड़ी; धावक नहीं

Doob Grass: Low-creeping mat + narrow blue-green leaves + 3–7 finger-like spikes in a whorl + distinctive fresh smell when crushed = Cynodon dactylon.

Goosegrass: A low, spreading grass with 3–10 straight finger-like spikes radiating from the stem top, tiny dark grain, and found on compacted disturbed soil = Eleusine indica (goosegrass).
