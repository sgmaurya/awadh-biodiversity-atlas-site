How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Bull Frog, as the Terai Cricket Frog profile puts it

Hoplobatrachus tigerinus

Massive size, with prominent longitudinal glandular folds, pointed snout, and loud roaring bellow

Indian Bull Frog: Very large green frog in pond + bright yellow breeding male + deep roaring call + monsoon season eastern UP = Indian Bull Frog. The size and yellow breeding color together are unique.

Terai Cricket Frog: Small agile grey-brown frog with cricket-like clicking call, warty glandular back, and semi-webbed toes along muddy paddy field bunds.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Indian Bull FrogTerai Cricket Frog
SizeLarge frog — females 14–17 cm, males 12–15 cm; the largest frog in the subcontinent—
Non-breeding colourOlive-green to brown-green above, with irregular dark spots and ridges—
Breeding malesTurn bright lemon-yellow overall during June–August, with vocal sacs turning black—
SkinSmooth with two prominent dorsolateral folds running from behind the eyes to the lower back—
TympanumLarge, circular ear disc behind the eye; in males, it is larger than the eye—
CallDeep, resonant roaring call — carries hundreds of metres, sounding like a motor engineRapid, high-pitched rhythmic clicking or ticking notes resembling a field cricket
Body size—Small, slender, agile frog
Dorsal pattern—Greyish-brown or olive with irregular dark blotches and short longitudinal glandular warts
Vertebral stripe—Often displays a narrow, distinct yellowish, pale green, or orange vertebral line down the back
Snout & Head—Moderately pointed snout with distinct canthus rostralis and prominent dark-ringed tympanum
Digits—Fingers free without webs; toes webbed at the base, with small rounded subarticular tubercles
