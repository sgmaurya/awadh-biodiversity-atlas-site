How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Mallow, as the Bloodberry profile puts it

Scarlet Mallow]] (Pentapetes phoenicea)

Larger; heart-shaped leaves; red five-petaled flowers, not white; wetland specialist

Bloodberry: Bloodberry is a small shrub with delicate thin leaves and striking bright red berries. The small white flowers and one-seeded fruits distinguish it from other red-fruited plants.

Scarlet Mallow: Scarlet Mallow is unmistakable: large soft herb with scarlet five-petaled flowers, found only in wet ground. No other Malvaceae in eastern UP has such brilliant red flowers.
