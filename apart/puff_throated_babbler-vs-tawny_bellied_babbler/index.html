Puff-throated Babbler or Tawny-bellied Babbler? पफ-थ्रोटेड बैबलर या टॉनी-बेलीड बैबलर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पफ-थ्रोटेड बैबलरPuff-throated BabblerPellorneum ruficeps
टॉनी-बेलीड बैबलरTawny-bellied BabblerDumetia hyperythra

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tawny-bellied Babbler, as the Puff-throated Babbler profile puts it

Dumetia hyperythra

Smaller size, uniform rufous-chestnut underbody without breast streaks, and olive-brown crown

Puff-throated Babbler: Small ground-dwelling bird with bright rufous-chestnut cap, white puffed throat, and boldly dark-streaked white breast.

Tawny-bellied Babbler: Tawny-bellied babbler is a small, dark babbler with a distinctive tawny or rufous belly. If larger and paler with a long tail, it is likely a common babbler.
