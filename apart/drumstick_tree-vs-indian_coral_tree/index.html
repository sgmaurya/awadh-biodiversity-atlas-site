Drumstick Tree or Indian Coral Tree? सहजन या पांगारा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Drumstick Tree, as the Indian Coral Tree profile puts it

Has tripinnate leaves with tiny leaflets; hanging cream-white fragrant flowers; ribbed green pods

सहजन — सफेद छोटे फूल, बारीक पत्तियां

Drumstick Tree: Small tree with soft corky grey bark, tripinnate leaves with tiny oval leaflets, hanging cream-white fragrant flowers, and long ribbed green pods, is the Drumstick Tree.

Indian Coral Tree: Medium deciduous tree with pale greenish-grey spiny bark, trifoliate leaves, and large spikes of bright crimson-red beak-like flowers appearing on bare branches in spring, is the Indian Coral Tree.
