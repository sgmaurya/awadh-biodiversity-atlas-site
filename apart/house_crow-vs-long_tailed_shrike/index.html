House Crow or Long-tailed Shrike? कौआ या लटोरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पक्षी कौआHouse CrowCorvus splendens
लटोराLong-tailed ShrikeLanius schach

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Crow, as the Long-tailed Shrike profile puts it

Much larger; slate neck; lacks black eye mask and rufous flanks; different beak shape

कौआ — बहुत बड़ा आकार, चेहरे पर मुखौटा नहीं

House Crow: Medium size, grey collar around the neck contrasting with glossy black plumage, flat forehead, and flat kaaa calls identify the House Crow.

Long-tailed Shrike: Black eye mask, grey upper back, bright orange-rufous flanks, a long black tail, and a heavy hooked bill identify the Long-tailed Shrike.
