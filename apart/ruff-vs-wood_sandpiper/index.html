How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Sandpiper, as the Ruff profile puts it

Wood Sandpiper]] (Tringa glareola)

Smaller; barred flanks; yellowish legs; no white rump patch

Ruff: Ruff is a medium-sized wader with a long neck held upright, long orange-yellow legs, and a distinctive white oval rump patch visible in flight. The combination of size, leg colour, and rump pattern distinguishes it from similar species.

Wood Sandpiper: A spotted wader with yellowish legs and pale underwings, in small flocks at shallow wetlands — is a Wood Sandpiper. The bold spotting and yellow legs distinguish it from Green Sandpiper. Paler and more spotted than Green Sandpiper.
