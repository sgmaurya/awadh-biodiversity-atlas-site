How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddyfield Warbler, as the Booted Warbler profile puts it

Paddyfield Warbler]] (Acrocephalus agricola)

Slightly larger; more olive tone; different habitat preference

Booted Warbler: Booted Warbler is a cryptic, plain brown warbler of scrubland with feathered tarsi (the "boots"). Best identified by call in many cases. Consult detailed guides for separation from similar cryptic species.

Paddyfield Warbler: Paddyfield warbler is a small, plain reed warbler best identified by its continuous, melodious warbling song given frequently in winter from exposed reed tops.
