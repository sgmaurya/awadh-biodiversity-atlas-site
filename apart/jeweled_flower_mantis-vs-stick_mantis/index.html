How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Stick Mantis, as the Jeweled Flower Mantis profile puts it

Stick Mantis]])

Extremely slender, stick-like straw-brown body; elongated prothorax; lives in tall dry grass

Jeweled Flower Mantis: Small green-and-white flower mantis with a prominent circular yellow-and-black jewel eye-spot on each forewing = Creobroter gemmatus.

Stick Mantis: A thin, stick-like insect with a small triangular head bearing two small horn-like crests, folded front legs held in "prayer" position, and a cryptic brown or tan colour on dried grass in dry habitats — this is the Stick Mantis. Larger, more robust mantises with different head shapes belong to other species.
