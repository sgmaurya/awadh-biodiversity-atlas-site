Western Mosquitofish or Blue Panchax? गम्बूसिया या पंचाक्स?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Western Mosquitofish, as the Blue Panchax profile puts it

Slightly larger (5–6 cm); upturned mouth; gravid spot in females; invasive, aggressive

बड़ी, मुँह ऊपर, आक्रामक

Western Mosquitofish: A drab, slender, tiny fish with an upturned mouth and a dark belly spot (in females) in still water is a gambusia; the gravid spot and live birth are diagnostic.

Blue Panchax: A small fish with an upturned mouth, metallic blue or green colouration in males, and a surface-hunting habit in shallow water and rice field margins = panchax.
