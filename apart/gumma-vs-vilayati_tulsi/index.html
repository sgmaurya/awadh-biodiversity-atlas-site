Gumma or Vilayati Tulsi? गूमा या विलायती तुलसी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गूमाGummaLeucas aspera
विलायती तुलसीVilayati TulsiHyptis suaveolens

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Vilayati Tulsi, as the Gumma profile puts it

Leaves larger, more coarsely serrate; flowers purple or pink, not white

पत्तियाँ बड़ी, फूल बैंगनी

Gumma, as the Vilayati Tulsi profile puts it

Smaller plant; spikes more compact; flowers pure white; less aggressive

छोटा पौधा, सफेद फूल, आक्रामक नहीं

Gumma: An erect, square-stemmed, hairy herb with rough lanceolate leaves and white flowers densely packed on long spikes is gumma.

Vilayati Tulsi: A robust, tall (1–2 m), square-stemmed herb with large, strongly aromatic leaves and dense pink or white flowers in panicles, aggressively spreading along roadsides is vilayati tulsi.
