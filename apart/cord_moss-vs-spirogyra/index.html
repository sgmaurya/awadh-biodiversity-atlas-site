Cord Moss or Water Silk? काई या पानी की काई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पानी की काईWater SilkSpirogyra

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Silk, as the Cord Moss profile puts it

Spirogyra]], Vaucheria*)

No upright leafy shoots; slippery texture; no spore capsules; purely aquatic

Water Silk: Bright green, fine, unbranched, slippery threads in still water that will not stay in the hand are water silk.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Cord MossWater Silk
Size0.5–3 cm tall; cushion-forming mats on damp surfaces—
ColourBright green to yellow-green (gametophyte); reddish-orange seta; brown capsuleClean green when growing; yellow-green and frothy with bubbles in hot sun; brown when dying
Leaves2–3 mm; soft, ovate-lanceolate; overlapping rosette; midrib (costa) visible—
SporophytePear-shaped drooping capsule on 2–4 cm twisted reddish stalk—
HygroscopyDry seta twists tightly; wet seta straightens — visible movement with humidity changes—
HabitatMoist bare soil, brick walls, fire-affected ground — first coloniser—
Form—Masses of fine, unbranched, bright grass-green threads, floating or draped over stones and plants
Feel—Slippery, silky, slimy; threads slide apart and cannot be held between fingers
Bubbles—Mats often lifted to the surface by trapped oxygen on sunny afternoons
Under a lens—Each thread a single row of cylindrical cells, each with one to several green spiral ribbons
Where—Still, shallow, sunlit, nutrient-rich water

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
