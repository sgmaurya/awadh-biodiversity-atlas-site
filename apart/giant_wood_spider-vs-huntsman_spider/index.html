Giant Wood Spider or Huntsman Spider? सोन मकड़ी या शिकारी मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सोन मकड़ीGiant Wood SpiderNephila pilipes
शिकारी मकड़ीHuntsman SpiderHeteropoda venatoria

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Wood Spider, as the Huntsman Spider profile puts it

Spins massive golden orb-webs in trees; body is highly elongated and brightly colored; found in forest canopies

विशाल जंगली मकड़ी — जाला बुनना, पेड़ पर रहना

Huntsman Spider: Flat crab-like body, leg span up to 12 cm, yellowish-brown colour, and rapid running without web-spinning identifies the Huntsman Spider.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Giant Wood SpiderHuntsman Spider
Female sizeLeg span 15–20 cm; body 3–5 cm — one of Asia's largest spiders—
Male sizeTiny — 0.5–1 cm body; leg span 2–3 cm — lives at periphery of female's web—
WebLarge (up to 1.5 m), orb web; silk noticeably yellow-golden in direct sunlight—
AbdomenYellow-and-black or silver with yellow spots—
LegsLong, feathery tufts at joints (tibial brushes) — distinctiveVery long, banded or spotted with fine black bristles, with the second pair of legs being slightly longer than the first
Habitat clueLarge web spanning between trees/shrubs at 1–3 m height—
Size & Shape—Large, flattened spider; legs are long and positioned laterally (crab-like posture), allowing rapid sideways movement
Colour—Yellowish-brown to greyish-brown; females have a dark band behind the eyes and a light border on the carapace; males have a dark, heart-shaped mark behind the eyes
Eyes—Eight small eyes arranged in two horizontal rows of four on the front of the carapace
Egg Sac—Females carry a flat, disc-shaped, papery-white egg sac (about 12–18 mm diameter) beneath their sternum using their chelicerae

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
