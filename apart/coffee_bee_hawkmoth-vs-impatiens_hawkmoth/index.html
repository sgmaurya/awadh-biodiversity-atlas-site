How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Impatiens Hawkmoth, as the Coffee Bee Hawkmoth profile puts it

Impatiens Hawkmoth]] (Theretra oldenlandiae)

Larger; less bee-like; lacks the clear dark wing tips; predominantly brown coloration

Coffee Bee Hawkmoth, as the Impatiens Hawkmoth profile puts it

Coffee Bee Hawkmoth]] (Cephonodes hylas)

Even smaller; different body colour; different habits

Coffee Bee Hawkmoth: The smallest hawkmoth; compact, bee-like body; dark-tipped pale wings; hovers at flowers with an audible hum; active in daylight; found on coffee, gardenia, or ixora plants — the Coffee Bee Hawkmoth.

Impatiens Hawkmoth: A small brown hawkmoth with a swift, erratic hovering flight at balsam flowers in evening light, combined with small brown larvae on impatiens foliage, identifies the Impatiens Hawkmoth.
