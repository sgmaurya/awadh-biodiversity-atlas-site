How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plain Prinia, as the Yellow-eyed Babbler profile puts it

Plain Prinia]] (Prinia inornata)

Smaller and slighter, pale brown, with a thin bill and a dark eye; tail flicked constantly

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.

Yellow-eyed Babbler: A long-tailed brown-and-white bird clambering in tall grass, with a yellow eye in an orange-red ring and a stout black bill, is the yellow-eyed babbler. Prinias are smaller and slighter with thin bills; grassbirds are streaked; jungle babblers are big, dull and on the ground.
