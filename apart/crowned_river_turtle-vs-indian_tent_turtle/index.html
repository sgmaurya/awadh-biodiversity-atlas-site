How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Tent Turtle, as the Crowned River Turtle profile puts it

Indian Tent Turtle]] (Pangshura tentoria)

Much smaller (15–22 cm carapace); lacks crown on head; has central ridge on carapace

Crowned River Turtle, as the Indian Tent Turtle profile puts it

Crowned River Turtle]] (Hardella thurjii)

Larger (carapace up to 35 cm); lacks central keel; flatter shell; different head markings

Crowned River Turtle: India's largest freshwater turtle with a distinctive crowned head is the Crowned River Turtle. Any large turtle in the Ghaghra river with a prominent head crown is likely this species.

Indian Tent Turtle: A medium-sized freshwater turtle with a distinct tent-like ridge running down the middle of its shell is an Indian Tent Turtle. The central keel is the key identifier.
