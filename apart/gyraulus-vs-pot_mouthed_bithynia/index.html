How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Ramshorn Snail, as the Pot-mouthed Bithynia profile puts it

Gyraulus convexiusculus

Flat, discoid, planorbid shell lacking an operculum; carries reddish haemoglobin blood

Small Ramshorn Snail: An extremely small (5–8 mm), flattened, disc-shaped snail on aquatic plants in ponds and ditches is a small ramshorn snail. It is an intermediate host for parasitic flukes; its presence may indicate disease risk for livestock and, rarely, humans.

Pot-mouthed Bithynia: Small ovate-conical freshwater snail with an expanded, thickened, pot-like aperture and a hard calcareous concentric operculum.
