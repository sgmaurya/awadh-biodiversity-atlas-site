How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jackal Jujube, as the Ceylon Caper profile puts it

Similar scrambling habit with hooked thorns; flowers tiny green; fruit a small black drupe

नन्हे हरे फूल, छोटे काले बेर जैसे फल

Ceylon Caper: A robust scrambling thorny climber with rusty young shoots and showy white flowers turning pink with a brush of purple stamens identifies Capparis zeylanica.

Jackal Jujube: A straggling thorny shrub with paired thorns (one straight, one hooked), asymmetrical leaves with silky golden undersides, and small black ripe drupes identifies Ziziphus oenopolia.
