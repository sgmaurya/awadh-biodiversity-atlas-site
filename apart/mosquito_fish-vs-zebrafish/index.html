Western Mosquitofish or Zebrafish? गम्बूसिया या ज़ेबरा मछली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Zebrafish, as the Western Mosquitofish profile puts it

Striped; larger; streamlined; much faster movement

धारीदार, बड़ी, तेज़ गति

Western Mosquitofish: A drab, slender, tiny fish with an upturned mouth and a dark belly spot (in females) in still water is a gambusia; the gravid spot and live birth are diagnostic.

Zebrafish: A small silvery fish with 7–8 sharp dark vertical stripes from head to tail, in rapid schools in monsoon water, is the zebrafish; the stripes are its most certain mark.
