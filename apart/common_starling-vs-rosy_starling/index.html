How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rosy Starling, as the Common Starling profile puts it

Rosy Starling]] (Pastor roseus)

Pink-and-black coloring; no white spotting in winter birds

Common Starling: Common Starling in winter is a dark bird heavily spotted with white, walking on the ground in flocks. If the bird shows white spotting only on head and neck with buff coloring, or if it is crested, it is a different species.

Rosy Starling: Medium-sized starling with a pastel rose-pink body, contrasting glossy black head, wings, and tail, flying in large, noisy, synchronized flocks in winter, is a Rosy Starling.
