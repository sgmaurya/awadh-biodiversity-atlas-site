Grey Pansy or Lemon Pansy? ग्रे पैंसी तितली या लेमन पैंसी तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lemon Pansy, as the Grey Pansy profile puts it

Larger (50–55 mm); forewing has yellow instead of orange band; orange patches on the hindwing

अधिक पीला-नारंगी रंग

Grey Pansy, as the Lemon Pansy profile puts it

Grey Pansy]] (Junonia atlites)

Duller grey-brown coloration; eyespots less vivid; smaller overall

Grey Pansy: Greyish-brown butterfly with black eyespots (with pale centres) on the hindwing, rapid erratic flight, ground perching: Grey Pansy.

Lemon Pansy: A small to medium-sized pale yellow butterfly with large, prominent black eyespots with iridescent blue or purple centers on both wings, flying rapidly over grassland, is the Lemon Pansy.
