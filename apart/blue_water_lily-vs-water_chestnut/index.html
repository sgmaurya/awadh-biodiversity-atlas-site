Blue Water Lily or Water Chestnut? नीलकमल या सिंघाड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीलकमलBlue Water LilyNymphaea nouchali
सिंघाड़ाWater ChestnutTrapa bispinosa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Chestnut, as the Blue Water Lily profile puts it

Leaves are diamond-shaped, smaller, in tight rosettes with swollen stems; fruits are thorny

सिंघाड़ा — तिकोने पत्ते, कांटेदार फल

Blue Water Lily: Flat, floating round leaves with a deep V-notch and wavy edges, combined with star-like, pale blue or violet flowers that open in the morning, identify Nymphaea nouchali.

Water Chestnut: Rhombic saw-toothed leaves arranged in flat rosettes, with spongy air-swollen stalks and a two-horned hard fruit, uniquely identify the Indian Water Chestnut.
