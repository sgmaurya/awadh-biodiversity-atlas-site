Blue-banded Bee or Carpenter Bee? नीली पट्टी मधुमक्खी या भँवरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Carpenter Bee, as the Blue-banded Bee profile puts it

Larger (20-25 mm); all black or with yellow markings; no blue bands

बड़ी काली मधुमक्खी — नीली पट्टी नहीं

Blue-banded Bee: A robust, solitary bee with shiny bright metallic blue bands on a black abdomen, buzzing loudly on flowers during the warm season, is an Amegilla species.

Carpenter Bee: A very large, glossy, all-black bee with dark iridescent wings and a loud hum, at big flowers or a round hole in dry bamboo, is the carpenter bee.
