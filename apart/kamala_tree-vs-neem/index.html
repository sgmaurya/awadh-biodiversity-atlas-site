Kamala Tree or Neem? रोहिनी या नीम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रोहिनीKamala TreeMallotus philippensis
नीमNeemAzadirachta indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Kamala Tree profile puts it

Pinnate leaves; small bitter fruits; evergreen

पंखदार पत्तियाँ; कड़वे फल

Kamala Tree: A medium deciduous tree with large simple leaves and distinctive bright red hairy fruits is Kamala Tree.

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.
