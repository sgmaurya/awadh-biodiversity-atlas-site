Lesser Banded Hornet: Medium-sized (15–20 mm) yellow and black hornet with thin bands and a yellow face; more aggressive than Oriental Hornet: Lesser Banded Hornet.

Oriental Hornet: Large (18–25 mm), yellow and black banded hornet with robust body and smooth sting; papery nest on buildings or trees: Oriental Hornet.
