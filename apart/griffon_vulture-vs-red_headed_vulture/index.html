How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-headed Vulture, as the Griffon Vulture profile puts it

Red-headed Vulture]] (Sarcogyps calvus)

Black, with a bare red head and neck, red legs and white patches at the thighs

Griffon Vulture: The immense wingspan, sandy-tawny body plumage, contrasting dark flight feathers, and distinct white ruff around the neck distinguish the Griffon Vulture.

Red-headed Vulture: A very large dark vulture with a distinctive bright red bare head and neck. No other large vulture in eastern UP shows this red head coloration. Soars at great heights; descends to carrion.
