Barn Owl or Indian Eagle-Owl? उल्लू या बड़ा उल्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

उल्लूBarn OwlTyto alba
बड़ा उल्लूIndian Eagle-OwlBubo bengalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Eagle-Owl, as the Barn Owl profile puts it

Much larger; prominent ear tufts; large orange eyes; heavily streaked brown plumage

बड़ा उल्लू — बड़ा आकार, आँख के ऊपर कलगी, नारंगी आँखें

Barn Owl: Medium-sized ghostly white owl with a distinct heart-shaped white facial disc, dark eyes, and a silent flight near buildings at night is a Barn Owl.

Indian Eagle-Owl: Very large owl with bright orange eyes, prominent vertical ear tufts, and heavily streaked brown plumage nesting on clay cliffs or ruins is an Indian Eagle-Owl.

Bare PartsPale pinkish-horn bill; legs are long and feathered down to the pinkish-grey toesBill is greenish-black; powerful legs and toes are heavily feathered down to the black talons
SoundsDoes not hoot; produces a blood-curdling screech or a dry, hissing rattleDeep, resonant double hoot: bu-bo or tu-whoo, repeated at intervals
Head—Massive round head with prominent vertical ear tufts (feather horns) and large staring orange eyes
