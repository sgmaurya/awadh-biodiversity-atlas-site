How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Fish Owl, as the Dusky Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Brown Fish Owl]] (Ketupa zeylonensis)

Coarsely streaked warm rufous-brown plumage, bare unfeathered lower tarsi, and prominent horizontal tufts; specialist fish catcher

Brown Fish Owl: Large brown-and-white owl with staring yellow eyes, ragged horizontal ear tufts, and bare yellow legs perching on branches overhanging water is a Brown Fish Owl.

Dusky Eagle-Owl: The uniform sooty-grey vermiculated plumage, erect dark ear tufts, bright lemon-yellow eyes, feathered tarsi, and preference for wooded water margins distinguish the Dusky Eagle-Owl.
