How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Cormorant, as the Oriental Darter profile puts it

Shorter neck; hooked bill; winter visitor

बड़ा पनकौवा

Great Cormorant: Great Cormorant is the largest cormorant in the region, nearly twice the size of Little Cormorant. Its large size, long neck, and large hooked bill are diagnostic. Note its yellow throat pouch (if visible) and bright green eyes in breeding birds.

Oriental Darter: The Oriental Darter is distinctive — the only water bird in the region with a long, slender, snake-like neck and a straight, sharply pointed bill. When swimming, only the neck and head are visible above water.
