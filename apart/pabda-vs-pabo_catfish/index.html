How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pabda, as the Pabo Catfish profile puts it

Lower jaw projects; maxillary barbels much longer, extending beyond anal fin origin; anal fin has 54–66 rays

Pabda: A small, flattened catfish with two dark spots (one on gill cover, one on tail base) and a benthic habit is a pabda.

Pabo Catfish: An elongate, scaleless silurid catfish lacking an adipose fin, with an equal or included lower jaw, short maxillary barbels not extending past the eye, and an anal fin of 66–71 rays, is Ompok pabo.

Anal Fin—Exceptionally long, carrying 66 to 71 branched rays; adipose fin absent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
