Eucalyptus or Shisham? सफेदा या शीशम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सफेदाEucalyptusEucalyptus tereticornis
शीशमShishamDalbergia sissoo

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Shisham, as the Eucalyptus profile puts it

Leaves compound, smaller; not aromatic; fruit a single dry pod

कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ, एक फली

Eucalyptus: A tall, straight tree with peeling white-grey bark, blue-grey lance-shaped leaves, and a powerful eucalyptus odour when crushed is eucalyptus.

Shisham: Compound leaves with 3 to 5 alternating, roundish leaflets ending in a sharp point, zig-zag leaf stalks, and thin papery pods identify Dalbergia sissoo.
