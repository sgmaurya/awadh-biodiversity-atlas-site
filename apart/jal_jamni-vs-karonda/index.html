Broom Creeper or Karonda? जल जमनी या करौंदा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Karonda, as the Broom Creeper profile puts it

Climbing shrub; leaves opposite, glossy; white flowers fragrant; dark purple berries

पत्तियाँ जोड़े में, फूल सफेद, खुशबूदार

Broom Creeper: Soft, non-woody climber + soft-hairy, cordate leaves + inconspicuous tiny flowers + small red berries = Cocculus hirsutus. The softness of the entire plant (stem and leaf hairs) distinguishes it from woody climbers.

Karonda: A spiny shrub with small, glossy, opposite leaves and dark purple, edible berries is karonda.
