How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pygmy Dartlet, as the Milky Dartlet Damselfly profile puts it

Pygmy Dartlet]])

Male abdomen green and black with bright orange terminal segments; entirely lacks chalky-white abdomen

Milky Dartlet Damselfly: A miniature damselfly flying very low through marsh grass whose male exhibits a gleaming chalky milky-white abdomen against a black-and-green thorax is the Milky Dartlet Damselfly.

Pygmy Dartlet: The tiniest damselfly of the region — body length under 22 mm, bright metallic blue (male) with black rings, in shallow vegetation-rich water.
