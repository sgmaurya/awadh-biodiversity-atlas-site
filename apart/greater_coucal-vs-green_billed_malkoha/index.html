How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Coucal, as the Green-billed Malkoha profile puts it

Greater Coucal]] (Centropus sinensis)

Much heavier build; glossy blue-black body with contrasting chestnut wings; heavy black bill; ruby-red eyes

Greater Coucal: Pheasant-like shape, glossy black body contrasting with bright chestnut wings, bright red eyes, and a slow walking habit on the ground identify the Greater Coucal.

Green-billed Malkoha: The slender greenish-grey body, bright apple-green bill, crimson orbital skin, and long graduated tail with white spots distinguish the Green-billed Malkoha from all heavy-bodied coucals.

Habit—Secretive arboreal cuckoo creeping through dense vine tangles
Upperparts—Oily dark green with metallic bluish-green sheen on mantle and wings
Underparts—Ashy-grey throat and breast washed with ochraceous buff; blackish belly
Eye Patch—Conspicuous bare patch of crimson-red orbital skin surrounding the eye
Tail—Very long, graduated, dark green-black; all rectrices tipped with white spots
Demeanour—Creeping deliberately through dense creepers like an arboreal rodent
