How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rosy Starling, as the Chestnut-tailed Starling profile puts it

Rosy Starling]] (Sturnus roseus)

Black and rose-pink (non-breeding); glossy black (breeding)

Chestnut-tailed Starling: Chestnut-tailed Starling is pale ashy-grey with chestnut tail visible in flight. White spots on underparts help distinguish from other starlings. Gregarious and vocal; often in large flocks at fruiting trees.

Rosy Starling: Medium-sized starling with a pastel rose-pink body, contrasting glossy black head, wings, and tail, flying in large, noisy, synchronized flocks in winter, is a Rosy Starling.
