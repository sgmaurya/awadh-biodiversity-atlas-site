How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Gull, as the White Orange-tip profile puts it

Common Gull]])

Chalky white wings heavily lined with greenish-black veins beneath; lacks any orange patch

Common Gull: Small white butterfly with black-tipped wings and swift, low flight over open grassland, is the Common Gull.

White Orange-tip: A medium-sized chalk-white pierid butterfly with a brilliant fiery orange patch enclosed in a broad black apical area on the forewing, flying around thorny caper scrub, is the White Orange-tip.
