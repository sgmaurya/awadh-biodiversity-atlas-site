How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pioneer, as the Yellow Orange Tip profile puts it

Pioneer]] (Belenois aurota)

White with black apex containing white patch; lacks orange coloration

Pioneer: A small pure white butterfly with a bold black apex containing a white patch on the forewing, flying over grassland or Brassica fields, is the Pioneer.

Yellow Orange Tip: A small white butterfly with a bright yellow-orange apex on the forewing, flying over gardens and fields, is the Yellow Orange Tip.
