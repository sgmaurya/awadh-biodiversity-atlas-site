How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spinach, as the Malabar Spinach profile puts it

Smaller, more delicate leaves; winter crop; different family

अलग पत्तियाँ, सर्दी की फसल

Malabar Spinach: A soft, fleshy, succulent climbing vine with thick waxy leaves and pink flowers, grown on trellises for tender green leaves, distinguishes Malabar spinach.

Spinach: Spinach (Spinacia oleracea) is recognized by its triangular-hastate leaf bases and dioecious, spiny or rounded axillary fruit clusters, whereas palak beet has thick ovate leaves and bisexual flowers.
