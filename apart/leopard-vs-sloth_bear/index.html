Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Leopard, as the Sloth Bear profile puts it

Leopard]] (Panthera pardus)

Medium spotted felid; golden coat with rosettes; agile climber; entirely different morphology

Leopard: The medium-to-large feline build, rich golden-yellow coat densely patterned with black rose-like rosettes, and white ear-spots identify the Leopard with certainty.

Sloth Bear: The completely shaggy black coat, pale protruding snout lacking upper front teeth, ivory-white sickle claws, and white chest chevron distinguish the Sloth Bear from all other Indian mammals.

Head MarksSolid black spots on forehead, cheeks, and muzzle; rounded ears with black back—
DemeanourFluid, silent, low-slung stalking gait; readily hauls prey into tree forks—
Chest Mark—Distinctive whitish or pale cream-coloured V- or Y-shaped marking on the chest
Muzzle—Long, mobile, pale dirty-white or mealy snout with protrusible lips
Claws—Enormous, curved, non-retractable, ivory-white claws on the forefeet
Forequarters—Heavily built, powerful shoulders and forelegs with rolling, lumbering gait
Feeding Sound—Incredibly loud sucking and huffing noises audible from afar while excavating
