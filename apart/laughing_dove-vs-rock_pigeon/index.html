Laughing Dove or Rock Pigeon? छोटी पंडुक या कबूतर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटी पंडुकLaughing DoveSpilopelia senegalensis
कबूतरRock PigeonColumba livia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rock Pigeon, as the Laughing Dove profile puts it

Much larger; stocky; blue-grey; lacks chequered neck patch; rolling coo

कबूतर — बहुत बड़ा और गठीला शरीर

Laughing Dove, as the Rock Pigeon profile puts it

Smaller; slender; rufous-brown; lacks wing bars; has a chequered collar; laughing call

छोटी पंडुक — बहुत छोटी और भूरी

Laughing Dove: Small size, warm pinkish-brown breast, distinctive rufous-and-black chequered collar on the front of the neck, and bubbling chuckling calls.

Rock Pigeon: Stocky body, slate-grey colouration (wild type) with two black wing bars, white rump, pink legs, and rolling groo-coo vocalizations identify the Rock Pigeon.
