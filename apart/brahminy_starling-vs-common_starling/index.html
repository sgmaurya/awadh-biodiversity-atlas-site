How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brahminy Starling, as the Common Starling profile puts it

Brahminy Starling]] (Sturnus pagodarum)

Resident; crested head; buff on head and neck; different range

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.

Common Starling: Common Starling in winter is a dark bird heavily spotted with white, walking on the ground in flocks. If the bird shows white spotting only on head and neck with buff coloring, or if it is crested, it is a different species.
