How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-wattled Lapwing, as the White-tailed Lapwing profile puts it

Yellow-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus malabaricus)

Smaller; yellow wattles on face; yellow bill

White-tailed Lapwing: A small pale sand-brown lapwing with distinctive white tail (visible perched and in flight), yellow legs, and loud whistling call is a White-tailed Lapwing. The white tail is diagnostic.

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
