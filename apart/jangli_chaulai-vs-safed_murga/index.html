Similar size; flower spikes greenish, more loose; leaves slightly different shape

Slender Amaranth: A slender, erect herb with alternate, smooth, ovate green leaves (not powdery, not spiny, not fleshy) and very thin greenish flower spikes is jangli chaulai.

Silver Cockscomb: An erect annual with narrow, lance-shaped green leaves and dense, tall, silvery-white flower spikes in monsoon gardens — that has edible tender leaves — is Celosia argentea.
