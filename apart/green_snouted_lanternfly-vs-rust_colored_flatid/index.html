Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rust-colored Flatid, as the Green-snouted Lanternfly profile puts it

Uniform rust-brown; moth-like; wings held tent-like; no long cephalic horn

कत्थई-भूरा रंग, तंबू जैसे पंख, कोई सींग नहीं

Green-snouted Lanternfly: Very large planthopper with a long, slender, upturned emerald-green cephalic snout dotted with white, reticulate green forewings, and brilliant turquoise-blue hindwings identifies Pyrops viridirostris.

Rust-colored Flatid: Broad wedge-shaped, moth-like planthopper with uniform rust-brown wings held vertically tent-like over the body and densely reticulate costal margins identifies Flata ferrugata.
