How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Cormorant, as the Oriental Darter profile puts it

Much smaller (50–55 cm); hooked bill

पनकौवा

Little Cormorant: A small, all-black diving bird in freshwater that surfaces far from where it dived and perches with wings spread open — is a Little Cormorant. The glossy black colour and diving habit are definitive.

Oriental Darter: The Oriental Darter is distinctive — the only water bird in the region with a long, slender, snake-like neck and a straight, sharply pointed bill. When swimming, only the neck and head are visible above water.
