Scabrous Porecrust or Turkey Tail? स्कैब्रस पोरक्रस्ट या तुर्की पूँछ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

स्कैब्रस पोरक्रस्टScabrous PorecrustEarliella scabrosa
तुर्की पूँछTurkey TailTrametes versicolor

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Turkey Tail, as the Scabrous Porecrust profile puts it

Finely velvety, multi-coloured zones

Entirely round, small pores

Scabrous Porecrust: Check the rough zoned top and basal crust: a scabrous, reddish-brown zonate bracket with a distinct dark resinous crust near the wood attachment and daedaloid pore tendencies identifies Earliella scabrosa.

Turkey Tail: Thin, leathery, fan-shaped bracket growing in overlapping shelves on dead wood, with multicolored concentric velvety bands on top, a white pore surface below, and white spore print, is Turkey Tail (Trametes).

Rot typeCauses intense white rot, bleaching and softening decayed hardwood timber—

Concentric zones—Clearly marked, multicolor bands of cream, tan, chocolate-brown, rusty-orange, and slate-grey
Cap surface—Covered in very fine, soft, velvety hairs, with alternating hairy and smooth bands

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
