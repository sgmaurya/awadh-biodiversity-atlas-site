Freshwater Pond Snail or Red-rimmed Melania? मीठे पानी का घोंघा या जल घोंघा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Freshwater Pond Snail, as the Red-rimmed Melania profile puts it

Shell is thin, amber-colored, egg-shaped with a short pointed tip; lacks spiral ridges

ताल घोंघा — पतला अंडाकार खोल, पीला-भूरा रंग

Freshwater Pond Snail: Small size (15–30 mm), fragile, translucent yellowish shell, extremely large body whorl with a short, sharply pointed spire, and absence of an operculum identify Lymnaea acuminata.

Red-rimmed Melania: Small, screw-like conical shell (15–25 mm) with 10–12 whorls, light brown with distinctive reddish spots, crawling on muddy canal beds, is the Red-rimmed Melania.
