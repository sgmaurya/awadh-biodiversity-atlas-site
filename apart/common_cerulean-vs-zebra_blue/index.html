Common Cerulean: A small blue butterfly with males bright cerulean-blue and females paler blue, distinguished by the orange or red spot at the angle of the hindwing underside, is the Common Cerulean.

Zebra Blue: A tiny blue butterfly with males bright metallic blue and females dull brown, distinguished by the bold zigzag or zebra-like line pattern on the pale underside, is the Zebra Blue.
