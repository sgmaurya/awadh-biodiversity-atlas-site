Bayur or Peepal? कनक चंपा या पीपल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कनक चंपाBayurPterospermum acerifolium
पीपलPeepalFicus religiosa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peepal, as the Bayur profile puts it

Heart-shaped leaves, not lobed; fig fruits, not winged capsules

दिल के आकार की पत्तियाँ; अंजीर जैसे फल

Bayur: A forest tree with large, distinctly maple-like lobed leaves and fragrant white flowers with orange bands, appearing in spring when most other forest trees are still leafless, is Bayur.

Peepal: A heart-shaped leaf with a long thin drip-tip + a large open-crowned tree + figs in pairs at leaf bases + village or temple location = peepal. The drip-tip is the most reliable single feature.
