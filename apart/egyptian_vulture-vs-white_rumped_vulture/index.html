Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-rumped Vulture, as the Egyptian Vulture profile puts it

Much larger (77–104 cm); darker plumage with dark flight feathers; whitish rump; longer neck

बड़ा गिद्ध — बहुत बड़ा, पीठ सफ़ेद

Egyptian Vulture: Small vulture (smallest of all vultures in India) + predominantly white plumage + contrasting black flight feathers + pale bare yellow head and neck + buoyant soaring = Egyptian Vulture. No other small white raptor should be confused with it.

White-rumped Vulture: White-rumped Vulture is the largest dark brown vulture with a sharply contrasting white rump patch. The contrast between dark body and white rump is the most diagnostic feature. No other large vulture in the region shows this pattern.
