Asian Koel or House Crow? कोयल या कौआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कोयलAsian KoelEudynamys scolopaceus
कौआHouse CrowCorvus splendens

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Crow, as the Asian Koel profile puts it

Grey nape; heavier bill; different call; no red eye

House Crow: Medium size, grey collar around the neck contrasting with glossy black plumage, flat forehead, and flat kaaa calls identify the House Crow.
