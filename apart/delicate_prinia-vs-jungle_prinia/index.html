Delicate Prinia or Jungle Prinia? डेलिकेट प्रिनिया या जंगल प्रिनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Prinia, as the Delicate Prinia profile puts it

Jungle prinia is larger, darker, with longer tail; prefers heavier cover

—

Delicate Prinia: Delicate prinia is a very small, plainly coloured warbler with a distinctively long tail relative to body size. If the bird is larger and greyer, it is an ashy prinia. If in dense jungle rather than grassland, likely a jungle prinia.

Jungle Prinia: Largest prinia, featuring a heavy stout bill (jet-black in summer), dull earthy-brown mantle, and graduated tail with broad white feather tips.
