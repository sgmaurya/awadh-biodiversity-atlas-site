How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kentish Plover, as the Pacific Golden Plover profile puts it

Kentish Plover]] (Charadrius alexandrinus)

Smaller (15–17 cm); shorter legs; different structure; different habitat

Kentish Plover: Kentish Plover is distinguished by its pale coloration, orange-red legs, black lore patch, and white supercilium. The absence of a white eye-ring and relatively long legs separate it from Little Ringed Plover.

Pacific Golden Plover: A small active plover with long dark legs running on mudflats with golden spotting on crown and back, giving "chu-wee" calls is Pacific Golden Plover. The long legs and golden spotting are diagnostic compared to American Golden Plover.
