How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Silver Hatchet Chela, as the Flying Barb profile puts it

Silver Hatchet Chela]])

Deep compressed keel on chest; barbels minute or absent; body much deeper and hatchet-shaped

Flying Barb: A tiny, slender surface minnow with extremely long hair-like maxillary barbels extending to the pelvic fins, wing-like pectoral fins, and a dark lateral stripe bordered by gold, is Esomus danrica.

Silver Hatchet Chela: A tiny, deep, hatchet-shaped silvery cyprinid with an acute cutting ventral keel extending continuously from throat to vent, and a filamentous trailing pelvic fin ray, is Chela cachius.
