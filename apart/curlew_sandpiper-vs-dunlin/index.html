How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Curlew Sandpiper, as the Dunlin profile puts it

Curlew Sandpiper]] (Calidris ferruginea)

Longer legs, noticeably more decurved bill throughout its length, and distinct clean white square rump in flight

Curlew Sandpiper: Small shorebird with a noticeably downcurved slender black bill and bright white square rump patch revealed in flight.

Dunlin: The Dunlin is larger than the Little Stint with a noticeably drooped bill tip; it lacks the clean white unbarred rump and longer legs of the Curlew Sandpiper.
