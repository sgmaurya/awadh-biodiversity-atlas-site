Black Pond Turtle or Indian Roofed Turtle? ब्लैक पॉन्ड कछुआ या पचेरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Pond Turtle, as the Indian Roofed Turtle profile puts it

Highly domed, heavily patterned, less aquatic

ज़्यादा गोल, ज़्यादा दागदार

Black Pond Turtle: India's smallest turtle with a hard dark shell decorated with yellow or orange spots is a Black Pond Turtle. If it is larger or lacks spotting, it is a different species.

Indian Roofed Turtle: A small dark turtle with a peaked carapace, yellow head stripes, yellow-streaked limbs, and webbed feet basking on a river bank is an Indian Roofed Turtle and is entirely harmless and protected.
