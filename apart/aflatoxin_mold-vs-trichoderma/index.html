How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Aflatoxin Mold, as the Trichoderma profile puts it

Aflatoxin Mold: A yellow-green powdery mould on stored grain, groundnut or maize is treated as A. flavus and the grain as unsafe; the colour of the mould does not tell you how much toxin is present, so the rule is the grain, not the mould.

Trichoderma: A bright green, cottony to powdery growth spreading fast over damp wood, compost or the surface of a soil medium, with a sweet coconut-like smell in culture, is a Trichoderma; on fruit or bread it is more likely Penicillium, and on grain Aspergillus. Only culture or a laboratory separates the species.
