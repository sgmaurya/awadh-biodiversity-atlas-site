Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cardon's Ladybird, as the Black Scale-eating Ladybird profile puts it

Pale creamy-yellow with black M-mark on pronotum and black stripes

हल्का पीला रंग और काली पट्टियां

Black Scale-eating Ladybird, as the Cardon's Ladybird profile puts it

Sub-hemispherical; completely shining jet-black with brown head; feeds on scales

बिल्कुल गोल, चमकीला जेट-काला शरीर, शल्क कीट खाना

Black Scale-eating Ladybird: A highly convex, sub-hemispherical ladybird beetle of uniform glossy jet-black coloration with a reddish-brown head feeding on scale insects identifies Chilocorus nigritus.

Cardon's Ladybird: A smooth pale yellow oval ladybird with an inverted black M-shaped mark on the pronotum and dark sutural and lateral stripes identifies Anegleis cardoni.
