Common Sand Boa or Red Sand Boa? डोमेन साँप या दोमुँहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

डोमेन साँपCommon Sand BoaEryx conicus
दोमुँहाRed Sand BoaEryx johnii

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Sand Boa, as the Common Sand Boa profile puts it

Larger (75–100 cm); uniform reddish-brown or blackish color without blotches; tail is completely rounded like a peg

दुमुँहा लाल साँप — बिना धब्बों का कत्थई रंग

Common Sand Boa, as the Red Sand Boa profile puts it

Smaller (50–75 cm), more pale/tan colored, without the red tones, body more sausage-like

छोटा, पीला-भूरा, लाल नहीं

Common Sand Boa: Small, very stout snake (50–75 cm) with irregular dark brown blotches on a tan body, a wedge-shaped digging head, and a short blunt tail mimicking the head, is the Common Sand Boa (Domein).

Red Sand Boa: A small-to-medium, uniformly reddish or blackish stout snake (75–100 cm) with a blunt, head-like tail in loose sandy soils is a red sand boa. It is harmless, protected by law, and beneficial for rodent control. Do not harm it.
