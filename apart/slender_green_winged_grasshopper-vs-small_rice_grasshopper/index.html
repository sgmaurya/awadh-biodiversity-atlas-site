How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Rice Grasshopper, as the Slender Green-winged Grasshopper profile puts it

Small Rice Grasshopper]])

Body greener, thicker; distinct dark brown lateral band from eye along pronotum; bluish hind tibiae

Slender Green-winged Grasshopper: Slender streamlined body without heavy dark lateral stripe, transparent wings with delicate sea-green base, and reddish lower hind leg = Aiolopus thalassinus.

Small Rice Grasshopper: A slender green grasshopper with long tegmina surpassing the abdomen tip, a broad dark lateral stripe, and blue-green flattened swimming hind tibiae is Oxya hyla.
