How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bengal Bushlark, as the Striated Grassbird profile puts it

Bulkier; shorter tail; found in drier grass

नई बुलबुल

Bengal Bushlark: The stout conical bill, heavily dark-spotted breast, and prominent chestnut wing panels identify the Bengal Bushlark (Baker 1926, pp. 337–338).

Striated Grassbird: A dark, heavily streaked warbler of tall wet grasses and reed beds. Smaller and darker than Ashy Prinia. Secretive; identify by song—a distinctive rambling warble. Look in dense vegetation along water bodies.
