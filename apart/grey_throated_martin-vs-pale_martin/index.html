How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-throated Martin, as the Pale Martin profile puts it

Grey-throated Martin]] (Riparia chinensis)

Smaller, resident bird; uniform grey-brown throat and breast lacking distinct breast-band; darker greyish-brown underparts

Grey-throated Martin: Grey-throated Martin is small, dark brown above with pale grey throat, always in loose flocks over open areas near water. Barn Swallow is larger, with red face markings and deeply forked tail.

Pale Martin: The Pale Martin is identified by its pale sandy-brown upperparts, clean white underparts with an indistinct, blurry greyish breast-band, and slightly notched tail, contrasting with the uniform dusky throat of the Grey-throated Martin and the metallic blue plumage of the Barn Swallow.
