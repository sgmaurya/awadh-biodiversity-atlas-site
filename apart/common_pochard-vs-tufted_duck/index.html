Common Pochard or Tufted Duck? कॉमन पोचार्ड या टफ्टेड बत्तख?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन पोचार्डCommon PochardAythya ferina
टफ्टेड बत्तखTufted DuckAythya fuligula

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tufted Duck, as the Common Pochard profile puts it

Smaller; male has purple-black head with tuft; different colouration

बहुत छोटी, काली, कलगी

Common Pochard, as the Tufted Duck profile puts it

Larger; male has chestnut head and grey back (not black); different body pattern

बड़ी, लाल सिर, भूरी पीठ

Common Pochard: A medium-sized diving duck with chestnut-red head and grey back (males), or brown head with pale eye-ring (females), with distinctive grey-banded black bill — is a Common Pochard.

Tufted Duck: A small diving duck with a black head and distinctive drooping crest on the crown (males), or brown head with smaller crest (females) — is a Tufted Duck. The white flanks of males are distinctive. Females show white under tail.
