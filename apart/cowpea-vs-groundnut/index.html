Cowpea or Groundnut? लोबिया या मूँगफली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मूँगफलीGroundnutArachis hypogaea

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cowpea, as the Groundnut profile puts it

Taller (60–100 cm); leaves with 3 leaflets; pods above ground

काला चना — लंबा, फली ऊपर

Cowpea: A legume with long, slender pods and brown seeds bearing a characteristic white "eye" is cowpea.

Groundnut: A bushy annual legume with compound pinnate leaves (2 pairs of leaflets), yellow flowers, and the distinctive characteristic that fruit develops underground in pods containing 1–3 oily seeds is groundnut.
