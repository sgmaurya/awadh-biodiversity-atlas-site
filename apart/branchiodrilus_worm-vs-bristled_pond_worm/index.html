ब्रिसल्ड पॉन्ड वर्म

Branchiodrilus Worm, as the Bristled Pond Worm profile puts it

Distinct lateral finger-like gills projecting along body flanks; larger size; lacks eye-spots

ब्रैंकियोड्रिलस वर्म

Branchiodrilus Worm: A slender aquatic worm bearing numerous paired, finger-like lateral respiratory gills extending along its body flanks is Branchiodrilus hortensis.

Bristled Pond Worm: A minute, transparent aquatic worm with dark eye-spots and stiff dorsal bristles that lacks any respiratory gills on its body or tail is Nais barbata.
