How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Scops Owl, as the Oriental Scops Owl profile puts it

Indian Scops Owl]] (Otus bakkamoena)

Larger and greyer, with a pale collar on the hind neck and a soft single-note call, not the Oriental's frog-like phrase

Indian Scops Owl: Small owl + prominent ear tufts + soft "poo-poo-poo" call + cavity roosting = Indian Scops Owl. The ear tufts are diagnostic and visible even in silhouette at night.

Oriental Scops Owl: The diminutive myna-sized build, prominent erectile ear tufts, bright lemon-yellow eyes, and monotonous frog-like whistling call distinguish the Oriental Scops Owl.
