Castor or Jatropha? अरंडी या रतनजोत?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अरंडीCastorRicinus communis
रतनजोतJatrophaJatropha curcas

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jatropha, as the Castor profile puts it

Smaller leaves with 3–5 shallow lobes, smooth not spiny fruit

पत्ती छोटी, फल चिकना

Castor, as the Jatropha profile puts it

Larger plant, up to 4 m; seeds are larger, with caruncles; more commonly cultivated

अरंडी — बड़ा पौधा, बड़े बीज

Castor: Giant palmately-cut leaves whose stalk joins the blade in from the edge, plus soft-spined round capsules with mottled seeds, is castor.

Jatropha: A shrub with large, hand-shaped lobed leaves, red flowers, and toxins in all parts is jatropha. Never ingest the seeds or leaves.
