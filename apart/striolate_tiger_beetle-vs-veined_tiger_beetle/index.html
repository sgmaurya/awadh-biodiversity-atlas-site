Striolate Tiger Beetle or Veined Tiger Beetle? धारीदार बाघ भृंग या नसदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striolate Tiger Beetle, as the Veined Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Markings form thin wavy longitudinal striations rather than broad confluent veins

बारीक लहरदार धारियां, नसों जैसी चौड़ी धारियां नहीं

Striolate Tiger Beetle: A small bronze tiger beetle on sandy shores with intricate narrow wavy whitish striations and marginal lunules on the elytra identifies Lophyra striolata.

Veined Tiger Beetle: A small bronze tiger beetle on river sand with broad, confluent ivory-white markings resembling leaf veins identifies Cylindera venosa.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Striolate Tiger BeetleVeined Tiger Beetle
HabitSmall, extremely agile tiger beetle inhabiting sandy and gravelly shoresSmall, fast-running tiger beetle frequenting sandy and gravelly riverbeds

LabrumPale yellowish, transverse with a small central toothPale testaceous, short and transverse with small dark apical teeth
LocomotionRapid sprinting combined with frequent low buzzing flights over sandScurrying sprints across river sand, flying short distances when alarmed
