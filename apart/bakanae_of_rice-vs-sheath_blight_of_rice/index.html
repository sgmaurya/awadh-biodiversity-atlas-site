Bakanae Disease of Rice or Sheath Blight of Rice? बकाने रोग या शीथ ब्लाइट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बकाने रोगBakanae Disease of RiceFusarium fujikuroi
शीथ ब्लाइटSheath Blight of RiceRhizoctonia solani

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sheath Blight of Rice, as the Bakanae Disease of Rice profile puts it

Oval lesions on the leaf sheaths at tillering; no elongation; seedlings normal

पर्णच्छद अंगमारी — बाद में, दागों से

Bakanae Disease of Rice: Abnormally tall, thin, pale seedlings scattered among normal ones in the nursery identify bakanae. Healthy seedlings in same bed confirm seed-borne origin.

Sheath Blight of Rice: Brown, water-soaked, coalescing lesions on leaf sheaths that girdle the stem in warm, wet monsoon conditions = sheath blight.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Bakanae Disease of RiceSheath Blight of Rice
Seedling heightAbnormally tall and slender, standing well above the healthy seedlings around it—
Leaf colourPale yellow to chlorotic (lack of green pigment); thin, spindly leaves—
Leaf appearanceThin, wiry, drawn-out; older leaves may show reddish or brownish tints—
Plant structureWeak, spindly stems; cannot stand upright; collapse under water or weight—
Root systemPoorly developed; sparse; dark or necrotic; weak anchor roots—
Nursery patternScattered affected plants among normal ones, because the infection came with individual seeds—
Initial lesion—Oval to circular water-soaked spots on leaf sheaths, starting at the base of the leaf
Lesion color—Tan to brown, with irregular dark brown or reddish margins; appears greasy
Lesion spread—Lesions coalesce and may wrap around the sheath, girding the stem
Lesion surface—May show concentric rings or a target-like pattern
Blade symptoms—Lesions on blades are more angular and irregular; confined between veins
Advanced stage—Severe infection kills the entire leaf sheath and can extend to adjacent sheaths
Sclerotia—In wet conditions, small hard bodies (sclerotia, size of mustard seeds) may be visible on lesions

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
