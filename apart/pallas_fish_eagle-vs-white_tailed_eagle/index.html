How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pallas's Fish Eagle, as the White-tailed Eagle profile puts it

Pallas's Fish Eagle]] (Haliaeetus leucoryphus)

Conspicuous broad white band across dark tail, pale golden-buff head and neck, dark rufous-brown body (Baker 1928, p. 112)

Pallas's Fish Eagle: The immense size, pale creamy-buff hood contrasting with dark rufous body, and broad white band across the middle of the dark tail distinguish Pallas's Fish Eagle.

White-tailed Eagle: The massive size, pale yellowish head and neck, huge yellow bill, unfeathered yellow lower tarsus, and all-white tail identify the adult White-tailed Eagle (Baker 1928, p. 110).
