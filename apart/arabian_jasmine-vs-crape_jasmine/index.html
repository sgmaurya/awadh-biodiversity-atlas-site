Arabian Jasmine or Crape Jasmine? मोगरा या चाँदनी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crape Jasmine, as the Arabian Jasmine profile puts it

Crape Jasmine]] (Tabernaemontana divaricata)

Flowers are larger (2–3 cm) with spiral petals; no true jasmine; different family (Apocynaceae)

Arabian Jasmine, as the Crape Jasmine profile puts it

Usually climbers or trailing vines; leaves are smaller and more delicate; flowers are tubular with a narrow throat

जीवा — सफ़ेद, नली जैसे फूल; बेल

Arabian Jasmine: A low, much-branched shrub with small, white, intensely fragrant, star-shaped flowers and opposite, glossy, ovate leaves is Arabian Jasmine.

Crape Jasmine: A cultivated shrub with opposite, glossy, dark green leaves and clusters of small, white, intensely fragrant flowers whose petals are arranged in a spiral pinwheel pattern, is Crape Jasmine.
