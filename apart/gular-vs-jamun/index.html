Cluster Fig or Jamun? गूलर या जामुन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गूलरCluster FigFicus racemosa
जामुनJamunSyzygium cumini

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jamun, as the Cluster Fig profile puts it

Leaves shiny, not rough; fruits in panicles on branch tips; blue-black when ripe

चिकनी पत्ती, नीले काले फल

Cluster Fig: A tree with sandpaper-rough leaves and figs growing directly on the trunk in clusters is the cluster fig; no other tree in the district combines these characters.

Jamun: Large evergreen tree with opposite, smooth, oblong leaves smelling of turpentine when crushed, greenish-white flowers, and purple-black ovoid berries in June–July identify the Jamun tree.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Cluster FigJamun
SizeSmall to medium deciduous tree, 5–15 m, with a spreading crown—
LeavesSimple, alternate, ovate to elliptic, 5–15 cm, with entire or slightly lobed margins, rough texture aboveOpposite, simple, oblong-oval leaves with a smooth surface, emitting a turpentine-like smell when crushed
FruitsFleshy syconia (fig fruits) 2–4 cm across, growing directly from the trunk and older branches in racemes, yellow-brown ripening to reddish-brown—
BarkGreyish-brown, smooth, with white latex exuding from cuts—
The testLook for figs growing from the trunk and thick branches, not the twig tips — this is the signature of the cluster fig—
Size & Shape—Large evergreen tree (15–25 m tall) with a dense, rounded, shady canopy
Bark & Trunk—Smooth, light grey bark on young branches, becoming rough and dark grey on mature trunks
Flowers—Small, greenish-white, sweet-scented flowers appearing in dense branched clusters (cymes)
Fruit & Seeds—Smooth, oblong or ovoid berries (1.5–3.5 cm long), green turning dark purple-black when ripe, with a single large seed

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
