Blue Tiger or Striped Tiger Butterfly? ब्लू टाइगर तितली या बाघ तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्लू टाइगर तितलीBlue TigerTirumala limniace
बाघ तितलीStriped Tiger ButterflyDanaus genutia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Tiger Butterfly, as the Blue Tiger profile puts it

Orange-brown coloration; heavy black veins; lacks the blue tone

धारीदार बाघ तितली — नारंगी-भूरा, काली धारियाँ

Blue Tiger, as the Striped Tiger Butterfly profile puts it

Blue-black with pale spots — no orange; larger

Blue Tiger: A dark blue butterfly with white spots on both wings, slow fluttering flight, and presence near milkweed plants identifies the Blue Tiger.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Blue TigerStriped Tiger Butterfly
SizeMedium-sized butterfly; body length 28–35 mm; wingspan 75–90 mm—
ColorationDeep blue or navy-blue on the dorsal surface; white spotting on both wings—
ForewingDark blue with a white oval patch and a row of white spots along the outer margin—
HindwingDark blue with a white discal patch (central spot) and white marginal spots—
UnderpartsPaler blue with darker markings and prominent white spots—
FlightSlow, weak, fluttering flight close to the groundSlow, gliding, unhurried — classic aposematic butterfly behaviour
Wingspan—70–95 mm — large butterfly
Upperside—Deep orange-tawny with heavy black venation; black border with two rows of white spots
Underside—Paler orange; same pattern; white spots in border
Body—Black with white spots; abdomen black
Similar species—Plain Tiger (D. chrysippus): orange but WITHOUT heavy black venation — looks plainer
