Milky Mushroom or Oyster Mushroom? दूधिया मशरूम या ढींगरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दूधिया मशरूमMilky MushroomCalocybe indica
ढींगरीOyster MushroomPleurotus ostreatus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oyster Mushroom, as the Milky Mushroom profile puts it

Fan-shaped cap; decurrent gills (running down stalk); off-centre stalk

ढींगरी, पंखे जैसा

Milky Mushroom: Pure white, round-capped mushroom growing in tight clusters on straw, with white free gills and white stalk = milky mushroom.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Milky MushroomOyster Mushroom
CapWhite to cream; 4–8 cm; convex when young, flattening with ageFan-shaped to broadly convex; 5–20 cm; smooth wavy margin
Cap textureSmooth, unblemished; silky sheen—
GillsWhite; closely crowded; free from stalk (not running down)White; crowded; decurrent (running down stalk); forking near base
StalkWhite, cylindrical, 3–5 cm; solid; base may have small bulbous expansionShort (1–3 cm); off-centre or lateral; white; firm
Spore printWhiteWhite to lilac-white
OdourMild, pleasant mushroom aroma—
GrowthAlways in clusters on straw substrate; never from soilAlways in overlapping tiered clusters on wood — never from soil
Cap colour—Grey to blue-grey when young; fades to cream or pale brown with age
Smell—Pleasant, distinctly mushroomy

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
