Amaltas or Bahera? अमलतास या बहेड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अमलतासAmaltasCassia fistula
बहेड़ाBaheraTerminalia bellirica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Amaltas, as the Bahera profile puts it

Smaller tree; leaves compound; fruit pods long, round, black, smooth

कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ, काली लंबी फली

Amaltas: Medium-sized tree with pinnately compound leaves, long cascading racemes of bright yellow flowers in summer, and dark cylindrical woody pods persisting for months identify the Amaltas tree.

Bahera: A large tree with leaves bunched at twig ends, hard winged grey-brown fruits, and leaflessness in April is bahera.
