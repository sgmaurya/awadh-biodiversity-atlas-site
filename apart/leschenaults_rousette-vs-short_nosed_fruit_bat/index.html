How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Short-nosed Fruit Bat, as the Leschenault's Rousette profile puts it

Cynopterus sphinx

Noticeably smaller (forearm length up to 74 mm; Menon 2014), with distinctive white-edged ears and wing bones, roosting among palm fronds

Leschenault's Rousette: Medium-sized brownish fruit bat with dog-like muzzle and short external tail, roosting in dark caves, temple vaults, and deep wells with tongue-clicking echolocation.

Greater Short-nosed Fruit Bat: A small brown bat hanging from a fruit tree with a short fox-like face and small ears is a Short-nosed Fruit Bat.
