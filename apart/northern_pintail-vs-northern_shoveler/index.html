Northern Pintail or Northern Shoveler? पिनटेल बत्तख या तिदारी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern Shoveler, as the Northern Pintail profile puts it

Northern Shoveler]] (Spatula clypeata)

Heavy, spoon-shaped black bill; male has a green head and chestnut flanks; lacks long pin-tail

Northern Pintail, as the Northern Shoveler profile puts it

Males have chocolate head and white breast, but bill fine and pointed

सुई हंस

Northern Pintail: Elegant, long-necked duck with a dark chocolate-brown head, white stripe running down the neck, and long pointed pin-like central tail feathers (male) is a Northern Pintail.

Northern Shoveler: The Northern Shoveler is the only duck in the region with a greatly enlarged, spatulate (spoon-shaped) bill. This feature is diagnostic at any distance.
