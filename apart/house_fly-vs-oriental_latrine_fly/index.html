House Fly or Oriental Latrine Fly? घरेलू मक्खी या हरी मक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घरेलू मक्खीHouse FlyMusca domestica
हरी मक्खीOriental Latrine FlyChrysomya megacephala

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Fly, as the Oriental Latrine Fly profile puts it

Grey-brown, smaller (5–6 mm), four dark thoracic stripes, never metallic green

भूरा, छोटा, धारियाँ हों

House Fly: Uniform grey-brown, small (6–7 mm), four dark thoracic stripes, found indoors and around food: House Fly.

Oriental Latrine Fly: A shiny green or metallic blue-green fly 6–8 mm, with a notably large head, found near latrines or decomposing matter, is the Oriental Latrine Fly.
