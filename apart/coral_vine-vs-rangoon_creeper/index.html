Coral Vine or Rangoon Creeper? कोरल वाइन या रंगून बेल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rangoon Creeper, as the Coral Vine profile puts it

रंगून बेल (Combretum indicum)]]

Woody twining vine without tendrils; flowers change from white to pink and dark red

Coral Vine, as the Rangoon Creeper profile puts it

कोरल वाइन (Antigonon leptopus)]]

Climbs by branched inflorescence tendrils; flowers uniformly rose-pink, not color-changing

Coral Vine: A climbing vine with deeply crinkled cordate leaves and long sprays of bright rose-pink flowers that terminate in delicate curly tendrils is Antigonon leptopus.

Rangoon Creeper: A woody climber with drooping clusters of long-tubed flowers that open pure white, turn pink, and darken to crimson on the same truss is Combretum indicum.
