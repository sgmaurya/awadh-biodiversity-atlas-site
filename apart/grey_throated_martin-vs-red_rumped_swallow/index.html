How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-rumped Swallow, as the Grey-throated Martin profile puts it

Red-rumped Swallow]] (Cecropis daurica)

Larger; reddish rump; buff undertail coverts

Grey-throated Martin: Grey-throated Martin is small, dark brown above with pale grey throat, always in loose flocks over open areas near water. Barn Swallow is larger, with red face markings and deeply forked tail.

Red-rumped Swallow: Red-rumped swallow shows a distinctive rufous rump and rusty throat. If the rump is dark and back is glossy dark blue, it is a barn swallow. If the tail has extremely long filaments beyond the fork, it may be a wire-tailed swallow.
