How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Six-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Cancellate Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Tiger Beetle]])

Greenish body with six discrete white spots on the margins; lacks branching latticed network

Cancellate Tiger Beetle: Metallic coppery-bronze tiger beetle of sandy river shores with an intricate, whitish-testaceous antler-like cancellate pattern across the elytra = Lophyra cancellata.

Six-spotted Tiger Beetle: A bright metallic green beetle, 10–15 mm, with white spots on its wing covers, running fast on bare sand near water and flying away when approached, is a tiger beetle.
