Black Drongo or Jungle Crow? भुजंगा या जंगली कौआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भुजंगाBlack DrongoDicrurus macrocercus
जंगली कौआJungle CrowCorvus macrorhynchos

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Drongo, as the Jungle Crow profile puts it

Much smaller; forked tail; white rictal spot; active aerial hunter

भुजंगा — बहुत छोटा, पूंछ दो हिस्सों में बंटी होती है

Black Drongo: A glossy black open-country bird on a wire, with a deep V-shaped forked tail and repeated sallies for insects, is Black Drongo in eastern UP farmland.

Jungle Crow: Large size, entirely glossy black plumage (no grey collar), steep forehead forming an angle with a massive arched black bill, and deep resonant calls.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Black DrongoJungle Crow
SizeMedium-sized bird (about 28 cm total length); slim, upright postureLarge bird (46–50 cm total length); robust and heavy-bodied
PlumageEntirely glossy blue-black (sheen visible in good light); juveniles duller brown-black—
TailLong and deeply forked (V-shaped or fish-tailed); outer feathers curve outward slightly—
Bill & LegsShort, black legs; fairly strong black bill with bristles at the base—
Favourite perchProminent lookouts: electricity wires, fence posts, bare branches, or cattle backs—
BehaviourSallies out to catch flying insects and returns to perch; mobs crows and raptors aggressively—
Colouration—Entirely glossy black plumage; lacks any grey collar; shows violet-blue sheen
Bill—Massive, thick, and black; upper mandible is distinctly arched (curved)
Head Shape—Domed forehead forming a steep, abrupt angle at the base of the bill
Throat—Throat feathers are glossy and hackle-like, showing a shaggy appearance
Call—Deep, resonant, hollow caw: kaaa-kaaa (deeper and more ringing than House Crow)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
