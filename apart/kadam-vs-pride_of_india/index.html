Kadam Tree or Pride of India? कदम्ब या जारुल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जारुलPride of IndiaLagerstroemia speciosa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pride of India, as the Kadam Tree profile puts it

Sometimes called "Kadamba" locally; but purple flowers, very different

Pride of India: A deciduous tree bare in dry season, with large glossy leaves, spectacular large crinkled-petal purple, pink, or white flowers in May–July, and flaking smooth bark with pale yellow inner wood.
