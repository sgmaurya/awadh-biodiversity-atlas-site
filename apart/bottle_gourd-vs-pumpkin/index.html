Bottle Gourd or Pumpkin? लौकी या कद्दू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pumpkin, as the Bottle Gourd profile puts it

Vine is massive, prostrate; flowers are yellow-orange; fruit is huge, yellow-orange, ribbed

कद्दू / कोहड़ा — पीले-नारंगी फूल, विशाल गोल पीले फल

Bottle Gourd, as the Pumpkin profile puts it

Flowers white, nocturnal; fruit bottle-shaped, becomes woody; different family

लौकी — सफेद फूल, रात को खिलते हैं, बोतल सा

Bottle Gourd: Climbing annual vine with broad velvety kidney-shaped leaves, large nocturnal white flowers, and elongated or bottle-shaped pale green edible fruits that dry into hard woody shells, is the Bottle Gourd (Lauki).

Pumpkin: A massive trailing vine with enormous palmately lobed leaves, large orange funnel-shaped flowers, and huge orange-brown thick-shelled round fruits is pumpkin; the mature vines are a landmark of monsoon fields.
