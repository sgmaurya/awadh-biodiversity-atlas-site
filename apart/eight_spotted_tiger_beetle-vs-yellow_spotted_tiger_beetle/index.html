Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle or Yellow-spotted Tiger Beetle? आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग या पीली-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Yellow-spotted Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Much larger; bright blue-green metallic sheen with eight spots

बहुत बड़ा आकार (20-24 मिमी), नीला-हरा रंग

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle: A large metallic coppery-blue tiger beetle on river sand with four bold creamy-yellow spots on each elytron (eight in total) identifies Cicindela octonotata.

Yellow-spotted Tiger Beetle: A medium-sized bronze tiger beetle on river sand with prominent discrete bright yellow rounded spots and a short middle band identifies Cicindela flavomaculata.
