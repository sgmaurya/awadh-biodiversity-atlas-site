How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Verditer Flycatcher, as the Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher profile puts it

Verditer Flycatcher]] (Eumyias thalassinus)

Larger (17 cm); verditer-blue plumage; winter visitor in higher elevations

Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher: Grey-headed Canary-flycatcher is a small grey-headed bird with yellow underparts that hangs upside-down in foliage. No other small flycatcher in eastern UP during winter combines these features.

Verditer Flycatcher: A small, blue and white flycatcher. Males are brilliantly blue-green; females duller but still blue. Hunts insects by sallying from exposed perches. Restless and active; characteristic clear whistled calls.
