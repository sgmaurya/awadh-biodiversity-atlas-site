How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Francolin, as the Common Quail profile puts it

Black Francolin]] (Francolinus francolinus)

Larger (33 cm); male entirely black; female darker overall

Black Francolin: Entirely black francolin with red legs and face patch = male Black Francolin. Brown, heavily barred = female Black Francolin. The distinctive loud call kataak-kataak is diagnostic.

Common Quail: Common Quail is a small, cryptic ground-dweller of grassland and stubble, identifiable by its small size, mottled plumage, and distinctive male call. The reluctant flight (short bursts) and ground behavior are characteristic.
