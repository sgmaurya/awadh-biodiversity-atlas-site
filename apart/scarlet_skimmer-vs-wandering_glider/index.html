How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wandering Glider, as the Scarlet Skimmer profile puts it

Wandering Glider]]

Golden-yellow body; flies continuously; does not perch frequently

Scarlet Skimmer: Medium-large size, uniform scarlet-red body with a flat, broad abdomen, thin black dorsal line, and lack of dark wing patches identify the Scarlet Skimmer.
