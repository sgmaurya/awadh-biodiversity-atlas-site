Red-vented Bulbul or Red-whiskered Bulbul? गुलदुम बुलबुल या गुलाबी बुलबुल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गुलदुम बुलबुलRed-vented BulbulPycnonotus cafer
गुलाबी बुलबुलRed-whiskered BulbulPycnonotus jocosus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-whiskered Bulbul, as the Red-vented Bulbul profile puts it

Prominent tall black crest; red patch behind the eye; white cheeks and underparts

सिपाही बुलबुल — ऊंची कलगी और सफेद गाल

Red-vented Bulbul, as the Red-whiskered Bulbul profile puts it

Lacks the red cheek patch; crest is short and tufted (not tall and pointed); scaly brown-black body; red under tail

देसी बुलबुल — लाल गाल का अभाव, छोटी कलगी, काला शरीर

Red-vented Bulbul: Black head with scaled breast, white rump, and a bright red vent patch is diagnostic for the Red-vented Bulbul. No other common bulbul in eastern UP has a red vent combined with a scaled black-and-brown breast.

Red-whiskered Bulbul: Medium-sized bulbul with a tall, pointed, forward-curving black crest, a bright red patch on its white cheek, and scarlet undertail coverts is a Red-whiskered Bulbul.
