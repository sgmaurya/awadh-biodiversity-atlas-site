How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bristled Grassbird, as the Indian Grassbird profile puts it

Chaetornis striata

Rounder ungraduated tail without white tips; five stiff rictal bristles along gape; streaked breast

Bristled Grassbird: Bristled Grassbird is a small, heavily streaked warbler of dense reed beds, with a loud harsh churring call. Its cryptic, streaked plumage and creeping behaviour through dense reeds distinguish it from other marsh warblers. The long tail and white underparts with brown flanks are diagnostic in good view.

Indian Grassbird: Secretive tall-grass dweller with unusually stout bill, boldly streaked black, rufous, and white mantle, and long graduated tail broadly tipped white.
