How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Sandpiper, as the Common Sandpiper profile puts it

Wood Sandpiper]] (Tringa glareola)

Prominently spotted upperparts; distinct white supercilium; longer, yellowish legs; lacks white wing-bar

Common Sandpiper: Small shorebird with olive-brown upperparts, white belly with a greyish smudge on the breast-side, constantly bobbing its tail, and flying low with stiff, bowed wings is a Common Sandpiper.

Wood Sandpiper: A spotted wader with yellowish legs and pale underwings, in small flocks at shallow wetlands — is a Wood Sandpiper. The bold spotting and yellow legs distinguish it from Green Sandpiper. Paler and more spotted than Green Sandpiper.
