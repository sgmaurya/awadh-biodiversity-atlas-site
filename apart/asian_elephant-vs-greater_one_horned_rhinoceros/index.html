Asian Elephant or Greater One-horned Rhinoceros? हाथी या गैंडा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गैंडाGreater One-horned RhinocerosRhinoceros unicornis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, as the Asian Elephant profile puts it

Greater One-horned Rhinoceros]] (Rhinoceros unicornis)

Massive odd-toed ungulate with single snout horn, deep skin armor folds, and no trunk

Asian Elephant, as the Greater One-horned Rhinoceros profile puts it

Asian Elephant]] (Elephas maximus)

Far larger proboscidean with elongated trunk, pillar limbs, lacking horn and armour shields

Asian Elephant: The immense columnar body, prominent double-domed forehead, elongated flexible trunk, and arched dorsal ridge immediately distinguish the Asian Elephant from all other animals.

Greater One-horned Rhinoceros: The massive armour-plated body with deep skin folds, three-toed feet, and single snout horn distinguish the Greater One-horned Rhinoceros from all other Asian megafauna.
