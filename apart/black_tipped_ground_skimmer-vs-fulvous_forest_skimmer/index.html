How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Fulvous Forest Skimmer, as the Black-tipped Ground Skimmer profile puts it

Fulvous Forest Skimmer]] (Neurothemis fulvia)

Larger; darker coloration; frequents forested areas near water

Black-tipped Ground Skimmer: A small, yellow-orange dragonfly with black-tipped wings, flying just above grass level in open grasslands, and repeatedly returning to the same low perch, is the Black-tipped Ground Skimmer.

Fulvous Forest Skimmer: Medium size, dark rust-brown wings with transparent black tips, and a preference for perching in shaded orchards and pond edges identify the Fulvous Forest Skimmer.
