How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plum-headed Parakeet, as the Alexandrine Parakeet profile puts it

Plum-headed Parakeet]] (Psittacula cyanocephala)

Smaller (33 cm); plum-coloured head in male; pale blue tail

Alexandrine Parakeet: Alexandrine Parakeet is the largest parakeet in the region. Males show a distinctive deep maroon (wine-red) patch on throat and breast. The long pointed tail and large size are unmistakable.

Plum-headed Parakeet: A small green parakeet with a distinctly blue-headed male and yellow-headed female, in noisy flocks, is a Plum-headed Parakeet. Larger parakeets with neck rings or red heads are different species.
