Common Pochard or Red-crested Pochard? कॉमन पोचार्ड या लालसर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन पोचार्डCommon PochardAythya ferina
लालसरRed-crested PochardNetta rufina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-crested Pochard, as the Common Pochard profile puts it

Larger; male has brighter red head and yellow legs; different silhouette

बड़ी, पीले पैर, अलग सिल्हूट

Common Pochard, as the Red-crested Pochard profile puts it

Male has grey back and black breast (not chestnut head); smaller

पीठ भूरी, छोटी

Common Pochard: A medium-sized diving duck with chestnut-red head and grey back (males), or brown head with pale eye-ring (females), with distinctive grey-banded black bill — is a Common Pochard.

Red-crested Pochard: A large diving duck with a chestnut-red head and bright yellow legs (males) — is a Red-crested Pochard. The yellow legs are diagnostic and unique among Indian pochards.
