How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-necked Falcon, as the Eurasian Hobby profile puts it

Red-necked Falcon]] (Falco chicquera)

Distinctive bright rufous crown and nape, white cheeks with narrow dark moustachial line, closely barred underparts (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Eurasian Hobby: The slaty-grey upperparts, bold black moustache contrasting with clean white throat, heavily streaked underparts, and rich rufous unstreaked thighs distinguish the Eurasian Hobby (Baker 1928, p. 42).

Red-necked Falcon: A small falcon with distinctive rufous cap and nape, blue-grey back, and white underparts with barring, soaring and hunting over grasslands and cultivated areas, is a Red-necked Falcon. The rufous head is diagnostic.

Soft PartsIris hazel to deep brown; bill pale slate with black tip; cere and orbital skin greenish-yellow; legs orange (Baker 1928, p. 42)—
Size—Small falcon; 25–28 cm long; compact
Plumage—Blue-grey back and wings; rufous cap and nape (red-neck)
Head—Rufous or red-tinged crown and nape; black "moustache" marks
Breast—Whitish to buff with fine rufous barring
Wings—Slate-blue or blue-grey
Tail—Blue-grey, dark-banded; relatively short and square
Feet—Yellow; powerful talons
Eyes—Dark; intense gaze
Behaviour—Soars and hunts from height; dives swiftly for small birds; vocal
Call—Loud, harsh screeching or kee-kee-kee calls
