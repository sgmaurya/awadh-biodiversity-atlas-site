How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cypress Vine, as the Obscure Morning Glory profile puts it

Feathery, cypress-like foliage; scarlet-red flowers

साइप्रेस जैसी पत्तियाँ, लाल फूल

Cypress Vine: A twining climber with extremely fine, feathery cypress-like foliage and bright scarlet star-shaped flowers is Cypress Vine.

Obscure Morning Glory: A hairy, slender climbing annual with small heart-shaped leaves and bright yellow morning flowers that close by afternoon is Obscure Morning Glory.
