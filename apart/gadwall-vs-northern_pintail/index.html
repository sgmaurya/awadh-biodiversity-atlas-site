Gadwall or Northern Pintail? गैडवॉल बत्तख या पिनटेल बत्तख?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गैडवॉल बत्तखGadwallMareca strepera
पिनटेल बत्तखNorthern PintailAnas acuta

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern Pintail, as the Gadwall profile puts it

Very slender neck; male has chocolate head and white breast; different shape

पतली गर्दन, अलग आकार

Gadwall: A medium-sized dabbling duck with grey-and-black coloration (males) and white speculum visible in flight — is a Gadwall. In flight, the white wing patch is diagnostic.

Northern Pintail: Elegant, long-necked duck with a dark chocolate-brown head, white stripe running down the neck, and long pointed pin-like central tail feathers (male) is a Northern Pintail.
