Asian Green Bee-eater or Blue-cheeked Bee-eater? हरा पतरंगा या ब्लू-चीक्ड बी-ईटर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Green Bee-eater, as the Blue-cheeked Bee-eater profile puts it

Smaller; no tail streamers; golden-yellow on throat; different head pattern

हरी मधु-चिड़िया

Asian Green Bee-eater: Small size, emerald-green body, golden-rufous wash on crown, black neck collar, red eyes, and two long black tail-streamers.

Blue-cheeked Bee-eater: Blue-cheeked Bee-eater shows turquoise-blue cheeks, green back, and elongated tail streamers. If the bird is smaller without tail streamers and has a golden-yellow throat, it is a Green Bee-eater. If the crown is chestnut or the bird is red overall, it is a different species.
