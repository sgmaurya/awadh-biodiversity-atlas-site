How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Body with brilliant metallic blue-green and yellow iridescent stripes; palps lack white brush tips (Caleb & Sankaran 2025)

Banded Phintella: A tiny, highly agile jumping spider with brilliant iridescent longitudinal bands of metallic blue-green and yellow running along both carapace and abdomen is Phintella vittata.

White-tipped Jumping Spider: A tiny jumping spider with a dark body, pale lateral stripes, and prominent white tips on its pedipalps and front leg tarsi, actively prowling low foliage without a web, is Icius alboterminus.
