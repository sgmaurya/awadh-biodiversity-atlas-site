Elongate Glassy Perchlet or Mola Carplet? चंदा या मोला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mola Carplet, as the Elongate Glassy Perchlet profile puts it

Silvery longitudinal band; not transparent; deeper body; herbivorous

महुआ / मोआ — चाँदी जैसी पट्टी, अपारदर्शी

Elongate Glassy Perchlet: Small size (under 8 cm), flat body, complete glass-like transparency showing internal skeleton, and projecting lower jaw identify the Elongate Glassy Perchlet.

Mola Carplet: A small deep-bodied silvery-golden fish with a tiny inferior mouth and rounded fins in aquatic weeds is the mola; the depth of body and small mouth distinguish it.
