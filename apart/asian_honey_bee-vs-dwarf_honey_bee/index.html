Asian Honey Bee or Dwarf Honey Bee? मधुमक्खी या छोटी मधुमक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मधुमक्खीAsian Honey BeeApis cerana
छोटी मधुमक्खीDwarf Honey BeeApis florea

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dwarf Honey Bee, as the Asian Honey Bee profile puts it

Very small (7–8 mm); single small exposed comb in shrubs; red-brown front abdomen

Asian Honey Bee, as the Dwarf Honey Bee profile puts it

Larger (10–12 mm); nests in dark cavities or boxes; abdomen has yellow-black stripes without an orange base

भारतीय मधुमक्खी / मौन — बड़ा आकार, दरारों में छत्ता

Dwarf Honey Bee: Tiny size (7–10 mm), black body with a bright orange-red base of the abdomen, and a small, exposed, single comb wrapped around a thin twig identify the Dwarf Honey Bee.
