Gangetic Ailia or Indian Potasi? गैंगेटिक आइलिया मछली या इंडियन पोटासी मछली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Potasi]])

Gangetic Ailia, as the Indian Potasi profile puts it

Gangetic Ailia]])

Dorsal fin completely absent; adipose fin very small; body more paper-thin and translucent

Gangetic Ailia: An elongate, paper-thin, translucent silvery catfish completely lacking a rayed dorsal fin, possessing a tiny adipose fin, eight long barbels, and an anal fin of 58–75 rays, is Ailia coila.

Indian Potasi: A slender, elongate, midwater-schooling scaleless catfish with eight barbels, a small dorsal fin, a long anal fin of 33–46 rays, and 3–4 distinct dark longitudinal stripes along the body, is Pachypterus atherinoides.
