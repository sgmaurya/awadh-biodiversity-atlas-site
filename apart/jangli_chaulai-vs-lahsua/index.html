Slender Amaranth or Lahsua? जंगली चौलाई या लहसुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जंगली चौलाईSlender AmaranthAmaranthus viridis
लहसुआLahsuaDigera muricata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lahsua, as the Slender Amaranth profile puts it

Leaves fleshy, often reddish-tinged; more prostrate growth; larger plant

पत्तियाँ रसदार, लाल रंग के

Slender Amaranth, as the Lahsua profile puts it

Leaves more finely hairy; flower spikes more prominent and elongated; less fleshy overall

पत्ती बाल-बाल वाली; स्पाइक लंबे

Slender Amaranth: A slender, erect herb with alternate, smooth, ovate green leaves (not powdery, not spiny, not fleshy) and very thin greenish flower spikes is jangli chaulai.

Lahsua: A low, spreading, fleshy herb with soft, alternate, obovate leaves often tinged reddish, and tiny inconspicuous flowers in axillary clusters is lahsua. The whole plant is succulent and palatable to livestock.
