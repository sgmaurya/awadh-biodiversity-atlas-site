Siberian Rubythroat or White-tailed Rubythroat? साइबेरियन रूबीथ्रोट या व्हाइट-टेल्ड रूबीथ्रोट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-tailed Rubythroat, as the Siberian Rubythroat profile puts it

White-tailed Rubythroat]] (Luscinia pectoralis)

Tail has broad pure white base and white tips on outer feathers; upperparts slaty-grey; broad black breast band

Siberian Rubythroat, as the White-tailed Rubythroat profile puts it

Siberian Rubythroat]] (Luscinia calliope)

Entire tail uniform olive-brown with no white base or tips; white moustachial and superciliary stripes; warm olive-brown upperparts

Siberian Rubythroat: The brilliant ruby-scarlet throat, double white facial stripes (eyebrow and cheek), uniform olive-brown upperparts, and plain brown unpatterned tail distinguish the male Siberian Rubythroat.

White-tailed Rubythroat: The striking pure white base and white tips on the outer tail feathers, slate-grey upperparts, and brilliant ruby throat bordered by black identify the male White-tailed Rubythroat.
