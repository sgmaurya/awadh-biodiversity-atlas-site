How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brooke's House Gecko, as the Kushmore's Gecko profile puts it

Hemidactylus brookii

Carries distinctly larger, sharply keeled trihedral tubercles arranged in 14 to 16 prominent rows across the dorsum (Smith 1935)

Brooke's House Gecko: A gecko with prominently rough, keeled, bark-like skin found climbing tree trunks and stone walls at night is Brooke's House Gecko.

Kushmore's Gecko: Small sandy-coloured gecko with 16 to 18 rows of moderate keeled tubercles inhabiting dry rural masonry and earth banks rather than polished domestic walls.
