Pale Grass Blue: A very small pale blue butterfly; males pale lilac-blue, females brownish; ventral pale with sparse spots; weak, low flight; in grassland and pulse fields.

Tiny Grass Blue: The tiniest blue butterfly fluttering just inches above village lawns and weed patches, displaying a distinct dark dot inside the forewing cell on its pale underside, is the Tiny Grass Blue.
