How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bachcha, as the Murius Vacha profile puts it

Eutropiichthys vacha

Gape of mouth extends well beyond the posterior margin of the eye; grows to larger adult size

Bachcha: A small (10–18 cm), silvery catfish with 4 barbels and a forked tail, often found in schools in flooded areas, is a Bachcha.

Murius Vacha: Silvery pelagic catfish with 4 pairs of barbels, sharp dorsal spine, and mouth cleft stopping at anterior eye.
