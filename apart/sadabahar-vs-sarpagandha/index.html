Sadabahar or Sarpagandha? सदाबहार या सर्पगंधा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सदाबहारSadabaharCatharanthus roseus
सर्पगंधाSarpagandhaRauvolfia serpentina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sarpagandha, as the Sadabahar profile puts it

Forest understory; lanceolate leaves opposite or in threes; glossy; forest habitat

वन की झाड़ी, पत्तियाँ लंबी

Sadabahar, as the Sarpagandha profile puts it

Pink or purple flowers; also toxic; cultivated, not wild forest; leaves more oval

गुलाबी-बैंगनी फूल, खेत में

Sadabahar: Herbaceous sprawling shrub, opposite oval leaves, 5-petalled flat-faced flowers with a contrasting dark eye, prolific fruiting on wasteground — sadabahar. Do not eat.

Sarpagandha: Glossy lanceolate leaves opposite or in threes, strong distinctive scent when crushed, small white-pink flowers, forest understory — sarpagandha. Do not eat any part.
