How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Fish Owl, as the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Brown Fish Owl]] (Ketupa zeylonensis)

Bright yellow irises, rufous-brown plumage, and completely unfeathered yellow tarsi

Brown Fish Owl: Large brown-and-white owl with staring yellow eyes, ragged horizontal ear tufts, and bare yellow legs perching on branches overhanging water is a Brown Fish Owl.

Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl: The immense size, dark brown eyes, long slanting ear-tufts, and fulvous underparts barred on the breast, the bars becoming broad spots on the abdomen (Baker 1927, p. 418), distinguish the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl.
