How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Snakehead, as the Squarehead Catfish profile puts it

Spotted Snakehead]])

Cylindrical body covered with scales; mouth moderate; pelvic fins present

Spotted Snakehead: A snakehead with white or pale spots on a dark body, medium size (20–35 cm), and a flattened snakehead form is a Spotted Snakehead.

Squarehead Catfish: An unmistakable, grotesque, toad-like catfish with a massive square head, minute eyes, and an immense mouth gaping from cheek to cheek, buried in mud, is Chaca chaca.
