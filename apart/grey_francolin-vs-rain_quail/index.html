How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey Francolin, as the Rain Quail profile puts it

Grey Francolin]] (Francolinus pondicerianus)

Much larger gamebird, finely vermiculated grey-brown plumage with distinct black-bordered throat ring

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.

Rain Quail: The prominent solid black breast patch and unbarred brown outer primaries distinguish the male Rain Quail from all other quails in India (Baker 1928).
