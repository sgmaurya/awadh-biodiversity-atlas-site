Brilliant scarlet-red body throughout; wings completely clear except for tiny amber basal streak

Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly: A small rusty-yellow or pale reddish dragonfly fluttering weakly through rice paddies and perching on leaves with its wings drooped downward like a parasol, displaying pale golden-amber wing bases, is the Paddyfield Parasol Dragonfly.

Scarlet Skimmer: Medium-large size, uniform scarlet-red body with a flat, broad abdomen, thin black dorsal line, and lack of dark wing patches identify the Scarlet Skimmer.
