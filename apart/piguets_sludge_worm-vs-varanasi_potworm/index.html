How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Piguet's Sludge Worm, as the Varanasi Potworm profile puts it

Aquatic tubificid with dorsal hair setae; lives in aquatic mud, not terrestrial soil

जलीय कीड़ा, लंबे बालों वाले कांटे

Piguet's Sludge Worm: A tiny aquatic worm building small mud chimneys on pond sediment, featuring an unsegmented tail zone and oar-shaped dorsal paddle setae, identifies Aulodrilus pigueti.

Varanasi Potworm: A minute, transparent, thread-like terrestrial white worm with two straight setae per bundle living in agricultural rhizosphere soil identifies Hemifridericia varanensis.
