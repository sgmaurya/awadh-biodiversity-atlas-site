Commander or Common Sergeant? कमांडर तितली या कॉमन सार्जेंट तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Commander, as the Common Sergeant profile puts it

Upperside rich ferruginous red with a broad white macular slightly-curved common discal band set in a dusky-black background (Bingham 1905, pp. 291–292, entry 310)

ऊपरी सतह गहरी लाल-भूरी जिस पर चौड़ी सफेद पट्टी (कमांडर तितली)

Commander: A large, powerful nymphalid butterfly with rich reddish-chestnut wings crossed by a brilliant, broad white central band, flying boldly around kadam and kaim trees, is the Commander.

Common Sergeant: A black-and-white nymphalid with a divided white streak in the forewing cell and a row of round black dots along the inner margin of the hindwing postdiscal band (highlighted in pale blue on a golden-ochraceous underside) is Parathyma perius (Bingham 1905, pp. 315–316; Evans 1932).
