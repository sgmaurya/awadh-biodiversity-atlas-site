Green Shield Bug or Saw-shouldered Stink Bug? हरा शील्ड बग या आरी-कंधा गंधी कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Saw-shouldered Stink Bug, as the Green Shield Bug profile puts it

Pronotal margins saw-toothed; body dull grey-brown; head elongate

कंधे आरी जैसे दांतेदार, मटमैला भूरा रंग

Green Shield Bug, as the Saw-shouldered Stink Bug profile puts it

Bright grass-green; much smaller; smooth rounded pronotal margins

चमकीला हरा शरीर, बहुत छोटा, चिकने कंधे

Green Shield Bug: Compact green shield bug with rounded unspined shoulders, contrasting brownish-purplish corium, and a distinct pale yellow tip to the green scutellum identifies Plautia crossota.

Saw-shouldered Stink Bug: Large grey-brown bark-dwelling shield bug with distinctly saw-toothed, serrate lateral margins on the pronotum and an elongate rostrum identifies Neohalys serricollis.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Green Shield BugSaw-shouldered Stink Bug
HabitModerately small, ovate, compact shield bug 10–11 mm long (Distant 1902)Large, robust, elongate-ovate bark-dwelling shield bug 17–23 mm long (as Halys dentatus, Distant 1902)
ColorationBright grass-green above, shiny, with brownish or purplish corium (Dallas 1851)Mottled grey-brown, ochraceous, coarsely and densely punctate with black (Distant 1902)
ScutellumLarge, triangular, green with a distinct creamy-white or pale yellow apex (Distant 1902)—
PronotumBroad, sloping anteriorly, lateral angles rounded and completely unspined (Distant 1902)Lateral margins conspicuously denticulate or saw-toothed with distinct teeth (Westwood 1837)
ConnexivumBroadly exposed, green, marked with small dark black spots at the incisures—
MembraneTransparent, pale brownish-bronze with subparallel longitudinal venation—
Head—Broad, elongate, lateral lobes (juga) rounded anteriorly and enclosing tylus (Distant 1902)
Rostrum—Very long, slender, four-segmented, extending backwards onto mid-abdomen
Legs—Stout, mottled brownish-ochraceous with dark punctation; tibiae sulcate
