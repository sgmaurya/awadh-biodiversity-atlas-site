Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Painted Stork, as the Eurasian Spoonbill profile puts it

Colourful plumage (pink wings); painted head

रंगीन बगुली

Eurasian Spoonbill: The Eurasian Spoonbill is unmistakable — the only winter wading bird in the region with a pure white body and an enormously enlarged, flattened, spoon-shaped bill. No other bird has this bill shape.

Painted Stork: A large, distinctly colourful stork with pink body, black wings, white head, and yellow bill, often in flocks at water bodies — is a Painted Stork. No other stork in eastern UP has this combination of colours.
