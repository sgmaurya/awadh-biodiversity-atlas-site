Asian Grass Frog or Indian Burrowing Frog? फुदकी मेंढक या बिलकारी मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Grass Frog, as the Indian Burrowing Frog profile puts it

Slender body; pointed head; lacks digging spades; makes long jumps; lives on wet grass

फुदकी मेंढक — छोटा और फुर्तीला, पिछले पैर पर कुदाल नहीं होती

Asian Grass Frog: Medium size (3–5 cm), rough warty skin with long wrinkles, pointed snout, and a light line running down the spine in many individuals.

Indian Burrowing Frog: Globose body shape, short head, short limbs, and a large black, spade-shaped metatarsal tubercle on the inner hind foot identify the Indian Burrowing Frog.
