Paddyfield Pipit, as the Tawny Pipit profile puts it

Paddyfield Pipit]] (Anthus rufulus)

Densely streaked breast and mantle; shorter tail; less upright posture; resident breeder of grassy meadows

Paddyfield Pipit: Small, slender pipit + rufous-brown plumage + ground-walking behaviour + frequent tail flicks + thin "pip" call + rice paddies and grasslands = Paddyfield Pipit. The long hind claw and habitat preference are key identification features.

Tawny Pipit: The unstreaked or virtually unstreaked breast, pale sandy unstreaked upperparts, very erect stance, and preference for dry sandy wastes separate the Tawny Pipit from the heavily streaked Paddyfield and Rosy Pipits.
