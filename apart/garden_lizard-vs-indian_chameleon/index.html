Oriental Garden Lizard or Indian Chameleon? गिरगिट या असली गिरगिट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गिरगिटOriental Garden LizardCalotes versicolor
असली गिरगिटIndian ChameleonChamaeleo zeylanicus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Chameleon, as the Oriental Garden Lizard profile puts it

True colour-change across whole body; zygodactyl feet; extremely slow

Oriental Garden Lizard, as the Indian Chameleon profile puts it

Flat body, normal eyes, pointed tail, normal feet — also called "गिरगिट" locally

Side by side आमने-सामने

Size30–40 cm total length (half of which is the long tail)20–30 cm including prehensile tail

EyesCan move independently — each eye can look in a different directionEach eye moves independently — turret-like; can look two directions simultaneously
BehaviourPerches upright on vertical surfaces; head-bobs repeatedly; does press-up displays—
Feet—Zygodactyl — toes fused into opposing groups (3+2 and 2+3) for gripping
Tail—Prehensile — used as a fifth limb; tightly coiled when resting
Movement—Slow, deliberate, swaying gait; clings to branches
