Indian Eagle-Owl or Steppe Eagle? बड़ा उल्लू या स्टेपी ईगल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

स्टेपी ईगलSteppe EagleAquila nipalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Eagle-Owl, as the Steppe Eagle profile puts it

Not an eagle; owl with feathered ear tufts; nocturnal; different size and shape

उल्लू, कान की कलगी

Indian Eagle-Owl: Very large owl with bright orange eyes, prominent vertical ear tufts, and heavily streaked brown plumage nesting on clay cliffs or ruins is an Indian Eagle-Owl.

Steppe Eagle: A very large brown eagle with golden or buff crown and nape, soaring slowly over open grasslands with slow wing beats and broad wings — is a Steppe Eagle. Golden Eagle is larger and more uniformly brown.

SoundsDeep, resonant double hoot: bu-bo or tu-whoo, repeated at intervals—
Plumage—Dark brown to golden-brown overall; paler nape and crown (variable)
Bill—Large, hooked, dark grey or blackish
Cere and eye—Yellow or orange cere; dark brown iris
Wings—Long, broad; fingers prominent in flight
Tail—Medium length; dark brown, square or slightly rounded
Legs and feet—Yellow or orange; large, powerful claws
Behaviour—Hunts by soaring or from perches; takes medium-sized prey on ground
