How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Paradise Flycatcher, as the Black-naped Monarch profile puts it

Terpsiphone paradisi

Larger bird with glossy black crested head and blue eye-ring; males possess extraordinarily long tail streamers

Black-naped Monarch: Vibrant cobalt-blue flycatcher of shaded understorey; male has velvety black nape patch, black throat crescent, and white belly; female grey-brown with grey-blue head.

Indian Paradise Flycatcher: Indian Paradise Flycatcher male is unmistakable with long white tail streamers and glossy black-white plumage. Female and non-breeding male are chestnut-buff and occur in tall forest canopy in summer (April–September). No other bird matches this combination.

Male plumage—Glossy black head, neck, chest; white underparts; black back; long white streamers from tail
Female plumage—Chestnut crown and back; buff underparts; shorter tail without streamers
Bill—Small, black, slightly hooked
Legs—Bluish-grey
Behaviour—Solitary or in pairs; stays in canopy; active flycatcher; acrobatic
