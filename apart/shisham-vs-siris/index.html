Shisham or Siris? शीशम या शिरीष?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

शीशमShishamDalbergia sissoo
शिरीषSirisAlbizia lebbeck

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Siris, as the Shisham profile puts it

Bipinnate compound leaves with many small leaflets; fruit is a large, thick straw-colored pod

सिरिस — चौड़ी फलियाँ

Shisham: Compound leaves with 3 to 5 alternating, roundish leaflets ending in a sharp point, zig-zag leaf stalks, and thin papery pods identify Dalbergia sissoo.

Side by side आमने-सामने

ShishamSiris
LeavesAlternate, pinnate compound (10–15 cm long); with 3–5 alternating (not opposite) roundish leaves ending in a sharp, sudden pointAlternately arranged, bipinnately compound (15–40 cm long); 2–4 pairs of pinnae, each carrying 6–10 pairs of large, oblong, asymmetrical leaflets
BarkThick, dark grey to brown, rough, peeling off in long vertical strips on older treesGreyish-brown, rough, cracking into irregular rectangular flakes on older trunks
PodsLight green when young, ripening to pale straw-brown; thin, flat, and papery (4–8 cm long) containing 1–4 flat seeds—
FlowersSmall, pea-like, creamy-white to pale yellow (5–8 mm long), arranged in short branched clustersGlobose, brush-like, fragrant clusters (5–8 cm diameter) of greenish-yellow to cream flowers with numerous long stamens
TrunkOften crooked or irregular in older wild trees; wood contains dark-brown durable heartwood—
Form—Large deciduous tree (15–25 m) with a broad, spreading canopy; older branches are grey-brown and smooth
Fruit—Large, flat, papery straw-yellow to brown pods (15–30 cm long, 3–5 cm wide), containing 6–12 flat rounded seeds; bulges over seeds are visible
Acoustics—Dry pods rattle continuously in the wind, a key auditory identification feature during the dry season

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
