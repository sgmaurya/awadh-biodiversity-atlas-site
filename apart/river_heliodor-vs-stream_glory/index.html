Much smaller; stout black-and-yellow body; male has white expanded legs and dark-tipped forewings

River Heliodor: A small, stoutly built damselfly of shaded running streams whose male has a bright golden-yellow-and-black abdomen, black-tipped forewings, and broad chalky-white legs used in courtship displays is the River Heliodor.

Stream Glory: A large, slender damselfly dancing butterfly-like over clear flowing streams whose glittering metallic emerald-green body is paired with brilliant, flashing metallic green hindwings is the Stream Glory.
