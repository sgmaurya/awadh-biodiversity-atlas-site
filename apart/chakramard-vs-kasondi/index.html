Sickle Senna, as the Coffee Senna profile puts it

Leaflets 2–4 pairs (fewer than kasondi); pods sickle-shaped; seeds less toxic

पत्तियाँ कम, फली हँसिया जैसी

Sickle Senna: Erect annual roadside herb + 3 pairs of obovate leaflets + gland between lowest leaflet pair + pungent smell when bruised + bright yellow flowers + very long curved sickle-shaped pods = Senna tora. The pod length and shape are unique in the eastern UP weed flora.

Coffee Senna: An erect herb with pinnate leaves (6–10 leaflet pairs), bright yellow flowers, and flat linear pods containing reddish-brown seeds is coffee senna. Seeds and unripe pods are toxic to cattle — do not allow livestock to graze on mature plants.
