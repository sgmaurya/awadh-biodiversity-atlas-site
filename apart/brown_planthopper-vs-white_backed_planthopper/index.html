Brown Planthopper or White-backed Planthopper? भूरा फुदका या व्हाइट-बैक्ड प्लांटहॉपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-backed Planthopper, as the Brown Planthopper profile puts it

Same place and size; a white stripe down the back; arrives earlier in the season

पीठ पर सफ़ेद धारी

Brown Planthopper, as the White-backed Planthopper profile puts it

Nilaparvata lugens

Brown delphacid planthopper lacking the bright white thoracic stripe; infests rice stem bases in late tillering

Brown Planthopper: Brown wedge-shaped hoppers in clusters at the base of rice just above the water, jumping when tapped, with round scorched patches in the field: brown planthopper.

White-backed Planthopper: Minute dark planthopper with a prominent creamy-white stripe down the middle of the back, congregating in dense colonies at the base of rice stems.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Brown PlanthopperWhite-backed Planthopper
Size3–4.5 mm; adults long-winged (4.5 mm) or short-winged (3 mm)—
ColourYellowish-brown to dark brown, with a pale line down the middle of the back—
ShapeWedge-shaped, wings held roof-like; jumps when disturbed—
Where on the plantAt the base of the tillers, just above the water, in clusters; not on the leaves—
NymphsPale cream to brown, wingless, in the same place—
Damage"Hopperburn": circular patches of yellowed, then dried, then collapsed plants spreading outward from a centre—
HoneydewSticky, with sooty mould on the lower stems—
White dorsal stripe—Conspicuous, bright creamy-white to yellowish-white longitudinal band along the mesonotum
Body coloration—Dark brown to blackish body contrasting sharply with the bright white dorsal stripe
Forewings—Subhyaline, pale brownish-grey forewings with a distinct dark spot at the apex of the clavus
Hind legs—Possesses a prominent, movable, serrated spur (calcar) at the apex of each hind tibia
Feeding location—Congregated in dense colonies at the lower stem bases and tillers of rice hills just above water

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
