Common Kingfisher or White-throated Kingfisher? राम चिरैया या किलकिला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

राम चिरैयाCommon KingfisherAlcedo atthis
किलकिलाWhite-throated KingfisherHalcyon smyrnensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-throated Kingfisher, as the Common Kingfisher profile puts it

Much larger; white breast; brown head and wings; blue only on back/wings

Common Kingfisher, as the White-throated Kingfisher profile puts it

Much smaller (16 cm); blue-green upperparts; orange belly; black bill; strictly hunts fish near water

राम चिरैया — बहुत छोटा आकार और पानी के पास रहना

White-throated Kingfisher: Chestnut-brown head and body, large pure white throat patch, brilliant blue back, and a large coral-red bill identify this species.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common KingfisherWhite-throated Kingfisher
SizeSmall, compact bird (16–17 cm total length); short-tailed, large-headedMedium-sized kingfisher (27–29 cm total length); stocky build
UpperpartsIridescent electric cobalt-blue to turquoise; bright blue back stripe—
UnderpartsRich warm orange-chestnut (rufous) underparts—
BillLong, straight, black, dagger-like billLarge, heavy, straight, and deep coral-red bill
FlightVery fast, direct, low over water; appears as a flashing blue streak—
VoiceSharp, high-pitched chee-chee whistle, usually given in flight—
Colouration—Chestnut-brown head, flanks, and lower belly; large, pure white throat and breast patch
Wings—Electric blue back and wings, with a bright white wing patch visible in flight
Legs—Short, weak legs that are coral-red
Behaviour—Perches on electric wires, fences, and high branches; makes a loud, laughing scream

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
