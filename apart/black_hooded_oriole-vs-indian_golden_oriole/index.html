Black-hooded Oriole or Indian Golden Oriole? ब्लैक-हुडेड ओरिओल या पीलक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पीलकIndian Golden OrioleOriolus kundoo

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-hooded Oriole, as the Indian Golden Oriole profile puts it

Black head; yellow underparts; different range and habitat (southern)

काली सिर वाली पीलक — दक्षिणी

Black-hooded Oriole: A bright yellow bird with a black head and upper body is a male Black-hooded Oriole. Greenish-yellow birds with longer bills are females. Entirely black or different pattern males are other species.

Indian Golden Oriole: Golden-yellow plumage (male) + black and yellow wings and tail + loud "hoe-ee" call + canopy feeding + spring arrival = Indian Golden Oriole. Females are duller but share the olive-yellow coloration and distinctive call.
