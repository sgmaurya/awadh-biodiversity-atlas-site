How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian River Shad, as the Ganges River Gizzard Shad profile puts it

Gudusia chapra

Body more elongated, mouth terminal without an inferior projection, lacks dark shoulder spot

Indian River Shad: A small, silvery, laterally compressed shad with a deep belly and schooling behaviour in rivers and large ponds is a chapra.

Ganges River Gizzard Shad: Small deep-bodied silvery river fish with a downward-curved mouth, dark shoulder spot, and razor-sharp serrated abdominal keel.

Shoulder spot—Dark diffuse bluish-black blotch situated behind the upper corner of the operculum
Snout structure—Prominent blunt snout with upper jaw slender at the tip and distinctly curved downwards
Dorsal fin—Single dorsal fin placed approximately midway along the back, lacking elongated filamentous rays
Abdominal scutes—Series of sharp, keeled abdominal scutes running along the ventral edge from throat to anal fin
