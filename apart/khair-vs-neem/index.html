Khair or Neem? खैर या नीम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

खैरKhairSenegalia catechu
नीमNeemAzadirachta indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Khair profile puts it

Pinnate leaves with toothed leaflets; flowers white, not yellow; no spines

नीम — दांतेदार पत्तियाँ

Khair: A tree with very fine, feathery, doubly pinnate leaves, fragrant yellow flower spikes, and very hard dark wood, is khair.

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.
