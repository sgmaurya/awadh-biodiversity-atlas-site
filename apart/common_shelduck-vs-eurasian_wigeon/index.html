How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Eurasian Wigeon, as the Common Shelduck profile puts it

Eurasian Wigeon]] (Anas penelope)

Much smaller (45–51 cm); different head and body pattern

Common Shelduck: A large (58–67 cm) duck with glossy green head, bright red bill, white underparts, and chestnut breast band is a male Common Shelduck. Pairs often rest on muddy banks or in shallow water. Much larger and more brightly patterned than other winter ducks.

Eurasian Wigeon: A dabbling duck with a chestnut-red head and buff crown (males) or rufous-brown head (females), with iridescent green wing patch in flight — is a Eurasian Wigeon. The head pattern of males is diagnostic and unmistakable.
