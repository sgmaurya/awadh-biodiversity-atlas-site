Maidenhair Creeper or Maidenhair Fern? मेडनहेयर क्रीपर या हंसराज?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Maidenhair Fern, as the Maidenhair Creeper profile puts it

Adiantum capillus-veneris]]`

Tufted terrestrial or rupestral fern with black shining stipes and fan-shaped leaflets; does not climb

Maidenhair Creeper: Look for the twining wiry climbing fronds scrambling over bushes with comb-like soral spikes along the fertile leaflet margins.

Maidenhair Fern: A fern with a jet-black wiry stalk and tiny fan-shaped leaflets that repel water is maidenhair fern (Adiantum capillus-veneris).

Texture—Paper-thin leaflets; silky appearance
Moisture—Leaflets are water-repellent; beaded water droplets do not wet the surface
