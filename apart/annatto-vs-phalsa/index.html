Annatto or Phalsa? अनाट्टो या फालसा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अनाट्टोAnnattoBixa orellana
फालसाPhalsaGrewia asiatica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Phalsa, as the Annatto profile puts it

Leaves cordate but serrate; flowers small yellow; fruit small edible berries

पत्तियाँ दांतेदार, फूल पीले, फल बेर जैसे

Annatto: Cordate-ovate leaves with long petioles, showy pinkish-white terminal flowers, and heart-shaped capsules covered with soft bristles containing bright orange-red dye-coated seeds identify Bixa orellana.

Phalsa: Low-branching shrub or small tree with coarse suborbicular leaves having 5–6 basal nerves, small yellow cymes, and dark red-purple edible drupes ripening in summer indicates Grewia asiatica.
