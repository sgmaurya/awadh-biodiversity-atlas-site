Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Sailer, as the Common Nawab profile puts it

Common Sailer]])

Distinctly smaller; black and white with sailing flight; lacks green bands or hindwing tails

Common Nawab: A large, powerfully built butterfly with a luminous apple-green central band across velvety black wings and two distinct pointed tails on each hindwing is the Common Nawab.

Common Sailer: A medium-sized black butterfly with white spots or bands on the wings and swift, low, erratic flight over grasslands is the Common Sailer.

Coloration—Deep black or dark brown wings with white striped bands
Forewing—Contains 2–3 irregular white spots or bands crossing the wing
Hindwing—Has a row of small white spots along the outer margin
Flight—Swift, erratic, zigzag flight; males patrol rapidly
Behaviour—Males are territorial; perch on low twigs and rapidly pursue other butterflies
