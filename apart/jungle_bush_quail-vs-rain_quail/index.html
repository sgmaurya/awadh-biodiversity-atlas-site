How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rain Quail, as the Jungle Bush Quail profile puts it

Rain Quail]] (Coturnix coromandelica)

Solid black breast patch in males, unbarred pale flanks with black streaks, and lacking rufous-chestnut throat

Jungle Bush Quail, as the Rain Quail profile puts it

Jungle Bush Quail]] (Perdicula asiatica)

Rufous-chestnut throat, close black-and-white zebra barring across lower plumage, lacking solid black breast patch

Jungle Bush Quail: The rufous-chestnut throat and face combined with close black-and-white zebra barring across the lower breast distinguish the male Jungle Bush Quail from all other Indian quails (Baker 1928).

Rain Quail: The prominent solid black breast patch and unbarred brown outer primaries distinguish the male Rain Quail from all other quails in India (Baker 1928).
