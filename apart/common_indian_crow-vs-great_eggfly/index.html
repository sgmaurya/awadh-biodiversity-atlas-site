Common Indian Crow or Great Eggfly? कौआ तितली या नील-चाँद तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कौआ तितलीCommon Indian CrowEuploea core
नील-चाँद तितलीGreat EggflyHypolimnas bolina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Eggfly, as the Common Indian Crow profile puts it

Blacker, with larger white patches in the forewing and a row of white spots; faster, jerkier flight; the mimic

ज़्यादा काली, बड़े सफ़ेद धब्बे, तेज़ उड़ान

Common Indian Crow, as the Great Eggfly profile puts it

Wings are uniform dark brown with double row of white margin spots; lacks the blue-halo spots; flight is slow and lazy

काली तितली — सुस्त उड़ान, बिना नीले घेरे के धब्बे

Common Indian Crow: A large, slow, dark brown butterfly with a margin of white dots and no tails is the common crow.

Great Eggfly: Large size, male has velvety black wings with four glowing blue-and-white egg-like spots, female is dark brown mimicking the Common Crow, and territorial perching behavior identify the Great Eggfly.

Side by side आमने-सामने

FlightSlow, gliding, unhurried, at head height; often along the same path back and forth—
CaterpillarBanded white, black and yellow-brown, with four pairs of long black fleshy filaments, on fig and milkweed—
Male Upperside—Velvety jet-black with a large oval white spot surrounded by a glowing blue halo on each wing
Female Upperside—Dark brown or blackish-brown; lacks the large blue-halo spots; mimics the toxic Common Crow butterfly
Underside—Both sexes are dull brown with a broad white band across the wings and small white spots
Behaviour—Territorially aggressive; perches on leaves and chases away other butterflies
