How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown-headed Barbet, as the Lineated Barbet profile puts it

Brown-headed Barbet]] (Psilopogon zeylanicus)

Head and breast brown, finely streaked, with an orange patch of bare skin round the eye; the Lineated has a paler, more boldly streaked head

Brown-headed Barbet: Large green barbet with brown head and extremely thick bill. The large size, brown head, and loud calls in early morning distinguish it from all other barbets.

Lineated Barbet: The chunky grass-green body, heavily white-streaked pale head and breast, stout yellowish bill, and bright yellow bare skin patch around the eye identify the Lineated Barbet.
