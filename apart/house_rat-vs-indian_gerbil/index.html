House Rat or Indian Gerbil? घर का चूहा या अंतरी चूहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अंतरी चूहाIndian GerbilTatera indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Rat, as the Indian Gerbil profile puts it

Much larger (20–25 cm), thicker tail, stouter build

बहुत बड़ा, भारी, मोटी पूँछ

House Rat: A dark-furred, climbing rat with a tail longer than its body, found in grain stores and climbing rafters, is a House Rat.

Indian Gerbil: A tawny-brown mouse (15–20 cm) with a long thin tail, large ears, large dark eyes, and a dark stripe along its back in arid fields or grain stores is an Indian gerbil. It runs and bounds rapidly; most visible at dusk and dawn.
