Sickle Senna or Rattleweed? चक्रमर्द या घुनघुनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sickle Senna, as the Rattleweed profile puts it

Pinnate leaves with 3 pairs of leaflets; flowers smaller; pods long and slender

चकवड़, संयुक्त पत्तियाँ, लंबी पतली फली

Sickle Senna: Erect annual roadside herb + 3 pairs of obovate leaflets + gland between lowest leaflet pair + pungent smell when bruised + bright yellow flowers + very long curved sickle-shaped pods = Senna tora. The pod length and shape are unique in the eastern UP weed flora.

Rattleweed: Erect subshrub with simple oblanceolate leaves notched at the apex, racemes of golden-yellow pea-flowers, and hairless inflated pods rattling when ripe identifies Crotalaria retusa.
