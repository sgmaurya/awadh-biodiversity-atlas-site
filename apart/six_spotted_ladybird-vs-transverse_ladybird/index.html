How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Transverse Ladybird, as the Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird profile puts it

Three pairs of wavy transverse bands (not six distinct spots); larger

ट्रांसवर्स लेडीबर्ड — पट्टियाँ, बड़ा

Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird: A small, dome-shaped orange-red beetle with six black spots arranged in distinctive zigzag lines (not round spots) and small white corner spots on the thorax identifies the Six-spotted Zigzag Ladybird.

Transverse Ladybird: Small size, dome-shaped orange-red elytra, a black thorax with two creamy corner spots, and three pairs of transverse, wavy black bands across the wings identify the Transverse Ladybird.
