How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow Monitor, as the Bengal Monitor profile puts it

Yellow coloration; strongly protected; rare in UP

Bengal Monitor, as the Yellow Monitor profile puts it

Bengal Monitor]] (Varanus bengalensis)

Much larger (80–120 cm); darker coloration; more robust; usually found in forests and wetlands

Yellow Monitor: A small, slender, golden-brown lizard with sharp claws and four legs that runs very fast is a Yellow Monitor. It is India's smallest monitor and is found in dry areas and on riverbanks.
