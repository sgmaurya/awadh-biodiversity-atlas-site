How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Handmaiden Moth, as the Cross-line Geometer Moth profile puts it

Day-flying black moth with transparent spots; totally different form

दिन में उड़ने वाला काला पतंगा, पारदर्शी धब्बे

Cross-line Geometer Moth: A delicate fawn-grey or greenish geometer moth with a single uninterrupted dark straight line running from the forewing apex across both wings identifies Traminda mundissima.

Handmaiden Moth: Small diurnal black moth with transparent spots on both wings and two bright orange-red rings on a dark body identifies Amata cyssea.
