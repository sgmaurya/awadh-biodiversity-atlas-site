Much smaller; bright marbled blue and black with trumpet-shaped abdomen; diurnal percher

Brown Dusk Hawk Dragonfly: A slender, chocolate-brown dragonfly with huge glowing emerald-green eyes and a paper-thin constricted waist, flying swiftly low over water only at dusk, is the Brown Dusk Hawk.

Trumpet Tail: The unique trumpet or bagpipe-shaped abdomen — narrow at base, expanded in middle, narrow again at tip — is instantly recognizable and diagnostic of this species.
