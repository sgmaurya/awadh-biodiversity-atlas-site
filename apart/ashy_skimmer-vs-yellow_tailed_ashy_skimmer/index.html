How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Larger; thorax ashy-grey, but terminal abdominal segments 8–10 are bright yellow-orange; perches on twigs

Ashy Skimmer: A small dragonfly perching flat on sun-baked stones or sand whose mature male is uniformly coated in pale powdery ashy-blue dust with bright yellow pterostigmata on clear wings is the Ashy Skimmer.

Yellow-tailed Ashy Skimmer: Small, ashy-grey dragonfly with distinctive yellow-orange abdomen tip distinguishes this species.
