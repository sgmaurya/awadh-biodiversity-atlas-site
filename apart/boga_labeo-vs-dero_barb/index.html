How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Boga Labeo, as the Dero Barb profile puts it

Lacks transverse groove on snout; 37–39 lateral line scales; distinct shoulder blotch present (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)

Boga Labeo: An elongate, streamlined silvery-orange minor carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of minute maxillary barbels, and 37–39 scales in the lateral line, is Labeo boga.

Dero Barb: An elongate, brownish-green carp with a distinct transverse groove across the prominent snout, inferior mouth with horny lower jaw covering, and 40–44 lateral line scales, is Bangana dero.
