Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddyfield Pipit, as the Long-billed Pipit profile puts it

Paddyfield Pipit]] (Anthus rufulus)

Smaller build, noticeably shorter bill, more upright stance, and heavily streaked breast

Long-billed Pipit: The large robust build, long heavy bill with pale lower base, and faintly streaked chest on dry stony terrain distinguish the Long-billed Pipit.

Paddyfield Pipit: Small, slender pipit + rufous-brown plumage + ground-walking behaviour + frequent tail flicks + thin "pip" call + rice paddies and grasslands = Paddyfield Pipit. The long hind claw and habitat preference are key identification features.
