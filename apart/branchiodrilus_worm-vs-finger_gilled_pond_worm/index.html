How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Finger-gilled Pond Worm, as the Branchiodrilus Worm profile puts it

Gills restricted to a specialized cup-like posterior branchial fossa; body flanks completely lack lateral gills

फिंगर-गिल्ड पॉन्ड वर्म

Branchiodrilus Worm, as the Finger-gilled Pond Worm profile puts it

Gills arranged along lateral body margins of anterior and mid-body segments; lacks a terminal posterior gill cup

ब्रैंकियोड्रिलस वर्म

Branchiodrilus Worm: A slender aquatic worm bearing numerous paired, finger-like lateral respiratory gills extending along its body flanks is Branchiodrilus hortensis.

Finger-gilled Pond Worm: A slender, transparent aquatic worm with a cup-shaped ciliated gill fossa bearing finger-like lobes at the tail tip is Dero digitata.
