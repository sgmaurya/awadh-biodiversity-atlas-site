बड़ा पनकौवा

Great Cormorant: Great Cormorant is the largest cormorant in the region, nearly twice the size of Little Cormorant. Its large size, long neck, and large hooked bill are diagnostic. Note its yellow throat pouch (if visible) and bright green eyes in breeding birds.

Indian Cormorant: The Indian Cormorant is a medium-sized black water bird with a distinctive brown patch on the upper neck. The hooked bill and fully webbed feet are diagnostic for cormorants. Size places it between Little (smaller) and Great (larger).
