How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Grebe, as the Great Crested Grebe profile puts it

Little Grebe]] (Tachybaptus ruficollis)

Much smaller (25–29 cm); compact; short-necked; completely different appearance

Great Crested Grebe, as the Little Grebe profile puts it

Much larger (48–56 cm); crested head; black-and-white neck

बहुत बड़ा; सिर पर कलगी

Great Crested Grebe: A large, elegant white-and-black grebe with long slender neck and dark cap on large open water bodies is Great Crested Grebe. The size, slender neck, and striking pattern are diagnostic compared to other grebes.

Little Grebe: The smallest grebe; a tiny compact brown-and-white diving bird with a loud wailing trill and backward leg placement — is a Little Grebe. The constant diving behaviour and inability to walk on land are distinctive.
