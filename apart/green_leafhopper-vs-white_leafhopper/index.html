How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Leafhopper, as the White Leafhopper profile puts it

Green colour; larger; overlapping hosts; expert examination needed

हरा, बड़ा

Green Leafhopper: A small bright green leafhopper 4–5 mm, jumping sideways on the undersides of paddy leaves with visible stippling damage, is the green leafhopper.

White Leafhopper: Tiny pale white leafhopper on rice or wheat leaf undersurface, jumping rapidly when disturbed; causing leaf yellowing or chlorosis: White Leafhopper (Cofana spectra).
