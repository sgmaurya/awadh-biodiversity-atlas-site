How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Coucal, as the Oriental Pied Hornbill profile puts it

Centropus sinensis

All black body with chestnut-bay wings; terrestrial skulker lacking heavy bill and casque

Greater Coucal: Pheasant-like shape, glossy black body contrasting with bright chestnut wings, bright red eyes, and a slow walking habit on the ground identify the Greater Coucal.

Oriental Pied Hornbill: Massive black-and-white canopy bird with immense yellow bill and casque, white belly, and white-tipped black tail.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Greater CoucalOriental Pied Hornbill
SizeLarge, heavy bird (47–52 cm total length); resembling a pheasant or crow with a long tail—
ColourationGlossy black body, head, and tail; contrasting bright chestnut-copper wings—
EyesDiagnostic, prominent ruby-red or crimson-red eyes in adults—
BillStrong, black, down-curved bill; similar to a crow but more hooked—
FlightClumsy and weak; alternate flapping and gliding, landing heavily in low bushes—
ForagingWalks on the ground or hops through low branches, cocking its long tail—
Plumage pattern—Glossy greenish-black body contrasting with pure white belly, thighs, and tail tips
Casque morphology—Large, sharp-fronted yellowish-white casque marked with variable black patches
Facial skin—Bare circumorbital skin and throat patch; white with black anterior mark in males
Tail pattern—Black tail with all outer rectrices broadly tipped with pure white
Flight pattern—Characteristic alternating heavy flapping and long glides on broad rounded wings
