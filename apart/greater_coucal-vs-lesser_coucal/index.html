Greater Coucal or Lesser Coucal? महोख या लेसर कौकल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Coucal, as the Greater Coucal profile puts it

Much smaller (38 cm); paler plumage; wings have darker bars; prefers wet grasslands, rare in Basti

छोटा महोख — आकार में काफी छोटा, दुर्लभ

Greater Coucal, as the Lesser Coucal profile puts it

Centropus sinensis

Noticeably larger and heavier bird; deep purplish-blue body; unstreaked non-breeding plumage

Greater Coucal: Pheasant-like shape, glossy black body contrasting with bright chestnut wings, bright red eyes, and a slow walking habit on the ground identify the Greater Coucal.

Lesser Coucal: Small, slender coucal with glossy blue-black body and chestnut wings in summer, transforming to cryptic streaked brown plumage in winter.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Greater CoucalLesser Coucal
SizeLarge, heavy bird (47–52 cm total length); resembling a pheasant or crow with a long tail—
ColourationGlossy black body, head, and tail; contrasting bright chestnut-copper wings—
EyesDiagnostic, prominent ruby-red or crimson-red eyes in adults—
BillStrong, black, down-curved bill; similar to a crow but more hooked—
FlightClumsy and weak; alternate flapping and gliding, landing heavily in low bushes—
ForagingWalks on the ground or hops through low branches, cocking its long tail—
Size & build—Substantially smaller and more slender than the familiar greater coucal
Breeding plumage—Glossy black head, body, and tail with deep blue gloss contrasting with chestnut wings
Non-breeding dress—Dull brown upperparts boldly streaked with pale fulvous; barred wings and underparts
Eye colour—Deep crimson-red iris in adults; dull greyish-brown in juveniles
Locomotion—Clambers deftly through tangled reed stalks and grass stems like a rail

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
