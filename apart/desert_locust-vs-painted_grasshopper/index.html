Desert Locust or Painted Grasshopper? रेगिस्तानी टिड्डी या रंगबिरंगा टिड्डा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रेगिस्तानी टिड्डीDesert LocustSchistocerca gregaria
रंगबिरंगा टिड्डाPainted GrasshopperPoekilocerus pictus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Desert Locust, as the Painted Grasshopper profile puts it

Dull yellow-brown body; lacks blue-and-yellow bands; feeds on crops, flies in massive swarms

रेगिस्तानी टिड्डी — पीला-भूरा शरीर, झुंड में उड़ान

Desert Locust: Huge, strong-flying grasshopper forming massive cohesive swarms, with pinkish or bright yellow coloration and long tegmina covered in dark spots.

Painted Grasshopper: Very large grasshopper (45–65 mm) with bright yellow and turquoise-blue bands on head, orange-and-black rings on the abdomen, feeding only on Aak leaves, is the Painted Grasshopper.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Desert LocustPainted Grasshopper
Gregarious adultLarge, robust grasshopper; pinkish when immature and turning bright yellow upon sexual maturity—
Solitary adultCryptic greyish-brown to green body blending seamlessly with sparse desert vegetation—
Wing structureLong, powerful, subhyaline tegmina dusted with irregular dark brown spots, extending beyond abdomen—
PronotumSmooth, saddles-shaped pronotum lacking a prominent crest or lateral carinae—
Swarming behaviorFlying in dense, cohesive, daylight swarms darkening the sky and settling en masse on crops—
Size—Very large, heavy-bodied grasshopper; female is up to 55–65 mm long, male slightly smaller
Colouration—Bright yellow and turquoise-blue longitudinal bands on head and thorax; abdomen has orange and black rings
Wings—Forewings (tegmina) are green-yellow with bold yellow spots; hindwings are bright brick-red, visible in flight
Antennae—Long, ringed with yellow and black bands
Defense secretion—Exudes a foul-tasting, stinging milky froth from thoracic glands when touched
Host Association—Found almost exclusively sitting and feeding on the leaves of Calotropis procera (Aak)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
