How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pointed Stink Bug, as the Dussumier's Seed Bug profile puts it

Grey-brown shield bug; much larger; head sharply pointed

मटमैला भूरा ढाल जैसा कीड़ा, सिर आगे से नुकीला

Dussumier's Seed Bug: Sanguineous red seed bug with two broad black stripes on the red pronotum, a black scutellum with a red tip, and black wing covers with a central transverse black bar identifies Graptostethus servus.

Pointed Stink Bug: Large greyish-brown shield bug with a strongly elongated, acutely triangular pointed head and a pale median line down the scutellum identifies Erthesina acuminata.
