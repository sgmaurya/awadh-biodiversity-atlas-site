Gangetic Hairfin Anchovy or Gangetic Mystus? फाँसा या कबासी टेंगरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कबासी टेंगराGangetic MystusMystus cavasius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gangetic Mystus, as the Gangetic Hairfin Anchovy profile puts it

Much larger; catfish with barbels

बहुत बड़ी कैटफिश

Gangetic Hairfin Anchovy: The smallest fish of the region, barely larger than a pinhead, with an extremely long anal fin and translucent body, is a Gangetic Hairfin Anchovy.

Gangetic Mystus: A small catfish with three pairs of barbels and serrated pectoral spines is a Gangetic Mystus. The spine causes pain if touched.
