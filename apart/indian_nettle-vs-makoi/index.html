Indian Nettle or Black Nightshade? कुप्पी या मकोय?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कुप्पीIndian NettleAcalypha indica
मकोयBlack NightshadeSolanum nigrum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Nightshade, as the Indian Nettle profile puts it

Usually smaller leaves, black berries not capsules, less hairy; from the nightshade family

छोटी पत्तियाँ, काली गोल फलियाँ

Indian Nettle: A bushy herb with broadly toothed, densely hairy leaves that may cause mild itching, and very small flowers in spikes is Acalypha indica.

Black Nightshade: Soft hairless plant, white star flowers with a yellow anther cone, and drooping bunches of pea-sized berries turning black: makoi. Green berries are not to be eaten.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Stem—Green or purplish, angled, sometimes with faint ridges
