Common Snipe or Jack Snipe? चाहा या छोटा चाहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

चाहाCommon SnipeGallinago gallinago
छोटा चाहाJack SnipeLymnocryptes minimus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jack Snipe, as the Common Snipe profile puts it

Much smaller (19 cm); shorter bill

छोटा चाहा

Common Snipe, as the Jack Snipe profile puts it

Distinctly larger; prominent pale median crown stripe; flushes at distance with harsh rasping call and rapid zig-zag flight

सामान्य चाहा

Common Snipe: Common Snipe is small with a very long straight bill. When flushed, it explodes into erratic zig-zag flight with a harsh "scaip" call. This combination is diagnostic.

Jack Snipe: A very small snipe that crouches until nearly trodden on, lacks a pale central crown stripe, and flushes silently with a low flutter is a Jack Snipe.
