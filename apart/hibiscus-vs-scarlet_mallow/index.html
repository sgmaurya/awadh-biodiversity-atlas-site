How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hibiscus, as the Scarlet Mallow profile puts it

Hibiscus]] sabdariffa* (Roselle)

Larger, three-parted calyx; fruit different; cultivated crop, not wild

Hibiscus: A cultivated shrub with large (6–10 cm), showy, five-petalled flowers in many colours, with a very long, prominent central column of stamens, and ovate, serrated leaves, is Hibiscus.

Scarlet Mallow: Scarlet Mallow is unmistakable: large soft herb with scarlet five-petaled flowers, found only in wet ground. No other Malvaceae in eastern UP has such brilliant red flowers.
