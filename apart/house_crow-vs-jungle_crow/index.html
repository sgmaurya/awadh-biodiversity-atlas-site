House Crow or Jungle Crow? कौआ या जंगली कौआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पक्षी कौआHouse CrowCorvus splendens
जंगली कौआJungle CrowCorvus macrorhynchos

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Crow, as the House Crow profile puts it

Larger; entirely glossy black (lacks grey collar); thicker, more arched bill; steeper forehead

जंगली कौआ — पूरी तरह से काला और भारी चोंच

House Crow, as the Jungle Crow profile puts it

Smaller; distinct grey collar around neck; flatter forehead; thinner, straight bill; bold behavior

कौआ — छोटा, गर्दन स्लेटी और चोंच पतली होती है

House Crow: Medium size, grey collar around the neck contrasting with glossy black plumage, flat forehead, and flat kaaa calls identify the House Crow.

Jungle Crow: Large size, entirely glossy black plumage (no grey collar), steep forehead forming an angle with a massive arched black bill, and deep resonant calls.
