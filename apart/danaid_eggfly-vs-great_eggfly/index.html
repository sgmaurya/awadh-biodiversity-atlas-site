How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Danaid Eggfly, as the Great Eggfly profile puts it

Male is similar but smaller; female mimics the toxic [[plain_tiger\

Plain Tiger]] (Danaus chrysippus) with orange wings and black margins

Danaid Eggfly: Slow-flying butterfly with iridescent blue-black wings (males) or highly variable female forms; white spots visible; on flowers in grasslands.

Great Eggfly: Large size, male has velvety black wings with four glowing blue-and-white egg-like spots, female is dark brown mimicking the Common Crow, and territorial perching behavior identify the Great Eggfly.
