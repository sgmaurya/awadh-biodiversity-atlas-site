Green Shield Bug or Southern Green Stink Bug? हरा शील्ड बग या हरा खटमल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Southern Green Stink Bug, as the Green Shield Bug profile puts it

Southern Green Stink Bug]] (Nezara viridula)

Entirely green, including corium; larger; humeral margin with 3 pale dots

Green Shield Bug: Compact green shield bug with rounded unspined shoulders, contrasting brownish-purplish corium, and a distinct pale yellow tip to the green scutellum identifies Plautia crossota.

Southern Green Stink Bug: Bright grass-green, shield-shaped flat bug about 12–15 mm long that releases a strong foul-smelling liquid when touched is the Southern Green Stink Bug.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Green Shield BugSouthern Green Stink Bug
HabitModerately small, ovate, compact shield bug 10–11 mm long (Distant 1902)—
ColorationBright grass-green above, shiny, with brownish or purplish corium (Dallas 1851)—
ScutellumLarge, triangular, green with a distinct creamy-white or pale yellow apex (Distant 1902)—
PronotumBroad, sloping anteriorly, lateral angles rounded and completely unspined (Distant 1902)—
ConnexivumBroadly exposed, green, marked with small dark black spots at the incisures—
MembraneTransparent, pale brownish-bronze with subparallel longitudinal venation—
Adult shape—Distinctive shield-shaped, flat-bodied bug; body length around 12–15 mm
Colouration—Entirely bright grass-green; has a row of three tiny white or yellow spots on the front edge of the scutellum
Nymphs—Highly variable; early stages are black with red and white spots, changing to green-and-yellow before adult phase
Defensive scent—Emits a strong, sharp, unpleasant-smelling oily fluid from glands on the sides of the thorax when touched
Sucking organ—A long, needle-like beak (rostrum) tucked under the head and body when not feeding

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
