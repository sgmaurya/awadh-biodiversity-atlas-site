Sambar or Swamp Deer? सांभर या बारहसिंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Swamp Deer, as the Sambar profile puts it

Swamp Deer]] (Rucervus duvaucelii)

Bright yellowish-brown or rufous coat; antlers have ten to twelve or more tines forming a crown; obligate swamp dweller

Sambar, as the Swamp Deer profile puts it

Sambar]] (Rusa unicolor)

Darker, uniform umber-brown to blackish-brown coat without spots; larger ears; antlers have three heavy tines; woodland dweller

Sambar: The massive horse-like size, uniform shaggy dark umber-brown coat devoid of white spots, large bat-like ears, and heavy three-tined antlers distinguish the Sambar from all other deer.

Swamp Deer: The large size, bright golden-rufous summer coat, splayed wetland hooves, and particularly the adult stag's intricate crown of ten to twelve or more antler tines distinguish the Swamp Deer from all others.
