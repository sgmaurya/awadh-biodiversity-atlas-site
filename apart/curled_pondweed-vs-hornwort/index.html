How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hornwort, as the Curled Pondweed profile puts it

Ceratophyllum demersum

Rootless submerged plant with stiff, repeatedly forked leaves; lacks flat ribbon-like ruffled blades

Curled Pondweed: Submerged freshwater plant with translucent, sessile, linear-oblong leaves featuring strikingly crinkled, wavy, serrulated margins.

Hornwort: A fully submerged, free-floating plant with needle-like, finely divided leaves in stiff whorls and no roots is hornwort.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Curled PondweedHornwort
Growth habitEntirely submerged perennial aquatic herb rooted in benthic mud by creeping rhizomes—
Leaf structureTranslucent, sessile, linear-oblong submerged leaves with strongly undulate, crinkled margins—
Leaf serrationDistinctly and finely serrulate margins with microscopic sharp teeth along the edges—
Stem formSlender, branched, slightly compressed stems reaching toward the surface—
TurionsProduces hard, prickly vegetative winter buds (turions) in leaf axils before summer dieback—
Plant habit—Entirely submerged, branching, free-floating, dark green
Leaves—Deeply divided, needle-like, arranged in whorls, rigid, stiff texture when handled
Roots—None; plant is fully independent of soil
Flowers—Tiny, obscure, emerging briefly at water surface
Fruit—Small, hard, spine-tipped, 1-seeded nut
Feel—Stiff, brittle; feels like fine wires when squeezed
