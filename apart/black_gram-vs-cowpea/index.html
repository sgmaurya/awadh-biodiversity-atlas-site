Black Gram or Cowpea? उड़द या लोबिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

उड़दBlack GramVigna mungo
लोबियाCowpeaVigna unguiculata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cowpea, as the Black Gram profile puts it

Larger plant; light brown seeds with a white hilum (eye); different taste

बड़ा पौधा, भूरी दालें

Black Gram, as the Cowpea profile puts it

Smaller, bushier; black seeds without eye; different taste

छोटा पौधा, काली दालें

Black Gram: A low, bushy legume with black gram-like seeds in dark brown pods is black gram.

Cowpea: A legume with long, slender pods and brown seeds bearing a characteristic white "eye" is cowpea.
