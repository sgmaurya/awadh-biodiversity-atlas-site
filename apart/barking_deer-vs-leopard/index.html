Barking Deer or Leopard? काकड़ या तेंदुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Leopard, as the Barking Deer profile puts it

Leopard]] (Panthera pardus)

Large feline carnivore; golden coat with black rosettes; primary predator of barking deer

Barking Deer: The diminutive stocky build, bright unspotted chestnut coat, raised facial ridges, long hairy antler pedicels, and dog-like barking call distinguish the Barking Deer from all other deer.

Leopard: The medium-to-large feline build, rich golden-yellow coat densely patterned with black rose-like rosettes, and white ear-spots identify the Leopard with certainty.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Barking DeerLeopard
HabitSmall, solitary, stocky forest deer creeping through dense undergrowthHighly agile, secretive large felid frequenting forest, scrub, and agricultural edges
PelageRich glossy chestnut-red or bright rufous-brown, unspotted in adultsPale yellow to rich golden-tawny ground colour densely marked with black rosettes
AntlersShort single-forked horns mounted on long, hair-covered bony pedestals—
Facial RidgesTwo prominent dark bony ridges running down the forehead (rib-faced deer)—
CaninesElongated, downward-projecting tusk-like upper canines in males—
PostureSloping carriage with hindquarters slightly higher than the withers—
Alarm CallExtremely loud, sharp, dog-like barking uttered repeatedly when alarmed—
Rosettes—Circular or polygonal clusters of small black spots enclosing a darker buff centre
Underparts—White on throat, breast, belly, and inner limbs, marked with solid black spots
Tail—Long, slender, spotted above with a white underside and black tip
Head Marks—Solid black spots on forehead, cheeks, and muzzle; rounded ears with black back
Demeanour—Fluid, silent, low-slung stalking gait; readily hauls prey into tree forks
