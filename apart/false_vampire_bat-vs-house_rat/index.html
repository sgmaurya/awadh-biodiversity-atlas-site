Greater False Vampire Bat or House Rat? बड़ा चमगादड़ या घर का चूहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Rat, as the Greater False Vampire Bat profile puts it

Fruit bat]] roost companions

Larger (forearm 15–20 cm), eat fruit only, different face structure

Greater False Vampire Bat: A medium-sized bat (wingspan 45–55 cm) with very large funnel-shaped ears, a prominent nose-leaf, and pale grey-brown fur, hanging from cave ceilings or old buildings during the day, is a greater false vampire bat. Despite the name, it is harmless and beneficial, feeding on insects and small prey. It is a natural pesticide.

House Rat: A dark-furred, climbing rat with a tail longer than its body, found in grain stores and climbing rafters, is a House Rat.
