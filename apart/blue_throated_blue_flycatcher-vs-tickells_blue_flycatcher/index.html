Blue-throated Blue Flycatcher or Tickell's Blue Flycatcher? ब्लू-थ्रोटेड फ्लाईकैचर या टिकेल्स ब्लू फ़्लाईकैचर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue-throated Blue Flycatcher, as the Tickell's Blue Flycatcher profile puts it

Longer tail; white loral patch on males; prefers higher elevations

नीली-गले की बुलबुल

Blue-throated Blue Flycatcher: The combination of deep blue upperparts, blue throat projecting like a wedge into the rich orange breast, and pure white belly identifies the male Blue-throated Blue Flycatcher.

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher: Tickell's Blue Flycatcher is a small blue flycatcher with peachy-buff underparts (in males). Males are brilliant blue; females duller. Favours shaded forest understory. If the bird is larger or lacks the peachy underparts, it is likely a different species.
