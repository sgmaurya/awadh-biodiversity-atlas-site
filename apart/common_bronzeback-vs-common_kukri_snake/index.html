Common Bronzeback Tree Snake or Common Kukri Snake? ब्रॉन्ज़बैक साँप या कुकरी साँप?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake, as the Common Kukri Snake profile puts it

Uniform green or brown, no bands; larger; tree-dwelling

हरा या भूरा, पट्टियाँ नहीं, पेड़ों पर रहता है

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake: A slender, uniformly green or bronze-brown snake moving fast along a tree branch during the day with a long prehensile tail is a Common Bronzeback and is completely harmless.

Common Kukri Snake: A small dark snake with white/cream bands on the back only (belly pale and unbanded) and clearly visible eyes that never bites is a Kukri Snake. Always harmless.
