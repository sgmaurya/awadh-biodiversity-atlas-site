Neem or Soapnut? नीम या रीठा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीमNeemAzadirachta indica
रीठाSoapnutSapindus mukorossi

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Soapnut profile puts it

Pinnate leaves too, but leaflets toothed and lopsided; fruit small, olive-like, yellow

पत्तियाँ कटी-फटी, फल छोटा

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.

Soapnut: A tall tree with long feathery leaves whose yellow-brown fruit lathers when rubbed in water is reetha; nothing else in the district does that.
