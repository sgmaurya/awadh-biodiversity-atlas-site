How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peregrine Falcon, as the Eurasian Hobby profile puts it

Peregrine Falcon]] (Falco peregrinus)

Much larger and heavier; broad black moustachial stripe; barred underparts rather than heavily streaked; powerful swift flight (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Eurasian Hobby, as the Peregrine Falcon profile puts it

Much smaller (28–35 cm); rufous thighs and underparts; russet cheeks

भरत

Eurasian Hobby: The slaty-grey upperparts, bold black moustache contrasting with clean white throat, heavily streaked underparts, and rich rufous unstreaked thighs distinguish the Eurasian Hobby (Baker 1928, p. 42).

Peregrine Falcon: Peregrine Falcon is readily identified by the combination of black cap, white cheeks, and distinctive black malar ("moustache") stripe. No other falcon in the region shows this facial pattern. The blue-grey back and medium-large size are also diagnostic.
