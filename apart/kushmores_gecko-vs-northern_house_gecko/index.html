How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern House Gecko, as the Kushmore's Gecko profile puts it

Hemidactylus flaviviridis

Noticeably larger in adult size with a smooth, velvety dorsum devoid of enlarged tubercles (Sharma 2002)

Kushmore's Gecko: Small sandy-coloured gecko with 16 to 18 rows of moderate keeled tubercles inhabiting dry rural masonry and earth banks rather than polished domestic walls.

Northern House Gecko: A small, pale gecko with smooth skin and a high-pitched chik-chik-chik call, found on house walls at night, is a Northern House Gecko.
