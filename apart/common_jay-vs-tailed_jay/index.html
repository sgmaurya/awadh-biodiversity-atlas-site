Common Jay or Tailed Jay? कॉमन जे तितली या टेल्ड जे तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Jay: Black butterfly with white patches on dark background; short tail on hindwing; on citrus and Ruta plants; fast flight with frequent perching.

Tailed Jay: Black butterfly with large white or greenish-white patches; longer, more obvious tail on hindwing than G. doson; on citrus and Ruta plants; larger overall.
