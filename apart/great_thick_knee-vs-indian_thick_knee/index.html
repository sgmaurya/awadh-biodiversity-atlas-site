How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Thick-knee, as the Indian Thick-knee profile puts it

Great Thick-knee]] (Esacus recurvirostris)

Larger (50 cm); long upturned bill; different habitat (river-beds)

Great Thick-knee: Large bird + extremely thick, long greenish legs + large head with prominent forehead + long curved bill + pale yellow eyes = Great Thick-knee. No other large wader has such thick legs.

Indian Thick-knee: Indian Thick-knee is identified by very large yellow eyes, long yellow legs with notably swollen "knees", and cryptic grey-brown camouflaged plumage. Nocturnal habits and ground-dwelling are defining. Large eyes are most distinctive feature.
