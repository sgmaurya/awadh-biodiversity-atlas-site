Jungle Babbler or Puff-throated Babbler? सातभाई या पफ-थ्रोटेड बैबलर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Babbler, as the Puff-throated Babbler profile puts it

Turdoides striata

Much larger size, uniform dull grey-brown plumage, yellowish-white eyes, and loud gregarious flocking habits

Jungle Babbler: Earthy grey-brown plumage, yellowish bill, staring pale-yellow eyes, and an obligate group size of 6–10 birds foraging on the ground.

Puff-throated Babbler: Small ground-dwelling bird with bright rufous-chestnut cap, white puffed throat, and boldly dark-streaked white breast.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Jungle BabblerPuff-throated Babbler
SizeMedium-sized bird (23–26 cm total length); slightly smaller than a Myna—
ColourationEarthy grey-brown plumage; pale whitish head and throat; tail slightly barred—
Eyes & BillYellowish-white bill; prominent, staring pale-yellow or cream eyes—
SocialityHighly gregarious; always seen in groups of 6–10 feeding on the ground—
BehaviourConstantly hops, turns over dry leaves, and churrs; very noisy—
Sentinel habitOne or two birds keep watch from branches while the rest feed on the ground—
Crown pattern—Distinctive rich rufous-chestnut cap contrasting with a pale buffy supercilium
Throat appearance—Clean white throat feathers loosely textured and conspicuously puffed out while singing
Breast markings—White breast and upper flanks boldly streaked with dark olive-brown to blackish drop-like spots
Upperparts—Warm olive-brown mantle with subtle paler feather shaft-streaks on neck and mantle
Habits—Strictly terrestrial, hopping continuously on forest floor and flicking leaf litter aside
