Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Lynx Spider, as the Shweta Lynx Spider profile puts it

Oxyopes salticus

Yellowish-brown body with bold dark brown lateral and abdominal stripes; lack chalky-white base color

Shweta Lynx Spider: Slender pale chalky-white lynx spider with hexagonal eye arrangement, two dark carapace stripes, and long black-spined legs.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Shweta Lynx SpiderStriped Lynx Spider
ColorationPale chalky-white to translucent yellowish-white with brown longitudinal stripes—

AbdomenElongated, tapering posteriorly, adorned with chalky chevrons and lateral linesMarked with a dark central stripe bordered by pale stripes, running from front to back
Hunting styleActive diurnal cursorial hunter pouncing directly on prey without spinning a web—
Form—Slender body; cephalothorax is high and narrow; abdomen is tapered at the rear (pencil-shaped)
Colour—Pale yellow, cream, or light tan ground colour; cephalothorax features 2–4 dark brown longitudinal lines
Legs—Very long, pale yellow, carrying prominent, long, black spines projecting at right angles
Eyes—Eight eyes arranged in a hexagonal pattern on a high head; provides excellent vision
Behaviour—Active runner and jumper on foliage; runs in short, rapid bursts; does not build prey webs

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
