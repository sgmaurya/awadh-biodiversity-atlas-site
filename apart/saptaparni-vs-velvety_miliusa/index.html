How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Devil Tree, as the Velvety Miliusa profile puts it

Alstonia scholaris]]

Whorled leaves with milky latex, small greenish-white daytime flowers, and long paired follicles

Devil Tree: A tree with leaves whorled in groups of seven or eight around the stem, long drooping seed pods, and white milky latex is saptaparni.

Velvety Miliusa: Softly velvety leaves, very long slender pedicels, and clusters of small distinct black berries distinguish Miliusa velutina from other regional annonaceous plants.
