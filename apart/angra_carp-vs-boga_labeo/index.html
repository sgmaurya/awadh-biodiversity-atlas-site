How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lateral line scales 37–39; body orange-tinted silver, lacking distinct dark longitudinal stripe

Angra Carp: An elongate, streamlined brownish-silvery minor carp with an overhanging snout bearing lateral lobes, a single pair of minute maxillary barbels, a dark longitudinal stripe along the flank, and 42–45 lateral line scales, is Labeo angra.

Boga Labeo: An elongate, streamlined silvery-orange minor carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of minute maxillary barbels, and 37–39 scales in the lateral line, is Labeo boga.
