Side by side आमने-सामने

Palmyra PalmWild Date Palm
HabitTall solitary palm, 15–30 m, trunk straight, black, often slightly swollen above the middle—
LeavesFan-shaped, 1–3 m across, stiff, blue-green, folded like a pleated fan, on a stalk 1–1.5 m with toothed edgesPinnate fronds (3–4.5 m long), dusty green-grey, clustered in a large dense crown; leaflets are stiff, sharply pointed, and arranged in multiple planes
Crown25–40 leaves in a round head; dead leaves hang in a skirt on young trees—
FlowersMale and female on separate trees; male spikes stout, finger-like, in a cluster; female spikes with round flower budsDioecious (male and female on separate trees); tiny, yellow-white, arranged on large, branched, drooping stalks
FruitRound, 12–18 cm, in bunches, purple-black with a yellow cap of sepals; three jelly-filled seed cavities when youngSmall oblong drupe (1.5–2.5 cm long); orange-yellow when ripe; contains a single grooved seed
SeedlingA pointed shoot from a deep-buried seed, with a sweet fleshy root (the "tadgola" sprout)—
Form—Solitary, unbranched palm tree (10–15 m); trunk is thick and completely covered with persistent, stair-like leaf bases
Spines—The base of each leaf frond carries sharp, hard, yellow spines (modified leaflets) up to 10 cm long
Root System—Thick, dense network of fibrous roots that bind soil tightly
