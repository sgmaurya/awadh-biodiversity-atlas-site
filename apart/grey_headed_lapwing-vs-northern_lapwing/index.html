Grey-headed Lapwing, as the Northern Lapwing profile puts it

Grey-headed Lapwing]] (Vanellus cinereus)

Plain grey head and neck, black pectoral band, yellow bill with black tip, yellow wattles, no crest (Grimmett et al. 2011)

Grey-headed Lapwing: Grey-headed Lapwing is distinguished by its slate-grey head, white cheeks, and black-and-white wing pattern in flight. The yellowish-green legs and wailing call are also diagnostic.

Northern Lapwing: The long slender recurved black crest, glossy dark green upperparts, cinnamon upper and under tail-coverts, and broad black breast patch distinguish the Northern Lapwing from all other Indian plovers (Baker 1929, p. 180).
