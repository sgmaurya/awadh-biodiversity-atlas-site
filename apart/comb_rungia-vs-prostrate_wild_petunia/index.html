Comb Rungia or Prostrate Wild Petunia? कॉम्ब रंगिया या प्रोस्ट्रेट वाइल्ड पेटुनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Prostrate Wild Petunia, as the Comb Rungia profile puts it

Ruellia prostrata]]`

Stems trailing; flowers solitary, large, pale purple-violet, trumpet-shaped; fruit a clavate capsule

Comb Rungia, as the Prostrate Wild Petunia profile puts it

Rungia pectinata]]`

Stems erect or diffuse; flowers tiny, in dense one-sided comb-like spikes with scarious bracts

Comb Rungia: Look for the dense, one-sided comb-like spikes with broad white-bordered bracts and tiny two-lipped pale blue flowers.

Prostrate Wild Petunia: Look for prostrate trailing stems with swollen purple nodes, solitary pale lilac morning flowers, and club-shaped capsules bursting in water.
