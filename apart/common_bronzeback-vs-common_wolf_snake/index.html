Common Bronzeback Tree Snake or Common Wolf Snake? ब्रॉन्ज़बैक साँप या कौड़िया साँप?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्रॉन्ज़बैक साँपCommon Bronzeback Tree SnakeDendrelaphis tristis
कौड़िया साँपCommon Wolf SnakeLycodon aulicus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Wolf Snake, as the Common Bronzeback Tree Snake profile puts it

Banded, terrestrial climber (not tree-specialist), smaller eyes, on walls/ground

बैंडेड, दीवारों पर चढ़ता है

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake: A slender, uniformly green or bronze-brown snake moving fast along a tree branch during the day with a long prehensile tail is a Common Bronzeback and is completely harmless.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common Bronzeback Tree SnakeCommon Wolf Snake
Size60–100 cm; very slender body—
ColourOlive-green, yellow-green, or bronze-brown dorsally; unmarked or with faint bands—
HeadPointed, not wider than neck; eyes large and prominentFlat and spatulate (pear-shaped), scarcely wider than the neck; snout is broad and rounded
TailLong and prehensile (about 1/3 of total length); used for climbing—
ScalesSmooth and glossy; 15 rows at midbodySmooth, shiny, and uniform on the back; lacks enlarged vertebral scales
BehaviourDiurnal; arboreal; fast-moving; no banding (unlike krait or kukri)Excellent climber; found inside houses, on rafters, or climbing walls at night; mimics krait behaviour when cornered but does not hiss
BehaviourNever bites when handled; may open mouth in threat display—
Form—Slender, slightly flattened body; tail is moderate and tapers to a fine point
Markings—10–20 broad white or cream-coloured crossbands that start immediately behind the head/neck and expand on the sides
Eyes—Small, entirely black, with vertical slit-like pupils (often hard to see)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
