Broad Bean or Chickpea? बाकला या चना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बाकलाBroad BeanVicia faba
चनाChickpeaCicer arietinum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chickpea, as the Broad Bean profile puts it

Much smaller plant; small seeds; different taste and cooking time

बहुत छोटा पौधा, छोटी दालें

Broad Bean, as the Chickpea profile puts it

Plants are much larger and thicker; leaflets are large and fleshy without serrations; pods are large and thick; less common.

बाकला / ब्रॉड बीन — विशाल मोटे पौधे, बड़े मोमी पत्रक, बड़ी मोटी फलियाँ

Broad Bean: A tall, sturdy winter legume with large white flowers bearing black spots and large thick pods containing large flattened seeds is broad bean.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Broad BeanChickpea
PlantErect herbaceous, sturdy, 40–80 cm tall, unbranched or few branches—
LeavesPinnately compound, alternate, leaflets oblong, 2–5 cm; prominent tendrilsAlternately arranged, pinnately compound (5–8 cm long) with 9–15 pairs of small, ovate leaflets carrying sharply serrated (toothed) margins
FlowersLarge, white with black spots/patches, in short axillary clustersSmall (8–12 mm long), pea-like, borne singly on long axillary stalks; color is typically pinkish-purple (Desi types) or pure white (Kabuli types)
PodsLarge, thick-walled, 10–15 cm long, green becoming dark brown at maturity, monopodial—
SeedsLarge, flattened, kidney-shaped, 12–15 mm, tan to brown with hilum lineAngular, wrinkled, beak-shaped seed (5–10 mm size) resembling a ram's head, ranging from dark brown (Desi) to cream-white (Kabuli)
Form—Small, erect, bushy annual herb (20–50 cm tall), covered in sticky glandular hairs
Glandular Hairs—Entire plant is covered in sticky glandular hairs that secrete a sour-tasting acid dew (malic and oxalic acid)
Fruit—Small, swollen, oblong, inflated pod (1.5–2.5 cm long, 1–1.5 cm wide), covered in sticky hairs, containing 1 or 2 wrinkled seeds

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
