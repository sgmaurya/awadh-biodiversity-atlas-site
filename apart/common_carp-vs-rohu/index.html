Common Carp or Rohu? कॉमन कार्प या रोहू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कॉमन कार्पCommon CarpCyprinus carpio
रोहूRohuLabeo rohita

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Common Carp profile puts it

Reddish gill covers; terminal mouth; no barbels; more streamlined

लाल गिल, मुँह सामने, कोई दाढ़ी नहीं

Common Carp: A golden-scaled, heavy, bottom-feeding fish with a downward mouth and long barbels, clouding the water as it digs, is the common carp; rohu and catla have terminal mouths and no barbels.

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.
