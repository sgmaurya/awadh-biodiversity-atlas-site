Star Jelly or Crystalwort? काई (ज़मीन की जेली वाली काई) या काई (ज़मीन की हरी गुलाबी काई)?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

काई (ज़मीन की जेली वाली काई)Star JellyNostoc commune
काई (ज़मीन की हरी गुलाबी काई)CrystalwortRiccia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crystalwort, as the Star Jelly profile puts it

Flat green rosettes with a visible structure of lobes; not slimy; do not vanish in a day

हरी लोब वाली पत्तियाँ, जेली नहीं

Star Jelly, as the Crystalwort profile puts it

Rubbery jelly with no shape, gone in a day of sun

जेली, आकार नहीं

Star Jelly: Olive-green rubbery jelly lying loose on bare soil the morning after rain, gone to black crust in the sun, is Nostoc.

Crystalwort: Coin-sized flat green rosettes of forking strap-like lobes pressed to damp soil, with no leaf or stem, are Riccia.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Star JellyCrystalwort
WetRubbery, gelatinous sheets or lobed blobs 1–10 cm across, olive-green to blue-green or brownish, lying loose on soil—
DryShrinks to a thin, brittle, black or dark brown crust that looks like scraps of burnt paper—
FeelFirm jelly, slippery, slightly elastic; no roots, no leaves, no smell—
Under a lensMade of countless beaded threads in jelly; a hand lens shows the granular look—
WhereBare damp soil in the open: courtyards, path edges, lawns, bunds, field surfacesBare damp soil, in the open or part shade, often in colonies covering a square metre
WhenAppears within hours of rain and vanishes into crust within a day or two of sun—
Form—Flat green rosettes 0.5–3 cm across, made of strap-like lobes that fork once or twice from the centre
Lobes—1–5 mm wide, fleshy, often with a groove down the middle; edges sometimes purple or with white hairs
Underside—Pale, with fine root-hairs (rhizoids) and sometimes purple scales; no true roots
Texture—Spongy-fleshy, matt; dries to a curled greyish scrap, revives when wetted
Reproduction—Spores in tiny dark swellings sunk in the lobes; no flowers, no seeds

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
