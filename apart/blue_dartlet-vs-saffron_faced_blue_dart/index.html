How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue Dartlet, as the Saffron-faced Blue Dart profile puts it

Blue Dartlet]] (Pseudagrion microcephalum)

Smaller (33–42 mm); entirely blue; lacks orange face

Blue Dartlet: Slender, needle-like sky-blue body in males with black rings, wings held folded closed along the back at rest, and widely separated eyes confirm the Blue Dartlet.

Saffron-faced Blue Dart: Orange-red face and head with blue abdomen in males (or orange head with greenish body in females) distinguishes this from all other local damselflies.
