How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brooke's House Gecko, as the Asian House Gecko profile puts it

Bark-like texture; back has regular rows of prominent, sharp tubercles; prefers outdoor trees and stone walls

जटिला छिपकली — खुरदरी छाल जैसी त्वचा

Brooke's House Gecko: A gecko with prominently rough, keeled, bark-like skin found climbing tree trunks and stone walls at night is Brooke's House Gecko.

Asian House Gecko: Smooth pinkish-grey skin, split digital lamellae under the toes, and a tail with small spines, combined with a loud chik-chik-chik nocturnal call, identify Hemidactylus frenatus.
