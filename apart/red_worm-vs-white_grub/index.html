How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Worm, as the White Grub profile puts it

Legless, segmented worm; reddish-purple with yellow stripes; lives in compost; completely different body plan.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Red WormWhite Grub
SizeSmall to medium worm; body length 60–120 mm; diameter 3–5 mm20–35 mm in length; head capsule width 4.5–6.0 mm
ColourReddish-purple to dark red, with alternating pale yellow rings between segments (tiger-like stripes)Creamy-white or translucent white body; the posterior end is swollen and dark (due to soil contents)
Segments80–120 segments; body is cylindrical and slightly flattened at the rear—
ClitellumThickened pale ring located around segments 24–32; prominent in adult worms—
MovementActive wiggler; moves rapidly when exposed to light, thrashing its body—
HabitatFound strictly in rich organic compost, manure piles, and vermi-beds; does not burrow into normal garden soil—
OdourReleases a pungent, yellowish, foul-smelling coelomic fluid when handled or squeezed—
Form—Plump, fleshy, C-shaped grub (larva) with three pairs of well-developed thoracic legs
Head—Prominent, hard, reddish-brown head capsule with strong, dark biting mandibles
Legs—Three pairs of hairy legs on the thorax (anterior body); abdomen is legless
Behaviour—Found buried in agricultural soil near crop roots; coils tightly when exposed to light

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
