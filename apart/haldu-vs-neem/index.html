Haldu or Neem? हल्दू या नीम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हल्दूHalduHaldina cordifolia
नीमNeemAzadirachta indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Haldu profile puts it

Leaflets smaller and toothed; flowers white, small; fruit a small drupe

नीम — दांतेदार पत्तियाँ

Haldu: A tall tree with large, cordate-leafed pinnate leaves, pink or white fragrant flowers, and long flat woody capsules, is haldu.

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.
