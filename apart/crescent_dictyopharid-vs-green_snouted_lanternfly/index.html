Crescent Dictyopharid or Green-snouted Lanternfly? चंद्राकार डिक्टियोफेरिड या हरी-थूथन लालटेन कीड़ा?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green-snouted Lanternfly, as the Crescent Dictyopharid profile puts it

Much larger; cephalic horn brilliant emerald-green; hindwings turquoise

बहुत बड़ा, चमकदार हरा सींग, नीले पंख

Crescent Dictyopharid, as the Green-snouted Lanternfly profile puts it

Much smaller; wings transparent with crescent mark; cephalic horn small

बहुत छोटा, पारदर्शी पंख, छोटा सींग

Crescent Dictyopharid: Slender wetland planthopper with an upturned cylindrical head horn and transparent hyaline wings bearing a prominent dark brown crescent-shaped band across the tip identifies Orthopagus lunulifer.

Green-snouted Lanternfly: Very large planthopper with a long, slender, upturned emerald-green cephalic snout dotted with white, reticulate green forewings, and brilliant turquoise-blue hindwings identifies Pyrops viridirostris.
