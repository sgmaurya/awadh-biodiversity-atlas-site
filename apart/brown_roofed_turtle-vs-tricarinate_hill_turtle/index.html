How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Roofed Turtle, as the Tricarinate Hill Turtle profile puts it

Pangshura smithii

Depressed brownish carapace with a dark vertebral stripe, fully webbed feet, and aquatic basking habits

Brown Roofed Turtle: A medium-sized turtle with a distinctly domed or roofed carapace without a sharp ridge is a Brown Roofed Turtle. It is easily distinguished from the Indian Tent Turtle by the absence of a central ridge.

Tricarinate Hill Turtle: High-domed terrestrial land turtle with three distinct sharp yellowish ridges along the dark carapace, club-like unwebbed feet, and reddish stripe over the eye.

Carapace keels—Three prominent, sharp longitudinal ridges (one vertebral, two costal) that persist throughout life
Keel color—Bright yellowish, cream, or pale orange, contrasting sharply with the dark shell (Smith 1931)
Plastron—Large, completely unhinged; pale yellow with dark reddish-brown central blotches
Feet & Digits—Completely terrestrial; digits lack webbed swimming membranes and end in stout blunt claws
Head pattern—Dark with a distinct red, orange, or yellow stripe running from the nostril over the eye to the neck
