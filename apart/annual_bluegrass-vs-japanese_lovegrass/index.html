Annual Bluegrass or Japanese Lovegrass? एनुअल ब्लू घास या जापानी लव घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

एनुअल ब्लू घासAnnual BluegrassPoa annua
जापानी लव घासJapanese LovegrassEragrostis tenella

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Japanese Lovegrass, as the Annual Bluegrass profile puts it

Monsoon-active annual; leaf sheaths with stiff white hairs at mouth; tiny spikelets

बरसात की घास, पत्ती पर सफेद बाल

Annual Bluegrass, as the Japanese Lovegrass profile puts it

Winter-active annual; leaves soft with boat-shaped tips; spikelets unhaired

जाड़ों की घास, नाव जैसे सिरे वाली पत्तियाँ

Annual Bluegrass: Bright green flaccid annual grass forming low winter tufts with boat-shaped (hooded) leaf tips and open pyramidal panicles bearing awnless ovate spikelets identifies Poa annua.

Japanese Lovegrass: Delicate annual grass with wiry culms, prominent long white hairs at the leaf sheath mouth, and effuse feathery panicles bearing minute spikelets with ciliate-keeled paleas identifies Eragrostis tenella.
