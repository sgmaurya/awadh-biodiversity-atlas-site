How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Worm, as the Morris's Earthworm profile puts it

Striped reddish-yellow banded body; emits pungent yellow fluid when pinched

लाल-पीली धारियों वाला शरीर, दबाने पर तीखी गंध

Morris's Earthworm: A medium-sized reddish-brown active worm with an annular clitellum on segments 14 to 16 and a continuous ring of fine setae around each segment identifies Perichaeta morrisi.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Morris's EarthwormRed Worm
HabitCylindrical, active, epigeic-endogeic burrowing megascolecid earthworm—
ColorationPurplish-brown to chestnut-red dorsally with a pale iridescent sheen—
ClitellumComplete ring-like collar occupying segments 14 to 16, smooth and paleThickened pale ring located around segments 24–32; prominent in adult worms
SetaeNumerous perichaetine setae arranged in a continuous ring around each segment—
MovementRapid, energetic crawling and thrashing escape responses when disturbedActive wiggler; moves rapidly when exposed to light, thrashing its body
Size—Small to medium worm; body length 60–120 mm; diameter 3–5 mm
Colour—Reddish-purple to dark red, with alternating pale yellow rings between segments (tiger-like stripes)
Segments—80–120 segments; body is cylindrical and slightly flattened at the rear
Habitat—Found strictly in rich organic compost, manure piles, and vermi-beds; does not burrow into normal garden soil
Odour—Releases a pungent, yellowish, foul-smelling coelomic fluid when handled or squeezed

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
