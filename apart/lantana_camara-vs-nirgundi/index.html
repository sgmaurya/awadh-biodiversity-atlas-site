Lantana or Nirgundi? पंचफूली या निर्गुंडी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lantana, as the Nirgundi profile puts it

Flowers are multicoloured, clustered; leaves lack the peppery smell; more aggressive as a weed

लंटाना, बुरी झाड़ी — रंग-बिरंगे फूल

Lantana: A 1–3 m shrub with square prickled stems + aromatic crushed leaves + multicoloured flower heads in one cluster + glossy black berries = Lantana camara. The multicoloured flower head is the most reliable single field character.

Nirgundi: A shrub with palmately compound, strongly aromatic leaves and violet-blue flowers in spikes, growing in poor ground, is nirgundi.
