Castor or Dhatura? अरंडी या धतूरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अरंडीCastorRicinus communis
धतूराDhaturaDatura metel

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dhatura, as the Castor profile puts it

Smaller, leaves not lobed, large trumpet flowers, spiny fruit but egg-shaped and with hard spines

तुरही जैसा फूल

Castor: Giant palmately-cut leaves whose stalk joins the blade in from the edge, plus soft-spined round capsules with mottled seeds, is castor.

Dhatura: Large roadside herb + large funnel-shaped white flower opening at dusk + large spiny round fruit + strong smell = Datura metel. The spiny capsule is the single most reliable feature — nothing else common in eastern UP has it.

Side by side आमने-सामने
