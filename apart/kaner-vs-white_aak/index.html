Kaner or White Aak? कनेर या सफेद आक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कनेरKanerNerium oleander
सफेद आकWhite AakCalotropis procera

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Aak, as the Kaner profile puts it

Leaves waxy, blue-grey; flowers white or pink with crown-like structure; different growth form

सफ़ेद आक — मोटी पत्तियाँ

Kaner: A cultivated shrub with narrow, leathery, dark green leaves and large, showy, fragrant pink, white, red, or yellow flowers, with long brown pods, is kaner. All parts are extremely poisonous.

White Aak: A soft, grey-blue, waxy-leaved shrub with pale pink or white waxy flowers and inflated green fruit pods, growing in dry wastelands, is white aak. The latex is toxic.
