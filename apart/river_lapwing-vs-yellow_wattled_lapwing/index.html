River Lapwing, as the Yellow-wattled Lapwing profile puts it

Smaller (22–25 cm); spiky crown; lacks face wattles; river sandbank habitat only

नदी टिटहरी

River Lapwing: The River Lapwing is the smallest lapwing in the region with a spiky crown and is STRICTLY found on exposed river sandbanks. Other lapwings have wattles or plain heads and occur in fields or grassland.

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
