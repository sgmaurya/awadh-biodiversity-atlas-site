Dark Grass Blue or Lesser Grass Blue? डार्क ग्रास ब्लू तितली या छोटी नीली तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Grass Blue, as the Dark Grass Blue profile puts it

Lesser Grass Blue]] (Zizina otis)

Larger; different spot pattern on underside; more distinct rings

Dark Grass Blue, as the Lesser Grass Blue profile puts it

Slightly larger; underside has darker spots with a central spot in the hindwing cell; upperside of male is darker violet

छोटी काली-नीली तितली — गहरा रंग, अधिक बिंदु

Dark Grass Blue: The smallest Indian butterfly; males dark blue, females brownish; ventral surface pale with black dots; very weak flight staying low over grass.

Lesser Grass Blue: Tiny size (under 24 mm wingspan), upperside pale violet-blue (male) or brown (female) with a dark border, underside pale grey with small black spots, and very low flight close to the grass canopy identify the Lesser Grass Blue.
