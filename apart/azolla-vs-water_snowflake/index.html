Water Fern or Water Snowflake? एज़ोला या छोटी कुमुदिनी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

एज़ोलाWater FernAzolla pinnata
छोटी कुमुदिनीWater SnowflakeNymphoides indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Fern, as the Water Snowflake profile puts it

Tiny fern-like rosettes (1–3 cm); no flowers; dark green turning reddish in sun

पपड़ी जैसी बहुत छोटी पत्तियाँ

Water Fern: Tiny floating triangular aquatic fern (1.0–2.5 cm) forming green-red mosaic mats on water, with branched feather-like leaves and hanging hair-like roots, is the Water Fern (Azolla).

Water Snowflake: Small round leaves with frilly petals in yellow or pale lilac, delicate growth, no mat formation — water snowflake.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Water FernWater Snowflake
Plant habitTiny, floating, branched annual or perennial aquatic fern, measuring 1.0–2.5 cm, forming dense velvet mats—
Fronds (Leaves)Scale-like, arranged in two overlapping rows; green in winter/shade, turning dark brick-red in hot sunlight—
RootsSimple, unbranched, hair-like roots (1–3 cm) hanging freely in the water column below the leavesThin, fibrous, floating or anchored in mud; lack the air-filled chambers of water lilies
Spore productionNon-flowering; reproduces via spores contained in tiny globular structures (sporocarps) under the leaves—
SymbiosisHas a symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing cyanobacteria inside leaf cavities—
Form—Floating or partially submerged aquatic herb; leaves scattered or in loose rosettes
Leaves—Orbicular to oval, 2–10 cm diameter, smooth waxy upper surface, often with darker blotches or mottling; underside purplish-red
Leaf stalk—Slender, flexible; variable length depending on water depth
Flowers—Yellow, white or pale lilac; 5 petals, fringed or toothed margins; 1.5–2 cm across; borne on short stalks at leaf axils
Fruit—Thin-walled capsule; many small seeds
