Asian Watergrass or Burgu Grass? एशियन जल घास या बरगू घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बरगू घासBurgu GrassEchinochloa stagnina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Burgu Grass, as the Asian Watergrass profile puts it

Robust stoloniferous grass; leaves without swollen spongy floats; panicle dense

मोटी तने वाली घास, फूले हुए डंठल नहीं

Asian Watergrass, as the Burgu Grass profile puts it

Floating grass with swollen spongy leaf sheaths; leaves short ovate

पत्तियाँ छोटी, फूले हुए स्पंज जैसे डंठल

Asian Watergrass: Free-floating aquatic grass with swollen spongy leaf sheaths, short ovate-oblong leaves with water-repellent undersides, and small triangular awned panicles identifies Hygroryza aristata.

Burgu Grass: Robust semi-aquatic grass rising from long floating spongy culms, with a distinct fringe of stiff hairs representing the ligule and dense nodding awned panicles, identifies Echinochloa stagnina.

CulmsSpongy, 30–100 cm long, floating, rooting profusely at nodes with feathery pinkish-green roots (Bor 1960)Spongy, decumbent and creeping in mud, rooting at lower submerged nodes (Bor 1960)
PetiolesSwollen, hollow, inflated into a spongy bladder functioning as a buoyant float—
LeavesOvate-oblong, 3–8 cm long, 10–20 mm wide, obtuse, glaucous, papillose beneath (Bor 1960)Linear, 15–45 cm long, 6–25 mm wide, with rough scabrid cutting margins (Duthie 1888)
InflorescenceTriangular or ovate open panicle 5–8 cm long, emerging erect above the water (Bor 1960)Dense, nodding, linear or pyramidal panicle 10–25 cm long with secund racemes (Bor 1960)
SpikeletsSolitary, lanceolate, 7–8 mm long, terminating in a long slender scaberulous awn 10–14 mm long (Bor 1960)—
Ligule—A prominent fringe of stiff yellowish hairs on lower leaves (Bor 1960)
Awns—Long, rigid, scabrid, 5–25 mm long, projecting from fertile spikelets (Bor 1960)
