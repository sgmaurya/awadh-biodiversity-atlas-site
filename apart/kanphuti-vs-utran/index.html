Balloon Vine or Utran? कनफूटी या उतरन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

उतरनUtranPergularia daemia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Balloon Vine, as the Utran profile puts it

Leaves bipinnate (compound); fruits are inflated pods, not horn-like follicles

पंख-सरीखी पत्तियाँ, गुब्बारे जैसे फल

Balloon Vine: A delicate, thin-stemmed climber with feathery compound leaves, small white flowers, and distinctive inflated, pale green, balloon-like fruits containing black seeds with white heart-shaped arils is kanphuti.

Utran: A weak climbing vine with opposite heart-shaped leaves, star-shaped fragrant pink flowers, and paired horn-like fruits is utran.
