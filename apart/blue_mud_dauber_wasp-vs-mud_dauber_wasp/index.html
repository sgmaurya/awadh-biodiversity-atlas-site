How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mud Dauber Wasp, as the Blue Mud-dauber Wasp profile puts it

Black and bright yellow with banded legs; builds its own multi-celled mud nests

काला और पीला शरीर, खुद मिट्टी का नया घोंसला बनाती है

Blue Mud-dauber Wasp: A glistening metallic sapphire-blue wasp with a remarkably thin thread-like petiole stocking spiders into mud nests identifies Chalybion bengalense.

Mud Dauber Wasp: Large black and reddish wasp constructing clusters of long mud cells under building eaves, hunting spiders: mud dauber wasp.
