European Roller or Oriental Dollarbird? यूरोपियन रोलर या ओरिएंटल डॉलरबर्ड?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Dollarbird, as the European Roller profile puts it

Oriental Dollarbird]] (Eurystomus orientalis)

Stocky dark greenish-brown plumage, heavy bright vermilion bill, prominent pale blue-green circular wing patch ('dollar') in flight (Baker 1927, p. 229)

European Roller, as the Oriental Dollarbird profile puts it

European Roller]] (Coracias garrulus)

Light cinnamon-rufous back, pastel turquoise head and underparts, black bill, longer tail (Baker 1927, p. 223)

European Roller: The combination of an unstreaked pastel greenish-blue head, throat, and underparts with a light cinnamon-rufous back readily separates the European Roller from the Indian Roller (Baker 1927, p. 223).

Oriental Dollarbird: The stout dark build, wide vermilion-red bill and legs, and broad pale blue band near the base of the outer primaries distinguish the Oriental Dollarbird (Baker 1927, p. 229).
