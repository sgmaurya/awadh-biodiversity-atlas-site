How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddyfield Warbler, as the West Himalayan Bush Warbler profile puts it

Pale rufous-brown above with brighter rump; distinct whitish supercilium behind eye; plain unspotted breast; square-tipped tail

धान फड़की

Paddyfield Warbler: Paddyfield warbler is a small, plain reed warbler best identified by its continuous, melodious warbling song given frequently in winter from exposed reed tops.

West Himalayan Bush Warbler: A skulking, unstreaked olive-brown warbler in dense grass with a rounded graduated tail and diffuse dark spots across the upper breast is a West Himalayan Bush Warbler.
