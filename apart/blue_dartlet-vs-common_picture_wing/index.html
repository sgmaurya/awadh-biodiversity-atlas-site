How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue Dartlet, as the Common Picture Wing profile puts it

Blue Dartlet]] (Agriocnemis pygmaea)

A tiny damselfly, wings clear and held closed over the body at rest; nothing like the broad patterned wings of a picture wing

Blue Dartlet: Slender, needle-like sky-blue body in males with black rings, wings held folded closed along the back at rest, and widely separated eyes confirm the Blue Dartlet.

Common Picture Wing: Small dragonfly with distinctly patterned wings showing dark and light patches, perching low on vegetation near shallow water, is the Common Picture Wing.
