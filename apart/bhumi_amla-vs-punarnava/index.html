Bhumi Amla or Punarnava? भुई आँवला या पुनर्नवा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पुनर्नवाPunarnavaBoerhavia diffusa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Punarnava, as the Bhumi Amla profile puts it

Larger plant; opposite leaves with white underside; sticky fruits

बड़ा पौधा, सफेद पत्ती

Bhumi Amla: A small herb with minute leaves and small red fruits in a disturbed field is bhumi amla.

Punarnava: Prostrate spreading weed with fleshy reddish-purple stems, unequal opposite leaves that are green above and whitish beneath, tiny pink flowers, and sticky, 5-ribbed club-shaped fruits, is Punarnava.

Side by side आमने-सामने
