How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddyfield Warbler, as the Moustached Warbler profile puts it

Paddyfield Warbler]] (Acrocephalus agricola)

Smaller; lacks facial marks; different proportions; finer bill

Moustached Warbler: A small brown warbler in dense reed beds with males showing distinctive black "moustache" marks (loral and eye-stripe) is Moustached Warbler. The crackling "tzik-tzik" flight call and bold facial marks on males are the best identification features.

Paddyfield Warbler: Paddyfield warbler is a small, plain reed warbler best identified by its continuous, melodious warbling song given frequently in winter from exposed reed tops.
