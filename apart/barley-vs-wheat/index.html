Barley or Wheat? जौ या गेहूं?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जौBarleyHordeum vulgare
गेहूंWheatTriticum aestivum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wheat, as the Barley profile puts it

Awns shorter or absent; spikelets arranged differently; grain larger, rounder

गेहूँ — बाली में सियाल छोटी या नहीं; दाना बड़ा, गोल

Barley, as the Wheat profile puts it

Spikes have much longer, parallel, upward-pointing bristles (awns); spikelets are arranged in a 6-row or 2-row layout; grains are husked.

जौ — अधिक लंबी बालियाँ, छः-पंक्ति विन्यास, छिलके वाले दाने

Barley: A winter cereal with stiff, long, prominent awns on every spikelet, erect posture, and ripening 10 days before wheat. Grows October to April.
