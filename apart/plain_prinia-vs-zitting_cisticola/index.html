Plain Prinia or Zitting Cisticola? मैदान फुदकी या जिंक फुदकी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जिंक फुदकीZitting CisticolaCisticola juncidis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Zitting Cisticola, as the Plain Prinia profile puts it

Much smaller; heavily streaked brown back; much shorter tail; performs bouncing display flight

जिंक फुदकी — छोटा आकार, धारीदार पीठ, छोटी पूँछ, विशेष उड़ान

Plain Prinia, as the Zitting Cisticola profile puts it

Much larger; much longer tail; unstreaked grey-brown back; lacks bouncing display flight

मैदान फुदकी — बड़ा आकार, लंबी पूँछ, धारी रहित पीठ

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.

Zitting Cisticola: Tiny grass-warbler with a heavily streaked brown back, a short white-tipped fan-shaped tail, making a bouncing flight high over grass while calling a repeating mechanical "zitt... zitt... zitt", is a Zitting Cisticola.
