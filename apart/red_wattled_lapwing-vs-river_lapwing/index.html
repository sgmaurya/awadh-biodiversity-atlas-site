Red-wattled Lapwing or River Lapwing? टिटहरी या नदी टिटहरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नदी टिटहरीRiver LapwingVanellus duvaucelii

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

River Lapwing, as the Red-wattled Lapwing profile puts it

Grey-brown throat; black crest; black patch on belly; lacks red wattle; restricted to riverbeds

नदी टिटहरी — लाल लोथड़े का अभाव, काली कलगी

Red-wattled Lapwing, as the River Lapwing profile puts it

Red face wattles; black and white plumage

लाल झलरें वाली टिटहरी

Red-wattled Lapwing: Medium size, long yellow legs, black head and chest, red facial wattle, and loud "did-he-do-it" alarm calls identify the Red-wattled Lapwing.

River Lapwing: The River Lapwing is the smallest lapwing in the region with a spiky crown and is STRICTLY found on exposed river sandbanks. Other lapwings have wattles or plain heads and occur in fields or grassland.
