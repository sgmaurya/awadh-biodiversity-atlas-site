How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Kestrel, as the Red-necked Falcon profile puts it

Common Kestrel]] (Falco punctatus) — if sympatric

Smaller; different coloration; different hunting habitat

Common Kestrel: Small rufous-brown falcon with pointed wings, long barred tail, and a black-tipped yellow bill, hovering stationary over crop fields, is a Common Kestrel.

Red-necked Falcon: A small falcon with distinctive rufous cap and nape, blue-grey back, and white underparts with barring, soaring and hunting over grasslands and cultivated areas, is a Red-necked Falcon. The rufous head is diagnostic.
