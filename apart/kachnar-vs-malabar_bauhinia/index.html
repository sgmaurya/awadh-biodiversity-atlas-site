How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kachnar, as the Malabar Bauhinia profile puts it

Bauhinia variegata]]`

Large showy fragrant pink or white flowers with purple markings; leaves not sour to the taste

Kachnar: A tree with bilobed, camel-footprint-shaped leaves and large pink striped flowers in winter, is kachnar.

Malabar Bauhinia: Look for the distinctly sour, acidic taste of the leaves and small dull whitish flowers in dense rusty-tomentose racemes.
