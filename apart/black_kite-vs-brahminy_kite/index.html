Black Kite or Brahminy Kite? चील या ब्राह्मणी चील?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

चीलBlack KiteMilvus migrans
ब्राह्मणी चीलBrahminy KiteHaliastur indus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brahminy Kite, as the Black Kite profile puts it

Distinctive white head and breast, chestnut body; square tail

Black Kite, as the Brahminy Kite profile puts it

Uniformly dark brown plumage; deeply forked tail; scavenger of dry areas, lacks chestnut-and-white pattern

चील — गहरा भूरा शरीर, दो-शाखी पूँछ, मुख्य रूप से कचरा बीनने वाली

Brahminy Kite: Bright chestnut body, contrasting white head and breast, and a rounded tail flying near rivers or lakes confirm the Brahminy Kite.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Black KiteBrahminy Kite
SizeMedium-sized raptor (55–65 cm total length); wingspan 130–150 cm; weight 600–1000 gMedium-sized raptor (44–52 cm total length); wingspan is around 110–125 cm
PlumageUniformly dark brown; slightly paler, streaked head and underparts—
TailDistinctly forked (notched) tail; twisted constantly to steer in flightRounded tail with a white or pale tip (distinguishes it from the forked-tailed Black Kite)
WingsLong, broad, deeply "fingered" wingtips (outer primaries)—
Bare partsBlack hooked bill with a yellow cere (fleshy base); yellow legsBill is pale greenish-yellow or horn-coloured with a yellow cere; legs and feet are yellow
BehaviourEffortless gliding in thermals; bold scavenger, will snatch food near markets—
Colouration—Adult has a striking white head, neck, and breast; contrasting bright chestnut body and wings
Immature—Uniformly dull brown and streaked with buff (resembles Black Kite but has rounded tail)
Habitat—Almost always seen near water bodies: major rivers, wetlands, or irrigated rice fields

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
