Jacquemont's Field Crab or Ganga River Prawn? खेत का केकड़ा या गंगा झींगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ganga River Prawn, as the Jacquemont's Field Crab profile puts it

Elongated body; long, thin legs; translucent carapace; active swimmer, does not burrow

झींगा — लंबा शरीर, पतले पैर, तैरना

Jacquemont's Field Crab: Mud-brown colouration, carapace wider than long, five pairs of legs with strong claws, and deep burrows in agricultural clay bunds identify Jacquemont's Field Crab.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Jacquemont's Field CrabGanga River Prawn
CarapaceBroad, slightly convex shield, wider than long (40–55 mm width); smooth surface—
ColourationDark chocolate-brown, olive-brown, or dull yellowish-brown; matches muddy soil—
ChelipedsStrong claws (chelipeds), slightly unequal in size; used for crushing food and defenseSecond pair greatly enlarged in adult males; banded blue-grey
Walking legsFour pairs of slender, hairy walking legs; lack swimming paddles—
EyesSmall eyes on movable stalks, retracting into protective sockets in the carapace—
Size—10–20 cm body length; male larger than female
Colour—Translucent grey-olive with bluish-green tints; scattered darker mottling
Rostrum—Long, upward-curved spike between eyes with teeth on both dorsal and ventral edges
Activity—Nocturnal — most active at night; caught by fishermen with cast nets
Habitat—Sandy-rocky river beds; undercuts, submerged logs — never brackish water
Spawn—Female carries 500–2,000 olive-green eggs on pleopods
