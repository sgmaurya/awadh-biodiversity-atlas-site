How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mallard, as the Common Shelduck profile puts it

Mallard]] (Anas platyrhynchos)

Much smaller (56–65 cm); different head and body pattern

Common Shelduck: A large (58–67 cm) duck with glossy green head, bright red bill, white underparts, and chestnut breast band is a male Common Shelduck. Pairs often rest on muddy banks or in shallow water. Much larger and more brightly patterned than other winter ducks.

Mallard: A large duck with a glossy green head and white chest (males) — is a Mallard. This is by far the most familiar duck species globally. Females shown blue speculum in flight and paired with green-headed males in winter.
