How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Quail, as the Rain Quail profile puts it

Common Quail]] (Coturnix coturnix)

Larger build, barred outer primaries, pale buff throat with anchor-mark in males, lacking black breast patch

Common Quail: Common Quail is a small, cryptic ground-dweller of grassland and stubble, identifiable by its small size, mottled plumage, and distinctive male call. The reluctant flight (short bursts) and ground behavior are characteristic.

Rain Quail: The prominent solid black breast patch and unbarred brown outer primaries distinguish the male Rain Quail from all other quails in India (Baker 1928).
