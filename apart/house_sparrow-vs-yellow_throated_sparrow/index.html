How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Sparrow, as the Yellow-throated Sparrow profile puts it

House Sparrow]] (Passer domesticus)

Darker brown; no yellow throat; inhabits human settlements

House Sparrow: Small brown bird in or near houses + male has black bib on throat + female has pale eyebrow = House Sparrow. Tree Sparrow has chestnut crown and black ear patch (both sexes), not a bib.

Yellow-throated Sparrow: Yellow-throated Sparrow is distinguished by its pale sandy-brown plumage and distinctive yellow throat patch. House Sparrow is darker, more robust, and prefers human settlements. Yellow-throated Sparrow flocks in open fields are distinctive.
