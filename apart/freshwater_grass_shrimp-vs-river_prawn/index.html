Freshwater Grass Shrimp or Ganga River Prawn? छोटा झींगा या गंगा झींगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटा झींगाFreshwater Grass ShrimpMacrobrachium lamarrei
घोंघे और केकड़े गंगा झींगाGanga River PrawnMacrobrachium gangeticum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ganga River Prawn, as the Freshwater Grass Shrimp profile puts it

Much larger (body 150–250 mm); male has massive, heavy, blue-colored claws; found in main river channels

गंगा झींगा — बहुत बड़ा आकार, भारी नीले पंजे

Freshwater Grass Shrimp, as the Ganga River Prawn profile puts it

Much smaller (3–6 cm); found in smaller streams and irrigation channels; no enlarged claws

Freshwater Grass Shrimp: Small, semi-transparent freshwater shrimp (30–60 mm) with a saw-like snout bearing 6–9 dorsal teeth, swimming with sudden jerks in shallow pond margins, is the Freshwater Grass Shrimp.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Freshwater Grass ShrimpGanga River Prawn
SizeSmall shrimp; adult total length around 30–60 mm (much smaller than the river prawn)10–20 cm body length; male larger than female
ColourationSemi-transparent, glass-like body; has fine light-brownish or greenish stripes across the abdomen—
ClawsSecond pair of walking legs (chelipeds) is modified into thin, delicate, smooth claws, equal in size—
RostrumLong, saw-like snout (rostrum) projecting forward from the head, curved upward at the tipLong, upward-curved spike between eyes with teeth on both dorsal and ventral edges
Rostrum Teeth6 to 9 sharp teeth on the upper (dorsal) edge and 3 to 5 teeth on the lower (ventral) edge—
Colour—Translucent grey-olive with bluish-green tints; scattered darker mottling
Chelipeds—Second pair greatly enlarged in adult males; banded blue-grey
Activity—Nocturnal — most active at night; caught by fishermen with cast nets
Habitat—Sandy-rocky river beds; undercuts, submerged logs — never brackish water
Spawn—Female carries 500–2,000 olive-green eggs on pleopods
