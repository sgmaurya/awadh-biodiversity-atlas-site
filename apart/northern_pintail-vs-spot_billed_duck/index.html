Northern Pintail or Indian Spot-billed Duck? पिनटेल बत्तख या गुगराल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern Pintail, as the Indian Spot-billed Duck profile puts it

Smaller; male has chocolate head, white breast; pointed tail

सर्दी का मेहमान, लंबी पूँछ

Northern Pintail: Elegant, long-necked duck with a dark chocolate-brown head, white stripe running down the neck, and long pointed pin-like central tail feathers (male) is a Northern Pintail.

Indian Spot-billed Duck: A large dabbling duck with a yellow bill bearing a black spot, in flocks on ponds — is a Spot-billed Duck. The spotted bill is diagnostic.
