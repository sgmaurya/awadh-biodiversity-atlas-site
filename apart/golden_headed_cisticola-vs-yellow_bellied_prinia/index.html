How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-bellied Prinia, as the Golden-headed Cisticola profile puts it

Prinia flaviventris

Slaty-grey crown; clean white throat contrasting with lemon-yellow belly; lacks golden cap

Golden-headed Cisticola: Tiny grassland warbler; breeding male has unstreaked golden-buff crown and short tail; unique buzzing and popping flight song.

Yellow-bellied Prinia: Small marshland prinia with dark slaty-grey head, white throat, bright yellow belly, and long graduated tail in tall riverine grass.

Tail—Long, strongly graduated tail, tipped with narrow whitish margins underneath
Bill & Legs—Blackish slender bill in breeding season; pale flesh-pink legs and feet
Behavior—Perches atop tall grass stems to sing, flicking its graduated tail vigorously
