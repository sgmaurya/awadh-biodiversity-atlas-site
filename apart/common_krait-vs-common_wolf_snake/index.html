Common Krait or Common Wolf Snake? करैत या कौड़िया साँप?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

करैतCommon KraitBungarus caeruleus
कौड़िया साँपCommon Wolf SnakeLycodon aulicus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Wolf Snake, as the Common Krait profile puts it

Non-venomous; white bands start immediately behind the head (not midbody); lacks enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales; eyes have vertical slits

भेड़िया सांप — हानिरहित, आँख की पुतली खड़ी

Common Krait, as the Common Wolf Snake profile puts it

Extremely venomous. White bands start midbody (not on the neck); possesses a row of large, hexagonal vertebral scales along the spine; snout is rounded; vertical pupils absent.

करैत — अत्यधिक विषैला, रीढ़ पर बड़ी षटकोणीय शल्कें, सिर पर धारियां नहीं

Common Krait: Highly polished steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody, combined with enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales along the backbone, identifies Bungarus caeruleus.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common KraitCommon Wolf Snake
FormSlender, cylindrical body; skin is highly polished, glossy, and iridescent steel-black or blue-blackSlender, slightly flattened body; tail is moderate and tapers to a fine point
Markings40–50 narrow white crossbands, often occurring in distinct pairs; bands are absent on the head/neck, starting midbody10–20 broad white or cream-coloured crossbands that start immediately behind the head/neck and expand on the sides
ScalesA row of distinctly enlarged, hexagonal vertebral scales running down the center of the backSmooth, shiny, and uniform on the back; lacks enlarged vertebral scales
HeadSmall, flat, scarcely distinct from the neck; eyes are small, dark, with indistinct pupilsFlat and spatulate (pear-shaped), scarcely wider than the neck; snout is broad and rounded
BehaviourStrictly nocturnal; inactive by day, coiling its body and hiding its head beneath the coils when threatenedExcellent climber; found inside houses, on rafters, or climbing walls at night; mimics krait behaviour when cornered but does not hiss
Eyes—Small, entirely black, with vertical slit-like pupils (often hard to see)
