How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bhant, as the Tube Flower profile puts it

Clerodendrum infortunatum]]`

Bushy shrub with large, opposite, ovate-cordate leaves; flowers pinkish-white in terminal pyramidal panicles

Bhant: A soft, spreading shrub with large, velvety leaves and white flowers in long spikes with colourful red or pink persistent calyces is bhant.

Tube Flower: Look for the tall hollow stems, leaves in whorls of three to five, and exceptionally long slender corolla tubes with protruding purple stamens.
