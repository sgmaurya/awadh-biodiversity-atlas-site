Brown-striped Semilooper or Sugarcane Looper? भूरी-धारी सेमीलूपर पतंगा या शुगरकेन लूपर पतंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भूरी-धारी सेमीलूपर पतंगाBrown-striped SemilooperMocis undata
शुगरकेन लूपर पतंगाSugarcane LooperMocis frugalis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sugarcane Looper, as the Brown-striped Semilooper profile puts it

Grey-brown (rather than pale red-brown); forewing with a diffused dark mark above the centre of vein 1 and a single oblique postmedial line (pale inwardly, red-brown outwardly), lacking the antemedial pale line and sinuous medial line of M. undata (Hampson 1894, pp. 526–527, entries 2573–2574). Both species may have a black spot above the forewing inner margin before the middle

धूसर-भूरा रंग और केवल एक तिरछी बाहरी रेखा (शुगरकेन लूपर पतंगा)

Brown-striped Semilooper, as the Sugarcane Looper profile puts it

Pale red-brown (rather than grey-brown) with an oblique antemedial pale line bordered by a diffused red-brown band, a sinuous medial line, a large indistinct reniform, and a dark postmedial line irregularly recurved at vein 2; hindwing ochreous fuscous (Hampson 1894, p. 526, as Remigia archesia). Note that both species may have a black spot above the forewing inner margin before the middle (Hampson 1894, pp. 526–527)

हल्का लाल-भूरा रंग, कई लहरदार पट्टियाँ और धूसर-गेरुआ पिछला पंख (भूरी-धारी सेमीलूपर पतंगा)

Brown-striped Semilooper: A pale red-brown moth with both an oblique pale antemedial line and an irregularly recurved postmedial line across a diffused red-brown band is Mocis undata (Hampson 1894, p. 526).

Sugarcane Looper: A grey-brown moth with an oblique postmedial line (pale inwardly, red-brown outwardly) and submarginal black specks, lacking the sinuous medial line and large reniform of M. undata, is Mocis frugalis (Hampson 1894, pp. 526–527).
