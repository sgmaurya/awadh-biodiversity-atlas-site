How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the Grey Bushchat profile puts it

Pied Bushchat]] (Saxicola caprata) — if sympatric

Males black and white; different habitat (usually wetter)

Grey Bushchat: A small, pale grey-brown chat with a distinctive dark tail, found on dry grasslands, flicking its tail nervously, is a Grey Bushchat. The pale plumage and dark tail are diagnostic.

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.
