Freshwater Garfish or Great Snakehead? सुइया या सौर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सुइयाFreshwater GarfishXenentodon cancila
सौरGreat SnakeheadChanna marulius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Snakehead, as the Freshwater Garfish profile puts it

Large, cylindrical body; snake-like head with no beak; rounded tail with orange eye-spot

सौर — बड़ा, बिना चोंच का सिर, भारी शरीर

Freshwater Garfish: Needle-like elongated body, both upper and lower jaws extended into a long tooth-armed beak, and habit of floating motionless just below the water surface identify the Freshwater Garfish.

Great Snakehead: Large size (up to 1m), snake-like head with plate scales, dark blotches on flanks, and a prominent black ocellus ringed in orange on the upper caudal fin base identify the Great Snakehead.
