Black-spotted Pierrot or Common Pierrot? ब्लैक-स्पॉटेड पियरो तितली या कॉमन पियरो तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Pierrot, as the Black-spotted Pierrot profile puts it

Common Pierrot]])

Distinctly larger; underside marked with bold broad black bars and blotches rather than fine individual spots

Black-spotted Pierrot: A small blue lycaenid butterfly fluttering around ber bushes, whose white underside is dotted with clearly separated, rounded black spots and gleaming marginal metallic specks, is the Black-spotted Pierrot.

Common Pierrot: Tiny bright blue butterfly (male) or duller brown female with pale spotted underside, always associated with ber (jujube) bushes, is the Common Pierrot.
