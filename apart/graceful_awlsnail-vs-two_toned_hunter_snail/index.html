Graceful Awlsnail or Two-toned Hunter Snail? ग्रेसफुल ऑवलस्नेल या टू-टोन्ड हंटर स्नेल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ग्रेसफुल ऑवलस्नेलGraceful AwlsnailAllopeas gracile
टू-टोन्ड हंटर स्नेलTwo-toned Hunter SnailGulella bicolor

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Two-toned Hunter Snail, as the Graceful Awlsnail profile puts it

Shorter cylindrical shell with four prominent calcareous teeth in aperture; predator

छोटा बेलनाकार खोल, मुंह पर चार दांत, शिकारी

Graceful Awlsnail, as the Two-toned Hunter Snail profile puts it

Much more slender, elongate shell; entirely herbivorous; aperture simple without internal teeth

लंबा पतला खोल, कोई दांत नहीं, शाकाहारी

Graceful Awlsnail: A minute, translucent, straw-yellow, awl-shaped turreted shell with seven to nine whorls and a toothless aperture crawling under flowerpots identifies Allopeas gracile.

Two-toned Hunter Snail: A minute cylindrical glassy shell revealing a vivid bright orange-yellow body within, with four distinct calcareous teeth inside the aperture, identifies Gulella bicolor.
