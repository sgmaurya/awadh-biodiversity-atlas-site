How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Moustached Warbler, as the West Himalayan Bush Warbler profile puts it

Strongly streaked dark crown and mantle; broad white supercilium bordered by black line; unspotted breast

मूंछों वाली फड़की

Moustached Warbler: A small brown warbler in dense reed beds with males showing distinctive black "moustache" marks (loral and eye-stripe) is Moustached Warbler. The crackling "tzik-tzik" flight call and bold facial marks on males are the best identification features.

West Himalayan Bush Warbler: A skulking, unstreaked olive-brown warbler in dense grass with a rounded graduated tail and diffuse dark spots across the upper breast is a West Himalayan Bush Warbler.

Habits—Extremely furtive; creeps like a mouse through low tangle; rarely perches exposed
