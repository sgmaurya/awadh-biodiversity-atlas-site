How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bar-headed Goose, as the Knob-billed Duck profile puts it

Bar-headed Goose]] (Anser indicus)

Winter visitor; lacks knob; has black bars on head

Bar-headed Goose: A large pale goose with a striking white head marked by two bold black bars, at rivers in winter — is a Bar-headed Goose. The head pattern is diagnostic and unmistakable.

Knob-billed Duck: The Knob-billed Duck is the largest duck in the region and the only one with a prominent black knob on the forehead (males). If a large dark duck with a forehead knob is seen, it is a Knob-billed Duck.
