How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Moon Moth, as the Eri Silkmoth profile puts it

Larger; has bold eye-spots and crescent-moon marking on wings; paler coloration

भारतीय चाँद पतंगा — बड़ा, आँख के निशान

Eri Silkmoth: Large pale cream or yellowish-brown moth with feathery antennae, wavy markings on wings, and strong association with castor plants, is the Eri Silkmoth.

Indian Moon Moth: A white-bodied, very pale green moth with a dark pink costal fascia on the forewing, round ochreous eyespots, and a pinkish-centred hindwing tail is Actias selene (Hampson 1892, pp. 13–14).

Side by side आमने-सामने

Eri SilkmothIndian Moon Moth
SizeLarge moth; body length 40–50 mm; wingspan 100–140 mm—
ColorationPale cream or yellowish-brown; forewing has wavy dark lines and darker markings—
ForewingContains a pale discal cell mark and wavy cross-lines; reddish-brown markings on outer edge—
HindwingPale yellow or cream with darker lines similar to forewing—
BodyStout, hairy, yellow-brown; females larger than malesHead, thorax, and abdomen white; palpi pink; prothorax with a dark pink band; legs pink (Hampson 1892, pp. 13–14)
AntennaeBroad, feathery (pectinate) in both sexes—
Ground colour—Very pale green wings, white at the base (Hampson 1892, p. 14)
Forewing markings—Dark pink costal fascia; outwardly-oblique pale yellow antemedial line; two inwardly-oblique slightly curved submarginal lines; pale yellow marginal band (Hampson 1892, p. 14)
Discal eyespot—Dark red-brown lunule at end of cell with a grey line on it, bounding inwardly a round ochreous spot with pinkish centre (Hampson 1892, p. 14)
Hindwing & tail—Similar to forewing; central portion of the tail pinkish (Hampson 1892, p. 14)
Female difference—Outer margin less excised and waved; yellow markings less developed; hindwing antemedial line absent; tail less pink (Hampson 1892, p. 14)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
