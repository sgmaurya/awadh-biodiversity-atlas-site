How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Banded Hornet, as the Greater Banded Hornet profile puts it

Smaller (20–22 mm); has first two abdominal segments yellow-orange; nests are larger, hanging openly

छोटा ततैया — छोटा आकार, दो नारंगी पट्टियाँ

Greater Banded Hornet: Very large hornet (25–35 mm) with a black body, a single broad orange-yellow band on the second abdominal segment, hunting bees and nesting in cavities, is the Greater Banded Hornet.

Lesser Banded Hornet: Medium-sized (15–20 mm) yellow and black hornet with thin bands and a yellow face; more aggressive than Oriental Hornet: Lesser Banded Hornet.
