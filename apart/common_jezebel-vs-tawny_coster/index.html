White wings above; underside of hindwings has bright yellow and red margin spots; flies high in tree canopies

Common Jezebel: A white butterfly with black margins on the forewing and narrow black margin on the hindwing, with striking orange-red spots on the underside, is the Common Jezebel.

Tawny Coster: Medium size, translucent orange wings with black spots and a broad black margin on hindwings, and very slow low flight close to the grass identify the Tawny Coster.
