Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Flying Barb, as the Finescale Razorbelly Minnow profile puts it

Esomus danrica

Extremely long, whip-like maxillary barbels extending to the pelvic fins; lacks ventral razor keel

Finescale Razorbelly Minnow: Slender silvery surface minnow with knife-like ventral keel from pelvic fins to anal fin, and minute scales exceeding 80 in lateral line.

Flying Barb: A tiny, slender surface minnow with extremely long hair-like maxillary barbels extending to the pelvic fins, wing-like pectoral fins, and a dark lateral stripe bordered by gold, is Esomus danrica.

Finescale Razorbelly MinnowFlying Barb
Body shapeElongate, strongly compressed laterally with a sharp, razor-like ventral keelElongate, strongly compressed, very slender (Hamilton 1822)
Scale sizeMinute, exceptionally fine scales with 80 to 90 scales in the lateral line—
Mouth orientationStrongly oblique, superior mouth directed upwards for surface feeding—
ColorationTranslucent silvery-white with a brilliant glittering silver lateral stripe—
HabitsSurface-swimming pelagic shoaler leaping out of water to evade predatory fish—
Size—Small freshwater cyprinid; reaches about 13 cm TL (Froese & Pauly 2025)
Mouth & Snout—Mouth small, directed obliquely upwards; lower jaw prominent (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Barbels—Two pairs; maxillary barbels exceptionally long, reaching pelvic or anal fin base (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Colour Pattern—Silvery-olive dorsally; a broad dark lateral band with a bright golden upper edge (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)
Pectoral Fins—Long and wing-like, reaching well past pelvic fin origin (Hamilton 1822)
Scales—Moderate cycloid scales; 27 to 34 scales in longitudinal series (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
