Bengali Barb: A slender silvery minnow with an oblique mouth, symphysial knob on the lower jaw, a single pair of short rostral barbels, and 40–44 lateral line scales, is Megarasbora elanga.

Mola Carplet: A small deep-bodied silvery-golden fish with a tiny inferior mouth and rounded fins in aquatic weeds is the mola; the depth of body and small mouth distinguish it.
