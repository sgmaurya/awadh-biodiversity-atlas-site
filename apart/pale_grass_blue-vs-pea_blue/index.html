Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pale Grass Blue, as the Pea Blue profile puts it

Smaller; lacks the long tails; brown coloration in females

घास-नीली — छोटी, पूँछ नहीं

Pale Grass Blue: A very small pale blue butterfly; males pale lilac-blue, females brownish; ventral pale with sparse spots; weak, low flight; in grassland and pulse fields.

Pea Blue: Tiny bright blue butterfly with distinctive long, thin tails tipped with black dots and orange eyespots on the hindwing, found on legume crops, is the Pea Blue.
