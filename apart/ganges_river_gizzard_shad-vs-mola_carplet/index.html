How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mola Carplet, as the Ganges River Gizzard Shad profile puts it

Amblypharyngodon mola

Slender minnow body without ventral serrated scutes; silvery with a prominent bright longitudinal band

Ganges River Gizzard Shad: Small deep-bodied silvery river fish with a downward-curved mouth, dark shoulder spot, and razor-sharp serrated abdominal keel.

Mola Carplet: A small deep-bodied silvery-golden fish with a tiny inferior mouth and rounded fins in aquatic weeds is the mola; the depth of body and small mouth distinguish it.
