How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-vented Bulbul, as the Black-crested Bulbul profile puts it

Red-vented Bulbul]] (Pycnonotus cafer)

Earth-brown plumage with dark scale-like markings, crimson-red undertail-coverts, white rump, and dark brown crest; widespread in gardens

Black-crested Bulbul: The entirely glossy jet-black head, throat, and crest, contrasted with brilliant golden-yellow underparts and pale yellow eyes, distinguish the Black-crested Bulbul from all other bulbuls.

Red-vented Bulbul: Black head with scaled breast, white rump, and a bright red vent patch is diagnostic for the Red-vented Bulbul. No other common bulbul in eastern UP has a red vent combined with a scaled black-and-brown breast.

Head & CrestEntire head, chin, and throat glossy jet-black, topped with a tall pointed crest—
UpperpartsWarm olive-yellow above, brightening to rich yellowish on rump and coverts—
UnderpartsUniform, brilliant bright golden-yellow from upper breast to under tail-coverts—
EyesStriking, pale cream-white to pale yellow irises contrasting with black head—
CallSweet, jerky, whistled phrase weet-tre-trippy-wet, with last three syllables repeated twice or more—
Plumage—Sooty-black head, throat and breast; brown back with scale-like greyish margins
Vent—Bright scarlet-red patch under the base of the tail; diagnostic at a glance
Rump—White, highly conspicuous when the bird flies or hops
Tail—Long, blackish tail, tipped with white feathers
Head crest—Small, partially erectile tuft or crest on the crown
