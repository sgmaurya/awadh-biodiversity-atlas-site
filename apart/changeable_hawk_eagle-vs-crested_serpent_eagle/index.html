Changeable Hawk-Eagle or Crested Serpent Eagle? शाह बाज़ या सर्प चील?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Serpent Eagle, as the Changeable Hawk-Eagle profile puts it

Spilornis cheela

Broad fan-like bushy crest; bare yellow face skin; broad white band across wings and tail

Changeable Hawk-Eagle: Woodland eagle with upright posture, long slender black crest, streaked breast, and densely feathered tarsi down to toes.

Crested Serpent Eagle: Crested Serpent Eagle is unique with its distinctive erectile crest and bright yellow eyes. No other eagle in eastern UP has this combination of features. The yellow spotting on dark wings is also diagnostic.
