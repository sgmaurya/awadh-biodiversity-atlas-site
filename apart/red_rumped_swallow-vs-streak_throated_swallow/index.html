Red-rumped Swallow or Streak-throated Swallow? लाल-कमर अबाबील या स्ट्रीक-थ्रोटेड स्वैलो?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

स्ट्रीक-थ्रोटेड स्वैलोStreak-throated SwallowPetrochelidon fluvicola

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-rumped Swallow, as the Streak-throated Swallow profile puts it

Rufous rump and cap; buff-coloured underparts; no throat streaks

पीठ का तिरछा

Red-rumped Swallow: Red-rumped swallow shows a distinctive rufous rump and rusty throat. If the rump is dark and back is glossy dark blue, it is a barn swallow. If the tail has extremely long filaments beyond the fork, it may be a wire-tailed swallow.

Streak-throated Swallow: A small, dark blue-black backed swallow with white underparts and distinctive black streaks on the white throat is a Streak-throated Swallow. Larger swallows with different throat patterns or longer tail wires are different species.
