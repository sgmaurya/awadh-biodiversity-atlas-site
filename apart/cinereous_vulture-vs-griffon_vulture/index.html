Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Griffon Vulture, as the Cinereous Vulture profile puts it

Griffon Vulture]] (Gyps fulvus)

Sandy-tawny or buff plumage, prominent pale ruff, and distinctly pale underwing coverts contrasting with dark flight feathers

Cinereous Vulture: The immense wingspan, uniform blackish-brown plumage lacking white rump or pale wing ruffs, heavily feathered dark thighs, and massive heavy bill identify the Cinereous Vulture.

Griffon Vulture: The immense wingspan, sandy-tawny body plumage, contrasting dark flight feathers, and distinct white ruff around the neck distinguish the Griffon Vulture.
