Lesser Whistling Duck or Northern Pintail? सीटी बत्तख या पिनटेल बत्तख?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Whistling Duck, as the Northern Pintail profile puts it

Lesser Whistling Duck]] (Dendrocygna javanica)

Rufous-brown body; long upright neck; lacks white markings and pointed tail; makes whistling calls

Lesser Whistling Duck: Small chestnut-brown duck, long neck and legs, rich rufous-chestnut upper-tail coverts (no white), yellow eye-ring, and distinctive whistling flight calls identify the Lesser Whistling Duck.

Northern Pintail: Elegant, long-necked duck with a dark chocolate-brown head, white stripe running down the neck, and long pointed pin-like central tail feathers (male) is a Northern Pintail.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Bare PartsBill is dark slate-grey to blackish; long legs and webbed feet are blue-greyBill is dark slate-grey with black edges; legs and feet are grey-blue
BehaviourOften perches on trees surrounding water; flies in compact flocks with whistle-like wing rustling—
Male tail—Diagnostic long, needle-pointed black central tail feathers (pins), extending 10–15 cm
Male neck—Dark chocolate-brown head with a white stripe running from the neck down to the chest
Female plumage—Cryptic mottled brown overall; slender grey neck; pointed (but much shorter) tail
Foraging—Feeds in shallow water by tipping forward (tail up, head down) to reach the bottom
