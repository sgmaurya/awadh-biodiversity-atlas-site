How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lemon Pansy, as the Blue Pansy profile puts it

Lemon Pansy]] (Junonia lemonias)

Pale yellow ground colour; no blue patches; smaller

Blue Pansy: A medium to large brown butterfly with distinctive bright iridescent blue patches or bands visible in certain lighting, combined with eyespots, is the Blue Pansy.

Lemon Pansy: A small to medium-sized pale yellow butterfly with large, prominent black eyespots with iridescent blue or purple centers on both wings, flying rapidly over grassland, is the Lemon Pansy.
