Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Roofed Turtle, as the Indian Eyed Turtle profile puts it

Pangshura smithii

Depressed brownish carapace with a dark vertebral stripe; lacks ringed ocelli on costal scutes

Brown Roofed Turtle: A medium-sized turtle with a distinctly domed or roofed carapace without a sharp ridge is a Brown Roofed Turtle. It is easily distinguished from the Indian Tent Turtle by the absence of a central ridge.

Indian Eyed Turtle: Domed freshwater turtle with eye-like ringed spots on each costal shield, a pointed snout, and three pale yellow stripes along each side of the neck.

Dorsal markings—Each costal scute carries an eye-like ocellus (a dark center surrounded by a narrow yellowish ring)
Vertebral stripe—A narrow, distinct yellowish or pale green stripe runs continuously along the spine (Smith 1931)
Head markings—Dark olive head adorned with three distinct, narrow yellowish stripes along each side of the neck
Snout—Prominently pointed, projecting forward into an acute beak-like profile
Plastron—Large, unhinged, pale yellow or cream with dark brown blotches on the shields
