How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Asian Mantis, as the Jeweled Flower Mantis profile puts it

Praying Mantis]])

Much larger, uniformly green; lacking eye-spots on forewings and pink hind wings

Jeweled Flower Mantis: Small green-and-white flower mantis with a prominent circular yellow-and-black jewel eye-spot on each forewing = Creobroter gemmatus.

Giant Asian Mantis: Large (60–90 mm) green insect + triangular head + folded raptorial forelegs + absolute stillness = praying mantis. Hierodula membranacea is the largest common mantis of UP's agricultural areas.
