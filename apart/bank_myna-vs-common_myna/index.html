Bank Myna or Common Myna? गंगा मैना या मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गंगा मैनाBank MynaAcridotheres ginginianus
मैनाCommon MynaAcridotheres tristis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Myna, as the Bank Myna profile puts it

Larger; yellow facial skin (not orange); no black ear patch; white wing patch larger; heavier build

साधारण मैना — पीली चमड़ी, भारी

Bank Myna, as the Common Myna profile puts it

Orange-red facial skin; black ear patch; prefers river banks

नदी मैना — नारंगी चमड़ी

Bank Myna: Smaller than Common Myna + orange-red (not yellow) facial skin + black ear patch + more gracile build + found in flocks near water = Bank Myna. The orange face and ear patch are diagnostic at a glance.

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.
