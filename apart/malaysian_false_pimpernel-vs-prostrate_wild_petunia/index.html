How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Prostrate Wild Petunia, as the Malaysian False Pimpernel profile puts it

Ruellia prostrata]]`

Larger prostrate herb with large pale violet trumpet flowers and club-shaped capsules

Malaysian False Pimpernel: Look for the square stems, small paired serrate leaves, and tiny two-lipped purplish flowers in leaf axils.

Prostrate Wild Petunia: Look for prostrate trailing stems with swollen purple nodes, solitary pale lilac morning flowers, and club-shaped capsules bursting in water.
