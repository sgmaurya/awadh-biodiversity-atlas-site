How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Stable Fly, as the Eye Fly profile puts it

Larger (7–8 mm); has a pointed proboscis that protrudes; painful bite

बड़ी, काटती है, सूंड बाहर

Eye Fly: Tiny black flies swarming around the eyes, nose, and mouth of people and cattle, entering the tear ducts and causing intense itching, are eye flies.

Stable Fly: Small grey-brown fly with four pale thoracic stripes and long straight proboscis, biting painfully on livestock or humans; larvae in cattle manure: Stable Fly (Stomoxys calcitrans).
