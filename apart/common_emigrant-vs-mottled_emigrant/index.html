Common Emigrant]] (Catopsilia pomona)

Brighter yellow (male) or whiter (female); underside lacks mottling and has more uniform coloration

Common Emigrant: Medium size, rapid straight flight, uniform lemon-yellow or greenish-white wings, and presence of pink spots (in pomona form) or clear wings (in crocale form) on underwings identify the Common Emigrant.

Mottled Emigrant: Small pale yellow (male) or white (female) butterfly with a distinctive mottled, dark-spotted and streaked underside, flying rapidly over grassland, is the Mottled Emigrant.
