How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Redshank, as the Marsh Sandpiper profile puts it

Spotted Redshank]] (Tringa erythropus)

Larger (30 cm); longer red legs; stouter bill

Marsh Sandpiper: Marsh Sandpiper is the smallest and most delicate sandpiper, with disproportionately long greenish-yellow legs, an extremely fine bill, and pale plain plumage. If the bird is larger or has red legs, it is a different species. If it bobs its tail, it is a Common Sandpiper.

Spotted Redshank: A tall, pale grey wader with long red legs and red bill, often wading in deep water alone, is a Spotted Redshank. The longer legs, paler plumage, and deeper wading distinguish it from the Common Redshank.
