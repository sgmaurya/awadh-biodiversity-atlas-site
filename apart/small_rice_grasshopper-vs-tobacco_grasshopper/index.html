Small Rice Grasshopper or Tobacco Grasshopper? स्मॉल राइस ग्रासहॉपर या टोबैको ग्रासहॉपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Rice Grasshopper, as the Tobacco Grasshopper profile puts it

Larger (15–20 mm); wings fully developed and functional; bright green colour

हरी टिड्डी, बड़ी, उड़ सकती है

Small Rice Grasshopper: A slender green grasshopper with long tegmina surpassing the abdomen tip, a broad dark lateral stripe, and blue-green flattened swimming hind tibiae is Oxya hyla.

Tobacco Grasshopper: A small (12–18 mm) grasshopper with shortened, crumpled-looking (crenulated) wings that cannot fly, found feeding on tobacco or vegetable leaves in a gregarious group — this is the Tobacco Grasshopper. Larger grasshoppers with fully developed wings that function in flight are different species.
