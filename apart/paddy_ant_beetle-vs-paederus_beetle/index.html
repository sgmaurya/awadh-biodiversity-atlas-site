Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rove Beetle, as the Paddy Ant Beetle profile puts it

Paederus fuscipes

Staphylinid rove beetle with extremely short elytra exposing most of flexible abdomen

Paddy Ant Beetle: Ant-like orange and bluish-black ground beetle running rapidly over rice stems, with an elongated neck-like pronotum.

Rove Beetle: Small, slender black beetle with orange-red wing covers, found near water during monsoon: Paederus rove beetle. Do not crush.

GaitExtremely fast cursorial runner over wet rice stems and muddy soil—
Size—Small to medium beetle; body length 7–10 mm
Colour—Predominantly black or dark grey; elytra (wing covers) typically reddish-orange or yellow-orange
Body shape—Elongate, flexible body; tapered abdomen; resembles a scorpion when abdomen is raised
Legs—Long, thin legs; typically pale or reddish
Behaviour—Attracted to lights at night; active near water during monsoon; moves rapidly
Habitat—Found in rice fields, marshes, and around water sources in evening and night hours
