Marbled Balloon Frog or Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog? गुब्बारा मेंढक या छोटा मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गुब्बारा मेंढकMarbled Balloon FrogUperodon taprobanicus
छोटा मेंढकOrnate Narrow-mouthed FrogMicrohyla ornata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog, as the Marbled Balloon Frog profile puts it

Tiny, 20–25 mm, with a dark arrow mark on the back; same small mouth; high cricket-like call

बहुत छोटा, पीठ पर तीर का निशान

Marbled Balloon Frog: A round, short-legged, tiny-mouthed frog painted with orange blotches on brown, that inflates when handled, is the balloon frog.

Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog: Tiny size (under 2.5 cm), smooth bronze skin with a dark diamond-arrow shape on the back, and a narrow pointed head identify this species.
