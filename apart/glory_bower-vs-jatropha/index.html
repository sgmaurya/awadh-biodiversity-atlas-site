Glory Bower or Jatropha? लंजई या रतनजोत?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jatropha, as the Glory Bower profile puts it

Lobed leaves, not simple; flowers red or pink; toxic latex

रतनजोत — पत्ती बँटी हुई, लाल फूल

Glory Bower: Simple opposite toothed leaves with small white flowers bearing purple stamens is Volkameria inermis.

Jatropha: A shrub with large, hand-shaped lobed leaves, red flowers, and toxins in all parts is jatropha. Never ingest the seeds or leaves.
