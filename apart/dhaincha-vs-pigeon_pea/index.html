Dhaincha: A tall, feathery-leaved monsoon annual with faintly prickly stems, purple-speckled yellow pea flowers and thread-thin pods, sown before paddy, is dhaincha.

Side by side आमने-सामने

DhainchaPigeon Pea
HabitSlender, erect, little-branched annual 1–3 m, often in sown stands as thick as a crop—
StemGreen, pithy, with scattered tiny prickles on the stem and leaf stalks (hence "bispinosa")—
LeavesPinnate, 10–30 cm, with 20–50 pairs of small narrow leaflets 1–2 cm; fold at nightAlternately arranged, trifoliate (3 leaflets); leaflets are oblong-lanceolate (5–10 cm long, 2–4 cm wide), velvety green above, pale silvery-green with fine hairs below
FlowersPea-like, yellow, 1–1.5 cm, speckled or streaked purple-brown on the back, 2–6 on a drooping stalkLarge (1.5–2 cm long), pea-like (papilionaceous), bright yellow or yellow with reddish-purple stripes on the back of the largest petal (standard)
PodsVery slender, 15–25 cm × 3 mm, straight or slightly curved, beaked, with 25–40 seeds—
RootsNodulated; in flooded soil also nodules on the lower stem—
Form—Tall, woody, branched leguminous shrub (1.5–3 m tall) with a deep taproot and ribbed, hairy green stems
Fruit—Linear-oblong, flat, hairy pod (5–9 cm long) marked with dark oblique lines or mottling between the seeds; ending in a sharp beak
Seeds—Round to oval (4–6 mm diameter), slightly compressed, light brown, tan, or cream-colored, with a small white scar (hilum)
