Gram Blue or Plains Cupid? ग्राम ब्लू तितली या प्लेन्स क्यूपिड तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plains Cupid, as the Gram Blue profile puts it

Plains Cupid]] (Luthrodes pandava)

Has prominent red spot on hindwing underside; slightly larger

Gram Blue, as the Plains Cupid profile puts it

Gram Blue]] (Euchrysops cnejus)

Smaller; black spots on hindwing underside without red; different wing pattern

Gram Blue: A small bright blue butterfly (males) or brownish (females), with small black spots on pale underside but no prominent red spots, hovering over gram and pulse crops in winter, is the Gram Blue.

Plains Cupid: A small blue butterfly with distinctive red spot on hindwing underside; males dark blue, females brownish; weak flight in grassland and gram fields.
