How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striated Grassbird, as the Yellow-eyed Babbler profile puts it

Striated Grassbird]] (Megalurus palustris)

Larger, heavily streaked above, dark eye, pointed tail; sings in a song-flight over reeds

Striated Grassbird: A dark, heavily streaked warbler of tall wet grasses and reed beds. Smaller and darker than Ashy Prinia. Secretive; identify by song—a distinctive rambling warble. Look in dense vegetation along water bodies.

Yellow-eyed Babbler: A long-tailed brown-and-white bird clambering in tall grass, with a yellow eye in an orange-red ring and a stout black bill, is the yellow-eyed babbler. Prinias are smaller and slighter with thin bills; grassbirds are streaked; jungle babblers are big, dull and on the ground.
