House Mouse or Little Indian Field Mouse? चुहिया या खेत की चुहिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Mouse, as the Little Indian Field Mouse profile puts it

Larger; smoother, softer fur; not spiny; longer tail relative to body

घरेलू चुहिया, बाल नरम

House Mouse: A delicate, tiny mouse with large ears, small eyes, and a thin tail found in kitchens and grain stores is a House Mouse.

Little Indian Field Mouse: The smallest mouse in eastern UP fields, with distinctly spiny coarse fur, small size (fist-sized when held), and active in daylight, is Mus booduga. Any larger mouse is a different species.
