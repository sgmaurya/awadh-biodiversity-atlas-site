बहुत बड़ा सिर, चाँदी, नहीं चिपचिपा

Common Carp: A golden-scaled, heavy, bottom-feeding fish with a downward mouth and long barbels, clouding the water as it digs, is the common carp; rohu and catla have terminal mouths and no barbels.
