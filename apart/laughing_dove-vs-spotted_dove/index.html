Laughing Dove or Spotted Dove? छोटी पंडुक या चितरोखा फाख्ता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटी पंडुकLaughing DoveSpilopelia senegalensis
चितरोखा फाख्ताSpotted DoveSpilopelia chinensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Dove, as the Laughing Dove profile puts it

Slightly larger; darker brown; neck has a broad black patch with white spots; different call

चित्तीदार पंडुक — गर्दन के पीछे सफेद चित्तीदार काला धब्बा

Laughing Dove, as the Spotted Dove profile puts it

Smaller (22 cm); no neck patch; rustier underparts; iridescent vinous patch on wing; call sounds like laughter ("do-do-do-cu-cu")

हँसती कबूतर — छोटी, लाल पंख पर दाग

Laughing Dove: Small size, warm pinkish-brown breast, distinctive rufous-and-black chequered collar on the front of the neck, and bubbling chuckling calls.

Spotted Dove: Medium dove with black-and-white spotted necklace patch on lower neck + white spots on wing coverts + rufous tone + melodious "coo-coo-cuckoo" call = Spotted Dove. The neck patch is diagnostic.
