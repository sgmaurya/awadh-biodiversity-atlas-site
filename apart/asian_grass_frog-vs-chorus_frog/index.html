Asian Grass Frog or Nilphamari Narrow-mouthed Frog? फुदकी मेंढक या छोटा टिड्डा मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Grass Frog, as the Nilphamari Narrow-mouthed Frog profile puts it

Much larger (25–40 mm), smoother skin, different breeding call

बहुत बड़ा, चिकना

Asian Grass Frog: Medium size (3–5 cm), rough warty skin with long wrinkles, pointed snout, and a light line running down the spine in many individuals.

Nilphamari Narrow-mouthed Frog: An extremely tiny (12–18 mm), plain-colored frog with a rapid "chirp-chirp-chirp" chorus is a chorus frog. Listen for the call to locate them; individual frogs are nearly impossible to spot visually.
