Bhant or Lantana? भांट या पंचफूली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भांटBhantClerodendrum infortunatum
पंचफूलीLantanaLantana camara

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lantana, as the Bhant profile puts it

Flowers are multicoloured and clustered tightly; leaves are rougher, smaller; invasive

लंटाना — रंग-बिरंगे फूल, छोटी पत्तियाँ

Bhant: A soft, spreading shrub with large, velvety leaves and white flowers in long spikes with colourful red or pink persistent calyces is bhant.

Lantana: A 1–3 m shrub with square prickled stems + aromatic crushed leaves + multicoloured flower heads in one cluster + glossy black berries = Lantana camara. The multicoloured flower head is the most reliable single field character.
