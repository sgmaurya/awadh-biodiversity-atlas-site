Grass Carp or Silver Carp? ग्रास कार्प या सिल्वर कार्प?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सिल्वर कार्पSilver CarpHypophthalmichthys molitrix

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Silver Carp, as the Grass Carp profile puts it

Smaller, upturned mouth; eyes on ventral surface

छोटी, मुँह ऊपर

Grass Carp, as the Silver Carp profile puts it

Darker colour; larger scales; terminal mouth adapted for grazing plants; no filter-feeding

गहरे रंग की, पत्तियाँ खाती है

Grass Carp: A large green cyprinid with a terminal mouth, no barbels, and eating water plants is grass carp. The absence of barbels (present in common carp) and the large size distinguish it from other carps in eastern UP ponds.

Silver Carp: A large, silvery, streamlined carp with small head, subterminal mouth, low-set eye, extreme sheen, and filter-feeding behaviour in ponds or aquaculture tanks = silver carp.
