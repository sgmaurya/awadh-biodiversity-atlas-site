Common Chickweed or Madras Carpet? कॉमन चिकवीड या मुस्तरु?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मुस्तरुMadras CarpetGrangea maderaspatana

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Chickweed, as the Madras Carpet profile puts it

Leaves opposite but broader; white flowers; more hairy

बहुत चौड़ी पत्तियाँ, सफेद फूल

Common Chickweed: Look for the single line of hairs along the succulent green stem and the five white petals each split so deeply that they appear as ten.

Madras Carpet: A tiny mat-forming winter annual with opposite miniature leaves and minute yellow discoid flower heads is Madras Carpet.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common ChickweedMadras Carpet
Plant habitWeak, flaccid, decumbent or procumbent bright green annual herb—
StemsMuch-branched, cylindrical, marked by a single distinct longitudinal line of hairs—
LeavesOpposite, ovate or cordate-acuminate; lower petioled, upper sessileOpposite, simple, spatulate to obovate, 0.3–0.8 cm, entire, hairless or slightly hairy
FlowersSmall, white, solitary or in loose terminal leafy cymesTiny, yellow, discoid flower heads, 2–3 mm across, in small clusters
PetalsFive white petals, bipartite nearly to the base, appearing as ten petals—
FruitOvoid-oblong capsule opening by six valves, releasing tiny tubercled seedsTiny achenes with minute pappus
Plant form—Prostrate, mat-forming winter annual, 5–15 cm tall, forming dense carpets
Stem—Slender, herbaceous, branching, rooting at nodes
The test—Very small plant; forms dense mat; opposite tiny leaves; yellow flower heads
