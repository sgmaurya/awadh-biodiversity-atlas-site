Plain Tiger or Tawny Coster? साधारण बाघ तितली या तौनी कोस्टर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तौनी कोस्टरTawny CosterAcraea terpsicore

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plain Tiger, as the Tawny Coster profile puts it

Much larger (wingspan ~75 mm); has white spots on black apex of forewings; flight is stronger and higher

प्लेन टाइगर — बड़ा आकार, पंखों के सिरों पर सफ़ेद धब्बे

Plain Tiger: Medium-sized tawny-orange wings with black margins containing white dots, and black forewing tips containing a diagonal white band, but lacking black vein outlines, identify Danaus chrysippus.

Tawny Coster: Medium size, translucent orange wings with black spots and a broad black margin on hindwings, and very slow low flight close to the grass identify the Tawny Coster.
