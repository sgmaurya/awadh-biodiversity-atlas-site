How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Orange Tip, as the White Orange-tip profile puts it

Great Orange Tip]])

Distinctly larger; orange apical patch lacks a complete inner black border

Great Orange Tip: A large white butterfly with a prominent bright orange-red apex patch on the forewing, flying with strong, swift flight, is the Great Orange Tip.

White Orange-tip: A medium-sized chalk-white pierid butterfly with a brilliant fiery orange patch enclosed in a broad black apical area on the forewing, flying around thorny caper scrub, is the White Orange-tip.
