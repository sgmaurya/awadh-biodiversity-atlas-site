Golden Jackal or Indian Wolf? सियार या भेड़िया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Wolf, as the Golden Jackal profile puts it

Much larger (20–25 kg); longer legs; grey colouring; found in open grasslands; rare in Basti

Golden Jackal, as the Indian Wolf profile puts it

Golden Jackal]] (Canis aureus)

Smaller (7–12 kg vs 30–40 kg); tawny-golden coat; smaller head; smaller teeth; more common around villages

Indian Wolf: Large canid (2–3 times heavier than a medium dog), grey-brown or buff coat, large head, yellow eyes, bushy dark-tipped tail, nocturnal solitary or paired movement in open grassland, with large tracks (~6–7 cm) in a near-straight line = Indian Wolf.
