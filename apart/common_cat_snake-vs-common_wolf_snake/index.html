How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Cat Snake, as the Common Wolf Snake profile puts it

Common Cat Snake]])

Non-venomous; light brown; has a wavy white/dark zig-zag band along the back; head has a dark Y-shaped mark; long slender tail.

Common Cat Snake: A slender, zig-zag banded snake with large eyes and vertical pupils, found in trees or buildings at night, is a Common Cat Snake. Diurnal snakes or water-dwelling snakes are different species.
