Doob Grass or Kusha Grass? दूब या कुश?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दूबDoob GrassCynodon dactylon
कुशKusha GrassDesmostachya bipinnata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Doob Grass, as the Kusha Grass profile puts it

Low, creeping, soft lawn grass with finger-like seed heads

ज़मीन पर फैलने वाली नरम घास

Doob Grass: Low-creeping mat + narrow blue-green leaves + 3–7 finger-like spikes in a whorl + distinctive fresh smell when crushed = Cynodon dactylon.

Kusha Grass: A stiff, sharp-leaved tussock with a dense upright straw-coloured brush of flattened spikelets, on usar or a sandy bank, is kusha.

Side by side आमने-सामने
