How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Hawk-Cuckoo, as the Banded Bay Cuckoo profile puts it

Common Hawk-Cuckoo]] (Hierococcyx varius)

Much larger, hawk-like posture, unbarred ashy-grey upperparts, rufous-washed breast with dark streaks (Baker 1927, p. 148)

Banded Bay Cuckoo: The brown and rufous barred upperparts, finely cross-barred whitish underparts, and bright red outer eye ring identify the Banded Bay Cuckoo (Baker 1927, pp. 157–158).

Common Hawk-Cuckoo: Medium grey and white cuckoo + loud two-syllable "piu-piu" or "brain-fever" call rising in pitch + spring arrival + fruit-eating = Common Hawk-Cuckoo. The call is unmistakable and diagnostic.

Side by side आमने-सामने

HabitSlender, secretive cuckoo perching in dense forest or heavily-wooded country (Baker 1927, pp. 158–159)—
UpperpartsRemainder of upper plumage barred brown and rufous; white specks on forehead (Baker 1927, p. 157)—
UnderpartsSides of head and neck, lower plumage, axillaries and under wing-coverts white or buffy-white with very fine wavy bars of brown (Baker 1927, pp. 157–158)—
Tail PatternMiddle tail-feathers rufous with black centres running in incomplete bars; tail tipped white and subtipped black (Baker 1927, p. 157)—
Bare PartsIris brown or slaty-brown with a bright red outer circle; bill greenish-yellow, culmen dark brown to black (Baker 1927, p. 158)—
Size—Medium cuckoo (30–33 cm total length); smaller and slimmer than [[pied_cuckoo\
Plumage—Grey upperparts; white or buff underparts with grey breast-band and barring on flanks
Head—Greyish-brown; slight crest
Back and wings—Dark grey or greyish-brown; flight feathers darker
Tail—Longish, graduated, grey with white tips to outer feathers
Eyes and face—Yellow orbital ring; dark eye; pale lores
Bill—Pale greenish-yellow, medium length, slightly downcurved
Distinctive call—Loud, sharp, two-syllable "piu-piu" or "piu-WHIT" repeated in succession, rising in pitch — famous as "Brain-fever, brain-fever"
