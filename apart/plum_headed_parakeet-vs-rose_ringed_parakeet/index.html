Plum-headed Parakeet or Rose-ringed Parakeet? टुइयाँ तोता या तोता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rose-ringed Parakeet, as the Plum-headed Parakeet profile puts it

Rose-ringed Parakeet]] (Psittacula krameri)

Larger (40 cm); male has pink and black neck ring; female lacks neck markings entirely

Plum-headed Parakeet, as the Rose-ringed Parakeet profile puts it

Male: red-purple head; female: grey head; smaller (36 cm); not commonly found in open farmland

आलू सिर तोता

Plum-headed Parakeet: A small green parakeet with a distinctly blue-headed male and yellow-headed female, in noisy flocks, is a Plum-headed Parakeet. Larger parakeets with neck rings or red heads are different species.

Rose-ringed Parakeet: Large all-green parrot + long pointed tail + loud screeching flight + eastern UP village = Rose-ringed Parakeet. The only parakeet of the region. Male's rose ring visible at close range; the pointed tail silhouette identifies the species even in fast overhead flight.
