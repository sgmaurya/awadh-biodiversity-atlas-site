Bar-headed Goose or Greylag Goose? राजहंस या कलहंस?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कलहंसGreylag GooseAnser anser

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greylag Goose, as the Bar-headed Goose profile puts it

Larger; uniform brown-grey plumage; no head bars

बड़ा, भूरा, कोई पट्टी नहीं

Bar-headed Goose, as the Greylag Goose profile puts it

Smaller; paler head and neck; two bold black bars on crown and nape

छोटा, सफेद सर, काली पट्टियाँ

Bar-headed Goose: A large pale goose with a striking white head marked by two bold black bars, at rivers in winter — is a Bar-headed Goose. The head pattern is diagnostic and unmistakable.

Greylag Goose: A large, overall grey-brown goose with an orange bill and orange legs, flying with loud honking calls — is a Greylag Goose. Bar-headed Goose is paler with bold black head bars.
