Rohu or Tor Mahseer? रोहू या तोर महासीर मछली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Tor Mahseer profile puts it

Mouth inferior with distinctly fringed lips; scales much smaller (40–44 in lateral line); lacks large bony dorsal spine (Daniels 2002)

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.

Tor Mahseer: A massive, deep-bodied golden-green carp with head length shorter than body depth, fleshy lips with a continuous labial lobe, four barbels, only 22–27 very large lateral line scales, and reddish fins, is Tor tor.
