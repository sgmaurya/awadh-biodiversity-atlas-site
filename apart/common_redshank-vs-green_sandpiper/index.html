Common Redshank or Green Sandpiper? कॉमन रेडशैंक या ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Sandpiper, as the Common Redshank profile puts it

Smaller and stockier; dark grey above, white below; dark legs

ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर

Common Redshank, as the Green Sandpiper profile puts it

Larger; bright red legs and bill base; paler plumage; white rump

रेडशैंक

Common Redshank: A medium wader with unmistakable bright red legs and reddish-orange bill, with white rump visible in flight, probing in mud — is a Common Redshank. The red legs are diagnostic. Greenshanks are paler and larger with greenish legs.

Green Sandpiper: A small dark-backed wader with white belly and dark underwings visible in flight, at small water bodies — is a Green Sandpiper. The dark back and dark underwing are diagnostic. Solitary or in pairs.
