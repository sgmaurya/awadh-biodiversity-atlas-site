How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dusky Eagle-Owl, as the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Dusky Eagle-Owl]] (Ketupa coromanda)

Smaller build, greyish plumage with fine vertical shaft streaks, and lacks broad dark spots on the abdomen

Dusky Eagle-Owl: The uniform sooty-grey vermiculated plumage, erect dark ear tufts, bright lemon-yellow eyes, feathered tarsi, and preference for wooded water margins distinguish the Dusky Eagle-Owl.

Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl: The immense size, dark brown eyes, long slanting ear-tufts, and fulvous underparts barred on the breast, the bars becoming broad spots on the abdomen (Baker 1927, p. 418), distinguish the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl.
