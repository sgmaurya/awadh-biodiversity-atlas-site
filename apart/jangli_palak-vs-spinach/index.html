Toothed Dock or Spinach? जंगली पालक या पालक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spinach, as the Toothed Dock profile puts it

Cultivated plant; smoother leaves; often larger; lacks prominent teeth on margins

पालक (पोषक) — पालतू पौधा, बड़ी पत्तियाँ

Toothed Dock: A winter herb with a basal rosette of large, prominently toothed, bright green leaves on reddish or purple stems; tiny greenish flowers in loose panicles; and small, winged, triangular fruits is jangli palak.

Spinach: Spinach (Spinacia oleracea) is recognized by its triangular-hastate leaf bases and dioecious, spiny or rounded axillary fruit clusters, whereas palak beet has thick ovate leaves and bisexual flowers.
