Brahminy Starling or Rosy Starling? ब्राह्मणी मैना या गुलाबी मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्राह्मणी मैनाBrahminy StarlingSturnia pagodarum
गुलाबी मैनाRosy StarlingPastor roseus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rosy Starling, as the Brahminy Starling profile puts it

Much larger; rose-pink body; glossy black head, wings, and tail; winter migrant in large flocks

गुलाबी मैना — गुलाबी शरीर, बड़े झुंड, सर्दियों का प्रवासी

Brahminy Starling, as the Rosy Starling profile puts it

Much smaller (19–21 cm); pale grey back; chestnut breast (lacks rose-pink); yellow-and-blue bill

ब्राह्मणी मैना — छोटा आकार, धूसर पीठ, कत्थई छाती, कलगीदार सिर

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.

Rosy Starling: Medium-sized starling with a pastel rose-pink body, contrasting glossy black head, wings, and tail, flying in large, noisy, synchronized flocks in winter, is a Rosy Starling.

BillYellow with a conspicuous greenish-blue base—
FlightSwift, direct flight with rapid wingbeats; wings show no white patches (unlike Common Myna)—
TailDark brown tail with broad white tips on the outer tail feathers, visible in flight—
Flocks—Extremely gregarious; always observed in large, active, noisy flocks of hundreds or thousands
Murmurations—Flocks fly in dense, synchronized, cloud-like formations before settling to roost
Bare Parts—Bill is pinkish-yellow with a dark tip in winter; legs and feet are pinkish-brown
