Chestnut-capped Babbler or Striated Babbler? चेस्टनट-कैप्ड बैबलर या स्ट्रिएटेड बैबलर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striated Babbler, as the Chestnut-capped Babbler profile puts it

Argya earlei

Much larger in body size, heavily streaked greyish-brown all over, with a long graduated tail and pale eyes

Chestnut-capped Babbler: Grassland babbler with bright chestnut-red crown cap, white forehead and supercilium, stout black bill, and black-streaked white throat.

Striated Babbler: A small, streaky brown babbler in noisy flocks with characteristic "chak-chak-chak" calls, moving through grassland and scrub, is a Striated Babbler. Other babblers have different plumage patterns or sizes.
