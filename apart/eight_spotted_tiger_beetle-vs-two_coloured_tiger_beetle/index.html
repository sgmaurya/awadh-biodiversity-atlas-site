Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle or Two-coloured Tiger Beetle? आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग या दो-रंगा बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंगEight-spotted Tiger BeetleCicindela octonotata
दो-रंगा बाघ भृंगTwo-coloured Tiger BeetleCicindela bicolor

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Two-coloured Tiger Beetle, as the Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Elytra uniformly metallic blue or green without pale spots; head/thorax coppery

पंखों पर कोई धब्बा नहीं होता, सिर और सीना तांबई

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Two-coloured Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Elytra marked with eight bold yellowish spots; occurs on river sand, not hard paths

पंखों पर आठ पीले धब्बे, पगडंडी की जगह नदी की रेत पर मिलना

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle: A large metallic coppery-blue tiger beetle on river sand with four bold creamy-yellow spots on each elytron (eight in total) identifies Cicindela octonotata.

Two-coloured Tiger Beetle: A tiger beetle on firm dirt paths with a bright coppery-red head and thorax contrasting against solid unspotted deep blue or green elytra identifies Cicindela bicolor.
