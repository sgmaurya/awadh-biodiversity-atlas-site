Cattle Egret or Little Egret? गाय बगुला या छोटा बगुला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गाय बगुलाCattle EgretBubulcus ibis
छोटा बगुलाLittle EgretEgretta garzetta

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Egret, as the Cattle Egret profile puts it

Slender build; black bill; black legs with bright yellow feet; feeds strictly in shallow water

छोटा बगुला — पतली गर्दन, काली चोंच और काले पैर-पीले पंजे

Cattle Egret, as the Little Egret profile puts it

Stockier; shorter yellow bill; yellow-green legs; breeding plumage has orange-buff patches; feeds on dry land

गाय बगुला — पीली चोंच, गठीली गर्दन और खेतों में चरना

Cattle Egret: Small size, stocky neck, yellow bill (orange in breeding), yellowish-green legs, and terrestrial foraging behavior alongside grazing livestock.

Little Egret: Slender white body, long black bill, black legs with bright yellow feet, and active wading foraging in shallow water identify the Little Egret.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Cattle EgretLittle Egret
SizeSmall, stocky egret (48–53 cm total length); neck relatively short and thickSmall, slender white egret (55–65 cm total length); neck long and thin
ColourationEntirely white in non-breeding plumage; yellow bill and yellowish-green legsEntirely pure white plumage throughout the year
Breeding PlumageDistinct orange-buff (golden) feathers on crown, breast, and back in monsoon—
BillRelatively short, stout, and yellow (turns orange-pink during peak breeding)Long, slender, straight, and completely black bill
LegsYellowish-grey or olive-green; turn bright pink-red for a few days during breeding—
BehaviourCommensal association with grazing cattle; runs alongside cows to catch flushed prey—
Legs & Feet—Black legs contrasting sharply with bright yellow toes ("yellow slippers")
Breeding Plumes—Two long, narrow plumes on the nape; fine lacy plumes (aigrettes) on the back
Foraging behaviour—Wades actively in shallow water; often shakes its feet to stir up mud and flush prey
