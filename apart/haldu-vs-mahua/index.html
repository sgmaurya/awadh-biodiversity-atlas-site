Haldu or Mahua? हल्दू या महुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हल्दूHalduHaldina cordifolia
महुआMahuaMadhuca longifolia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mahua, as the Haldu profile puts it

Leaflets smaller; flowers fragrant, white/cream; fruit a large drupe

महुआ — फूल सफ़ेद

Haldu: A tall tree with large, cordate-leafed pinnate leaves, pink or white fragrant flowers, and long flat woody capsules, is haldu.

Side by side आमने-सामने

HalduMahua
SizeDeciduous tree 12–25 m tall, with a straight trunk and spreading crown—
LeavesPinnate, 30–60 cm, with 4–8 pairs of opposite leaflets, each 10–18 cm, cordate at baseClustered at branch tips, oblong-elliptic, 15–30 cm, leathery, glabrous
FlowersPink or white, tubular, in terminal panicles, April–MayCream/yellowish, fleshy, tubular, nocturnal, strongly fragrant; fall intact to ground
FruitLong, flat, woody capsule (siliqua), 30–60 cm, brown when dryFleshy berry, 3–5 cm, green → yellow; 1–4 large seeds
BarkGrey-brown, fissuredDark grey-brown, longitudinally fissured, rough
WoodYellow to golden, hard, odorous—
The testFresh bark or wood has a distinct, pleasant aromatic smell—
Tree form—Medium–large (10–20 m), dense spreading crown
Season clue—March–April nights: strong sweet scent; fallen cream flowers cover ground by dawn

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
