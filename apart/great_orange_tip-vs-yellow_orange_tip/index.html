Great Orange Tip or Yellow Orange Tip? ग्रेट ऑरेंज टिप तितली या येलो ऑरेंज टिप तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ग्रेट ऑरेंज टिप तितलीGreat Orange TipHebomoia glaucippe

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow Orange Tip, as the Great Orange Tip profile puts it

Yellow Orange Tip]] (Ixias pyrene)

Much smaller (45–55 mm); more yellow-orange than deep orange; weaker flight

Great Orange Tip, as the Yellow Orange Tip profile puts it

Great Orange Tip]] (Hebomoia glaucippe)

Much larger; more orange coloration on wings; deeper orange apex

Great Orange Tip: A large white butterfly with a prominent bright orange-red apex patch on the forewing, flying with strong, swift flight, is the Great Orange Tip.

Yellow Orange Tip: A small white butterfly with a bright yellow-orange apex on the forewing, flying over gardens and fields, is the Yellow Orange Tip.
