मैदा मॉथ — और छोटी

Convolvulus Hawkmoth: A medium-sized grey-brown hawkmoth with white cross-bands on the wings, hovering at morning glory or related flowers like a hummingbird, with a long coiled proboscis, is Agrius convolvuli.

Oleander Hawkmoth: A large, green or pink-tinged hawkmoth with a long coiled proboscis, hovering at flowers like a hummingbird, and found on oleander, is Daphnis nerii.
