How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Vetch, as the Yellow Vetchling profile puts it

Common vetch]] (Vicia sativa)

True leaflets present (not just stipules); purple or reddish flowers; different pod structure

Common Vetch: A trailing or climbing winter legume with feathery pinnate leaves ending in a tendril, small purple-pink papilionaceous flowers, and linear pods containing round seeds is ankri.

Yellow Vetchling: A slender climbing annual with no true leaflets (only large stipules and tendrils), yellow papilionaceous flowers, and small pods in pulse fields is Yellow Vetchling.
