House Fly or Oriental Fruit Fly? घरेलू मक्खी या फल मक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Fly, as the Oriental Fruit Fly profile puts it

Larger (6–7 mm), grey-black body, lacks the distinctive wing pattern and behaviour

साधारण घर की मक्खी, काली, फलों में नहीं देखी जाती

House Fly: Uniform grey-brown, small (6–7 mm), four dark thoracic stripes, found indoors and around food: House Fly.

Oriental Fruit Fly: Small reddish-brown fly with dark S-shaped markings on wings, found around ripening or fermenting mango and guava, with larvae tunnelling inside the fruit and causing internal rot.

Side by side आमने-सामने

House FlyOriental Fruit Fly

WingsHyaline, held tent-like over the body at rest—
LegsDark; moderately long and hairy—
MouthpartsSponging type (siphon-like); used to dissolve solids and drink liquids—
Adult size—6–8 mm, compact with reddish-brown wings
Wing pattern—Characteristic dark S-shaped or banded pattern on transparent wings
Behaviour—Attracted to ripening and fermenting fruits; rests on fruit surface
Larval sign—Brown, crescent-shaped eggs inserted under fruit skin; rotting flesh inside
Damage symptom—Punctures and discoloured spots on fruit; internal rot and fermentation
