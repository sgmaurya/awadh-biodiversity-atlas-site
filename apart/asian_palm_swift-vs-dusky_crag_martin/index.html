How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dusky Crag Martin, as the Asian Palm Swift profile puts it

Sooty-brown body; short tail with white spots; nests on rocky cliffs or stone buildings

चट्टानी अबाबील — सुस्त भूरा, पूँछ पर सफ़ेद धब्बे

Asian Palm Swift: Small size, uniform pale brown colouration, deeply forked tail, and constant association with palm trees identify the Asian Palm Swift.

Dusky Crag Martin: A small, uniform dusky-brown swallow without any white or contrasting patches, found in flight over rocky areas and cliffs, is a Dusky Crag Martin. The lack of contrasting plumage, small size, and cliff habitat are diagnostic.
