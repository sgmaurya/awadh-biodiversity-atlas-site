Asian Pied Starling or Common Myna? गुलाम मैना या मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गुलाम मैनाAsian Pied StarlingGracupica contra
मैनाCommon MynaAcridotheres tristis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Myna, as the Asian Pied Starling profile puts it

Brown, not black; yellow facial patch, not orange orbital ring; white wing patch only in flight

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.
