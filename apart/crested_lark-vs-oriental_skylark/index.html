How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Lark, as the Oriental Skylark profile puts it

Crested Lark]] (Galerida cristata)

Larger; much more prominent crest; different call

Crested Lark: Crested Lark is readily identified by its prominent, pointed, erectile crest and buff-brown coloration. Walking on open ground with upright posture and raised crest is diagnostic.

Oriental Skylark: The Oriental Skylark is a small, brown, streaked lark of fields and open ground. Its small size, short bill, and bouncy flight are distinctive. If the bird is larger with a more prominent crest, it is a Crested Lark.
