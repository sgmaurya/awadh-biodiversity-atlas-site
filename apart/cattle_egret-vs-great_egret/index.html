Cattle Egret or Great Egret? गाय बगुला या बड़ा बगुला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बड़ा बगुलाGreat EgretArdea alba

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Egret, as the Cattle Egret profile puts it

Great Egret]] (Ardea alba)

Much larger (90 cm); extremely long S-shaped neck; black legs; yellow bill

Cattle Egret, as the Great Egret profile puts it

Smaller (50–66 cm); buffier neck; usually in groups on cattle; yellow bill

छोटा, गायों के साथ

Cattle Egret: Small size, stocky neck, yellow bill (orange in breeding), yellowish-green legs, and terrestrial foraging behavior alongside grazing livestock.

Great Egret: A large, pure-white heron standing alone in shallow water with a long yellow bill and black legs — is a Great Egret. The size and solitary hunting behaviour distinguish it from smaller egrets.
