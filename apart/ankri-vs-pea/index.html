Common Vetch or Pea? अंकरी या मटर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अंकरीCommon VetchVicia sativa
मटरPeaPisum sativum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pea, as the Common Vetch profile puts it

Larger leaflets (5–10 cm); larger flowers (2.5–4 cm); more robust

मटर — बड़ी पत्तियाँ, बड़े फूल

Common Vetch: A trailing or climbing winter legume with feathery pinnate leaves ending in a tendril, small purple-pink papilionaceous flowers, and linear pods containing round seeds is ankri.

Pea: A cool-season legume with compound pinnate leaves ending in tendrils, white/pink/purple papilionaceous flowers, and linear pods containing round green peas is Pisum sativum.
