How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Long-legged Marsh Skimmer, as the Coral-tailed Cloud Wing profile puts it

Purple-blue body (not dark); clear wings (not clouded); long legs

लंबी पिंडली व्याधपतंग

Coral-tailed Cloud Wing: Small to medium dragonfly with characteristically clouded (smoky) wings and dark body with bright coral-red abdomen tip (in males) distinguishes this species.

Long-legged Marsh Skimmer: Small to medium dragonfly with conspicuously long legs (diagnostic) and purple-blue or reddish body colour distinguishes this marsh skimmer.
