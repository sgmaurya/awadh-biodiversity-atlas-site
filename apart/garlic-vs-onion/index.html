Garlic or Onion? लहसुन या प्याज़?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

प्याज़OnionAllium cepa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Garlic, as the Onion profile puts it

Segmented bulb (cloves); narrower, flattened leaves; stronger smell; harvested same season as onion

लहसुन — कलियों में विभाजित

Garlic: Garlic (Allium sativum) is distinguished by its flat, solid, keeled leaves and compound bulb of multiple cloves, whereas onion has hollow, fistular leaves and a single tunicated bulb.

Onion: A medium-height plant with long, hollow, linear leaves, with a round, papery-skinned bulb swelling above the soil surface is onion (Allium cepa).
