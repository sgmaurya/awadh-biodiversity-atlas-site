How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-rumped Swallow, as the Dusky Crag Martin profile puts it

Red-rumped Swallow]] (Cecropis daurica) — partial migrant

Larger; red rump and cap; pale underparts; different habitat

Dusky Crag Martin, as the Red-rumped Swallow profile puts it

Dusky Crag Martin]] (Ptyonoprogne concolor)

Larger, dusky brown; no rufous on rump; different behaviour; cliff-dwelling

Dusky Crag Martin: A small, uniform dusky-brown swallow without any white or contrasting patches, found in flight over rocky areas and cliffs, is a Dusky Crag Martin. The lack of contrasting plumage, small size, and cliff habitat are diagnostic.

Red-rumped Swallow: Red-rumped swallow shows a distinctive rufous rump and rusty throat. If the rump is dark and back is glossy dark blue, it is a barn swallow. If the tail has extremely long filaments beyond the fork, it may be a wire-tailed swallow.
