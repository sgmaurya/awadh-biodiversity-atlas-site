How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-headed Gull, as the Pallas's Gull profile puts it

Black-headed Gull]] (Ichthyaetus ichthyaetus poiocephalus)

[Same species, different subspecies — not applicable]

Black-headed Gull: Black-headed Gull in winter has white head with dark ear-patch, red-orange bill, medium grey back, and red-orange legs. The medium size, ear-patch pattern, and red bill and legs are diagnostic. Compare carefully with Brown-headed Gull which has more extensive head coloring.

Pallas's Gull: A large white gull with black cap on head, dark slate-grey back, and powerful pale bill is Pallas's Gull. The size and black cap are diagnostic compared to other gulls.
