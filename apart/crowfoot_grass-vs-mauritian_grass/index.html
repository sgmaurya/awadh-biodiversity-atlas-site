How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crowfoot Grass, as the Mauritian Grass profile puts it

Finger-like inflorescence; more robust plant

क्रो फुट — उँगली जैसी शाखाएँ

Crowfoot Grass: A low spreading grass with 4–8 strongly curved, claw-like spikes, found on damp, alkaline or salt-affected soil = Dactyloctenium aegyptium (crowfoot grass).

Mauritian Grass: A medium-height tufted grass with soft leaves and a loose panicle of distinctive black-fringed spikelets is Apluda mutica.
