How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Tailorbird, as the Ashy Prinia profile puts it

Common Tailorbird]] (Orthotomus sutorius, BRD-0015)

Bright rufous crown; greener back; stitched-leaf nest; louder tuwit-tuwit call

Ashy Prinia, as the Common Tailorbird profile puts it

Grey-brown above, no rufous crown; tail waved rather than cocked

Size11–13 cm total length — smaller than a [[house_sparrow\Tiny, active bird (10–13 cm total length); weight 6–10 g; smaller than a sparrow
UpperpartsAshy grey-brown in winter; more rufous-brown in breeding season—
UnderpartsPale buffy-white; washed pale rufous on flanksClean white to pale cream, washed with buff on the flanks
TailLong, frequently cocked upward — most diagnostic feature; proportionally shorter in breeding seasonFrequently cocked upright; breeding males have two elongated central tail feathers
BillShort, fine, slightly downcurved—
CallLoud repeated tseet-tseet-tseet or buzzy churring — heard far more often than bird is seen—
BehaviourSkulks in dense bushes; drops deeper into vegetation when approached; never stays stillCreeps actively through dense low bushes; constructs stitched-leaf nests
Crown—Bright rufous-chestnut crown cap, contrasting with olive-green upperparts
Bill & Legs—Long, slender, slightly decurved pale bill; pinkish-flesh legs
Voice—Loud, persistent, repetitive tuwit-tuwit-tuwit calls, heard year-round
