How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo, as the Pin-striped Tit-babbler profile puts it

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo]] (Dicrurus paradiseus)

Large [[black_drongo\

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo: The prominent forward-curving forehead crest and twin long bare tail wires tipped with twisted flat rackets distinguish the Greater Racket-tailed Drongo from all other drongo species.

Pin-striped Tit-babbler: The diminutive size, rufous crown, and bright pale-yellow breast adorned with delicate black pin-stripes distinguish the Pin-striped Tit-babbler from all other undergrowth species.
