Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth or Oleander Hawkmoth? छोटा डेथ्स हेड बाक पतंगा या कनेर का पतंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oleander Hawkmoth, as the Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth profile puts it

Forewings boldly patterned with green and pink swirls; no skull mark

पंखों पर हरा और गुलाबी घुमावदार रंग, खोपड़ी का निशान नहीं

Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth: A large dark hawkmoth with an ochraceous skull marking on the thorax, yellow hindwings without a black basal patch, and a squeaking defense call identifies Acherontia styx.

Oleander Hawkmoth: A large, green or pink-tinged hawkmoth with a long coiled proboscis, hovering at flowers like a hummingbird, and found on oleander, is Daphnis nerii.

Size—Large moth, wingspan 80-110 mm; one of the largest moths in the region
Colour—Predominantly soft green or grey-green with pink or reddish tones on the wings and thorax
Wings—Elongate and pointed; forewings with complex patterns of green, grey and pink
Body—Stout, muscular, greenish; thorax large and powerful
Proboscis—Long, thin, coiled under the head; unrolls to feed from flowers
Flight—Swift, hovering like a hummingbird; flies in daylight and at dusk
