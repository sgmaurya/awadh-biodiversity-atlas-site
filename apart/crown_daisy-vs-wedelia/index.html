Crown Daisy or Wedelia? गुलदाउदी या वेडेलिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गुलदाउदीCrown DaisyGlebionis coronaria
वेडेलियाWedeliaSphagneticola trilobata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crown Daisy, as the Wedelia profile puts it

Erect winter annual; leaves deeply 2-pinnatisect; heads larger, white and yellow

सीधा खड़ा पौधा, बारीक कटी पत्तियाँ

Crown Daisy: Deeply twice-cut feathery leaves paired with large solitary daisy flower heads of bright yellow or bicoloured white-and-yellow identifies Glebionis coronaria.

Wedelia: Spreading creeping groundcover with succulent three-lobed leaves rooting at nodes, bearing bright golden-yellow daisy heads on erect stalks identifies Sphagneticola trilobata.
