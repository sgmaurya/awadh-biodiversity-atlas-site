Barred Wolf Snake or Common Krait? बार्ड वुल्फ़ स्नेक या करैत?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बार्ड वुल्फ़ स्नेकBarred Wolf SnakeLycodon striatus
करैतCommon KraitBungarus caeruleus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Krait, as the Barred Wolf Snake profile puts it

Extremely venomous. Bands start mid-body (not on neck); large hexagonal vertebral scales; rounded head

करैत — अत्यधिक विषैला

Barred Wolf Snake, as the Common Krait profile puts it

Non-venomous; smaller; white bands are more speckled and contain black dots; lacks hexagonal vertebral scales

चित्तीदार भेड़िया सांप — हानिरहित

Barred Wolf Snake: Any banded snake with a grid-like or barred pattern (white bands with black spots inside) found in a house is a harmless Barred Wolf Snake. If the bands are solid white without spots inside, and the head is solid black, it is a Common Krait and is dangerous.

Common Krait: Highly polished steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody, combined with enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales along the backbone, identifies Bungarus caeruleus.
