How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-headed Fish-Eagle, as the Pallas's Fish Eagle profile puts it

Grey-headed Fish Eagle]] (Icthyophaga ichthyaetus)

Grey head, white belly, and tail white with a broad terminal blackish band

Grey-headed Fish-Eagle: Massive river eagle with pale grey head, dark brown breast, gleaming white belly, and white tail tipped with broad black band.

Pallas's Fish Eagle: The immense size, pale creamy-buff hood contrasting with dark rufous body, and broad white band across the middle of the dark tail distinguish Pallas's Fish Eagle.
