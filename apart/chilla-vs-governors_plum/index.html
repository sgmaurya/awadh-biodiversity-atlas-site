How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Governor's Plum, as the Chilla profile puts it

Flacourtia indica]]

Thorny trunk and branches with rounded fleshy edible purple-red berries

Chilla: Gland-dotted tomentose leaves and three-valved yellow capsules splitting to expose bright scarlet arillate seeds distinguish Casearia tomentosa from thorny scrub shrubs.

Governor's Plum: Crooked thorny small tree with simple crenate leaves, branched trunk spines, small yellowish-green dioecious flowers, and dark purple plum-like berries ripening in summer indicates Flacourtia indica.
