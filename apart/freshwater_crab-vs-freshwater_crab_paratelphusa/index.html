How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jacquemont's Field Crab, as the Spiny Paddy Crab profile puts it

Paratelphusa jacquemontii]]

The other field crab profiled here; its front margin lacks the single sharp spine of Sartoriana

Spiny Paddy Crab: Smooth, polished reddish-brown back with one sharp spine at the end of each front margin, in paddy fields, ponds and canals of the plain = Sartoriana spinigera.

Jacquemont's Field Crab: Mud-brown colouration, carapace wider than long, five pairs of legs with strong claws, and deep burrows in agricultural clay bunds identify Jacquemont's Field Crab.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Spiny Paddy CrabJacquemont's Field Crab
SizeA small to medium field crab, broader than long; no measured series was found for this profile, so no figure is given—
CarapaceBroad, robust and deep; the top smooth, gently convex, almost polished, with an H-shaped groove behind the eyes (Bangladesh fauna account)Broad, slightly convex shield, wider than long (40–55 mm width); smooth surface
SpineOne sharp epibranchial spine at the end of each curved front side-margin — the spinigera of the name; no other large spines—
ColourReddish-brown above, the underside light yellowish; some animals with minute brown spots (Bangladesh fauna account)—
ClawsUnequal in both sexes; the palm broad and stout, the movable finger longer than the palm—
LegsFour pairs of stout walking legs; sideways gait—
BurrowsRound or oval openings on bunds and channel banks with excavated soil beside them—
Colouration—Dark chocolate-brown, olive-brown, or dull yellowish-brown; matches muddy soil
Chelipeds—Strong claws (chelipeds), slightly unequal in size; used for crushing food and defense
Walking legs—Four pairs of slender, hairy walking legs; lack swimming paddles
Eyes—Small eyes on movable stalks, retracting into protective sockets in the carapace

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
