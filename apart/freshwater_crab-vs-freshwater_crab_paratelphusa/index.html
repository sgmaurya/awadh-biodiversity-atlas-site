Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Guerin's Freshwater Crab, as the Jacquemont's Field Crab profile puts it

Larger; carapace has a more prominent ridge (crest) behind the eyes; prefers rocky streams

केकड़ा — बड़ा, पथरीली धाराओं में पाया जाना

Jacquemont's Field Crab: Mud-brown colouration, carapace wider than long, five pairs of legs with strong claws, and deep burrows in agricultural clay bunds identify Jacquemont's Field Crab.

Guerin's Freshwater CrabJacquemont's Field Crab
SizeCarapace 3–5 cm wide — palm-sized—
CarapaceBroadly oval, flattened; olive-brown to greenish-blackBroad, slightly convex shield, wider than long (40–55 mm width); smooth surface
UndersideCream to pale orange—
Claws (chelipeds)Robust; dominant claw larger in males; mottled orange-brown—
WalkingSideways crab gait; four pairs of walking legs—
BurrowsRound entrances 2–4 cm diameter on earthen channel banks; excavated soil visible—
ActivityNocturnal and crepuscular — night torch searches most productive—
Colouration—Dark chocolate-brown, olive-brown, or dull yellowish-brown; matches muddy soil
Chelipeds—Strong claws (chelipeds), slightly unequal in size; used for crushing food and defense
Walking legs—Four pairs of slender, hairy walking legs; lack swimming paddles
Eyes—Small eyes on movable stalks, retracting into protective sockets in the carapace
