How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rattleweed, as the Phasey Bean profile puts it

Crotalaria retusa]]`

Leaves simple, oblanceolate; flowers bright yellow; pods inflated and hollow

Phasey Bean: Look for trifoliolate leaves, long erect racemes, deep maroon-red flowers, and long slender cylindrical pods that twist spirally upon splitting.

Rattleweed: Erect subshrub with simple oblanceolate leaves notched at the apex, racemes of golden-yellow pea-flowers, and hairless inflated pods rattling when ripe identifies Crotalaria retusa.

Seeds—Loose inside the dry inflated pod, rattling audibly when shaken in the wind
