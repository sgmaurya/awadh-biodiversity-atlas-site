How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Moorhen, as the Ruddy-breasted Crake profile puts it

Common Moorhen]] (Gallinula chloropus)

Much larger; black plumage; white undertail coverts; red frontal shield

Common Moorhen: Slate-black waterbird with a bright red bill and frontal shield tipped with yellow, a white line along the side, swimming with a jerky head-bobbing motion, is a Common Moorhen.

Ruddy-breasted Crake: Ruddy-breasted crake is a small crake with a distinctive rufous or rusty breast and barred black-and-white undertail coverts. The harsh, distinctive call is diagnostic.
