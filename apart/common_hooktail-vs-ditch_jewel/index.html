How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ditch Jewel, as the Common Hooktail profile puts it

Brachythemis contaminata

Common libellulid dragonfly with contiguous compound eyes and distinct orange patches on wings

Common Hooktail: Sandy-yellow and black clubtail dragonfly with widely separated eyes, perching flat on river sand, with prominent leaf-like abdominal expansions and male hooked appendages.

Ditch Jewel: Small size, bright yellow-orange body in males with a distinct orange band in the middle of each wing, and habitat preference for polluted drains identify the Ditch Jewel.

Male Colour—Bright reddish-orange body; wings have a distinct, broad orange band extending from the base
Female Colour—Pale yellow-brown body with dark markings; transparent wings with small yellow base
Eyes—Large reddish-brown eyes, meeting at the top of the head
Behaviour—Perches close to the ground or water surface on weeds and stones; active in large groups
