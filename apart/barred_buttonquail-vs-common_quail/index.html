How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Quail, as the Barred Buttonquail profile puts it

Common Quail]] (Coturnix coturnix)

Much larger (18–20 cm); male has distinct facial pattern; completely different call

Barred Buttonquail: A tiny, barred quail giving a distinctive resonant "doo-doo-doo" call from grasslands and cultivated fields is Barred Buttonquail. The call and small size are diagnostic.

Common Quail: Common Quail is a small, cryptic ground-dweller of grassland and stubble, identifiable by its small size, mottled plumage, and distinctive male call. The reluctant flight (short bursts) and ground behavior are characteristic.
