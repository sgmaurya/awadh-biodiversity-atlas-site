Taiga Flycatcher]] (Ficedula albicilla)

Male has white oval patch on back (not continuous collar); different voice

Red-breasted Flycatcher, as the Taiga Flycatcher profile puts it

Near-identical winter visitor, scarcer here; male has an orange throat and breast; uppertail coverts brown, not black; call a drier rattle

रेड-ब्रेस्टेड फ्लाईकैचर

Red-breasted Flycatcher: The only small Ficedula flycatcher with red-orange breast and belly (both sexes). Males have bold black head and white forehead and neck-patch. Hunts insects by sallying from exposed perches.

Taiga Flycatcher: Taiga Flycatcher is a pied (black-and-white) flycatcher with a white forehead and eye-ring, perching upright. If the bird is brown and speckled, it is a Spotted Flycatcher. If the throat is white but without white forehead, it is a different species.
