How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Eurasian Collared Dove, as the Oriental Turtle Dove profile puts it

Eurasian Collared Dove]] (Streptopelia decaocto)

Larger; buff coloured; broader neck collar

Eurasian Collared Dove: Sandy-grey body, neat black half-collar on the back of the neck, bright red eyes, and a three-syllable coo-COO-coo call.

Oriental Turtle Dove: The Oriental Turtle Dove is a small, grey-buff dove with a characteristic black and white striped patch on the side of the neck. Its soft colouring and mournful cooing call are distinctive.
