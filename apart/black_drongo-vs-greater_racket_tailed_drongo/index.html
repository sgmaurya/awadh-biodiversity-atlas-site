How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo, as the Black Drongo profile puts it

Has long racket-shaped tail streamers; forest species, not common open farmland bird

रैकेट-पूँछ भुजंगा

Black Drongo, as the Greater Racket-tailed Drongo profile puts it

Black Drongo]] (Dicrurus macrocercus)

Smaller size; lacks the frontal crest; tail is deeply forked but lacks elongated racket streamers; frequents open fields

Black Drongo: A glossy black open-country bird on a wire, with a deep V-shaped forked tail and repeated sallies for insects, is Black Drongo in eastern UP farmland.

Greater Racket-tailed Drongo: The prominent forward-curving forehead crest and twin long bare tail wires tipped with twisted flat rackets distinguish the Greater Racket-tailed Drongo from all other drongo species.
