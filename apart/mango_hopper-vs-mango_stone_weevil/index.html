Mango Hopper or Mango Stone Weevil? आम का फुदका या आम का गुठली घुन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mango Hopper, as the Mango Stone Weevil profile puts it

Not a weevil; damages skin and flesh, not the stone

फुदका, गुठली नहीं खाता

Mango Hopper: Tiny, pale green to yellowish-brown wedge-shaped insect on the underside of mango leaves, jumping when disturbed, causing yellowing and bleaching of the leaf, almost certainly the mango hopper.

Mango Stone Weevil: Large brown weevil, infesting the stone of developing Mango, with larval tunnels inside and emergence holes at harvest: mango stone weevil.

Snout—Prominent, slightly curved; characteristic of genus
Elytra—Coarsely punctate, with fine longitudinal ridges
Body shape—Robust, stocky, anterior end broader
Damage in fruit—Brown discolouration of the stone; interior hollowed
Emergence hole—Irregular, 2–3 mm opening in the stone at harvest
Infested fruit—May appear normal externally until advanced infestation
