Babool or Subabul? बबूल या सुबबूल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बबूलBaboolAcacia nilotica
सुबबूलSubabulLeucaena leucocephala

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Subabul, as the Babool profile puts it

Lacks thorns entirely; has white puff-puff flowers; pods are thin, flat, and not constricted; highly invasive.

सुबबूल — कांटे रहित, चपटी फलियाँ

Babool, as the Subabul profile puts it

Native. Possesses long, sharp white spines; flowers are bright yellow globes (not white); pods are thick, constricted between seeds (necklace-like).

देशी बबूल — कांटेदार शाखाएं, पीले फूल, माला जैसी फलियां

Side by side आमने-सामने

BaboolSubabul
FormSmall to medium-sized tree (5–15 m) with a dense, spreading, hemispherical or umbrella-shaped canopySmall tree or large shrub (5–10 m); trunk is slender with a loose, open, branched crown
SpinesPaired, straight, sharp stipular thorns (1–5 cm long) at leaf bases, pointing slightly backward; bright white on mature twigsEntirely spineless. The branches lack thorns (unlike native acacias)
LeavesAlternately arranged, bipinnately compound, feathery (4–10 cm long); with 2–8 pairs of pinnae, each carrying 10–25 pairs of tiny oblong leafletsBipinnate compound leaves (10–25 cm long) with 4–9 pairs of pinnae, each pinna carrying 11–17 pairs of small, pointed leaflets
FlowersBright yellow, sweet-scented, spherical flower heads (1–1.5 cm diameter) clustered in leaf axils on slender stalksGlobular, white to cream-coloured flower heads (1.5–2.5 cm diameter) that look like fuzzy powder-puffs
FruitElongated, flat, thick grey-green pod (7–15 cm long) strongly constricted between the seeds, giving a bead-like appearance; turns greyish-brown when dryFlat, straight, thin, translucent green pods turning paper-brown when ripe (10–15 cm long), hanging in dense clusters
BarkDark brown to blackish, rough, deeply cracked with vertical fissures; yields red-brown gum when injured—
Seeds—Small, oval, shiny brown, flat seeds (15–20 per pod) that rattle inside dry pods
