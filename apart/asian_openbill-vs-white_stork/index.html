Asian Openbill or White Stork? घोंघिल या हाजी लगलग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घोंघिलAsian OpenbillAnastomus oscitans
हाजी लगलगWhite StorkCiconia ciconia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Stork, as the Asian Openbill profile puts it

Pure white body; black flight feathers; bright red bill with no gap; winter visitor

सफेद सारस — लाल चोंच और चोंच में अंतर का अभाव

Asian Openbill: Medium stork size, white/grey body with black wings, dull yellowish bill with a prominent central gap in adults, and long pinkish-grey legs identify the Asian Openbill.

White Stork: A very large white stork with black wing feathers and bright red legs and bill, walking slowly in shallow water or wet grasslands — is a White Stork. Unmistakable in size and colour pattern.
