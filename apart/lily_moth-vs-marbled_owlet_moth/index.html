How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lily Moth, as the Marbled Owlet Moth profile puts it

Boldly patterned in black, yellow, and red; feeds on lilies; totally different

काला, पीला और लाल रंग, लिली के पौधों पर रहना

Lily Moth: Medium-sized dark-brown moth with white transverse bands on forewing and pale hindwings, visiting lily, hibiscus, or rose flowers at night; or solitary caterpillar on flowering plant leaves: Lily Moth (Polytela gloriosae).

Marbled Owlet Moth: A tiny owlet moth with chalky white forewings intricately marbled with olive-brown and grey, mimicking bird droppings, identifies Micracontia batisella.
