How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Robin, as the White-rumped Shama profile puts it

Indian Robin]] (Saxicoloides fulicatus)

Small, uniform brownish-black or dark brown; chestnut patch under tail base; lacks white rump and has short tail

Indian Robin: Small upright bird with characteristic tail-cocking + glossy black male with white rump (or rufous female with white rump) + clear whistled song = Indian Robin. The combination of tail-cocking behaviour and white rump is diagnostic.

White-rumped Shama: The combination of an intensely deep chestnut-rufous belly, prominent white rump patch, and extremely long graduated tail distinguishes the White-rumped Shama from all other thrushes and robins.
