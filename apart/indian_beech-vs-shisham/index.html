Indian Beech or Shisham? करंज या शीशम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

करंजIndian BeechMillettia pinnata
शीशमShishamDalbergia sissoo

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Shisham, as the Indian Beech profile puts it

Has alternate leaflets; small yellow flowers; flat, thin paper-like pods

शीशम — छोटे एकान्तर पत्ते, चपटी कागजी फलियाँ

Indian Beech: Medium semi-evergreen tree with glossy pinnate leaves (5–9 leaflets), pale pink pea-like flowers in summer, and short, flat, thick woody beaked oval pods containing a single seed, is the Indian Beech.

Shisham: Compound leaves with 3 to 5 alternating, roundish leaflets ending in a sharp point, zig-zag leaf stalks, and thin papery pods identify Dalbergia sissoo.
