How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Danaid Eggfly, as the Blue Tiger profile puts it

Black head and body; lacks the prominent white patches

अंड-चित्ती तितली — काला शरीर

Blue Tiger: A dark blue butterfly with white spots on both wings, slow fluttering flight, and presence near milkweed plants identifies the Blue Tiger.

Danaid Eggfly: Slow-flying butterfly with iridescent blue-black wings (males) or highly variable female forms; white spots visible; on flowers in grasslands.
