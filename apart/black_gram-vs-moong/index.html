Black Gram or Green Gram? उड़द या मूँग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

उड़दBlack GramVigna mungo
मूँगGreen GramVigna radiata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Gram, as the Black Gram profile puts it

Green seeds; different taste and uses; smaller plant

हरी दालें

Black Gram: A low, bushy legume with black gram-like seeds in dark brown pods is black gram.

Green Gram: A bushy, compact legume with trifoliate leaves, small pale yellow flowers, and slender, straight, bright green pods containing tiny, glossy, bright green seeds; harvested as a dry seed crop in September–October is moong.
