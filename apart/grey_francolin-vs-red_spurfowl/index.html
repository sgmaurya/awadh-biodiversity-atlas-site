How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey Francolin, as the Red Spurfowl profile puts it

Grey Francolin]] (Francolinus pondicerianus)

Grey-brown and finely barred, with a buff throat edged by a dark line; a bird of open scrub and fields rather than forest undergrowth

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.

Red Spurfowl: The rich rufous-chestnut plumage, bare brick-red orbital eye ring, coral-red legs with sharp spurs, and absence of a fleshy head comb distinguish the Red Spurfowl.
