Saffron-faced Blue Dart or Three-lined Dart? सैफ़्रन-फ़ेस्ड ब्लू डार्ट या थ्री-लाइन्ड डार्ट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Three-lined Dart, as the Saffron-faced Blue Dart profile puts it

Blue face (not orange); different pattern

तीन धारी व्याधपतंग

Saffron-faced Blue Dart, as the Three-lined Dart profile puts it

Orange-red face (not blue); lacks three stripes

केसरी चेहरा व्याधपतंग

Saffron-faced Blue Dart: Orange-red face and head with blue abdomen in males (or orange head with greenish body in females) distinguishes this from all other local damselflies.

Three-lined Dart: Blue damselfly with three prominent black longitudinal stripes (one central, two lateral) on the thorax distinguishes this species.
