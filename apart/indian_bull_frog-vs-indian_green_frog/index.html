Indian Bull Frog or Indian Green Frog? पहाड़ मेंढक या हरा मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पहाड़ मेंढकIndian Bull FrogHoplobatrachus tigerinus
हरा मेंढकIndian Green FrogEuphlyctis hexadactylus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Bull Frog, as the Indian Green Frog profile puts it

Much larger (80–150 mm); deep croaks; webbed toes to the tip; longitudinal ridges

बहुत बड़ा, गहरी आवाज़, सब अँगुलियाँ झिल्ली वाली

Indian Bull Frog: Very large green frog in pond + bright yellow breeding male + deep roaring call + monsoon season eastern UP = Indian Bull Frog. The size and yellow breeding color together are unique.

Indian Green Frog: A bright green, slender frog with a pointed snout and golden eyes, calling in monsoon pools with a rapid curr-curr-curr trill, is the Indian green frog; the slender body and green colour are the key.

Side by side आमने-सामने

TympanumLarge, circular ear disc behind the eye; in males, it is larger than the eye—
CallDeep, resonant roaring call — carries hundreds of metres, sounding like a motor engineA loud, rapid curr-curr-curr trill, sometimes described as a whirring sound
Colour—Bright green, yellow-green, or olive-green; paler below; usually unmarked
Body—Slender, elongate, and slightly flattened dorsoventrally
Head—Moderate; the snout pointed; tympanum (eardrum) distinct
Eyes—Prominent, dorsal, golden-yellow
Limbs—Long and powerful; the digits of the hind feet webbed except the last one
