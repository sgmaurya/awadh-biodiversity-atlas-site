Ivy Gourd or Ridge Gourd? कुंदरू या तोरई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कुंदरूIvy GourdCoccinia grandis
तोरईRidge GourdLuffa acutangula

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ridge Gourd, as the Ivy Gourd profile puts it

Larger fruits (15–25 cm), ridged, pale yellow when ripe; leaves more deeply lobed

तोरई — बड़ी फली, लकीरें

Ivy Gourd, as the Ridge Gourd profile puts it

Much smaller fruits (2–4 cm), smooth, orange when ripe; thinner, more delicate vine

कुंदरू — छोटे फल, नारंगी

Ivy Gourd: A delicate, thin-stemmed climber with small oval green leaves and clusters of small green fruits that ripen to orange-red is kundru.

Ridge Gourd: A vigorous climbing vine with broad, scabrous, palmately lobed leaves, large yellow flowers, and long (15–25 cm) pale-green fruits with 8–10 prominent lengthwise ridges is ridge gourd.
