How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Himalayan Flameback, as the Lesser Yellownape profile puts it

Himalayan Flameback]] (Dinopium shorii)

Golden-olive mantle, distinct red or yellow crest, and warm buff face with double dark cheek-stripe

Himalayan Flameback: Golden-backed woodpecker with three toes, double black line enclosing a pale brownish throat streak, and white-streaked crown in female.

Lesser Yellownape: The bright golden-yellow nuchal crest combined with bright green upperparts and barred brown-and-white belly separates the Lesser Yellownape from all other green woodpeckers (Baker 1927).
