How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Slender Skimmer, as the Lesser Emperor profile puts it

Much smaller; zebra-striped green-black thorax; very slender dumbbell-shaped abdomen; rests frequently

हरी-चित्तीदार व्याधपतंग — छोटी, अक्सर बैठना

Lesser Emperor: Large size, powerful continuous flight over open water, green-brown thorax, and a bright sky-blue saddle on abdominal segment 2 identify the Lesser Emperor.

Slender Skimmer: Medium size, bright green-and-black striped thorax, very slender black abdomen with strongly swollen green segments 1–3 (dumbbell shape), and green eyes identify the Slender Skimmer.
