Goonch or Long-whiskered Catfish? गूँच या आर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गूँचGoonchBagarius bagarius
आरLong-whiskered CatfishSperata aor

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Long-whiskered Catfish, as the Goonch profile puts it

Smaller (max 60 cm); different body proportions; more slender

आर — छोटी, पतली

Goonch: The largest catfish in eastern UP waters, with a massive flattened head, long maxillary barbels, and a powerfully built body, is a Goonch. No other catfish reaches this size.

Long-whiskered Catfish: A large catfish (35–60 cm) with 4 barbels (whiskers), two of which are very long (reaching beyond gill slits), is a Long-whiskered Catfish. The long outer barbels are diagnostic.
