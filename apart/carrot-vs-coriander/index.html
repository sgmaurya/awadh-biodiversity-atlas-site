Carrot or Coriander? गाजर या धनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गाजरCarrotDaucus carota
धनियाCorianderCoriandrum sativum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Carrot, as the Coriander profile puts it

Leaves are feathery and hairy; flower umbel has a dark red central flower; roots are thick

जंगली गाजर — रोएँदार पत्तियाँ, बीच में लाल फूल

Carrot: Carrot (Daucus carota) is distinguished by its feathery tripinnate foliage, white umbels with a central dark purple sterile floret, and fleshy storage root, whereas fennel has bright yellow flowers and sweet anise aroma.

Coriander: Erect winter annual herb with dimorphic leaves (broad lower, thread-like upper), sweet citrus-spice scent, and flat compound umbels of white-pink flowers producing round ribbed dry seeds, is Coriander (Dhaniya).

FruitOblong schizocarp (cremocarp) covered with bristly hooked spines along ribsA small, globose, dry ribbed schizocarp (3–5 mm diameter), splitting into two half-fruits when fully dry
Plant habit—Erect, smooth, branching, highly aromatic annual herb growing up to 30–60 cm high
Dimorphic leaves—Lower leaves are broad, fan-shaped with lobed margins; upper leaves are finely dissected into narrow, thread-like segments
Aromatic scent—Every part of the plant, especially when crushed, releases a fresh, citrusy, sweet spice scent
