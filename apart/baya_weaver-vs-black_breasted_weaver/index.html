Baya Weaver, as the Black-breasted Weaver profile puts it

Baya Weaver]] (Ploceus philippinus)

Smaller; breeding male has yellow and buff plumage; different nest structure

Baya Weaver: Conical bill with streaked sparrow-like plumage. Breeding males with solid yellow crowns and breast, combined with retort-shaped hanging nests on palm or thorny trees, identify the Baya Weaver.

Black-breasted Weaver: The Black-breasted Weaver is easily identified by the breeding male's striking black face mask and black breast. The colonial nesting in hanging nests is distinctive. Females are duller but still show the characteristic weaver build and behaviour.
