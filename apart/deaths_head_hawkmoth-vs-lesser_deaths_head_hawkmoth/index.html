Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth or Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth? कालमुख पतंगा या छोटा डेथ्स हेड बाक पतंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कालमुख पतंगाGreater Death's Head HawkmothAcherontia lachesis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth, as the Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth profile puts it

Smaller wingspan (~90 mm); thoracic skull pattern is less defined; hindwings have narrower black bands

छोटा कालमुख पतंगा — छोटा आकार

Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth, as the Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth profile puts it

Larger; hindwing has a broad black basal patch; thoracic skull pattern darker

बड़ा आकार, पिछले पंख के आधार पर बड़ा काला धब्बा

Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth: Massive size (wingspan >100 mm), dark mottled forewings, and a prominent yellow-and-black human skull-like pattern on the thorax identify the Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth.

Lesser Death's Head Hawkmoth: A large dark hawkmoth with an ochraceous skull marking on the thorax, yellow hindwings without a black basal patch, and a squeaking defense call identifies Acherontia styx.
