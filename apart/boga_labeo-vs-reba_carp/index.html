How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Reba Carp, as the Boga Labeo profile puts it

Reba Carp]])

Rostral barbels present (maxillary absent); scales have distinct hexagonal dark borders forming stripes

Boga Labeo: An elongate, streamlined silvery-orange minor carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of minute maxillary barbels, and 37–39 scales in the lateral line, is Labeo boga.

Reba Carp: An elongate, streamlined silvery minor carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of short rostral barbels, and dark hexagonal scale outlines forming subtle longitudinal lines, is Cirrhinus reba.
