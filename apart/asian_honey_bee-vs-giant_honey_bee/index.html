Asian Honey Bee or Giant Honey Bee? मधुमक्खी या रॉक बी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मधुमक्खीAsian Honey BeeApis cerana
रॉक बीGiant Honey BeeApis dorsata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Honey Bee, as the Asian Honey Bee profile puts it

Much larger (17–20 mm); builds single large exposed comb on cliffs, tall trees; very aggressive

Asian Honey Bee, as the Giant Honey Bee profile puts it

Much smaller (10–12 mm); nests in cavities (hollow trees, boxes); less aggressive; abdomen is more uniformly striped

भारतीय मधुमक्खी — छोटा आकार, कोटरों में घोंसला

Giant Honey Bee: Large body size (17–20 mm), dark smoky wings, yellow-and-black striped abdomen, nesting in huge, single, open combs hanging from high structures, identifies Apis dorsata.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Asian Honey BeeGiant Honey Bee
SizeWorker 11–13 mm; smaller than European honey bee (A. mellifera, 15–18 mm)—
ColourAbdomen with alternating amber and dark brown/black bands; thorax dark brown—
ColonyMedium — 6,000–10,000 workers (mellifera: 50,000+)—
NestSingle comb in cavity (hollow tree, wall); not exposed like Apis dorsata—
BehaviourMore defensive than mellifera; rapid absconding when disturbed—
Form—Large, slender bee with a long, dark abdomen; significantly larger than other honey bees
Coloration—Head and thorax are blackish-brown; abdominal segments are yellowish-orange at the base and black at the tip
Wings—Long, smoky or slightly dusky wings, held flat over the back when resting
Nesting—Builds a single, massive, open comb (up to 1.5 meters wide) hanging vertically from high places
Defense—Highly defensive; performs "shimmering" movements across the comb to ward off predators
Behavior—Excellent long-distance flyer; exhibits crepuscular foraging (active during dusk and moonlit nights)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
