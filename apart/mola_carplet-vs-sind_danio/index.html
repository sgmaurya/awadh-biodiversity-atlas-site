How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mola Carplet, as the Sind Danio profile puts it

Mola Carplet]])

Dorsal fin has a short base (7 branched rays); body lacks brilliant blue longitudinal stripes

Mola Carplet: A small deep-bodied silvery-golden fish with a tiny inferior mouth and rounded fins in aquatic weeds is the mola; the depth of body and small mouth distinguish it.

Sind Danio: A deep-bodied, laterally compressed small cyprinid lacking barbels, possessing a long-based dorsal fin of 15–18 branched rays and three bright blue stripes on the posterior body, is Devario devario.
