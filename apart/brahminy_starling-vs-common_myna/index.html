Brahminy Starling or Common Myna? ब्राह्मणी मैना या मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्राह्मणी मैनाBrahminy StarlingSturnia pagodarum
मैनाCommon MynaAcridotheres tristis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Myna, as the Brahminy Starling profile puts it

Larger; dark brown body; yellow skin behind the eye; bold white wing patches visible in flight

देसी मैना — बड़ा आकार, आँख के पीछे पीली त्वचा, सफ़ेद पंख के धब्बे

Brahminy Starling, as the Common Myna profile puts it

Rusty-orange underparts; grey-white head; black cap; slimmer

पहाड़ी मैना / ब्राह्मणी मैना

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.

Behaviour—Walks confidently on the ground (doesn't hop); head tilted, alert; highly vocal
