Common Tailorbird or Red-whiskered Bulbul? दर्जिन या गुलाबी बुलबुल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दर्जिनCommon TailorbirdOrthotomus sutorius
गुलाबी बुलबुलRed-whiskered BulbulPycnonotus jocosus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Tailorbird, as the Red-whiskered Bulbul profile puts it

Much smaller (10–12 cm); bright green back; rust-coloured crown; lacks crest and red vent

दर्जी चिड़िया — छोटा आकार, हरी पीठ, लाल सिर, लंबी चोंच

Red-whiskered Bulbul: Medium-sized bulbul with a tall, pointed, forward-curving black crest, a bright red patch on its white cheek, and scarlet undertail coverts is a Red-whiskered Bulbul.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common TailorbirdRed-whiskered Bulbul
SizeTiny, active bird (10–13 cm total length); weight 6–10 g; smaller than a sparrowMedium-sized bulbul (18–20 cm total length); slender build
CrownBright rufous-chestnut crown cap, contrasting with olive-green upperparts—
UnderpartsClean white to pale cream, washed with buff on the flanks—
TailFrequently cocked upright; breeding males have two elongated central tail feathers—
Bill & LegsLong, slender, slightly decurved pale bill; pinkish-flesh legs—
BehaviourCreeps actively through dense low bushes; constructs stitched-leaf nests—
VoiceLoud, persistent, repetitive tuwit-tuwit-tuwit calls, heard year-round—
Crest—Tall, pointed, velvety-black crest that curves slightly forward
Red Whiskers—Diagnostic bright crimson-red feather patch behind and below the eye
Colouration—Brown upperparts; white throat and belly; black head; bright scarlet-red undertail coverts
Shoulder Collar—A dark shoulder band (collar) that is broken in the center of the breast
Song—A lively, bouncing, musical whistle of 3–4 notes

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
