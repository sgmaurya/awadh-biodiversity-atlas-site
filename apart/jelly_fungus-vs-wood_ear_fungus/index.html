Fan-shaped Jelly Fungus or Wood Ear Fungus? पीली जेली या कान की खुम्बी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Fan-shaped Jelly Fungus, as the Wood Ear Fungus profile puts it

Much smaller; spatula-shaped or fan-shaped; bright orange-yellow color; grows on pine or deciduous logs

नारंगी जेली खुम्बी — छोटा आकार, चमकीला नारंगी रंग

Wood Ear Fungus: Gelatinous, ear-shaped reddish-brown bracket fungus growing on dead hardwood branches, drying hard and black, and swelling back to a rubbery lobe when wet, is the Wood Ear Fungus (Kan ki Khumbi).

Side by side आमने-सामने

Fan-shaped Jelly FungusWood Ear Fungus
FormTiny, spatula-shaped, spoon-shaped, or fan-shaped gelatinous bodies (5–15 mm tall)—
ColorBright orange-yellow to golden-yellow when moist, drying to a dark reddish-orange or blackish crustDark reddish-brown to purplish-brown, turning dark brown or blackish when dry
TextureFirmly gelatinous, elastic, and rubbery when wet; hard, brittle, and horn-like when dryGelatinous, rubbery, elastic, semi-translucent when wet; drying hard and shriveled, expanding back when wet
AttachmentAttached to wood by a narrow, flattened stalk-like base, growing in rows out of cracks in the wood—
Gills & PoresAbsent; the smooth outer surface of the fan-like head produces the microscopic spores—
Fruit body—Ear-shaped, wavy-lobed, cup-like, concave on the inner side and convex on the outer side, 3–10 cm wide
Outer surface—Dull, covered in a very fine, dense layer of greyish, velvety microscopic hairs
Inner surface—Smooth, glossy, wavy, often showing vein-like ridges resembling the folds of an ear
