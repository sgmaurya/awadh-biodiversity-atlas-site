How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Olive Barb, as the Chagunio Carp profile puts it

Olive Barb]])

Dorsal spine serrated; snout lacks tubercles; body olive-green; 30–34 lateral line scales (Talwar & Jhingran 1991)

Chagunio Carp: A pinkish-silvery carp with an elevated back, a stout serrated dorsal spine, four barbels, small scales (44–47), and sharp facial tubercles in adult males, is Chagunius chagunio.

Olive Barb: A large, robust silvery-olive barb with four distinct barbels and a stout serrated dorsal spine, lacking bold isolated black spots on the flanks, is Systomus sarana.
