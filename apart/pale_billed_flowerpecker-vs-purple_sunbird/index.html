How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purple Sunbird, as the Pale-billed Flowerpecker profile puts it

Purple Sunbird]] (Cinnyris asiaticus)

Larger (12 cm); glossy purple-black male; different structure

Pale-billed Flowerpecker: Pale-billed Flowerpecker is the tiniest bird in the region with a pale bill and olive-green plumage. No other bird is this small with this combination of features.

Pale-billed FlowerpeckerPurple Sunbird
SizeSmallest bird in region; 9–10 cm total length10–11 cm; 5–8 g — among India's smallest resident birds

EyesDark—
Male (breeding)—Metallic blue-purple-green upperparts and throat; maroon-crimson chest band; blackish belly
Male (eclipse)—Olive-brown above, pale yellow below — retains dark streak on throat as ID mark
Female—Olive-brown above; pale yellow-white below; faint eyebrow stripe
Call—Sharp rapid chwit-chwit; male sings from exposed perch — heard before seen
Behaviour—Perches to probe flowers; brief hovering only; tail frequently pumped
