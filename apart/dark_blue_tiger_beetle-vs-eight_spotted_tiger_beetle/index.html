Dark Blue Tiger Beetle or Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle? गहरा-नीला बाघ भृंग या आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Dark Blue Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Very large; elytra deep velvety blue with brilliant green suture, sides, and apex, and large yellow spots on each side (Fowler 1912, p. 381)

मखमली नीले पंखों पर चमकीले हरे किनारे और बड़े पीले धब्बे (आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग)

Dark Blue Tiger Beetle: A large (22–23 mm), uniform dark cyaneous-black tiger beetle with shining green margins, less prominent eyes, and an abdomen without reddish margins or apex is Cicindela cyanea (Fowler 1912, p. 406).

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle: A large metallic coppery-blue tiger beetle on river sand with four bold creamy-yellow spots on each elytron (eight in total) identifies Cicindela octonotata.
