Ruddy-breasted Crake or White-breasted Waterhen? रडी-ब्रेस्टेड क्रेक या डहुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ruddy-breasted Crake, as the White-breasted Waterhen profile puts it

Much smaller (20 cm); rufous-chestnut breast and face; olive-brown back; rare and shy

रुडी क्रेक — छोटा आकार, लाल-भूरा छाती

Ruddy-breasted Crake: Ruddy-breasted crake is a small crake with a distinctive rufous or rusty breast and barred black-and-white undertail coverts. The harsh, distinctive call is diagnostic.

White-breasted Waterhen: Dark slate-grey upperparts, starkly contrasting clean white face and breast, long greenish legs, and a bright rufous-orange undertail patch identify the White-breasted Waterhen.
