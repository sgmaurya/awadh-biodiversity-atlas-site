How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern Shoveler, as the Ruddy Shelduck profile puts it

Much larger bill (shovel-like); male has iridescent green head

बड़ी चम्मच जैसी चोंच, अलग रंग

Northern Shoveler: The Northern Shoveler is the only duck in the region with a greatly enlarged, spatulate (spoon-shaped) bill. This feature is diagnostic at any distance.

Ruddy Shelduck: A large, cinnamon-rufous duck with pale head and black neck collar (males), on large water bodies in winter — is a Ruddy Shelduck. Females are paler buff.
