White-backed Planthopper or White Leafhopper? व्हाइट-बैक्ड प्लांटहॉपर या सफ़ेद फुदका?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-backed Planthopper, as the White Leafhopper profile puts it

Different shape; wings have white marking on back (not all pale)

अलग पंख पैटर्न

White-backed Planthopper: Minute dark planthopper with a prominent creamy-white stripe down the middle of the back, congregating in dense colonies at the base of rice stems.

White Leafhopper: Tiny pale white leafhopper on rice or wheat leaf undersurface, jumping rapidly when disturbed; causing leaf yellowing or chlorosis: White Leafhopper (Cofana spectra).
