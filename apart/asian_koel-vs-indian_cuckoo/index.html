How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Koel, as the Indian Cuckoo profile puts it

Asian Koel]] (Eudynamys scolopaceus)

Larger (42–46 cm); males glossy black with green bill; females heavily barred; different hosts

Indian Cuckoo: Indian Cuckoo is identified by its distinctive loud kook-kook-kook call, small size (23–26 cm), dark male and barred brown female. If the bird is larger (30+ cm) with a glossy black male, it is likely an Asian Koel or Hawk-Cuckoo.
