Bhang or Castor? भांग या अरंडी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भांगBhangCannabis sativa
अरंडीCastorRicinus communis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Castor, as the Bhang profile puts it

Leaves palmate but not serrated and not as deeply divided; seed pod distinctive; poisonous seeds

अरंडी — अलग बीज, जहरीले

Bhang: A tall, aromatic herb with deeply palmately divided, serrated leaves; small green flowers; and a distinctive musty smell, is bhang (Cannabis sativa).

Castor: Giant palmately-cut leaves whose stalk joins the blade in from the edge, plus soft-spined round capsules with mottled seeds, is castor.
