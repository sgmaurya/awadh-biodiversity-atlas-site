Devil's Claw or Indian Nettle? बिच्छू या कुप्पी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Devil's Claw, as the Indian Nettle profile puts it

Stinging hairs more pronounced; smaller, narrower leaves; fruits with stinging bristles

बहुत ज़्यादा दंश वाले बाल, छोटी पत्तियाँ

Devil's Claw: Large softly-hairy leaves + pink-purple funnel flowers + hard woody fruit with two prominent curved, barbed horns = Martynia annua. The horns are unmistakable once you have seen them.

Indian Nettle: A bushy herb with broadly toothed, densely hairy leaves that may cause mild itching, and very small flowers in spikes is Acalypha indica.
