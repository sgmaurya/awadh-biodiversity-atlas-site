Himalayan Griffon or White-rumped Vulture? हिमालयी गिद्ध या गिद्ध?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-rumped Vulture, as the Himalayan Griffon profile puts it

White-rumped Vulture]] (Gyps bengalensis)

Smaller (75–80 cm); very dark brown body; white rump patch

Himalayan Griffon, as the White-rumped Vulture profile puts it

Himalayan Griffon]] (Gyps himalayensis)

Much paler brown; whitish neck and underparts; white rump less contrasting

Himalayan Griffon: Himalayan Griffon is the largest and palest vulture regularly seen, with pale brown body, whitish neck, and yellow eye. If it is darker brown overall, it is likely White-rumped Vulture. If it has a distinctive white rump patch, it is definitely White-rumped, not Himalayan.

White-rumped Vulture: White-rumped Vulture is the largest dark brown vulture with a sharply contrasting white rump patch. The contrast between dark body and white rump is the most diagnostic feature. No other large vulture in the region shows this pattern.
