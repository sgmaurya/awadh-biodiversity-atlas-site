Common Greenshank or Green Sandpiper? कॉमन ग्रीनशैंक या ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Sandpiper, as the Common Greenshank profile puts it

Smaller; dark olive-green back; white belly; different flight pattern

ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर

Common Greenshank, as the Green Sandpiper profile puts it

Much larger; pale greenish legs; paler overall; upturned bill

ग्रीनशैंक

Common Greenshank: A large pale wader with greenish-grey legs, upturned bill, and white rump — is a Common Greenshank. Larger and paler than Redshank, with greenish (not red) legs. Often solitary. When in doubt, check bill angle — Greenshank's bill is subtly upturned.

Green Sandpiper: A small dark-backed wader with white belly and dark underwings visible in flight, at small water bodies — is a Green Sandpiper. The dark back and dark underwing are diagnostic. Solitary or in pairs.
