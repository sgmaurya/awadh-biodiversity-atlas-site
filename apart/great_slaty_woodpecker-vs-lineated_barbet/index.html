How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Great Slaty Woodpecker, as the Lineated Barbet profile puts it

Great Slaty Woodpecker]] (Mulleripicus pulverulentus)

Massive slaty-grey woodpecker; tree trunk climber with dagger bill; lacking green plumage

Great Slaty Woodpecker: The gigantic body size, uniform powdery slaty-grey colour without golden or red mantle plumage, swan-like neck, and social group foraging separate the Great Slaty Woodpecker from all flamebacks.

Lineated Barbet: The chunky grass-green body, heavily white-streaked pale head and breast, stout yellowish bill, and bright yellow bare skin patch around the eye identify the Lineated Barbet.
