How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Steppe Eagle, as the Greater Spotted Eagle profile puts it

Steppe Eagle]] (Aquila nipalensis)

Larger and longer-winged; pale nape, broad pale bar along the underwing, gape line reaching the back of the eye; the commonest large brown eagle in winter

Greater Spotted Eagle: Greater Spotted Eagle is distinguished by its large size, dark brown plumage with white spots on wings and back, yellow eyes and legs, and powerful flight. The white spots are more prominent than in Indian Spotted Eagle.

Steppe Eagle: A very large brown eagle with golden or buff crown and nape, soaring slowly over open grasslands with slow wing beats and broad wings — is a Steppe Eagle. is larger and more uniformly brown.
