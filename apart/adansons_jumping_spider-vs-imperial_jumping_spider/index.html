Imperial Jumping Spider: A jumping spider with an elevated, coppery-orange eye region bordered by white stripes, heavy dark front legs, and active daytime hunting on sunny leaves, is Thyene imperialis.

Adanson's Jumping SpiderImperial Jumping Spider
FormSmall, compact spider; cephalothorax is broad and high; abdomen is oval—
Male ColourDark brown to black; features a striking white crescent-shaped band on the back of the head and white spots on the abdomen—
Female ColourUniformly lighter sandy-brown or light brown, with less distinct darker chevrons on the abdomen—
EyesTwo massive, shiny, forward-facing principal eyes; turns its body to face observersLarge forward-facing anterior median eyes with acute stereoscopic vision (Metzner 2025)
MovementQuick, rapid runs followed by sudden pauses; hops or jumps over cracks and onto prey—
BehaviourStrictly active hunter; does not construct webs for capturing prey; spins a small silk cell for resting and egg-laying—
Female Size—Body length up to 9 mm (Sebastian & Peter 2009)
Male Size—Body length up to 8 mm (Metzner 2025)
Male Coloration—Striking cephalothorax with reddish-orange or golden cap and white lateral stripes (Rossi 1846)
Female Coloration—Cryptic sandy-yellow or pale brown with subtle mottled abdominal chevrons (Sebastian & Peter 2009)
Legs—First pair of legs in males distinctly robust, dark, and fringed with stiff black setae (Rossi 1846)
Hunting Style—Free-ranging diurnal stalk-and-pounce predator without a capture web
