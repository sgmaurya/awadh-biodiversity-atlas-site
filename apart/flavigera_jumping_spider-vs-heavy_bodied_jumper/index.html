How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Heavy-bodied Jumper, as the Flavigera Jumping Spider profile puts it

Hyllus semicupreus

Much larger body size; high rounded carapace; dense grey-brown setae; normal unflattened body

Flavigera Jumping Spider: Extremely flattened, compact beetle-mimicking jumping spider with broad rectangular carapace, golden-bronze sheen, and swollen forelegs.

Heavy-bodied Jumper: Robust, dark, metallic-sheened jumping spider on vegetation foliage, hunting in stalk-freeze-pounce sequence, = Hyllus semicupreus.
