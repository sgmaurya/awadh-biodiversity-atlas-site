Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pool Barb, as the Olive Barb profile puts it

Pool Barb]])

Smaller fish lacking barbels completely; has a distinct round black spot on caudal base

Olive Barb: A large, robust silvery-olive barb with four distinct barbels and a stout serrated dorsal spine, lacking bold isolated black spots on the flanks, is Systomus sarana.

Pool Barb: Small size (under 9 cm), silvery body, absence of whiskers, a smooth dorsal spine, a black spot at the base of the tail, and a second black spot on the dorsal fin identify the Pool Barb.
