How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-headed Lapwing, as the River Lapwing profile puts it

Grey-headed Lapwing]] (Vanellus cinereus)

Grey head; different plumage; winter visitor

Grey-headed Lapwing: Grey-headed Lapwing is distinguished by its slate-grey head, white cheeks, and black-and-white wing pattern in flight. The yellowish-green legs and wailing call are also diagnostic.

River Lapwing: The River Lapwing is the smallest lapwing in the region with a spiky crown and is STRICTLY found on exposed river sandbanks. Other lapwings have wattles or plain heads and occur in fields or grassland.
