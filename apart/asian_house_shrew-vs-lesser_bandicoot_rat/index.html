Asian House Shrew or Lesser Bandicoot Rat? छछूंदर या घुँस?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छछूंदरAsian House ShrewSuncus murinus
घुँसLesser Bandicoot RatBandicota bengalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Bandicoot Rat, as the Asian House Shrew profile puts it

Much larger (200–500 g); coarse bristly hair; broad head with orange incisors; aggressive

घुँस — बहुत बड़ा, खुरदरे बाल, चौड़ा सिर

Asian House Shrew: Pointed mobile snout, slate-grey velvety coat, thick hairless tail, musky odor, running in rapid straight dashes while emitting high squeaks, identify the Asian House Shrew.

Lesser Bandicoot Rat: Large size, coarse grey-black coat with erect bristles, snout rounded downward, tail distinctly shorter than the head-body length, and a highly aggressive growling behavior identify the Lesser Bandicoot Rat.

Side by side आमने-सामने

EyesExtremely small, black, bead-like eyes with very poor vision—
TailShort, thick at the base, tapering, and almost completely hairlessCoarse and scaly, always distinctly shorter than the head-body length
ScentSecretes a strong, pungent, musk-like odor from glands on its flanks—
BehaviourRuns in frantic, rapid, straight dashes along wall bases, making high-pitched squeaksHighly aggressive; growls, grunts, and chatter teeth when cornered or threatened
Fur—Coarse, dark grey-brown with stiff black guard hairs (bristles) standing erect when angry
Head—Broad and blunt head with a rounded, downward-curved snout; orange-yellow incisors
Burrows—Excavates large soil mounds around deep, complex burrows with multiple entries
