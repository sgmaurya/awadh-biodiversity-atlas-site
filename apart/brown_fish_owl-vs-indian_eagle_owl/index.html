Brown Fish Owl or Indian Eagle-Owl? मछली उल्लू या बड़ा उल्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मछली उल्लूBrown Fish OwlKetupa zeylonensis
बड़ा उल्लूIndian Eagle-OwlBubo bengalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Eagle-Owl, as the Brown Fish Owl profile puts it

Indian Eagle-Owl]] (Bubo bengalensis)

Heavily feathered legs and toes; vertical ear tufts; orange eyes; lives in rocky ravines, not near water

Brown Fish Owl, as the Indian Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Brown Fish Owl]] (Ketupa zeylonensis)

Bare (unfeathered) yellow legs; horizontal ear tufts; yellow eyes; lives strictly near water, feeds on fish

Brown Fish Owl: Large brown-and-white owl with staring yellow eyes, ragged horizontal ear tufts, and bare yellow legs perching on branches overhanging water is a Brown Fish Owl.

Indian Eagle-Owl: Very large owl with bright orange eyes, prominent vertical ear tufts, and heavily streaked brown plumage nesting on clay cliffs or ruins is an Indian Eagle-Owl.

ColourationRufous-brown upperparts with dark brown streaks; pale buff underparts with vertical streaksTawny-brown upperparts, heavily streaked and barred with black; buff underparts with bold dark streaks
HabitatStrictly riverine or associated with wetlands; sits on branches hanging directly over water—
Hunting ActionSnatches prey from the water surface or wades in shallows; does not plunge-dive—
Flight—Heavy but completely silent flight on broad, rounded wings; glides low along ravines
Bare Parts—Bill is greenish-black; powerful legs and toes are heavily feathered down to the black talons
Sounds—Deep, resonant double hoot: bu-bo or tu-whoo, repeated at intervals
