Pied Kingfisher or White-throated Kingfisher? छोटा किलकिला या किलकिला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-throated Kingfisher, as the Pied Kingfisher profile puts it

White-throated Kingfisher]] (Halcyon smyrnensis)

Bright blue back; chocolate-brown body; striking white throat; large red bill; hunts away from water

Pied Kingfisher, as the White-throated Kingfisher profile puts it

Pied Kingfisher]] (Ceryle rudis)

Black-and-white speckled pattern; black bill; hovers vertically over water; piscivorous

Pied Kingfisher: Medium size, black-and-white speckled pattern with a black crest, dagger-like black bill, and characteristic vertical hovering over open water identify the Pied Kingfisher.

White-throated Kingfisher: Chestnut-brown head and body, large pure white throat patch, brilliant blue back, and a large coral-red bill identify this species.
