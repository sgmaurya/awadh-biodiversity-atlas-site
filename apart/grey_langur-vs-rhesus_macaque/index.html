Northern Plains Grey Langur or Rhesus Macaque? लंगूर या बंदर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लंगूरNorthern Plains Grey LangurSemnopithecus entellus
बंदरRhesus MacaqueMacaca mulatta

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Northern Plains Grey Langur, as the Rhesus Macaque profile puts it

Much larger and slender; tail is longer than the head-body length; face is solid black with grey-white body fur; lacks red rump

लंगूर / हनुमान लंगूर — काला चेहरा, लंबी पूंछ

Northern Plains Grey Langur: A large grey monkey with a very long thin tail, black face and hands, living in noisy groups in trees is a Northern Plains Grey Langur.

Rhesus Macaque: Stocky brown body, bare pink face, and a distinct coppery-orange or rusty-red patch on the lower back and thighs identify Macaca mulatta.
