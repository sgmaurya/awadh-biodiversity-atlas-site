Billygoat Weed or Blue Billygoat Weed? जंगली पुदीना या नीला अजगन्धा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जंगली पुदीनाBillygoat WeedAgeratum conyzoides
नीला अजगन्धाBlue Billygoat WeedAgeratum houstonianum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue Billygoat Weed, as the Billygoat Weed profile puts it

Flower heads are larger and more crowded, with longer and more numerous blue-violet florets; sticky hairs on calyx.

बड़ा जंगली पुदीना — बड़े फूल, अधिक नीला रंग

Billygoat Weed, as the Blue Billygoat Weed profile puts it

Involucral bracts glabrous or sparsely hairy, oblong, acute; heads smaller

पत्तियाँ कम रोएंदार, फूल थोड़े छोटे और हल्के

Blue Billygoat Weed: Softly hairy herb with glandular-acuminate involucral bracts, deltoid-ovate leaves, and dense fluffy heads of bright lavender-blue florets identifies Ageratum houstonianum.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Billygoat WeedBlue Billygoat Weed
FormErect, branching winter annual herb (30–100 cm tall), covered with soft, white spreading hairs—
LeavesOpposite, ovate leaves (2–8 cm long) with blunt teeth (crenate) and a hairy surface, smelling strongly of goat/mint when crushed—
FlowersSmall, discoid flower heads (4–6 mm diameter) grouped in flat-topped clusters; color is pale blue, violet, or white—
StemCylindrical, green or purplish stem, densely covered in long, soft white hairs—
FruitsSmall, dry, black achenes (1.5–2 mm long) with 5-awned scales, easily dispersed by wind and animal fur—
Habit—Erect or decumbent hairy annual herb 25–80 cm tall (Gaur 1999)
Foliage—Opposite, deltoid-ovate, coarsely crenate-serrate, covered in soft hairs (Duthie 1903)
Capitula—Dense terminal corymbs of fluffy lavender-blue or mauve heads (Gaur 1999)
Involucre—Bracts narrow, linear-lanceolate, densely covered with sticky glandular hairs (Gaur 1999)
Odour—Distinct coumarin-like or goat-like musky scent emitted when bruised
Pappus—Composed of 5 aristate scales with slender awns exceeding the achene (Hooker 1881)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
