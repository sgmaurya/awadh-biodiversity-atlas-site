How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Bush Quail, as the Grey Francolin profile puts it

Smaller (13 cm); spotted brown plumage; group roosts together; quiet

जंगली बटेर

Grey Francolin, as the Jungle Bush Quail profile puts it

Grey Francolin]] (Francolinus pondicerianus)

Much larger gamebird, finely barred grey-brown body with black-bordered rufous throat and upright stance

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.

Jungle Bush Quail: The rufous-chestnut throat and face combined with close black-and-white zebra barring across the lower breast distinguish the male Jungle Bush Quail from all other Indian quails (Baker 1928).
