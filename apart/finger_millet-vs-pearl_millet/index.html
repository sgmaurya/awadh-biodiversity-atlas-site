Finger Millet or Pearl Millet? मंडुआ या बाजरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मंडुआFinger MilletEleusine coracana
बाजराPearl MilletCenchrus americanus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pearl Millet, as the Finger Millet profile puts it

Pearl Millet]])

Single dense cylindrical spike; much denser bristles; taller plant

Finger Millet, as the Pearl Millet profile puts it

Finger Millet]])

Much shorter; panicles are finger-like with 4–5 branches; grains are smaller

Finger Millet: A compact grass with 4–8 finger-like panicles spreading from the top, each bearing tiny dark grains, distinguishes finger millet.

Pearl Millet: A compact erect grass with a dense, cylindrical, bristly grain spike containing pearl-like tiny grains distinguishes pearl millet.

Finger MilletPearl Millet
