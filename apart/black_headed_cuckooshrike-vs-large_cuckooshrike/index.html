How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Cuckooshrike, as the Black-headed Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Large Cuckooshrike]] (Coracina macei)

Much larger bird; lacks solid black hood; pale grey overall with dark eye-mask in male; heavy hooked bill

Black-headed Cuckooshrike: The adult male's jet-black hood sharply demarcated from a pale grey back and bright white abdomen instantly identifies the Black-headed Cuckooshrike from the larger, uniform-grey Large Cuckooshrike and the dark slaty Black-winged Cuckooshrike.

Large Cuckooshrike: A large (25–28 cm), uniformly grey-brown perching bird with a stout hooked bill and rounded shape, gleaning caterpillars in tree foliage, is a Large Cuckooshrike. Smaller shrike species are brightly coloured or patterned and sit on exposed perches.
