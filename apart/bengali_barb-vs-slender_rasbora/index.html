How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bengali Barb: A slender silvery minnow with an oblique mouth, symphysial knob on the lower jaw, a single pair of short rostral barbels, and 40–44 lateral line scales, is Megarasbora elanga.

Slender Rasbora: A slender, elongate silvery cyprinid lacking barbels, marked with a bold continuous blue-black mid-lateral stripe edged in gold running from eye to caudal tip, is Rasbora daniconius.
