How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Indian Kaloula, as the Nilphamari Narrow-mouthed Frog profile puts it

Slightly larger (25–35 mm), more colorful with dark bands or blotches; different call

बड़ा, रंगीन, बैंडें

Nilphamari Narrow-mouthed Frog: An extremely tiny (12–18 mm), plain-colored frog with a rapid "chirp-chirp-chirp" chorus is a chorus frog. Listen for the call to locate them; individual frogs are nearly impossible to spot visually.

Common Indian Kaloula: A tiny (25–35 mm), globular, vividly striped red-brown frog in rain pools during monsoon is a painted kaloula. Listen for the rapid "dhup-dhup" call. It spends the day buried in soil.
