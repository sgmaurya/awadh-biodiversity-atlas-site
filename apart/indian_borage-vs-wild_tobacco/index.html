Indian Borage or Wild Tobacco? छोटा कुलफा या बन तंबाकू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wild Tobacco, as the Indian Borage profile puts it

Nicotiana plumbaginifolia]]

Herb with sticky glandular hairs, long-tubed nocturnal white flowers, and capsules

Indian Borage, as the Wild Tobacco profile puts it

Trichodesma indicum]]

Coarse herb with bristly stinging hairs and broad nodding pale blue flowers

Indian Borage: Harshly bristly foliage, nodding pale blue flowers with a prominent woolly staminal cone, and four glossy nutlets clearly distinguish Trichodesma indicum.

Wild Tobacco: Sticky glandular-hairy stems, crinkly basal leaves, and long slender greenish-white tubular flowers that open at dusk identify Nicotiana plumbaginifolia.
