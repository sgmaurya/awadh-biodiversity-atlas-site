Greater Painted-snipe or Jack Snipe? राज चाहा या छोटा चाहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

राज चाहाGreater Painted-snipeRostratula benghalensis
छोटा चाहाJack SnipeLymnocryptes minimus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Painted-snipe, as the Jack Snipe profile puts it

Broad white eye-patches; greenish wings with buff ocelli; bill decurved at tip; non-migratory marsh dweller

राज चाहा

Greater Painted-snipe: Greater painted-snipe is a small, colourful wader with a distinctive long but relatively short bill (compared to true snipes) and pale eye-ring. If larger and with a much longer bill, it is a true snipe. Unique sexual dimorphism (bright female, dull male) helps confirm identity.

Jack Snipe: A very small snipe that crouches until nearly trodden on, lacks a pale central crown stripe, and flushes silently with a low flutter is a Jack Snipe.
