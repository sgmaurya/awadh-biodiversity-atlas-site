Prishniparni or Salparni? पृश्निपर्णी या सालपर्णी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सालपर्णीSalparniPleurolobus gangeticus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Salparni, as the Prishniparni profile puts it

Pleurolobus gangeticus]]

Undershrub with simple unifoliolate leaves and curved jointed pods with hooked hairs

Prishniparni, as the Salparni profile puts it

Uraria picta]]

Robust subshrub with multiple narrow leaflets variegated with white, and long dense spikes

Prishniparni: Narrow multi-foliolate leaves with a distinctive white central clouding and dense tail-like cylindrical flower spikes easily distinguish Uraria picta from other woodland legumes.

Salparni: Single-foliolate leaves, slender semi-woody stems, and narrow curved pods with minute hooked hairs immediately separate Pleurolobus gangeticus from multi-foliolate legumes.
