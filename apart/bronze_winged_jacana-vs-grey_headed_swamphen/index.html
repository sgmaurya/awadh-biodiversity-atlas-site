Bronze-winged Jacana or Grey-headed Swamphen? दल-पीपी या कैम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bronze-winged Jacana, as the Grey-headed Swamphen profile puts it

Smaller; bronze-green wings, white eye stripe, spider-like toes, yellow frontal shield

दल-पीपी — छोटी, कांस्य पंख, आँखों के ऊपर सफ़ेद पट्टी

Bronze-winged Jacana: Glossy black body, bronze wings, a broad white stripe running behind the eye, and extremely long spider-like toes identify the Bronze-winged Jacana.

Grey-headed Swamphen: Bright purplish-blue body, pale greyish head, massive red bill and frontal shield, and long pinkish-red legs identify the Grey-headed Swamphen.
