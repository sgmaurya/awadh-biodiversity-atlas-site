लाल ड्रैगनफ्लाई — लाल पूँछ, गहरा धड़

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk: Medium dragonfly with crimson-red abdomen tip and male body appearing pale pinkish-white due to pruinose coating distinguishes this species.

Slender Skimmer: Medium size, bright green-and-black striped thorax, very slender black abdomen with strongly swollen green segments 1–3 (dumbbell shape), and green eyes identify the Slender Skimmer.
