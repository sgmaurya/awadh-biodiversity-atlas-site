Cucumber, as the Snake Gourd profile puts it

Cucumber: A tender climbing vine with deeply lobed rough leaves, bright yellow flowers, and long slender green prickly cucumber fruits harvested tender is cucumber; it is the most common garden vegetable.

Snake Gourd: An extremely long, thin, snake-like, smooth, dark green fruit (60–90 cm) on a climbing vine with large lobed leaves and night-blooming white flowers distinguishes snake gourd.
