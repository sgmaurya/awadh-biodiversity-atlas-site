Peepal or Wild Almond? पीपल या जंगली बादाम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जंगली बादामWild AlmondSterculia foetida

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peepal, as the Wild Almond profile puts it

Heart-shaped simple leaves; fig fruits

दिल की आकृति; अंजीर जैसे फल

Peepal: A heart-shaped leaf with a long thin drip-tip + a large open-crowned tree + figs in pairs at leaf bases + village or temple location = peepal. The drip-tip is the most reliable single feature.

Wild Almond: A large tree with enormous palm-shaped leaves split into 5–7 lobes and distinctive boat-shaped reddish seed pods splitting to release almond-like seeds is Wild Almond.

Side by side आमने-सामने

PeepalWild Almond
Size & ShapeLarge deciduous or semi-evergreen tree (20–30 m tall) with a massive, wide-spreading crown—
LeavesShiny, heart-shaped (cordate) leaves with a characteristically long, narrow, pointed drip-tip (2–5 cm)Large, palmately lobed, 15–30 cm across, with 5–7 lobes
Leaf PetioleLong, slender, flexible stalks (5–10 cm) that cause the leaves to tremble and shimmer in the slightest breeze—
BarkSmooth, pale grey to whitish on young trees, becoming scaly with thin papery flakes on older trunksGreyish-brown, slightly rough
Fruit (Figs)Small, round (1–1.5 cm diameter) syconia borne in pairs in leaf axils; green ripening to dark purple-black—
Growth HabitFrequently begins life as a hemi-epiphyte in wall crevices, building cracks, or forks of other trees—
Size—Large deciduous tree, 15–25 m tall, broad spreading crown
Flowers—Small, dark reddish-brown, clustered, malodorous (pungent smell)
Fruit—4–5 boat-shaped follicles, 7–10 cm long, reddish, splitting to release seeds
Seeds—Large, oval, smooth, 2–3 cm long, resembling almonds, red or brown
The test—Huge palmate leaves and large boat-shaped seed pods splitting open identify it distinctly
