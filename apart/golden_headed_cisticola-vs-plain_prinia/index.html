How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plain Prinia, as the Golden-headed Cisticola profile puts it

Prinia inornata

Much larger size; uniform sandy-buff upperparts; longer graduated tail lacking bold golden tones

Golden-headed Cisticola: Tiny grassland warbler; breeding male has unstreaked golden-buff crown and short tail; unique buzzing and popping flight song.

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.

Breeding male crownUnstreaked bright golden-yellow to rich buff crown, nape, and face—
Non-breeding & femaleHeavily black-streaked crown and mantle with warm cinnamon-buff flanks—

UnderpartsWarm unstreaked golden-buff wash across throat, breast, and flanks—
Nest habitMaster tailor that stitches living grass blades together with spider silk—
Size—Small, slender warbler (13–14.5 cm total length); dominated by a long, narrow tail
Colouration—Plain, unstreaked olive-brown upperparts; warm buffy-white throat, breast, and belly
Tail—Long, graduated, brownish tail; tail feathers have dark subterminal spots and white tips
Supercilium—A narrow, pale whitish stripe running from the bill to just behind the eye
Behaviour—Constantly hops in low vegetation; flicks and wags its tail from side to side
Bare Parts—Bill is black in summer, turning brown in winter; legs are pale brownish-pink
