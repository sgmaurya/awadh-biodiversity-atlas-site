Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Scorpion, as the Giant Water Bug profile puts it

Smaller, 30–45 mm, with a long thin breathing tube at the tail like a sting; the real "pani ka bichhu"

पूँछ पर लंबी पतली नली

Giant Water Bug: A flat brown bug longer than a matchbox with mantis-like front legs and oar-like hind legs, at a pond or under a light, is the giant water bug.

Water Scorpion: A thin brown insect 30–45 mm long, with a long thread-like breathing tube projecting from the tail, camouflaged among aquatic vegetation in a pond, is a water scorpion.
