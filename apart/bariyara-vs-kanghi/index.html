Common Wireweed or Indian Mallow? बरियारा या कंघी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बरियाराCommon WireweedSida acuta
कंघीIndian MallowAbutilon indicum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Wireweed, as the Indian Mallow profile puts it

Leaves narrower, more lanceolate; flowers smaller (0.5–0.8 cm); fruit a disc with 5 segments, no hooks

पत्तियाँ सँकरी, फूल छोटे, फल 5 टुकड़ों में

Common Wireweed: A slender annual weed (30–90 cm) with pale yellow flowers and round, slightly bristly seed capsules splitting into five parts, found on roadsides and field edges, is Sida acuta. The wiry stems are the source of the common name "wireweed."

Indian Mallow: Soft, hairy herb + heart-shaped leaves + bright yellow flowers + disc-shaped fruit breaking into hooked segments = Abutilon indicum. The bright yellow pollen is diagnostic.
