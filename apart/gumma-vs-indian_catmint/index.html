Gumma or Indian Catmint? गूमा या इंडियन कैटमिंट?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gumma, as the Indian Catmint profile puts it

Leucas aspera

Erect herb; pure white two-lipped flowers in dense globular axillary verticillasters

Gumma: An erect, square-stemmed, hairy herb with rough lanceolate leaves and white flowers densely packed on long spikes is gumma.

Indian Catmint: Look for the stiffly erect, square stem covered in white hairs, coarsely serrate leaves that smell of camphor, and dense verticillasters of two-lipped violet flowers.
