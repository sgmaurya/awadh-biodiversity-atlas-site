Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Mormon, as the Common Grass Yellow profile puts it

Much larger; black with white/red spots; strong flight; feeds on citrus

सामान्य मॉर्मन — बहुत बड़ा, काला-लाल रंग, लंबी पूँछ

Common Grass Yellow: Small, sulphur-yellow butterfly with black outer margins on its wings, flying very low over grass and weeds, is the Common Grass Yellow.

Common Mormon: Large black swallowtail butterfly with cream-white spots on hindwings (male) or a female mimicking toxic red-spotted Rose butterflies, whose caterpillars eat citrus leaves, is the Common Mormon.
