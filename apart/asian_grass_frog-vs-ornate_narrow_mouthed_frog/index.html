Asian Grass Frog or Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog? फुदकी मेंढक या छोटा मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog]])

Asian Grass Frog, as the Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog profile puts it

Asian Grass Frog]])

Larger (35–55 mm); warty skin; pointed snout; makes cricket-like call; jumps long distances

Asian Grass Frog: Medium size (3–5 cm), rough warty skin with long wrinkles, pointed snout, and a light line running down the spine in many individuals.

Ornate Narrow-mouthed Frog: Tiny size (under 2.5 cm), smooth bronze skin with a dark diamond-arrow shape on the back, and a narrow pointed head identify this species.
