How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gumma, as the Squirrel's Tail profile puts it

Leucas aspera]]`

Small annual herb with linear-lanceolate leaves and globose whorls of white flowers

Gumma: An erect, square-stemmed, hairy herb with rough lanceolate leaves and white flowers densely packed on long spikes is gumma.

Squirrel's Tail: Look for the grey-woolly shrub with thick velvety leaves and dense cylindrical fuzzy white spikes like squirrel tails.
