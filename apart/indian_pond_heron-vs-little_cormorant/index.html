How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Pond Heron, as the Little Cormorant profile puts it

Much smaller; brown plumage at rest; white wings in flight

छोटा भूरा बगुला

Indian Pond Heron: A short-necked, hunched brown heron at the water's edge in eastern UP — that flashes brilliant white wings when it flies — is almost certainly Ardeola grayii. The "brown sitting, white flying" colour change is the most reliable single feature.

Little Cormorant: A small, all-black diving bird in freshwater that surfaces far from where it dived and perches with wings spread open — is a Little Cormorant. The glossy black colour and diving habit are definitive.
