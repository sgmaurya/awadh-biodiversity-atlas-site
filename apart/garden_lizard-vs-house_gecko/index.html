Oriental Garden Lizard or Asian House Gecko? गिरगिट या घर की छिपकली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Garden Lizard, as the Asian House Gecko profile puts it

Much larger; diurnal; scales are large and keeled; has movable eyelids; lives in bushes

गिरगिट — बड़ा आकार, दिन में सक्रिय

Asian House Gecko: Smooth pinkish-grey skin, split digital lamellae under the toes, and a tail with small spines, combined with a loud chik-chik-chik nocturnal call, identify Hemidactylus frenatus.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Oriental Garden LizardAsian House Gecko
Size30–40 cm total length (half of which is the long tail)—
BodyHighly laterally compressed; angular profile—
CrestRow of enlarged scales along nape and spine — most visible in males—
ColourResting = brown to grey-brown; breeding male = vivid orange-red head and throat—
EyesCan move independently — each eye can look in a different direction—
BehaviourPerches upright on vertical surfaces; head-bobs repeatedly; does press-up displays—
Form—Small, flattened body with a broad head; skin is smooth with small, scattered round tubercles on the back
Coloration—Pale pinkish-grey, tan, or yellowish-white; can change shade slightly to match surroundings
Feet—Expanded toe pads (subdigital lamellae) split in the middle; clawed digits for vertical climbing
Tail—Slender, cylindrical, with small whorls of spines; easily detached and regenerated
Vocalisation—A loud, repetitive chirping or clicking sound like chik-chik-chik, produced in rapid succession

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
