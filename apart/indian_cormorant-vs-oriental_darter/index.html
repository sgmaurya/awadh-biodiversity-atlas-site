Indian Cormorant or Oriental Darter? जलकाग या पनडुब्बी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Darter, as the Indian Cormorant profile puts it

Slimmer profile; straight pointed bill (not hooked); longer neck

पनडुब्बी

Indian Cormorant, as the Oriental Darter profile puts it

Shorter neck; hooked bill; stockier build

जलकाग

Indian Cormorant: The Indian Cormorant is a medium-sized black water bird with a distinctive brown patch on the upper neck. The hooked bill and fully webbed feet are diagnostic for cormorants. Size places it between Little (smaller) and Great (larger).

Oriental Darter: The Oriental Darter is distinctive — the only water bird in the region with a long, slender, snake-like neck and a straight, sharply pointed bill. When swimming, only the neck and head are visible above water.
