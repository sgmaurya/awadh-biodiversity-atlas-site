Large Razorbelly Minnow or Silver Hatchet Chela? लार्ज रेज़रबेली मिनो मछली या सिल्वर हैचेट चेला मछली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Silver Hatchet Chela, as the Large Razorbelly Minnow profile puts it

Silver Hatchet Chela]])

Much smaller; deep hatchet-shaped body; ventral keel extends from throat to vent; pelvic fin has a trailing filament

Large Razorbelly Minnow, as the Silver Hatchet Chela profile puts it

Large Razorbelly Minnow]])

Body much larger and elongate-cylindrical; mouth large; lateral line scales over 80

Large Razorbelly Minnow: A slender, elongate, surface-dwelling silvery minnow with an upward-directed mouth, an acute razor keel behind the pelvic fins, and 86–110 scales along the lateral line, is Salmostoma bacaila.

Silver Hatchet Chela: A tiny, deep, hatchet-shaped silvery cyprinid with an acute cutting ventral keel extending continuously from throat to vent, and a filamentous trailing pelvic fin ray, is Chela cachius.
