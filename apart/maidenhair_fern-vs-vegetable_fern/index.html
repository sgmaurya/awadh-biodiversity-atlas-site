Maidenhair Fern or Vegetable Fern? हंसराज या वेजिटेबल फर्न?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Maidenhair Fern, as the Vegetable Fern profile puts it

Adiantum capillus-veneris

Delicate fan-shaped pinnules on wiry black stipes; reniform sori beneath reflexed lobes

Maidenhair Fern: A fern with a jet-black wiry stalk and tiny fan-shaped leaflets that repel water is maidenhair fern (Adiantum capillus-veneris).

Vegetable Fern: Look for the robust terrestrial marsh habit, bipinnate arching fronds with anastomosing venation, and characteristic paired back-to-back linear sori along the veinlets.

Side by side आमने-सामने

FrondFeathery, delicate; bright green; 20–40 cm tall—
LeafletsTiny, fan-shaped or wedge-shaped; arranged alternately along rachis—
RachisShiny black or dark purple; very slender and graceful—
SoriTiny rounded clusters at leaflet margins; covered by flap-like indusiumElongated along veinlets, linear, solitary or paired back-to-back (diplazioid)
TexturePaper-thin leaflets; silky appearance—
MoistureLeaflets are water-repellent; beaded water droplets do not wet the surface—
Habit—Large, robust terrestrial rhizomatous fern forming erect or suberect micro-trunks
Rhizome—Thick, woody, erect to ascending, densely clothed with dark brown lanceolate scales
Fronds—Bipinnate to tripinnatifid, large, arching; stipes smooth, green to brownish, scaly at base
Pinnae—Alternate, oblong-lanceolate; pinnules serrate to lobed with veins anastomosing into pairs
Fiddleheads—Young emerging croziers tightly coiled, succulent, green, sparsely scaly, highly edible
