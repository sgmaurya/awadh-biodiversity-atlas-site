Red-naped Ibis or Asian Woolly-necked Stork? काला बुज़ा या लगलग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Woolly-necked Stork, as the Red-naped Ibis profile puts it

Larger; stork not ibis; white on neck and collar

बड़ा, सारस, सफेद पट्टी

Red-naped Ibis: A large, glossy-black ibis with a red nape patch and smaller flock size — is a Red-naped Ibis. The red nape is diagnostic; Black-headed Ibis has no red at all.

Asian Woolly-necked Stork: A large black-and-white stork with a distinctive white woolly collar at the base of the neck — is a Woolly-necked Stork. The collar is unique and diagnostic.
