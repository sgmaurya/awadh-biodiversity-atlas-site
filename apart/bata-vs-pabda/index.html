Bata: A medium-sized silvery carp 15–30 cm long with a robust build, moderate-sized scales, and no distinctive markings, found in rivers and large ponds during monsoon, is Labeo bata; gill raker count (80–120) and scale counts confirm identification.

Pabda: A small, flattened catfish with two dark spots (one on gill cover, one on tail base) and a benthic habit is a pabda.
