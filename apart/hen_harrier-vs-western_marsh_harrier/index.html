Hen Harrier or Western Marsh Harrier? हेन हैरियर या दलदल बाज?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दलदल बाजWestern Marsh HarrierCircus aeruginosus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Western Marsh Harrier, as the Hen Harrier profile puts it

Larger; darker overall; males have grey wing-coverts, not all-grey; different head pattern

दलदल हैरियर

Hen Harrier: Hen Harrier is distinguished by its silver-grey back in males (or brown streaking in females), white rump visible in both sexes, and characteristic low quartering flight. Males are more uniformly coloured than Pied Harrier; females are less heavily marked than Marsh Harrier.

Western Marsh Harrier: Large dark brown raptor with cream-coloured crown and throat (female) or tri-coloured grey wings (male) quartering slowly and low over reeds with wings in a shallow V is a Western Marsh Harrier.
