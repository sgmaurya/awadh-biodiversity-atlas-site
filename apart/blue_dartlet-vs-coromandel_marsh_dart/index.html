Blue Dartlet or Coromandel Marsh Dart? नीली व्याधपतंग या कोरोमंडल मार्श डार्ट?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coromandel Marsh Dart, as the Blue Dartlet profile puts it

Bright citron-yellow body; larger; lacks blue and black patterns

Blue Dartlet: Slender, needle-like sky-blue body in males with black rings, wings held folded closed along the back at rest, and widely separated eyes confirm the Blue Dartlet.

Coromandel Marsh Dart: Tiny damselfly with bright red (male) or bright yellow-orange (female) body, slow fluttering flight over marshy vegetation, often in tandem pairs, is the Coromandel Marsh Dart.
