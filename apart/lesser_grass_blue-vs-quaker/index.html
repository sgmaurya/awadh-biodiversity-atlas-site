Lesser Grass Blue, as the Quaker profile puts it

Lesser Grass Blue]])

Underside greyish-brown with numerous dark ringed spots; flies actively in open sunny grasslands rather than deep shade

Lesser Grass Blue: Tiny size (under 24 mm wingspan), upperside pale violet-blue (male) or brown (female) with a dark border, underside pale grey with small black spots, and very low flight close to the grass canopy identify the Lesser Grass Blue.

Quaker: A tiny butterfly fluttering weakly strictly in deep shade, with spotless creamy white underwings marked only by faint brown lines and tiny costal specks, is the Quaker.
