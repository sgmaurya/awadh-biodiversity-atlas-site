Mottled Wood Owl or Spotted Owlet? मॉटल्ड वुड आउल या चित्तीदार उल्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Owlet, as the Mottled Wood Owl profile puts it

Spotted Owlet]] (Athene brama)

Much smaller (20 cm); has distinct white spots on head and body; yellow eyes

Mottled Wood Owl: A dark brown, medium-sized owl with deep "hoo-hoo-hoo" calls from wooded areas is a Mottled Wood Owl. Smaller owls with obvious spots are Spotted Owlets. Larger owls near water or with ear tufts are different species.

Spotted Owlet: Small size, white spots on greyish-brown upperparts, yellow eyes, lack of ear tufts, and loud chattering calls in pairs confirm the Spotted Owlet.
