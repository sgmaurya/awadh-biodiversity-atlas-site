How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Booted Warbler, as the Bristled Grassbird profile puts it

Booted Warbler]] (Hippolais caligata)

Smaller (11–12 cm); short tail; plain plumage without heavy streaking

Booted Warbler: Booted Warbler is a cryptic, plain brown warbler of scrubland with feathered tarsi (the "boots"). Best identified by call in many cases. Consult detailed guides for separation from similar cryptic species.

Bristled Grassbird: Bristled Grassbird is a small, heavily streaked warbler of dense reed beds, with a loud harsh churring call. Its cryptic, streaked plumage and creeping behaviour through dense reeds distinguish it from other marsh warblers. The long tail and white underparts with brown flanks are diagnostic in good view.
