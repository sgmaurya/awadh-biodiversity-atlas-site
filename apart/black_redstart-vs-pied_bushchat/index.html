Black Redstart or Pied Bushchat? थिरथिरा या पड्डू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पड्डूPied BushchatSaxicola caprata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the Black Redstart profile puts it

Male black and white (not all black); resident; different tail pattern

पाइड बुशचैट

Black Redstart: A small black (male) or dark grey-brown (female) songbird with a striking orange-red tail base and rump, on rocky or open areas — is a Black Redstart. The orange-red tail is diagnostic. Males are striking and unmistakable.

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.
