Line-marked Tiger Beetle or Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle? रेखा-चिह्नित बाघ भृंग या दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Line-marked Tiger Beetle, as the Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Elytra with an inverted V-shaped central marking and broader whitish-testaceous margins rather than five discrete spots (Fowler 1912, pp. 363–364)

पंखों पर धब्बों की बजाय सफेद किनारी और V-आकार का निशान (रेखा-चिह्नित बाघ भृंग)

Line-marked Tiger Beetle: A small dull dark tiger beetle with red trochanters, obscure green-coppery head and pronotum, and interrupted whitish margins with a central inverted V-shaped mark is Cylindera grammophora (Fowler 1912, pp. 363–364).

Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle: A tiger beetle with metallic front parts, elytra subparallel-sided in the male and slightly widened behind in the female, and five white spots on each dark elytron (the fifth joining a narrow apical marginal line) is Cylindera decempunctata (Fowler 1912, p. 353).

Side by side आमने-सामने

Elytra groundDull, dark, usually with a greenish reflection at base, distinctly granulate anteriorly (Fowler 1912, p. 363)—
Elytra markingsMargins mostly whitish testaceous; crescent-shaped shoulder spot, central inverted V-shaped marking, and dilated apical marking (Fowler 1912, pp. 363–364)—
LegsMetallic with red trochanters (Fowler 1912, p. 364)Metallic with reddish or pitchy red trochanters (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
UndersideThickly set with white tomentose pubescence except central body, head, and genae (Fowler 1912, p. 364)Cyaneous or green, with distinct pubescence at the sides (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
Elytra—Dark with hardly any metallic reflection (or obscurely metallic), centre of disc smooth; five white spots on each elytron (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
Spot pattern—One at shoulder, a round one on disc before middle, two irregular ones at middle, fifth near apex joining a narrow marginal white line (Fowler 1912, p. 353)
