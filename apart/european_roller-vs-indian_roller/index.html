How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Roller, as the European Roller profile puts it

Indian Roller]] (Coracias benghalensis)

Rufous-brown breast with lilac-white streaks; deep brownish-olive mantle; dark blue nuchal patch; lack of uniform pastel turquoise underparts (Baker 1927, p. 224)

European Roller: The combination of an unstreaked pastel greenish-blue head, throat, and underparts with a light cinnamon-rufous back readily separates the European Roller from the Indian Roller (Baker 1927, p. 223).

Indian Roller: A stocky, medium bird perching on a wire over open farmland, rufous-brown at rest, with a sudden flash of brilliant turquoise-blue when it flies — is the Indian Roller. Nothing else in eastern UP creates this effect.

Side by side आमने-सामने

European RollerIndian Roller
HabitStocky roller frequenting open country round about cultivation, grass plains and plateaus—
Head & UnderpartsPale blue on whole head, neck, and lower parts, streaked with brilliant smalt-blue on breast and throat (Baker 1927, p. 223)—
Mantle & ScapularsBack, scapulars, and innermost secondaries light rufous-brown (Baker 1927, p. 223)—
WingsInner wing-coverts deep blue, outer pale dull blue; quills black overlain with deep blue (Baker 1927, p. 223)—
TailLateral tail-feathers bluish-green with broad pale blue terminal band, outermost pair tipped blue-black (Baker 1927, p. 223)—
Soft PartsIris pale brown to hazel-brown; bill brownish-black to black; legs and feet dull yellow (Baker 1927, p. 223)—
Size—30–34 cm total length; stocky, compact build
Plumage (perched)—Rufous-brown back and head; pale lilac-purple throat and breast
Plumage (in flight)—Wing coverts brilliant turquoise-blue; flight feathers deep cobalt-blue with a broad turquoise band
Bill—Stout, slightly hooked, black
Favourite perch—Power lines, bare treetops, fence posts — exposed lookout posts over farmland
Behaviour—Sits motionless on perch, drops suddenly to ground to capture prey, then returns to a perch

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
