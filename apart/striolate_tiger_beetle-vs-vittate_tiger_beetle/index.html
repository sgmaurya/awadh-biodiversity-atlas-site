How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striolate Tiger Beetle, as the Vittate Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Lophyra striolata

Related tiger beetle with broken humeral and marginal elytral lunules rather than straight linear stripes

Striolate Tiger Beetle: A small bronze tiger beetle on sandy shores with intricate narrow wavy whitish striations and marginal lunules on the elytra identifies Lophyra striolata.

Vittate Tiger Beetle: Fast-running dark green tiger beetle on river sandbars with long linear yellow stripes and exposed whitish mandibles.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Striolate Tiger BeetleVittate Tiger Beetle
HabitSmall, extremely agile tiger beetle inhabiting sandy and gravelly shores—
ColorationDark bronze or greenish-bronze with coppery reflections—
Elytral patternIntricately marked with thin, wavy whitish lines and marginal lunules—
LabrumPale yellowish, transverse with a small central tooth—
LocomotionRapid sprinting combined with frequent low buzzing flights over sand—
Elytra—Dark velvety green to blackish with long yellowish longitudinal linear stripes
Head and pronotum—Dark coppery-bronze and greenish, finely striate within compound eyes
Mouthparts—Large exposed whitish labrum; long sickle-shaped mandibles with black tips
Eyes—Conspicuously large, globose, bulging compound eyes adapted for visual hunting
Gait—Extremely fast sprinting bursts across bare sand interspersed with short flights

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
