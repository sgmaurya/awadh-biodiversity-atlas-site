Brown Planthopper or Sugarcane Pyrilla? भूरा फुदका या पायरिला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भूरा फुदकाBrown PlanthopperNilaparvata lugens
पायरिलाSugarcane PyrillaPyrilla perpusilla

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Planthopper, as the Sugarcane Pyrilla profile puts it

Smaller (3–4.5 mm); infests rice not sugarcane; different host; found at the base of plant, not on leaf undersurface

धान का फुदका, छोटा, तने पर

Brown Planthopper: Brown wedge-shaped hoppers in clusters at the base of rice just above the water, jumping when tapped, with round scorched patches in the field: brown planthopper.

Sugarcane Pyrilla: Brown, robust insect in dense clusters on the undersides of Sugarcane leaves, with white powder on nymphs and sooty mould below: sugarcane pyrilla.
