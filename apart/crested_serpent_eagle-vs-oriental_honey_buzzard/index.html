How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crested Serpent Eagle, as the Oriental Honey Buzzard profile puts it

Crested Serpent Eagle]] (Spilornis cheela)

Similar crested head but yellow spotting on wings visible; hunts snakes, not insects

Crested Serpent Eagle: Crested Serpent Eagle is unique with its distinctive erectile crest and bright yellow eyes. No other eagle in eastern UP has this combination of features. The yellow spotting on dark wings is also diagnostic.

Oriental Honey Buzzard: Oriental Honey Buzzard's distinctive erectile crest and small hooked bill with bare frontal shield are diagnostic. Watch for it hunting near wasp and bee nests. The small bill (compared to other eagles) is distinctive.
