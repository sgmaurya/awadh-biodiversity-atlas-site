How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brahminy Kite, as the Grey-headed Fish-Eagle profile puts it

Haliastur indus

Much smaller raptor; rich chestnut body contrasting with pure white head and breast; rounded tail

Brahminy Kite: Bright chestnut body, contrasting white head and breast, and a rounded tail flying near rivers or lakes confirm the Brahminy Kite.

Grey-headed Fish-Eagle: Massive river eagle with pale grey head, dark brown breast, gleaming white belly, and white tail tipped with broad black band.
