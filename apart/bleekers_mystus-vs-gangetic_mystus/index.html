Maxillary barbels much longer, extending past the caudal fin tip

Bleeker's Mystus: A small scaleless bagrid catfish with eight barbels, whose maxillary barbels reach the anal fin, and possessing an exceptionally long adipose fin originating immediately behind the dorsal fin, is Mystus bleekeri.

Gangetic Mystus: A small catfish with three pairs of barbels and serrated pectoral spines is a Gangetic Mystus. The spine causes pain if touched.
