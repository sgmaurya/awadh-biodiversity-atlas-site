African Globe Thistle or East Indian Globe Thistle? गोरखमुंडी या गोरखमुंडी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

East Indian Globe Thistle, as the African Globe Thistle profile puts it

Leaves spinous-serrate; compound heads larger, more purplish; closely allied

पत्तियाँ अधिक कांटेदार, सिर थोड़े बड़े

African Globe Thistle: Strongly aromatic annual herb with distinctly winged green stems and compact purple globular compound flower heads growing on drying alluvium identifies Sphaeranthus senegalensis.

East Indian Globe Thistle: A small, soft-stemmed herb with tiny leaves and very distinctive compact, round, spiky purple flower heads is Sphaeranthus indicus.
