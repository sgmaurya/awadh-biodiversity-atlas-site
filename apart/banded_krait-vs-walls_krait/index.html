How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Banded Krait, as the Wall's Krait profile puts it

Bungarus fasciatus

Strikingly patterned with alternating broad yellow and black rings of equal width along the entire body and a blunt tail tip

Banded Krait: A bright yellow-and-blue-black banded snake in rice paddies or wetland areas at night is a banded krait. It is venomous and should be left alone.

Wall's Krait: Large glossy black krait with distinct paired white crossbars and broad hexagonal vertebral scales, native to the Oudh plains.
