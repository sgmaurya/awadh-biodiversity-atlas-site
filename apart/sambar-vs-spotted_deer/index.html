Sambar or Spotted Deer? सांभर या चित्रा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Deer, as the Sambar profile puts it

Spotted Deer]] (Axis axis)

Much smaller size; bright golden-rufous coat with permanent white spots; slender build; gregarious open-glade habits

Sambar, as the Spotted Deer profile puts it

Sambar]] (Rusa unicolor)

Substantially larger size; uniform dark umber-brown or greyish coat without spots; coarse maned neck in males

Sambar: The massive horse-like size, uniform shaggy dark umber-brown coat devoid of white spots, large bat-like ears, and heavy three-tined antlers distinguish the Sambar from all other deer.

Spotted Deer: The permanent bright white spots across a golden-rufous coat, three-tined lyrate antlers, and medium slender build distinguish the Spotted Deer from all other regional deer.
