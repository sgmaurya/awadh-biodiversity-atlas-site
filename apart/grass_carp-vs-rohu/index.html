How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grass Carp, as the Rohu profile puts it

Grass Carp]] (Ctenopharyngodon idella)

Cylindrical body; scales with dark margin; no reddish fins; more silvery

Grass Carp: A large green cyprinid with a terminal mouth, no barbels, and eating water plants is grass carp. The absence of barbels (present in common carp) and the large size distinguish it from other carps in eastern UP ponds.

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.
