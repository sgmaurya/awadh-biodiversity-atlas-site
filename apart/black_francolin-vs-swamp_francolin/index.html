How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Francolin, as the Swamp Francolin profile puts it

Francolinus francolinus

Male is jet black below with round white flank spots and rich chestnut neck collar

Black Francolin: Entirely black francolin with red legs and face patch = male Black Francolin. Brown, heavily barred = female Black Francolin. The distinctive loud call kataak-kataak is diagnostic.

Swamp Francolin: Large waterlogged marsh francolin with bright chestnut throat, white-shafted brown back, and boldly streaked black-and-white breast.

Upperparts—Brown finely barred and vermiculated with buffy-white, with white feather shafts
Tail pattern—Deep chestnut outer tail feathers contrasting with brown central rectrices
Habits—Skulking ground bird of waterlogged marshes; flies reluctantly with rapid whirring
