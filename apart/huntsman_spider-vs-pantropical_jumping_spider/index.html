Huntsman Spider or Pantropical Jumping Spider? शिकारी मकड़ी या कूदने वाली मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

शिकारी मकड़ीHuntsman SpiderHeteropoda venatoria
कूदने वाली मकड़ीPantropical Jumping SpiderPlexippus paykulli

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pantropical Jumping Spider, as the Huntsman Spider profile puts it

Much smaller (body length under 12 mm); moves with sudden jumps rather than rapid running; has large anterior median eyes

कूदने वाली मकड़ी — छोटा आकार, उछल कर चलना

Huntsman Spider: Flat crab-like body, leg span up to 12 cm, yellowish-brown colour, and rapid running without web-spinning identifies the Huntsman Spider.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Huntsman SpiderPantropical Jumping Spider
Size & ShapeLarge, flattened spider; legs are long and positioned laterally (crab-like posture), allowing rapid sideways movement—
ColourYellowish-brown to greyish-brown; females have a dark band behind the eyes and a light border on the carapace; males have a dark, heart-shaped mark behind the eyes—
LegsVery long, banded or spotted with fine black bristles, with the second pair of legs being slightly longer than the first—
EyesEight small eyes arranged in two horizontal rows of four on the front of the carapaceTwo enormous forward-facing principal eyes — most diagnostic feature; turns to face observer
Egg SacFemales carry a flat, disc-shaped, papery-white egg sac (about 12–18 mm diameter) beneath their sternum using their chelicerae—
Size—Male 8–10 mm; female 10–12 mm — compact, box-shaped body
Male—Jet-black with bold white lateral stripes on abdomen; white spots at rear; legs banded black-white
Female—Brownish-grey with pale chevron pattern; less contrasted
Movement—Short quick dashes; freezes; turns to face you; never builds a prey-catching web
Chelicerae—Iridescent blue-green in male (visible in direct sunlight)
Habitat—Walls, window sills, door frames, vegetation — ubiquitous on plastered surfaces

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
