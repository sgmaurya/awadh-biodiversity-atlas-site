Mottled Loach or Pool Barb? चितकबरी सुकटी या सिधरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

चितकबरी सुकटीMottled LoachAcanthocobitis botia
सिधरीPool BarbPuntius sophore

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pool Barb, as the Mottled Loach profile puts it

Puntius sophore

Deep, compressed silvery body; prominent round black spot on caudal peduncle; lacks mottled bands

Mottled Loach: Mottled stream loach with no subocular spine, 3 barbel pairs, V-shaped caudal bands, and an upper caudal ocellus.

Pool Barb: Small size (under 9 cm), silvery body, absence of whiskers, a smooth dorsal spine, a black spot at the base of the tail, and a second black spot on the dorsal fin identify the Pool Barb.
