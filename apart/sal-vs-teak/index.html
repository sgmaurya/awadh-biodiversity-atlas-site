Sal or Teak? साल या सागवान?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सालSalShorea robusta
सागवानTeakTectona grandis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Teak, as the Sal profile puts it

Leaves much larger; flowers white; fruit a capsule with a cork wing

टीक — बड़ी पत्तियाँ

Sal, as the Teak profile puts it

Has smaller, glossy, smooth leaves; seed has five twisting wings; bark is deeply fissured; evergreen

साल — चिकने पत्ते, पंखदार बीज, गहरी छाल

Sal: A very tall tree with rough, leathery, obovate leaves and pale wood, with winged fruits, in a temple or forest setting, is sal.

Teak: Large tree with straight grey trunk, massive sandpaper-rough leaves (30–60 cm) that fall in winter, and terminal clusters of small white flowers followed by papery balloon-like fruits, is the Teak.
