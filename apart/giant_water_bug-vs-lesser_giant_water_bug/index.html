How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Water Bug, as the Lesser Giant Water Bug profile puts it

Lethocerus indicus

Distinctly larger species with pale yellow longitudinal stripes on head and pronotum

Giant Water Bug: A flat brown bug longer than a matchbox with mantis-like front legs and oar-like hind legs, at a pond or under a light, is the giant water bug.

Lesser Giant Water Bug: Huge, cryptically brown, flattened aquatic true bug with powerful raptorial front legs, stout beak, and retractile breathing siphons.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Giant Water BugLesser Giant Water Bug
Size60–80 mm long, the largest true bug in India—
ShapeFlat, oval, boat-like; brown to olive-brown with faint darker mottling—
Front legsThick, folded, raptorial, like a mantis's, for seizing prey—
Hind legsFlattened and fringed with hairs, used as oarsFlattened, swimming-adapted middle and hind legs fringed with dense marginal setae
HeadShort, with a stout sucking beak folded beneath; large eyes—
Tail endTwo short retractable breathing straps that are pushed to the surface for air—
BehaviourHangs head-down under the surface with the tail tip at the air; flies strongly at night—
Body size & shape—Very large, dorsoventrally flattened, elongate-oval brown true bug with cryptic bark-like coloration
Forelegs—Powerful raptorial forelegs with enlarged femora and curved tibiae specialized for grasping prey
Mouthparts—Short, stout, highly sclerotized piercing rostrum (beak) capable of delivering a severe bite
Breathing apparatus—Pair of short, retractile caudal respiratory siphons at abdominal apex used at water surface

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
