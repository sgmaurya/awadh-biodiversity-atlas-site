How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Russell's Viper, as the Red Sand Boa profile puts it

Highly venomous (dangerous!), body with distinct leaf-like rings, larger head, aggressive

अत्यंत जहरीला, गोल धब्बे, तीखा

Red Sand Boa: A small-to-medium, uniformly reddish or blackish stout snake (75–100 cm) with a blunt, head-like tail in loose sandy soils is a red sand boa. It is harmless, protected by law, and beneficial for rodent control. Do not harm it.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Red Sand BoaRussell's Viper
Size & BuildSmall to medium (75–100 cm), stout, compact body with little taper from body to tail—
TailVery short, flat, blunt, rounded at the tip, closely resembling the head—
Color & PatternReddish-brown, orange-brown, or deep blackish-brown dorsally; uniform or with faint darker bands; belly lighter—
ScalesSmooth to slightly keeled; those on the tail are notably keeled and roughHeavily keeled (ridged) dorsal scales, giving the snake a rough, dry texture
HeadSmall, flat, wedge-shaped, not distinct from neck; eyes small, positioned dorsolaterallyDistinctly triangular, flat-topped, and twice as wide as the neck; snout is blunt and raised
BehaviourCryptic; attempts to bury itself in loose soil when disturbed; raises tail defensively—
Form—Stout, heavy-bodied snake; tail is short and tapers rapidly
Markings—Three longitudinal rows of dark brown oval spots with black borders and white edges, forming a chain-like pattern
Nostrils—Extremely large and conspicuous, positioned on the sides of the snout
Eyes—Large, with gold-flecked irises and distinct vertical, slit-like pupils
Defence—Coils tightly, raises the front of its body, and produces a remarkably loud, continuous pressure-cooker-like hiss

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
