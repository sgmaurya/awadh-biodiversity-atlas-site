How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gadwall, as the Falcated Duck profile puts it

Gadwall]] (Mareca strepera)

Male has plain brown-grey head without crest or bronze gloss, lacks long drooping sickle tertials; white speculum (Baker 1929, p. 426)

Falcated Duck: The shimmering green and bronze-crested head, crescent-barred breast, and long drooping sickle-shaped tertials distinguish the male Falcated Duck (Baker 1929, pp. 424–425).

Gadwall: A medium-sized dabbling duck with grey-and-black coloration (males) and white speculum visible in flight — is a Gadwall. In flight, the white wing patch is diagnostic.
