How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-tailed Stonechat, as the White-throated Bushchat profile puts it

White-tailed Stonechat]] (Saxicola leucurus)

Lacks white throat; instead has black throat in male

White-tailed Stonechat: Males are distinguished by the white tail (other stonechats lack this). Females are trickier but White-tailed shows a pale or whitish tail.

White-throated Bushchat: White-throated Bushchat males are distinguished by the combination of black cap, black back, white throat, and white rump. The white throat is the key diagnostic feature from all other similar species. Females require closer inspection but retain the distinctive white throat.
