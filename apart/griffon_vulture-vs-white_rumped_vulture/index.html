How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-rumped Vulture, as the Griffon Vulture profile puts it

White-rumped Vulture]] (Gyps bengalensis)

Smaller and much darker; adults blackish with a white rump and a white bar on the underwing

Griffon Vulture: The immense wingspan, sandy-tawny body plumage, contrasting dark flight feathers, and distinct white ruff around the neck distinguish the Griffon Vulture.

White-rumped Vulture: White-rumped Vulture is the largest dark brown vulture with a sharply contrasting white rump patch. The contrast between dark body and white rump is the most diagnostic feature. No other large vulture in the region shows this pattern.
