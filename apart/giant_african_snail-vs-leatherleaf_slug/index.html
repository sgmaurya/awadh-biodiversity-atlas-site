Giant African Snail or Tropical Leatherleaf Slug? अफ्रीकी घोंघा या काला स्लग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant African Snail, as the Tropical Leatherleaf Slug profile puts it

Much larger (80–100 mm); has a shell; conical shape

बहुत बड़ा, घोल है

Giant African Snail: A very large (8–10 cm), tan-brown, conical snail with a thick slime trail, feeding openly on vegetation at night, is the giant African snail; no native snail approaches this size.

Tropical Leatherleaf Slug: A black or dark brown, smooth, shiny slug of medium size without a shell, leaving a copious slime trail, is the tropical leatherleaf slug; the leather-like texture and black colour are distinctive.
