How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-winged Cuckooshrike, as the Long-tailed Minivet profile puts it

Coracina melaschistos

Uniform dark slaty-grey plumage with black wings and tail; lacks any bright red or yellow colors

Black-winged Cuckooshrike: Black-winged Cuckooshrike has a pale grey body with sharply contrasting black wings. Drongos are all-black with longer, forked tails. The Cuckooshrike moves more deliberately and hangs from branches searching foliage.

Long-tailed Minivet: Slender canopy passerine with long graduated tail; male jet-black and scarlet with isolated inverted-U wing loop; female olive-grey with bright yellow forehead and throat.
