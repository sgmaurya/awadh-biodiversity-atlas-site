How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crown Daisy, as the Oriental False Hawksbeard profile puts it

Much larger robust plant; leaves deeply 2-pinnatisect; daisy heads much larger

बड़ा पौधा, बारीक कटी पत्तियाँ, बड़ा फूल

Crown Daisy: Deeply twice-cut feathery leaves paired with large solitary daisy flower heads of bright yellow or bicoloured white-and-yellow identifies Glebionis coronaria.

Oriental False Hawksbeard: Low basal leaf rosette with milky sap bearing slender branched stalks of tiny bright yellow ligulate flower heads in shaded spots identifies Youngia japonica.
