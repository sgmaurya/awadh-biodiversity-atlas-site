How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Castor Woolly Bear Moth, as the Bordered Tussock Moth profile puts it

Forewings grey-brown with round spots; abdomen crimson with black bands

पंखों पर गोल कत्थई धब्बे, पेट लाल-काला

Bordered Tussock Moth: A whitish to pale grey tussock moth with feathery male antennae and dark wavy submarginal borders on the wings identifies Lymantria marginata.

Castor Woolly Bear Moth: Densely hairy or bristly black caterpillar with bright yellow or red bands on castor, neem, or gular; moth with dark banded wings and woolly body: Castor Woolly Bear Moth (Olepa ricini).
