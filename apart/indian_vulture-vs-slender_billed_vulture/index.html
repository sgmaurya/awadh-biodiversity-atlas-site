How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Slender-billed Vulture, as the Indian Vulture profile puts it

Slender-billed Vulture]] (Gyps tenuirostris)

Darker body plumage, totally bare blackish head and neck lacking down, narrower slender bill with horny-black cere, and tree-nesting habit (Baker 1928)

Indian Vulture, as the Slender-billed Vulture profile puts it

Indian Vulture]] (Gyps indicus)

Paler sandy plumage, downy crown, stouter yellowish bill with dull greenish cere, and cliff-nesting habit (Baker 1928)

Indian Vulture: The pale sandy-buff body, stout yellowish-horny bill with dull dirty greenish cere and sparsely downy head distinguish the Indian Vulture from the Slender-billed Vulture. Its rump is creamy-white too, but "sometimes hardly visible" (Baker 1928, p. 16), so a white rump alone does not make a White-rumped Vulture.

Slender-billed Vulture: The completely bare dark head and neck without down, slender dark bill with horny-black cere, and habit of nesting singly in huge cotton-trees distinguish the Slender-billed Vulture from its congeners (Baker 1928).
