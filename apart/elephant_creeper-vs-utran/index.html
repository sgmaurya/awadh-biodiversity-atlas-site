Elephant Creeper or Utran? समुद्रसोख या उतरन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Utran, as the Elephant Creeper profile puts it

Smaller leaves; yellow flowers; different family (Convolvulaceae); thinner stems

पत्तियाँ छोटी, पीले फूल

Elephant Creeper: A vigorous climbing vine with very large heart-shaped leaves that are distinctly silvery-hairy (woolly) on the underside and bearing large pink-purple dawn-opening flowers is elephant creeper.

Utran: A weak climbing vine with opposite heart-shaped leaves, star-shaped fragrant pink flowers, and paired horn-like fruits is utran.
