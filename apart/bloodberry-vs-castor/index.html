Much larger shrub; palmately lobed leaves; large red spiky fruit

Bloodberry: Bloodberry is a small shrub with delicate thin leaves and striking bright red berries. The small white flowers and one-seeded fruits distinguish it from other red-fruited plants.

Castor: Giant palmately-cut leaves whose stalk joins the blade in from the edge, plus soft-spined round capsules with mottled seeds, is castor.
