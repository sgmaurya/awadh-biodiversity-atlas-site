Black-crowned Night Heron or Grey Heron? रातबगुला या ग्रे बगुला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ग्रे बगुलाGrey HeronArdea cinerea

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey Heron, as the Black-crowned Night Heron profile puts it

Much larger; very long neck and legs; grey plumage; active primarily during the day

ग्रे बगुला — बहुत बड़ा, लंबा कद, दिन में शिकारी

Black-crowned Night Heron: Stocky, short-necked heron with a glossy black crown and back, grey wings, white belly, and large red eyes, roosting in groups during the day, is a Black-crowned Night Heron.

Grey Heron: Very tall grey-and-white wading bird with a black superciliary crest and a yellow dagger-like bill, standing motionless in shallow water, is a Grey Heron.
