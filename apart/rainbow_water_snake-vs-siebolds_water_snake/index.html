How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rainbow Water Snake, as the Siebold's Water Snake profile puts it

Rainbow Water Snake]])

Smaller (50–80 cm); bright yellow ventral surface with orange spots; keeled scales

Rainbow Water Snake: Any small water snake in shallow wetlands with a drab back and brilliantly yellow or orange-red belly is a Rainbow Water Snake. The color contrast is unmistakable.

Siebold's Water Snake: Any large, uniformly dark water snake in deep river channels is likely Siebold's Water Snake. The absence of stripes or checkering and the laterally compressed tail are the key identifiers.
