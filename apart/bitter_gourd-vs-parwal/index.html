Bitter Gourd or Pointed Gourd? करेला या परवल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

करेलाBitter GourdMomordica charantia
परवलPointed GourdTrichosanthes dioica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bitter Gourd, as the Pointed Gourd profile puts it

Smaller, bitter, warty fruits; leaves deeply lobed; strong bitter taste

करेला — कड़वा, ऊबड़ फल

Bitter Gourd: Slender climbing vine with deeply lobed leaves, yellow flowers, and warty green spindle-shaped fruits that split open to show red-coated seeds when orange, is the Bitter Gourd.

Pointed Gourd: A delicate climbing vine with heart-shaped leaves, dioecious white flowers, and distinctive elongate, pointed, dark green fruits with white longitudinal stripes; bears green fruits continuously from June to November, is parwal.
