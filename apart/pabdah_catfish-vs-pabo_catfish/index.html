How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pabdah Catfish, as the Pabo Catfish profile puts it

Pabdah Catfish]])

Lower jaw projects strongly beyond upper jaw; anal fin has 50–56 rays; maxillary barbels reach pectoral fin

Pabdah Catfish: An elongate, scaleless silurid catfish lacking an adipose fin, with an upturned lower jaw, an anal fin of 50–56 rays, and maxillary barbels reaching only to the pectoral fin, is Ompok pabda.

Pabo Catfish: An elongate, scaleless silurid catfish lacking an adipose fin, with an equal or included lower jaw, short maxillary barbels not extending past the eye, and an anal fin of 66–71 rays, is Ompok pabo.
