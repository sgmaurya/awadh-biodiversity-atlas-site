How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Patha, as the Broom Creeper profile puts it

Patha]] (Cissampelos pareira)

A related menispermaceous climber with peltate, velvety leaves and small greenish flowers; jal jamni's leaves are not peltate and its ripe berries are black

Broom Creeper: Soft, non-woody climber + soft-hairy, cordate leaves + inconspicuous tiny flowers + small red berries = Cocculus hirsutus. The softness of the entire plant (stem and leaf hairs) distinguishes it from woody climbers.

Patha: A soft-stemmed climber with distinctive large, round, velvety leaves that feel like cloth, twining over hedges and producing small black fruits is patha.
