How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Two-striped Pearl Moth, as the Small Emerald Moth profile puts it

Reddish-brown with large square clear transparent windows; snout moth

कत्थई-लाल रंग, पंखों पर दो बड़ी पारदर्शी खिड़कियां

Small Emerald Moth: A tiny pale emerald-green geometer moth with reddish-brown discal spots and chequered reddish dots along the outer wing margins identifies Comostola laesaria.

Two-striped Pearl Moth: Reddish-brown forewings bearing two large square glassy transparent windows combined with a mulberry or fig feeding habit identify Glyphodes bivitralis.

Forewing—Rich reddish-brown with two distinct square glassy hyaline patches
Hindwing—Ochraceous-brown with a large basal hyaline window and dark border
Abdomen—Slender brown abdomen, often bearing a small anal hair-tuft in males
Larva—Slender translucent green caterpillar feeding within folded leaves
