Adusa, as the Tube Flower profile puts it

Justicia adhatoda]]`

Large shrub with opposite lanceolate leaves, dense spikes of white two-lipped flowers with purple veins

Adusa: A sturdy shrub with large, glossy, opposite leaves and dense white-and-purple flower spikes is adusa.

Tube Flower: Look for the tall hollow stems, leaves in whorls of three to five, and exceptionally long slender corolla tubes with protruding purple stamens.
