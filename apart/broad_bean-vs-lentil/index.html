Broad Bean or Lentil? बाकला या मसूर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बाकलाBroad BeanVicia faba
मसूरLentilLens culinaris

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lentil, as the Broad Bean profile puts it

Shorter plant; flat lens-shaped seeds; different flowers; no black spots on flowers

चपटी दालें, छोटा पौधा

Broad Bean: A tall, sturdy winter legume with large white flowers bearing black spots and large thick pods containing large flattened seeds is broad bean.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Broad BeanLentil
PlantErect herbaceous, sturdy, 40–80 cm tall, unbranched or few branches—
LeavesPinnately compound, alternate, leaflets oblong, 2–5 cm; prominent tendrilsPinnately compound (2–5 cm long) with 4–7 pairs of narrow, oblong-linear leaflets (8–15 mm long); leaf axis ends in a short, unbranched tendril or bristle
FlowersLarge, white with black spots/patches, in short axillary clustersTiny (4–6 mm long), pale blue, lilac, or white, pea-like (papilionaceous) in structure, borne singly or in small clusters of 2–4 on long, slender stalks
PodsLarge, thick-walled, 10–15 cm long, green becoming dark brown at maturity, monopodial—
SeedsLarge, flattened, kidney-shaped, 12–15 mm, tan to brown with hilum lineFlat, biconvex (lens-shaped), 3–6 mm in diameter; seeds are grey-brown or greenish-black on the outside, and split open to reveal a bright orange-red or yellow interior
Form—Small, erect or spreading, delicate annual herb (20–45 cm tall), densely covered in fine soft hairs
Fruit—Short, flat, smooth, oblong pod (10–15 mm long, 6–8 mm wide), containing 1 or 2 lens-shaped seeds

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
