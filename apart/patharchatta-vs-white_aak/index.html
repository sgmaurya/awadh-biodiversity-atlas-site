Patharchatta or White Aak? पत्थरचट्टा या सफेद आक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

पत्थरचट्टाPatharchattaBryophyllum pinnatum
सफेद आकWhite AakCalotropis procera

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Aak, as the Patharchatta profile puts it

Leaves not compound; flowers pink with crown; much larger and more woody

बड़ा, गहरे हरे पत्ती

Patharchatta: A succulent with thick, glaucous, fleshy pinnate leaves and miniature plantlets at the leaf margins, in a wasteland or garden, is patharchatta.

White Aak: A soft, grey-blue, waxy-leaved shrub with pale pink or white waxy flowers and inflated green fruit pods, growing in dry wastelands, is white aak. The latex is toxic.
