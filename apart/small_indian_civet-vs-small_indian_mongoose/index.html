How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Indian Civet, as the Small Indian Mongoose profile puts it

Small Indian Civet]] (Viverricula indica)

Nocturnal viverrid with banded tail, dark body spots and longitudinal stripes; pointed ears

Small Indian Civet: Terrestrial habit (crawls low on the ground), pale yellow-grey coat with distinct black spots on the flanks, and a long tail with 6–8 alternating black and white rings, identify the Small Indian Civet.

Small Indian Mongoose: The diminutive slender build, finely punctated golden-brown coat, tapering non-flared tail, and diurnal foraging habits distinguish the Small Indian Mongoose from other small carnivores.
