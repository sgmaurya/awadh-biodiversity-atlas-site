Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Cormorant, as the Little Grebe profile puts it

Darker; longer neck; different diving technique; glossy plumage

काला पनकौवा

Little Cormorant: A small, all-black diving bird in freshwater that surfaces far from where it dived and perches with wings spread open — is a Little Cormorant. The glossy black colour and diving habit are definitive.

Little Grebe: The smallest grebe; a tiny compact brown-and-white diving bird with a loud wailing trill and backward leg placement — is a Little Grebe. The constant diving behaviour and inability to walk on land are distinctive.
