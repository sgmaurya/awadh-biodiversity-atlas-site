Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oyster Mushroom, as the False Parasol profile puts it

Oyster Mushroom]]** (Pleurotus spp.)

Fan-shaped cap; decurrent gills (running down stalk); off-centre stalk

False Parasol: Any large white or pale mushroom with green gills is a Chlorophyllum species and should be presumed toxic. To confirm: make a spore print on white paper; if the print is green, do not eat. Parasol mushroom has white gills and white spore print.
