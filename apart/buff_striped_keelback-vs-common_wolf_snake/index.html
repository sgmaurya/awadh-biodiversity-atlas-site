Buff-striped Keelback or Common Wolf Snake? सीता की लट या कौड़िया साँप?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Wolf Snake, as the Buff-striped Keelback profile puts it

Common Wolf Snake]] [REP-0012])

Strictly nocturnal; has broad white/cream crossbands (not longitudinal stripes); flat spatulate head; smooth shiny scales.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Buff-striped KeelbackCommon Wolf Snake
FormSlender, cylindrical body; head is distinct from the neck; tail is long and thinSlender, slightly flattened body; tail is moderate and tapers to a fine point
MarkingsTwo bright, cream-yellow or buff longitudinal stripes running down the back from neck to tail10–20 broad white or cream-coloured crossbands that start immediately behind the head/neck and expand on the sides
ColourOlive-green, greyish-brown, or bronze ground colour, often with black spots between the stripes—
ScalesStrongly keeled (ridged) dorsal scales, giving the skin a rough textureSmooth, shiny, and uniform on the back; lacks enlarged vertebral scales
HeadDistinctly defined, with large, symmetrical shields; snout is roundedFlat and spatulate (pear-shaped), scarcely wider than the neck; snout is broad and rounded
EyesLarge, with gold-flecked irises and round pupilsSmall, entirely black, with vertical slit-like pupils (often hard to see)
BehaviourExtremely gentle and non-aggressive; active during daylight hours near wet vegetationExcellent climber; found inside houses, on rafters, or climbing walls at night; mimics krait behaviour when cornered but does not hiss

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
