Common Leopard or Grey Pansy? कॉमन लेपर्ड तितली या ग्रे पैंसी तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ग्रे पैंसी तितलीGrey PansyJunonia atlites

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Leopard, as the Grey Pansy profile puts it

Brown with white spots; lacks prominent eyespots; orange markings on hindwing

कत्थई रंग, सफ़ेद धब्बे

Common Leopard: An orange-brown butterfly with 6–10 irregular black spots on the forewing and rapid, direct flight over open ground is the Common Leopard.

Grey Pansy: Greyish-brown butterfly with black eyespots (with pale centres) on the hindwing, rapid erratic flight, ground perching: Grey Pansy.
