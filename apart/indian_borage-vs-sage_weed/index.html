Indian Borage or Sage Weed? छोटा कुलफा या कमरकस?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sage Weed, as the Indian Borage profile puts it

Salvia plebeia]]

Aromatic herb with square stems, two-lipped small lilac flowers, and non-prickly foliage

Indian Borage, as the Sage Weed profile puts it

Trichodesma indicum]]

Coarse prickly weed with stinging hairs, nodding pale blue flowers with a woolly cone

Indian Borage: Harshly bristly foliage, nodding pale blue flowers with a prominent woolly staminal cone, and four glossy nutlets clearly distinguish Trichodesma indicum.

Sage Weed: Distinctly four-angled grooved stems, opposite wrinkled crenate leaves, and dense broom-like panicles of tiny two-lipped lilac flowers yielding mucilaginous seeds identify Salvia plebeia.
