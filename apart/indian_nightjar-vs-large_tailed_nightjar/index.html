How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Nightjar, as the Large-tailed Nightjar profile puts it

Indian Nightjar]] (Caprimulgus asiaticus) — if sympatric

Smaller; shorter tail; different call (churri-churri)

Indian Nightjar: Medium-sized cryptic brown bird + perches lengthwise on branch or ground + large eye + loud "chop-chop-chop" call at night + cryptic daytime behaviour = Indian Nightjar. The rhythmic call is unmistakable.

Large-tailed Nightjar: A large nightjar with a distinctly long tail, cryptic mottled plumage, found perched lengthwise on branches by day, and giving a loud chuck-chuck-chuck call at night is a Large-tailed Nightjar. The long tail is diagnostic.
