How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Least Pipistrelle, as the Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat profile puts it

Least Pipistrelle]] (Pipistrellus tenuis)

Tiny size; fluttering erratic flight; dull brownish-grey fur

Least Pipistrelle: India's smallest bat, no bigger than a large thumb, with fast fluttering flight often seen around lights is a Least Pipistrelle. If the bat is noticeably larger, it belongs to a different species.

Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat: The Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat is distinguished by its medium size, warm yellowish-brown fur, robust muzzle, and early, steady flight at twilight (Blanford 1888, p. 321).

ColourPale brown, greyish-brown, or rusty brown dorsally; paler ventrally—
WingsNarrow and pointed; relatively short for body size—
EarsSmall and simple; no prominent tragus—
FaceSmall and delicate; pug-nosed appearance—
FeetVery small; claws minute—
BehaviourFast, erratic flier; often seen fluttering around lights; very social—
Build & Size—Medium robust bat; head and body are 3 inches long, tail 2, forearm less than 2 inches (Blanford 1888)
Ears & Tragus—Short subtriangular ears, rounded tips; tragus long, narrow, curved forward and inward (Blanford 1888)
Dorsal Fur—Yellowish-brown above, varying to chestnut, golden brown, or greyish brown (Blanford 1888)
Ventral Fur—Pale dull buff, yellowish-grey, or dirty white below (Blanford 1888)
Flight Habit—Emerges early in twilight; flies more slowly and steadily than small pipistrelles (Blanford 1888)
