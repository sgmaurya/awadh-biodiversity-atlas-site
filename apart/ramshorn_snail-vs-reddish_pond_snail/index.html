Ramshorn Snail or Reddish Pond Snail? चपटा घोंघा या रेडिश पॉन्ड स्नेल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ramshorn Snail, as the Reddish Pond Snail profile puts it

Flat, disc-shaped, planorbid coil; blood red due to haemoglobin; no spire

चपटा गोल चक्र जैसा खोल, लाल खून

Ramshorn Snail: Flat, discoidal shell (~12–18 mm) resembling a coiled ram's horn, left-handed coiling, and a dark reddish-brown body with thread-like tentacles identify the Ramshorn Snail.

Reddish Pond Snail: A fragile, translucent reddish-amber ovate freshwater shell with a short acute spire and an enormous body whorl occupying most of the shell length identifies Radix rufescens.
