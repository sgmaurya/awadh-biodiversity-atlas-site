Egyptian Vulture or Griffon Vulture? सफ़ेद गिद्ध या ग्रिफॉन गिद्ध?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सफ़ेद गिद्धEgyptian VultureNeophron percnopterus
ग्रिफॉन गिद्धGriffon VultureGyps fulvus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Griffon Vulture, as the Egyptian Vulture profile puts it

Very large (94–110 cm); overall tawny-buff; naked head paler; appears tan in flight

गिद्ध — बहुत बड़ा, सुनहरा भूरा

Egyptian Vulture: Small vulture (smallest of all vultures in India) + predominantly white plumage + contrasting black flight feathers + pale bare yellow head and neck + buoyant soaring = Egyptian Vulture. No other small white raptor should be confused with it.

Griffon Vulture: The immense wingspan, sandy-tawny body plumage, contrasting dark flight feathers, and distinct white ruff around the neck distinguish the Griffon Vulture.
