How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Basker, as the Scarlet Marsh Hawk profile puts it

Noticeably larger; hindwing has two extensive dark reddish-brown basal patches; abdomen has black dorsal markings

Scarlet Basker: A robust blood-red dragonfly perching high over open water with broad dark reddish-brown basal patches on the hindwings and triangular black dorsal spots on abdominal segments 8 and 9 is the Scarlet Basker.

Scarlet Marsh Hawk: A compact, small dragonfly with an unspotted scarlet-red abdomen and a small, distinct amber-red patch restricted to the extreme base of each hindwing is the Scarlet Marsh Hawk.
