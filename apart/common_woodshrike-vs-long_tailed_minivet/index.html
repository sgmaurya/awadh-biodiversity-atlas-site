How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Woodshrike, as the Long-tailed Minivet profile puts it

Tephrodornis pondicerianus

Grey-brown woodshrike with dark eye-mask, white supercilium, and square tail with white edges

Common Woodshrike: The Common Woodshrike is a distinctive pied (black and white) bird with a stout bill and alert behaviour. The combination of white supercilium, black cap, and white patch on back is diagnostic.

Long-tailed Minivet: Slender canopy passerine with long graduated tail; male jet-black and scarlet with isolated inverted-U wing loop; female olive-grey with bright yellow forehead and throat.
