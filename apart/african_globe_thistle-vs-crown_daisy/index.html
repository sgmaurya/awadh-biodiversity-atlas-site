African Globe Thistle or Crown Daisy? गोरखमुंडी या गुलदाउदी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

African Globe Thistle, as the Crown Daisy profile puts it

Stems winged; flower heads spherical, purple, compact; leaves undivided

तने पर पंखदार किनारियां, गोल बैंगनी फूल

African Globe Thistle: Strongly aromatic annual herb with distinctly winged green stems and compact purple globular compound flower heads growing on drying alluvium identifies Sphaeranthus senegalensis.

Crown Daisy: Deeply twice-cut feathery leaves paired with large solitary daisy flower heads of bright yellow or bicoloured white-and-yellow identifies Glebionis coronaria.
