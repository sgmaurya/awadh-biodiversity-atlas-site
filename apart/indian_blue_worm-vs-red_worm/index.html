Indian Blue Worm or Red Worm? नीला केंचुआ या कम्पोस्ट केंचुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मिट्टी के जीव नीला केंचुआIndian Blue WormPerionyx excavatus
कम्पोस्ट केंचुआRed WormEisenia fetida

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Worm, as the Indian Blue Worm profile puts it

Reddish-brown with yellow stripes; slower-moving; survives in dry manure beds; cultivated in vermi-compost; larger

लाल-पीली धारी, धीमा, सूख भी सहता है

Indian Blue Worm, as the Red Worm profile puts it

Native to Asia; has a bluish-violet iridescent sheen; runs very fast; lacks yellow stripes; common in wild wet compost.

Indian Blue Worm: Small, fast, blue-violet iridescent worm in wet leaf litter = Indian blue worm. Reddish worm with yellow stripes in compost bed = red worm (Eisenia fetida).
