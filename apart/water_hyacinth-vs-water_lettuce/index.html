Water Hyacinth or Water Lettuce? जलकुंभी या जलपत्ता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जलकुंभीWater HyacinthEichhornia crassipes
जलपत्ताWater LettucePistia stratiotes

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Lettuce, as the Water Hyacinth profile puts it

Soft, velvety, pale green rosette; no inflated petiole; no flowers visible

जल पालक / जल गोभी

Water Hyacinth, as the Water Lettuce profile puts it

Much larger; glossy green leaves with swollen leaf stalks; showy violet flower spikes

जलकुंभी — सूजे हुए डंठल, बैंगनी फूल

Water Hyacinth: Floating mat + round shiny leaves + swollen spongy petioles + feathery purple-black roots = water hyacinth. The inflated petiole is unmistakable once seen.

Water Lettuce: Spongy, pale green, velvet-textured sessile leaves arranged in a tight cabbage-like floating rosette identify Pistia stratiotes.

Habitat Class—Free-floating aquatic plant; floats freely on the surface of standing or slow-moving water
