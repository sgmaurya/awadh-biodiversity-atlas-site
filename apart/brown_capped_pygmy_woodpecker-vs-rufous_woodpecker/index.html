How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker, as the Rufous Woodpecker profile puts it

Yungipicus nanus

Very tiny sparrow-sized woodpecker; brown crown and barred black-and-white mantle

Brown-capped Pygmy Woodpecker: Brown-capped Pygmy is the smallest woodpecker in the region with a brown cap (both sexes), white back with dark barring, and restless behaviour on thin twigs. No other species combines all these features at this size.

Rufous Woodpecker: Medium-sized woodpecker with entire body uniform warm rufous-chestnut finely barred with black, lacking any crown crest.

Tail structure—Short, stiff, wedge-shaped black-barred rufous tail used as climbing prop
Bill morphology—Short, stout, slightly curved blackish bill adapted for tearing ant carton
Nesting habits—Obligate symbiont excavating nesting cavities directly inside living acrobat ant nests
