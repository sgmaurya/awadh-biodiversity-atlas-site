Social Velvet Spider or Spiny Orb-weaver? सामूहिक मकड़ी या कंटकदार मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सामूहिक मकड़ीSocial Velvet SpiderStegodyphus sarasinorum
कंटकदार मकड़ीSpiny Orb-weaverGasteracantha kuhli

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spiny Orb-weaver, as the Social Velvet Spider profile puts it

Solitary spider; hard, flat body with 6 sharp spines; builds perfect, small vertical orb webs

कंटकदार मकड़ी — अकेली रहने वाली, शरीर पर कांटे

Social Velvet Spider, as the Spiny Orb-weaver profile puts it

Dull grey-brown body; lacks spines; lives in large social groups within sheet-webs

सामूहिक मकड़ी — मखमली भूरा शरीर, समूह में रहना

Social Velvet Spider: Dull grey-brown, velvety spider living with dozens of others inside a large, dense, shared silk sheet wrapped around thorny branches is the Social Velvet Spider.

Spiny Orb-weaver: Small, hard, flat-bodied spider (8–10 mm) with a white/yellow top bearing six sharp black spines, sitting in the center of a vertical orb web, is the Spiny Orb-weaver.

Side by side आमने-सामने

SizeSmall, robust spider; female body length 8–10 mm, male slightly smallerSmall spider; female body length 8–10 mm, male is tiny (3–4 mm) and lacks large spines
LegsShort, thick, and hairy, adapted for running inside the dense web tunnels—
Abdomen—Hard, leathery, wider than long, resembling a crab shell; dorsum is white or yellow with black spots and markings
Spines—Six sharp, black spines projecting from the margin: two on each side and two at the rear
Position—Sits directly in the open hub (center) of the web during the day
