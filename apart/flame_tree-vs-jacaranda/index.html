Flame Tree or Jacaranda? गुलमोहर या नीली गुलमोहर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Flame Tree, as the Jacaranda profile puts it

Red and yellow flowers; leaflets larger; different tree shape

लाल-पीले फूल; बड़ी पत्तियाँ

Flame Tree: Large tree with smooth grey bark, feathery bipinnate leaves, a wide flat canopy, brilliant orange-red flowers in summer, and very long, flat, sword-like woody pods, is the Flame Tree (Gulmohar).

Jacaranda: A tree with very fine, delicate, fern-like bipinnate leaves and brilliant blue to purple flowers appearing in spring before new leaves is Jacaranda.
