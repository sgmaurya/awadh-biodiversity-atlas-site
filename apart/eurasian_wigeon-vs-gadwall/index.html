Eurasian Wigeon or Gadwall? यूरेशियन विजन या गैडवॉल बत्तख?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gadwall, as the Eurasian Wigeon profile puts it

Males lack red head; females lack rufous head; different plumage pattern

नर का सिर लाल नहीं, फीका रंग

Eurasian Wigeon, as the Gadwall profile puts it

Males have rufous head and buff crown; females have rufous head; different plumage pattern

सिर लाल-भूरा, अलग पट्टी

Eurasian Wigeon: A dabbling duck with a chestnut-red head and buff crown (males) or rufous-brown head (females), with iridescent green wing patch in flight — is a Eurasian Wigeon. The head pattern of males is diagnostic and unmistakable.

Gadwall: A medium-sized dabbling duck with grey-and-black coloration (males) and white speculum visible in flight — is a Gadwall. In flight, the white wing patch is diagnostic.
