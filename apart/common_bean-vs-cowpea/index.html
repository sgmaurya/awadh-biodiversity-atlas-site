Common Bean or Cowpea? राजमा या लोबिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लोबियाCowpeaVigna unguiculata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cowpea, as the Common Bean profile puts it

Cowpea]] [FLR-0271])

Leaflets are narrower; pods are round and smooth; seeds are smaller and comma-shaped

Common Bean: A legume with three-leaflet compound leaves, papilionaceous flowers in clusters, and long straight pods containing kidney-shaped seeds of variable color distinguishes common bean.

Cowpea: A legume with long, slender pods and brown seeds bearing a characteristic white "eye" is cowpea.
