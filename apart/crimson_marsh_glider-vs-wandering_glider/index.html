Crimson Marsh Glider or Wandering Glider? लाल व्याधपतंग या सुनहरी व्याधपतंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लाल व्याधपतंगCrimson Marsh GliderTrithemis aurora
सुनहरी व्याधपतंगWandering GliderPantala flavescens

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wandering Glider, as the Crimson Marsh Glider profile puts it

Golden-yellow body; does not perch frequently; glides continuously over open spaces

Crimson Marsh Glider, as the Wandering Glider profile puts it

Male bright red, not golden; female yellow-brown; perches frequently

Crimson Marsh Glider: Medium size, pinkish-crimson body with purple venation at the wing bases, and frequent perching behavior identify the Crimson Marsh Glider.
