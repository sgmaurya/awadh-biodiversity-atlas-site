How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chalky Percher, as the Scarlet Marsh Hawk profile puts it

Chalky Percher]])

Mature males are covered in chalky-blue pruinescence, not scarlet-red

Chalky Percher: A small, compact dragonfly perching on exposed stick tips over water whose mature male has a brilliant chalky-white pruinose body, metallic green eyes, and small amber patches at the hindwing base is the Chalky Percher.

Scarlet Marsh Hawk: A compact, small dragonfly with an unspotted scarlet-red abdomen and a small, distinct amber-red patch restricted to the extreme base of each hindwing is the Scarlet Marsh Hawk.
