Nirgundi or Pencil Tree? निर्गुंडी या थूहर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

थूहरPencil TreeEuphorbia tirucalli

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Nirgundi, as the Pencil Tree profile puts it

Leaves larger and more persistent; no caustic latex; opposite arrangement; aromatic when crushed

पत्तियाँ बड़ी, पौधे में गंध

Nirgundi: A shrub with palmately compound, strongly aromatic leaves and violet-blue flowers in spikes, growing in poor ground, is nirgundi.

Pencil Tree: A pencil-thick-stemmed, minimally branching shrub with sparse tiny leaves and white caustic latex is pencil tree; no other common boundary plant has such characteristic pencil-like stems.
