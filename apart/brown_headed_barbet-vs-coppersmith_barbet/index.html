Brown-headed Barbet or Coppersmith Barbet? बड़ा बसंता या छोटा बसंता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coppersmith Barbet, as the Brown-headed Barbet profile puts it

Coppersmith Barbet]] (Psilopogon haemacephalus)

Much smaller; crimson forehead and breast patch, yellow throat, streaked underparts; the metallic tuk-tuk-tuk

Brown-headed Barbet, as the Coppersmith Barbet profile puts it

Brown-headed Barbet]] (Psilopogon zeylanicus)

Large (28 cm); brown head (not red); larger and less common

Brown-headed Barbet: Large green barbet with brown head and extremely thick bill. The large size, brown head, and loud calls in early morning distinguish it from all other barbets.

Coppersmith Barbet: Small stocky green bird with bright red head and red breast + repetitive "tuk-tuk-tuk" call = Coppersmith Barbet. The red head is diagnostic.
