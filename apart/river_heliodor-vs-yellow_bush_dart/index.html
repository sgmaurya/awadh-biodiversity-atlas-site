Stouter body; abdomen bright yellow and black; male has white expanded legs and black-tipped forewings

River Heliodor: A small, stoutly built damselfly of shaded running streams whose male has a bright golden-yellow-and-black abdomen, black-tipped forewings, and broad chalky-white legs used in courtship displays is the River Heliodor.

Yellow Bush Dart: An extremely slender, thread-like damselfly flitting weakly in deep shade near water with a dark bronzy body, a white tail tip, and conspicuously expanded bright golden-yellow legs is the Yellow Bush Dart.
