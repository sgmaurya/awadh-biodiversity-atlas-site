Asian Koel or Pied Cuckoo? कोयल या चातक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कोयलAsian KoelEudynamys scolopaceus
चातकPied CuckooClamator jacobinus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Koel, as the Pied Cuckoo profile puts it

Male all glossy black (no white patch); red eye; larger; resident (not migrant)

कोयल — सब काली, साल भर

Pied Cuckoo: Large black-and-white bird + loud "piu-piu-piu" wailing call + arrives with monsoon + fruit-eating = Pied Cuckoo. The call is diagnostic and unforgettable.
