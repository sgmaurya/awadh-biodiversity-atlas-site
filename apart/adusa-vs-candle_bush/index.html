Adusa or Candle Bush? अडूसा या दादमर्दन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दादमर्दनCandle BushSenna alata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Adusa, as the Candle Bush profile puts it

Smaller inflorescence; white flowers with purple spots; smaller leaves

अडूसा — सफेद फूल, छोटी बाली

Adusa: A sturdy shrub with large, glossy, opposite leaves and dense white-and-purple flower spikes is adusa.

Candle Bush: A shrub with compound leaves and a distinctly yellow, candle-shaped flower spike is Senna alata.
