How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Emigrant, as the Common Jezebel profile puts it

Common Emigrant]] (Catopsilia pomona)

Smaller; pale yellow or white; underside lacks the distinctive red spot pattern

Common Emigrant: Medium size, rapid straight flight, uniform lemon-yellow or greenish-white wings, and presence of pink spots (in pomona form) or clear wings (in crocale form) on underwings identify the Common Emigrant.

Common Jezebel: A white butterfly with black margins on the forewing and narrow black margin on the hindwing, with striking orange-red spots on the underside, is the Common Jezebel.
