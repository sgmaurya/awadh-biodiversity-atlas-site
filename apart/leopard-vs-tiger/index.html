Leopard or Tiger? तेंदुआ या बाघ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तेंदुआLeopardPanthera pardus
बाघTigerPanthera tigris

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tiger, as the Leopard profile puts it

Tiger]] (Panthera tigris)

Substantially larger; reddish-orange coat with bold vertical black stripes (not rosettes); does not haul prey into trees

Leopard, as the Tiger profile puts it

Leopard]] (Panthera pardus)

Considerably smaller; golden-yellow coat marked with rosettes rather than vertical stripes; climbs trees readily

Leopard: The medium-to-large feline build, rich golden-yellow coat densely patterned with black rose-like rosettes, and white ear-spots identify the Leopard with certainty.

Tiger: The immense body size, bright orange coat patterned with bold vertical black stripes, and gigantic rounded pugmarks distinguish the Tiger from all other Asian felids.
