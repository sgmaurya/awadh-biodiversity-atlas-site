Common Redshank or Wood Sandpiper? कॉमन रेडशैंक या वुड सैंडपाइपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Sandpiper, as the Common Redshank profile puts it

Smaller; spotted upperparts; greenish legs; white rump with darker sides

वुड सैंडपाइपर

Common Redshank, as the Wood Sandpiper profile puts it

Much larger; bright red legs and bill base

रेडशैंक

Common Redshank: A medium wader with unmistakable bright red legs and reddish-orange bill, with white rump visible in flight, probing in mud — is a Common Redshank. The red legs are diagnostic. Greenshanks are paler and larger with greenish legs.

Wood Sandpiper: A spotted wader with yellowish legs and pale underwings, in small flocks at shallow wetlands — is a Wood Sandpiper. The bold spotting and yellow legs distinguish it from Green Sandpiper. Paler and more spotted than Green Sandpiper.
