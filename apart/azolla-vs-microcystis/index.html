How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Fern, as the Microcystis profile puts it

Tiny floating fern with distinct lobed leaves; forms a mat; non-toxic

छोटी पत्तियों वाली पानी की फर्न

Water Fern: Tiny floating triangular aquatic fern (1.0–2.5 cm) forming green-red mosaic mats on water, with branched feather-like leaves and hanging hair-like roots, is the Water Fern (Azolla).

Microcystis: Smooth, cloudy, pea-green to blue-green water with earthy or foul smell, starting in May and persisting through October, in eutrophic ponds = likely Microcystis bloom. Warn people and livestock.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Water FernMicrocystis
Plant habitTiny, floating, branched annual or perennial aquatic fern, measuring 1.0–2.5 cm, forming dense velvet mats—
Fronds (Leaves)Scale-like, arranged in two overlapping rows; green in winter/shade, turning dark brick-red in hot sunlight—
RootsSimple, unbranched, hair-like roots (1–3 cm) hanging freely in the water column below the leaves—
Spore productionNon-flowering; reproduces via spores contained in tiny globular structures (sporocarps) under the leaves—
SymbiosisHas a symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing cyanobacteria inside leaf cavities—
Appearance—Cloudy, opaque water; pea-green to bright green to blue-green or olive-brown colour
Texture—Smooth, slimy film or suspension; not individual filaments visible to the eye
Smell—Earthy, musty smell (resembles clean algae) OR foul, rotten smell (indicates toxin production)
Distribution—Entire water body clouded; concentrates at surface where wind pushes it; can form a scum at edges
Scum—A slimy, sticky layer or mat may form on the water surface; bright green or blue-green colour; does not float like water hyacinth
Particle size—Microscopic; individual colonies 10–150 µm (not visible to naked eye but visible as a suspension)
No filaments—Unlike Nostoc (which forms distinct jelly blobs) or Spirogyra (which forms threads), Microcystis is a cloudy suspension

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
