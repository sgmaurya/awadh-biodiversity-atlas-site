Blue Billygoat Weed or Congress Grass? नीला अजगन्धा या गाजर घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीला अजगन्धाBlue Billygoat WeedAgeratum houstonianum
गाजर घासCongress GrassParthenium hysterophorus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Congress Grass, as the Blue Billygoat Weed profile puts it

Leaves deeply dissected; flower heads tiny, white; stems grooved

गाजर घास, पत्तियाँ कटी हुई, नन्हे सफेद फूल

Blue Billygoat Weed: Softly hairy herb with glandular-acuminate involucral bracts, deltoid-ovate leaves, and dense fluffy heads of bright lavender-blue florets identifies Ageratum houstonianum.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Blue Billygoat WeedCongress Grass
HabitErect or decumbent hairy annual herb 25–80 cm tall (Gaur 1999)—
FoliageOpposite, deltoid-ovate, coarsely crenate-serrate, covered in soft hairs (Duthie 1903)—
CapitulaDense terminal corymbs of fluffy lavender-blue or mauve heads (Gaur 1999)—
InvolucreBracts narrow, linear-lanceolate, densely covered with sticky glandular hairs (Gaur 1999)—
OdourDistinct coumarin-like or goat-like musky scent emitted when bruised—
PappusComposed of 5 aristate scales with slender awns exceeding the achene (Hooker 1881)—
Leaves—Deeply pinnately lobed like carrot leaves — hence gajar ghaas; soft and hairy; light grey-green
Stem—Green, highly branched, covered in white hairs; woody at base in older plants
Height—30 cm to 1.5 m depending on soil and moisture
Flowers—Tiny white heads (2–3 mm) in flat-topped clusters; blooms nearly year-round
Smell—Mild unpleasant odour when leaves are crushed
Seeds—Tiny, black, wedge-shaped; one plant produces 10,000–25,000 per season
Habitat—Roadsides, disturbed land, field margins — never in intact native vegetation

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
