How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Skimmer, as the Common Picture Wing profile puts it

Scarlet Skimmer]] (Crocothemis servilia)

Larger; uniform bright red body; wings lack the distinctive pattern of dark and light patches

Common Picture Wing: Small dragonfly with distinctly patterned wings showing dark and light patches, perching low on vegetation near shallow water, is the Common Picture Wing.

Scarlet Skimmer: Medium-large size, uniform scarlet-red body with a flat, broad abdomen, thin black dorsal line, and lack of dark wing patches identify the Scarlet Skimmer.
