Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk or Long-legged Marsh Skimmer? क्रिमसन-टेल्ड मार्श हॉक या लॉन्ग-लेग्ड मार्श स्किमर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk, as the Long-legged Marsh Skimmer profile puts it

Pale pinkish, not purple-blue; shorter legs; different tail colour

लाल पूँछ व्याधपतंग

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk: Medium dragonfly with crimson-red abdomen tip and male body appearing pale pinkish-white due to pruinose coating distinguishes this species.

Long-legged Marsh Skimmer: Small to medium dragonfly with conspicuously long legs (diagnostic) and purple-blue or reddish body colour distinguishes this marsh skimmer.
