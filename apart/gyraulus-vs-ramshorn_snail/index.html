How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Ramshorn Snail, as the Ramshorn Snail profile puts it

Much smaller shell diameter (4–6 mm); thin, transparent whorls; pale yellow-brown

छोटा चपटा घोंघा — अत्यंत छोटा आकार

Small Ramshorn Snail: An extremely small (5–8 mm), flattened, disc-shaped snail on aquatic plants in ponds and ditches is a small ramshorn snail. It is an intermediate host for parasitic flukes; its presence may indicate disease risk for livestock and, rarely, humans.

Ramshorn Snail: Flat, discoidal shell (~12–18 mm) resembling a coiled ram's horn, left-handed coiling, and a dark reddish-brown body with thread-like tentacles identify the Ramshorn Snail.
