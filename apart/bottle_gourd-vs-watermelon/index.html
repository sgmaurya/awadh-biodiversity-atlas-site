Bottle Gourd or Watermelon? लौकी या तरबूज?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

तरबूजWatermelonCitrullus lanatus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bottle Gourd, as the Watermelon profile puts it

Flowers white, nocturnal; fruit bottle-shaped; rind becomes woody

लौकी — सफेद फूल, बोतल सा फल, कड़ा छिलका

Bottle Gourd: Climbing annual vine with broad velvety kidney-shaped leaves, large nocturnal white flowers, and elongated or bottle-shaped pale green edible fruits that dry into hard woody shells, is the Bottle Gourd (Lauki).

Watermelon: A sprawling vine with large deeply lobed leaves, large bright yellow flowers, and huge smooth striped melons with red juicy flesh is watermelon; it is the most common summer fruit in village fields.
