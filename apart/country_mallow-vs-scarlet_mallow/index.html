How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Country Mallow, as the Scarlet Mallow profile puts it

Country Mallow]] (Sida cordifolia)

Heart-shaped leaves, much smaller yellow flowers, not aquatic

Country Mallow: Country Mallow has heart-shaped (cordate) leaves and yellow flowers. Caesar Weed has lobed leaves and pink flowers.

Scarlet Mallow: Scarlet Mallow is unmistakable: large soft herb with scarlet five-petaled flowers, found only in wet ground. No other Malvaceae in eastern UP has such brilliant red flowers.
