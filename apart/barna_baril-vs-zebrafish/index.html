How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Zebrafish, as the Barna Baril profile puts it

Danio rerio

Small minnow with distinct horizontal blue and gold stripes; completely lacks vertical crossbars

Barna Baril: Slender silvery river fish with 7 to 11 dark blue vertical bars, complete absence of barbels, and black-edged dorsal and caudal fins.

Zebrafish: A small silvery fish with 7–8 sharp dark vertical stripes from head to tail, in rapid schools in monsoon water, is the zebrafish; the stripes are its most certain mark.
