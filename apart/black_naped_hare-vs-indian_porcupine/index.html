Black-naped Hare or Indian Crested Porcupine? खरगोश या साही?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

स्तनधारी खरगोशBlack-naped HareLepus nigricollis
साहीIndian Crested PorcupineHystrix indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Crested Porcupine, as the Black-naped Hare profile puts it

Covered in long, sharp black-and-white quills; much larger and heavier; nocturnal rodent

साही — कांटेदार शरीर, बड़ा आकार

Black-naped Hare: Long ears, grizzled rufous-brown coat, long hind legs, and a distinctive black patch on the back of the neck distinguish the Black-naped Hare from other lagomorphs.

Indian Crested Porcupine: Large rodent body covered in long black-and-white banded quills, a crest of thin white bristles on the head, and tail tipped with hollow rattle quills, identify Hystrix indica.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Black-naped HareIndian Crested Porcupine
Size & ShapeMedium-sized mammal; body length 40–60 cm, weight 1.5–3.5 kg; long hind legs adapted for fast leaping—
ColourRufous-brown fur mixed with black on the back, white underparts; a distinct dark black patch on the back of the neck (nape)—
Head & EarsLarge head with long, erect ears (up to 10 cm) tipped with black; large, dark brown eyes situated laterally—
TailShort, bushy tail, rufous-brown above and white below, held down when runningShort, fitted with specialized short, hollow "rattle quills" at the tip that clatter when shaken
LimbsHind limbs are significantly longer and stronger than forelimbs, allowing high-speed bounds—
Form—Large, heavy-bodied rodent with a stocky build and short, strong clawed limbs
Quills—Covered in long, sharp, rigid black-and-white banded quills (up to 30 cm long) on the back and tail
Crest—A prominent crest of long, thin, soft white-tipped bristles extending from the crown of the head to the upper back
Activity—Strictly nocturnal; lives in deep, self-excavated underground burrow networks
