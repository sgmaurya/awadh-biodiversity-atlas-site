Black-rumped Flameback]] (Dinopium benghalense)

Golden-yellow mantle, boldly patterned black-and-white head with crimson crest, and black rump

Black-rumped Flameback: Small golden-yellow woodpecker with black rump + rapid drumming + sharp "kriek" call = Black-rumped Flameback. The black rump is diagnostic.

Grey-headed Woodpecker: The crimson crown and black nape in the male, black and grey crest and nape in the female, dull olive-green underparts, and bright yellow-green rump distinguish the Grey-headed Woodpecker (Baker 1927).
