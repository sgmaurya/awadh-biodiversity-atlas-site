Rust-colored Flatid or Variegated Spittlebug? कत्थई चपटा फुदका या रंग-बिरंगा थूक कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Variegated Spittlebug, as the Rust-colored Flatid profile puts it

Jet-black with white and orange spots; bright red legs; leaping bug

चमकदार काला शरीर, नारंगी-सफेद चकत्ते

Rust-colored Flatid, as the Variegated Spittlebug profile puts it

Uniform rust-brown; wings held vertically tent-like; no bright leg color

पूरा शरीर कत्थई-भूरा, पंख छत की तरह खड़े

Rust-colored Flatid: Broad wedge-shaped, moth-like planthopper with uniform rust-brown wings held vertically tent-like over the body and densely reticulate costal margins identifies Flata ferrugata.

Variegated Spittlebug: Medium-sized jumping froghopper with shining jet-black wings boldly spotted with orange and creamy-white, contrasting with bright orange-red legs identifies Callitettix versicolor.
