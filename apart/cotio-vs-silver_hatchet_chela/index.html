How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Body rhomboidal; dorsal fin has a serrated spine; anal fin has 29–36 rays

Cotio: A deep-bodied, laterally compressed silvery cyprinid with a serrated bony dorsal spine and an extraordinarily long anal fin of over 28 rays is Osteobrama cotio.

Silver Hatchet Chela: A tiny, deep, hatchet-shaped silvery cyprinid with an acute cutting ventral keel extending continuously from throat to vent, and a filamentous trailing pelvic fin ray, is Chela cachius.
