How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Honey Buzzard, as the Changeable Hawk-Eagle profile puts it

Pernis ptilorhynchus

Small pigeon-like head; bare scaly yellow tarsi lacking plumage; distinctive tail barring

Changeable Hawk-Eagle: Woodland eagle with upright posture, long slender black crest, streaked breast, and densely feathered tarsi down to toes.

Oriental Honey Buzzard: Oriental Honey Buzzard's distinctive erectile crest and small hooked bill with bare frontal shield are diagnostic. Watch for it hunting near wasp and bee nests. The small bill (compared to other eagles) is distinctive.
