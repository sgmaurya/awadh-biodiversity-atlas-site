Vetiver or Marvel Grass? खस या मार्वल घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

खसVetiverChrysopogon zizanioides
मार्वल घासMarvel GrassDichanthium annulatum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Vetiver, as the Marvel Grass profile puts it

Much taller robust tussock; leaves stiff and spongy; large purple pyramidal panicle

बहुत लंबी खस घास, बड़ी बैंगनी बाली

Vetiver: A tall non-spreading clump grass with a loose purplish plume whose roots smell of khus is vetiver; no other grass of the district has scented roots.

Marvel Grass: Tufted perennial grass with a conspicuous ring of white hairs at each stem node and subdigitate silky purplish racemes with fine twisted awns identifies Dichanthium annulatum.

Side by side आमने-सामने

VetiverMarvel Grass
HabitDense, erect, clump-forming grass 1–2 m, clumps up to a metre across, never spreading by runnersTufted perennial grass 30–100 cm tall, forming dense leafy tussocks (Bor 1960)
LeavesLong, narrow, stiff, 45–100 cm × 1 cm, folded at the base, rough-edged, green turning strawLinear, 5–30 cm long, 3–6 mm wide, glaucous green, scabrid on the margins (Duthie 1888)
StemsUpright, smooth, with a pithy core—
Flower headAn open, oblong panicle 15–40 cm of many whorled branches, purple-brown to grey, August–November—
SpikeletsIn pairs, one stalked, with short awns and a bristly base—
RootsThe clue: massive, spongy, straight-down roots that smell of khus when crushed or dried—
Culms—Erect or geniculately ascending, smooth; nodes densely bearded with white hairs (Bor 1960)
Ligule—Short, membranous, ciliate, 1–2 mm long (Bor 1960)
Inflorescence—Subdigitate panicle of 2–9 purplish-pink racemes 3–7 cm long clustered at culm apex (Bor 1960)
Awns—Slender, twisted, geniculate, 1.5–2.5 cm long, emerging from fertile spikelets (Bor 1960)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
