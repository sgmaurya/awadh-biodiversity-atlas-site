Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Minivet, as the Long-tailed Minivet profile puts it

Pericrocotus cinnamomeus

Much smaller body length; male has grey upperparts and orange wash; common resident of open scrub

Long-tailed Minivet: Slender canopy passerine with long graduated tail; male jet-black and scarlet with isolated inverted-U wing loop; female olive-grey with bright yellow forehead and throat.

Small Minivet: The smallest minivet; males striking black-and-orange, females dull grey-and-yellow. Always in organized flocks with soft contact calls. Active gleaners in trees; coordinated flock movement distinctive.
