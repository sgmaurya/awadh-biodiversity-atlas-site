Common Coot or Grey-headed Swamphen? लालखर या कैम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लालखरCommon CootFulica atra
कैमGrey-headed SwamphenPorphyrio poliocephalus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-headed Swamphen, as the Common Coot profile puts it

Much larger; bright purple-blue body; massive red bill and legs; lives in reeds

कैम — बहुत बड़ा आकार, चमकीला नीला रंग, बड़ी लाल चोंच

Common Coot: Entirely soot-black waterbird with a distinctive white bill and white frontal shield, swimming in open water and diving frequently, is a Common Coot.

Grey-headed Swamphen: Bright purplish-blue body, pale greyish head, massive red bill and frontal shield, and long pinkish-red legs identify the Grey-headed Swamphen.

Bare PartsEyes are bright red; legs are greenish-grey; toes have large, scallop-shaped swimming lobes—
Take-offSkitters across the water surface with rapid wingbeats before taking flight—
SwimmingSwims in open water, often bobbing its head; dives completely under water for weeds—
Bill & Shield—Extremely heavy, thick red bill and a bright crimson-red frontal shield on the forehead
Legs—Long, pale red to pinkish-red legs and very long, slender toes; lacks webbing
Tail—Short, flicking tail showing white under-tail coverts when walking
