Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Swamp Deer, as the Greater One-horned Rhinoceros profile puts it

Swamp Deer]] (Rucervus duvaucelii)

Large cervid with branched antlers and slender hooves; co-occurs in tall grasslands

Greater One-horned Rhinoceros: The massive armour-plated body with deep skin folds, three-toed feet, and single snout horn distinguish the Greater One-horned Rhinoceros from all other Asian megafauna.

Swamp Deer: The large size, bright golden-rufous summer coat, splayed wetland hooves, and particularly the adult stag's intricate crown of ten to twelve or more antler tines distinguish the Swamp Deer from all others.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Greater One-horned RhinocerosSwamp Deer
HabitEnormous, solitary, semi-aquatic megaherbivore of alluvial floodplainsLarge, gregarious wetland specialist deer of floodplains and reed swamps
HornSingle keratinous horn situated on the snout above the nostrils—
SkinThick, armour-plated grey hide divided into massive shields by deep folds—
TuberclesRaised rounded tubercle-like bosses studding the flanks, shoulders, and rump—
LimbsColumnar, pillar-like legs terminating in three hoofed toes—
Upper LipSemi-prehensile, pointed upper lip adapted for grasping tall grasses and browse—
TailModerately slender tail with stiff lateral bristles along the terminal margins—
Coat in Winter—Rich yellowish-brown or sandy-buff above, paler on belly and throat
Coat in Summer—Bright rufous-brown, often marked with faint white spots along spine
Antlers—Spectacular multi-tined crowns in stags, typically bearing ten to fourteen points
Hooves—Splayed, broadened hooves adapted for walking across waterlogged marshland
Underparts—Whitish under chin, throat, belly, and ventral surface of tail
Demeanour—Wades deeply into swamps and grazes while submerged to the belly

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
