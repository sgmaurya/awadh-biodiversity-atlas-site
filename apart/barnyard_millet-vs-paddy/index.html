Barnyard Millet or Paddy? साँवा या धान?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

साँवाBarnyard MilletEchinochloa frumentacea
धानPaddyOryza sativa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddy, as the Barnyard Millet profile puts it

Taller (90–150 cm), more delicate; panicle drooping; grain larger (6–8 mm)

चावल — लंबा, नाज़ुक; बाली झूलती है; दाना बड़ा

Barnyard Millet: A robust monsoon grass with broad blue-green leaves, a thick golden bristly seed head, and small oval grain; grows in wet fields July to November.

Paddy: Flooded field + hollow jointed stems + auricles at leaf sheath junction + drooping grain head = rice. Barnyard grass (savan) is the most common lookalike but has no auricles and a different seed arrangement.
