How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tawny Coster, as the Painted Lady profile puts it

Tawny Coster]])

Smaller; orange without black patches; different flight

Painted Lady: Medium-sized butterfly with distinctive reddish-orange upperwing marked with black and white patches; fast, erratic flight; seen on thistles and flowers year-round.

Tawny Coster: Medium size, translucent orange wings with black spots and a broad black margin on hindwings, and very slow low flight close to the grass identify the Tawny Coster.
