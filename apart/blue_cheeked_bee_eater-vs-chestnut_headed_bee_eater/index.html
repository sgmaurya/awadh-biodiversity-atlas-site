Blue-cheeked Bee-eater or Chestnut-headed Bee-eater? ब्लू-चीक्ड बी-ईटर या चेस्टनट-हेडेड बी-ईटर?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chestnut-headed Bee-eater, as the Blue-cheeked Bee-eater profile puts it

Chestnut head; different coloration; no turquoise cheeks

शाहबलूत सिर मधु-चिड़िया

Blue-cheeked Bee-eater, as the Chestnut-headed Bee-eater profile puts it

Merops persicus

Blue cheeks and yellow-and-chestnut throat; green crown and mantle; long central tail pins

Blue-cheeked Bee-eater: Blue-cheeked Bee-eater shows turquoise-blue cheeks, green back, and elongated tail streamers. If the bird is smaller without tail streamers and has a golden-yellow throat, it is a Green Bee-eater. If the crown is chestnut or the bird is red overall, it is a different species.

Chestnut-headed Bee-eater: Small colorful bee-eater with rich chestnut crown and back, yellow throat, azure rump, and completely square-ended tail with no pins.

Rump colour—Vibrant azure-blue lower back and rump visible in flight
Bill structure—Long, slender, gently decurved black bill suited for snatching flying insects
