Catla or Mrigal? कतला या मृगल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कतलाCatlaCatla catla
मृगलMrigalCirrhinus mrigala

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mrigal, as the Catla profile puts it

Pointed snout; small inferior mouth; bottom feeder; cylindrical body

Catla, as the Mrigal profile puts it

Much larger head; upward-directed mouth; deep body; surface feeder

कतला — बड़ा सिर, ऊपर मुँह

Mrigal: Streamlined silver body, downward-pointing mouth with thin smooth lips, and yellowish-orange pelvic/anal fins identify the Mrigal.
