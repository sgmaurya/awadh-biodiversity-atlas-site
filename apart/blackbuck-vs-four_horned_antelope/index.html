Blackbuck or Four-horned Antelope? काला हिरण या चौसिंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Four-horned Antelope, as the Blackbuck profile puts it

Four-horned Antelope]] (Tetracerus quadricornis)

Smaller size; dull rufous-brown coat; males possess four short, smooth horns

Blackbuck, as the Four-horned Antelope profile puts it

Blackbuck]] (Antilope cervicapra)

Larger size; adult males black-and-white with long spiralled horns; inhabits open plains

Blackbuck: Mature male Blackbuck are instantly identified by long spiralled horns and striking black-and-white pelage, whereas does are slender, unhorned, and yellowish-fawn (Blanford 1891, p. 522).

Four-horned Antelope: Solitary habits, small size, dull pale brown pelage, with a more or less rufous tinge above, and four horn points in adult males distinguish the species; Blanford (1891, p. 520) noted: "In jungle this species and hog-deer (Cervus porcinus) may easily be mistaken the one for the other".
