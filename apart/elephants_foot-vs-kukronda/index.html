Elephant's Foot or Kukronda? एलीफेंट्स फुट या कुकरौंधा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kukronda, as the Elephant's Foot profile puts it

Blumea lacera

Erect aromatic glandular herb without basal rosette; yellow discoid flower heads lacking cordate bracts

Elephant's Foot: Harsh scabrid forest herb with ground-hugging cabbage-like basal leaf rosette, wiry branched stems, and purple flowers subtended by three cordate bracts.

Kukronda: Strongly aromatic (camphoraceous odour) herb + rough-textured, sticky leaves + dense, flat-topped cluster of bright yellow, tiny flowers = Blumea lacera. Crush a leaf — the characteristic sharp odour is unmistakable and diagnostic.
