Job's Tears or Jungle Rice? जॉब्स टियर्स घास या सावाँ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Rice, as the Job's Tears profile puts it

Smaller grass; leaves narrower; spikelets in 4-rowed unawned racemes; no beads

साँवाँ, छोटे बिना मोतियों वाले दाने

Job's Tears: Robust maize-like wetland grass bearing hard, shiny, pear-shaped or globose stony beads out of which dangle male flower spikes identifies Coix lacryma-jobi.

Jungle Rice: A hairy-leaved, medium-sized grass in wet places, with small spiky spikelets that cling to cloth, and grain 2–3 mm, maturing in 90–120 days = Echinochloa colona (jungle rice).
