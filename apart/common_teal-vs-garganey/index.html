Common Teal or Garganey? छोटी मुर्गाबी या गार्गनी बत्तख?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गार्गनी बत्तखGarganeySpatula querquedula

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Teal, as the Garganey profile puts it

Male has a dark green patch on a chestnut head; female is very similar but lacks the bold eye-stripes

छोटी बत्तख — हरे रंग का सिर, सफ़ेद भौंह का अभाव

Common Teal: Common Teal is the smallest winter duck, with males showing a chestnut head and green eye-stripe. If the duck is larger and the bill spatulate (spoon-like), it is a Shoveler. If the head is glossy green (not chestnut), it is a Mallard.

Garganey: Small, teal-sized duck with a chocolate-brown head, a bold curved white stripe (eyebrow) behind the eye, and a fast, twisting flight in flocks is a Garganey.
