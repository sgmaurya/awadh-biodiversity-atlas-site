How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tropical Tent-web Spider, as the Theis's Orb-weaver profile puts it

Cyrtophora citricola

Highly tuberculated abdomen with prominent bifurcated humps; non-sticky horizontal tent-web

Theis's Orb-weaver: Nocturnal orb-weaver with pale creamy-yellow wavy folium along dorsal midline of abdomen and two yellow comma spots beneath.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Theis's Orb-weaverTropical Tent-web Spider
Abdomen patternConspicuous creamy-yellow median longitudinal band (folium) bordered by wavy black lines—
Ventral markingsDeep blackish patch enclosed between two distinct bright yellow comma-shaped spots—
CarapaceYellowish-brown to reddish-brown with a narrow dark brown median line—
LegsYellowish-brown, heavily banded with dark brown or blackish annulations and stiff spines—
Web habitConstructs a large vertical sticky orb web at dusk and rests in a curled leaf retreat by day—
Form—Stout, humped body; abdomen has a series of pairs of prominent tubercles (humps) on the back
Colour—Highly variable; ground colour ranges from grey, dark brown, to almost black, with white or yellowish spots
Female Size—Medium-sized; body length 10.0–15.0 mm
Male Size—Tiny; body length 2.5–3.5 mm (often goes unnoticed at the edge of the female's web)
Web Structure—A horizontal, dense mesh sheet (dome) suspended by an irregular vertical scaffolding of silk lines above and below
Colony—Semi-social behavior; multiple females build individual webs within a shared framework of support silk

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
