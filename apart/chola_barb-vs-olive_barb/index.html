How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chola Barb, as the Olive Barb profile puts it

Chola Barb]])

Bears only two maxillary barbels; distinct dark spot on base of dorsal and caudal fins

Chola Barb: A small barb (8–12 cm) with a dark spot at the base of the tail, found in schools in ponds and rivers, is a Chola Barb.

Olive Barb: A large, robust silvery-olive barb with four distinct barbels and a stout serrated dorsal spine, lacking bold isolated black spots on the flanks, is Systomus sarana.
