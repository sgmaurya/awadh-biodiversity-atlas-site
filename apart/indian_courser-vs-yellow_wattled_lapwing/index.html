Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-wattled Lapwing, as the Indian Courser profile puts it

Yellow-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus malabaricus)

Conspicuous bright yellow facial wattles, black cap, yellow legs, and loud alarm calls when taking flight

Indian Courser: The rich rufous crown, black-and-white eye stripes, chestnut underparts with black belly patch, and ivory-white legs readily identify the Indian Courser (Baker 1929).

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
