How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian House Gecko, as the Kushmore's Gecko profile puts it

Hemidactylus frenatus

Possesses very small, sparsely scattered dorsal tubercles and distinct reddish or brownish tail bands

Asian House Gecko: Smooth pinkish-grey skin, split digital lamellae under the toes, and a tail with small spines, combined with a loud chik-chik-chik nocturnal call, identify Hemidactylus frenatus.

Kushmore's Gecko: Small sandy-coloured gecko with 16 to 18 rows of moderate keeled tubercles inhabiting dry rural masonry and earth banks rather than polished domestic walls.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Asian House GeckoKushmore's Gecko
FormSmall, flattened body with a broad head; skin is smooth with small, scattered round tubercles on the back—
ColorationPale pinkish-grey, tan, or yellowish-white; can change shade slightly to match surroundingsPale sandy grey to light yellowish-brown with irregular dark undulating bands
FeetExpanded toe pads (subdigital lamellae) split in the middle; clawed digits for vertical climbing—
TailSlender, cylindrical, with small whorls of spines; easily detached and regeneratedCylindrical, tapering, bearing distinct whorls of small pointed tubercles
VocalisationA loud, repetitive chirping or clicking sound like chik-chik-chik, produced in rapid succession—
Body size—Small, slender gecko with a depressed head and body
Dorsal scalation—Granular scales interspersed with 16 to 18 rows of small trihedral tubercles (Smith 1935)
Digits—Free, moderately dilated, with divided adhesive scansors underneath
Ventral surface—Whitish to cream, covered with smooth imbricate scales
