Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purple Heron, as the Eurasian Bittern profile puts it

Purple Heron]] (Ardea purpurea)

Much longer and slender neck with black longitudinal stripes, narrower dagger bill, chestnut-purple underparts (Baker 1929, p. 337)

Eurasian Bittern: The heavy golden-buff body with bold black vermiculations, black crown, and barred wings distinguish the Eurasian Bittern (Baker 1929, pp. 370–371).

Purple Heron: Large, slender wading bird with a dark reddish-chestnut and grey body, thin snake-like neck with black stripes, hiding in dense reeds, is a Purple Heron.

Size—Large, slender wading bird (78–90 cm total length); wingspan around 120–140 cm
Colouration—Dark reddish-chestnut and dark grey body; wings are slate-grey; belly is blackish-brown
Neck—Extremely thin, long, snake-like neck with prominent black and rufous longitudinal stripes
Head Pattern—Black crown with a thin black crest; black stripe running down the sides of the neck
Bare Parts—Long, thin, yellow bill; eyes are pale yellow; long legs and toes are dark greenish-yellow
