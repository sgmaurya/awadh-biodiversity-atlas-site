Spiny Orb-weaver or Yellow Sac Spider? कंटकदार मकड़ी या पीली थैली मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow Sac Spider, as the Spiny Orb-weaver profile puts it

Pale yellow-green; lacks spines; does not build webs; hunts at night

पीली थैली मकड़ी — पीला-हरा शरीर, जाला नहीं

Spiny Orb-weaver: Small, hard, flat-bodied spider (8–10 mm) with a white/yellow top bearing six sharp black spines, sitting in the center of a vertical orb web, is the Spiny Orb-weaver.

Yellow Sac Spider: Small, pale yellow-green spider (8–10 mm) with distinct jet-black-tipped jaws, hiding in a tube-like white silk sac inside a rolled leaf during the day, is the Yellow Sac Spider (C. melanostomum). Note: C. triviale and C. indicum are co-occurring sister species in UP, separable only by microscopy.
