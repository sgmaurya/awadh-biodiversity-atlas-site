How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purple Orchid Tree, as the Malabar Bauhinia profile puts it

Bauhinia purpurea]]`

Large purple orchid-like flowers with 3 to 4 fertile stamens; leaves larger, cleft deeper

Malabar Bauhinia: Look for the distinctly sour, acidic taste of the leaves and small dull whitish flowers in dense rusty-tomentose racemes.

Purple Orchid Tree: A semi-deciduous tree with distinctly bilobed leaves and large, showy purple or pink orchid-like flowers with darker veining. Flowers appear in autumn and early winter on nearly bare branches.
