Dwarf Honey Bee or Giant Honey Bee? छोटी मधुमक्खी या रॉक बी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटी मधुमक्खीDwarf Honey BeeApis florea
रॉक बीGiant Honey BeeApis dorsata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giant Honey Bee, as the Dwarf Honey Bee profile puts it

Very large (17–20 mm); builds huge exposed combs on high cliffs or tall structures

सारंग / डम्भर — विशाल आकार, अत्यंत आक्रामक

Dwarf Honey Bee, as the Giant Honey Bee profile puts it

Very small (7–10 mm); builds a small single comb in low bushes; abdomen has red-orange front segments

छोटी मधुमक्खी — अत्यंत छोटा आकार, कम ऊँचाई पर छत्ता

Dwarf Honey Bee: Tiny size (7–10 mm), black body with a bright orange-red base of the abdomen, and a small, exposed, single comb wrapped around a thin twig identify the Dwarf Honey Bee.

Giant Honey Bee: Large body size (17–20 mm), dark smoky wings, yellow-and-black striped abdomen, nesting in huge, single, open combs hanging from high structures, identifies Apis dorsata.

Form—Large, slender bee with a long, dark abdomen; significantly larger than other honey bees
Coloration—Head and thorax are blackish-brown; abdominal segments are yellowish-orange at the base and black at the tip
Nesting—Builds a single, massive, open comb (up to 1.5 meters wide) hanging vertically from high places
Defense—Highly defensive; performs "shimmering" movements across the comb to ward off predators
Behavior—Excellent long-distance flyer; exhibits crepuscular foraging (active during dusk and moonlit nights)
