How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Babbler, as the Striated Babbler profile puts it

Jungle Babbler]] (Argya striata)

Plain grey-brown without streaks, pale eye, in noisy groups in gardens and groves

Jungle Babbler: Earthy grey-brown plumage, yellowish bill, staring pale-yellow eyes, and an obligate group size of 6–10 birds foraging on the ground.

Striated Babbler: A small, streaky brown babbler in noisy flocks with characteristic "chak-chak-chak" calls, moving through grassland and scrub, is a Striated Babbler. Other babblers have different plumage patterns or sizes.
