How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake, as the Northern Bronzeback profile puts it

Dendrelaphis tristis

Possesses a distinct pale buff or yellowish-white vertebral stripe, smaller vertebral scales, and different scale counts (Smith 1943)

Common Bronzeback Tree Snake: A slender, uniformly green or bronze-brown snake moving fast along a tree branch during the day with a long prehensile tail is a Common Bronzeback and is completely harmless.

Northern Bronzeback: Slender bronze tree snake with large golden eyes, dark temporal streak, and brilliant turquoise interstitial skin visible when inflated in orchard trees.
