Banded Bay Cuckoo or Grey-bellied Cuckoo? बैंडेड बे कुक्कू या ग्रे-बेलीड कुक्कू?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey-bellied Cuckoo, as the Banded Bay Cuckoo profile puts it

Grey-bellied Cuckoo]] (Cacomantis passerinus)

Adult male has uniform dark grey upperparts and belly with white vent; hepatic female lacks close wavy underpart barring (Baker 1927, p. 154)

Banded Bay Cuckoo, as the Grey-bellied Cuckoo profile puts it

More reddish-brown underparts; more rufous colouring; different call

बाइड कोयल

Banded Bay Cuckoo: The brown and rufous barred upperparts, finely cross-barred whitish underparts, and bright red outer eye ring identify the Banded Bay Cuckoo (Baker 1927, pp. 157–158).

Grey-bellied Cuckoo: A small, compact, uniformly grey cuckoo with bright yellow eye-ring, giving a distinctive two-note "piu-piu" call is Grey-bellied Cuckoo. Males are particularly vocal during breeding season.
