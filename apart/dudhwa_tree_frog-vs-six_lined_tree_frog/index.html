Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Six-lined Tree Frog, as the Dudhwa Tree Frog profile puts it

Polypedates taeniatus

Slender tree frog with prominent dark longitudinal stripes running down the entire length of the back

Dudhwa Tree Frog: Small arboreal tree frog with expanded adhesive toe discs that constructs white foam nests on vegetation overhanging forest rainwater pools.

Six-lined Tree Frog: A medium-sized tree frog with six distinct golden longitudinal stripes on the body is a Six-lined Tree Frog. The prominent adhesive toe discs and arboreal habits confirm identification.

Ventral surfaceWhitish to cream, with distinctly granular texture on the belly and thighs—
Nesting habitConstructs white frothy foam nests on leaves and branches suspended directly over water—
Size—Medium (40–55 mm snout-vent length)
Colour—Yellowish-brown or olive-green above
Markings—Six distinctive golden or dark longitudinal stripes running down the body
Head—Pointed snout; prominent eyes with gold irises
Toes—Adhesive discs on toe tips for climbing
Limbs—Long and slender; adapted for arboreal movement
Behaviour—Nocturnal; strictly arboreal; excellent climber
