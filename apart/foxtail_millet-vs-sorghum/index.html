How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sorghum, as the Foxtail Millet profile puts it

Sorghum]])

Much taller plant; much larger grains; compact panicle

Foxtail Millet: An erect grass with a dense, slightly drooping, cylindrical spike resembling a fox's tail, containing tiny round grains, distinguishes foxtail millet.

Sorghum: A tall grass with thick starch-filled stem, waxy gray-green leaves, and a compact dark cylindrical panicle of round grains distinguishes sorghum.
