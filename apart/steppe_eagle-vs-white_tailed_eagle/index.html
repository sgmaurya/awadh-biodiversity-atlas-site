How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Steppe Eagle, as the White-tailed Eagle profile puts it

Steppe Eagle]] (Aquila nipalensis)

Feathered tarsus down to toes, double wing-bar in immature, square-cut tail, nostrils oval (Baker 1928, p. 70)

Steppe Eagle: A very large brown eagle with golden or buff crown and nape, soaring slowly over open grasslands with slow wing beats and broad wings — is a Steppe Eagle. is larger and more uniformly brown.

White-tailed Eagle: The massive size, pale yellowish head and neck, huge yellow bill, unfeathered yellow lower tarsus, and all-white tail identify the adult White-tailed Eagle (Baker 1928, p. 110).
