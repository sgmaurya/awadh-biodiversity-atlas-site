Dwarf Snakehead or Spotted Snakehead? ड्वार्फ स्नेकहेड या गिरई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Snakehead, as the Dwarf Snakehead profile puts it

Channa punctata

Scattered black spots on body; non-mouthbrooding nest builder; pectoral fins lack concentric bands

Dwarf Snakehead: Small snakehead with concentric dark-and-pale bands on pectoral fins, reddish fin rims, and paternal mouthbrooding.

Spotted Snakehead: A snakehead with white or pale spots on a dark body, medium size (20–35 cm), and a flattened snakehead form is a Spotted Snakehead.
