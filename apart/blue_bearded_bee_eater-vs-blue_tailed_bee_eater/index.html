Blue-bearded Bee-eater or Blue-tailed Bee-eater? ब्लू-बियर्डेड बी-ईटर या बड़ा पतरिंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue-tailed Bee-eater, as the Blue-bearded Bee-eater profile puts it

Blue-tailed Bee-eater]] (Merops philippinus)

Smaller size; bright blue rump and tail; chestnut throat; black eye-mask; long central tail pins; perches openly on wires

Blue-bearded Bee-eater: The combination of very large size, square tail without central elongated pins, heavy curved bill, and bright azure-blue throat beard distinguishes the Blue-bearded Bee-eater from all Merops species.

Blue-tailed Bee-eater: Small, multicoloured flyer (green, yellow, blue) + long tail streamers + loud "krrrip" call in flight + aerial insect hunting + spring arrival = Blue-tailed Bee-eater. The elongated tail streamers are diagnostic.
