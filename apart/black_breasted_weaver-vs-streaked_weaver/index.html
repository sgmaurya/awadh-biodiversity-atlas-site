How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-breasted Weaver, as the Streaked Weaver profile puts it

Black-breasted Weaver]] (Ploceus benghalensis)

Males have black breast patch; different coloration pattern

Black-breasted Weaver: The Black-breasted Weaver is easily identified by the breeding male's striking black face mask and black breast. The colonial nesting in hanging nests is distinctive. Females are duller but still show the characteristic weaver build and behaviour.

Streaked Weaver: A small, heavily streaked buff-yellow weaver with large hanging woven nests in reed beds or trees near water is Streaked Weaver. The distinctive woven nests and highly gregarious colonial behaviour are diagnostic.
