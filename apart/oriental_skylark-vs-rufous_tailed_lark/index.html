How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Skylark, as the Rufous-tailed Lark profile puts it

Oriental Skylark]] (Alauda gulgula)

Conspicuous crest, heavily streaked upperparts and breast, dark brown tail edged white, no rufous rump (Baker 1926, p. 319)

Oriental Skylark: The Oriental Skylark is a small, brown, streaked lark of fields and open ground. Its small size, short bill, and bouncy flight are distinctive. If the bird is larger with a more prominent crest, it is a Crested Lark.

Rufous-tailed Lark: The unstreaked dark brown body combined with deep rufous rump and black-tipped rufous tail identifies the Rufous-tailed Lark (Baker 1926, p. 350).

Upperparts—Dark brown with obsolete paler edges and darker centres showing more definitely on crown (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Underparts—Lower plumage, axillaries and under wing-coverts deep rufous; throat and upper breast paler and streaked with black (Baker 1926, p. 350)
Bare Parts—Bill dark horny-brown, fleshy on base of lower mandible; legs and feet pale fleshy to fleshy-brown (Baker 1926, p. 350)
