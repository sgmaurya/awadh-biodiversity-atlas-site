European Honey Bee or Indian Stingless Bee? युरोपीय मधुमक्खी या छोटी मधुमक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

European Honey Bee, as the Indian Stingless Bee profile puts it

Much larger (14–16 mm); rounder abdomen; introduced; very aggressive when defending the nest

विदेशी मक्खी, बहुत बड़ी

European Honey Bee: Medium-sized honeybee (12–15 mm) with orange-brown abdomen bands, managed in wooden hive boxes, visiting agricultural flowers in large numbers, is the European Honey Bee.

Indian Stingless Bee: Tiny, dark bee (3–4 mm), buzzing loudly on flowers, no stinger, foraging in warm hours: Indian Stingless Bee.

Colour—Predominantly black or very dark brown with sparse golden hairs on the thorax
Body shape—Compact, stocky build; wings appear darker than Apis bees
Wings—Narrow, translucent; iridescent sheen visible in certain lights
Stinger—Completely absent — cannot sting (hence "stingless")
Nest—Small, hidden colonies in cavities of trees, walls, or artificial hives
Foraging—Buzzes audibly while visiting flowers; forages during warm, sunny hours
