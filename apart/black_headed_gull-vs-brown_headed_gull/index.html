Black-headed Gull or Brown-headed Gull? ब्लैक-हेडेड गल या ब्राउन-हेडेड गल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown-headed Gull, as the Black-headed Gull profile puts it

Brown-headed Gull]] (Chroicocephalus brunnicephalus)

Brown head in winter (not black ear-patch); slightly larger

Black-headed Gull, as the Brown-headed Gull profile puts it

Black-headed Gull]] (Larus ridibundus)

Much darker head (black in breeding); slightly larger

Black-headed Gull: Black-headed Gull in winter has white head with dark ear-patch, red-orange bill, medium grey back, and red-orange legs. The medium size, ear-patch pattern, and red bill and legs are diagnostic. Compare carefully with Brown-headed Gull which has more extensive head coloring.

Brown-headed Gull: A medium-sized white gull with reddish bill and legs, wintering on large water bodies in winter flocks. Brown or dusky head patch on white head is diagnostic for non-breeding Brown-headed Gull.
