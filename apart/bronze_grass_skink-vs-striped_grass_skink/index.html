Bronze Grass Skink or Striped Grass Skink? ब्रॉन्ज ग्रास स्किंक या स्ट्राइप्ड ग्रास स्किंक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Grass Skink, as the Bronze Grass Skink profile puts it

Eutropis dissimilis

Possesses distinct pale greenish-white longitudinal stripes along the back bordered with black lines (Sharma 2002)

Bronze Grass Skink, as the Striped Grass Skink profile puts it

Eutropis macularia

Smaller, lacks distinct pale longitudinal stripes; dorsal scales have 5 to 7 sharp keels, and males display scarlet throats (Smith 1935)

Bronze Grass Skink: Small shiny bronze skink with 5 to 7 keels on each dorsal scale; breeding males show bright scarlet throat and flanks.

Striped Grass Skink: Medium-sized sandy skink with three prominent pale greenish-white longitudinal stripes running down an olive-brown back along riverbank grasslands.
