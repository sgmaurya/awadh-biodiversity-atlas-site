Arrowleaf Sida or Common Wireweed? अतीबला या बरियारा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Wireweed, as the Arrowleaf Sida profile puts it

Leaves lanceolate, rounded at base, serrate throughout; mericarp awns longer

पत्तियाँ नुकीली, पूरे किनारे पर कांटेदार

Arrowleaf Sida, as the Common Wireweed profile puts it

Leaf more distinctly rhomboid; stem more pubescent; fruit less bristly

बला — पत्ती हीरे जैसी, स्टेम रोएँदार

Arrowleaf Sida: Tough fibrous subshrub with alternate rhomboid-lanceolate leaves cuneate and entire at base, bearing pale yellow morning-opening flowers and 8–10 awned mericarps identifies Sida rhombifolia.

Common Wireweed: A slender annual weed (30–90 cm) with pale yellow flowers and round, slightly bristly seed capsules splitting into five parts, found on roadsides and field edges, is Sida acuta. The wiry stems are the source of the common name "wireweed."
