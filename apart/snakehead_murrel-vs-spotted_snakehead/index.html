Snakehead Murrel or Spotted Snakehead? सौर या गिरई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गिरईSpotted SnakeheadChanna punctata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Snakehead, as the Snakehead Murrel profile puts it

Much smaller (under 25 cm); olive-green with black dots; lacks chevron bands

फूल-सौर — छोटा आकार, धब्बेदार शरीर

Snakehead Murrel, as the Spotted Snakehead profile puts it

Dark bars instead of white spots; more robust head

धारीदार गिरई — सफ़ेद धब्बे नहीं

Snakehead Murrel: Elongated cylindrical body with a flat snake-like head, chevron-shaped dark bands on the sides, white belly, and absence of tail ocellus identify Channa striata.

Spotted Snakehead: A snakehead with white or pale spots on a dark body, medium size (20–35 cm), and a flattened snakehead form is a Spotted Snakehead.
