Bathua or Spiny Amaranth? बथुआ या कांटे वाली चौलाई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कांटे वाली चौलाईSpiny AmaranthAmaranthus spinosus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spiny Amaranth, as the Bathua profile puts it

Has sharp spines at leaf bases; leaves are not powdery; fruit is a spiny cluster

कांटे वाली चौलाई — पत्ती के आधार पर कांटे

Bathua, as the Spiny Amaranth profile puts it

Leaves are goosefoot-shaped, covered in white waxy powder; lacks spines

बथुआ — बत्तख के पैर जैसे पत्ते, सफेद पाउडर

Bathua: Erect winter annual herb with goosefoot-shaped leaves covered in white waxy powder, and tiny greenish-white flower spikes at the branch tips, is Bathua.

Spiny Amaranth: Erect green weed with branched stems, bearing a pair of sharp, needle-like white spines (5–15 mm) at the base of every leaf stem, and greenish cylindrical flower spikes, is Spiny Amaranth.
