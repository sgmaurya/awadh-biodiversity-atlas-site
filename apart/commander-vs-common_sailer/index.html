How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Sailer, as the Commander profile puts it

Common Sailer]])

Ground colour is deep black rather than fiery reddish-chestnut; slow sailing flight

Commander: A large, powerful nymphalid butterfly with rich reddish-chestnut wings crossed by a brilliant, broad white central band, flying boldly around kadam and kaim trees, is the Commander.

Common Sailer: A medium-sized black butterfly with white spots or bands on the wings and swift, low, erratic flight over grasslands is the Common Sailer.
