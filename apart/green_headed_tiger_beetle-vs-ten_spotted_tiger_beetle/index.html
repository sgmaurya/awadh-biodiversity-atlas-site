Green-headed Tiger Beetle or Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle? हरे-सिर वाला बाघ भृंग या दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कीट दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंगTen-spotted Tiger BeetleCylindera decempunctata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Green-headed Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Five discrete small white spots on each elytron rather than broad bold white patches and a separated round preapical spot (Fowler 1912, p. 353)

प्रत्येक पंख पर पाँच छोटे अलग-अलग सफेद धब्बे (दस-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग)

Green-headed Tiger Beetle, as the Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Head and pronotum brilliant metallic green; bold white markings including a broad sinuate central patch rather than five small spots (Fowler 1912, p. 362)

चमकदार हरा सिर और वक्ष, बड़े सफेद निशान (हरे-सिर वाला बाघ भृंग)

Green-headed Tiger Beetle: A small tiger beetle with a brilliant metallic green head and pronotum, dark blue-black elytra, and a separated large round white spot before the apex is Cylindera erudita (Fowler 1912, pp. 362–363).

Ten-spotted Tiger Beetle: A tiger beetle with metallic front parts, elytra subparallel-sided in the male and slightly widened behind in the female, and five white spots on each dark elytron (the fifth joining a narrow apical marginal line) is Cylindera decempunctata (Fowler 1912, p. 353).
