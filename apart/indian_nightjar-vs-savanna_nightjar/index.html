बड़ी चपाक — कम सामान्य

Indian Nightjar, as the Savanna Nightjar profile puts it

Indian Nightjar]] (Caprimulgus asiaticus)

Noticeably smaller; upperparts paler buff-grey with distinct rufous-tawny half-collar; call is a distinctive clicking tuk-tuk-tuk-krooo; outer tail feathers only tipped white

Indian Nightjar: Medium-sized cryptic brown bird + perches lengthwise on branch or ground + large eye + loud "chop-chop-chop" call at night + cryptic daytime behaviour = Indian Nightjar. The rhythmic call is unmistakable.

Savanna Nightjar: The finely vermiculated brownish-grey plumage lacking a distinct collar, the male's entirely white outer tail feathers, and the sonorous flight call distinguish the Savanna Nightjar.
