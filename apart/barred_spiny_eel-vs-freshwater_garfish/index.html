How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Freshwater Garfish, as the Barred Spiny Eel profile puts it

Xenentodon cancila

Long, slender beaked surface predator with needle-like toothed jaws; lacks dorsal spines and rostral lobe

Barred Spiny Eel: Small spiny eel with fleshy trilobed snout, 24 to 26 short dorsal spines, and rounded caudal fin completely separate from dorsal and anal fins.

Freshwater Garfish: Needle-like elongated body, both upper and lower jaws extended into a long tooth-armed beak, and habit of floating motionless just below the water surface identify the Freshwater Garfish.
