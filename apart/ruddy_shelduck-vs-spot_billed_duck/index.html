Ruddy Shelduck or Indian Spot-billed Duck? सुर्खाब या गुगराल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Spot-billed Duck, as the Ruddy Shelduck profile puts it

Smaller; brown; spotted bill; year-round resident

छोटी, भूरी, धब्बेदार चोंच

Ruddy Shelduck: A large, cinnamon-rufous duck with pale head and black neck collar (males), on large water bodies in winter — is a Ruddy Shelduck. Females are paler buff.

Indian Spot-billed Duck: A large dabbling duck with a yellow bill bearing a black spot, in flocks on ponds — is a Spot-billed Duck. The spotted bill is diagnostic.
