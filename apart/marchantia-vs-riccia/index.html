Common Liverwort or Crystalwort? काई (गुच्छेदार) या काई (ज़मीन की हरी गुलाबी काई)?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

काई (गुच्छेदार)Common LiverwortMarchantia polymorpha
काई (ज़मीन की हरी गुलाबी काई)CrystalwortRiccia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crystalwort, as the Common Liverwort profile puts it

Smaller thalli (coin-sized), forked lobes, no umbrella structures

छोटी, कोई छतरी नहीं

Common Liverwort: A flat, fleshy green mat with lobed edges and umbrella-like stalked discs in the centre, on damp walls or rocks, is a liverwort of the genus Marchantia.

Crystalwort: Coin-sized flat green rosettes of forking strap-like lobes pressed to damp soil, with no leaf or stem, are Riccia.

Side by side आमने-सामने
