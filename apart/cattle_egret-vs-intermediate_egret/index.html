How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cattle Egret, as the Intermediate Egret profile puts it

Cattle Egret]] (Bubulcus ibis)

Smaller (45–50 cm); stouter yellow bill; often terrestrial (not aquatic); buff plumage in breeding season

Cattle Egret: Small size, stocky neck, yellow bill (orange in breeding), yellowish-green legs, and terrestrial foraging behavior alongside grazing livestock.

Intermediate Egret: A medium-sized white egret with a yellow bill and black legs, wading in shallow water in rivers and rice paddies — this is the Intermediate Egret. The Great Egret is larger and darker-legged. The Little Egret is smaller with a black bill. The Cattle Egret is smaller and more terrestrial.
