Black-winged Kite, as the Common Kestrel profile puts it

Black-winged Kite]] (Elanus caeruleus)

Pure white and pale grey plumage; jet-black shoulders; red eyes; hovers in mid-air

Black-winged Kite: Small grey-and-white raptor, prominent jet-black shoulders, bright red eyes, and a stationary hovering flight over open fields identify the Black-winged Kite.

Common Kestrel: Small rufous-brown falcon with pointed wings, long barred tail, and a black-tipped yellow bill, hovering stationary over crop fields, is a Common Kestrel.

PerchFrequently perches on dead tree branches or telephone poles at field edges—

Female plumage—Uniformly rufous-brown back and tail, both heavily barred with dark brown
Bare Parts—Yellow cere, eye-ring, legs, and feet; bill is blue-grey with a black tip; claws are black and sharp
