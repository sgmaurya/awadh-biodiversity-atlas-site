Common Mormon or Common Rose? सामान्य मॉर्मन या कॉमन रोज़ तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कीट सामान्य मॉर्मनCommon MormonPapilio polytes
कॉमन रोज़ तितलीCommon RosePachliopta aristolochiae

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Rose, as the Common Mormon profile puts it

Red head and body; red spots on hindwing; flight is slow and fluttering; toxic model

लाल-शरीर गुलाब — लाल सिर और शरीर, धीमी उड़ान

Common Mormon, as the Common Rose profile puts it

Black head and thorax; mimic form lacks the bright red thorax; slower wing pattern

कॉमन मॉर्मन — काले सिर और छाती, अनुकरणकर्ता

Common Mormon: Large black swallowtail butterfly with cream-white spots on hindwings (male) or a female mimicking toxic red-spotted Rose butterflies, whose caterpillars eat citrus leaves, is the Common Mormon.

Common Rose: A medium-sized, bright red swallowtail with a black apex, white spots on the forewing tip, a white median band on the hindwing, and a slow, fluttering flight is the Common Rose.
