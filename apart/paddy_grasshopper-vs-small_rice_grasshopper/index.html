How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Paddy Grasshopper, as the Small Rice Grasshopper profile puts it

Similar green coloration but tegmina and wings are distinctly shorter, scarcely reaching or only slightly surpassing abdominal apex; larger body

धान टिड्डा

Paddy Grasshopper: Green 20–35 mm grasshopper with brown lateral stripes + in paddy field + August–October = Oxya fuscovittata with high confidence. The only common green grasshopper of paddy fields in eastern UP.

Small Rice Grasshopper: A slender green grasshopper with long tegmina surpassing the abdomen tip, a broad dark lateral stripe, and blue-green flattened swimming hind tibiae is Oxya hyla.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Paddy GrasshopperSmall Rice Grasshopper
SizeMedium-sized grasshopper; males 20–28 mm, females significantly larger at 28–35 mm—
ColourGreen body with distinctive dark brown lateral stripes running from the eyes to the pronotum margins—
WingsGreenish-brown forewings covering the abdomen, with translucent yellow-green hindwings visible in flight—
HindlegsLong and powerful yellow-green legs with comb-like spines on the tibia, adapted for strong leaping—
HabitatClosely associated with paddy fields, sugarcane fields, and adjacent tall grassy bunds—
Coloration—Bright grass-green body with a prominent dark brown lateral stripe
Lateral stripe—Broad dark fascia extends from behind the eye across the pronotum to tegmina bases
Hind tibiae—Bluish-green or pale blue with black-tipped spines; laterally expanded for swimming
Head—Conical, fastigium of vertex short and rounded; antennae filiform
Tegmina—Fully developed, surpassing the abdomen tip, translucent brownish-green
Ovipositor—Female ovipositor valves short, robust, and furnished with distinct small teeth
