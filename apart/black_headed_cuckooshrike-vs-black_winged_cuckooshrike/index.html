How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-winged Cuckooshrike, as the Black-headed Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Black-winged Cuckooshrike]] (Lalage melaschistos)

Dark slaty-grey body with contrasting black wings and tail; lacks clean white belly and black hood

Black-headed Cuckooshrike: The adult male's jet-black hood sharply demarcated from a pale grey back and bright white abdomen instantly identifies the Black-headed Cuckooshrike from the larger, uniform-grey Large Cuckooshrike and the dark slaty Black-winged Cuckooshrike.

Black-winged Cuckooshrike: Black-winged Cuckooshrike has a pale grey body with sharply contrasting black wings. Drongos are all-black with longer, forked tails. The Cuckooshrike moves more deliberately and hangs from branches searching foliage.
