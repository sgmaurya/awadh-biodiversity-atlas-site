How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Ironweed, as the Elephant's Foot profile puts it

Cyanthillium cinereum

Slender branched herb with loose terminal corymbs of purple florets, lacking basal rosette and cordate bracts

Elephant's Foot: Harsh scabrid forest herb with ground-hugging cabbage-like basal leaf rosette, wiry branched stems, and purple flowers subtended by three cordate bracts.

Little Ironweed: A weedy herb with rough, hairy, alternate leaves and small purple flower heads (not spikes) that are clustered, rough to touch and bitter-tasting, growing on roadsides, is sahadevi.

Roots—Taproot system, penetrating 15–20 cm deep
