How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-crowned Woodpecker, as the Streak-throated Woodpecker profile puts it

Leiopicus mahrattensis

Much smaller body, dark brown-and-white barred mantle, and yellow-orange crown

Streak-throated Woodpecker: Bright grass-green upperparts with brilliant yellow rump and finely streaked throat.

Yellow-crowned Woodpecker: Yellow-crowned woodpecker is a small woodpecker with a distinctive golden crest (in males) and golden rump. If smaller and lacking the golden features, it may be a pygmy woodpecker. If larger with a grey head, it is a grey-headed woodpecker.

Rump—Yellow or golden (diagnostic)
Tail—Black; stiff and pointed
Wings—Black with white markings
Behaviour—Active on tree trunks and branches; drums on wood
