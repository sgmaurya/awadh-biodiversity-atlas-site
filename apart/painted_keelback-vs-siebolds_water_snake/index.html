How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Painted Keelback, as the Siebold's Water Snake profile puts it

Painted Keelback]] (Xenochrophis cerasogaster)

Smaller (50–90 cm); bright yellow or orange longitudinal stripes; found in marshes and rice paddies, not deep rivers

Painted Keelback: Any keeled, striped water snake found near wetlands and rice paddies is a Painted Keelback. It will flee into water when approached. The longitudinal yellow or orange stripes are distinctive.

Siebold's Water Snake: Any large, uniformly dark water snake in deep river channels is likely Siebold's Water Snake. The absence of stripes or checkering and the laterally compressed tail are the key identifiers.
