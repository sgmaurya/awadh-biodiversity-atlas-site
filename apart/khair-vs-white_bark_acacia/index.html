How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Khair, as the White-bark Acacia profile puts it

Senegalia catechu]]`

Prickles hooked and recurved; flowers borne in cylindrical elongated spikes, not globose heads

Khair: A tree with very fine, feathery, doubly pinnate leaves, fragrant yellow flower spikes, and very hard dark wood, is khair.

White-bark Acacia: Look for the characteristic pale chalky yellowish-white bark, straight paired spines, and large branching panicles of globose creamy-yellow heads.
