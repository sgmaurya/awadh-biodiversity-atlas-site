How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Adusa, as the Squirrel's Tail profile puts it

Justicia adhatoda]]`

Glabrous or puberulous shrub with large opposite leaves; flowers large, showy, two-lipped, white with purple veins

Adusa: A sturdy shrub with large, glossy, opposite leaves and dense white-and-purple flower spikes is adusa.

Squirrel's Tail: Look for the grey-woolly shrub with thick velvety leaves and dense cylindrical fuzzy white spikes like squirrel tails.
