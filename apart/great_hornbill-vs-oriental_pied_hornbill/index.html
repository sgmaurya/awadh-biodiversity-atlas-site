How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Pied Hornbill, as the Great Hornbill profile puts it

Oriental Pied Hornbill]] (Anthracoceros albirostris)

Noticeably smaller; sharp black-and-white body with ivory-white casque lacking concave double horns; white belly

Great Hornbill: The gigantic body, bright yellow concave double-horned casque, and pure white tail with a single broad black band immediately separate the Great Hornbill from both grey and pied hornbills.

Oriental Pied Hornbill: Massive black-and-white canopy bird with immense yellow bill and casque, white belly, and white-tipped black tail.
