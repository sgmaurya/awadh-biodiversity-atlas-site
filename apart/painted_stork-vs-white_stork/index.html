Painted Stork or White Stork? जांघिल या हाजी लगलग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Stork, as the Painted Stork profile puts it

White Stork]]

Pure white (vagrant); no pink or black flight feathers

Painted Stork, as the White Stork profile puts it

Painted Stork]] (Mycteria leucocephala)

Naked head; multicoloured plumage (pink, black, white); larger bill

Painted Stork: A large, distinctly colourful stork with pink body, black wings, white head, and yellow bill, often in flocks at water bodies — is a Painted Stork. No other stork in eastern UP has this combination of colours.

White Stork: A very large white stork with black wing feathers and bright red legs and bill, walking slowly in shallow water or wet grasslands — is a White Stork. Unmistakable in size and colour pattern.
