Japanese Lovegrass or Marvel Grass? जापानी लव घास या मार्वल घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Marvel Grass, as the Japanese Lovegrass profile puts it

Perennial tufted grass; stems with ring of white hairs at nodes; racemes awned

बहुवर्षीय घास, गांठों पर सफेद बाल

Japanese Lovegrass: Delicate annual grass with wiry culms, prominent long white hairs at the leaf sheath mouth, and effuse feathery panicles bearing minute spikelets with ciliate-keeled paleas identifies Eragrostis tenella.

Marvel Grass: Tufted perennial grass with a conspicuous ring of white hairs at each stem node and subdigitate silky purplish racemes with fine twisted awns identifies Dichanthium annulatum.

HabitDelicate tufted annual grass 10–50 cm tall with slender erect or ascending culms (Bor 1960)Tufted perennial grass 30–100 cm tall, forming dense leafy tussocks (Bor 1960)
