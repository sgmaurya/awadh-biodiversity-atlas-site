How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cattle Egret, as the Purple Heron profile puts it

Cattle Egret]] (Bubulcus ibis)

Much smaller; entirely white; sits with livestock in dry fields

Cattle Egret: Small size, stocky neck, yellow bill (orange in breeding), yellowish-green legs, and terrestrial foraging behavior alongside grazing livestock.

Purple Heron: Large, slender wading bird with a dark reddish-chestnut and grey body, thin snake-like neck with black stripes, hiding in dense reeds, is a Purple Heron.
