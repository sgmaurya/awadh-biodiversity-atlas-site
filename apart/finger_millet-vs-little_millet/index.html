How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Finger Millet, as the Little Millet profile puts it

Finger Millet]] (Eleusine coracana)

Taller (60–90 cm); seed head has 5–8 distinct finger-like spikes; grain reddish

Finger Millet: A compact grass with 4–8 finger-like panicles spreading from the top, each bearing tiny dark grains, distinguishes finger millet.

Little Millet: A fine, slender grass with narrow leaves, a light drooping panicle, and tiny golden grains; matures in 70–80 days in upland fields with minimal water.
