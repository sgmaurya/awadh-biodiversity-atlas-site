How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coromandel Marsh Dart, as the Yellow Bush Dart profile puts it

Coromandel Marsh Dart]])

Entire body uniform bright citron-yellow; legs simple, not dilated; sun-loving, flies over open ponds

Coromandel Marsh Dart: Tiny damselfly with bright red (male) or bright yellow-orange (female) body, slow fluttering flight over marshy vegetation, often in tandem pairs, is the Coromandel Marsh Dart.

Yellow Bush Dart: An extremely slender, thread-like damselfly flitting weakly in deep shade near water with a dark bronzy body, a white tail tip, and conspicuously expanded bright golden-yellow legs is the Yellow Bush Dart.
