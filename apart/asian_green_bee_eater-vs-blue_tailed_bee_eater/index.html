Asian Green Bee-eater or Blue-tailed Bee-eater? हरा पतरंगा या बड़ा पतरिंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हरा पतरंगाAsian Green Bee-eaterMerops orientalis
बड़ा पतरिंगाBlue-tailed Bee-eaterMerops philippinus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue-tailed Bee-eater, as the Asian Green Bee-eater profile puts it

Much larger (30 cm); blue rump and tail; brown throat; visitor to wetlands

बड़ा पतरंगा — नीली पूंछ और भूरा गला

Asian Green Bee-eater, as the Blue-tailed Bee-eater profile puts it

Smaller; shorter tail (no streamers); greener overall; different call

छोटा पतरिंगा — हरा, छोटी पूँछ

Asian Green Bee-eater: Small size, emerald-green body, golden-rufous wash on crown, black neck collar, red eyes, and two long black tail-streamers.

Blue-tailed Bee-eater: Small, multicoloured flyer (green, yellow, blue) + long tail streamers + loud "krrrip" call in flight + aerial insect hunting + spring arrival = Blue-tailed Bee-eater. The elongated tail streamers are diagnostic.
