Common Sand Boa or Indian Python? डोमेन साँप या अजगर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

डोमेन साँपCommon Sand BoaEryx conicus
अजगरIndian PythonPython molurus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Python, as the Common Sand Boa profile puts it

Much larger (3–4.5 m); has a distinct arrowhead mark on head; scales are completely smooth

अजगर — विशाल आकार, सिर पर तीर का निशान

Common Sand Boa, as the Indian Python profile puts it

Much smaller (50–75 cm); body is very stout, tail is extremely blunt; lacks arrowhead mark; smooth scales

दुमुँहा साँप — छोटा आकार, कुंद पूंछ, चिकनी त्वचा

Common Sand Boa: Small, very stout snake (50–75 cm) with irregular dark brown blotches on a tan body, a wedge-shaped digging head, and a short blunt tail mimicking the head, is the Common Sand Boa (Domein).

Indian Python: Massive stout-bodied snake (3.0–4.5 m) with a prominent dark arrowhead mark on a flat head, asymmetrical brown blotches on a yellow-tan body, and cat-like vertical pupils, is the Indian Python (Ajgar).

Head Pattern—Distinctive, dark, spearhead or arrowhead mark on the top of the flat pinkish-grey head
Body Markings—Yellowish-buff or tan body, covered with massive, dark brown, rectangular, asymmetrical blotches
Pupil—Vertical slit (cat-like pupil) in gold-colored eyes, with distinct heat-sensitive pits along the jaw scales
