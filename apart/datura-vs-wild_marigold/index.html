Dhatura or Wild Marigold? धतूरा या वाइल्ड मैरीगोल्ड?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dhatura, as the Wild Marigold profile puts it

धतूरा (Datura metel)]]

Coarse sprawling shrub with large white funnel-shaped solitary flowers; spiny capsule

Dhatura: Large roadside herb + large funnel-shaped white flower opening at dusk + large spiny round fruit + strong smell = Datura metel. The spiny capsule is the single most reliable feature — nothing else common in eastern UP has it.

Wild Marigold: A tall, grooved wasteland weed with finely pinnate gland-dotted leaves releasing an intense pungent citrus-terpene aroma and dense clusters of narrow cylindric yellow heads is Tagetes minuta.
