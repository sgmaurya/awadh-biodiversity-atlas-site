How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crowned River Turtle, as the Red-crowned Roofed Turtle profile puts it

Crowned River Turtle]] (Hardella thurjii)

Large shell; head dark with distinctive orange or yellow stripes running through eyes

Crowned River Turtle: India's largest freshwater turtle with a distinctive crowned head is the Crowned River Turtle. Any large turtle in the Ghaghra river with a prominent head crown is likely this species.

Red-crowned Roofed Turtle: Smith gives a shell 390 mm long, a pale brown neck with seven red or reddish-brown stripes, and, after Anderson, a brilliant red top to the male's head (Smith 1931, p. 132).
