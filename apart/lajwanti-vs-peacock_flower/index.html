Touch-me-not or Peacock Flower? लाजवंती या कृष्णचूड़ा?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Touch-me-not, as the Peacock Flower profile puts it

Sensitive plant]] (Mimosa pudica)

Leaves close when touched; much smaller; pink flowers

Touch-me-not: Low-growing, prickly, creeping plant + bipinnate feathery leaves that fold instantly on touch + pink pompom flowers = Mimosa pudica. The touch-sensitivity alone is diagnostic — nothing else in eastern UP common vegetation responds this way.

Peacock Flower: A semi-deciduous shrub with large, bipinnate leaves composed of many small leaflets, and showy flowers with orange or yellow petals and bright red stamens extending prominently beyond the petals, resembling a peacock's tail, is Peacock Flower.
