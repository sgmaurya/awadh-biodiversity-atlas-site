Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Garden Lizard, as the Fan-throated Lizard profile puts it

Oriental Garden Lizard]] (Calotes versicolor)

Larger; a crest of spines along the neck and back; no throat fan; breeding males with a red head

Fan-throated Lizard, as the Oriental Garden Lizard profile puts it

Fan-throated Lizard]])

Much smaller (10–15 cm); male has brilliant blue-orange dewlap

Fan-throated Lizard: A small, slender lizard with a distinctive coloured fan at the throat (males) is a Fan-throated Lizard. Larger lizards with dorsal crests running the length of the back are different species.
