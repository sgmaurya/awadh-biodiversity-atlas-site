Water Fern or Giant Salvinia? एज़ोला या सालविनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

एज़ोलाWater FernAzolla pinnata
सालविनियाGiant SalviniaSalvinia molesta

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Fern, as the Giant Salvinia profile puts it

Tiny fern-like rosettes (1–3 cm), reddish in sun, finely divided fronds; no feathery lower structures

बहुत छोटी, लाल-हरी, घनी

Water Fern: Tiny floating triangular aquatic fern (1.0–2.5 cm) forming green-red mosaic mats on water, with branched feather-like leaves and hanging hair-like roots, is the Water Fern (Azolla).

Giant Salvinia: Floating, oval, vivid green fronds in pairs with a fuzzy texture and feathery brown structures underneath = giant salvinia. Frond papillae and exponential growth are key.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Water FernGiant Salvinia
Plant habitTiny, floating, branched annual or perennial aquatic fern, measuring 1.0–2.5 cm, forming dense velvet mats—
Fronds (Leaves)Scale-like, arranged in two overlapping rows; green in winter/shade, turning dark brick-red in hot sunlight—
RootsSimple, unbranched, hair-like roots (1–3 cm) hanging freely in the water column below the leaves—
Spore productionNon-flowering; reproduces via spores contained in tiny globular structures (sporocarps) under the leaves—
SymbiosisHas a symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing cyanobacteria inside leaf cavities—
Form—Floating fern; fronds in oval or fan-like rosettes; forms dense mats
Upper fronds—Oval to obovate, 2–5 cm long, vivid green, smooth; arranged in pairs along a midrib
Lower fronds—Highly modified; submerged, hair-like, feathery, dark brown or reddish; act as false roots and anchors
Hair-like structures—On upper frond surface; distinctive papillae (bumps) give a fuzzy appearance
Growth pattern—Exponential; colonies double in 12–16 days in warm water
Stolons—Horizontal runners link plants, forming continuous mats across water surface
