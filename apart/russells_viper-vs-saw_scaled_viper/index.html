Russell's Viper or Saw-scaled Viper? कोरिआला या फुरसा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कोरिआलाRussell's ViperDaboia russelii
फुरसाSaw-scaled ViperEchis carinatus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Saw-scaled Viper, as the Russell's Viper profile puts it

Saw-scaled Viper]] [REP-0011])

Much smaller (38–55 cm); distinct white trident-shaped mark on the head; threat display involves rubbing body scales to make a sawing sound.

Russell's Viper, as the Saw-scaled Viper profile puts it

Russell's Viper]] [REP-0010])

Much larger (100–140 cm); distinct chain-like dark oval spots on the back; makes a loud pressure-cooker-like hiss; lacks the head trident mark.

FormStout, heavy-bodied snake; tail is short and tapers rapidly—

MarkingsThree longitudinal rows of dark brown oval spots with black borders and white edges, forming a chain-like patternPale wavy lateral line on the sides; dark brown spots on the back with white edges
ScalesHeavily keeled (ridged) dorsal scales, giving the snake a rough, dry textureHeavily keeled; lateral scales are arranged in oblique rows and carry serrated (saw-like) ridges
NostrilsExtremely large and conspicuous, positioned on the sides of the snout—
EyesLarge, with gold-flecked irises and distinct vertical, slit-like pupilsLarge, golden-brown with vertical, slit-like pupils
DefenceCoils tightly, raises the front of its body, and produces a remarkably loud, continuous pressure-cooker-like hissCoils into a figure-eight configuration and rubs its lateral scales together to produce a loud, continuous sawing sound
Size—Small, stout snake; total length 38–55 cm; tail is very short and thin
Movement—Sidewinding locomotion on loose sand; leaves J-shaped tracks
