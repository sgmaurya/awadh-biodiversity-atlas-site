Bhang or Indian Mallow? भांग या कंघी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भांगBhangCannabis sativa
कंघीIndian MallowAbutilon indicum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Mallow, as the Bhang profile puts it

Leaves simple, not palmately divided; flowers yellow or orange; different growth form

सरल पत्तियाँ, पीले फूल

Bhang: A tall, aromatic herb with deeply palmately divided, serrated leaves; small green flowers; and a distinctive musty smell, is bhang (Cannabis sativa).

Indian Mallow: Soft, hairy herb + heart-shaped leaves + bright yellow flowers + disc-shaped fruit breaking into hooked segments = Abutilon indicum. The bright yellow pollen is diagnostic.

StemErect, hairy, angularHerbaceous, soft-hairy, greenish; not woody
The testThe deeply palmate leaves and distinctive aroma are diagnostic—
Habit—Annual or short-lived perennial herb, 60–120 cm, soft-hairy throughout
Hairs—Soft stellate (star-shaped) hairs throughout, giving a fuzzy appearance
