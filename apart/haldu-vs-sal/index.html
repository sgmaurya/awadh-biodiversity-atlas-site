Haldu or Sal? हल्दू या साल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हल्दूHalduHaldina cordifolia
सालSalShorea robusta

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sal, as the Haldu profile puts it

Leaflets obovate, not cordate; different flowers; no long capsules

साल — अलग पत्तियाँ

Haldu, as the Sal profile puts it

Leaflets cordate; flowers pink; very different fruit

हल्दू — कोर्डेट पत्तियाँ

Haldu: A tall tree with large, cordate-leafed pinnate leaves, pink or white fragrant flowers, and long flat woody capsules, is haldu.

Sal: A very tall tree with rough, leathery, obovate leaves and pale wood, with winged fruits, in a temple or forest setting, is sal.
