Mallard or Ruddy Shelduck? नीलसर या सुर्खाब?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mallard, as the Ruddy Shelduck profile puts it

Smaller; male has metallic green head; no rufous body

छोटी, हरा सिर, कोई नारंगी नहीं

Mallard: A large duck with a glossy green head and white chest (males) — is a Mallard. This is by far the most familiar duck species globally. Females shown blue speculum in flight and paired with green-headed males in winter.

Ruddy Shelduck: A large, cinnamon-rufous duck with pale head and black neck collar (males), on large water bodies in winter — is a Ruddy Shelduck. Females are paler buff.
