How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Grey Mongoose, as the Small Indian Mongoose profile puts it

Indian Grey Mongoose]] (Herpestes edwardsii)

Noticeably larger with coarser iron-grey grizzled pelage and a bushy, pale reddish-tipped tail

Small Indian Mongoose: The diminutive slender build, finely punctated golden-brown coat, tapering non-flared tail, and diurnal foraging habits distinguish the Small Indian Mongoose from other small carnivores.

Indian Grey MongooseSmall Indian Mongoose
SizeBody 35–45 cm; tail 25–35 cm; total length 60–80 cm; weight 0.9–1.5 kg—
ColourGrizzled grey-brown — each hair banded alternately black and white/grey—
BodyElongated, low-slung with short legs; sinuous, fluid gait—
TailLong bushy tail tapering to a dark tipTapering tail shorter than the head and body, lacking an expanded black or red tip
FacePointed snout; small round ears; beady dark eyes—
ActivityDiurnal — active 6–9 AM and 3–6 PM; moves in short rapid dashes—
Habit—Slender, agile, solitary small carnivore of fields, open scrub, and village environs
Body Size—Distinctly smaller and more delicately built than the Common Grey Mongoose
Pelage—Olive-brown or grizzled grey-brown, speckled throughout with fine golden-yellow flecks
Muzzle—Pointed, slender muzzle with a bare, flesh-coloured or dark pink rhinarium
Ears—Small, rounded, and closely flattened against the sides of the head
Legs—Very short limbs terminating in sharp, slightly curved fossorial claws

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
