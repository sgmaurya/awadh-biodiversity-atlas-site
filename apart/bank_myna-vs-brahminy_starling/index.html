Bank Myna or Brahminy Starling? गंगा मैना या ब्राह्मणी मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गंगा मैनाBank MynaAcridotheres ginginianus
ब्राह्मणी मैनाBrahminy StarlingSturnia pagodarum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brahminy Starling, as the Bank Myna profile puts it

Orange underparts; rusty-brown; slimmer; grey-white head; different call

पहाड़ी मैना

Bank Myna: Smaller than Common Myna + orange-red (not yellow) facial skin + black ear patch + more gracile build + found in flocks near water = Bank Myna. The orange face and ear patch are diagnostic at a glance.

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.
