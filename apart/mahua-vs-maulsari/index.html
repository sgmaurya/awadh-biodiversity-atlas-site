Mahua or Maulsari? महुआ या मौलसरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

महुआMahuaMadhuca longifolia
मौलसरीMaulsariMimusops elengi

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Maulsari, as the Mahua profile puts it

Also Sapotaceae; flowers smaller, fragrant; fruits smaller ovoid; not edible in same way

Maulsari: A large evergreen tree with dark glossy oblong leaves, intensely fragrant small white-yellow flowers in spring, and small sweet brown fruit in summer is maulsari; nothing else in UP is so fragrant.
