Mrigal: Streamlined silver body, downward-pointing mouth with thin smooth lips, and yellowish-orange pelvic/anal fins identify the Mrigal.

Reba Carp: An elongate, streamlined silvery minor carp with an inferior mouth, a single pair of short rostral barbels, and dark hexagonal scale outlines forming subtle longitudinal lines, is Cirrhinus reba.
