Indian Jujube or Wood Apple? बेर या कैथा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बेरIndian JujubeZiziphus mauritiana
कैथाWood AppleLimonia acidissima

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Apple, as the Indian Jujube profile puts it

Leaves are compound (winged stems); flowers are reddish-dull; fruits are large, hard, grey woody balls; lacks the small drupes.

कैथा — संयुक्त पत्ते, गोल लकड़ी जैसा फल

Indian Jujube, as the Wood Apple profile puts it

Leaves are simple, oval, with three main veins from the base; fruits are small, smooth, yellow-red drupes with a hard stone.

बेर — सरल पत्ते, छोटा मीठा फल
