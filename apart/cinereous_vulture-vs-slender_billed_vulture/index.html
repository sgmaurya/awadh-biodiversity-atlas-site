How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cinereous Vulture, as the Slender-billed Vulture profile puts it

Cinereous Vulture]] (Aegypius monachus)

Immense size, uniform dark blackish-brown plumage, and heavily feathered neck ruff

Cinereous Vulture: The immense wingspan, uniform blackish-brown plumage lacking white rump or pale wing ruffs, heavily feathered dark thighs, and massive heavy bill identify the Cinereous Vulture.

Slender-billed Vulture: The completely bare dark head and neck without down, slender dark bill with horny-black cere, and habit of nesting singly in huge cotton-trees distinguish the Slender-billed Vulture from its congeners (Baker 1928).
