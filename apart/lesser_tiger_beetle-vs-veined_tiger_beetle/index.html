Lesser Tiger Beetle or Veined Tiger Beetle? छोटा बाघ भृंग या नसदार बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटा बाघ भृंगLesser Tiger BeetleCylindera minuta
नसदार बाघ भृंगVeined Tiger BeetleCylindera venosa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Veined Tiger Beetle, as the Lesser Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Slightly larger; elytral markings form broader, vein-like white streaks

थोड़ा बड़ा (9-11 मिमी), पंखों पर नसों जैसी चौड़ी धारियां

Lesser Tiger Beetle, as the Veined Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Smaller; elytral markings narrower, fragmented, and not vein-like

छोटा आकार (7-9 मिमी), पंखों पर पतले अलग-अलग निशान

Lesser Tiger Beetle: A diminutive bronze tiger beetle running rapidly over wet mud with delicate narrow white lunules identifies Cylindera minuta.

Veined Tiger Beetle: A small bronze tiger beetle on river sand with broad, confluent ivory-white markings resembling leaf veins identifies Cylindera venosa.
