How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cinereous Vulture, as the Indian Vulture profile puts it

Cinereous Vulture]] (Aegypius monachus)

Immense build, uniform blackish-brown plumage throughout, and heavily feathered dark thighs

Cinereous Vulture: The immense wingspan, uniform blackish-brown plumage lacking white rump or pale wing ruffs, heavily feathered dark thighs, and massive heavy bill identify the Cinereous Vulture.

Indian Vulture: The pale sandy-buff body, stout yellowish-horny bill with dull dirty greenish cere and sparsely downy head distinguish the Indian Vulture from the Slender-billed Vulture. Its rump is creamy-white too, but "sometimes hardly visible" (Baker 1928, p. 16), so a white rump alone does not make a White-rumped Vulture.
