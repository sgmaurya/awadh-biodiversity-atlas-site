Jungle Cat or Small Indian Civet? जंगली बिल्ली या कस्तूरी बिलाव?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jungle Cat, as the Small Indian Civet profile puts it

Cat-like head and body; uniform sandy-grey coat; lacks flank spots; tail is shorter with 2–3 dark terminal rings

जंगली बिल्ली — लंबी टांगें, शरीर धब्बे रहित

Small Indian Civet: Terrestrial habit (crawls low on the ground), pale yellow-grey coat with distinct black spots on the flanks, and a long tail with 6–8 alternating black and white rings, identify the Small Indian Civet.
