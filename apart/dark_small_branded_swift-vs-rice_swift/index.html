How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dark Small Branded Swift, as the Rice Swift profile puts it

Dark Small Branded Swift]])

Very similar; slightly smaller; darker overall; different wing pattern details; requires close examination

Dark Small Branded Swift: A very small, dark brown to black-brown butterfly with prominent white spots and bands on dark wings, with extremely rapid flight in rice paddies, is the Dark Small Branded Swift.

Rice Swift: A small, dull brown butterfly with reddish-brown patches, very rapid flier staying low in grass and rice paddies, is the Rice Swift.
