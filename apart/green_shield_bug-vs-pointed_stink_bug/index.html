Green Shield Bug or Pointed Stink Bug? हरा शील्ड बग या नुकीला गंधी कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हरा शील्ड बगGreen Shield BugPlautia crossota
नुकीला गंधी कीड़ाPointed Stink BugErthesina acuminata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pointed Stink Bug, as the Green Shield Bug profile puts it

Greyish-brown, mottled with black punctures; head sharply pointed; much larger

मटमैला भूरा शरीर, बहुत नुकीला तिकोना सिर

Green Shield Bug, as the Pointed Stink Bug profile puts it

Body bright grass-green; much smaller; head rounded, not pointed

चमकीला हरा शरीर, बहुत छोटा, गोल सिर

Green Shield Bug: Compact green shield bug with rounded unspined shoulders, contrasting brownish-purplish corium, and a distinct pale yellow tip to the green scutellum identifies Plautia crossota.

Pointed Stink Bug: Large greyish-brown shield bug with a strongly elongated, acutely triangular pointed head and a pale median line down the scutellum identifies Erthesina acuminata.
