Kamala Tree or Mahua? रोहिनी या महुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

रोहिनीKamala TreeMallotus philippensis
महुआMahuaMadhuca longifolia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mahua, as the Kamala Tree profile puts it

Leaves smaller, glossy; flowers sweet-scented, clustered

छोटी चमकदार पत्तियाँ; मीठी महक

Kamala Tree: A medium deciduous tree with large simple leaves and distinctive bright red hairy fruits is Kamala Tree.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Kamala TreeMahua
SizeMedium deciduous tree, 8–15 m tall, straight trunk—
LeavesLarge, ovate, 10–20 cm, often with smaller leaflets at base (tripinnate)Clustered at branch tips, oblong-elliptic, 15–30 cm, leathery, glabrous
FlowersSmall, greenish-yellow, in drooping panicles, March–JuneCream/yellowish, fleshy, tubular, nocturnal, strongly fragrant; fall intact to ground
FruitDistinctive, covered with reddish glandular hairs (from afar appears reddish)Fleshy berry, 3–5 cm, green → yellow; 1–4 large seeds
Fruit colourBright red when mature, turning russet-brown when dry—
SeedsLarge, black, glossy—
The testRed glandular fruit covered with glands is distinctive; rub fruit to see red powder stain—
Tree form—Medium–large (10–20 m), dense spreading crown
Bark—Dark grey-brown, longitudinally fissured, rough
Season clue—March–April nights: strong sweet scent; fallen cream flowers cover ground by dawn
