Black-bellied Tern or River Tern? ब्लैक-बेलीड टर्न या नदी कुर्री?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

River Tern, as the Black-bellied Tern profile puts it

Larger (33–35 cm); white belly (not dark); orange bill

नदी कुर्री

Black-bellied Tern, as the River Tern profile puts it

Black-bellied Tern]] (Sterna acuticauda) — if sympatric

Smaller (28–29 cm); dark belly and vent; shorter tail fork

Black-bellied Tern: Small white tern with black cap + distinctive dark belly and vent + short yellow bill + yellow legs = Black-bellied Tern. The dark belly is the most diagnostic feature distinguishing it from other river terns.

River Tern: White tern with black cap + orange bill + deeply forked white tail + grey back = River Tern. Hovers and plunge-dives for fish. Medium-sized tern with distinctive orange bill.
