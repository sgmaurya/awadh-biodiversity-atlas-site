How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Pierrot, as the Malayan profile puts it

Castalius rosimon

Black-and-white lycaenid butterfly with striking black bands and spots on white wings; frequents open sunny scrub

Common Pierrot: Tiny bright blue butterfly (male) or duller brown female with pale spotted underside, always associated with ber (jujube) bushes, is the Common Pierrot.

Malayan: Tiny black-and-white lycaenid fluttering weakly in deep humid shade, with a spotted underside and minute hindwing tooth.
