How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Baillon's Crake, as the Ruddy-breasted Crake profile puts it

Smaller; greyer overall; less rufous; winter visitor

—

Baillon's Crake: A tiny, secretive crake in dense reed beds with long greenish legs, giving rapid "kirrip-kirrip" calls is Baillon's Crake. The size, secretive behaviour, and distinctive call are diagnostic.

Ruddy-breasted Crake: Ruddy-breasted crake is a small crake with a distinctive rufous or rusty breast and barred black-and-white undertail coverts. The harsh, distinctive call is diagnostic.
