Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brahminy Starling, as the Jungle Myna profile puts it

Sturnia pagodarum

Loose black crest on crown; bright rich rufous-fawn underparts; bluish bill with yellow tip

Brahminy Starling: Small starling with pale grey upperparts, rich orange-chestnut underparts, a long drooping black crest, and a yellow-and-blue bill is a Brahminy Starling.

Jungle Myna: Slaty-grey myna with erect nasal crest at base of bill, piercing pale eye, and no bare skin around eye.
