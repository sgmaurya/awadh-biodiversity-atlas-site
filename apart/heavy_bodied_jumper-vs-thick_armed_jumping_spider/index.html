How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Heavy-bodied Jumper, as the Thick-armed Jumping Spider profile puts it

Hyllus semicupreus

Much larger body size; heavy grey-brown body with golden hairs; lacking swollen front legs

Heavy-bodied Jumper: Robust, dark, metallic-sheened jumping spider on vegetation foliage, hunting in stalk-freeze-pounce sequence, = Hyllus semicupreus.

Thick-armed Jumping Spider: Tiny jumping spider with massively swollen, paddle-like, black-fringed first pair of legs and dark bronze-sheened body.
