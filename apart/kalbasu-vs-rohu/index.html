Kalbasu or Rohu? कालबासु या रोहू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कालबासुKalbasuLabeo calbasu
रोहूRohuLabeo rohita

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Kalbasu profile puts it

Lighter, bluish-grey dorsally with reddish scales; mouth is terminal (not downward-facing); lips are not fringed; lacks prominent barbels

रोहू — हल्की नीली-धूसर, सीधा मुँह, झिलमिल होंठ रहित

Kalbasu: Deep purplish-black body, ventral mouth with fringed lips, four distinct barbels, and bottom-dwelling habit identify the Kalbasu.

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.
