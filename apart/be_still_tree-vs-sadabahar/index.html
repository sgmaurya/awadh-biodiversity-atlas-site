Be-still Tree or Sadabahar? बड़ी चंद्रिका या सदाबहार?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sadabahar, as the Be-still Tree profile puts it

Smaller plant, flowers larger and more colorful (pink/white with contrasting center), leaves opposite not whorled

छोटा पौधा, रंगीन फूल, पत्तियाँ आमने-सामने

Be-still Tree: An evergreen shrub with glossy leaves arranged in whorls of four, small pale pink fragrant flowers, and shiny black berries is Rauvolfia tetraphylla.

Sadabahar: Herbaceous sprawling shrub, opposite oval leaves, 5-petalled flat-faced flowers with a contrasting dark eye, prolific fruiting on wasteground — sadabahar. Do not eat.
