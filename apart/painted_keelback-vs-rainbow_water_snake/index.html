How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Painted Keelback, as the Rainbow Water Snake profile puts it

Painted Keelback]] (Xenochrophis cerasogaster)

Larger (50–90 cm); dark with longitudinal yellow stripes (not ventral); keeled scales; darker belly

Painted Keelback: Any keeled, striped water snake found near wetlands and rice paddies is a Painted Keelback. It will flee into water when approached. The longitudinal yellow or orange stripes are distinctive.

Rainbow Water Snake: Any small water snake in shallow wetlands with a drab back and brilliantly yellow or orange-red belly is a Rainbow Water Snake. The color contrast is unmistakable.
