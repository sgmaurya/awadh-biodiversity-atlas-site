Billygoat Weed or Wedelia? जंगली पुदीना या वेडेलिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Billygoat Weed, as the Wedelia profile puts it

Erect annual; flower heads small, bluish-purple or white; leaves ovate

खड़े तने, छोटे नीले-बैंगनी फूल

Wedelia: Spreading creeping groundcover with succulent three-lobed leaves rooting at nodes, bearing bright golden-yellow daisy heads on erect stalks identifies Sphagneticola trilobata.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Billygoat WeedWedelia
FormErect, branching winter annual herb (30–100 cm tall), covered with soft, white spreading hairs—
LeavesOpposite, ovate leaves (2–8 cm long) with blunt teeth (crenate) and a hairy surface, smelling strongly of goat/mint when crushedOpposite, simple, fleshy, usually 3-lobed with toothed margins, dark glossy green (Pruski 1996)
FlowersSmall, discoid flower heads (4–6 mm diameter) grouped in flat-topped clusters; color is pale blue, violet, or white—
StemCylindrical, green or purplish stem, densely covered in long, soft white hairs—
FruitsSmall, dry, black achenes (1.5–2 mm long) with 5-awned scales, easily dispersed by wind and animal fur—
Habit—Spreading, mat-forming perennial herb with creeping stems rooting at nodes (Pruski 1996)
Stems—Cylindrical, green or purplish, strigose to glabrescent, rooting freely (Gaur 1999)
Capitula—Solitary, terminal or axillary, 2.0–3.5 cm across, on long erect peduncles (Pruski 1996)
Rays—Bright yellow to golden yellow, 8–13 per head, with 3-toothed tips (Gaur 1999)
Discs—Numerous, tubular, fertile, bright yellow with brownish anther tips

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
