How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Minnie Root, as the Philippine Violet profile puts it

Herbaceous tuberous plant with violet trumpet flowers; explosive cylindrical pods

कंद वाली जड़ी-बूटी, चटकने वाली लंबी फलियां

Minnie Root: Fleshy finger-like tuberous roots combined with showy violet-blue funnel flowers and explosive water-sensitive brown capsules immediately identify Ruellia tuberosa.

Philippine Violet: An unarmed hairy perennial shrub with violet-blue trumpet flowers and comb-like pectinate-toothed outer calyx lobes identifies Barleria cristata.
