Common Rosefinch or Red Avadavat? तूती या लाल मुनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लाल मुनियाRed AvadavatAmandava amandava

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Avadavat, as the Common Rosefinch profile puts it

Red Munia]] (Amandava amandava)

Much smaller; bright red overall; different shape

Common Rosefinch: Common Rosefinch males show rosy-red plumage (duller in winter); females are heavily streaked with unstreaked belly. If the bird shows a black bib and grey crown, it is a House Sparrow. If it is entirely red and tiny, it is a Red Munia.

Red Avadavat: Very small finch + brilliant red breeding male or buff female + flock living + near water + grass seed feeding + soft "zee" calls = Red Avadavat. The breeding male's bright red plumage is unmistakable.
