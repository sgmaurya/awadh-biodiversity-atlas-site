How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Sandpiper, as the Common Sandpiper profile puts it

Green Sandpiper]] (Tringa ochropus)

Darker blackish-green upperparts; white rump visible in flight; lacks white wing-bar; does not bob tail as persistently

Common Sandpiper: Small shorebird with olive-brown upperparts, white belly with a greyish smudge on the breast-side, constantly bobbing its tail, and flying low with stiff, bowed wings is a Common Sandpiper.

Green Sandpiper: A small dark-backed wader with white belly and dark underwings visible in flight, at small water bodies — is a Green Sandpiper. The dark back and dark underwing are diagnostic. Solitary or in pairs.
