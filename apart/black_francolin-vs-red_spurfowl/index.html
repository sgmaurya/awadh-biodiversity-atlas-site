Black Francolin or Red Spurfowl? काला तीतर या छोटी जंगली मुर्गी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Spurfowl, as the Black Francolin profile puts it

Red-rufous plumage; forest habitat

लाल तीतर

Black Francolin: Entirely black francolin with red legs and face patch = male Black Francolin. Brown, heavily barred = female Black Francolin. The distinctive loud call kataak-kataak is diagnostic.

Red Spurfowl: The rich rufous-chestnut plumage, bare brick-red orbital eye ring, coral-red legs with sharp spurs, and absence of a fleshy head comb distinguish the Red Spurfowl.

Spurs—Males carry multiple sharp spurs on each tarsus; females carry fewer or none
Alarm Call—Rapid, ringing, crowing cackle uttered while running into dense cover
