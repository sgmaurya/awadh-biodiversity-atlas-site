Black-winged Kite or White-eyed Buzzard? कपसी या तीसा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कपसीBlack-winged KiteElanus caeruleus
तीसाWhite-eyed BuzzardButastur teesa

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-winged Kite, as the White-eyed Buzzard profile puts it

Distinct grey-and-white plumage; jet-black shoulder patches; bright red eyes; hovers in mid-air

कपसी — सफ़ेद-धूसर शरीर, काले कंधे, लाल आँखें

Black-winged Kite: Small grey-and-white raptor, prominent jet-black shoulders, bright red eyes, and a stationary hovering flight over open fields identify the Black-winged Kite.

White-eyed Buzzard: Medium-sized brown-and-white raptor, conspicuous white eyes, white throat with black stripes, orange-yellow cere, sitting upright on a fence post is a White-eyed Buzzard.
