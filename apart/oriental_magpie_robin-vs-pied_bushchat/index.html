Oriental Magpie-Robin or Pied Bushchat? दहियर या पड्डू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Bushchat, as the Oriental Magpie-Robin profile puts it

Pied Bushchat]] (Saxicola caprata)

Smaller (13 cm); male is jet-black with white wing patch and rump (lacks white belly and breast); female is brown

Oriental Magpie-Robin, as the Pied Bushchat profile puts it

Oriental Magpie-Robin]] (Copsychus saularis)

Much larger (19–21 cm); glossy blue-black body with a white belly; long tail; lacks white rump

Oriental Magpie-Robin: Neat black-and-white pattern (grey-and-white in females), long white wing bar, white outer tail feathers, and a tail that is frequently cocked upward identify the Oriental Magpie-Robin.

Pied Bushchat: Small, upright bird perched boldly on wires or weed stalks, where the male is jet-black with a white wing bar and white rump, and the female is brown with a rusty-orange rump, is a Pied Bushchat.
