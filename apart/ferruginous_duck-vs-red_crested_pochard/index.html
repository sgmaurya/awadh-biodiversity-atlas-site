Ferruginous Duck or Red-crested Pochard? कुर्चिया या लालसर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लालसरRed-crested PochardNetta rufina

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ferruginous Duck, as the Red-crested Pochard profile puts it

Smaller; male has mahogany head (less bright); white eye

छोटी, गहरा भूरा सिर, सफेद आँख

Ferruginous Duck: A small, uniformly dark brown duck with distinctive white eye-ring (both sexes). Small bill and dainty appearance. Dives and dabbles; occurs in flocks on lakes and tanks.

Red-crested Pochard: A large diving duck with a chestnut-red head and bright yellow legs (males) — is a Red-crested Pochard. The yellow legs are diagnostic and unique among Indian pochards.
