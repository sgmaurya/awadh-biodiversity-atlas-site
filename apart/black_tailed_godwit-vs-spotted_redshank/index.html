How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Redshank, as the Black-tailed Godwit profile puts it

Spotted Redshank]] (Tringa erythropus)

Smaller (29–30 cm); shorter bill; different plumage and posture; different behaviour

Black-tailed Godwit: A long-legged, long-billed wader with white rump and wholly black tail with white base is Black-tailed Godwit. The white rump and black tail are diagnostic in flight.

Spotted Redshank: A tall, pale grey wader with long red legs and red bill, often wading in deep water alone, is a Spotted Redshank. The longer legs, paler plumage, and deeper wading distinguish it from the Common Redshank.
