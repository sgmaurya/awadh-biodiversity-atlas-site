Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rice Blast Fungus, as the Bakanae Disease of Rice profile puts it

Diamond-shaped leaf lesions with grey centre; appears on older leaves; not affecting seedling height

धान का झोंका — पत्तियों पर बीमारी, पौध लंबी नहीं होती

Bakanae Disease of Rice: Abnormally tall, thin, pale seedlings scattered among normal ones in the nursery identify bakanae. Healthy seedlings in same bed confirm seed-borne origin.

Rice Blast Fungus: Spindle-shaped, tan-centred, dark-bordered lesion on rice leaf in July–August = rice blast. Every other disease is either smaller, differently shaped, or less common in eastern UP.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Bakanae Disease of RiceRice Blast Fungus
Seedling heightAbnormally tall and slender, standing well above the healthy seedlings around it—
Leaf colourPale yellow to chlorotic (lack of green pigment); thin, spindly leaves—
Leaf appearanceThin, wiry, drawn-out; older leaves may show reddish or brownish tints—
Plant structureWeak, spindly stems; cannot stand upright; collapse under water or weight—
Root systemPoorly developed; sparse; dark or necrotic; weak anchor roots—
Nursery patternScattered affected plants among normal ones, because the infection came with individual seeds—
Leaf lesion—Spindle-shaped or diamond-shaped lesions on rice leaves; tan or grey-brown centre with darker borders
Lesion size—0.5–2 cm long; distinctive necrotic (dead) zone in the middle
Lesion margin—Dark brown or reddish-purple; sharply defined
Lesion centre—Ashy grey to tan; may show concentric rings of spore-bearing structures (conidia) visible as fine powder under magnification
Collar lesion—Often appears first on leaf collar (junction of leaf blade and sheath); coalesces to form large necrotic patches
Panicle symptom—Bleached (whitened) or black panicles; grains shrivelled and empty; infection at panicle base (neck blast) causes entire panicle to droop
Stem infection—Brown to black discolouration at the base of tillers and nodes
Symptom severity—Can kill entire shoot if fungus enters nodes; premature ripening and grain death

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
