Much larger; palmately lobed leaves; pink flowers; spiny burr-like fruit

Caesar Weed: Caesar Weed is hairy and has palmately lobed leaves with pink flowers and spiny burr-like fruits. Country Mallow has entire (unsplit) heart-shaped leaves. False Mallow is much smaller with yellow flowers.

False Mallow: False Mallow is small with tiny yellow flowers and a distinctive spiky ball-fruit. Larger Malvaceae have bigger flowers and different leaf shapes.
