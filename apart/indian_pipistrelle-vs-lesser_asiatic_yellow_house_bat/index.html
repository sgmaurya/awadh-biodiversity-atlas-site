How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Pipistrelle, as the Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat profile puts it

Indian Pipistrelle]] (Pipistrellus coromandra)

Much smaller size; erratic flickering flight; dark blackish-brown coat

Indian Pipistrelle: Tiny size (forearm length under 31 mm), dark brown fur, rapid zigzag flight close to structures at dusk, and ultrasonic hunting calls identify the Indian Pipistrelle.

Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat: The Lesser Asiatic Yellow House Bat is distinguished by its medium size, warm yellowish-brown fur, robust muzzle, and early, steady flight at twilight (Blanford 1888, p. 321).

FurSoft and dense; dark grey-brown to chocolate-brown dorsally; slightly lighter below—
WingsThin, dark brown wing membranes (patagium) extending to the base of the toes—
EarsSmall, rounded ears with a distinct, short, curved inner ear flap (tragus)—
FlightFluttering, erratic, and zigzag flight pattern close to tree canopies and roofs at dusk—
Build & Size—Medium robust bat; head and body are 3 inches long, tail 2, forearm less than 2 inches (Blanford 1888)
Ears & Tragus—Short subtriangular ears, rounded tips; tragus long, narrow, curved forward and inward (Blanford 1888)
Dorsal Fur—Yellowish-brown above, varying to chestnut, golden brown, or greyish brown (Blanford 1888)
Ventral Fur—Pale dull buff, yellowish-grey, or dirty white below (Blanford 1888)
Flight Habit—Emerges early in twilight; flies more slowly and steadily than small pipistrelles (Blanford 1888)
