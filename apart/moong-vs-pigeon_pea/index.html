Green Gram or Pigeon Pea? मूँग या अरहर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मूँगGreen GramVigna radiata
अरहरPigeon PeaCajanus cajan

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pigeon Pea, as the Green Gram profile puts it

Taller (1–2 m), with longer internodes; larger leaflets; longer pods (6–8 cm) containing 3–4 larger seeds

अरहर — बड़ा पौधा, लंबी फली

Green Gram: A bushy, compact legume with trifoliate leaves, small pale yellow flowers, and slender, straight, bright green pods containing tiny, glossy, bright green seeds; harvested as a dry seed crop in September–October is moong.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Green GramPigeon Pea
Plant formBushy annual herb, 30–50 cm tall, densely branched—
LeavesAlternate, compound, trifoliate (3 leaflets), 5–10 cm, ovate, softAlternately arranged, trifoliate (3 leaflets); leaflets are oblong-lanceolate (5–10 cm long, 2–4 cm wide), velvety green above, pale silvery-green with fine hairs below
FlowersSmall, pale yellow, in short clusters (racemes), 0.5–1 cmLarge (1.5–2 cm long), pea-like (papilionaceous), bright yellow or yellow with reddish-purple stripes on the back of the largest petal (standard)
PodsCylindrical, 7–10 cm, straight, green when immature, containing 7–10 seeds—
SeedsSmall, 3–5 mm, glossy, bright green (fresh) or yellow-green (dried)Round to oval (4–6 mm diameter), slightly compressed, light brown, tan, or cream-colored, with a small white scar (hilum)
StemHerbaceous, angular, slightly hairy—
Form—Tall, woody, branched leguminous shrub (1.5–3 m tall) with a deep taproot and ribbed, hairy green stems
Fruit—Linear-oblong, flat, hairy pod (5–9 cm long) marked with dark oblique lines or mottling between the seeds; ending in a sharp beak
