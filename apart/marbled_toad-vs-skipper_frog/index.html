Marbled Toad or Skittering Frog? चितकबरा भेंडुक या कूद मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Skittering Frog, as the Marbled Toad profile puts it

Always at the water, skitters across it; smooth and spotted

हमेशा पानी पर

Marbled Toad: A pale, marbled, warty toad whose head has no black ridges is the marbled toad; ridges mean the common toad.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Marbled ToadSkittering Frog
Size50–85 mm; females larger4–7 cm snout-vent length
ColourPale olive, grey or sandy, marbled with darker blotches; often a pale line down the back; belly whitishOlive-green to brown with irregular darker spots; dark mid-dorsal line often present
HeadWithout the bony black ridges that run along the eyes of the common toad; the skin of the head smooth—
SkinWarty but less so than the common toad; the parotoid glands behind the eyes oval, moderateSmooth with small tubercles; not warty like toads
ToesWebbed about halfway; a pale tubercle on the heel—
CallA long, rattling, pulsed trill, lower and more drawn-out than the common toad's—
Eyes—Protruding, positioned on top of the head to look above water while body is submerged
Hind legs—Long and powerful with fully webbed feet
Behaviour—Startled frogs skitter rapidly across the water surface before diving
