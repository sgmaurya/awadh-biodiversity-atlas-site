Striped Catfish or Tengra Catfish? टेंगरा या टेंगरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Catfish, as the Tengra Catfish profile puts it

Slightly larger; darker stripes; occurs in different geographic range

एक अन्य टेंगरा — गहरी धारियाँ

Striped Catfish: Small size (under 18 cm), scale-less body with 4–5 dark parallel stripes, and maxillary barbels extending to the pelvic fins identify Mystus vittatus.

Tengra Catfish: A small, striped catfish 5–8 cm long with three pairs of prominent barbels and distinctive vertical light bars or stripes on a darker body, found in ponds and streams, is Mystus tengara; microscopic examination of gill raker count and spine morphology confirms identification.
