Adusa or Porcupine Flower? अडूसा या कटसरैया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Adusa, as the Porcupine Flower profile puts it

Much taller thornless shrub with large white two-lipped flowers

बिना कांटों वाली बड़ी झाड़ी, बड़े सफेद फूल

Adusa: A sturdy shrub with large, glossy, opposite leaves and dense white-and-purple flower spikes is adusa.

Porcupine Flower: A low bushy shrub armed with sharp axillary yellowish thorns bearing bright golden-yellow tubular flowers identifies Barleria prionitis.
