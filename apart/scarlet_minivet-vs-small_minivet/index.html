How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Small Minivet, as the Scarlet Minivet profile puts it

Small Minivet]] (Pericrocotus cinnamomeus)

Much smaller build, male has grey upperparts with orange-red breast, and female has pale yellow wash

Scarlet Minivet, as the Small Minivet profile puts it

Larger; brighter scarlet in male; different habitat

चमकीली सहेली

Scarlet Minivet: The large size, glossy black head and mantle contrasting with fiery scarlet underparts in the male, and grey back, orange-yellow forehead and bright yellow underparts in the female identify the Scarlet Minivet.

Small Minivet: The smallest minivet; males striking black-and-orange, females dull grey-and-yellow. Always in organized flocks with soft contact calls. Active gleaners in trees; coordinated flock movement distinctive.
