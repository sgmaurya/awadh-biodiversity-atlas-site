How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spiny Amaranth, as the East Indian Globe Thistle profile puts it

Taller, spines on stem and petioles; red flowers in dense spikes, not globose heads

स्पाइनी पौधा, लाल फूल की बालियाँ

East Indian Globe Thistle: A small, soft-stemmed herb with tiny leaves and very distinctive compact, round, spiky purple flower heads is Sphaeranthus indicus.

Spiny Amaranth: Erect green weed with branched stems, bearing a pair of sharp, needle-like white spines (5–15 mm) at the base of every leaf stem, and greenish cylindrical flower spikes, is Spiny Amaranth.
