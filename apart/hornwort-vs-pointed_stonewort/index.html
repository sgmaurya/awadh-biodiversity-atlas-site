How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hornwort, as the Pointed Stonewort profile puts it

Ceratophyllum demersum

Free-floating rootless plant with stiff, repeatedly forked leaves bearing fine marginal spinules

Hornwort: A fully submerged, free-floating plant with needle-like, finely divided leaves in stiff whorls and no roots is hornwort.

Pointed Stonewort: Soft, completely smooth, translucent green branching macro-alga with branchlets ending in sharp mucronate tips, lacking rough calcification.
