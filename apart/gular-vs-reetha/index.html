Cluster Fig or Soapnut? गूलर या रीठा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गूलरCluster FigFicus racemosa
रीठाSoapnutSapindus mukorossi

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Soapnut, as the Cluster Fig profile puts it

Pinnate leaves with no terminal leaflet; fruit is a saponin-rich drupe, not a fig

पंखनुमा पत्तियाँ, साबुन का फल

Cluster Fig: A tree with sandpaper-rough leaves and figs growing directly on the trunk in clusters is the cluster fig; no other tree in the district combines these characters.

Soapnut: A tall tree with long feathery leaves whose yellow-brown fruit lathers when rubbed in water is reetha; nothing else in the district does that.
