Bronze-winged Jacana or Pheasant-tailed Jacana? दल-पीपी या पिहो?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pheasant-tailed Jacana, as the Bronze-winged Jacana profile puts it

Pheasant-tailed Jacana]] (Hydrophasianus chirurgus)

Breeding plumage is white and chocolate-brown with a long tail; non-breeding is pale with a yellow necklace; prefers open water

Bronze-winged Jacana, as the Pheasant-tailed Jacana profile puts it

Bronze-winged Jacana]] (Metopelus hypoleucus)

Larger; chestnut-brown on upperparts; occurs in southeast Asia (absent from eastern UP)

Bronze-winged Jacana: Glossy black body, bronze wings, a broad white stripe running behind the eye, and extremely long spider-like toes identify the Bronze-winged Jacana.

Pheasant-tailed Jacana: A small wader with extremely long, unwebbed toes, walking on floating lotus leaves and lily pads in wetlands — is a Pheasant-tailed Jacana. The long toes and small size are diagnostic. Breeding males show dramatic long tail streamers.
