How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Verditer Flycatcher, as the Ultramarine Flycatcher profile puts it

Verditer Flycatcher]] (Eumyias thalassinus)

Larger size, entirely bright electric copper-blue plumage without white eyebrow or belly, and black eye-patch

Ultramarine Flycatcher: The combination of small size, bright white supercilium, dark blue upperparts, and white underparts with blue breast sides immediately distinguishes the male Ultramarine Flycatcher (Baker 1924).

Verditer Flycatcher: A small, blue and white flycatcher. Males are brilliantly blue-green; females duller but still blue. Hunts insects by sallying from exposed perches. Restless and active; characteristic clear whistled calls.
