Indigo Flash or Large Oakblue? इंडिगो फ्लैश या बड़ा ओकब्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Oakblue, as the Indigo Flash profile puts it

Arhopala amantes]]

Much larger butterfly with mottled dark underside and iridescent purple-blue upperside

Indigo Flash, as the Large Oakblue profile puts it

Rapala varuna]]

Wings upperside dark indigo-black, underside plain greyish with a single narrow white-edged band

Indigo Flash: Notice the dark indigo-blue gloss of the male in flight, the smooth earthy brown underside with a neat white-edged line, and ber shrub associations.

Large Oakblue: Look for the large size, vibrant metallic purple-blue upperside in flight, and the intricate dark brown underside with green tornal scales when settled.
