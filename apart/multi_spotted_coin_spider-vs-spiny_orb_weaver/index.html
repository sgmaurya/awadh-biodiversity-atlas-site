Multi-spotted Coin Spider: A flat, coin-like spider with scalloped edges and numerous circular pitted spots, sitting motionless head-down against tree bark within a narrow vertical ladder web, is Herennia multipuncta.

Spiny Orb-weaver: Small, hard, flat-bodied spider (8–10 mm) with a white/yellow top bearing six sharp black spines, sitting in the center of a vertical orb web, is the Spiny Orb-weaver.
