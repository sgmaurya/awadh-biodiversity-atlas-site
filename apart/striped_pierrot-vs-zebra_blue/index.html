How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Zebra Blue, as the Striped Pierrot profile puts it

Underside covered with wavy grey-brown zebra-like bands rather than black stripes and dots on a white ground

Striped Pierrot: A small lycaenid butterfly flitting around ber trees whose creamy underside features confluent black basal stripes rather than isolated dots is the Striped Pierrot.

Zebra Blue: A tiny blue butterfly with males bright metallic blue and females dull brown, distinguished by the bold zigzag or zebra-like line pattern on the pale underside, is the Zebra Blue.
