Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Himalayan Flameback, as the Greater Flameback profile puts it

Himalayan Flameback]] (Dinopium shorii)

Smaller size; yellowish-olive mantle; lacks clean white hindneck stripe; three toes; female has brownish crest

Greater Flameback: The combination of four toes, a brilliant red lower back and rump, clean white hindneck stripe, and boldly scaled breast pattern distinguishes the Greater Flameback from three-toed flamebacks.

Himalayan Flameback: Golden-backed woodpecker with three toes, double black line enclosing a pale brownish throat streak, and white-streaked crown in female.

HindneckProminent clean white stripe extending down the nape and hindneck—

Breast PatternWhite or buffy feathers broadly bordered with black, forming scaled appearance—
Throat pattern—Double black line running down the centre of the throat enclosing a pale brownish patch
Mantle and back—Brilliant golden-olive to orange-gold upperparts with scarlet wash across lower back and rump
Underparts—Buff-white underbody with black-bordered scales, creating bold chain-like markings on breast
Feet morphology—Tridactyl foot arrangement possessing only three functional toes (two forward, one rear)
