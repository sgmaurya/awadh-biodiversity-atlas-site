How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peacock Pansy, as the Yellow Pansy profile puts it

Peacock Pansy]] (Junonia almana)

Similar eyespots; darker overall; different colouration; smaller eyespots with purple instead of blue centers

Peacock Pansy: Medium size, bright orange-brown upperwings, two very large peacock-like eyespots (with double blue-white pupils on the hindwings), and seasonal dry-season leaf camouflage identify the Peacock Pansy.

Yellow Pansy: A bright orange-yellow butterfly with very large, iridescent-centred eyespots on orange-yellow wings, found in open grasslands, is the Yellow Pansy.
