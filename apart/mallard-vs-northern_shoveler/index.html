How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mallard, as the Northern Shoveler profile puts it

Mallard]] (Anas platyrhynchos)

Larger; bill normal-sized, not spatulate

Mallard: A large duck with a glossy green head and white chest (males) — is a Mallard. This is by far the most familiar duck species globally. Females shown blue speculum in flight and paired with green-headed males in winter.

Northern Shoveler: The Northern Shoveler is the only duck in the region with a greatly enlarged, spatulate (spoon-shaped) bill. This feature is diagnostic at any distance.
