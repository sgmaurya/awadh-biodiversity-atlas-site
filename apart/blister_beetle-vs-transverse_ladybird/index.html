Red Blister Beetle or Transverse Ladybird? छाला भृंग या लेडीबर्ड बीटल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लेडीबर्ड बीटलTransverse LadybirdCoccinella transversalis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Transverse Ladybird, as the Red Blister Beetle profile puts it

Much smaller; hard rounded elytra; not elongated; not toxic

Transverse Ladybird: Small size, dome-shaped orange-red elytra, a black thorax with two creamy corner spots, and three pairs of transverse, wavy black bands across the wings identify the Transverse Ladybird.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Red Blister BeetleTransverse Ladybird
Size15–25 mm; elongated, soft-bodiedSmall, rounded beetle; body length 5–7 mm; width 4–5 mm
ColourBrilliant orange-red elytra (wing covers) with black spots or irregular bands—
BodySoft, flexible; head narrower than thorax (characteristic of Meloidae)—
WarningAposematic — bright colours warn predators of toxicity—
BehaviourAggregated feeding on flowers; releases haemolymph when disturbed (autohemorrhage)—
Colouration—Convex, shiny orange-red to brick-red wings (elytra); black head and thorax
Elytra Pattern—A black suture stripe down the center; 3 pairs of transverse, wavy black bands across the wings
Thorax—Solid black with two distinct, small creamy-white spots on the front corners
Antennae—Short, clubbed antennae used for smelling aphid colonies

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
