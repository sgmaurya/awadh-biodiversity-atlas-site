Bael or Wood Apple? बेल या कैथा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Apple, as the Bael profile puts it

Wood Apple]] (Limonia acidissima)

Leaves have 5–7 small leaflets on a winged stalk; fruit is smaller with a rough, grey scurfy shell and highly acidic pulp

Bael: Compound leaves with exactly three leaflets, sharp axillary thorns, and a large round woody-shelled fruit containing sweet orange-yellow pulp identify Aegle marmelos.

Side by side आमने-सामने

FlowersGreenish-white or pale cream-colored, 4–5 petalled (1.5–2 cm across), forming sweet-scented clustersSmall (1.0–1.5 cm), dull red, pinkish, or greenish-white, arranged in loose terminal clusters
BarkGreyish-brown, rough, corky, and shallowly furrowed on mature trunks—
Form—Small to medium-sized tree (8–12 m) with a loose, thorny, irregular crown; branches are drooping
Spines—Sharp, straight spines (1–4 cm long) present at the leaf axils on younger branches
Pulp—Dark brown, sticky, fibrous, with a strong aromatic, sour-sweet, and pungent smell when the shell is broken
