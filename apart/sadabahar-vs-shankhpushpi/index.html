Sadabahar or Shankhpushpi? सदाबहार या शंखपुष्पी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सदाबहारSadabaharCatharanthus roseus
शंखपुष्पीShankhpushpiEvolvulus alsinoides

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sadabahar, as the Shankhpushpi profile puts it

Flowers larger (15–20 mm), pink or white; opposite leaves; perennial shrub

बड़े फूल, झाड़ी

Sadabahar: Herbaceous sprawling shrub, opposite oval leaves, 5-petalled flat-faced flowers with a contrasting dark eye, prolific fruiting on wasteground — sadabahar. Do not eat.

Shankhpushpi: A small, trailing herb with tiny blue-pink funnel flowers opening in the morning and closing by midday is shankhpushpi.
