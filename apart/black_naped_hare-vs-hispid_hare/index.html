How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-naped Hare, as the Hispid Hare profile puts it

Indian Hare / Black-naped Hare]] (Lepus nigricollis)

Much longer ears; softer greyish-brown fur; distinct black velvety patch across nape

Black-naped Hare: Long ears, grizzled rufous-brown coat, long hind legs, and a distinctive black patch on the back of the neck distinguish the Black-naped Hare from other lagomorphs.

Hispid Hare: The Hispid Hare is separated from the Indian Hare by its very short ears (shorter than the skull), small eyes, coarse bristly coat, and tail brown throughout, darker above (Blanford 1891, p. 454).
