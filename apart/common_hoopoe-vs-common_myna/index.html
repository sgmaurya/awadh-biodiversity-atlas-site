Common Hoopoe or Common Myna? हुदहुद या मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मैनाCommon MynaAcridotheres tristis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Myna, as the Common Hoopoe profile puts it

Similar size on the ground; lacks crest and long curved bill; uniform brown with yellow eye patch

मैना — भूरा शरीर, पीली आँख का टुकड़ा, छोटी चोंच

Common Hoopoe: The combination of a long decurved bill, sandy-buff body, bold black-and-white wings, and a spectacular erectile crest separates the Common Hoopoe from all other Indian birds.

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common HoopoeCommon Myna
SizeMedium-sized slender bird (25–32 cm total length); approximately size of a MynaMedium-sized starling (23–26 cm total length); stocky build
CrestLarge, crown-like fan of black-tipped, sandy-buff feathers; erectile at will—
ColourationSandy-orange head, neck, and breast; black-and-white striped wings and tail—
BillLong, thin, slightly down-curved, and dark; adapted for soil probing—
FlightDistinctive undulating, erratic flight resembling a giant butterfly—
ForagingWalks actively on the ground, probing lawns and loose soil—
Plumage—Brown body, glossy black hood (head and neck); white patches on wings
Bare parts—Bright yellow bill and legs; prominent bare yellow skin patch behind and around the eye
In flight—Conspicuous white wing flash covering about one-third of the wing
Post-ocular skin—Bare yellow patch, diagnostic at a glance
Behaviour—Walks confidently on the ground (doesn't hop); head tilted, alert; highly vocal

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
