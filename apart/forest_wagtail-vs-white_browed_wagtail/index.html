Forest Wagtail or White-browed Wagtail? फॉरेस्ट वैगटेल या ममूला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-browed Wagtail, as the Forest Wagtail profile puts it

White-browed Wagtail]] (Motacilla maderaspatensis)

Large, boldly pied black-and-white plumage with a single solid black bib; wags tail vertically; strictly near water

Forest Wagtail: The olive-brown upperparts, double black breast band, bold yellowish-white wing bars, and unique lateral (side-to-side) tail swaying distinguish the Forest Wagtail from all Motacilla wagtails.

White-browed Wagtail: The only wagtail with a bold white eyebrow stripe. Year-round resident of water margins, never in fields.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Forest WagtailWhite-browed Wagtail
HabitSolitary or paired woodland wagtail of shaded forest floors and quiet trails—
SizeSlender wagtail roughly the size of a [[white_browed_wagtail\Small wagtail, 20–21 cm including long tail
Breast PatternStriking double black breast band: a solid upper crescent and broken lower bar—
Tail MovementUniquely wags its tail side-to-side (laterally) rather than up and down—
WingsBroad yellowish-white double wing bars and conspicuous pale wing patches—
UpperpartsUniform olive-brown above, with prominent whitish supercilium and dark eye-stripe—
PerchingFreely perches across horizontal branches, unlike open-country wagtails—
Distinctive feature—Bold white supercilium (eyebrow); darker crown and back
Plumage—Dark grey-brown above; white supercilium; white throat and underparts; darker on flanks
Tail—Long, constantly wagged; dark with white edges
Behaviour—Runs rapidly on ground near water; territorial and vocal
Habitat—Strictly on water margins and riparian zones; not in fields
Season—Year-round resident

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
