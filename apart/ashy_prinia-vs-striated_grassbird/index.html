How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashy Prinia, as the Striated Grassbird profile puts it

Ashy Prinia]] (Prinia socialis)

Shorter tail; ashy-grey above; often in gardens and scrub

Striated Grassbird: A dark, heavily streaked warbler of tall wet grasses and reed beds. Smaller and darker than Ashy Prinia. Secretive; identify by song—a distinctive rambling warble. Look in dense vegetation along water bodies.
