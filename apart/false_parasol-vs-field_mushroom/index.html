How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

False Parasol, as the Field Mushroom profile puts it

False Parasol]])

False Parasol: Any large white or pale mushroom with green gills is a Chlorophyllum species and should be presumed toxic. To confirm: make a spore print on white paper; if the print is green, do not eat. Parasol mushroom has white gills and white spore print.
