Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Skimmer, as the Fulvous Forest Skimmer profile puts it

Scarlet Skimmer]]

Larger; bright scarlet-red body; clear wings without dark brown coloration

Fulvous Forest Skimmer: Medium size, dark rust-brown wings with transparent black tips, and a preference for perching in shaded orchards and pond edges identify the Fulvous Forest Skimmer.

Scarlet Skimmer: Medium-large size, uniform scarlet-red body with a flat, broad abdomen, thin black dorsal line, and lack of dark wing patches identify the Scarlet Skimmer.
