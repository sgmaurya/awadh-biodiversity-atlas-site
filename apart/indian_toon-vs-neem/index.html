Indian Toon or Neem? टून या नीम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Indian Toon profile puts it

Has curved simple pinnate leaves; flowers are white; fruit is a green-yellow edible drupe

नीम — मुड़े हुए पत्ते, सफेद फूल, मीठे फल

Indian Toon: Large tree with rough grey-brown fissured bark, pinnate leaves with long-pointed lanceolate leaflets, terminal drooping clusters of small white flowers, and small woody star-shaped dry fruits, is the Indian Toon.

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.
