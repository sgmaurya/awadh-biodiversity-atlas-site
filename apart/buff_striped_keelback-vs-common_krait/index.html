How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Krait, as the Buff-striped Keelback profile puts it

Extremely venomous. Steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody; has a row of large hexagonal vertebral scales; lacks longitudinal stripes.

करैत — अत्यधिक विषैला, आड़ी पट्टियां

Common Krait: Highly polished steel-black body with narrow white crossbands starting midbody, combined with enlarged hexagonal vertebral scales along the backbone, identifies Bungarus caeruleus.
