How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Country Mallow, as the Caesar Weed profile puts it

Country Mallow]])

Leaves heart-shaped, not palmately lobed; fruit 5-chambered, hairy, not spiny

Caesar Weed, as the Country Mallow profile puts it

Caesar Weed]] (Urena lobata)

Palmately lobed leaves, not cordate; pink flowers; larger burr-like fruit

Caesar Weed: Caesar Weed is hairy and has palmately lobed leaves with pink flowers and spiny burr-like fruits. Country Mallow has entire (unsplit) heart-shaped leaves. False Mallow is much smaller with yellow flowers.

Country Mallow: Country Mallow has heart-shaped (cordate) leaves and yellow flowers. Caesar Weed has lobed leaves and pink flowers.
