Pygmy Dartlet, as the White-dartlet profile puts it

Male thorax green and black, never developing dense white pruinescence; terminal abdomen segments orange

Pygmy Dartlet: The tiniest damselfly of the region — body length under 22 mm, bright metallic blue (male) with black rings, in shallow vegetation-rich water.

White-dartlet: A miniature damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose mature male has a frosted chalky-white thorax and basal abdomen contrasting sharply with bright fiery-orange terminal segments is the White-dartlet.
