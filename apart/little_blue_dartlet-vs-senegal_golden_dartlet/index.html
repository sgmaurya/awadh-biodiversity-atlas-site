Little Blue Dartlet: A miniature damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose male has a black-and-blue striped thorax, black abdomen with blue basal rings, and entirely bright azure-blue segments 8 and 9 is the Little Blue Dartlet.

Senegal Golden Dartlet: Small damselfly with bright yellow-orange head and thorax (male) or polymorphic females (yellow, green, or brown forms), in shallow vegetation-rich water.
