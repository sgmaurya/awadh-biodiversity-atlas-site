Orange Jasmine or White Aak? कामिनी या सफेद आक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White Aak, as the Orange Jasmine profile puts it

Much larger leaves and flowers; milky latex; waxy pink flowers

बहुत बड़ी पत्तियाँ; गुलाबी फूल; लेटेक्स

Orange Jasmine: A small, compact, evergreen shrub with tiny glossy aromatic leaves and profusely-produced small white highly-fragrant flowers is Orange Jasmine.

White Aak: A soft, grey-blue, waxy-leaved shrub with pale pink or white waxy flowers and inflated green fruit pods, growing in dry wastelands, is white aak. The latex is toxic.
