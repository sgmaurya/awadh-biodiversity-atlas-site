Dussumier's Seed Bug or Spotted Dirt Bug? दुसुमियर सीड बग या चित्तीदार डर्ट बग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Dirt Bug, as the Dussumier's Seed Bug profile puts it

Dark brown to blackish; much smaller; forelegs swollen with spines

गहरे भूरे-काले रंग का, बहुत छोटा, मोटे कांटेदार पैर

Dussumier's Seed Bug, as the Spotted Dirt Bug profile puts it

Bright scarlet-red and black; twice as large; forelegs slender, unspined

चमकीला लाल-काला, बड़ा कीड़ा, पतले पैर

Dussumier's Seed Bug: Sanguineous red seed bug with two broad black stripes on the red pronotum, a black scutellum with a red tip, and black wing covers with a central transverse black bar identifies Graptostethus servus.

Spotted Dirt Bug: Small dark brown ground-dwelling seed bug with conspicuously swollen spined front thighs, constricted two-lobed pronotum, and white spots on the wings identifies Pseudopachybrachius guttus.
