Grey-headed Lapwing or Yellow-wattled Lapwing? ग्रे-हेडेड लैपविंग या ज़र्दी टिटहरी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-wattled Lapwing, as the Grey-headed Lapwing profile puts it

Yellow-wattled Lapwing]] (Vanellus malabaricus)

Smaller; yellow wattles at base of bill; resident; lacks grey head

Grey-headed Lapwing, as the Yellow-wattled Lapwing profile puts it

Grey-headed Lapwing]] (Vanellus cinereus) — winter visitor

Grey head; winter visitor only (October–March)

Grey-headed Lapwing: Grey-headed Lapwing is distinguished by its slate-grey head, white cheeks, and black-and-white wing pattern in flight. The yellowish-green legs and wailing call are also diagnostic.

Yellow-wattled Lapwing: Yellow face wattles + brown and white plumage + yellow legs = Yellow-wattled Lapwing. Other lapwings either lack wattles, have red wattles, or have different plumage patterns.
