Crimson Marsh Glider or Ditch Jewel? लाल व्याधपतंग या पीली व्याधपतंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ditch Jewel, as the Crimson Marsh Glider profile puts it

Ditch Jewel]]

Smaller; orange-yellow body; wings with prominent yellow-orange bands; pollution indicator

Crimson Marsh Glider, as the Ditch Jewel profile puts it

Crimson Marsh Glider]]

Pinkish-crimson body; clear wings with amber base only; prefers cleaner slow water

Crimson Marsh Glider: Medium size, pinkish-crimson body with purple venation at the wing bases, and frequent perching behavior identify the Crimson Marsh Glider.

Ditch Jewel: Small size, bright yellow-orange body in males with a distinct orange band in the middle of each wing, and habitat preference for polluted drains identify the Ditch Jewel.
