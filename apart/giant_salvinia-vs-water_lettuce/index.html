Giant Salvinia or Water Lettuce? सालविनिया या जलपत्ता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Lettuce, as the Giant Salvinia profile puts it

Velvety pale-green rosette; no visible lower appendages; larger leaves (3–8 cm)

मख़मली, हल्का हरा, कोई निचली संरचना नहीं

Giant Salvinia: Floating, oval, vivid green fronds in pairs with a fuzzy texture and feathery brown structures underneath = giant salvinia. Frond papillae and exponential growth are key.

Water Lettuce: Spongy, pale green, velvet-textured sessile leaves arranged in a tight cabbage-like floating rosette identify Pistia stratiotes.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Giant SalviniaWater Lettuce
FormFloating fern; fronds in oval or fan-like rosettes; forms dense mats—
Upper frondsOval to obovate, 2–5 cm long, vivid green, smooth; arranged in pairs along a midrib—
Lower frondsHighly modified; submerged, hair-like, feathery, dark brown or reddish; act as false roots and anchors—
Hair-like structuresOn upper frond surface; distinctive papillae (bumps) give a fuzzy appearance—
Growth patternExponential; colonies double in 12–16 days in warm water—
StolonsHorizontal runners link plants, forming continuous mats across water surface—
Habitat Class—Free-floating aquatic plant; floats freely on the surface of standing or slow-moving water
Leaves—Wedge-shaped, pale yellowish-green, arranged in a tight cabbage-like rosette; soft velvety texture
Leaf Hairs—Covered in dense, soft, water-repellent hairs (trichomes) that trap air and keep the leaf dry
Petiole—Session leaves; lacks any distinct leaf stalks or petioles
Flowers—Tiny, greenish-white, inconspicuous; hidden in the center of the rosette inside a small leaf-like bract
Roots—Massive, feathery, dangling roots (up to 50 cm long); suspended in the water column
