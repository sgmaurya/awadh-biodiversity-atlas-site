How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tree Pipit, as the Rosy Pipit profile puts it

Tree Pipit]] (Anthus trivialis)

Prefers dry woodland edges and groves; perches freely in trees; lacks yellow underwing coverts; different call

Rosy Pipit: The heavily streaked blackish-olive upperparts, yellow underwing coverts and axillaries, habitat restricted to wet marshy ground, and pinkish breast in late winter separate the Rosy Pipit from both the resident Paddyfield Pipit and the arboreal Tree Pipit.

Tree Pipit: The Tree Pipit is a slender pipit with fine streaking, often seen perching on trees and launching to the ground. Its habit of returning from ground to tree is distinctive. If the bird stays on ground and appears darker and less buffy, it is likely Olive-backed Pipit.
