How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chalky Percher: A small, compact dragonfly perching on exposed stick tips over water whose mature male has a brilliant chalky-white pruinose body, metallic green eyes, and small amber patches at the hindwing base is the Chalky Percher.

Yellow-tailed Ashy Skimmer: Small, ashy-grey dragonfly with distinctive yellow-orange abdomen tip distinguishes this species.
