How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Razorbelly Minnow, as the Gangetic Ailia profile puts it

Large Razorbelly Minnow]])

Cyprinid with scales; rayed dorsal fin present; completely lacks barbels

Gangetic Ailia: An elongate, paper-thin, translucent silvery catfish completely lacking a rayed dorsal fin, possessing a tiny adipose fin, eight long barbels, and an anal fin of 58–75 rays, is Ailia coila.

Large Razorbelly Minnow: A slender, elongate, surface-dwelling silvery minnow with an upward-directed mouth, an acute razor keel behind the pelvic fins, and 86–110 scales along the lateral line, is Salmostoma bacaila.
