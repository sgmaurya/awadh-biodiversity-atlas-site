How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher, as the Indian Paradise Flycatcher profile puts it

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher]] (Cyornis tickelliae)

Small (16–17 cm); blue plumage; different habitat (understory)

Indian Paradise Flycatcher: Indian Paradise Flycatcher male is unmistakable with long white tail streamers and glossy black-white plumage. Female and non-breeding male are chestnut-buff and occur in tall forest canopy in summer (April–September). No other bird matches this combination.

Tickell's Blue Flycatcher: Tickell's Blue Flycatcher is a small blue flycatcher with peachy-buff underparts (in males). Males are brilliant blue; females duller. Favours shaded forest understory. If the bird is larger or lacks the peachy underparts, it is likely a different species.
