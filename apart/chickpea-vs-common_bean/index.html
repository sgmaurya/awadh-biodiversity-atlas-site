Chickpea or Common Bean? चना या राजमा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

राजमाCommon BeanPhaseolus vulgaris

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chickpea, as the Common Bean profile puts it

Chickpea]] [FLR-0056])

Much smaller bushy plant; leaflets are tiny and serrated; pods are swollen and inflated

Common Bean: A legume with three-leaflet compound leaves, papilionaceous flowers in clusters, and long straight pods containing kidney-shaped seeds of variable color distinguishes common bean.

Side by side आमने-सामने

ChickpeaCommon Bean
FormSmall, erect, bushy annual herb (20–50 cm tall), covered in sticky glandular hairsErect bushy or climbing annual herb (30–60 cm tall), with three-leaflet compound leaves
LeavesAlternately arranged, pinnately compound (5–8 cm long) with 9–15 pairs of small, ovate leaflets carrying sharply serrated (toothed) marginsAlternate, trifoliate; leaflets are ovate, 8–15 cm long, dark green, with entire margins and prominent veins
Glandular HairsEntire plant is covered in sticky glandular hairs that secrete a sour-tasting acid dew (malic and oxalic acid)—
FlowersSmall (8–12 mm long), pea-like, borne singly on long axillary stalks; color is typically pinkish-purple (Desi types) or pure white (Kabuli types)Axillary clusters, papilionaceous, color variable (white, pink, purple, or bicolored), 1–2 cm long
FruitSmall, swollen, oblong, inflated pod (1.5–2.5 cm long, 1–1.5 cm wide), covered in sticky hairs, containing 1 or 2 wrinkled seeds—
SeedsAngular, wrinkled, beak-shaped seed (5–10 mm size) resembling a ram's head, ranging from dark brown (Desi) to cream-white (Kabuli)Kidney-shaped, 1–1.5 cm, color variable (white, red, black, or patterned); oily and starch-rich
Pods—Long, straight, cylindrical, 10–15 cm long, containing 6–8 seeds; mature pods are dry and brittle
