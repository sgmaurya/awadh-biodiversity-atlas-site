Gilled Tubificid Worm or Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm? गिल्ड ट्यूबीफिसिड वर्म या हॉफमीस्टर्स स्लज वर्म?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हॉफमीस्टर्स स्लज वर्मHoffmeister's Sludge WormLimnodrilus hoffmeisteri

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm, as the Gilled Tubificid Worm profile puts it

Lacks finger-like respiratory gills on posterior tail; body thinner

पूंछ पर कोई गलफड़े नहीं होते, शरीर अधिक पतला

Gilled Tubificid Worm, as the Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm profile puts it

Substantially larger; bears conspicuous paired branchial gills along posterior tail

काफी बड़ा आकार, पूंछ पर उंगली जैसे गलफड़े

Gilled Tubificid Worm: An aquatic reddish worm in pond mud with conspicuous paired finger-like gills lining the dorsal and ventral margins of its waving tail identifies Branchiura sowerbyi.

Hoffmeister's Sludge Worm: A slender reddish-pink worm forming trembling carpets in polluted organic sludge, lacking hair setae and external gills, with a bonnet-tipped penis sheath identifies Limnodrilus hoffmeisteri.
