Johnson Grass or Kans Grass? बरू या काँस?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kans Grass, as the Johnson Grass profile puts it

Kans Grass]] (Saccharum spontaneum)

Shorter and less robust; silver-white feathery flower plumes (not branched brown panicles); grows mainly on riverbanks

Johnson Grass: A tall (1–2.5 m), stout, densely tufted grass with persistent underground rhizomes, long leaves, and large branched brown panicles, found in irrigated fields and along canal banks — is Johnson grass. The hazard: young green forage is safe, but wilted or stressed plants may cause cyanide poisoning in cattle.

Kans Grass: Tall clump-forming grass (1.5–4 m) growing on river sands, with narrow leaves having cutting glass-like margins and a white central vein, and massive silver-white feathery flower plumes waving in autumn, is the Kans Grass.
