How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oleander Hawkmoth, as the Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth profile puts it

Wings and body patterned with green, white, and pink swirls; lacks the skull pattern

कनेर का हॉकमोथ — हरा-सफेद ज़िग-ज़ैग पैटर्न

Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth: Massive size (wingspan >100 mm), dark mottled forewings, and a prominent yellow-and-black human skull-like pattern on the thorax identify the Greater Death's Head Hawkmoth.

Oleander Hawkmoth: A large, green or pink-tinged hawkmoth with a long coiled proboscis, hovering at flowers like a hummingbird, and found on oleander, is Daphnis nerii.

SoundProduces a loud, high-pitched squeak when disturbed or handled—
Colour—Predominantly soft green or grey-green with pink or reddish tones on the wings and thorax
Wings—Elongate and pointed; forewings with complex patterns of green, grey and pink
Body—Stout, muscular, greenish; thorax large and powerful
Proboscis—Long, thin, coiled under the head; unrolls to feed from flowers
Flight—Swift, hovering like a hummingbird; flies in daylight and at dusk
