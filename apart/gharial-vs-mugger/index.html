Gharial, as the Mugger profile puts it

gharial]] (Gavialis gangeticus)

Extremely narrow, elongated snout; much more piscivorous; separate genus

Gharial: An extremely long, narrow-snouted crocodilian in deep Ghaghra or Girwa channels is a Gharial. It is a fish-specialist, the world's rarest crocodilian, and protected by law. Report any sighting to the atlas.

Mugger: A large, broad-headed, heavily armored crocodile in the Ghaghra or Rapti rivers is a Mugger and is dangerous. Observe from a distance.
