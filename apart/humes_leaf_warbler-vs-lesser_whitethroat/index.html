Hume's Leaf Warbler or Lesser Whitethroat? ह्यूम वार्बलर या लेसर व्हाइटथ्रोट?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Whitethroat, as the Hume's Leaf Warbler profile puts it

Lesser Whitethroat]] (Curruca curruca)

Grey (not green); male has black ear patch; larger

Hume's Leaf Warbler, as the Lesser Whitethroat profile puts it

Hume's Leaf Warbler]] (Phylloscopus humei)

Greenish above; eyebrow stripe; smaller, more rounded; no black ear patch

Hume's Leaf Warbler: Hume's Leaf Warbler is a tiny greenish warbler with a pale eyebrow stripe and white underparts, restlessly gleaning in vegetation. If the bird is grey with a black ear patch, it is Lesser Whitethroat. If it shows a yellow wing-bar or yellow-tinged underparts, it is a different species.

Lesser Whitethroat: Lesser Whitethroat is a small, restless grey-and-white warbler with a black ear patch (male). If the bird is greenish with an eyebrow stripe, it is Hume's Leaf Warbler. If the bird is noticeably larger with rufous wing-coverts, it is Common Whitethroat (rare in eastern UP).
