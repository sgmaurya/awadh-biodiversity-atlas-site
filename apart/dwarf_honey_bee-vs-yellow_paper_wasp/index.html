Dwarf Honey Bee or Yellow Paper Wasp? छोटी मधुमक्खी या ततैया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dwarf Honey Bee, as the Yellow Paper Wasp profile puts it

Much smaller; hairy body; lacks the petiole waist; builds exposed wax combs on twigs

छोटी मधुमक्खी — छोटा आकार, रोएँदार

Dwarf Honey Bee: Tiny size (7–10 mm), black body with a bright orange-red base of the abdomen, and a small, exposed, single comb wrapped around a thin twig identify the Dwarf Honey Bee.

Yellow Paper Wasp: Medium-to-large size (18–24 mm), slender yellow body with thin black bands, narrow waist, and open paper comb nest with a single hanging stalk identify the Yellow Paper Wasp.

BehaviourDocile; crawls in dense sheets over the comb surface; flies with a soft hum—
Body Shape—Slender, elongate body; abdomen connects to the thorax via a narrow waist (petiole)
Nest—An open, single-tier papery comb hanging from a central stalk under roofs or branches
Stinger—Females possess a smooth stinger that can sting repeatedly; males are stingless
