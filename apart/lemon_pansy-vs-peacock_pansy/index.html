Lemon Pansy or Peacock Pansy? लेमन पैंसी तितली या मयूर पंख तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peacock Pansy, as the Lemon Pansy profile puts it

Peacock Pansy]] (Junonia almana)

Larger; eyespots with red or orange centers; more elaborate pattern

Lemon Pansy, as the Peacock Pansy profile puts it

Lemon Pansy]])

Dark brown upperwings with pale yellow spots and smaller eyespots; flight is lower and more fluttering

Lemon Pansy: A small to medium-sized pale yellow butterfly with large, prominent black eyespots with iridescent blue or purple centers on both wings, flying rapidly over grassland, is the Lemon Pansy.

Peacock Pansy: Medium size, bright orange-brown upperwings, two very large peacock-like eyespots (with double blue-white pupils on the hindwings), and seasonal dry-season leaf camouflage identify the Peacock Pansy.
