How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Alternaria Leaf Spot, as the Curvularia Leaf Spot profile puts it

Concentric rings (target pattern); larger spots (5–10 mm)

बड़े निशान, वलय

Alternaria Leaf Spot: Look at the spore chains and beak length under magnification: conidia produced in branched chains with short conical beaks indicate Alternaria alternata, whereas solitary conidia with very long whiplike beaks signify Alternaria solani.

Curvularia Leaf Spot: Curved or slightly oval dark spots on rice or pulse leaves in monsoon (rice) or winter (pulses) = Curvularia.
