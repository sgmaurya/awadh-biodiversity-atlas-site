Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Honey Bee, as the Blue-banded Bee profile puts it

Much smaller; brown body; no blue bands; forms colonies

शहद की मधुमक्खी — छोटी, भूरी, पट्टियाँ नहीं

Blue-banded Bee: A robust, solitary bee with shiny bright metallic blue bands on a black abdomen, buzzing loudly on flowers during the warm season, is an Amegilla species.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Asian Honey BeeBlue-banded Bee
SizeWorker 11–13 mm; smaller than European honey bee (A. mellifera, 15–18 mm)Medium to large bee, body length 12-18 mm
ColourAbdomen with alternating amber and dark brown/black bands; thorax dark brown—
ColonyMedium — 6,000–10,000 workers (mellifera: 50,000+)—
NestSingle comb in cavity (hollow tree, wall); not exposed like Apis dorsata—
BehaviourMore defensive than mellifera; rapid absconding when disturbedFast, direct flight; buzzes loudly while foraging on flowers
Colouration—Black or dark brown body with 1-3 bright metallic blue bands on the abdomen
Stripes—Bands are iridescent, bright cyan or turquoise blue, sharply contrasting with black
Eyes—Large, dark compound eyes; no ocelli visible
Solitary—Never in groups; each bee forages individually

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
