How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Green Bee-eater, as the Blue-bearded Bee-eater profile puts it

Asian Green Bee-eater]] (Merops orientalis)

Much smaller size; bright emerald green body with rufous crown; black necklace; long pointed central tail streamers

Asian Green Bee-eater: Small size, emerald-green body, golden-rufous wash on crown, black neck collar, red eyes, and two long black tail-streamers.

Blue-bearded Bee-eater: The combination of very large size, square tail without central elongated pins, heavy curved bill, and bright azure-blue throat beard distinguishes the Blue-bearded Bee-eater from all Merops species.
