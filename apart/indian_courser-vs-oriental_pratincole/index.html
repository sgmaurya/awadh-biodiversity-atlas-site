How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Courser, as the Oriental Pratincole profile puts it

Indian Courser]] (Cursorius coromandelicus)

Much longer pale legs, upright carriage, bright rufous crown with white and black eye-stripes, lacks forked tail (Baker 1929, p. 86)

Indian Courser: The rich rufous crown, black-and-white eye stripes, chestnut underparts with black belly patch, and ivory-white legs readily identify the Indian Courser (Baker 1929).

Oriental Pratincole: The combination of olive-brown upperparts, deep chestnut underwing-coverts, forked tail with white base, and black-bordered buff throat identifies the Oriental Pratincole (Baker 1929, p. 91).
