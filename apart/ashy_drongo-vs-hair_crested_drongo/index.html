Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashy Drongo, as the Hair-crested Drongo profile puts it

Dicrurus leucophaeus

Slaty-grey plumage without glossy metallic spangles; crimson iris; deeply forked tail

Ashy Drongo: Ashy Drongo is pale grey throughout with a long forked tail. Black Drongo is glossy black and resident year-round. If the bird is grey, not black, and seen in winter, it is likely Ashy Drongo.

Hair-crested Drongo: Large black drongo with outward and upward curled tail outer tips, long downcurved bill, and hair-like crest plumes on forehead.
