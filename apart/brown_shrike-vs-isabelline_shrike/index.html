इसाबेलाइन श्राइक

Brown Shrike, as the Isabelline Shrike profile puts it

Brown Shrike]] (Lanius cristatus)

Rusty-brown back; prominent black eye-stripe; darker overall

Brown Shrike: Brown Shrike shows a rusty-chestnut back with a black eye-stripe and white underparts. If the tail is rufous on a pale sandy bird, it is an Isabelline Shrike. If the back is dark slate-grey, it is a different species.

Isabelline Shrike: Isabelline Shrike is distinguished by its pale sandy-buff to pale rufous plumage, less prominent eye-stripe, and relatively long tail. Brown Shrike is darker with rusty-chestnut back and more prominent eye-stripe. In the field, Isabelline Shrike appears paler and less boldly marked overall.
