Orange-breasted Green Pigeon or Yellow-footed Green Pigeon? बरियल या हरियल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बरियलOrange-breasted Green PigeonTreron bicinctus
हरियलYellow-footed Green PigeonTreron phoenicopterus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon, as the Orange-breasted Green Pigeon profile puts it

Treron phoenicopterus

Bright yellow legs and feet, prominent lilac shoulder patch, and lacks orange breast band

Orange-breasted Green Pigeon, as the Yellow-footed Green Pigeon profile puts it

Large green pigeon; but orange breast patch; two-coloured wing (no wine-red patch)

नारंगी सीने वाली हरियल

Orange-breasted Green Pigeon: Bright green pigeon with coral-red legs; male shows striking lilac and deep orange breast bands, female shows blue-grey nape and unbarred cinnamon undertail.

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon: Large green pigeon with bright yellow feet, wine-red wing patch, and loud "pis-pis" call from the canopy = Yellow-footed Green Pigeon. The combination of yellow feet and maroon wing is diagnostic.
