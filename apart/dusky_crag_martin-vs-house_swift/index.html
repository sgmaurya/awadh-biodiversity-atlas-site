Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Dusky Crag Martin, as the House Swift profile puts it

Dull brown; short tail with white spots; lacks white rump; cup nest

चट्टानी अबाबील — धूसर भूरा शरीर, बिना सफ़ेद कमर

Dusky Crag Martin: A small, uniform dusky-brown swallow without any white or contrasting patches, found in flight over rocky areas and cliffs, is a Dusky Crag Martin. The lack of contrasting plumage, small size, and cliff habitat are diagnostic.

House Swift: Stocky dark body, prominent white rump band, white throat, square-ended tail, and loud screaming group-flight identify the House Swift.
