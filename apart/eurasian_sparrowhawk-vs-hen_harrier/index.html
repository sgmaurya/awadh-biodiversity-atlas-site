How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hen Harrier, as the Eurasian Sparrowhawk profile puts it

Hen Harrier]] (Circus cyaneus)

Much larger (male 43–47 cm); different shape; white rump; completely different behaviour and habitat

Eurasian Sparrowhawk: A small, fast-flying blue-grey (male) or brown (female) raptor with short, broad, rounded wings and long barred tail, hunting small birds at high speed from cover is Eurasian Sparrowhawk. The sexual dimorphism and hunting behaviour are diagnostic.

Hen Harrier: Hen Harrier is distinguished by its silver-grey back in males (or brown streaking in females), white rump visible in both sexes, and characteristic low quartering flight. Males are more uniformly coloured than Pied Harrier; females are less heavily marked than Marsh Harrier.
