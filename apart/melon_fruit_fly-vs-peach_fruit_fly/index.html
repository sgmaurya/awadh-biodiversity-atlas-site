Melon Fruit Fly or Peach Fruit Fly? मेलन फ्रूट फ्लाई या आड़ू की फल मक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Peach Fruit Fly, as the Melon Fruit Fly profile puts it

Bactrocera zonata

Reddish-brown dacine fly with clear wings lacking complete dark costal band, infesting peach and guava

Melon Fruit Fly, as the Peach Fruit Fly profile puts it

Smaller; different host preference (melons, squash); wing pattern distinctive

अलग आकार, अलग पौधे

Melon Fruit Fly: Reddish-brown fruit fly with bright yellow thoracic stripes, dark T-shaped abdominal band, and wings marked with a brown costal band and apical spot.

Peach Fruit Fly: Small yellow-orange fly with pale banded wings, puncturing developing stone fruits (peach, ber) to lay eggs; infested fruit shows fermentation and rot with visible larvae inside: Peach Fruit Fly (Bactrocera zonata).
