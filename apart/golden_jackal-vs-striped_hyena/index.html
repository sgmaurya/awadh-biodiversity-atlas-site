Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Golden Jackal, as the Striped Hyena profile puts it

Smaller, slenderer, no stripes, different coloration, less powerful jaws

सियार: छोटा, पतला, ख़ैर के रंग

Striped Hyena: A large, stocky, powerfully-built carnivore with distinctive vertical black stripes on a tawny body, short tail, and a humped back, found in ravines and scrub, is a Striped Hyena and should be approached only at a distance with extreme caution.
