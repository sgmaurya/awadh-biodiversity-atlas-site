How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-crowned Night Heron, as the Eurasian Bittern profile puts it

Black-crowned Night Heron]] (Nycticorax nycticorax)

Solid glossy black mantle and crown, white neck plumes, unstreaked pale grey underparts (Baker 1929, p. 359)

Black-crowned Night Heron: Stocky, short-necked heron with a glossy black crown and back, grey wings, white belly, and large red eyes, roosting in groups during the day, is a Black-crowned Night Heron.

Eurasian Bittern: The heavy golden-buff body with bold black vermiculations, black crown, and barred wings distinguish the Eurasian Bittern (Baker 1929, pp. 370–371).

Side by side आमने-सामने

Black-crowned Night HeronEurasian Bittern
SizeMedium-sized, stocky heron (58–65 cm total length); relatively short neck and legs—
ColourationAdult has a glossy black crown and back; pale grey wings and tail; pure white underparts—
EyesBright, glowing ruby-red eyes in adults; orange-brown in immature birds—
PlumesAdults have 2–3 long, thin, white ribbon-like plumes extending from the nape—
ImmatureCompletely brown body heavily streaked and spotted with white and buff (looks like a bittern)—
Bare PartsBill is thick, black, and pointed; legs are pale yellow, turning pinkish-red when breeding—
Habit—Heavy-bodied, cryptically patterned heron skulking in dense reeds and rising noiselessly when disturbed
Plumage—Back and interscapulars black with broad ochre-buff edges; lower back, rump and tail pale ochreous with black bars (Baker 1929, pp. 370–371)
Crown & Moustache—Crown and nape black; blackish line from gape below cheeks; buff central line on throat streaked black (Baker 1929, pp. 370–371)
Neck Plumes—Fore-neck to vent pale yellowish-buff with broken streaks of brown; bars on sides of breast (Baker 1929, p. 371)
Bill & Eye—Bill greenish-yellow, culmen darker; iris yellow or whitey-yellow; lores green to livid blue (Baker 1929, p. 371)
Legs & Feet—Legs and feet dull pale greenish, more yellow at joints and on sole (Baker 1929, p. 371)
