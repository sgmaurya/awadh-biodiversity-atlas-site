Green-billed Malkoha or Lesser Coucal? ग्रीन-बिल्ड मालकोहा या लेसर कौकल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Coucal, as the Green-billed Malkoha profile puts it

Lesser Coucal]] (Centropus bengalensis)

Smaller size; dark brown and chestnut plumage; ungraduated tail; frequents wet grasslands rather than forest lianas

Green-billed Malkoha: The slender greenish-grey body, bright apple-green bill, crimson orbital skin, and long graduated tail with white spots distinguish the Green-billed Malkoha from all heavy-bodied coucals.

Lesser Coucal: Small, slender coucal with glossy blue-black body and chestnut wings in summer, transforming to cryptic streaked brown plumage in winter.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Green-billed MalkohaLesser Coucal
HabitSecretive arboreal cuckoo creeping through dense vine tangles—
UpperpartsOily dark green with metallic bluish-green sheen on mantle and wings—
UnderpartsAshy-grey throat and breast washed with ochraceous buff; blackish belly—
BillHeavy, curved, apple-green tinged with red at the base and gape—
Eye PatchConspicuous bare patch of crimson-red orbital skin surrounding the eye—
TailVery long, graduated, dark green-black; all rectrices tipped with white spots—
DemeanourCreeping deliberately through dense creepers like an arboreal rodent—
Size & build—Substantially smaller and more slender than the familiar greater coucal
Breeding plumage—Glossy black head, body, and tail with deep blue gloss contrasting with chestnut wings
Non-breeding dress—Dull brown upperparts boldly streaked with pale fulvous; barred wings and underparts
Eye colour—Deep crimson-red iris in adults; dull greyish-brown in juveniles
Locomotion—Clambers deftly through tangled reed stalks and grass stems like a rail

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
