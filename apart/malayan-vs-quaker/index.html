Malayan or Quaker? मलायन या क्वेकर तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Quaker, as the Malayan profile puts it

Completely tailless lycaenid with rounded hindwings; lacks subbasal dark spots on underside

क्वेकर तितली के पिछले पंख पर कोई पूंछ नहीं होती और पंखों का निचला हिस्सा सादा होता है

Malayan: Tiny black-and-white lycaenid fluttering weakly in deep humid shade, with a spotted underside and minute hindwing tooth.

Quaker: A tiny butterfly fluttering weakly strictly in deep shade, with spotless creamy white underwings marked only by faint brown lines and tiny costal specks, is the Quaker.
