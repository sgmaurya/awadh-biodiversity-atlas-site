How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Field Bindweed, as the Trailing Coldenia profile puts it

Trailing vine with twining stems; pink or white flowers in funnel shape; larger leaves

बेल, गुलाबी फूल, बड़ी पत्तियाँ

Field Bindweed: Trailing or twining winter vine with arrowhead-shaped leaves, small white or pink funnel-shaped flowers that close by noon, and a deep white root system, is the Field Bindweed.

Trailing Coldenia: A tiny, flat-spreading herb with minute leaves, small blue flowers, and a sticky texture that seems to glue itself to the ground is Coldenia procumbens.
