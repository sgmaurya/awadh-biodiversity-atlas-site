Babool, as the Amaltas profile puts it

Bipinnate compound leaves; yellow flower balls; has long white thorns

बबूल — कांटेदार, छोटे पत्ते

Amaltas: Medium-sized tree with pinnately compound leaves, long cascading racemes of bright yellow flowers in summer, and dark cylindrical woody pods persisting for months identify the Amaltas tree.

Side by side आमने-सामने

AmaltasBabool
Size & ShapeMedium-sized tree (10–15 m tall) with a spreading, open, and airy crown—
BarkSmooth, ash-grey bark on young trees, becoming rough, brown, and scaly on older trunksDark brown to blackish, rough, deeply cracked with vertical fissures; yields red-brown gum when injured
LeavesLarge, pinnately compound leaves (15–40 cm long) with 4–8 pairs of large, smooth, ovate leafletsAlternately arranged, bipinnately compound, feathery (4–10 cm long); with 2–8 pairs of pinnae, each carrying 10–25 pairs of tiny oblong leaflets
FlowersBright yellow flowers arranged in long, pendulous, drooping clusters (30–50 cm long)Bright yellow, sweet-scented, spherical flower heads (1–1.5 cm diameter) clustered in leaf axils on slender stalks
Fruit & SeedsLong, dark brown or blackish cylindrical woody pods (30–60 cm long), containing flat seeds embedded in a sticky black pulp—
Form—Small to medium-sized tree (5–15 m) with a dense, spreading, hemispherical or umbrella-shaped canopy
Spines—Paired, straight, sharp stipular thorns (1–5 cm long) at leaf bases, pointing slightly backward; bright white on mature twigs
Fruit—Elongated, flat, thick grey-green pod (7–15 cm long) strongly constricted between the seeds, giving a bead-like appearance; turns greyish-brown when dry
