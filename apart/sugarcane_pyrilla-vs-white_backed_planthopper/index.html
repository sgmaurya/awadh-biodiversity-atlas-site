Sugarcane Pyrilla or White-backed Planthopper? पायरिला या व्हाइट-बैक्ड प्लांटहॉपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-backed Planthopper, as the Sugarcane Pyrilla profile puts it

On rice; has white stripe down the back; different host

सफ़ेद धारी वाला

Sugarcane Pyrilla: Brown, robust insect in dense clusters on the undersides of Sugarcane leaves, with white powder on nymphs and sooty mould below: sugarcane pyrilla.

White-backed Planthopper: Minute dark planthopper with a prominent creamy-white stripe down the middle of the back, congregating in dense colonies at the base of rice stems.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Sugarcane PyrillaWhite-backed Planthopper
Adult colourBrown to dark brown, with pale markings—
Size6–8 mm long; larger than rice planthopper—
ShapeRobust, wedge-shaped; wings held roof-like—
LocationAlways on the undersurfaces of leaves; under leaf sheaths—
ClusteringDense colonies, sometimes hundreds per tiller—
NymphsPale yellowish-brown, wingless, scale-like appearance—
White powderNymphs covered with waxy white powder—
DamageLeaves yellowed, curled, drying from the tip—
HoneydewCopious sticky residue and black sooty mould—
White dorsal stripe—Conspicuous, bright creamy-white to yellowish-white longitudinal band along the mesonotum
Body coloration—Dark brown to blackish body contrasting sharply with the bright white dorsal stripe
Forewings—Subhyaline, pale brownish-grey forewings with a distinct dark spot at the apex of the clavus
Hind legs—Possesses a prominent, movable, serrated spur (calcar) at the apex of each hind tibia
Feeding location—Congregated in dense colonies at the lower stem bases and tillers of rice hills just above water

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
