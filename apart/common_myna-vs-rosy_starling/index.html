Common Myna or Rosy Starling? मैना या गुलाबी मैना?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मैनाCommon MynaAcridotheres tristis
गुलाबी मैनाRosy StarlingPastor roseus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Myna, as the Rosy Starling profile puts it

Dark brown plumage (lacks rose-pink); yellow skin behind the eye; yellow bill; white wing patches

देसी मैना — भूरा शरीर, आँख के पीछे पीली त्वचा, सफ़ेद पंख के धब्बे

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.

Rosy Starling: Medium-sized starling with a pastel rose-pink body, contrasting glossy black head, wings, and tail, flying in large, noisy, synchronized flocks in winter, is a Rosy Starling.
