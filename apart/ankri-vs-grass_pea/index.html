How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Vetch, as the Grass Pea profile puts it

Vicia sativa

Leaves with multiple pairs of leaflets; stems wingless; flowers purplish-blue

Common Vetch: A trailing or climbing winter legume with feathery pinnate leaves ending in a tendril, small purple-pink papilionaceous flowers, and linear pods containing round seeds is ankri.

Grass Pea: Trailing winter legume with two-winged stems, paired narrow grass-like leaflets with tendrils, blue butterfly flowers, and winged pods.
