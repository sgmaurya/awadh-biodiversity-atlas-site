How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Castor Woolly Bear Moth, as the Handmaiden Moth profile puts it

Large robust nocturnal moth with spotted grey wings and scarlet abdomen

रात में उड़ने वाला बड़ा पतंगा, चित्तीदार पंख

Castor Woolly Bear Moth: Densely hairy or bristly black caterpillar with bright yellow or red bands on castor, neem, or gular; moth with dark banded wings and woolly body: Castor Woolly Bear Moth (Olepa ricini).

Handmaiden Moth: Small diurnal black moth with transparent spots on both wings and two bright orange-red rings on a dark body identifies Amata cyssea.
