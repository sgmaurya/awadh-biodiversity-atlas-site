Jackfruit or Monkey Jack? कटहल या बड़हल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कटहलJackfruitArtocarpus heterophyllus
बड़हलMonkey JackArtocarpus lacucha

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Monkey Jack, as the Jackfruit profile puts it

Much smaller tree; fruit is small (5–10 cm), irregular, yellow-orange when ripe; leaves are velvety beneath

बड़हल — छोटा पेड़, छोटा खट्टा-मीठा फल

Jackfruit, as the Monkey Jack profile puts it

Artocarpus heterophyllus

Tremendous syncarp covered in sharp conical spines; leaves smooth and glossy beneath

Jackfruit: Large evergreen tree with leathery dark green leaves, exuding sticky white latex when cut, bearing massive spiny green-yellow fruits directly on the main trunk, is the Jackfruit.

Monkey Jack: Spreading latex-bearing tree with large velvety-backed leaves and irregular, unspined yellowish-orange sour-sweet syncarps.
