How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Green Sandpiper, as the Ruff profile puts it

Green Sandpiper]] (Tringa ochropus)

Smaller; darker plumage; white rump but different overall colouring

Green Sandpiper: A small dark-backed wader with white belly and dark underwings visible in flight, at small water bodies — is a Green Sandpiper. The dark back and dark underwing are diagnostic. Solitary or in pairs.

Ruff: Ruff is a medium-sized wader with a long neck held upright, long orange-yellow legs, and a distinctive white oval rump patch visible in flight. The combination of size, leg colour, and rump pattern distinguishes it from similar species.
