White Orange-tip or Yellow Orange Tip? व्हाइट ऑरेंज-टिप तितली या येलो ऑरेंज टिप तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Upperside ground colour is bright sulphur-yellow in males rather than chalky white

White Orange-tip: A medium-sized chalk-white pierid butterfly with a brilliant fiery orange patch enclosed in a broad black apical area on the forewing, flying around thorny caper scrub, is the White Orange-tip.

Yellow Orange Tip: A small white butterfly with a bright yellow-orange apex on the forewing, flying over gardens and fields, is the Yellow Orange Tip.
