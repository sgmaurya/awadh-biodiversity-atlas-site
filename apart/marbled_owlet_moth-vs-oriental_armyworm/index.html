How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Marbled Owlet Moth, as the Oriental Armyworm profile puts it

Eublemma baccalix

Much smaller noctuid moth with marbled grey-white markings; does not clip cereal ear-heads

Marbled Owlet Moth: A tiny owlet moth with chalky white forewings intricately marbled with olive-brown and grey, mimicking bird droppings, identifies Micracontia batisella.

Oriental Armyworm: Ochraceous-brown noctuid moth with a single glistening white dot at the center of the forewing, whose striped larvae march in hordes and sever grain earheads.

AntennaeSimple, slender, and filiform in both sexes—
Adult moth—Pale greyish-ochraceous to brick-reddish forewings dusted with minute dark specks
Wing markings—Small, distinct pure white speck at the lower angle of the cell bordered internally by a dark spot
Larval stripes—Smooth, plump caterpillar with alternating pale and dark longitudinal stripes along the body
Damage symptom—Severe leaf defoliation and characteristic severed mature grain panicles littered on the field soil
Gregarious march—Full-grown larvae march en masse in marching army-like columns between adjacent crop fields
