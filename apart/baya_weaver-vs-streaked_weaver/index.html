Baya Weaver, as the Streaked Weaver profile puts it

Baya Weaver]] (Ploceus philippinus)

Breeding male has yellow head; non-breeding similar but yellower overall; very similar size

Baya Weaver: Conical bill with streaked sparrow-like plumage. Breeding males with solid yellow crowns and breast, combined with retort-shaped hanging nests on palm or thorny trees, identify the Baya Weaver.

Streaked Weaver: A small, heavily streaked buff-yellow weaver with large hanging woven nests in reed beds or trees near water is Streaked Weaver. The distinctive woven nests and highly gregarious colonial behaviour are diagnostic.
