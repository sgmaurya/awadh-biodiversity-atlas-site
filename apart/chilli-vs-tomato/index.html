Chilli or Tomato? मिर्च या टमाटर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मिर्चChilliCapsicum annuum
टमाटरTomatoSolanum lycopersicum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tomato, as the Chilli profile puts it

Spreading hairy vine; leaves are deeply divided; flowers are yellow; fruit is red and juicy

टमाटर — पीले फूल, रोएँदार पत्तियाँ, रसीले फल

Chilli, as the Tomato profile puts it

Leaves are smooth, lanceolate; flowers are small, white; fruit is an elongated, hot pod

मिर्च — सफेद फूल, तीखे लंबे फल

Chilli: Small branching bush with smooth lanceolate leaves, small white drooping flowers, and elongated hollow green-red berries with a hot spicy placenta, is the Chilli.

Tomato: Spreading annual hairy herb with deeply divided leaves, small star-like yellow flowers, and juicy red or orange berries, is the Tomato.

Side by side आमने-सामने

ChilliTomato
Plant habitSmall, erect, branching annual or short-lived perennial herb up to 30–80 cm highSpreading, scrambling annual herb up to 60–180 cm high, requiring stick supports (staking) to stand upright
LeavesSimple, alternately arranged, lanceolate or ovate, smooth, dark green, with pointed tipsAlternately arranged, deeply pinnately divided (15–30 cm long), covered with fine sticky glandular hairs
FlowersSmall (8–12 mm), solitary, white or greenish-white, nodding or hanging downwards in leaf jointsSmall (10–15 mm), bright yellow, star-like, hanging in small clusters of 3 to 12 along the stem
FruitAn elongated, cylindrical, hollow berry (2–15 cm long) containing a hot placenta, turning from green to bright red when ripeA fleshy, juicy, globose or oval berry, turning from green to bright glossy red, yellow, or pink when ripe
SeedsNumerous, flat, small (2–3 mm), kidney-shaped, cream-colored, attached to the central seed core—
Odor—The leaves and stems exude a strong, characteristic resinous smell when brushed or bruised
