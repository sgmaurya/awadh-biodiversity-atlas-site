Lesser Tiger Beetle or Line-marked Tiger Beetle? छोटा बाघ भृंग या रेखा-चिह्नित बाघ भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lesser Tiger Beetle, as the Line-marked Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Dull olivaceous; elytral markings much narrower and occasionally very obscure; trochanters metallic rather than red (Fowler 1912, p. 366)

फीका जैतूनी रंग, बहुत संकरे निशान और धातुई पैर (छोटा बाघ भृंग)

Lesser Tiger Beetle: A diminutive bronze tiger beetle running rapidly over wet mud with delicate narrow white lunules identifies Cylindera minuta.

Line-marked Tiger Beetle: A small dull dark tiger beetle with red trochanters, obscure green-coppery head and pronotum, and interrupted whitish margins with a central inverted V-shaped mark is Cylindera grammophora (Fowler 1912, pp. 363–364).

Side by side आमने-सामने

Lesser Tiger BeetleLine-marked Tiger Beetle
HabitDiminutive, highly active tiger beetle inhabiting moist open ground—
ColorationBronze, greenish-coppery, or dark brown with subtle metallic sheen—
Elytral patternMarked with narrow whitish lunules: humeral lunule, middle band, and apical lunule—
SizeNoticeably small and slender compared to typical riverine tiger beetlesSmall tiger beetle (Fowler 1912, p. 364)
LocomotionRapid scurrying runs across wet mud with frequent short buzzing flights—
Head and pronotum—Obscure green and coppery reflections; head strongly striated between eyes; pronotum narrow, almost straight-sided (Fowler 1912, p. 363)
Elytra ground—Dull, dark, usually with a greenish reflection at base, distinctly granulate anteriorly (Fowler 1912, p. 363)
Elytra markings—Margins mostly whitish testaceous; crescent-shaped shoulder spot, central inverted V-shaped marking, and dilated apical marking (Fowler 1912, pp. 363–364)
Legs—Metallic with red trochanters (Fowler 1912, p. 364)
Underside—Thickly set with white tomentose pubescence except central body, head, and genae (Fowler 1912, p. 364)

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
