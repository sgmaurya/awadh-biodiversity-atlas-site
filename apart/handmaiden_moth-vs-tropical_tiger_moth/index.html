How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tropical Tiger Moth, as the Handmaiden Moth profile puts it

Much larger; forewings solid grey-brown with a central white spot; not clear-winged

बहुत बड़ा, भूरे पंख जिन पर एक सफेद धब्बा होता है

Handmaiden Moth: Small diurnal black moth with transparent spots on both wings and two bright orange-red rings on a dark body identifies Amata cyssea.

Tropical Tiger Moth: A small moth with striking black forewing with white spots and bright red or orange hindwings, combined with a brightly coloured hairy caterpillar (black with yellow/orange bands) on papaya or milkweed, identifies the Tropical Tiger Moth.

Size—Small moth; wingspan 30–38 mm
Colour—Black or dark brown base; hindwings bright red, orange, or yellow
Body—Black with bright markings; warning coloration
Larva—Black with yellow or orange bands; covered with tufts of hair; warning coloration
