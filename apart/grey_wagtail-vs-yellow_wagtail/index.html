Grey Wagtail or Western Yellow Wagtail? ग्रे खंजन या पिलकिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Western Yellow Wagtail, as the Grey Wagtail profile puts it

Larger; yellow head in breeding males; less yellow overall in non-breeding; lacks long tail feature

पिलकिया / पीली खंजन

Grey Wagtail, as the Western Yellow Wagtail profile puts it

Larger; long tail; grey back; yellow restricted to undertail-coverts

ग्रे खंजन

Grey Wagtail: Grey Wagtail is distinguished by its very long tail, grey back, yellow underparts (at least on undertail-coverts), and strong association with flowing water. The tail's constant bobbing motion is characteristic of all wagtails.

Western Yellow Wagtail: Yellow Wagtail is distinguished by its small size, yellow head and underparts (in males), gregarious behaviour on open ground, and shorter tail than Grey Wagtail. The yellow coloration on head distinguishes it from White Wagtail and other species.
