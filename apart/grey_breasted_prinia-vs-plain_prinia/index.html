How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Plain Prinia, as the Grey-breasted Prinia profile puts it

Plain Prinia]] (Prinia inornata)

Lacks grey breast; more rufous above; buff underparts

Grey-breasted Prinia: Grey-breasted Prinia is found in reed beds and grasslands, with pale grey breast contrasting with brown upperparts. Plain Prinia is in drier grasslands, more buff-coloured, and lacks the grey breast.

Plain Prinia: Small, plain brown bird with a long graduated tail, cream underparts, and no stripes on its back, hopping restlessly in grass or bushes, is a Plain Prinia.
