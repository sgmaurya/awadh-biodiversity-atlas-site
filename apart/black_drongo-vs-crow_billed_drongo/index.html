How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black Drongo, as the Crow-billed Drongo profile puts it

Black Drongo]] (Dicrurus macrocercus)

More slender build, noticeably thinner bill, deeply forked tail with widely splayed tips, and white rictal spot

Black Drongo: A glossy black open-country bird on a wire, with a deep V-shaped forked tail and repeated sallies for insects, is Black Drongo in eastern UP farmland.

Crow-billed Drongo: The heavy, stout bill combined with a shallow tail fork, glossy steel-blue black plumage, and white-dotted under wing-coverts separates the Crow-billed Drongo (Baker 1924).
