How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk, as the Brown-backed Red Marsh Hawk profile puts it

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk]])

Thorax dark purplish-violet or plum-coloured; abdomen bright crimson-purple

Brown-backed Red Marsh Hawk: A robust dragonfly perching low on sunny canal banks whose dark reddish-brown thorax contrasts sharply with an unspotted, bright blood-red abdomen is the Brown-backed Red Marsh Hawk.

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk: Medium dragonfly with crimson-red abdomen tip and male body appearing pale pinkish-white due to pruinose coating distinguishes this species.
