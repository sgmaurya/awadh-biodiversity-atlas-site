Male has apple-green thorax with black stripes and orange tip; lacks bright crimson-red basal abdomen

Nurse's Pixie Damselfly: A tiny damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose male displays a crimson-red thorax, crimson basal abdomen, yellow midriff, and bright azure-blue terminal spot is Nurse's Pixie Damselfly.

Pygmy Dartlet: The tiniest damselfly of the region — body length under 22 mm, bright metallic blue (male) with black rings, in shallow vegetation-rich water.
