Indian Scops Owl or Spotted Owlet? छोटा उल्लू या चित्तीदार उल्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छोटा उल्लूIndian Scops OwlOtus bakkamoena
चित्तीदार उल्लूSpotted OwletAthene brama

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Spotted Owlet, as the Indian Scops Owl profile puts it

Lacks ear tufts; stockier build; white spots on plumage; louder "ji-ji-ji" call; different roost behaviour

चित्तीदार उल्लू — कलगी नहीं है

Indian Scops Owl: Small owl + prominent ear tufts + soft "poo-poo-poo" call + cavity roosting = Indian Scops Owl. The ear tufts are diagnostic and visible even in silhouette at night.

Spotted Owlet: Small size, white spots on greyish-brown upperparts, yellow eyes, lack of ear tufts, and loud chattering calls in pairs confirm the Spotted Owlet.
