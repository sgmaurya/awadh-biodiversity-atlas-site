Marbled Balloon Frog or Indian Burrowing Frog? गुब्बारा मेंढक या बिलकारी मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गुब्बारा मेंढकMarbled Balloon FrogUperodon taprobanicus
बिलकारी मेंढकIndian Burrowing FrogSphaerotheca breviceps

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Burrowing Frog, as the Marbled Balloon Frog profile puts it

Rounder nose, no painted blotches, bigger mouth; spade on heel; croaking call

रंगीन धब्बे नहीं

Marbled Balloon Frog: A round, short-legged, tiny-mouthed frog painted with orange blotches on brown, that inflates when handled, is the balloon frog.

Indian Burrowing Frog: Globose body shape, short head, short limbs, and a large black, spade-shaped metatarsal tubercle on the inner hind foot identify the Indian Burrowing Frog.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Marbled Balloon FrogIndian Burrowing Frog
Size50–75 mm; a stout, round frogSmall to medium size (40–60 mm snout-vent length); stout, globose body shape
ShapeBody globular, head small and pointed, mouth tiny, limbs short; cannot leap far—
ColourDark brown to grey-brown with large irregular orange, red or yellow blotches, especially along the sides and on the limbs; belly pale and spotted—
SkinSmooth above, slightly granularSmooth to finely warty skin; folds above the ears (tympanum)
FeetFingers and toes with small discs at the tips; two spades on the heel for digging—
DefenceInflates with air to a ball, lowers its head and presents its back; the skin exudes a sticky, bitter fluid—
CallA deep, loud, resonant "whoooonk", repeated, from a pool after heavy rain; like a bullfrog's but shorter—
Digging Spades—Prominent, shovel-like, black metatarsal tubercle on the inner edge of hind feet
Head—Broad and short head with a rounded snout; eyes large and protruding
Colouration—Sandy-brown or olive-grey dorsum with dark mottling; white or marbled underside
Movement—Crawls or makes clumsy, short hops; burrows backward into sandy soil in seconds

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
