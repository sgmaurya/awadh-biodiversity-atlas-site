Copperpod or Neem? पीला गुलमोहर या नीम?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीमNeemAzadirachta indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Neem, as the Copperpod profile puts it

Leaflets with toothed margins; white flowers; different shape

नीम — कटी पत्तियाँ, सफेद फूल

Copperpod: A large, fast-growing tree with finely feathery bipinnate leaves and spectacular golden-yellow flowers with orange-red markings in May–June. One of the brightest-flowering trees in the district.

Neem: Compound leaves with strongly toothed, asymmetric-base leaflets + strong bitter smell when crushed + found near village settlements = neem. Bakain (Melia azedarach) is the closest lookalike but its leaflets have a symmetric base.
