How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bar-headed Goose, as the Common Crane profile puts it

Bar-headed Goose]] (Anser indicus)

Much smaller (75 cm); goose (not crane); different shape

Bar-headed Goose: A large pale goose with a striking white head marked by two bold black bars, at rivers in winter — is a Bar-headed Goose. The head pattern is diagnostic and unmistakable.

Common Crane: Common Crane has a distinctive black and white neck pattern with white cheek patch, grey body, and black flight feathers. The white cheek patch clearly separates it from Sarus Crane, which has a bare red head. Common Crane is smaller than Sarus Crane.
