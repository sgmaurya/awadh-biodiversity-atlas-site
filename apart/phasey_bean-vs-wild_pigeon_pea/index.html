How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Phasey Bean, as the Wild Pigeon Pea profile puts it

Macroptilium lathyroides]]`

Erect or twining herb; flowers dark maroon-red; pods very slender, cylindrical, twisting spirally

Phasey Bean: Look for trifoliolate leaves, long erect racemes, deep maroon-red flowers, and long slender cylindrical pods that twist spirally upon splitting.

Wild Pigeon Pea: Look for the trailing or climbing habit, velvety trifoliolate leaves, small yellow flowers with red-veined standard, and flat hairy pods with deep transverse furrows.
