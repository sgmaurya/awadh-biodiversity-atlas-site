How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gangetic Ailia, as the Taakree Catfish profile puts it

Gangetic Ailia]])

Rayed dorsal fin completely absent; anal fin longer (58–75 rays); body more translucent

Gangetic Ailia: An elongate, paper-thin, translucent silvery catfish completely lacking a rayed dorsal fin, possessing a tiny adipose fin, eight long barbels, and an anal fin of 58–75 rays, is Ailia coila.

Taakree Catfish: A medium-sized, scaleless silvery schilbeid catfish with eight barbels, a rayed dorsal fin armed with a sharp spine, a small adipose fin, and an anal fin of 43–52 rays, is Proeutropiichthys taakree.
