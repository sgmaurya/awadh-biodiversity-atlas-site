Parasol Mushroom or Termite Mushroom? छत्री खुम्बी या दीमक छत्ता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Parasol Mushroom, as the Termite Mushroom profile puts it

Parasol mushroom]])

No perforatorium; large shaggy scales on cap; ring on stem

Parasol Mushroom: Large umbrella-like mushroom with cream scales, a central brown nipple, a tall stipe with a snakeskin pattern, a free-moving double ring, and a white spore print, is the Parasol Mushroom.

Termite Mushroom: If it is large, white, growing near a termite mound, and has a sharp spike at the centre of the cap — it is a Termitomyces. The spike (perforatorium) is unique to this genus and cannot be confused.
