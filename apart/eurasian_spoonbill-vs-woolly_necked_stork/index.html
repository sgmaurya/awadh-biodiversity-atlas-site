Eurasian Spoonbill or Asian Woolly-necked Stork? चमचा या लगलग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लगलगAsian Woolly-necked StorkCiconia episcopus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Woolly-necked Stork, as the Eurasian Spoonbill profile puts it

Black and white plumage; heavier bill

ऊँची टाँगों वाली बगुली

Eurasian Spoonbill: The Eurasian Spoonbill is unmistakable — the only winter wading bird in the region with a pure white body and an enormously enlarged, flattened, spoon-shaped bill. No other bird has this bill shape.

Asian Woolly-necked Stork: A large black-and-white stork with a distinctive white woolly collar at the base of the neck — is a Woolly-necked Stork. The collar is unique and diagnostic.
