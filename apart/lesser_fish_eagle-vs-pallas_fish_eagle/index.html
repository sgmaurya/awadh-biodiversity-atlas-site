How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pallas's Fish Eagle, as the Lesser Fish Eagle profile puts it

Pallas's Fish Eagle]] (Haliaeetus leucoryphus)

Much larger build, pale buff-whitish hood, and broad white band across the middle of the tail

Lesser Fish Eagle, as the Pallas's Fish Eagle profile puts it

Lesser Fish Eagle]] (Icthyophaga humilis)

Smaller build, greyish head and breast, and darker tail with blackish subterminal band (Baker 1928)

Lesser Fish Eagle: The combination of an ashy-grey head and breast, pure white lower belly, and tail pattern with dark brown central feathers and mottled lateral feathers distinguishes the Lesser Fish Eagle (Baker 1928).

Pallas's Fish Eagle: The immense size, pale creamy-buff hood contrasting with dark rufous body, and broad white band across the middle of the dark tail distinguish Pallas's Fish Eagle.
