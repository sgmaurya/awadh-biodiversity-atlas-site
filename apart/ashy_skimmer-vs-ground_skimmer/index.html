How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ground Skimmer, as the Ashy Skimmer profile puts it

Eyes bright blue-grey; abdomen has distinct black markings on segments 8–10; wings lack ochreous pterostigma

Ashy Skimmer: A small dragonfly perching flat on sun-baked stones or sand whose mature male is uniformly coated in pale powdery ashy-blue dust with bright yellow pterostigmata on clear wings is the Ashy Skimmer.

Ground Skimmer: Small size, mature male has a powder-blue body with a black tail tip, female is yellow-brown, and habit of perching flat on the bare ground identify the Ground Skimmer.
