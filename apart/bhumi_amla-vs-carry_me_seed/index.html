Bhumi Amla or Carry-me Seed? भुई आँवला या भूमि आँवला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

भुई आँवलाBhumi AmlaPhyllanthus fraternus
भूमि आँवलाCarry-me SeedPhyllanthus amarus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Carry-me Seed, as the Bhumi Amla profile puts it

Similar; fruits smaller, more sparse; leaflets fewer per rachis

बहुत छोटी फलियाँ

Bhumi Amla, as the Carry-me Seed profile puts it

Slightly smaller, flowers minute and greenish; fruits often reddish. Difficult to distinguish without close inspection. Both are used interchangeably in Ayurveda

दोनों बहुत मिलते-जुलते हैं; विशेषज्ञ की मदद से पहचानें

Bhumi Amla: A small herb with minute leaves and small red fruits in a disturbed field is bhumi amla.

Carry-me Seed: A delicate-looking, branched herb with fern-like small pinnate leaves and tiny greenish flowers followed by small round fruits is Phyllanthus amarus.
