How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Blue-tailed Bee-eater, as the Chestnut-headed Bee-eater profile puts it

Merops philippinus

Green head with black eye-stripe; brown mantle; blue tail with long central streamers

Blue-tailed Bee-eater: Small, multicoloured flyer (green, yellow, blue) + long tail streamers + loud "krrrip" call in flight + aerial insect hunting + spring arrival = Blue-tailed Bee-eater. The elongated tail streamers are diagnostic.

Chestnut-headed Bee-eater: Small colorful bee-eater with rich chestnut crown and back, yellow throat, azure rump, and completely square-ended tail with no pins.

Throat pattern—Bright yellow chin and throat bordered below by a rich chestnut and black gorget
Tail structure—Square-ended green tail completely lacking the elongated central pin-feathers of other bee-eaters
Rump colour—Vibrant azure-blue lower back and rump visible in flight
