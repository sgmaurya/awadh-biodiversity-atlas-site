How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Chestnut-capped Babbler, as the Indian Grassbird profile puts it

Timalia pileata

Bright chestnut crown cap, white forehead, black-streaked white throat, and plain unstreaked back

Chestnut-capped Babbler: Grassland babbler with bright chestnut-red crown cap, white forehead and supercilium, stout black bill, and black-streaked white throat.

Indian Grassbird: Secretive tall-grass dweller with unusually stout bill, boldly streaked black, rufous, and white mantle, and long graduated tail broadly tipped white.
