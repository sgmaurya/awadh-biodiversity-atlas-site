How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coromandel Marsh Dart, as the Little Blue Dartlet profile puts it

Coromandel Marsh Dart]])

Much larger; entire body bright citron-yellow; entirely lacks blue and black colouration

Coromandel Marsh Dart: Tiny damselfly with bright red (male) or bright yellow-orange (female) body, slow fluttering flight over marshy vegetation, often in tandem pairs, is the Coromandel Marsh Dart.

Little Blue Dartlet: A miniature damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose male has a black-and-blue striped thorax, black abdomen with blue basal rings, and entirely bright azure-blue segments 8 and 9 is the Little Blue Dartlet.
