Indian Python or Red Sand Boa? अजगर या दोमुँहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

अजगरIndian PythonPython molurus
दोमुँहाRed Sand BoaEryx johnii

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Python, as the Red Sand Boa profile puts it

Much larger (3–4.5 m), arrowhead mark on head, thick build

विशाल, सिर पर तीर निशान

Indian Python: Massive stout-bodied snake (3.0–4.5 m) with a prominent dark arrowhead mark on a flat head, asymmetrical brown blotches on a yellow-tan body, and cat-like vertical pupils, is the Indian Python (Ajgar).

Red Sand Boa: A small-to-medium, uniformly reddish or blackish stout snake (75–100 cm) with a blunt, head-like tail in loose sandy soils is a red sand boa. It is harmless, protected by law, and beneficial for rodent control. Do not harm it.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Size & GirthThe largest snake in UP, reaching 3.0–4.5 meters in length, with a thick, muscular body as wide as a human thigh—
Head PatternDistinctive, dark, spearhead or arrowhead mark on the top of the flat pinkish-grey head—
Body MarkingsYellowish-buff or tan body, covered with massive, dark brown, rectangular, asymmetrical blotches—
PupilVertical slit (cat-like pupil) in gold-colored eyes, with distinct heat-sensitive pits along the jaw scales—
TailShort, tapering rapidly, muscular, dark blackish-brown on the undersideVery short, flat, blunt, rounded at the tip, closely resembling the head
Size & Build—Small to medium (75–100 cm), stout, compact body with little taper from body to tail
Color & Pattern—Reddish-brown, orange-brown, or deep blackish-brown dorsally; uniform or with faint darker bands; belly lighter
Scales—Smooth to slightly keeled; those on the tail are notably keeled and rough
Head—Small, flat, wedge-shaped, not distinct from neck; eyes small, positioned dorsolaterally
Behaviour—Cryptic; attempts to bury itself in loose soil when disturbed; raises tail defensively
