How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Indian River Shad profile puts it

Much larger (20–60 cm); head and mouth larger; body deeper; not planktivorous

बहुत बड़ी

Indian River Shad: A small, silvery, laterally compressed shad with a deep belly and schooling behaviour in rivers and large ponds is a chapra.

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.
