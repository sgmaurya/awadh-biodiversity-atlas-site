Green Sandpiper or Wood Sandpiper? ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर या वुड सैंडपाइपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Wood Sandpiper, as the Green Sandpiper profile puts it

Similar size but more spotted upperparts; pale rump; yellowish legs

वुड सैंडपाइपर

Green Sandpiper, as the Wood Sandpiper profile puts it

Smaller; uniformly dark-backed; dark legs; dark underwing

ग्रीन सैंडपाइपर

Green Sandpiper: A small dark-backed wader with white belly and dark underwings visible in flight, at small water bodies — is a Green Sandpiper. The dark back and dark underwing are diagnostic. Solitary or in pairs.

Wood Sandpiper: A spotted wader with yellowish legs and pale underwings, in small flocks at shallow wetlands — is a Wood Sandpiper. The bold spotting and yellow legs distinguish it from Green Sandpiper. Paler and more spotted than Green Sandpiper.
