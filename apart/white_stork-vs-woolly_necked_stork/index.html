Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Woolly-necked Stork, as the White Stork profile puts it

Darker overall; bronze-black back and wings; white belly; woolly neck

गहरा, ऊन जैसी गर्दन

White Stork: A very large white stork with black wing feathers and bright red legs and bill, walking slowly in shallow water or wet grasslands — is a White Stork. Unmistakable in size and colour pattern.

Asian Woolly-necked Stork: A large black-and-white stork with a distinctive white woolly collar at the base of the neck — is a Woolly-necked Stork. The collar is unique and diagnostic.
