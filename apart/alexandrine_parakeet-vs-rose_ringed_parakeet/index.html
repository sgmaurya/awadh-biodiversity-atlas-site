Alexandrine Parakeet or Rose-ringed Parakeet? हीरामन तोता या तोता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rose-ringed Parakeet]] (Psittacula krameri)

Much smaller (40–43 cm); red and black neck ring on male

Alexandrine Parakeet, as the Rose-ringed Parakeet profile puts it

Much larger (53–58 cm); male has large red shoulder patch; deeper red bill

सिकंदरी तोता — बड़ा, कंधे पर लाल

Alexandrine Parakeet: Alexandrine Parakeet is the largest parakeet in the region. Males show a distinctive deep maroon (wine-red) patch on throat and breast. The long pointed tail and large size are unmistakable.

Rose-ringed Parakeet: Large all-green parrot + long pointed tail + loud screeching flight + eastern UP village = Rose-ringed Parakeet. The only parakeet of the region. Male's rose ring visible at close range; the pointed tail silhouette identifies the species even in fast overhead flight.
