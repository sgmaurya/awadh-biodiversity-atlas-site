Indian Eagle-Owl, as the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl profile puts it

Indian Eagle-Owl]] (Bubo bengalensis)

Bright orange-yellow irises, tawny-buff plumage with prominent streaks, and open country habit

Indian Eagle-Owl: Very large owl with bright orange eyes, prominent vertical ear tufts, and heavily streaked brown plumage nesting on clay cliffs or ruins is an Indian Eagle-Owl.

Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl: The immense size, dark brown eyes, long slanting ear-tufts, and fulvous underparts barred on the breast, the bars becoming broad spots on the abdomen (Baker 1927, p. 418), distinguish the Spot-bellied Eagle-Owl.

Legs & Feet—Heavily feathered legs to the toes, which are dusky yellow with pale horny claws (Baker 1927, p. 418)
