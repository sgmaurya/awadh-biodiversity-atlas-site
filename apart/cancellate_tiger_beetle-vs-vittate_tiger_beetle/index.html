How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pattern consists of continuous longitudinal pale stripes rather than antler-like cancellate bands

Cancellate Tiger Beetle: Metallic coppery-bronze tiger beetle of sandy river shores with an intricate, whitish-testaceous antler-like cancellate pattern across the elytra = Lophyra cancellata.

Vittate Tiger Beetle: Fast-running dark green tiger beetle on river sandbars with long linear yellow stripes and exposed whitish mandibles.
