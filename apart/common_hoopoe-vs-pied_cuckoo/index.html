Common Hoopoe or Pied Cuckoo? हुदहुद या चातक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

चातकPied CuckooClamator jacobinus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Hoopoe, as the Pied Cuckoo profile puts it

Brown with black-and-white striped wings and back; prominent crest; different call

फुदकी — भूरी, धारीदार पंख

Common Hoopoe: The combination of a long decurved bill, sandy-buff body, bold black-and-white wings, and a spectacular erectile crest separates the Common Hoopoe from all other Indian birds.

Pied Cuckoo: Large black-and-white bird + loud "piu-piu-piu" wailing call + arrives with monsoon + fruit-eating = Pied Cuckoo. The call is diagnostic and unforgettable.
