Red Pumpkin Beetle or Transverse Ladybird? लाल कद्दू कीड़ा या लेडीबर्ड बीटल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लेडीबर्ड बीटलTransverse LadybirdCoccinella transversalis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Transverse Ladybird, as the Red Pumpkin Beetle profile puts it

Dome-shaped; black wavy bands on elytra; predatory, beneficial insect

सोनपंखी — गोल शरीर, काली पट्टियाँ

Red Pumpkin Beetle: Oblong orange-red body, blackish-grey underside of the abdomen, and feeding damage on pumpkin or gourd leaves distinguish the Red Pumpkin Beetle.

Transverse Ladybird: Small size, dome-shaped orange-red elytra, a black thorax with two creamy corner spots, and three pairs of transverse, wavy black bands across the wings identify the Transverse Ladybird.
