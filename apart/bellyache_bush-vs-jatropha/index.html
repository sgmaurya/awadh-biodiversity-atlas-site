How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jatropha, as the Bellyache Bush profile puts it

Jatropha]] (Jatropha curcas)

Less distinctly red; pink flowers; naturalized in some areas

Bellyache Bush: A shrub with distinctly reddish palmately lobed leaves and bright red flowers in dense clusters is Jatropha gossypiifolia. Do not ingest.

Jatropha: A shrub with large, hand-shaped lobed leaves, red flowers, and toxins in all parts is jatropha. Never ingest the seeds or leaves.
