Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon, as the Spotted Dove profile puts it

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon]] (Treron phoenicopterus)

Much larger and heavier (35 cm); green body; yellow feet; frugivore; different habitat

Spotted Dove: Medium dove with black-and-white spotted necklace patch on lower neck + white spots on wing coverts + rufous tone + melodious "coo-coo-cuckoo" call = Spotted Dove. The neck patch is diagnostic.

Yellow-footed Green Pigeon: Large green pigeon with bright yellow feet, wine-red wing patch, and loud "pis-pis" call from the canopy = Yellow-footed Green Pigeon. The combination of yellow feet and maroon wing is diagnostic.
