Brown Planthopper or Mango Hopper? भूरा फुदका या आम का फुदका?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

आम का फुदकाMango HopperIdioscopus clypealis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Planthopper, as the Mango Hopper profile puts it

Wedge-shaped like mango hopper, but brown; found exclusively on rice at the plant base

धान का फुदका, भूरा, पानी पर

Brown Planthopper: Brown wedge-shaped hoppers in clusters at the base of rice just above the water, jumping when tapped, with round scorched patches in the field: brown planthopper.

Mango Hopper: Tiny, pale green to yellowish-brown wedge-shaped insect on the underside of mango leaves, jumping when disturbed, causing yellowing and bleaching of the leaf, almost certainly the mango hopper.
