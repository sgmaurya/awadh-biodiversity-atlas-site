How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mola Carplet, as the Cotio profile puts it

Mola Carplet]])

Body less compressed; dorsal fin lacks a serrated bony spine; anal fin much shorter

Cotio: A deep-bodied, laterally compressed silvery cyprinid with a serrated bony dorsal spine and an extraordinarily long anal fin of over 28 rays is Osteobrama cotio.

Mola Carplet: A small deep-bodied silvery-golden fish with a tiny inferior mouth and rounded fins in aquatic weeds is the mola; the depth of body and small mouth distinguish it.
