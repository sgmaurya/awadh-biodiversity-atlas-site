Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cuchia, as the Barred Spiny Eel profile puts it

Monopterus cuchia

Complete lack of fins and dorsal spines; robust muddy-brown body with tiny rudimentary gill opening

Barred Spiny Eel: Small spiny eel with fleshy trilobed snout, 24 to 26 short dorsal spines, and rounded caudal fin completely separate from dorsal and anal fins.

Cuchia: A long, dark, eel-like fish with no side fins, a single gill opening below the head, and a single nostril at the mouth, living in mud, is the cuchia; the air-breathing chamber and dormancy distinguish it from true eels.
