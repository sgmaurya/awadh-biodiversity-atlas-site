How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Rohu, as the Silver Carp profile puts it

Larger head; larger mouth; golden-bronze colour; not filter-feeding

बड़ा सिर, बड़ा मुँह, सुनहरा रंग

Rohu: Large Indian carp + reddish-orange fins + wrinkled lower lip + mid-water behavior in rivers/ponds = rohu. The red fins fade quickly after death — identify in the water or immediately after capture.

Silver Carp: A large, silvery, streamlined carp with small head, subterminal mouth, low-set eye, extreme sheen, and filter-feeding behaviour in ponds or aquaculture tanks = silver carp.
