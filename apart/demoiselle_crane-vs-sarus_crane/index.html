Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Vastly larger size, bare crimson head and neck, long pink legs, and resident status

Demoiselle Crane, as the Sarus Crane profile puts it

Much smaller; black neck with white ear tufts; winter visitor

बहुत छोटा; सर्दी में

Demoiselle Crane: The compact build, soft pearl-grey body, solid black foreneck with drooping breast plumes, and silky white feather plumes behind ruby-red eyes identify the Demoiselle Crane.

Sarus Crane: An enormous grey bird standing 150+ cm tall, in pairs, with a red bare head, in a paddy field or wetland — is a Sarus Crane. Nothing else in UP looks like this.
