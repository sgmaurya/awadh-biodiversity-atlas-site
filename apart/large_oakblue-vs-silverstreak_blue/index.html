Large Oakblue or Silverstreak Blue? बड़ा ओकब्लू या सिल्वरस्ट्रीक ब्लू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बड़ा ओकब्लूLarge OakblueArhopala amantes
सिल्वरस्ट्रीक ब्लूSilverstreak BlueIraota timoleon

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Silverstreak Blue, as the Large Oakblue profile puts it

Iraota timoleon]]

Possesses a prominent, jagged silver-white streak on the hindwing underside

Large Oakblue, as the Silverstreak Blue profile puts it

Lacks the bold, jagged silver-white central streak beneath, having instead an intricate pattern of ochre-ringed spots

निचले पंखों पर चांदी की पट्टी नहीं होती, बल्कि गोल बादामी घेरेदार धब्बे होते हैं

Large Oakblue: Look for the large size, vibrant metallic purple-blue upperside in flight, and the intricate dark brown underside with green tornal scales when settled.

Silverstreak Blue: Look for the powerful canopy flight around fig trees and the unmistakable, brilliant jagged silver-white central streak on the chocolate-brown underside.
