How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kachnar, as the Ashoka profile puts it

Leaves bilobed; flowers pink or purple; fruit a flat pod; not cauliflorous

कचनार — दो भागों की पत्ती

Kachnar: A tree with bilobed, camel-footprint-shaped leaves and large pink striped flowers in winter, is kachnar.

Ashoka: A tree with new pink-red pinnate leaves and small orange-red flowers directly on the trunk, in a temple garden, is the sacred ashoka.

Side by side आमने-सामने

KachnarAshoka
SizeDeciduous tree 5–8 m tall, with a spreading crownSemi-evergreen tree, 6–12 m tall, with a dense, rounded crown
LeavesSimple, bilobed to emarginate, 10–20 cm, shaped like a camel's footprint; new leaves reddishPinnate, 30–60 cm, with 6–10 pairs of elliptic leaflets, 8–15 cm; young leaves copper to pink-red, turning dark green
FlowersLarge, showy, pink to magenta with white or purple stripes, five petals, in terminal racemes, January–MarchClusters of small, bright orange-red to yellow, fragrant flowers in dense bunches on the trunk and older branches (cauliflorous), December–March
FruitLong, flat, brown pod (legume), 20–35 cm, with several seedsPod-like follicles, 15–25 cm long, flat, dark brown, ripening April–June
BarkGrey-brown, smooth when youngDark grey, smooth when young, becoming furrowed
The testThe bilobed leaf shape is distinctive and diagnosticLook for the distinctive young pink leaves and cauliflorous orange flowers
