Spotted Coffee Grasshopper or Tobacco Grasshopper? स्पॉटेड कॉफ़ी ग्रासहॉपर या टोबैको ग्रासहॉपर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tobacco Grasshopper, as the Spotted Coffee Grasshopper profile puts it

Slender, elongate body; uniform green or brownish; conical pointed head; no bright aposematic crimson abdomen

चपटी टिड्डी

Spotted Coffee Grasshopper: Rugose tuberculate yellow pronotum, dark tegmina covered with discrete yellow spots, and bright crimson-banded abdomen = Aularches miliaris.

Tobacco Grasshopper: A small (12–18 mm) grasshopper with shortened, crumpled-looking (crenulated) wings that cannot fly, found feeding on tobacco or vegetable leaves in a gregarious group — this is the Tobacco Grasshopper. Larger grasshoppers with fully developed wings that function in flight are different species.
