Crown of Thorns or Pencil Tree? क्राउन ऑफ थॉर्न्स या थूहर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

थूहरPencil TreeEuphorbia tirucalli

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pencil Tree, as the Crown of Thorns profile puts it

Pencil-like smooth cylindrical green branches completely devoid of thorns

पेंसिल जैसी चिकनी हरी टहनियां, कोई कांटे नहीं

Crown of Thorns: A low succulent shrub with grey spiny stems, obovate leaves near tips, and pairs of bright scarlet-red rounded floral bracts with milky sap identifies Euphorbia milii.

Pencil Tree: A pencil-thick-stemmed, minimally branching shrub with sparse tiny leaves and white caustic latex is pencil tree; no other common boundary plant has such characteristic pencil-like stems.

Size—Shrub or small tree 2–5 m tall, tree-like form, stiff and columnar
Branches—Erect, pencil-thick, green or grey-green, four-angled or ridged, minimally branching
Flowers—Inconspicuous, cymose clusters; yellowish; May to September
Fruit—Trilobate capsule, 5–8 mm
Latex—Milky latex in every green stem; it burns skin and can damage the eyes. Never touch it to test the plant
