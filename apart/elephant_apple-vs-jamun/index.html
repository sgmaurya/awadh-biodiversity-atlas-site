How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jamun, as the Elephant Apple profile puts it

जामुन (Syzygium cumini)]]

Opposite simple leaves without prominent parallel ribs; dark purple edible berries

Elephant Apple: An evergreen tree with enormous sharply serrate leaves showing parallel side-veins and heavy green pomiform fruits wrapped in thickened fleshy sepals is Dillenia indica.

Jamun: Large evergreen tree with opposite, smooth, oblong leaves smelling of turpentine when crushed, greenish-white flowers, and purple-black ovoid berries in June–July identify the Jamun tree.
