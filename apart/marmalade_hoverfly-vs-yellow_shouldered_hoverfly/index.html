How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Marmalade Hoverfly, as the Yellow-shouldered Hoverfly profile puts it

Episyrphus balteatus

Abdomen with double black bands on each tergite; lacks solid bright yellow shoulder stripes

Marmalade Hoverfly: A small fly with alternating orange-yellow and black bands on the abdomen, hovering near flowering plants and aphid colonies, is Episyrphus balteatus.

Yellow-shouldered Hoverfly: Slender black hoverfly with bold bright yellow lateral shoulder stripes, entirely yellow scutellum, and banded abdomen.

ColourBlack thorax with striking orange-yellow and black bands on the abdomen—
PatternAlternating bands of dark and bright orange-yellow, resembling marmalade stripes—

EyesLarge, covering most of the head; excellent vision for predationHemispherical reddish-brown eyes, contiguous in males and separated in females
BehaviourHovers motionless in air, then darts; visits flowers repeatedly—
Thorax—Shiny black dorsum bordered laterally by vivid sulfur-yellow shoulder stripes
Scutellum—Wholly bright sulfur-yellow, strongly contrasting with the dark scutum
Abdomen—Alternating black and bright yellow transverse bands with slight basal constriction
Flight habit—Rapid hovering motionlessly in mid-air over flowering canopies before darting
