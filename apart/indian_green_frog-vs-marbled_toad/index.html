Indian Green Frog or Marbled Toad? हरा मेंढक या चितकबरा भेंडुक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Marbled Toad, as the Indian Green Frog profile puts it

Toad; warty; no enlarged disc on fingers; shorter legs; call is a trill

भेंडुक, मस्से वाला, छोटी टाँगें

Indian Green Frog: A bright green, slender frog with a pointed snout and golden eyes, calling in monsoon pools with a rapid curr-curr-curr trill, is the Indian green frog; the slender body and green colour are the key.

Marbled Toad: A pale, marbled, warty toad whose head has no black ridges is the marbled toad; ridges mean the common toad.

Side by side आमने-सामने
