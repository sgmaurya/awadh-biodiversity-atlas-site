Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tawny Eagle, as the Indian Scops Owl profile puts it

Daytime raptor; different shape and behaviour; no ear tufts

शिकारी चील — दिन का पक्षी

Indian Scops Owl: Small owl + prominent ear tufts + soft "poo-poo-poo" call + cavity roosting = Indian Scops Owl. The ear tufts are diagnostic and visible even in silhouette at night.

Tawny Eagle: Tawny Eagle is medium-sized with uniform tawny-brown plumage and pale head. If the bird is much larger, darker, or shows golden crown, it is a different species. The pale head contrasting with brown body is diagnostic.
