Drumstick Tree or Devil Tree? सहजन या सप्तपर्णी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सप्तपर्णीDevil TreeAlstonia scholaris

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Drumstick Tree, as the Devil Tree profile puts it

Leaves compound, feathery, not whorled; seed pods are shorter and thicker; no white latex

कटी-फटी पत्तियाँ, छोटी फलियाँ

Drumstick Tree: Small tree with soft corky grey bark, tripinnate leaves with tiny oval leaflets, hanging cream-white fragrant flowers, and long ribbed green pods, is the Drumstick Tree.

Devil Tree: A tree with leaves whorled in groups of seven or eight around the stem, long drooping seed pods, and white milky latex is saptaparni.
