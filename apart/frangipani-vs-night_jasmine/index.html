Frangipani or Night Jasmine? गुलचीन या हरसिंगार?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

हरसिंगारNight JasmineNyctanthes arbor-tristis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Night Jasmine, as the Frangipani profile puts it

Small leaves; white small flowers; black berries

छोटी पत्तियाँ; सफ़ेद छोटे फूल; काली बेरियाँ

Frangipani: A small tree with large glossy leaves clustered at branch tips and magnificent fragrant five-petalled flowers (white with red/pink centre) is Frangipani.

Night Jasmine: Small tree with square young branches, extremely rough sandpaper-like leaves, and sweet-scented white flowers with bright orange center tubes that open only at night and drop by sunrise, is the Night Jasmine.

Side by side आमने-सामने

SizeSmall deciduous tree or large shrub, 3–8 m tall—
LeavesLarge, oblong-lanceolate, 20–40 cm, glossy, clustered at branch tipsOpposite, ovate, rough, and covered in tiny stiff hairs, feeling exactly like sandpaper when touched

Flower colourVariable: white with pink/red throat, or pink throughout—
Flower arrangementIn clusters (cymes) at branch tips—
StemThick, succulent, pale greyish-green—
The testLarge glossy leaves clustered at tips and highly fragrant flowers are distinctive—
Tree habit—Small tree or large shrub up to 4–10 meters high, with a rough, grey, drooping branch system
Fruit—Flat, heart-shaped, compressed brown capsule, splitting into two halves containing one seed each
Flowering phase—Flowers open at dusk (around 6–7 PM) and drop completely by sunrise (around 5–6 AM)
