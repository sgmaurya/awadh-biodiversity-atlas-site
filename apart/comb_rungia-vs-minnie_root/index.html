How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Minnie Root, as the Comb Rungia profile puts it

Ruellia tuberosa]]`

Erect herb with thick tuberous roots and large showy blue-violet trumpet flowers

Comb Rungia: Look for the dense, one-sided comb-like spikes with broad white-bordered bracts and tiny two-lipped pale blue flowers.

Minnie Root: Fleshy finger-like tuberous roots combined with showy violet-blue funnel flowers and explosive water-sensitive brown capsules immediately identify Ruellia tuberosa.

CorollaSmall, two-lipped, pale blue, lilac, or white with purple throat spots—
Habit—Erect or ascending perennial herb with thick fascicled spindle-shaped tuberous roots
Flowers—Showy trumpet-shaped blossoms, bright violet-blue or mauve, with five spreading rounded lobes
Fruit—Cylindrical brown capsule that explodes violently when moistened, ejecting disc-like seeds
Roots—Prominent cluster of elongated finger-like tuberous roots radiating from the root crown
