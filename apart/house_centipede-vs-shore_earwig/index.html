House Centipede or Shore Earwig? घरेलू कनखजूरा या चिमटा कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घरेलू कनखजूराHouse CentipedeScutigera coleoptrata
चिमटा कीड़ाShore EarwigLabidura riparia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Centipede, as the Shore Earwig profile puts it

Many legs, not six

बहुत सी टाँगें

House Centipede: Slender, yellowish-grey arthropod with exactly 15 pairs of very long, banded legs, moving with extreme speed on indoor walls and floors, is the House Centipede.

Shore Earwig: A large pale striped insect with six legs and a pair of pincers at the tail, on sand or at a light, is the shore earwig.

Side by side आमने-सामने

House CentipedeShore Earwig
Leg CountExactly 15 pairs of legs (30 legs total) in adults; legs increase in length toward the rear—
Leg StyleVery long, delicate, banded with dark and light yellow rings; rear legs are as long as the body—
Body ShapeRigid, flat, yellowish-grey body with three dark longitudinal stripes running down the back—
SpeedMoves extremely fast across floors and walls, sometimes stopping suddenly—
DistinctionCentipede vs Millipede: Centipedes have ONE pair of legs per body segment, move fast, and are predators. Millipedes (like flat-backed millipedes) have TWO pairs of legs per segment, move slowly, and eat dead plants.—
Size—20–30 mm, large for an earwig
Colour—Pale yellowish-brown with darker brown stripes along the body and dark marks on the head
Forceps—A pair of pincers at the tail: long, curved and toothed in the male, straighter and shorter in the female
Wings—Short leathery forewings covering folded hindwings; it flies to lights but rarely by day
Body—Flattened, flexible, elongate; antennae long and beaded
Behaviour—Runs fast; raises the forceps over its back when threatened; burrows in sand

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
