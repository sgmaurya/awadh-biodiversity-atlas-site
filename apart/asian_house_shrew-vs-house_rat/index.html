Asian House Shrew or House Rat? छछूंदर या घर का चूहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घर का चूहाHouse RatRattus rattus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Rat, as the Asian House Shrew profile puts it

Rodent; has a rounded snout; large eyes; long, hairy tail; lacks musk glands; climbs walls

चूहा — गोल नाक, बड़ी आँखें, गंध रहित

Asian House Shrew: Pointed mobile snout, slate-grey velvety coat, thick hairless tail, musky odor, running in rapid straight dashes while emitting high squeaks, identify the Asian House Shrew.

House Rat: A dark-furred, climbing rat with a tail longer than its body, found in grain stores and climbing rafters, is a House Rat.
