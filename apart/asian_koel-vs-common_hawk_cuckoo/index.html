Asian Koel or Common Hawk-Cuckoo? कोयल या पपीहा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कोयलAsian KoelEudynamys scolopaceus
पपीहाCommon Hawk-CuckooHierococcyx varius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asian Koel, as the Common Hawk-Cuckoo profile puts it

Larger; darker overall; heavily barred underparts; red eye; resident, not migratory

कोयल — बड़ी, अधिक काली, साल भर

Common Hawk-Cuckoo: Medium grey and white cuckoo + loud two-syllable "piu-piu" or "brain-fever" call rising in pitch + spring arrival + fruit-eating = Common Hawk-Cuckoo. The call is unmistakable and diagnostic.

Back and wings—Dark grey or greyish-brown; flight feathers darker
Tail—Longish, graduated, grey with white tips to outer feathers
Eyes and face—Yellow orbital ring; dark eye; pale lores
Distinctive call—Loud, sharp, two-syllable "piu-piu" or "piu-WHIT" repeated in succession, rising in pitch — famous as "Brain-fever, brain-fever"
