Kalbasu or Kuria Labeo? कालबासु या कुरिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kuria Labeo, as the Kalbasu profile puts it

Smaller scales; dark longitudinal lines along body scale rows; smaller, lighter body

करौंच / कुरीया — छोटे शल्क, शरीर पर बारीक काली रेखाएँ

Kalbasu, as the Kuria Labeo profile puts it

Labeo calbasu

Body uniform dark slate-black to purplish; significantly larger scales (40 to 44 in lateral line); four conspicuous barbels

Kalbasu: Deep purplish-black body, ventral mouth with fringed lips, four distinct barbels, and bottom-dwelling habit identify the Kalbasu.

Kuria Labeo: Deep-bodied river carp with fringed lips and remarkably fine scales (70 to 85 in lateral line); dark longitudinal flank stripes.
