Banded Gourami or Climbing Perch? खोसटी या कवई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Climbing Perch, as the Banded Gourami profile puts it

Serrated gill cover that spreads; thicker body; no elaborate stripes

गलफड़ा आरीदार, शरीर कड़ा

Banded Gourami: A striped lateral-compressed fish with long thread-like ventral fins and a labyrinth organ is a gourami; the banded gourami is distinguished by 4–5 dark stripes and no elaborate colour in females.

Climbing Perch: A hard, spiny, grey fish with a saw-edged gill cover that survives out of water and walks on wet ground is the climbing perch.
