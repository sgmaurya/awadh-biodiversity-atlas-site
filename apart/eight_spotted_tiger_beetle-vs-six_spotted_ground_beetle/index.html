Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle or Six-spotted Ground Beetle? आठ-चित्तीदार बाघ भृंग या छह-चित्तीदार शिकारी भृंग?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

छह-चित्तीदार शिकारी भृंगSix-spotted Ground BeetleAnthia sexguttata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Six-spotted Ground Beetle, as the Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Much larger, velvety black with six bold white spots; flightless; terrestrial

बहुत बड़ा, मखमली काला शरीर जिस पर छह सफेद धब्बे

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Six-spotted Ground Beetle profile puts it

Smaller; metallic blue-green; eight spots; winged and flies rapidly

छोटा, उड़ने में सक्षम, नीला-हरा रंग और आठ धब्बे

Eight-spotted Tiger Beetle: A large metallic coppery-blue tiger beetle on river sand with four bold creamy-yellow spots on each elytron (eight in total) identifies Cicindela octonotata.

Six-spotted Ground Beetle: A very large flightless velvety-black ground beetle with six bold white spots and massive mandibles that sprays acid identifies Anthia sexguttata.
