Giloy or Malu Creeper? गिलोय या मालू बेल?

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Giloy, as the Malu Creeper profile puts it

Tinospora cordifolia]]

Slender succulent climber with heart-shaped simple leaves and thin aerial roots

Giloy: Heart-shaped deciduous leaves on a climbing woody vine with hanging aerial rootlets, on neem or mango trees in a village setting = giloy. No other common eastern UP climber has all four features together.

Malu Creeper: Gigantic climbing woody habit, massive deeply cleft leaves, paired tendrils, and huge rusty-velvety flat pods distinguish Phanera vahlii from erect Bauhinia trees and herbaceous climbers.
