Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-bellied Drongo, as the Hair-crested Drongo profile puts it

Dicrurus caerulescens

Contrasting pure white belly and vent; smaller size; deeply forked tail without curls

Hair-crested Drongo: Large black drongo with outward and upward curled tail outer tips, long downcurved bill, and hair-like crest plumes on forehead.

White-bellied Drongo: White-bellied Drongo is distinguished by its glossy blue-black plumage with a striking white belly. The combination of white belly, crested head, red eye, and small size is unique among drongos.
