Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Glassy Fish, as the Gangetic Leaffish profile puts it

Parambassis ranga

Translucent, laterally compressed glass-like body; yellow fins with bright blue margins; small mouth

Gangetic Leaffish: Mottled brassy-brown leaffish with cavernous protrusible mouth extending past eye, and 12 to 14 sharp dorsal spines; weed dweller.

Indian Glassy Fish: A tiny (6–8 cm), transparent fish with large eyes, visible internal organs, and a silvery longitudinal stripe is unmistakably an Indian Glassy Fish.
