Field Bindweed or Obscure Morning Glory? हिरनखुरी या वन कलमी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Field Bindweed, as the Obscure Morning Glory profile puts it

Smaller, white or pink flowers; different leaf shape; different growth habit

सफेद-गुलाबी फूल

Field Bindweed: Trailing or twining winter vine with arrowhead-shaped leaves, small white or pink funnel-shaped flowers that close by noon, and a deep white root system, is the Field Bindweed.

Obscure Morning Glory: A hairy, slender climbing annual with small heart-shaped leaves and bright yellow morning flowers that close by afternoon is Obscure Morning Glory.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Field BindweedObscure Morning Glory
Plant habitSlender, trailing or twining perennial vine, growing 20–100 cm long, wrapping counter-clockwise around crops—
LeavesAlternately arranged, simple, arrowhead-shaped (sagittate or hastate), with two sharp pointing lobes at the baseAlternate, ovate to heart-shaped, 2–6 cm, hairy, entire or slightly lobed at base
FlowersTrumpet or funnel-shaped (1.5–2.5 cm wide), single or in pairs, pure white or marked with light pink bandsSolitary or in small clusters, funnel-shaped, yellow to orange-yellow, 1.5–2.5 cm across
Root systemExtensive, deep, fleshy white taproot system that spreads horizontally and shoots up new plants—
Flowering phaseFlowers open in the early morning sunlight and close tight by noon or early afternoon—
Stem—Twining, herbaceous, slender, hairy, purple or green
Fruit—Globular capsule, 0.8–1 cm diameter
Tendrils—Twining stem provides climbing mechanism
The test—Small heart-shaped leaves; yellow morning flowers; hairy twining herb of field margins
