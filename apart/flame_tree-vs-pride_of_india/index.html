How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Flame Tree, as the Pride of India profile puts it

Finely pinnate leaves; red-orange flowers; flat red pods

गुलमोहर — लाल फूल, पंखनुमा पत्तियाँ

Flame Tree: Large tree with smooth grey bark, feathery bipinnate leaves, a wide flat canopy, brilliant orange-red flowers in summer, and very long, flat, sword-like woody pods, is the Flame Tree (Gulmohar).

Pride of India: A deciduous tree bare in dry season, with large glossy leaves, spectacular large crinkled-petal purple, pink, or white flowers in May–July, and flaking smooth bark with pale yellow inner wood.
