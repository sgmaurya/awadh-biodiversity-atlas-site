Japanese Mazus or Scarlet Pimpernel? माज़ुस या कृष्णनील?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Scarlet Pimpernel, as the Japanese Mazus profile puts it

Leaves more rounded with red dots; flowers solitary in axils, not in racemes; larger overall

लाल बिंदु वाली पत्तियाँ, बड़े फूल

Japanese Mazus: A tiny, hairy plant with opposite rounded leaves and pale purple-pink bilabiate flowers in clusters, growing in wet rice fields in winter, is Mazus pumilus.

Scarlet Pimpernel: A delicate, creeping herb with tiny opposite leaves bearing red dots and small red or blue solitary flowers is scarlet pimpernel.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Japanese MazusScarlet Pimpernel
Plant formSmall, creeping to ascending annual, 10–30 cm, with fine hairsSlender, creeping to ascending annual herb, 15–40 cm
LeavesOpposite, obovate to spatulate, 0.5–1.5 cm, blunt-tipped, entire marginsOpposite, sessile or short-petioled, oval-oblong, 0.8–1.5 cm, with red dots
FlowersSmall, pale purple to pink, bilabiate (2-lipped), 4–6 mm across, in terminal racemesFive-petaled, red, orange-red, pink, or blue, solitary at leaf axils, 5–8 mm across
StemSlender, hairy, rooting at lower nodesSquare in cross-section, hairless or slightly hairy
FruitSmall, globular capsule, 2–3 mmGlobular capsule, 0.4–0.6 cm, containing many tiny seeds
The testSmall herbaceous plant with opposite leaves and tiny pink-purple flowers in wet fieldsObserve red dots on leaves and small solitary flowers

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
