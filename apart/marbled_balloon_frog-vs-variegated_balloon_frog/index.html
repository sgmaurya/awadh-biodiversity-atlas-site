How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Marbled Balloon Frog, as the Variegated Balloon Frog profile puts it

Uperodon systoma

Noticeably larger (SVL up to 65 mm), more globular in body shape, with continuous dark reticulated marbling on the back (Chanda 2002)

Marbled Balloon Frog: Heavily built, globular balloon-shaped burrowing frog with marbled olive-and-black pattern, tiny pointed head, and spade-like digging tubercles.

Variegated Balloon Frog: Small plump burrowing frog with dark brown blotches on a greyish-brown ground, tiny mouth, and two spade-like metatarsal tubercles on the hindfoot.
