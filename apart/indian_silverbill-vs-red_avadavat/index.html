Indian Silverbill or Red Avadavat? सफ़ेद गले की मुनिया या लाल मुनिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Avadavat, as the Indian Silverbill profile puts it

Red Munia]] (Estrilda astrild) — if rare visitor

Red rump; red bill; red eye-stripe; underparts streaked

Indian Silverbill, as the Red Avadavat profile puts it

Indian Silverbill]] (Euodice malabarica) — if confused

Grey and white plumage; black bill with pale lower mandible; different habitat and behaviour

Indian Silverbill: Tiny brown finch with distinctive silver-white bill and white rump, always in flocks, soft "chi-chi-chi" calls = Indian Silverbill. The pale bill is diagnostic.

Red Avadavat: Very small finch + brilliant red breeding male or buff female + flock living + near water + grass seed feeding + soft "zee" calls = Red Avadavat. The breeding male's bright red plumage is unmistakable.
