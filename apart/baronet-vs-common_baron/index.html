Baronet or Common Baron? बैरोनेट या कॉमन बैरन तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Baron, as the Baronet profile puts it

Larger butterfly with olive-brown uppersides, lacking bright tawny-orange ground colour

बड़ा आकार, जैतूनी-भूरा रंग और नारंगी रंग का अभाव

Baronet: Instantly recognized by its rich tawny-orange upperside with bold black bars, flat sun-basking posture, and bright red underside with white discal band.

Common Baron: A medium-large dark butterfly with distinctive white and coloured patches on dark wings, found in forest margins and near suitable host trees, is the Common Baron.
