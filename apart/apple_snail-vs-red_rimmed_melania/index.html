Freshwater Apple Snail or Red-rimmed Melania? घोंघा या जल घोंघा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जल घोंघाRed-rimmed MelaniaMelanoides tuberculata

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red-rimmed Melania, as the Freshwater Apple Snail profile puts it

Melanoides tuberculata]] (Red-rimmed Melania)

Much smaller (10–25 mm); narrow, pointed spiral shell; in silt-bottom ponds

Freshwater Apple Snail, as the Red-rimmed Melania profile puts it

Indian Apple Snail]] (Pila globosa)

Much larger (50–70 mm); round, ball-like green shell; lays white egg masses

Red-rimmed Melania: Small, screw-like conical shell (15–25 mm) with 10–12 whorls, light brown with distinctive reddish spots, crawling on muddy canal beds, is the Red-rimmed Melania.

Shell Texture—Hard, sculpted with fine vertical ribs and spiral ridges
Colouration—Light brown, greyish-yellow, or tan, marked with distinctive reddish-brown dots and streaks aligned with the spiral
Hiding Habit—Burrows into mud or silt during the day; climbs rocks and vegetation at night
