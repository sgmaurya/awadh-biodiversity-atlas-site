Red-headed Vulture or White-rumped Vulture? राज गिद्ध या गिद्ध?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गिद्धWhite-rumped VultureGyps bengalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-rumped Vulture, as the Red-headed Vulture profile puts it

Smaller (75–80 cm); bare head cream or yellow, not red; body paler brown

पीले सिर वाला गिद्ध

Red-headed Vulture: A very large dark vulture with a distinctive bright red bare head and neck. No other large vulture in eastern UP shows this red head coloration. Soars at great heights; descends to carrion.

White-rumped Vulture: White-rumped Vulture is the largest dark brown vulture with a sharply contrasting white rump patch. The contrast between dark body and white rump is the most diagnostic feature. No other large vulture in the region shows this pattern.
