Common Kestrel or Shikra? केस्ट्रेल या शिकरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

केस्ट्रेलCommon KestrelFalco tinnunculus
शिकराShikraAccipiter badius

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Shikra, as the Common Kestrel profile puts it

Rounded wings (not pointed); grey back; barred underparts; hunts from cover, does not hover

शिकरा — गोल पंख, धूसर पीठ, झाड़ियों से शिकार

Common Kestrel: Small rufous-brown falcon with pointed wings, long barred tail, and a black-tipped yellow bill, hovering stationary over crop fields, is a Common Kestrel.

Shikra: Small grey-and-white forest hawk with fine rufous barring on the breast, yellow/orange eyes, and short rounded wings perching upright in leafy trees is a Shikra.
