How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striated Grassbird, as the Indian Grassbird profile puts it

Megalurus palustris

Significantly larger total length; long graduated unpatterned tail; duller sandy-brown coloration

Indian Grassbird: Secretive tall-grass dweller with unusually stout bill, boldly streaked black, rufous, and white mantle, and long graduated tail broadly tipped white.

Striated Grassbird: A dark, heavily streaked warbler of tall wet grasses and reed beds. Smaller and darker than Ashy Prinia. Secretive; identify by song—a distinctive rambling warble. Look in dense vegetation along water bodies.
