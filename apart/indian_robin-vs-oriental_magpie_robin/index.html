Indian Robin or Oriental Magpie-Robin? कालचुरी या दहियर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

दहियरOriental Magpie-RobinCopsychus saularis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oriental Magpie-Robin, as the Indian Robin profile puts it

Similar size; white wing patch and breast; found in wetter forests

जंगली कालचुरी — सफेद छाती

Indian Robin: Small upright bird with characteristic tail-cocking + glossy black male with white rump (or rufous female with white rump) + clear whistled song = Indian Robin. The combination of tail-cocking behaviour and white rump is diagnostic.

Oriental Magpie-Robin: Neat black-and-white pattern (grey-and-white in females), long white wing bar, white outer tail feathers, and a tail that is frequently cocked upward identify the Oriental Magpie-Robin.
