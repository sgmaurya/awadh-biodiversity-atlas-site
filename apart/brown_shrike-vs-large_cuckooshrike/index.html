How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Shrike, as the Large Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Smaller; rufous crown and back; different bill shape

काला लटेरा

Brown Shrike: Brown Shrike shows a rusty-chestnut back with a black eye-stripe and white underparts. If the tail is rufous on a pale sandy bird, it is an Isabelline Shrike. If the back is dark slate-grey, it is a different species.

Large Cuckooshrike: A large (25–28 cm), uniformly grey-brown perching bird with a stout hooked bill and rounded shape, gleaning caterpillars in tree foliage, is a Large Cuckooshrike. Smaller shrike species are brightly coloured or patterned and sit on exposed perches.
