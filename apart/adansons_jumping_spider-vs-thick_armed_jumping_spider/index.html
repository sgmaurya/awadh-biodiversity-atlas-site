How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Adanson's Jumping Spider, as the Thick-armed Jumping Spider profile puts it

Hasarius adansoni

Slender normal first pair of legs; male has white crescent on carapace and white pedipalp tufts

Thick-armed Jumping Spider: Tiny jumping spider with massively swollen, paddle-like, black-fringed first pair of legs and dark bronze-sheened body.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Adanson's Jumping SpiderThick-armed Jumping Spider
FormSmall, compact spider; cephalothorax is broad and high; abdomen is oval—
Male ColourDark brown to black; features a striking white crescent-shaped band on the back of the head and white spots on the abdomen—
Female ColourUniformly lighter sandy-brown or light brown, with less distinct darker chevrons on the abdomen—
EyesTwo massive, shiny, forward-facing principal eyes; turns its body to face observersEnormous anterior median eyes providing binocular vision; smaller lateral eyes
MovementQuick, rapid runs followed by sudden pauses; hops or jumps over cracks and onto preyDeliberate, jerky stalking movement punctuated by sudden rapid forward leaps
BehaviourStrictly active hunter; does not construct webs for capturing prey; spins a small silk cell for resting and egg-laying—
Forelegs—Greatly enlarged, thickened, and flattened first pair of legs with a dense ventral fringe of black hairs
Body color—Glossy dark brown to jet-black with metallic bronze reflections and white scale patches
Cephalothorax—High, box-like, flat-topped cephalic region with vertical sides

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
