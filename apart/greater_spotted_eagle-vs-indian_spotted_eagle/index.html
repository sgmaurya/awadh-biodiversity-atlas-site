Greater Spotted Eagle or Indian Spotted Eagle? ग्रेटर स्पॉटेड ईगल या इंडियन स्पॉटेड ईगल?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Spotted Eagle, as the Greater Spotted Eagle profile puts it

Indian Spotted Eagle]] (Clanga hastata)

Smaller; duller spots; resident; darker overall

Greater Spotted Eagle, as the Indian Spotted Eagle profile puts it

Greater Spotted Eagle]] (Clanga clanga) — winter visitor

Larger (65–72 cm); darker overall; less prominent spotting; winter visitor only

Greater Spotted Eagle: Greater Spotted Eagle is distinguished by its large size, dark brown plumage with white spots on wings and back, yellow eyes and legs, and powerful flight. The white spots are more prominent than in Indian Spotted Eagle.

Indian Spotted Eagle: Medium-sized brown eagle with pale spots on back and wing coverts, pale streaked underparts, and yellow legs. Year-round in forest edges. Medium size with distinctive spotting separates it from both larger and smaller raptors.
