Arrowleaf Sida or Touch-me-not? अतीबला या लाजवंती?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Touch-me-not, as the Arrowleaf Sida profile puts it

Compound bipinnate leaves sensitive to touch; pink globose flower balls

छुईमुई, छूने पर पत्तियाँ बंद होती हैं

Arrowleaf Sida: Tough fibrous subshrub with alternate rhomboid-lanceolate leaves cuneate and entire at base, bearing pale yellow morning-opening flowers and 8–10 awned mericarps identifies Sida rhombifolia.

Touch-me-not: Low-growing, prickly, creeping plant + bipinnate feathery leaves that fold instantly on touch + pink pompom flowers = Mimosa pudica. The touch-sensitivity alone is diagnostic — nothing else in eastern UP common vegetation responds this way.
