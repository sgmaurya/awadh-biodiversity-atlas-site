Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Grey Hornbill, as the Great Hornbill profile puts it

Indian Grey Hornbill]] (Ocyceros birostris)

Much smaller; brownish-grey plumage; small pointed black-and-pale casque; widespread in villages and groves

Great Hornbill: The gigantic body, bright yellow concave double-horned casque, and pure white tail with a single broad black band immediately separate the Great Hornbill from both grey and pied hornbills.

Indian Grey Hornbill: Large grey-brown bird with a long tail, distinctive flap-and-glide flight, and a massive curved bill topped by a pointed casque, moving in the canopy of mature trees, is an Indian Grey Hornbill.

Flight PatternHeavy, undulating flight with loud roaring wingbeats audible from far away—
Size—Large bird (50–60 cm total length) with a long tail and a massive curved bill
Colouration—Uniform brownish-grey overall; pale grey neck and underparts; dark primary feathers
Tail Pattern—Long, graduated tail; grey with a black band near the end and broad white tips
Flight—Distinctive undulating flap-and-glide sequence; wings make a rustling sound in flight
Behaviour—Strictly arboreal; moves actively in the canopy of mature fruiting trees
