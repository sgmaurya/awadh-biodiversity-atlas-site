How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Barn Owl, as the Mottled Wood Owl profile puts it

Barn Owl]] (Tyto alba)

Heart-shaped facial disc; pale golden-buff plumage; black eyes

Barn Owl: Medium-sized ghostly white owl with a distinct heart-shaped white facial disc, dark eyes, and a silent flight near buildings at night is a Barn Owl.

Mottled Wood Owl: A dark brown, medium-sized owl with deep "hoo-hoo-hoo" calls from wooded areas is a Mottled Wood Owl. Smaller owls with obvious spots are Spotted Owlets. Larger owls near water or with ear tufts are different species.
