How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Yellow-spotted Tiger Beetle, as the Striolate Tiger Beetle profile puts it

Markings form discrete rounded yellow spots rather than fine wavy striations

धारियों की जगह गोल पीले धब्बे

Striolate Tiger Beetle: A small bronze tiger beetle on sandy shores with intricate narrow wavy whitish striations and marginal lunules on the elytra identifies Lophyra striolata.

Yellow-spotted Tiger Beetle: A medium-sized bronze tiger beetle on river sand with prominent discrete bright yellow rounded spots and a short middle band identifies Cicindela flavomaculata.
