How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Purple Orchid Tree, as the Kachnar profile puts it

Flowers deep magenta or purple; leaves sometimes less deeply lobed; less common

गहरे बैंगनी फूल

Kachnar, as the Purple Orchid Tree profile puts it

Similar bilobed leaves, but flowers have more irregular variegation; blooms January–March

अन्य कनियार — फूल अलग नमूने के, बाद में खिलते हैं

Kachnar: A tree with bilobed, camel-footprint-shaped leaves and large pink striped flowers in winter, is kachnar.

Purple Orchid Tree: A semi-deciduous tree with distinctly bilobed leaves and large, showy purple or pink orchid-like flowers with darker veining. Flowers appear in autumn and early winter on nearly bare branches.
