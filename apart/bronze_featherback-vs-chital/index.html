Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

Smaller (30–40 cm); bronze-coloured; less distinct blotches; different tooth structure

Bronze Featherback: Flat knife-like body, uniform copper-bronze colouration, a small feather-like dorsal fin, and a continuous anal fin confluent with the caudal fin identify the Bronze Featherback.

Clown Knifefish: A large, extremely flattened, dark fish 50–100 cm long with a wave-like fin running the entire length of back and belly, blotchy coloration, and large mouth with sharp teeth, found in rivers and deep ponds, is Chitala chitala; fin ray counts and tooth morphology confirm identification.
