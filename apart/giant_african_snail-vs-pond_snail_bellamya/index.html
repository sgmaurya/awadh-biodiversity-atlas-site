Giant African Snail or Banded Pond Snail? अफ्रीकी घोंघा या घोंघी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घोंघीBanded Pond SnailBellamya bengalensis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Banded Pond Snail, as the Giant African Snail profile puts it

Much smaller (25–40 mm); aquatic; thinner shell; spiral bands

बहुत छोटी, पानी की

Giant African Snail: A very large (8–10 cm), tan-brown, conical snail with a thick slime trail, feeding openly on vegetation at night, is the giant African snail; no native snail approaches this size.

Banded Pond Snail: A thumb-top-sized, pointed, banded, lidded pond snail that gives birth to live shelled young is the banded pond snail.
