Brinjal or Potato? बैंगन या आलू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बैंगनBrinjalSolanum melongena
आलूPotatoSolanum tuberosum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Potato, as the Brinjal profile puts it

Potato]] (Solanum tuberosum)

Grows from underground tubers; leaves are pinnate; flowers are white or pale purple; fruit is green, toxic

Brinjal, as the Potato profile puts it

Larger plant; spiny leaves and stems; large purple fruits hanging from branches; woody sub-shrub

बैंगन — बड़ा पौधा, कांटे, बड़े फल

Brinjal: Erect branching sub-shrub with velvety lobed leaves, star-like purple flowers with yellow centers, and large glossy purple or green edible berries, is the Brinjal.

Potato: A medium-height bushy plant with compound pinnate leaves, white or pink flowers in clusters, and large underground tubers (kanda) with eyes (buds) on the surface is potato.
