How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Starling, as the Common Myna profile puts it

Speckled iridescent plumage; short tail; pointed bill; winter visitor

तारा मैना — सर्दियों का आगंतुक

Common Myna: Brown-and-black bird with yellow bill + large yellow bare eye-patch + white wing flash + yellow legs + confident walking gait = Common Myna. No other common eastern UP bird has the yellow eye-patch + white wing combination.

Common Starling: Common Starling in winter is a dark bird heavily spotted with white, walking on the ground in flocks. If the bird shows white spotting only on head and neck with buff coloring, or if it is crested, it is a different species.
