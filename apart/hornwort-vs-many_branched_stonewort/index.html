How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Hornwort, as the Many-branched Stonewort profile puts it

True flowering plant with dichotomously branched stiff, brittle, toothed whorled leaves; rootless free-floating habit

जल-कुंभी / हॉर्नवॉर्ट

Hornwort: A fully submerged, free-floating plant with needle-like, finely divided leaves in stiff whorls and no roots is hornwort.

Many-branched Stonewort: A soft, unencrusted submerged alga with twice-branched whorled branchlets surrounded by a clear gelatinous mucilage cloud is Nitella hyalina.

Side by side आमने-सामने

HornwortMany-branched Stonewort
Plant habitEntirely submerged, branching, free-floating, dark green—
LeavesDeeply divided, needle-like, arranged in whorls, rigid, stiff texture when handled—
RootsNone; plant is fully independent of soil—
FlowersTiny, obscure, emerging briefly at water surface—
FruitSmall, hard, spine-tipped, 1-seeded nut—
FeelStiff, brittle; feels like fine wires when squeezed—
Habit—Submerged, delicate, bushy charophyte alga growing rooted in fine mud
Branching—Heteroclemous; nodes bear two distinct tiers of branchlets forming dense whorls
Mucilage—Fresh growing whorls and gametangia typically enveloped in a clear mucous cloud
Color—Bright translucent grass-green to dark emerald-green; soft and flexible
Calcification—Weakly calcified or uncalcified; lacks the brittle crust typical of most stoneworts
Axis—Slender central axis without a cortex; internodes clear and transparent
