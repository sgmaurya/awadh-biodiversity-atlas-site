How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Oval St Andrew's Cross Spider, as the Garden Cross Spider profile puts it

Similar size; pale tan or yellow-brown; less red coloration; web geometry differs slightly

Garden Cross Spider, as the Oval St Andrew's Cross Spider profile puts it

Similar size; has red or orange spots on abdomen; darker overall coloration

Garden Cross Spider: Small black-and-white striped orb-weaver with distinctive red or orange spots on abdomen, in low vegetation, web 25–35 cm, = Argiope pulchella.

Oval St Andrew's Cross Spider: Pale tan or buff-coloured Argiope with subdued patterning, in open grassland or field margins, = Argiope aemula.
