How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Decorative Silver Orb Spider, as the Long-jawed Orb-weaver profile puts it

Abdomen is silvery-white with bright green and yellow patterns, shaped like an oval; builds vertical webs

चांदी जैसी मकड़ी — चांदी जैसा चमकीला गोल पेट

Decorative Silver Orb Spider: Tiny silvery-green spider with elongated, pointed abdomen, on horizontal web in vegetation near water, = Leucauge decorata.

Long-jawed Orb-weaver: Slender, straw-yellow spider with very long legs and forward-projecting jaws, resting in a stick-like pose on reeds or building horizontal webs over water, is the Long-jawed Orb-weaver.
