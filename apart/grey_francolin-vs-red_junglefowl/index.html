How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey Francolin, as the Red Junglefowl profile puts it

Grey Francolin]] (Ortygornis pondicerianus)

Compact, rounded body without comb or long arched tail; intricately barred greyish-brown plumage with buff throat

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.

Red Junglefowl: The erect red comb, golden neck-hackles, arched glossy sickle tail feathers, and slender horizontal posture distinguish the wild Red Junglefowl from other regional gamebirds.
