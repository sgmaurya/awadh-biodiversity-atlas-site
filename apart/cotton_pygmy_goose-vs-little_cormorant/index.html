How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Cormorant, as the Cotton Pygmy-goose profile puts it

Different bird family; black all over; dives

पनकौवा

Cotton Pygmy-goose: The Cotton Pygmy-goose is the smallest duck and the only one with a distinctly patterned male (white face with black spots). If on vegetation-dense water, small size, and male pattern is diagnostic.

Little Cormorant: A small, all-black diving bird in freshwater that surfaces far from where it dived and perches with wings spread open — is a Little Cormorant. The glossy black colour and diving habit are definitive.
