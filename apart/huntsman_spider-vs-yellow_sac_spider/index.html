Huntsman Spider or Yellow Sac Spider? शिकारी मकड़ी या पीली थैली मकड़ी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

शिकारी मकड़ीHuntsman SpiderHeteropoda venatoria
पीली थैली मकड़ीYellow Sac SpiderCheiracanthium melanostomum

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Huntsman Spider, as the Yellow Sac Spider profile puts it

Much larger (body 20–30 mm); crab-like legs; mottled grey-brown; found on walls and trees

शिकारी मकड़ी — बहुत बड़ा आकार, मटमैला भूरा रंग

Huntsman Spider: Flat crab-like body, leg span up to 12 cm, yellowish-brown colour, and rapid running without web-spinning identifies the Huntsman Spider.

Yellow Sac Spider: Small, pale yellow-green spider (8–10 mm) with distinct jet-black-tipped jaws, hiding in a tube-like white silk sac inside a rolled leaf during the day, is the Yellow Sac Spider (C. melanostomum). Note: C. triviale and C. indicum are co-occurring sister species in UP, separable only by microscopy.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Huntsman SpiderYellow Sac Spider
Size & ShapeLarge, flattened spider; legs are long and positioned laterally (crab-like posture), allowing rapid sideways movement—
ColourYellowish-brown to greyish-brown; females have a dark band behind the eyes and a light border on the carapace; males have a dark, heart-shaped mark behind the eyes—
LegsVery long, banded or spotted with fine black bristles, with the second pair of legs being slightly longer than the first—
EyesEight small eyes arranged in two horizontal rows of four on the front of the carapace—
Egg SacFemales carry a flat, disc-shaped, papery-white egg sac (about 12–18 mm diameter) beneath their sternum using their chelicerae—
Size—Small spider; female body length around 8–10 mm, male slightly smaller
Colouration—Uniform pale yellow-green to straw-yellow body; carapace is slightly darker
Chelicerae—Black-tipped jaws (melanostomum = black-mouthed), contrasting with the yellow head
No Capture Web—Found wandering on foliage at night; does not spin horizontal or vertical orb webs
Daytime Retreat—Hides inside a small, white, tube-like silk sac built inside curled or rolled leaves

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
