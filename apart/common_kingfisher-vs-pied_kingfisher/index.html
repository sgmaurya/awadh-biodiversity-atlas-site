Common Kingfisher or Pied Kingfisher? राम चिरैया या छोटा किलकिला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Pied Kingfisher, as the Common Kingfisher profile puts it

Black and white (no orange); hovers extensively over water

Common Kingfisher, as the Pied Kingfisher profile puts it

Common Kingfisher]] (Alcedo atthis)

Much smaller (16 cm); brilliant blue-green upperparts; orange underparts; does not hover vertically; perches in shade

Pied Kingfisher: Medium size, black-and-white speckled pattern with a black crest, dagger-like black bill, and characteristic vertical hovering over open water identify the Pied Kingfisher.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Common KingfisherPied Kingfisher
SizeSmall, compact bird (16–17 cm total length); short-tailed, large-headedMedium-sized kingfisher (around 25–30 cm total length); much larger than the Common Kingfisher
UpperpartsIridescent electric cobalt-blue to turquoise; bright blue back stripe—
UnderpartsRich warm orange-chestnut (rufous) underparts—
BillLong, straight, black, dagger-like billLong, heavy, straight, and jet-black bill; shaped like a dagger
FlightVery fast, direct, low over water; appears as a flashing blue streak—
VoiceSharp, high-pitched chee-chee whistle, usually given in flight—
Plumage—Striking black-and-white speckled and barred pattern; white underparts; prominent black crest
Sexual Dimorphism—Males have two black breast bands (upper broad, lower thin); females have a single broken breast band
Behaviour—Hovers stationary vertically in the air before diving headfirst into the water; very loud and screechy
