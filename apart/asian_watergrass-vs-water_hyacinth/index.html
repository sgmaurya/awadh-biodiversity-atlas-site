Asian Watergrass or Water Hyacinth? एशियन जल घास या जलकुंभी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जलकुंभीWater HyacinthEichhornia crassipes

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Hyacinth, as the Asian Watergrass profile puts it

Broad round leaves; swollen spongy petioles; large violet-blue flowers

जलकुंभी, बड़े बैंगनी फूल

Asian Watergrass: Free-floating aquatic grass with swollen spongy leaf sheaths, short ovate-oblong leaves with water-repellent undersides, and small triangular awned panicles identifies Hygroryza aristata.

Water Hyacinth: Floating mat + round shiny leaves + swollen spongy petioles + feathery purple-black roots = water hyacinth. The inflated petiole is unmistakable once seen.
