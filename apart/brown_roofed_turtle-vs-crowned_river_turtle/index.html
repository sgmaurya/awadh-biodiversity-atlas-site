Brown Roofed Turtle or Crowned River Turtle? ब्राउन रूफ़्ड कछुआ या क्राउन्ड रिवर कछुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crowned River Turtle, as the Brown Roofed Turtle profile puts it

Crowned River Turtle]] (Hardella thurjii)

Much larger (carapace up to 35 cm); lacks doming; different head markings

Brown Roofed Turtle, as the Crowned River Turtle profile puts it

Brown Roofed Turtle]] (Pangshura smithii)

Smaller (16–24 cm carapace); lacks crowned head; domed shell

Brown Roofed Turtle: A medium-sized turtle with a distinctly domed or roofed carapace without a sharp ridge is a Brown Roofed Turtle. It is easily distinguished from the Indian Tent Turtle by the absence of a central ridge.

Crowned River Turtle: India's largest freshwater turtle with a distinctive crowned head is the Crowned River Turtle. Any large turtle in the Ghaghra river with a prominent head crown is likely this species.
