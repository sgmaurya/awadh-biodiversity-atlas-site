How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sage Weed, as the Common Knotgrass profile puts it

Salvia plebeia]]

Erect aromatic herb with square stems, broad petioled leaves, and dense panicles of lilac flowers

Common Knotgrass: Prostrate ground-hugging mat habit, silvery fringed stipular sheaths (ochreas), tiny leaves, and minute shining 3-gonous nutlets distinguish Polygonum plebeium.

Sage Weed: Distinctly four-angled grooved stems, opposite wrinkled crenate leaves, and dense broom-like panicles of tiny two-lipped lilac flowers yielding mucilaginous seeds identify Salvia plebeia.
