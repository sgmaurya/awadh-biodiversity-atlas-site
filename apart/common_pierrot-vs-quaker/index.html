How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Pierrot, as the Quaker profile puts it

Common Pierrot]])

Boldly marked below with heavy black bars and bands; visits sunny ber bushes

Common Pierrot: Tiny bright blue butterfly (male) or duller brown female with pale spotted underside, always associated with ber (jujube) bushes, is the Common Pierrot.

Quaker: A tiny butterfly fluttering weakly strictly in deep shade, with spotless creamy white underwings marked only by faint brown lines and tiny costal specks, is the Quaker.
