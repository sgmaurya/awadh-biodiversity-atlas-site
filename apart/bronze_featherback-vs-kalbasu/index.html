Bronze Featherback or Kalbasu? मोई या कालबासु?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kalbasu, as the Bronze Featherback profile puts it

Cylindrical body with thick scales; downward mouth with barbels; separate tail fin

कालबासु — बेलनाकार शरीर, काला रंग, मूँछ

Bronze Featherback: Flat knife-like body, uniform copper-bronze colouration, a small feather-like dorsal fin, and a continuous anal fin confluent with the caudal fin identify the Bronze Featherback.

Kalbasu: Deep purplish-black body, ventral mouth with fringed lips, four distinct barbels, and bottom-dwelling habit identify the Kalbasu.
