Common Crane or Sarus Crane? कूंज या सारस?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

सारसSarus CraneAntigone antigone

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Sarus Crane, as the Common Crane profile puts it

Sarus Crane]] (Antigone antigone)

Resident; taller (150–180 cm); bare red head and neck

Common Crane, as the Sarus Crane profile puts it

Smaller; grey with black-and-white head; winter visitor only

सर्दी में आता है; काला-सफेद सिर

Common Crane: Common Crane has a distinctive black and white neck pattern with white cheek patch, grey body, and black flight feathers. The white cheek patch clearly separates it from Sarus Crane, which has a bare red head. Common Crane is smaller than Sarus Crane.

Sarus Crane: An enormous grey bird standing 150+ cm tall, in pairs, with a red bare head, in a paddy field or wetland — is a Sarus Crane. Nothing else in UP looks like this.
