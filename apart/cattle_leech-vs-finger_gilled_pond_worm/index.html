How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Cattle Leech, as the Finger-gilled Pond Worm profile puts it

Distinctly larger flattened annelid with anterior and posterior suckers; dorsoventrally flattened muscular body

जोंक

Indian Cattle Leech: A dark brown-black slimy worm 2–7 cm long with two suckers (small front, large back), found in moist grass near cattle, is Hirudinaria granulosa; microscopic examination of jaw structure confirms identification.

Finger-gilled Pond Worm: A slender, transparent aquatic worm with a cup-shaped ciliated gill fossa bearing finger-like lobes at the tail tip is Dero digitata.
