How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Streak-throated Woodpecker, as the Grey-headed Woodpecker profile puts it

Streak-throated Woodpecker]] (Picus xanthopygaeus)

Streaked throat, scaly-patterned underparts, and pale yellowish-green upperparts

Grey-headed Woodpecker: The crimson crown and black nape in the male, black and grey crest and nape in the female, dull olive-green underparts, and bright yellow-green rump distinguish the Grey-headed Woodpecker (Baker 1927).

Streak-throated Woodpecker: Bright grass-green upperparts with brilliant yellow rump and finely streaked throat.
