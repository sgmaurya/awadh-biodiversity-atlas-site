Bael or Elephant Apple? बेल या चलता?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

बेलBaelAegle marmelos
चलताElephant AppleDillenia indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Elephant Apple, as the Bael profile puts it

Large simple leaves with prominent parallel ribs; large white flowers; grows near streams

चलता — बड़ी पत्तियां, जंगली फल

Bael: Compound leaves with exactly three leaflets, sharp axillary thorns, and a large round woody-shelled fruit containing sweet orange-yellow pulp identify Aegle marmelos.

Elephant Apple: An evergreen tree with enormous sharply serrate leaves showing parallel side-veins and heavy green pomiform fruits wrapped in thickened fleshy sepals is Dillenia indica.
