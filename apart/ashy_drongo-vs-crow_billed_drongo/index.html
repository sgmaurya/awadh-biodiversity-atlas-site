How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashy Drongo, as the Crow-billed Drongo profile puts it

Ashy Drongo]] (Dicrurus leucophaeus)

Slaty-grey plumage, crimson-red iris, more deeply forked tail, and slender bill

Ashy Drongo: Ashy Drongo is pale grey throughout with a long forked tail. Black Drongo is glossy black and resident year-round. If the bird is grey, not black, and seen in winter, it is likely Ashy Drongo.

Crow-billed Drongo: The heavy, stout bill combined with a shallow tail fork, glossy steel-blue black plumage, and white-dotted under wing-coverts separates the Crow-billed Drongo (Baker 1924).
