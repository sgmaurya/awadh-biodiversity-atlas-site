Cucumber or Watermelon? खीरा या तरबूज?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

खीराCucumberCucumis sativus
तरबूजWatermelonCitrullus lanatus

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Watermelon, as the Cucumber profile puts it

Fruit much larger (20–60 cm), round, striped, sweet red flesh; leaves less lobed

तरबूज — विशाल, गोल, मीठा

Cucumber, as the Watermelon profile puts it

Much smaller fruit (15–30 cm); longer, more slender; prickly skin

खीरा — लंबा, पतला, काँटेदार

Cucumber: A tender climbing vine with deeply lobed rough leaves, bright yellow flowers, and long slender green prickly cucumber fruits harvested tender is cucumber; it is the most common garden vegetable.

Watermelon: A sprawling vine with large deeply lobed leaves, large bright yellow flowers, and huge smooth striped melons with red juicy flesh is watermelon; it is the most common summer fruit in village fields.
