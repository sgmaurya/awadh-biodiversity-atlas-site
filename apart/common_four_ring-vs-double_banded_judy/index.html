Common Four-ring, as the Double-banded Judy profile puts it

Common Four-ring]])

Greyish-brown with yellow-ringed black eye-spots; flies weakly in open sunny grasslands rather than deep ravine shade

Common Four-ring: A medium-sized brown butterfly with distinctive four dark eyespots visible on the pale hindwing underside is the Common Four-ring.

Double-banded Judy: A medium-small maroon-brown butterfly with two straight pale bands across the forewings, perching on shaded leaves with half-open wings and pivoting rapidly in circles, is the Double-banded Judy.
