How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Tickell's Leaf Warbler, as the Smoky Warbler profile puts it

Tickell's Leaf Warbler]] (Phylloscopus affinis)

Bright yellow underparts and vivid yellow supercilium; lacks dusky-green suffusion below; feeds in shrubs and open scrub

Smoky Warbler: The distinctly soot-tinged smoky-brown upperparts, absence of wing bars, dull oil-green underparts, and strict attachment to dense low marsh vegetation distinguish the Smoky Warbler from brighter, arboreal leaf-warblers.

Tickell's Leaf Warbler: Very small warbler with a pale yellowish supercilium and two faint wing bars on a greenish wing. Consult detailed guides for separation from similar Phylloscopus warblers.
