Litchi or Soapnut? लीची या रीठा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

लीचीLitchiLitchi chinensis
रीठाSoapnutSapindus mukorossi

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Soapnut, as the Litchi profile puts it

Same family, but fruit is one smooth drupe; leaflets fewer; no terminal leaflet either

पत्तियाँ कम जोड़ी; फल गोल, चिकना, झाग देता है

Litchi, as the Soapnut profile puts it

Same family; leaflets fewer and leathery; fruit red and warty

फल लाल, खुरदरा

Litchi: A medium evergreen tree with grooved pinnate leaves, fragrant greenish flowers in early spring, and bumpy bright red sweet white-fleshed fruits in May–June is litchi; no wild tree looks like it in UP.

Soapnut: A tall tree with long feathery leaves whose yellow-brown fruit lathers when rubbed in water is reetha; nothing else in the district does that.
