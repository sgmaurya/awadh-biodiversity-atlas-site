How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Minnie Root, as the Prostrate Wild Petunia profile puts it

Ruellia tuberosa]]`

Erect herb with swollen spindle-shaped tuberous roots and large dark blue-purple flowers

Minnie Root: Fleshy finger-like tuberous roots combined with showy violet-blue funnel flowers and explosive water-sensitive brown capsules immediately identify Ruellia tuberosa.

Prostrate Wild Petunia: Look for prostrate trailing stems with swollen purple nodes, solitary pale lilac morning flowers, and club-shaped capsules bursting in water.
