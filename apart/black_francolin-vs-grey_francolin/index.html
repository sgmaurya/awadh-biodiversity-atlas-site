Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Grey Francolin, as the Black Francolin profile puts it

Grey Partridge]] (Francolinus pondicerianus) — if range overlaps

Grey (not black); red face; smaller; different call

Black Francolin, as the Grey Francolin profile puts it

Black Francolin]] (Francolinus francolinus)

Heavier; male entirely black with white spots (female similar to Grey Francolin); red legs; calls louder "ka-tee, ka-tee"

Black Francolin: Entirely black francolin with red legs and face patch = male Black Francolin. Brown, heavily barred = female Black Francolin. The distinctive loud call kataak-kataak is diagnostic.

Grey Francolin: A dark partridge 30+ cm long, with red legs and red eye-patch, flushing with a whirring flight from grassland with a loud "hi-hoo" call = Grey Francolin.
