Commander or Common Nawab? कमांडर तितली या कॉमन नवाब तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Commander, as the Common Nawab profile puts it

Commander]])

Bright reddish-chestnut ground colour with a pure white discal band; lacks the dual pointed tails on each hindwing

Commander: A large, powerful nymphalid butterfly with rich reddish-chestnut wings crossed by a brilliant, broad white central band, flying boldly around kadam and kaim trees, is the Commander.

Common Nawab: A large, powerfully built butterfly with a luminous apple-green central band across velvety black wings and two distinct pointed tails on each hindwing is the Common Nawab.
