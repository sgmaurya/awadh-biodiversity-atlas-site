Pointed Stink Bug or Saw-shouldered Stink Bug? नुकीला गंधी कीड़ा या आरी-कंधा गंधी कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नुकीला गंधी कीड़ाPointed Stink BugErthesina acuminata
आरी-कंधा गंधी कीड़ाSaw-shouldered Stink BugNeohalys serricollis

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Saw-shouldered Stink Bug, as the Pointed Stink Bug profile puts it

Pronotal margins strongly saw-toothed; body mottled grey-brown; head broader

कंधे आरी जैसे दांतेदार, सिर चौड़ा

Pointed Stink Bug, as the Saw-shouldered Stink Bug profile puts it

Pronotal margins finely crenulate, not saw-toothed; head sharply pointed

कंधे आरी जैसे नहीं, सिर आगे से बहुत नुकीला

Pointed Stink Bug: Large greyish-brown shield bug with a strongly elongated, acutely triangular pointed head and a pale median line down the scutellum identifies Erthesina acuminata.

Saw-shouldered Stink Bug: Large grey-brown bark-dwelling shield bug with distinctly saw-toothed, serrate lateral margins on the pronotum and an elongate rostrum identifies Neohalys serricollis.
