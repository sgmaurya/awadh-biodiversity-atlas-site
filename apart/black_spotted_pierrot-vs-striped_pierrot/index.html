Black-spotted Pierrot or Striped Pierrot? ब्लैक-स्पॉटेड पियरो तितली या स्ट्राइप्ड पियरो तितली?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Striped Pierrot, as the Black-spotted Pierrot profile puts it

Striped Pierrot]])

Black-spotted Pierrot, as the Striped Pierrot profile puts it

Black-spotted Pierrot]])

Underside markings in basal and discal areas are separated into distinct round dots rather than fused into continuous stripes

Black-spotted Pierrot: A small blue lycaenid butterfly fluttering around ber bushes, whose white underside is dotted with clearly separated, rounded black spots and gleaming marginal metallic specks, is the Black-spotted Pierrot.

Striped Pierrot: A small lycaenid butterfly flitting around ber trees whose creamy underside features confluent black basal stripes rather than isolated dots is the Striped Pierrot.
