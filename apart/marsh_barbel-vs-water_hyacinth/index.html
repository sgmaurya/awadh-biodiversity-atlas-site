Marsh Barbel or Water Hyacinth? तालमखाना या जलकुंभी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Water Hyacinth, as the Marsh Barbel profile puts it

Floating plant with swollen petioles; purple-blue flowers in a spike; leaves broader and more succulent

तैरता हुआ पौधा, फूल नीले-बैंगनी, मोटी पत्तियाँ

Marsh Barbel: A water-rooted plant with variable leaves (thin in water, broad in air) and purple axillary flowers, rooting at nodes to spread, is Hygrophila auriculata.

Water Hyacinth: Floating mat + round shiny leaves + swollen spongy petioles + feathery purple-black roots = water hyacinth. The inflated petiole is unmistakable once seen.

RootsAdventitious from nodes; feathery submerged rootsDense, feathery, dark purple-black, hanging suspended in water; 30–100 cm long
HabitatStrictly aquatic; cannot grow in dry land—
Form—Free-floating perennial aquatic herb forming dense interlocking mats
Petioles—Swollen, bulbous, spongy, and air-filled; 5–12 cm long; provide buoyancy
Stolons—Horizontal runners producing clone daughter plants, enabling rapid vegetative spread
