How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

House Swift, as the Crested Treeswift profile puts it

House Swift]] (Apus affinis)

Much smaller, sooty-black plumage with bright white throat and conspicuous square white rump; cannot perch on branches; blunt tail

Crested Treeswift: The erect pointed crest, long deeply forked tail, silky blue-grey body, chestnut cheeks in males, and unique habit of perching upright on bare branches distinguish the Crested Treeswift.

House Swift: Stocky dark body, prominent white rump band, white throat, square-ended tail, and loud screaming group-flight identify the House Swift.

PerchingRegularly perches upright on exposed bare twigs, unlike true swifts—
Colouration—Sooty blackish-brown body; contrasting bright white rump band and white throat patch
Wings & Tail—Long, broad, sickle-shaped wings; short, almost square tail (lacks fork)
Nesting—Messy mud-and-feather nests glued under concrete eaves, arches, or old structures
Flight—Super-fast, swooping flight with rapid wing beats and brief glides; highly social
