How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bay-backed Shrike, as the Large Cuckooshrike profile puts it

Bay-backed Shrike]] (Lanius vittatus)

Smaller (19–20 cm); hooked bill; grey back with rufous wings

Bay-backed Shrike: Bay-backed shrike is a small shrike with a distinctively warm chestnut or bay back (never black or grey). If the back is black, it is a long-tailed shrike. If the back is duller and the bird is a winter visitor, it may be a brown shrike.

Large Cuckooshrike: A large (25–28 cm), uniformly grey-brown perching bird with a stout hooked bill and rounded shape, gleaning caterpillars in tree foliage, is a Large Cuckooshrike. Smaller shrike species are brightly coloured or patterned and sit on exposed perches.
