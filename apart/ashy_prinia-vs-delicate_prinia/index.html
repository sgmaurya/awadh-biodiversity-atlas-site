How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Ashy Prinia, as the Delicate Prinia profile puts it

Ashy Prinia]] (Prinia socialis)

Ashy prinia is larger, greyer overall, with a shorter tail

Delicate Prinia: Delicate prinia is a very small, plainly coloured warbler with a distinctively long tail relative to body size. If the bird is larger and greyer, it is an ashy prinia. If in dense jungle rather than grassland, likely a jungle prinia.
