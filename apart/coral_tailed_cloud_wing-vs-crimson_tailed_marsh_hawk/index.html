Coral-tailed Cloud Wing or Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk? कोरल-टेल्ड क्लाउड विंग या क्रिमसन-टेल्ड मार्श हॉक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk, as the Coral-tailed Cloud Wing profile puts it

Pale pinkish, not dark; clear wings; white pruinose coating

पीली घंटी

Coral-tailed Cloud Wing: Small to medium dragonfly with characteristically clouded (smoky) wings and dark body with bright coral-red abdomen tip (in males) distinguishes this species.

Crimson-tailed Marsh Hawk: Medium dragonfly with crimson-red abdomen tip and male body appearing pale pinkish-white due to pruinose coating distinguishes this species.
