How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Large Oakblue, as the Common Gem profile puts it

Arhopala amantes]]

Substantially larger with dark chocolate-brown underside and metallic blue-green tornal spot

Common Gem: Notice the small size, sudden flashes of brilliant turquoise in high tree perches, and the intricate red-brown marbled underside without tails.

Large Oakblue: Look for the large size, vibrant metallic purple-blue upperside in flight, and the intricate dark brown underside with green tornal scales when settled.
