Jamun or Tendu? जामुन या तेंदू?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

जामुनJamunSyzygium cumini
तेंदूTenduDiospyros melanoxylon

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Jamun, as the Tendu profile puts it

Leaves smaller, lanceolate; flowers in clusters; fruit different

जामुन — छोटी पत्ती, अलग फूल

Jamun: Large evergreen tree with opposite, smooth, oblong leaves smelling of turpentine when crushed, greenish-white flowers, and purple-black ovoid berries in June–July identify the Jamun tree.

Tendu: A deciduous tree with smooth, glossy, entire, leathery leaves and sticky black sweet fruit in summer, is tendu.
