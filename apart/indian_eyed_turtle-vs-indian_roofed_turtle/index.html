Indian Eyed Turtle or Indian Roofed Turtle? इंडियन आइड कछुआ या पचेरा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Roofed Turtle, as the Indian Eyed Turtle profile puts it

Pangshura tecta

Features a high peaked vertebral spike on the third vertebral scute, and a bright red crescent behind each eye (Smith 1931)

Indian Eyed Turtle: Domed freshwater turtle with eye-like ringed spots on each costal shield, a pointed snout, and three pale yellow stripes along each side of the neck.

Indian Roofed Turtle: A small dark turtle with a peaked carapace, yellow head stripes, yellow-streaked limbs, and webbed feet basking on a river bank is an Indian Roofed Turtle and is entirely harmless and protected.

Size—Carapace length 20–30 cm; moderately small to medium
Head—Olive-brown; two yellowish stripes from eye to ear region
Limbs—Olive-brown with yellow streaks or bands; webbed feet adapted for swimming
Behaviour—Aquatic; often seen basking on banks; alert and swift in water
