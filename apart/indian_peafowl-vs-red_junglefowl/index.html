How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Peafowl, as the Red Junglefowl profile puts it

Indian Peafowl]] (Pavo cristatus)

Substantially larger size; male has massive iridescent blue-green eyed train; female dull grey-brown with pale throat

Red Junglefowl: The erect red comb, golden neck-hackles, arched glossy sickle tail feathers, and slender horizontal posture distinguish the wild Red Junglefowl from other regional gamebirds.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Indian PeafowlRed Junglefowl
SizeLarge bird; Male 1.9–2.3 m total length (including train); Female 1.0–1.1 m; weight 3–6 kg—
Male plumageIridescent blue-green head, neck, and breast; long ornamental train with eyespots (ocelli)Fiery golden-red neck hackles, dark maroon mantle, glossy green-black arched tail
Female plumageMottled brown overall; white face and belly; metallic green collar on neck; no trainCryptic mottled rufous-brown, finely vermiculated with dark brown
CrestSpatulated fan-like blue-green crest on top of the head in both sexes—
In flightPowerful but heavy short-distance flier; loud wing beats; brown flight feathers—
VoiceLoud, screaming, far-carrying pi-yaon call, especially active during monsoon—
Habit—Cursorial gamebird inhabiting sal forests and dense undergrowth
Head Adornments—Male has erect fleshy red comb and pendulous throat wattles; white ear-patch
Tail—Long, arched, laterally compressed sickle feathers in male; short in female
Tarsus—Long, slate-grey to greenish-grey; male armed with a sharp curved spur
Carriage—Slender, horizontal, horizontal tail carriage differing from heavy domestic breeds

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
