How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Common Greenshank, as the Pied Avocet profile puts it

Common Greenshank]] (Tringa nebularia)

Greenish legs; straight bill; smaller overall

Common Greenshank: A large pale wader with greenish-grey legs, upturned bill, and white rump — is a Common Greenshank. Larger and paler than Redshank, with greenish (not red) legs. Often solitary. When in doubt, check bill angle — Greenshank's bill is subtly upturned.

Pied Avocet: A large black-and-white wader with a distinctive upturned bill and grey-blue legs is a Pied Avocet. The upturned bill and white body with black cap and back pattern are unmistakable.
