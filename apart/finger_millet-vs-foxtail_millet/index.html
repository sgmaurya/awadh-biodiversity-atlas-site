Finger Millet or Foxtail Millet? मंडुआ या काकुन?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Foxtail Millet, as the Finger Millet profile puts it

Foxtail Millet]])

Finger Millet, as the Foxtail Millet profile puts it

Finger Millet]])

Panicles are finger-like with 4–5 branches; much shorter plant

Finger Millet: A compact grass with 4–8 finger-like panicles spreading from the top, each bearing tiny dark grains, distinguishes finger millet.

Foxtail Millet: An erect grass with a dense, slightly drooping, cylindrical spike resembling a fox's tail, containing tiny round grains, distinguishes foxtail millet.
