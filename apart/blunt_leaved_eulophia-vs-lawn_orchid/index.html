Blunt-leaved Eulophia or Lawn Orchid? ब्लंट-लीव्ड यूलोफिया या लॉन ऑर्किड?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Lawn Orchid, as the Blunt-leaved Eulophia profile puts it

लॉन ऑर्किड (Zeuxine strateumatica)]]

Much smaller dwarf orchid; dense spike of small white flowers; narrow grass-like foliage

Blunt-leaved Eulophia, as the Lawn Orchid profile puts it

ब्लंट-लीव्ड यूलोफिया (Eulophia obtusa)]]

Much larger plant; broad pleated leaves; nodding yellow flowers in lax raceme

Blunt-leaved Eulophia: A tall marshland orchid with pleated leaves and large nodding yellow flowers bearing lilac veins on the lip in wet Terai meadows is Eulophia obtusa.

Lawn Orchid: A diminutive winter-blooming orchid with grass-like leaves and a dense upright spike of waxy white-and-yellow flowers growing in wet turf is Zeuxine strateumatica.
