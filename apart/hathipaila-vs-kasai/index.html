Hathipaila or Kasai? हाथीपायला या कसई?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kasai, as the Hathipaila profile puts it

Simple leaves with straight parallel veins; thorny young trunk; small drupes

पत्तियाँ सरल, तने पर कांटे

Hathipaila: Swollen fluted elephant-foot trunk base, papery peeling bark, bipinnate leaves with rhomboid leaflets, and long flat chestnut pods hanging from cliff-clinging branches distinguish Indopiptadenia oudhensis.

Kasai: Alternate rigid leaves with 15–25 pairs of prominent straight parallel veins running to the margins, coupled with conical spines on younger trunks, identify Bridelia retusa.
