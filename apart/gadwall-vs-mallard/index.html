Gadwall or Mallard? गैडवॉल बत्तख या नीलसर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

नीलसरMallardAnas platyrhynchos

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Mallard, as the Gadwall profile puts it

Much larger; male has glossy green head; different overall appearance

बहुत बड़ी, हरा सिर

Gadwall: A medium-sized dabbling duck with grey-and-black coloration (males) and white speculum visible in flight — is a Gadwall. In flight, the white wing patch is diagnostic.

Mallard: A large duck with a glossy green head and white chest (males) — is a Mallard. This is by far the most familiar duck species globally. Females shown blue speculum in flight and paired with green-headed males in winter.
