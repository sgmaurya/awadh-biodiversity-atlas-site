Brahmi or Asiatic Pennywort? ब्राह्मी या मंडूकपर्णी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

ब्राह्मीBrahmiBacopa monnieri
मंडूकपर्णीAsiatic PennywortCentella asiatica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Asiatic Pennywort, as the Brahmi profile puts it

Leaves are round or kidney-shaped with crenate margins; stalks are long and thin; flowers are reddish; grows in damp soils

गोटू कोला / मंडूकपर्णी — गोल कटे हुए पत्ते, लम्बा डंठल

Brahmi, as the Asiatic Pennywort profile puts it

Leaves are thick, fleshy, spatula-shaped with smooth edges, sessile; flowers are larger, blue-white.

ब्राह्मी — मांसल चिकने पत्ते, तने से सीधे जुड़े हुए

Brahmi: Low creeping habit, succulent spatulate opposite leaves with a bitter taste, and tiny five-petaled white or pale blue flowers identify the Brahmi plant.

Side by side आमने-सामने

BrahmiAsiatic Pennywort
Size & HabitSmall, creeping, succulent perennial herb; forms dense green mats on wet soil or shallow water—
StemsSoft, fleshy, prostrate stems (10–30 cm long) that root easily at the nodes (rooting nodes)Slender, reddish or green stolons that root at nodes where they touch damp soil
LeavesOblanceolate to spatulate, thick, fleshy, opposite leaves (1–2 cm long) with entire margins and rounded tipsRound, kidney-shaped (reniform) leaves (1–4 cm diameter) with crenate (scalloped) margins and long, erect petioles
FlowersSmall, solitary, bell-shaped flowers (8–10 mm diameter) with five petals; colour ranges from white to pale blue or lavenderTiny, sessile, white to pinkish-purple flowers (1–2 mm size) borne in small, flat-topped clusters (umbels) near the leaf base
FruitsSmall, ovoid, capsules containing numerous tiny, dark brown seeds—
Form—Small, low-growing, creeping perennial herb; spreads via long, slender horizontal runners (stolons)
Taste—Leaves have a characteristically mild, sweet-bitter, herbal taste and green aroma when crushed

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
