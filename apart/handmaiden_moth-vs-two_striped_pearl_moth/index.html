Handmaiden Moth or Two-striped Pearl Moth? हैंडमेडन पतंगा या द्वि-धारी मोती पतंगा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Handmaiden Moth, as the Two-striped Pearl Moth profile puts it

Body black with orange rings; diurnal; wings with small oval hyaline dots

शरीर काला और नारंगी, दिन में उड़ने वाला, छोटे गोल पारदर्शी बिंदु

Handmaiden Moth: Small diurnal black moth with transparent spots on both wings and two bright orange-red rings on a dark body identifies Amata cyssea.

Two-striped Pearl Moth: Reddish-brown forewings bearing two large square glassy transparent windows combined with a mulberry or fig feeding habit identify Glyphodes bivitralis.
