Milkweed Bug or Red Cotton Bug? मिल्कवीड बग या लाल कपास कीड़ा?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Cotton Bug, as the Milkweed Bug profile puts it

Larger (10–14 mm); more elongate; different host plants (cotton, [[hibiscus\

hibiscus]]); red and black pattern less distinct

Milkweed Bug: A small (10–12 mm) red and black bug feeding in large aggregations on Calotropis flowers or seed pods, with fully developed functional wings — this is the Milkweed Bug. Larger red-and-black bugs with different feeding ecology belong to other families.
