How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Impatiens Hawkmoth, as the Rosetta Hawkmoth profile puts it

Has two distinct silvery-white longitudinal stripes along the abdomen

पेट पर दो चमकीली चांदी जैसी सफेद धारियां होती हैं

Impatiens Hawkmoth: A small brown hawkmoth with a swift, erratic hovering flight at balsam flowers in evening light, combined with small brown larvae on impatiens foliage, identifies the Impatiens Hawkmoth.

Rosetta Hawkmoth: A sleek buff-brown hawkmoth with an oblique streaked forewing, a tiny black discal dot, and pinkish-red on the hindwing base identifies Hippotion rosetta.

Habit—Medium-sized, sleek aerodynamic hawkmoth capable of hovering flight
Forewing—Pale buff to greyish-brown with delicate longitudinal oblique streaks
Discal dot—Minute, sharp, distinct black dot in the forewing cell
Hindwing—Dark fuscous-brown with a rosy-pink or pale reddish basal patch
Abdomen—Sleekly tapered with delicate paired pale dorsal and lateral lines
Caterpillar—Green or brown with prominent snake-like eye-spots on anterior segments
