Climbing Perch or Cuchia? कवई या कुचिया?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कवईClimbing PerchAnabas testudineus
कुचियाCuchiaMonopterus cuchia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Climbing Perch, as the Cuchia profile puts it

Much smaller (5–20 cm); dorsal and anal fins present; no burrows

बहुत छोटी, पंख हैं

Climbing Perch: A hard, spiny, grey fish with a saw-edged gill cover that survives out of water and walks on wet ground is the climbing perch.

Cuchia: A long, dark, eel-like fish with no side fins, a single gill opening below the head, and a single nostril at the mouth, living in mud, is the cuchia; the air-breathing chamber and dormancy distinguish it from true eels.
