How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Black-hooded Oriole, as the Maroon Oriole profile puts it

Black-hooded Oriole]] (Oriolus xanthornus)

Bright golden-yellow body with complete jet-black hood and pinkish bill, lacking maroon or crimson tones

Black-hooded Oriole: A bright yellow bird with a black head and upper body is a male Black-hooded Oriole. Greenish-yellow birds with longer bills are females. Entirely black or different pattern males are other species.

Maroon Oriole: The glossy black hood combined with deep crimson-maroon body in the male, or black hood with streaked white underparts in the female, instantly identifies the Maroon Oriole (Baker 1926).
