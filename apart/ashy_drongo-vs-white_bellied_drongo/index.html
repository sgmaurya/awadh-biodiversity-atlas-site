Ashy Drongo or White-bellied Drongo? ऐशी ड्रोंगो या व्हाइट-बेलीड ड्रोंगो?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

White-bellied Drongo, as the Ashy Drongo profile puts it

White-bellied Drongo]] (Dicrurus caerulescens)

Smaller; white belly patch; white-tipped tail feathers

Ashy Drongo, as the White-bellied Drongo profile puts it

Ashy Drongo]] (Dicrurus leucophaeus)

Greyish overall; no white belly; different habitat

Ashy Drongo: Ashy Drongo is pale grey throughout with a long forked tail. Black Drongo is glossy black and resident year-round. If the bird is grey, not black, and seen in winter, it is likely Ashy Drongo.

White-bellied Drongo: White-bellied Drongo is distinguished by its glossy blue-black plumage with a striking white belly. The combination of white belly, crested head, red eye, and small size is unique among drongos.
