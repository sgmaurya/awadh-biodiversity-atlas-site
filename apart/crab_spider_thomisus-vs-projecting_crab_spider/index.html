How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Crab Spider Thomisus, as the Projecting Crab Spider profile puts it

Thomisus unidentatus

Epigyne and palpal organ morphology differs; ocular projections less divergent; body paler

Crab Spider Thomisus: Flat, crab-like spider on flower with legs splayed sideways, colour matching the flower, = Thomisus unidentatus.

Projecting Crab Spider: Crab-like ambush spider on flowers with distinct conical horn-like ocular projections and two posterolateral abdominal humps.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Crab Spider ThomisusProjecting Crab Spider
Size6–10 mm body length; legspan 15–20 mm—
BodyFlat, crab-like; legs spread sideways—
ColourHighly variable — white, pink, green, yellow, brown; matches flower colour—
PostureSplayed, sitting flat on flower; front legs raised, ready to grab—
Hunting styleAmbush predator; waits motionless on flowers—
HabitatFlowers, flower buds, low vegetation—
Lateral gait—Laterigrade leg orientation allowing effortless sideways and backward crab-like walking
Carapace horns—Prominent horn-like conical projections or tubercles rising above the lateral eyes
Abdomen shape—Pentagonal or trapezoidal abdomen, broadest behind, carrying two conical posterolateral humps
Coloration—Chalky-white to pale yellowish-green, often marked with reddish-brown spots or transverse bands
Ambush habit—Sits motionlessly concealed within flower blossoms with front legs spread wide in an embrace
