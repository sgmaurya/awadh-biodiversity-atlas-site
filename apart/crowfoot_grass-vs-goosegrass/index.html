Crowfoot Grass or Goosegrass? मकरा या कोदई घास?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

मकराCrowfoot GrassDactyloctenium aegyptium
कोदई घासGoosegrassEleusine indica

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Goosegrass, as the Crowfoot Grass profile puts it

Spikes are straight, not curved; spikelets lack prominent spines; occurs on compacted non-saline soil

कोदई — सीधी स्पाइक, कम काँटे

Crowfoot Grass, as the Goosegrass profile puts it

Spikes are strongly curved (claw-like), not straight; spikelets have spines

क्राउनफुट — बेंट स्पाइक, काँटों वाली

Crowfoot Grass: A low spreading grass with 4–8 strongly curved, claw-like spikes, found on damp, alkaline or salt-affected soil = Dactyloctenium aegyptium (crowfoot grass).

Goosegrass: A low, spreading grass with 3–10 straight finger-like spikes radiating from the stem top, tiny dark grain, and found on compacted disturbed soil = Eleusine indica (goosegrass).
