House Fly or Stable Fly? घरेलू मक्खी या डंक मक्खी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

घरेलू मक्खीHouse FlyMusca domestica
डंक मक्खीStable FlyStomoxys calcitrans

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Stable Fly, as the House Fly profile puts it

Slightly larger (7–8 mm); has a dark, pointed proboscis that protrudes forward; painful bite

काटने वाली मक्खी — लम्बी सूई जैसी सूंड़, काटती है

House Fly, as the Stable Fly profile puts it

Non-biting; proboscis is sponging type (folded); four stripes less distinct

घर की मक्खी, काटती नहीं

House Fly: Uniform grey-brown, small (6–7 mm), four dark thoracic stripes, found indoors and around food: House Fly.

Stable Fly: Small grey-brown fly with four pale thoracic stripes and long straight proboscis, biting painfully on livestock or humans; larvae in cattle manure: Stable Fly (Stomoxys calcitrans).
