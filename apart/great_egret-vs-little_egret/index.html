Great Egret or Little Egret? बड़ा बगुला या छोटा बगुला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Little Egret, as the Great Egret profile puts it

Smaller (55–65 cm); black bill with yellow lores; yellow feet; more active

छोटा, पीले पैर

Great Egret, as the Little Egret profile puts it

Great Egret]] (Ardea alba)

Much larger (90 cm); extremely long S-shaped neck; yellow bill; black feet

Great Egret: A large, pure-white heron standing alone in shallow water with a long yellow bill and black legs — is a Great Egret. The size and solitary hunting behaviour distinguish it from smaller egrets.

Little Egret: Slender white body, long black bill, black legs with bright yellow feet, and active wading foraging in shallow water identify the Little Egret.
