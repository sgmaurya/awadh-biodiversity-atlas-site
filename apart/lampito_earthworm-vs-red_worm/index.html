How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Worm, as the Pasture Earthworm profile puts it

Smaller (6–12 cm); reddish-purple with yellow stripe between segments; surface dweller, not deep burrower

छोटा, धारीदार, सतह पर

Pasture Earthworm: Medium brown earthworm (10–20 cm), deep burrows in soil, saddle-like clitellum: Lampito mauritii.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Pasture EarthwormRed Worm
SizeMedium earthworm; body length 10–20 cm; diameter 5–8 mmSmall to medium worm; body length 60–120 mm; diameter 3–5 mm
ColourBrown or dark brown; sometimes with reddish tinge on anterior endReddish-purple to dark red, with alternating pale yellow rings between segments (tiger-like stripes)
SegmentsApproximately 100–150 segments; cylindrical body80–120 segments; body is cylindrical and slightly flattened at the rear
ClitellumSaddle-like thickened band, typically reddish, located around segments 14–16Thickened pale ring located around segments 24–32; prominent in adult worms
Mucus sheathProduces a thin mucus layer on the body surface—
TextureSmooth, moist, slightly slimy to the touch—
BehaviourBurrows in soil; emerges when soil is saturated or during monsoon rains—
Movement—Active wiggler; moves rapidly when exposed to light, thrashing its body
Habitat—Found strictly in rich organic compost, manure piles, and vermi-beds; does not burrow into normal garden soil
Odour—Releases a pungent, yellowish, foul-smelling coelomic fluid when handled or squeezed
