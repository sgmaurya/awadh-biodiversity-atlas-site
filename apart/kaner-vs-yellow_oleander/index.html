Kaner or Yellow Oleander? कनेर या पीली कनेर?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

कनेरKanerNerium oleander
पीली कनेरYellow OleanderCascabela thevetia

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Kaner, as the Yellow Oleander profile puts it

Leaves are broader and arranged in whorls of three; flowers are pink or red, flat-topped (not tubular); also highly toxic.

लाल कनेर — विपरीत चौड़ी पत्तियां, गुलाबी/लाल चपटे फूल

Kaner: A cultivated shrub with narrow, leathery, dark green leaves and large, showy, fragrant pink, white, red, or yellow flowers, with long brown pods, is kaner. All parts are extremely poisonous.
