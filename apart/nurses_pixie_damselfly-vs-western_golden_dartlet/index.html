How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Male thorax and basal abdomen segments 1–4 are bright scarlet-crimson; lacks golden-yellow thorax

Nurse's Pixie Damselfly: A tiny damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose male displays a crimson-red thorax, crimson basal abdomen, yellow midriff, and bright azure-blue terminal spot is Nurse's Pixie Damselfly.

Western Golden Dartlet: A tiny damselfly perching low in wetland grass whose male displays a green-and-black thorax, bright golden-yellow basal abdomen, and an azure-blue terminal patch on segment 8 is the Western Golden Dartlet.
