How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Coucal, as the Sirkeer Malkoha profile puts it

Greater Coucal]] (Centropus sinensis)

Deep glossy black body with bright chestnut-bay wings and ruby-red eyes; lacks the bright cherry-red bill and spiny neck shafts

Greater Coucal: Pheasant-like shape, glossy black body contrasting with bright chestnut wings, bright red eyes, and a slow walking habit on the ground identify the Greater Coucal.

Sirkeer Malkoha: The stout cherry-red bill with a yellow tip, sandy-brown plumage with stiff spiny shaft-streaks, and white-tipped graduated tail distinguish the Sirkeer Malkoha from coucals and other cuckoos.
