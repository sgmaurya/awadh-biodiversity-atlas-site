Clown Knifefish or Garua Bachcha? चीतल या गरुआ?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

चीतलClown KnifefishChitala chitala
गरुआGarua BachchaClupisoma garua

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Garua Bachcha, as the Clown Knifefish profile puts it

Catfish; barbels; different body shape; smaller eyes

कैटफिश — तार होते हैं

Clown Knifefish: A large, extremely flattened, dark fish 50–100 cm long with a wave-like fin running the entire length of back and belly, blotchy coloration, and large mouth with sharp teeth, found in rivers and deep ponds, is Chitala chitala; fin ray counts and tooth morphology confirm identification.

Garua Bachcha: A medium catfish with four pairs of very long, thread-like barbels (especially the maxillary pair) found in rocky flowing water is a Garua Bachcha.
