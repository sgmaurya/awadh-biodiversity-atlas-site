How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

River Tern, as the Little Tern profile puts it

River Tern]] (Sterna aurantia)

Much larger (33–35 cm); orange bill; deeper tail fork

Little Tern: The tiniest tern species in eastern UP, with white plumage, black cap, yellow bill, and light tail fork, plunge-diving for very small fish, is a Little Tern. Size difference from River Tern is immediately obvious.

River Tern: White tern with black cap + orange bill + deeply forked white tail + grey back = River Tern. Hovers and plunge-dives for fish. Medium-sized tern with distinctive orange bill.
