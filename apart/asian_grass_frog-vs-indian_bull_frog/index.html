Asian Grass Frog or Indian Bull Frog? फुदकी मेंढक या पहाड़ मेंढक?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Indian Bull Frog, as the Asian Grass Frog profile puts it

Much larger (up to 170 mm); green and yellow breeding colors; heavy body; massive leaps

बड़ा मेंढक — आकार में बहुत बड़ा और भारी शरीर

Asian Grass Frog: Medium size (3–5 cm), rough warty skin with long wrinkles, pointed snout, and a light line running down the spine in many individuals.

Indian Bull Frog: Very large green frog in pond + bright yellow breeding male + deep roaring call + monsoon season eastern UP = Indian Bull Frog. The size and yellow breeding color together are unique.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Tympanum—Large, circular ear disc behind the eye; in males, it is larger than the eye
