How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Red Cotton Bug, as the Black-horned Cotton Stainer profile puts it

Red Cotton Bug]])

White bands across ventral abdomen; black spot on each corium; smaller body; feeds on cotton

Black-horned Cotton Stainer: Large orange-red bug with completely black antennae and black wing membrane, lacking distinct circular black wing spots, found on tree trunks or flowers = Probergrothius nigricornis.

AggregationsGathers in conspicuous clusters on host tree trunks and surrounding leaf litter—
Size—14–18 mm; elongated, capsule-shaped body
Colour—Bright red-orange body with black triangular markings; black head and legs
Nymphs—Bright red, wingless; all 5 instars distinctly red — look like small adults
Aggregation—Found in groups — adults and nymphs together on the same plant
Movement—Slow-moving; does not fly readily when disturbed
Host plants—Cotton, okra, hibiscus, Calotropis — always on Malvaceae or Apocynaceae
