Cattle Egret or Grey Heron? गाय बगुला या ग्रे बगुला?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

गाय बगुलाCattle EgretBubulcus ibis
ग्रे बगुलाGrey HeronArdea cinerea

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Cattle Egret, as the Grey Heron profile puts it

Much smaller (48–53 cm); entirely white plumage (with buff feathers when breeding); short bill; follows cattle

गाय बगुला — बहुत छोटा, सफ़ेद रंग, खेतों में घूमना

Cattle Egret: Small size, stocky neck, yellow bill (orange in breeding), yellowish-green legs, and terrestrial foraging behavior alongside grazing livestock.

Grey Heron: Very tall grey-and-white wading bird with a black superciliary crest and a yellow dagger-like bill, standing motionless in shallow water, is a Grey Heron.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Cattle EgretGrey Heron
SizeSmall, stocky egret (48–53 cm total length); neck relatively short and thickVery tall wading bird (90–100 cm total length); wingspan around 155–175 cm
ColourationEntirely white in non-breeding plumage; yellow bill and yellowish-green legsAsh-grey upperparts; white head and neck with black streaks down the foreneck; white underparts
Breeding PlumageDistinct orange-buff (golden) feathers on crown, breast, and back in monsoon—
BillRelatively short, stout, and yellow (turns orange-pink during peak breeding)—
LegsYellowish-grey or olive-green; turn bright pink-red for a few days during breeding—
BehaviourCommensal association with grazing cattle; runs alongside cows to catch flushed prey—
Head Pattern—Black stripe runs from the eye to the back of the head, ending in a long black crest
Bare Parts—Bill is yellowish-horn, turning brighter orange-yellow when breeding; legs are greenish-brown
Flight—Heavy, slow wingbeats; neck pulled back into a tight 'S'-shape; legs extend far behind the tail
Hunting—Stands completely motionless in shallow water or stalks very slowly

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
