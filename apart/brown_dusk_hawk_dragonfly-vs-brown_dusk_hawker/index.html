How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Brown Dusk Hawk Dragonfly, as the Brown Dusk Hawker profile puts it

Brown Dusk Hawk Dragonfly]])

Noticeably smaller; abdomen deeply petiolate (waisted); wings carry small dark brown basal spots

Brown Dusk Hawk Dragonfly: A slender, chocolate-brown dragonfly with huge glowing emerald-green eyes and a paper-thin constricted waist, flying swiftly low over water only at dusk, is the Brown Dusk Hawk.

Brown Dusk Hawker: A very large, long-bodied dragonfly with a black T-shaped mark on the frons and glowing sapphire-green eyes, flying with astounding speed over water strictly at dusk, is the Brown Dusk Hawker.
