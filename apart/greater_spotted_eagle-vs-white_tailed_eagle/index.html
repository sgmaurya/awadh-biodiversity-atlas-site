How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Greater Spotted Eagle, as the White-tailed Eagle profile puts it

Greater Spotted Eagle]] (Clanga clanga)

Fully feathered tarsus to base of toes, dark rounded tail, broader wings, different silhouette (Baker 1928, p. 69)

Greater Spotted Eagle: Greater Spotted Eagle is distinguished by its large size, dark brown plumage with white spots on wings and back, yellow eyes and legs, and powerful flight. The white spots are more prominent than in Indian Spotted Eagle.

White-tailed Eagle: The massive size, pale yellowish head and neck, huge yellow bill, unfeathered yellow lower tarsus, and all-white tail identify the adult White-tailed Eagle (Baker 1928, p. 110).
