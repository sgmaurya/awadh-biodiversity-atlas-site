How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Gangetic Mystus, as the Garua Bachcha profile puts it

Smaller; three pairs of barbels; serrated spines

तीन दाढ़ी

Gangetic Mystus: A small catfish with three pairs of barbels and serrated pectoral spines is a Gangetic Mystus. The spine causes pain if touched.

Garua Bachcha: A medium catfish with four pairs of very long, thread-like barbels (especially the maxillary pair) found in rocky flowing water is a Garua Bachcha.
