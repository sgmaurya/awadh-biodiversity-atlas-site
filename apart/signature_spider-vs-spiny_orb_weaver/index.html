Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Signature Spider, as the Spiny Orb-weaver profile puts it

Much larger; silver-and-yellow striped body; builds large vertical webs with a bold X-shaped silk cross

हस्ताक्षरी मकड़ी — बड़ा आकार, एक्स-आकार की पट्टी

Spiny Orb-weaver: Small, hard, flat-bodied spider (8–10 mm) with a white/yellow top bearing six sharp black spines, sitting in the center of a vertical orb web, is the Spiny Orb-weaver.

Side by side आमने-सामने

Signature SpiderSpiny Orb-weaver
Female size14–20 mm body length; legspan 30–40 mm—
Male sizeMuch smaller — 5–6 mm; brownish, less boldly marked—
Body colourBold yellow with black and silvery-white transverse bands — strongly contrasting—
LegsYellow with black bands; held in X-shape (legs paired I+II, III+IV)—
WebVertical orb, 30–50 cm diameter; anchored in low vegetation at 50 cm–2 m—
StabilimentumThick zigzag silk ribbon through web centre — the single most diagnostic feature—
PoseFemale sits head-down at web hub; legs paired in clear X-pattern—
SeasonPeak September–November; monsoon and post-monsoon—
Abdomen—Hard, leathery, wider than long, resembling a crab shell; dorsum is white or yellow with black spots and markings
Spines—Six sharp, black spines projecting from the margin: two on each side and two at the rear
Size—Small spider; female body length 8–10 mm, male is tiny (3–4 mm) and lacks large spines
Web Type—Perfect, vertical, circular orb web suspended between branches, often with white silk tufts
Position—Sits directly in the open hub (center) of the web during the day

Profiled, not yet recorded. This account is written from published sources for eastern Uttar Pradesh. Nobody has yet confirmed this species in the project area, so nothing here is an eyewitness report.
