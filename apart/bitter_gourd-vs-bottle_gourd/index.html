Bitter Gourd or Bottle Gourd? करेला या लौकी?

Two look-alikes of the Basti–Ayodhya countryside side by side: what each profile says tells them apart, then their identification tables in one view.

How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Bottle Gourd, as the Bitter Gourd profile puts it

Vine is much larger; leaves are broad, velvety; flowers are white, nocturnal; fruit is smooth, sweet

लौकी — बड़े मखमली पत्ते, सफेद फूल, चिकने फल

Bitter Gourd, as the Bottle Gourd profile puts it

Bitter Gourd]] (Momordica charantia)

Vine has smaller, deeply lobed leaves; flowers are yellow; fruit is warty, green, and bitter

Bitter Gourd: Slender climbing vine with deeply lobed leaves, yellow flowers, and warty green spindle-shaped fruits that split open to show red-coated seeds when orange, is the Bitter Gourd.

Bottle Gourd: Climbing annual vine with broad velvety kidney-shaped leaves, large nocturnal white flowers, and elongated or bottle-shaped pale green edible fruits that dry into hard woody shells, is the Bottle Gourd (Lauki).
