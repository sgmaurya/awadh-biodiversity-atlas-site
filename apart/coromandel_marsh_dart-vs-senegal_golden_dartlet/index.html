How to tell them apart कैसे पहचानें

Coromandel Marsh Dart, as the Senegal Golden Dartlet profile puts it

Bright red, not yellow-orange; longer body

लाल व्याधपतंग

Coromandel Marsh Dart: Tiny damselfly with bright red (male) or bright yellow-orange (female) body, slow fluttering flight over marshy vegetation, often in tandem pairs, is the Coromandel Marsh Dart.

Senegal Golden Dartlet: Small damselfly with bright yellow-orange head and thorax (male) or polymorphic females (yellow, green, or brown forms), in shallow vegetation-rich water.
