Migrants and visitors प्रवासी

52 winter visitors from Central Asia, Siberia, Europe and the Himalayas; 7 summer visitors that come with the heat and the monsoon; and the insects, fish and birds that move with the season. Arrival and departure months are from the standard handbooks and three published surveys of wetlands in and beside the region (Bakhira, Parvati Arga and the Ayodhya jheels), not from our own records.
